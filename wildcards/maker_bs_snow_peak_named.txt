Styled in a design language of calm Japanese minimalism with refined metal craft. Brushed titanium, satin stainless steel, dark cast iron and pale natural wood combined in slim, precise, softly rounded geometric forms that stack, nest and pack flat. Palette is silver, charcoal, black, sand, olive and off-white with very sparse colour. Surfaces are matte and uncluttered, edges are thin and clean, and joints are neat and understated. Quiet, thoughtful, tidy and made to last. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
