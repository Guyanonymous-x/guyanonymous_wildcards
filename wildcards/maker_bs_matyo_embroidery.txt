In the style of Matyo folk embroidery of Mezokovesd in Hungary. Embroidery is worked in dense, thick, glossy cotton or silk thread on black, white or dark blue linen and cotton cloth, on blouses, aprons, tablecloths, cushion covers, pillowcases and scarves. The main motifs are huge, full-blown roses, bold tulips, carnations, leaf sprays, buds, and tendrils in a heavily stylised form, often with a central flower surrounded by several smaller blossoms and swirling leaves, in mirrored symmetry. The palette is bright, high-contrast and joyful, with red, pink, magenta, orange, yellow, light blue and green on black, or navy, crimson and deep green on white. Stitches are tight satin stitch with a slightly raised, padded texture and often black or gold outlines. The look is richly decorated, colourful, joyous and festive, as if a flower garden was embroidered over every surface.
