Styled in a design language of plain and sturdy construction in pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Slender turned posts, arching slats, woven tape or rush texture and tapered legs give light, upright forms. Flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, small wooden knobs and a smooth surface define the details. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and natural honey wood. Thin bent wood bands with swallowtail joints and copper tacks hold shapes together. The mood is serene, orderly, utilitarian and pure. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
