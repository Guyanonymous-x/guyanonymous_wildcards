In the style of London Underground posters, early twentieth century transport graphics built from flat, hand-cut shapes. Outlines are crisp and forms are reduced to geometric blocks with gentle lithographic grain and slightly chalky texture. A bold red ring crossed by a horizontal blue bar acts as the recurring emblem. Compositions are balanced, decorative and optimistic, with cubist and art deco rhythm in repeated shapes.
In the style of Deutsche Bahn, clean, efficient, clinical modern public design with white bodies, a bright red stripe along the lower edge and light grey bands. Aerodynamic pointed forms, smooth tapered openings and sharp clean lines. Functionalist, orderly and precise, with polished grey surfaces and glass.
In the style of SNCF, polished French modernism with streamlined, pointed forms in a glossy two-tone scheme of blue-grey and deep silver or white, accented by a sweeping carmine red or magenta slash. Rounded smooth volumes and slim openings. Elegant, clean curves, fluid and streamlined.
In the style of Swiss Federal Railways, precise, punctual, perfectly legible Swiss modernist design. A red square with a white equal-armed cross and two-way arrows is the main mark. Red and white surfaces with clean rounded stripes. A white analogue clock face on a plain black ring, with a thin black minute hand, a bold black hour hand and a red second hand tipped with a disc. Polished steel and bright red.
In the style of NYC Subway signage, rectangular name panels with bold neutral sans-serif capitals in white on black, with a thin white border line, set against tiled walls. Coloured circles or diamonds with a single bold white letter or numeral in red, green, blue, orange, yellow, purple, brown and grey. Glossy white ceramic tiling with coloured mosaic name tablets, steel columns painted dark green and fluorescent light. Tidy arrows, clear hierarchy and a modernist grid-manual look of 1970s transit design, gritty scuffed steel and tile, strong urban functionalism.
In the style of Hong Kong Tramways, busy, nostalgic, neon-lit, crowded city design. Tall, slim, upright boxy bodies with two storeys of tall rectangular windows in slim frames, a slightly curved roof and open balcony fronts upstairs. Lower body in deep forest green with a cream or pale yellow stripe along the window line, and a square box with white characters on black.
In the style of Star Ferry, leisurely, nostalgic, salty and civic harbour charm. Low, broad forms with rounded overhanging fronts, a white upper level and thin white railing. Deep bottle green below clean white, with a gold rubbing line, black cap, brass fittings and cream rings. Creamy paint, varnished timber and round porthole shapes. Skyscrapers behind, hazy sky and dark teal water.
