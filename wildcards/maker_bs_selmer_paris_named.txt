In the style of Selmer Paris, elegant curved brass forms finished in warm honey-gold lacquer or silver plating. Details are neatly arranged, with pearly rounded buttons, and flared curves carry fine engraving of swirling floral scrollwork in the Art Deco manner. Lines are long and gently bent, with wide flared ends. Rims are finely drawn and guards are tidy. Black plush linings contrast with the metal. The mood is jazz-age, Parisian, tidy and rich in warm gold. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Selmer Paris in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
