Styled in a design language of sunny, festive, Andean and luminous. A glowing golden-yellow colour fills clear glass, with yellow and red fields and a bold red banner. A circular emblem holds a sun-face and stepped pre-Columbian geometric shapes in red, black and gold. Palette is canary yellow, red, black and white. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
