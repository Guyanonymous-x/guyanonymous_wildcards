Styled in a design language of tidy, bright, practical, collectable and slightly retro miniature diecast design. Small compact forms in one glossy solid enamel colour such as white, red, yellow, light blue or metallic silver, with clear glazing, silver painted trim, tiny printed badges and parts that really open. Chrome five-spoke details on small black rubber. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
