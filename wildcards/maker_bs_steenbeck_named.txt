Styled in a design language of flatbed film editing tables with a long low desk-like body in pale grey or grey-green painted steel, two or more pairs of horizontal round reel plates on top, a small hooded viewing screen and a sound head between them, chunky chrome control levers and knobs, black rubber rollers guiding film strips, and a solid, precise, workshop-of-cinema character.
