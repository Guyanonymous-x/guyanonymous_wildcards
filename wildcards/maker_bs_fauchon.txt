Styled in a design language of glossy, high-fashion French delicatessen with striking contrasts. Shiny hot fuchsia-pink and black with gold, in rectangular forms with thick laminated finishes and sleek geometry. Logotype in thin white or gold serif capitals within a rich black panel, with ribbons in fuchsia and cream. Objects line up in tidy rows against black and pink. Palette is fuchsia pink, black, gold and white. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
