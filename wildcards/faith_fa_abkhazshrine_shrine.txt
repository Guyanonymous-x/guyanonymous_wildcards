the shrine being Lashkendar, on a wooded mountaintop above the town of Tkuarchal, where the roofless walls of a small old stone church stand among box and hornbeam trees, a slab over its doorway carved in low relief with two long-bodied beasts facing each other
the shrine being Inal-kuba, in the high valley of Pskhu deep in the mountains, a meadow under a gray rock peak ringed by ridges of dark fir forest, with snow lying in the gullies above and a lone giant beech at the meadow's edge as the praying tree
the shrine being Ldzaa-nykha, near the Black Sea shore at Pitsunda, a patch of old oaks on a low rise among tall straight pines, the blue line of the sea showing between the trunks and the ground brown with fallen needles and oak leaves
