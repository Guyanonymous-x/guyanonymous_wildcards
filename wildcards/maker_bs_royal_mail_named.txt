Styled in a design language of traditional Victorian civic pride in deep post-office red glossy enamel on cast iron with a domed cap. Small profile silhouette portraits in an upper corner on a coloured field, perforated edges. Deep red and gold, solid, official and dependable. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
