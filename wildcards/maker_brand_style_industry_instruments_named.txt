In the style of Gibson, rich, warm, heavy and luxurious. Carved arched forms with a glossy cherry red or golden sunburst finish, cream binding around the edges and a flamed maple figure. Bold mother-of-pearl block or trapezoid inlays on dark rosewood, gold-plated hardware, chrome-covered details, gold barrel shapes and a swept-back crown-like silhouette with a pearl inlay mark. Overall the mood is generous and showy yet refined, with deep saturated colour, warm gloss and a confident sense of heirloom weight.

In the style of Korg, functional, technical, compact, precise late 1970s and 1980s electronic design with a futuristic hi-fi flavour.
