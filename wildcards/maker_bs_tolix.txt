In the style of Tolix, French industrial pressed sheet-steel furniture, with a stackable A-shaped chair made of a single formed sheet of steel, rounded backrest with a perforated or plain seat, simple tapered tubular legs with reinforced foot caps. Matte lacquered, powder-coated and galvanised metal in red, dark green, yellow, black, grey and raw zinc, with slightly dented, utilitarian patina. Simple, cafe terrace and factory canteen look, tough, thin and urban.
