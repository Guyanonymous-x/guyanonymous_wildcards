on a lotus throne at the center sits Kuntu Zangpo, the primordial buddha of Bon, a man in his thirties with sky-blue skin, wholly nude and unadorned, seated cross-legged in the vajra posture with each foot sole up on the opposite thigh, his joined hands resting palm up in his lap over the groin, as Bon paintings show him, a rainbow halo behind him
Tonpa Shenrab Miwoche, the founder of Bon, a man in his thirties with deep blue skin, in a jeweled crown and silk robes, sits in meditation on a lotus throne with his hands in his lap, a ring of rainbow light behind him
Shenlha Okar, the god of white light, a man in his thirties with pure white skin, and Satrig Ersang, the great mother of wisdom, a woman in her thirties with golden-yellow skin, sit side by side on lotus thrones, each crowned and jeweled in a ring of rainbow light
Sherab Chamma, the loving mother of wisdom, a woman in her thirties in silk robes and jewels, sits on a lotus in a ring of white light holding a vase, and around her sit enlightened beings in their thirties in silk robes on smaller lotuses
