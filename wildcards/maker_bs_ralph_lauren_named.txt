Styled in a design language of nostalgic old-money American heritage, part ivy campus, part country club, part western ranch. Palette is deep forest green, navy, burgundy, camel, cream and crisp white, with a bright red accent. Patterns include cable knit, madras and tartan plaids, herringbone tweed, Fair Isle bands, blanket stripes and stars and stripes motifs. Materials are oxford cloth, wool, corduroy, weathered saddle leather and brass buttons. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
