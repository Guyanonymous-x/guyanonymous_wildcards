Styled in a design language of hand-woven silk with a lustrous, slightly slubby shimmer. Surfaces show iridescent two-tone shot effects in jewel colours such as sapphire, emerald, ruby, saffron, fuchsia, plum and turquoise, plus rich plains. Prints show stylised animals, tropical leaves, orchids, lotus, bamboo, pagoda motifs and bold stripes, drawn in a flat painterly style with generous colour fields. Rainbow-like ranges of colour sit side by side. Warm, exotic, elegant mood with teak tones and soft tropical light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
