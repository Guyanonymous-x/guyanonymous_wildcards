In the style of Shaker Mount Lebanon, plain and sturdy construction in pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Slender turned posts, arching slats, woven tape or rush texture and tapered legs give light, upright forms. Flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, small wooden knobs and a smooth surface define the details. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and natural honey wood. Thin bent wood bands with swallowtail joints and copper tacks hold shapes together. The mood is serene, orderly, utilitarian and pure. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Shaker Mount Lebanon in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
