Styled in a design language of graceful, silky, ornate and festive identity in orchid purple and gold. White grounds with a sweeping band in shades of magenta, violet and gold, merging into a curved mark of a tall stylised flame-like roof curve built from gold and magenta. Spaces use purple and orange, silk-like fabrics in orchid pink, patterned carpets and orchids as accents. Silk wrap shapes in orchid purple, gold or magenta with a small flower corsage and a neat bun. Rich violet, magenta and gold, with classical dancers and royal barges for special occasions. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
