the women in their twenties to sixties, bare above the waist in the old way of the southern towns, wear knee-length wrap skirts of woven mulberry-bark cloth dyed brown and yellow, their long black hair in a single braid
the women in their thirties to seventies wear long wrap skirts of soft tanned deerskin and fringed shawls of woven bark fiber over one shoulder and across the breasts, white shell beads at the neck and wrists
women in their twenties to fifties carry pots of new corn on their heads to the plaza, painted clay jars with swirling red and white designs, their wrap skirts of plain brown cloth tied at the hip
