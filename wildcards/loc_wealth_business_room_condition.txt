the room of 1800 with a resident steward, rent days twice a year and the ledgers written in one hand for thirty years
the same room in 1880 with a professional land agent, a letter copying press, printed forms and a separate agent's office in the market town, which is the change that begins to empty this room
the room in 1925 with the outlying farms sold, forty ledgers closed and the safe holding share certificates rather than leases
still working, with a telephone on the rent table and a typewriter on the standing desk
the room as a flat's kitchen in a divided house, with the ledger shelving still in place behind a dresser
the room in a house in probate, which is where the lawyers sit and where the inheritance-tax valuation is actually done, surrounded by the documents that caused it
the room mothballed with the ledgers boxed to a county archive and the shelving empty and labeled
the room open to the weather, where the safe is the last thing standing
