above the dome of the sky lies the Sky World of the Haudenosaunee, a land of longhouses and fields with a great Celestial Tree whose white blossoms give the light, a hole torn at its roots through which Sky Woman, a woman in her thirties in a deerskin dress, falls toward the sea below
the upper world of the Mississippian peoples holds the Birdman, a man in his thirties with falcon wings spread wide, a falcon mask over his eyes, a beaded apron and a mace in his hand
the sky world holds the Thunderbirds, great birds with wings as wide as the sky, lightning flashing from their eyes, flying above the clouds over the lake
