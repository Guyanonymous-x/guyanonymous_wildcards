Styled in a design language of playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde. Oversized, sculptural forms in chunky glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, with thick geometric rims, rounded squares, pointed wings and big wraparound shields. Metal in silver or gold with fine joints, and tinted transparent areas in blue, green, orange or mirror gradient. Small engraved logo marks in tiny white capitals. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
