Styled in a design language of solid, heavy, trustworthy mid-century Indian steel design. Tall rectangular boxy volumes of welded sheet steel with rounded corners, squat secure proportions and a single bold vertical chrome pull. Surfaces are thick baked enamel with a faint orange peel texture in grey green, olive, cream, rust brown, pale blue, dark green or black, with thin gold pinstripes and brass fittings. Round, oversized control details feel reassuringly chunky. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
