Styled in a design language of blunt, cheeky two-tone design. Thick glossy near-black surfaces contrast with a bright sunshine yellow field, with a red oval or ribbon label and a tiny union-flag-like accent. Forms are squat, rounded, heavy and soft-cornered, with ridged shoulders and a plain broad base. Palette is near-black, yellow, signal red, green and white, with gold foil in luxury variants and playful colour swaps in limited editions. Finish is high gloss with sharp highlights. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
