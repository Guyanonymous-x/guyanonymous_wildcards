Styled in a design language of bold, rugged expedition design with a technical, mountain-inspired look. Strong colour blocking in red, black, white, yellow, royal blue and teal, split by diagonal seams and panels. Materials are glossy ripstop nylon, quilted puffy baffles, fleece, matte coated fabrics and webbing, with reflective piping and taped edges. Shapes are chunky, padded and geometric, and the brand has a confident, adventurous, high-performance character with oversized graphics and clean flat colour fields. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
