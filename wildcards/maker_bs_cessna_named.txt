Styled in a design language of friendly, simple, practical design from the 1960s and 1970s. Boxy but approachable white forms with softened corners, generous wraparound windows and plain, honest shapes that are easy to read. Painted sweeping side stripes in red, blue, gold or green add a note of optimism. Sturdy, unfussy, utility-first and casual, with a weekend-adventure cheerfulness and clean enamel finish. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
