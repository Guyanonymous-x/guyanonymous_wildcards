Styled in a design language of hand-moulded clay coated in milky-white chalk for a smooth matte porcelain-like finish. Forms are plump, stylised, simple and rounded, with bell-shaped bodies, tiny heads and exaggerated puffy volumes. Painting is crisp, playful and poster-bright in red, orange, yellow, blue, green and black, using large circles, checks, diagonal stripes, wavy lines, dots and rhombuses, with rosy circular cheeks and small black dot eyes. Small details are picked out in glittering gold leaf. Cheerful, flat, geometric and slightly naive, like a patterned doll made of ceramic icing.
