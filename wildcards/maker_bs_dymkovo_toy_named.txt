Styled in a design language of hand-moulded clay coated in milky-white chalk for a smooth matte porcelain-like finish. Forms are plump, stylised, simple and rounded, with bell-shaped bodies, tiny heads and exaggerated puffy volumes. Painting is crisp, orange, yellow, blue, green and black, using large circles, checks, diagonal stripes, wavy lines, dots and rhombuses, with rosy circular cheeks and small black dot eyes. Small details are picked out in glittering gold leaf. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
