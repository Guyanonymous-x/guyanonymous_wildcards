In the style of Penkala, precise, slim, early Art Deco and quietly luxurious. Slim cylindrical forms in glossy black, mottled red-and-black, pale marbled tones, dark blue or olive, with gold-plated or nickel bands, tapered ends and long clean lines finished with a small ball tip. Metal parts have fine ribbed or knurled grips, silver or gold finish and geometric engraved stripes. Cases are lined with velvet in dark green or burgundy and gold-stamped. Everything has a hand-finished glow, smooth seams, tiny engraved lines and a small stamped maker mark, giving a jewel-like object feel in muted period colours. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
