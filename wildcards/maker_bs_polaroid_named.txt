Styled in a design language of nostalgic, friendly and playful 1970s consumer design. Forms are boxy and softly rounded in cream, white or black plastic, with dark leatherette panels and glossy surfaces. The signature motif is a band of five parallel rainbow stripes in red, orange, yellow, green and blue, stepping across a clean white or deep black field. Palette is warm chocolate brown, tan and off-white with one bright red rectangular accent. Square shapes with thick white borders and a wider bottom margin recur everywhere, like frames and labels. Finish is soft, warm and slightly faded. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
