In the style of Land Rover, rugged, honest and expedition-ready utility for any product. Upright, flat-sided, squared-off forms with bluff faces, flat slab panels and exposed hinges, bolts and rivets. Matt and satin finishes in olive, bronze green, sand, pale grey, poppy red and white, with an oval green badge with gold-edged lettering as the recurring mark. Canvas, webbing, strapped-on carry frames, rubber mats and simple round dials carry a worked-hard spirit. Modern versions add clean utilitarian surfaces in black plastic, fabric and a bold exposed structural beam look. Photography shows mud, dust, rivers, dunes and wild landscapes in golden light, with vintage press-ad charm.
