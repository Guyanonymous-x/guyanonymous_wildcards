beside him stands Miti, his wife, a woman in her forties with a broad face, in a parka of spotted reindeer fur with a wide trim of beads and dyed hide at the hem and cuffs
behind him a great gray whale lies stranded at the edge of the sea, its broad back mottled with pale patches, and people in fur clothing run down the shore to welcome it
around him graze reindeer with branching antlers and thick gray-brown coats, and a yaranga, a round tent of dark smoked reindeer hides stretched over a frame of poles, stands behind him
in his beak or his hand he holds the sun, a shining gold disk he has stolen back for the people, its light spilling across the snow around his feet
