the souls of the dead, men and women in their thirties to eighties in masi cloth wraps, walk in a line along the shore past Udu Point and the village of Navunievu
on the road a soul-killer waits behind a rock with a heavy club, striking down a soul of a man in his forties who falls on the sand
the souls pass a pandanus tree on the cliff top, its long spiky leaves and thick stilt roots, each soul throwing a token at it as it goes by
