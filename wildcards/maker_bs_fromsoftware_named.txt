In the style of FromSoftware, bleak, decaying dark-fantasy atmosphere where beauty survives only as ruin. Crumbling gothic architecture, fog-filled distances, battered, tarnished metal, tattered cloth and grotesque, towering monstrous forms. Desaturated palette of ash grey, rust brown and sickly green, broken by a single shaft or glow of warm golden light. Sparse, moody lighting with deep shadow and heavy atmosphere. Any text is small, plain and elegant in a quiet serif face. Intricate weathered detail on every surface. Melancholic, ominous, lonely and solemn, with a scale that makes the viewer feel small. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
