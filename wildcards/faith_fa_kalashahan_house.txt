the jestak han, a low square house of stacked stone and heavy timber beams, its wooden front carved all over with rows of rosettes, zigzags and horned animal heads, the roof beam ending in a pair of carved ram's horns
the jestak han of Bumburet, a long hall of dark weathered cedar with carved pillars along its open front, its carved door frame dark and smooth with age
inside the jestak han, a dim room of dark wood with a fire pit in the floor, its four heavy cedar pillars carved with rows of cloven hoof shapes and blackened by smoke and a pair of wild goat horns over the altar shelf
