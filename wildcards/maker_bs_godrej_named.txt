In the style of Godrej, solid, heavy, trustworthy mid-century Indian steel design. Tall rectangular boxy volumes of welded sheet steel with rounded corners, squat secure proportions and a single bold vertical chrome pull. Surfaces are thick baked enamel with a faint orange peel texture in grey green, olive, cream, rust brown, pale blue, dark green or black, with thin gold pinstripes and brass fittings. Round, oversized control details feel reassuringly chunky. Marks are looping cursive signature lettering in white, deep red or purple. Mood is secure, domestic, durable and post-independence.
