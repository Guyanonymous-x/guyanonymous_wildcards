a set of Guatemalan worry dolls, tiny handmade dolls in a small woven pouch, each about as long as an adult's fingertip. Each doll is a few sticks bound together and wrapped tightly in bright thread and scraps of woven cloth, with a tiny round head and a dot of a face. They are dressed in the colors of Guatemalan weaving, red, yellow, green, blue, purple and pink, the women in skirts and the men in trousers and hats. Six of them lie spilled out of their pouch, a little bag of striped woven cotton with a drawstring, beside a tiny wooden box painted with flowers. __doll_worry_dolls_var__.
