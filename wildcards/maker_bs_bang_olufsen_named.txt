Styled in a design language of serene, sculptural Scandinavian luxury. Forms are slim, tall, softly geometric and minimalist, with tapering cones, pyramids and thin floating plates. Materials are anodised and brushed aluminium, finely perforated metal mesh in tiny repeating patterns, polished black glass, oiled oak, dark walnut, wool fabric in charcoal and warm grey, and soft natural tan leather. Edges are razor sharp with tiny even gaps and hairline seams, with large circular elements and delicate pearl-blasted textures. Palette is silver, graphite, black, natural wood and muted earthy tones such as sand, terracotta and sage. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
