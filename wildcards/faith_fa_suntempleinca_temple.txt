the Coricancha at Cusco in Peru, the first temple of the empire, long halls of dark gray stone around a courtyard, a great curved wall at one end, a band of gold plate as wide as two hands along the top of the walls under the thatch, through an open doorway the inner walls of the sun's own hall sheeted with beaten gold, and below it a terraced garden set with life-size corn plants and llamas made of gold and silver
the Torreón of Machu Picchu in Peru, a small tower of pale granite with one perfectly curved wall wrapped around a natural rock outcrop, a window in the curve placed so that the first sun of the June solstice falls across the carved rock inside, a cave shrine with stepped stonework beneath it and steep green peaks all around
the Temple of the Sun at Ingapirca in the highlands of Ecuador, a long oval platform of green-gray stone blocks with rounded ends, twice the height of a man, a small two-roomed building with a thatched roof on top reached by a stair, low walls of storerooms and lodgings below it and open grassy hills around
the sun temple of Vilcashuamán in the highlands of Ayacucho in Peru, standing on three stacked terraces faced with fitted stone, the wall of the middle terrace set with a row of tall niches narrower at the top, a wide plaza before it and at the far side a stepped stone platform with a double seat cut from one block
the sun temple of Pisac above the Sacred Valley of Peru, a wall of rose-gray fitted stone curving in a half circle around a natural rock carved into a short upright post, the hitching post of the sun, on a narrow spur between farming terraces that drop in green steps to the river far below
the Sacred Rock on the Island of the Sun in Lake Titicaca in Bolivia, a long crag of brown sandstone as high as a house, one face covered with sheets of gold and the other hung with fine bright cloth, a round stone basin before it, a maze of stone rooms for the priests nearby and deep blue water beyond
the sun temple of Pachacamac on the desert coast of Peru near Lima, a stepped pyramid of sun-dried mud brick on a hill above the Pacific Ocean, its terrace walls plastered and painted deep red, a row of tall niches along the seaward terrace, bare sand hills around it and two rocky islets in the gray sea
