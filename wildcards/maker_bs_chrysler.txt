In the style of Chrysler, late 1950s and early 60s American cars of the forward look, very low and wide with tall swept-back tail fins, a long slender profile and a huge expanse of glass in a wraparound windshield. A wide mouth grille with a bright bar, canted dual headlamps in chrome pods, and flowing side chrome with a contrasting colour panel. A pentastar or winged badge, big round tail lamps, and wheel covers with a radial spoke look. Interiors with push-button gearshift on the dash, a bold sculpted panel and luxurious fabric. Colours of turquoise, flamingo pink, cream and charcoal. Space-age, optimistic and airy.
