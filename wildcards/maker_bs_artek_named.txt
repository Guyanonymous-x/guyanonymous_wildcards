Styled in a design language of Finnish modernist design in pale laminated birch, with a single bent L-shaped support that curls into a smooth scroll beneath the surface. Forms are simple, round and light, with lightly curved plywood, soft rounded edges and visible blond grain. Natural birch and white lacquer are lifted by colourful lacquered parts in red, yellow, blue and green, black linoleum and striped woven webbing or canvas. Warm, humane Scandinavian functionalism with a nordic apartment and school hall mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
