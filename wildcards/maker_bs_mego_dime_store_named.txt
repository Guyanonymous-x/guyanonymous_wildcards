Styled in a design language of cheap 1960s variety-store novelty toys of thin, brightly colored hollow plastic and stamped tin, simple molded shapes sprayed in red, yellow and blue with a few gold foil stickers, sold on small hanging cards under clear plastic bubbles. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
