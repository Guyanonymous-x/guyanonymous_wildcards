In the style of Thebe Magugu, contemporary design that combines sharp tailoring with storytelling prints, with structured lines, pleats, crisp shirting and strong shoulders. Materials include wool, silk, crisp cotton and leather, with printed illustrations of portraits, plants, historical imagery and map-like graphics. Palette moves between deep forest green, burgundy, cobalt blue, mustard, cream, black and pastel pink, with precise seams, contrasting piping and nipped waists. Playful and intellectual, polished, artful and modern, in clean gallery light. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Thebe Magugu in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
