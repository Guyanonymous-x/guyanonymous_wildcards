the estate before about 1850, its business done at the steward's table in the house
the office of 1880 with a professional agent, a clerk, a card index and eighty-four ledgers, which is the arrangement here
the office of 1910 with a typewriter, a telephone, a bicycle and then a motor car, and the agent managing three estates from it
the office of 1920 during the great sales, when a third of England's land changed hands between 1918 and 1922 and this room is where every one of those sales was prepared, and a great many of the plans in its press were drawn to break the estate up into lots
the office of 1950 with the estate at a quarter of its area, the agent part-time and the ledgers closed
the office today as a working estate office with a computer, a planning file and the 1842 map still on the wall, which is extremely common and is a genuinely unbroken continuity
the office as a holiday-cottage booking office, a farm shop's back room or a solicitor's branch
the office mothballed, plans to a county archive, which is where most surviving estate plan collections now are
the office open to the weather, where the paper is the whole content and goes immediately, so a derelict estate office contains nothing and tells you nothing
