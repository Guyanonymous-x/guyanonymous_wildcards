In the style of Ganado Trading Post Navajo Weavings, handwoven wool with a deep brick red ground, charcoal black, ivory and soft grey, in strong contrast. A bold central diamond with stepped, serrated edges, or a cross, sits in the centre, with smaller diamonds or hooks echoing in the corners. A single frame with notches or crosses surrounds it with neat edges. The surface is tight and flat with a slight handspun irregularity. Geometry is crisp, rectilinear and strongly symmetrical, with a warm desert look.
