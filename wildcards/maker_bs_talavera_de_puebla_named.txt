Styled in a design language of thick creamy white glossy tin glaze painted by hand in cobalt blue, yellow, green, orange and black. Flowers, birds, vines, geometric borders and Moorish-inspired arabesques are laid down in bold brushwork, with slightly uneven edges, drips and pooling glaze. Colours stay bright and flat, edged with a thin dark outline, with white ground left between motifs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
