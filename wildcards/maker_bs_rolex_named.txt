In the style of Rolex, solid, imposing, polished, status-heavy Swiss luxury. Robust round forms in polished and brushed stainless steel or warm yellow gold, with fluted edges, satin and mirror finishes side by side and a tiny five-pointed crown motif as the recurring emblem. Deep forest green is the signature colour, paired with gold, white, black, blue and cream. Lettering is crisp, upright serif capitals in gold or white, widely spaced. Packaging and graphics use rich green leather-grain, embossed gold crests and heavy lacquered boxes. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
