Styled in a design language of royal-court batik, hand-drawn and stamped on cotton and silk in elaborate classical patterns, with diagonal blade-like bands, circle grids, star flowers and lotus motifs filling the cloth evenly in fine linework. Colours are dignified earth tones of sogan brown, midnight indigo, cream white and soft black, with occasional touches of gold paint. Surfaces are hand-finished with a slightly crackled wax texture. Dignified, traditional, courtly mood, in dim museum-like light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
