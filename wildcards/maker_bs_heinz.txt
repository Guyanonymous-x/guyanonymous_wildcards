Styled in a design language of heritage American label design built on a keystone-shaped emblem. A pointed arched cream panel with a fine tomato-red outline and a bold central shield holds ornate white and cream serif capitals set on a gentle curve, with small banner ribbons, an established-year line and tiny fine print. Palette is tomato red, warm cream, antique gold and deep navy, with a vivid mustard yellow or leaf green accent for variants. Surfaces are glossy printed paper, tinted glass and enamel with crisp thin keylines. Lettering is proud, slightly antique and wholesome. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
