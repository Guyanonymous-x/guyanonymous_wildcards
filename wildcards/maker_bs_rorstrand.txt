Styled in a design language of gentle, light, sophisticated, calm and domestic quiet Scandinavian modernism. White glossy surfaces with softly shaded cobalt-blue flowers painted along the edges, loose organic leaf outlines and a soft airbrush-like gradient, on plain curving silhouettes. Slender rounded lines, shallow volumes and thin, slightly rounded edges. Other treatments offer rustic mottled blue-and-white, green-and-brown speckled glaze, and trailing lines of blue stripes. Earlier decoration shows delicate printed lace, chinoiserie scenes and sprays of green leaves. Restrained palette of white, blue, soft green and brown.
