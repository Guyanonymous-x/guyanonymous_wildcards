at the foot of a deep square shaft cut into the yellow sandstone of Carthage, a low chamber holds the body of a man in his fifties laid on his back in a wooden coffin, wrapped in linen, his head on a stone pillow
in Sidon a white marble coffin carved in the shape of a wrapped body lies in a rock-cut room, its lid carved with the calm face of a woman in her forties with curled hair in the Greek manner
in a tomb at Kerkouane the burned bones of a woman in her sixties rest in a clay urn with a lid, set in a niche of the rock wall beside a second urn of her husband
the funeral party of the dead man, kin in their thirties to sixties in long wool robes and conical caps, lowers his coffin down the shaft on ropes, a priest in his fifties in a white linen robe with a shaved head pouring oil at the top
