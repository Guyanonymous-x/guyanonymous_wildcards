Styled in a design language of no-brand minimalism, quiet, modest and thoughtful. Plain, simple, softly rounded geometric forms with no logos, no ornament and no unnecessary features. Natural, raw, unbleached materials such as pale wood, kraft paper, cotton, linen, white ceramic and matte plastic in off-white, beige, oatmeal, grey, charcoal and muted navy. Finishes are matte, soft and tactile, with neat even stitching and honest construction. Lettering is small, plain, lightweight sans-serif in black or grey on neutral labels. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
