Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy-duty, utilitarian machinery. Big rounded, bulbous masses with deep glazing, stubby add-on volumes and a long tapering tail. Riveted aluminium skin with visible fasteners and panel seams. Colour is either matte olive drab and grey, or white and red for rescue. No ornament, everything functional, strong, workmanlike and built to lift and endure. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
