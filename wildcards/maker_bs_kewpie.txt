Styled in a design language of cute, wholesome, babyish and nostalgic Japanese branding built around a chubby baby mascot. Soft squeezable forms in clear or translucent white plastic with bright red accents and star-shaped detailing that makes fine ridged lines. Net-like orange-red cross-hatched lattice patterns appear. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
