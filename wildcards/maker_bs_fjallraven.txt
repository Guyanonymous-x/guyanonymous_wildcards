Styled in a design language of rugged, minimal Scandinavian northern design. Materials are dense waxed cotton-poly canvas, sturdy nylon and soft wool in earthy tones of khaki, olive, forest green, navy, charcoal, sand and mustard, with occasional red, orange and pale blue accents. Shapes are simple geometric blocks with straight seams, bold colour blocking and contrast seam lines. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
