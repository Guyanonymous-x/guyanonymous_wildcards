the sikerei, men in their thirties to seventies with brown skin covered in fine blue-black titi tattoos, lines and curves over the chest, back, arms and legs, wear only red loincloths of beaten bark cloth, bare above, and headbands of rattan stuck with red hibiscus flowers and long green leaves
the sikerei, lean men in their forties to sixties with long black hair tied back, their tattooed bodies bare above loincloths of bark cloth, strands of red, yellow, white and green glass beads looped over their chests and small brass bells tied at their waists
the sikerei, older men in their fifties to eighties with gray hair and sinewy tattooed limbs, in bark-cloth loincloths, garlands of white and yellow flowers and fresh palm leaves at their necks and bands of yellow and red beads above their elbows
