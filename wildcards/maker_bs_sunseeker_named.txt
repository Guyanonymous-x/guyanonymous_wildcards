Styled in a design language of sleek, low, sporty British luxury. A raked, aggressive forward slope, long white glossy surfaces and dark tinted wraparound glazing. Bold swooping stripes sweep along the sides, with polished stainless details and teak-clad surfaces. Speed-minded, status-conscious, polished and flamboyant, with a high-glamour resort look and a cool white palette. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
