In the style of Zojirushi, Japanese kitchen appliances and vacuum flasks with a calm, trustworthy domestic look. Rice cookers are plump rounded boxes or domes with soft corners, in cream white, pearl grey or brushed stainless steel with a single dark glass or black panel carrying a small pale green backlit display and simple rounded buttons. Vacuum bottles are slim cylinders in satin stainless steel or soft pastel enamel with a flip top push button lid, a sturdy folding carry handle and a small shoulder strap. Hot water dispensers are tall tanks with a smooth glossy body and a neat spout lever. The company mark is a small side view elephant silhouette beside a red or black wordmark in friendly rounded letters. Surfaces are matte or satin, never flashy, with fine seams and tidy rubber gaskets. Instruction icons are tiny line pictograms. Mood is gentle, practical, clean and quietly cheerful, with the reassuring weight of well made kitchen equipment.
