Shot through a standard 720nm infrared filter and converted to black and white, the image shows a black-and-white image with a wide, smooth tonal range, white foliage and a deep gray sky. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
