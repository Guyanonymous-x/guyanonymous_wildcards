a dwelling with no connection to any utility network, where every input is captured
stored and rationed on site, and whose design is therefore an exercise in arithmetic, electricity from a solar array of one to three kilowatts with a battery bank sized for three to five days of autonomy
a charge controller and an inverter, and an annual problem concentrated entirely in the eight weeks either side of the winter solstice when a northern array produces almost nothing
often requiring a generator or a wind turbine as backup
water from roof catchment to a tank with filtration, or from a borehole with a pump that is itself a large electrical load
sanitation by composting toilet with the compost handled on site
heat from wood cut
split
stacked and seasoned a year ahead
cooking by gas or wood
insulation taken to a very high standard because every joule is expensive
appliances chosen by consumption rather than by preference so there is no kettle
no tumble dryer and no electric shower
a building small enough that all of this is possible, and an occupant who understands their own systems because nobody else will come out to fix them
