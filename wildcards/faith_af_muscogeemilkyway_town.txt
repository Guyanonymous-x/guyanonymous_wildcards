at the west end of the road of stars stands the town of the good souls, a square ground of four open arbors around a bright fire, the dead dancing around it
the good souls, men and women in their forties to nineties in long ribbon shirts, beaded sashes and patchwork dresses, greet the newly dead at the gate of the spirit town
the spirit land in the west, rolling green hills and wide rivers under the stars, round houses of bark and thatch along the banks and smoke rising from their fires
