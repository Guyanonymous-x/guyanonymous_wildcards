Styled in a design language of crystal clear, flawless, heavy optical glass with pure transparency and thick, polished walls. Shapes are clean, simple and modernist, with crisp curves, tapered columns, deep facets and thick bases. Decoration is minimal, thin engraved lines of animals and plants, or sharp geometric cuts that refract light. Colour appears rarely, in subtle tints such as pale gold or aqua. The mood is cool, luminous, precise and quiet, like a block of ice shaped by hand. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
