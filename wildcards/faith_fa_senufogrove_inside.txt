a small round house in the clearing with walls of red clay and a cone-shaped roof of thatch reaching low, its one doorway closed by a carved wooden door showing a row of masked heads, a crocodile and a tortoise in low relief
three low open shelters of thick posts and thatch standing along the edge of the clearing, big drums on carved legs and long wooden trumpets stored under one, clay pots and bundles of raffia fiber under the others
a low mound of clay at the foot of the greatest tree darkened with years of offerings, white feathers stuck to its surface, with small clay pots set upside down around it
