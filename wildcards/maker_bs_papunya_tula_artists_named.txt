In the style of Papunya Tula Artists, dense fields of small round dots applied by hand, creating a shimmering, hypnotic, optical texture. Compositions are seen from above like maps, built from concentric circles, U-shapes, curving travel lines, meandering parallel bands and spreading wavy tracks. Palette is earthy, red-brown, burnt orange, ochre, yellow, charcoal, black, white and cream, sometimes with vivid pink or blue. Backgrounds are often dark, with dots layered so the image reads as both a map and an abstract field. Matte, slightly raised surface. Rhythmic, symmetrical, very graphic and earthy, like aerial views of red desert country. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
