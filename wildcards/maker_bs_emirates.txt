Styled in a design language of lavish, polished desert-luxury. Gleaming white grounds meet vivid ruby red in confident fields, with flowing Arabic calligraphy in red as the central mark, matched by a refined Latin wordmark beneath. Accents use rich gold, champagne, deep burgundy and warm sand beige, with soft gradients and satin sheen. Surfaces feel premium, with polished lacquer, burnished metal, supple leather and fine woven fabric. Shapes are smooth, rounded and elegant with tapering curves that echo the swoops of calligraphy. Small badges are tidy rounded rectangles in red with white lettering. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
