In the style of the Bauhaus workshops, German functional modernism using primary geometry, circles, squares and triangles, with tubular steel chairs, chromed frames, black leather straps, flat plywood, bent wood and white-painted walls. Palette of primary red, yellow and blue with black, white and grey. Slim cantilever stools, nesting tables, geometric tea infusers in nickel, tapestries with abstract rectangles and woven grids, lamps of opal glass spheres on nickel stems, sans-serif lowercase lettering. Rational, austere, factory-ready elegance.
