Styled in a design language of dependable postwar German craftsmanship. Palette is walnut brown, cream, soft gold, hammered grey-green, black and bright orange. Materials are figured wood veneer, brushed gold and chrome metal trim, cream and brown plastic and fine woven fabric. Forms are plain, solid, reliable, modest and long-lasting in character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
