Styled in a design language of fresh Finnish modernism built on oversized, hand-drawn graphic prints. Giant simplified poppy-like blooms, stripes, dots, checks and organic blobs fill the surface at huge scale, cropped boldly at the edges. Palette is vivid and flat, with poppy red, hot pink, orange, cobalt, deep navy, leaf green and black on crisp white or inky dark grounds. Shapes have soft brushed edges, a naive painterly quality and no shading. Materials are sturdy cotton, linen-like weaves, ceramic glaze and coated paper with a matte finish. Everything feels joyful, democratic, bright and playfully abstract. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
