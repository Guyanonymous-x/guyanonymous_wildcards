In the style of Sabyasachi, opulent Indian bridal and occasion wear, heavy hand-embroidered lehengas, saris and sherwanis in deep earthy jewel colours such as rich red, burnt orange, marigold, mustard, moss green, teal and faded rose, with a vintage, slightly distressed and layered look. Surfaces carry dense zardozi gold embroidery, resham thread, sequins, mirror work, tassels, block-printed florals and Bengal tiger or paisley motifs on silk, velvet, organza and tulle. Veils and dupattas are sheer with scalloped borders. Jewellery is chunky antique gold with uncut diamonds, polki, big emeralds, pearl ropes and layered chokers. Regal, romantic, heritage mood, warm golden light, heirloom richness and a Kolkata faded-palace feel.
