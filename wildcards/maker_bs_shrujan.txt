In the style of Shrujan, Kutch embroidery collective textiles, densely hand-stitched cotton and silk in bright thread on dark or plain ground, with tiny mirrors (abhla) sewn into chain stitch, herringbone and buttonhole borders. Motifs are repeating peacocks, parrots, flowers, mango shapes, geometric diamonds, stars and tiny mandala medallions arranged in tight bands and symmetrical grids. Colours are vivid magenta, scarlet, turquoise, canary yellow, green and white against black, indigo or deep red. Used for cushions, bags, wall hangings, shawls, blouses and skirts. Highly textured, glittering, tactile surfaces of shimmering mirrors and tight thread, folk-craft joyfulness.
