Styled in a design language of extremely fine, cobweb-thin knitted lace so light it could be drawn through a ring. Composition is a calm, symmetrical central field framed by a border and a distinct edge. Patterns are airy open-work motifs, small raised bobbles, diamond lozenges, leaf shapes, fir cones, bell flowers, snowflakes and ripples, finished with a scalloped, saw-toothed edge of tiny points. Colour is natural white or ivory, sometimes pale grey, soft peach or faded rose. The texture is fuzzy, misty and ethereal, with a delicate halo of loose fibres, like frost on a window or cobwebs in morning dew. Delicate, geometric and romantic, with mathematical patience. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
