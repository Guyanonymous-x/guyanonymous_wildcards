Styled in a design language of 1960s and 1970s detailed plastic model kits with chrome-plated parts for cars, airbrushed paint and decals with racing stripes and hot rod flames, sold in boxes with a crisp painted illustration of the finished model. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
