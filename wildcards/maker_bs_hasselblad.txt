Styled in a design language of serious, sculptural Scandinavian precision. Cubic square-profiled forms in satin black paint with bright chrome trim, or all-black crinkle texture, sometimes with brushed gold or exposed carbon-weave special editions. Modular blocks that clip together, with deep matte black, graphite grey and silver. The emblem is a small boxed H in white on black. Packaging is dark matte boxes with fine foil mark, soft black foam and leather-grain wrapping. Heavy, exact, minimal and engineered. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
