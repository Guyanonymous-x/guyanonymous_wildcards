Styled in a design language of raw, intricate, spiritual and richly patterned flat sculpture cut from recycled steel. Panels are flattened, drawn with chalk, then chiselled and punched so the line work appears as cut-through openings and raised ridges. Subjects are a tree of life, mermaids, birds, fish, sun faces, spirits with elaborate headdresses and crosses, drawn with symmetrical swirling leaves, scales, dotted outlines, hearts and curling vines. The metal is left dark grey and rust-brown, sometimes polished to rough silver with burn marks, or hand-painted in bright enamel like red, yellow and turquoise. Shapes are oval, round or irregular with ragged cut edges. A rugged industrial material turned into delicate lace. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
