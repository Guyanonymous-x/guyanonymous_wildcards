Styled in a design language of bright, cheerful, playful German retro fun. Palette is glossy primary red, sunshine yellow, orange, lime green and clear sky blue, with metallic gold foil and deep black outlines for contrast. Materials are translucent jelly-like gloss, crinkly shiny metallic film and clear windows showing a jumble of small chunky shapes, soft, smooth and slightly sugar-dusted. Forms are small, plump, rounded and simple. Colour does the shouting, and the whole language feels sunny, sweet, nostalgic and instantly readable. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
