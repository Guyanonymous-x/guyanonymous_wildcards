In the style of Moschino, witty, surreal, cheeky Italian pop design. Recurring motifs are a solid red heart, a pair of mirrored question marks inside a circle, a teddy-bear silhouette drawn in simple black and white line, and oversized playful icons. Palette is jet black, chalk white, signal red, hot pink, sunny yellow and glittering gold. Surfaces include glossy enamel, black sequins, gold foil, quilted metallic sheen and flat printed colour. Trompe-l'oeil humour turns ordinary forms into ironic, exaggerated parodies. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Moschino in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
