massive stone tombs in the middle of the village, each a great flat slab of pale gray limestone set on four thick stone legs over the grave, carved on the sides with buffalo horns, crocodiles and human figures
an old tomb in the shape of a stone house, its upright slabs carved with horses and roosters, moss and lichen growing in the cracks and the edges worn round by rain
the open stone ground between the tombs, where a water buffalo lies slain for a funeral, killed with a single deep machete cut to the side of its neck, its blood pooling dark red on the stones
a newer tomb of smooth gray concrete painted with horns and stars in white and red, beside a tall carved stone post for offerings topped with a flat stone
