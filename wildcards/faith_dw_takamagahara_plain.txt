the High Plain spreads among white clouds, broad green rice paddies divided by low earth banks, a palace of unpainted cypress wood with a thatched roof and crossed beams at its ends, and tall cedars around it
the Heavenly River Yasu runs across the plain, its wide bed of pale stones dry and open, where the myriad kami sit gathered in council around a great sakaki tree hung with a mirror and strings of jewels
the Heavenly Rock Cave opens in a high cliff, a great boulder rolled across its mouth, roosters on a perch before it and a sakaki tree hung with a round bronze mirror, curved jewels and strips of white and blue cloth
