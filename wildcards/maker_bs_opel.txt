Styled in a design language of neat, sporty, mass-market German design. Palette of signal yellow, bright orange, lime green, sky blue, white and black, plus period greys and enamel red. Clean ring-and-bolt geometry, dynamic diagonals, crisp and youthful, with a pragmatic, friendly, quietly sporty character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
