In the style of Bang & Olufsen, Danish luxury audio and television design. Slender, tall, minimalist forms in brushed and polished aluminium, black glass and pale oiled wood, with thin plates floating in space, tapered pyramid or cone speakers, and fabric-covered grilles in charcoal or warm grey. Controls are almost invisible, with hairline touch strips, a single large circular dial, a slim slide-down panel hiding hidden buttons, and subtle white or amber displays. Edges are razor-sharp with tiny perfectly even gaps. Palette is silver, black, natural oak, dark walnut and occasional soft earthy tones. Lettering is tiny, thin and lowercase. Mood is refined, sculptural and serene.
