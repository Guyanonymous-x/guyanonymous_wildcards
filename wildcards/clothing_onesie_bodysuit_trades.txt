It is a complete costume of bricklayer in one piece, shown full length from collar to ankles, with a work shirt and high-visibility vest, work trousers on the legs, a hard hat (as the hood), boots (as the feet), a trowel sewn or attached to the suit. The upper half carries the top and the leg tubes carry the lower garment, so the whole outfit reads at once. Novelty embellishments, scalloped felt edging and metallic foil-print accents that catch the light. It is a novelty costume garment, made to be bright, playful, over-the-top and a pleasure to touch.
