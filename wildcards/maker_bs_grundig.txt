In the style of Grundig, German radios and tape recorders of the 1950s and 1960s. Wood-veneer cabinets, brushed gold or chrome piano keys, large round glass scales with illuminated tuning markers and a magic-eye indicator, and pale cream or brown plastic knobs. Reel-to-reel recorders have grey-green hammerite finishes, small VU meters and neat rows of lettered buttons. A diamond-shaped mark sits on the front. Mood is solid, warm and middle-class.
