Styled in a design language of Finnish glass design with organic fluid forms, undulating leaf-like curves, free-form wavy edges like lake shores, simple cylinders with rings of bumps, and small bird shapes in colour. Palette of crystal clear, smoky grey, sea green, cobalt blue, burnt orange and ruby. Smooth, rounded, minimal and soft, with moulded ripples evoking ice, water and birch bark. Nordic calm and functional beauty. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
