at sunset, the river gold, the sun dropping low over the western shore and laying a wide path of molten yellow and orange across the water, the sky glowing amber and rose, the city towers their glass blazing orange in the light, swans and moored boats turned to small dark shapes on the bright surface
on a hot summer day, the river blue, a hard cloudless sky and dazzling white light, the water a deep vivid blue ruffled by the afternoon sea breeze, the sand almost white, the lawns dry and straw-colored at the edges, sharp black shadows under the trees and sails scattered all across the wide reach
in winter rain, the river gray, low dark clouds trailing curtains of rain over the water, the surface dull slate and pitted with thousands of small rings, the far shore and the city towers faded to pale shapes in the mist, the paperbark trunks soaked dark, puddles on the path and the lawns a fresh bright green
