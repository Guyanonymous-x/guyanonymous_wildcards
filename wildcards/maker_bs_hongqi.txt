In the style of Hongqi, Chinese state limousines and luxury cars with a huge waterfall grille of tall chrome vertical bars, a flag-shaped red badge on the bonnet, a long stately body, a formal upright roofline, and rounded square headlamps. Early cars of the 1960s have a palace-lantern style rear lamp, thin pillars, white sidewall tyres, wide chrome bumpers and a large wheelbase. Modern cars have a flowing long bonnet with a central ridge and a slim full-width tail lamp. Interior of red wood, cream leather and silk-like fabrics. Colours of black, deep red, gold and ivory. Majestic, ceremonial and traditional.
