Styled in a design language of British modernist moulded polypropylene, with one-piece seamless forms on four splayed legs in primary red, orange, white, black and lime, rounded rectangular profiles and gently flared edges. Plastic cubes, plain plywood and bent-ply surfaces, shiny high-impact polymer with a matte texture, simple geometry and mass-produced elegance. Pop, optimistic and space-age 1960s, with a friendly, bold, uncluttered look that suits mass production. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
