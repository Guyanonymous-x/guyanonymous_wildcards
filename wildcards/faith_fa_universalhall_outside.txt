the forecourt, a ring of small standing stones and a carved wooden bench beside the doors, bicycles in a rack, a wooden board reading "UNIVERSAL HALL" in carved and gilded letters, and a glass case of posters for the week's concerts
the back of the hall, a café terrace with wooden tables under umbrellas, a turf-roofed extension, herb planters, and community members and guests in their thirties to seventies with mugs and plates
the front doors, tall panels of thick stained glass set in heavy wood, swirling bands of deep blue, violet, amber and green flowing upward across both doors in the shapes of rising flames and leaves, made by the artist James Hubbell
