Fireworks burst in white and gold over the water, and streamers of colored smoke drift between the redwood trunks.
A small wooden landing stage juts into the lake beside the altar, where the ferryman brings the boat in and the hooded figures lift the effigy out.
The wooden carving of the club's patron saint, Saint John of Nepomuk, stands on a stump at the water's edge, robed and bearded, one finger pressed to his lips.
