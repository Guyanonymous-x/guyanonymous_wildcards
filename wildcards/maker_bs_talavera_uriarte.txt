In the style of Talavera Uriarte, tin-glazed earthenware with a thick, glossy milky white glaze and hand-painted decoration in cobalt blue, with accents of yellow, green, orange, black and sometimes turquoise. Patterns are flowers, vines, leaves, birds, rosettes and geometric borders, painted freely with a loose touch and a slightly raised, shiny paint. Plates, jars, jugs, basins and wall tiles are rounded and weighty, with visible pinholes and small unevenness in the glaze. Tiles combine into large courtyard panels in blue and white. The mood is Mexican colonial, rustic, festive and handmade.
