In the style of Honda, Japanese cars of the 1990s and 2000s with low sleek bonnets, wide thin grilles with an H badge, thin swept-back wedge headlamps, and a low cab-forward cockpit with big glass area. Compact, tidy, rounded shapes with a cut-off kammback rear and slim tail lamps. Sports versions have a high-revving red badge, a rear wing, round exhaust, and bright five-spoke alloy wheels. Interiors with clear white-dial gauges, a snug cockpit, a short shifter with a round knob and grey or black fabric. Colours of championship white, Milano red, Rio yellow, Berlina black and silver. Precise, light and clever.
