Styled in a design language of athletic, light, vintage, understated, slightly nerdy-cool 1970s-80s Japanese performance design. Low, sleek forms in smooth suede mixed with light nylon mesh, slightly flared bases and gently curved ends. Two crossed diagonal stripes in a contrasting colour run along the side, usually white on navy, or red, yellow and blue on pale grey, with a thin scalloped overlay. Thick wedges of cream or white foam over crepe or waffle-patterned gum rubber. Earthy sporty colourways such as cream with burgundy, navy with sky blue, or grey with orange and lime. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
