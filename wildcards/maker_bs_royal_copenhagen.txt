In the style of Royal Copenhagen, Danish porcelain with hand-painted cobalt blue underglaze patterns on white, such as ribbed fluted edges with delicate lace-like pierced rims, small blue flowers and intricate half-lace and full-lace motifs. Tableware, tureens and ornamental figurines with soft pastel overglaze, three small wavy lines as base mark, thin translucent body, crisp brushwork, symmetrical and orderly, heirloom dining table elegance.
