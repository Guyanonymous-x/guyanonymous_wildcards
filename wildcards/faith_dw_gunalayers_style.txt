painted in the bright flat colors of present-day Guna painters of Panama City, the layers drawn as stacked bands of deep blue, green, red and gold, every figure outlined in black and filled with fine repeated patterns
drawn in the manner of the Guna picture-drawings of the healing chants, rows of small stick figures, houses, trees and animals painted in red, black and blue on pale wooden boards, each band of figures one layer of the world
carved and painted in the manner of the nuchu, the small wooden healing figures of Guna healers, figures of pale balsa and dark hardwood in brimmed hats and long coats standing on each layer against painted mola patterns
