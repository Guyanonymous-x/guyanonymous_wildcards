Styled in a design language of loud, fast, race-ready graphic punch. Signature fluorescent lime green dominates, cut by black, white and occasional pale blue in sharp angular slashes, jagged shards, diagonal speed bands and torn-edge digital camouflage patterns. Surfaces are high-gloss, carbon-weave and matte black with tinted metallic highlights. Aggressive, athletic and unapologetically vivid. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
