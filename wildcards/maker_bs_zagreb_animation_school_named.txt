In the style of Zagreb animation school, modernist flat 2D look with minimal geometric characters made of simple shapes, thin line work, little or no shading and large blank backgrounds in single flat colours. Witty visual metaphors, angular stylised figures with stick-like limbs, abstract pop-art colour blocks in red, yellow, black and sky blue, jazzy off-balance compositions and a sophisticated deadpan, urbane 1960s graphic-design look. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
