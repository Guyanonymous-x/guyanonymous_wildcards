Styled in a design language of lightweight, stripped-back and purposeful British racing minimalism. Thin moulded shells in low wedge or rounded teardrop outlines, with bare edges, wide swelling curves and nothing added that is not needed. Racing green is paired with a single bright yellow band, alongside black with gold pinstriping, white, orange and metallic silver. Surfaces are glossy fibreglass-like sheen, exposed aluminium and thin carbon weave. Colour blocking is simple, sharp and light in spirit. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
