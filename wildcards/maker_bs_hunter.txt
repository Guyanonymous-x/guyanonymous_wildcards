Styled in a design language of polished British countryside practicality with a glossy, waterproof finish. Smooth, seamless, sleek rubber and coated surfaces have a high sheen, with a subtle matte bloom, in deep racing green, navy, black, olive and bright seasonal colours such as red, yellow and pink. Shapes are clean, rounded and streamlined with minimal seams and tidy moulded edges. Heritage touches include tweedy checks, waxed cotton, brass hardware, dark green boxes and thin white rules. Refined, tough, rain-ready, quietly classic and tidy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
