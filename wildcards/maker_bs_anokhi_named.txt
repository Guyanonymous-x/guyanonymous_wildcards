Styled in a design language of hand-block-printed cotton with fine repeating floral sprigs, vines, small buta motifs, lattice screens, paisleys and stripes printed in natural-looking dyes on soft white or cream voile. Colours are indigo, madder red, saffron, sage green, mustard, rose pink and charcoal, in two or three tones per print with slightly offset, hand-stamped edges. Shapes are loose and simple, with bound seams, quilting and small tassels. Fresh, breezy, craft-made mood, with sunlit whitewashed tones and a feeling of folded printed cloth. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
