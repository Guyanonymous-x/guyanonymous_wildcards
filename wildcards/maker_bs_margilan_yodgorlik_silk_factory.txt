In the style of Margilan Yodgorlik Silk Factory, Uzbek hand woven silk ikat textiles. Fabric is a lustrous silk, with the pattern made by tying and dyeing the warp threads before weaving, so every motif has softly blurred, feathered edges. Designs are bold and flowing, such as large almond shapes, teardrop and flame forms, pomegranates, jagged blossoms, stripes and zigzag bands in saturated jewel colours, with ruby red, magenta, saffron yellow, indigo, emerald green and black, outlined by thin white lines on a deep ground. Atlas silk is shiny and light, while adras blends silk and cotton for a matte feel. Finished goods include long robes, coats, headscarves, cushions and wall hangings. The dyeing uses natural vegetable and mineral dyes, so colours glow but are a little uneven. Workshops show racks of skeins dyed and hanging, wooden looms with weavers seated at the loom. Mood is vivid, sumptuous, handmade and silky.
