In the style of Missoni, knitted patterns of zigzag chevrons, flame stitch and wavy stripes, woven in rainbow colour gradients that blend from teal to orange, pink, purple, ochre and cream. Fine lightweight wool and rayon texture with crochet-like openwork, relaxed drape and stretch, flowing long shapes. Colours shift in tonal bands, creating bold yet soft optical vibrations. A 1970s bohemian luxury look. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
