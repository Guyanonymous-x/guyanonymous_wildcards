In the style of Royal Air Maroc, warm, hospitable, ornate and sunny identity of red and green geometry. White grounds with a vivid red area carrying a green five pointed star inside a round white emblem, and a slim green and red stripe. The name appears in Latin and Arabic script in bold dark red. Smart dark red with a fine scarf and decorative pin. Blue skies over terracotta walls, tilework in blue, green and white and palm trees. Carved latticework repeats an eight pointed star. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
