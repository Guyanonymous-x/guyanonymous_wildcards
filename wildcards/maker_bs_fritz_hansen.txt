In the style of Fritz Hansen, Danish modern furniture with sculptural moulded laminated wood shells in a curvy ant, butterfly or egg silhouette, pinched waist and no visible join between seat and back. Slim chromed steel tube legs on three or four points, upholstery in tight leather or tweed wool, coloured lacquered veneer in black, white, lime green, deep red and warm walnut. Swivel pod chairs on a spiderlike cast aluminium base, generous cushioning, glossy and smooth surfaces. Elegant, airy, hotel lobby and Copenhagen design office feel with smooth organic flow, never fussy.
