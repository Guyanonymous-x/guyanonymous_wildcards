Styled in a design language of monumental, upright and hushed grandeur. Tall formal verticals, long smooth rolling volumes and flat slab surfaces, with a single thin bright line drawn along the length of a form. Mirror-polished metal with slim fluted vertical rhythm, and deep lustrous colour in claret, silver sand, midnight blue or black, often in two tones with a lower band of silver. Hand-stitched cream or tan leather, deep wool pile, burr walnut veneer with fine inlay and weighty turned metal details. Dignified, imposing, effortless and quiet.
