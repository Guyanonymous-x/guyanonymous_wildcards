Wuriupranili, the sun woman, crosses the sky as a woman in her forties with dark brown skin, painted in red and yellow ochre, carrying a burning torch of stringybark held high in one hand
Japarra, the moon man, a man in his fifties painted in white ochre, walks across the night sky, his body shining pale, the stars around him in rows of white dots
the sky is painted as bands of ochre color above the islands, red for the dawn, yellow for the day and black for the night, the sun and moon as round disks within them
