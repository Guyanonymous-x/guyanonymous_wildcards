Styled in a design language of a glossy modern take on Chinese imperial and 1930s dress, with form-fitting lines, mandarin collars, cloth knot fastenings, side slits and piped edges. Colours are intense and playful, such as imperial yellow, lacquer red, fuchsia, jade green, turquoise, orange and purple, in a candy-bright palette alongside black and gold. Satin, brocade, jacquard and embroidered silk carry dragons, peonies, double happiness symbols, bats, clouds and lotus flowers, with padded quilting. Lacquered red surfaces, tassels and enamel inlays add richness. Cheerful, nostalgic, theatrical glamour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
