Styled in a design language of a flat but layered surface treatment where every element looks like a sheet of coloured paper lying on another, with soft diffuse shadows beneath to show height. Surfaces are flat solid colour with no bevels or gloss, in bold indigo, teal, pink, orange and purple, alongside clean white and light grey sheets with gently rounded corners. Round floating discs carry a single white pictogram and cast a soft shadow. Icons are simple geometric glyphs with even line weight. Generous spacing, tidy grids, circular ripple shapes and cheerful flat colour blocks. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
