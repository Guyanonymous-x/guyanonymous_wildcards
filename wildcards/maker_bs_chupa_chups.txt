Styled in a design language of playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop. Simple round shapes and clean sticks of form, with a glossy, candy-like shine and thin crinkled shimmer. The mark is a bright daisy-like flower with a yellow circle in the middle and red petals, with the name in friendly rounded white and yellow letters, on bright red, yellow or orange. Flavours are signalled by colour, pink, orange, green, cola brown and lemon yellow, some swirled with two colours. Hot-pink or red fields radiate outward in a sunburst. Cartoon children and sugary cheerfulness.
