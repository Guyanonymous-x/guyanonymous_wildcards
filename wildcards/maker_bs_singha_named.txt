Styled in a design language of sunny, tropical, festive heraldic design. A deep red and gold border frames a central mythical lion in a rearing pose with a curly mane, tufted tail, raised paw and fierce expression, drawn in old engraving style and flanked by ornamental curls. Surfaces are glossy deep red with gold and white detail and gold foil accents that add a sense of ceremony. Amber brown, brown and cream complete the palette. Chilled condensation, spicy grilled food and beach light. Lively and bold. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
