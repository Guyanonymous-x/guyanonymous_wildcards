Styled in a design language of overwrought, odd and unforgettable American design. A tall vertical horse-collar shape set in the middle of a wide bar, wingtip boomerang forms, side sculpting with a bright spear and a cut-away dip along the flank. Two-tone pink and black, turquoise and white, yellow and brown. Open pillarless transparency, chrome rims with whitewall roundness and a rolling dome of graphics add oddness. Ambitious, fussy and strange.
