In the style of Rotring, precision technical drawing pens and mechanical pencils with a slim, hexagonal or round metal barrel in matte black or silver, with a knurled grip and a red ring around the top. The shape is industrial and tool-like, with a long conical metal nose, a retractable sleeve, a clip and tiny printed line-width numbers in white on the barrel. Colours are black, brushed chrome and a vivid red accent. Technical pens use colour-coded end caps and ink cartridges. Finishes are crisp and engineered, and the mood is draughtsman, architect studio and exacting accuracy.
