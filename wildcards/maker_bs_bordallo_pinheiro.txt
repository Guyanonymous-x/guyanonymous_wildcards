Styled in a design language of naturalistic majolica, whimsical, sculptural, rustic and slightly surreal with cheeky humour. Forms are moulded as oversized cabbage leaves with veined, ruffled edges and vivid green glaze with a pale ribbed back. Realistic creatures such as lobsters, snakes, frogs, lizards, snails, beetles and flies crawl across surfaces in lifelike detail, along with sunflowers. Glazes are glossy, juicy and saturated, leaf green, mustard yellow, turquoise, coral, tomato red, aubergine purple and cream. Colours are exaggerated, textures lumpy and tactile, glistening as if freshly wet, with an almost trompe loeil effect. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
