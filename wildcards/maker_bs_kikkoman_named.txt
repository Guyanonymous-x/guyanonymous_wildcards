Styled in a design language of precise, balanced, restrained Japanese practicality. A bold red and black hexagonal crest with a stylised white mark sits on plain white or black fields with generous empty space. Forms are slender, softly faceted and gently tapered, with small glossy red cone accents. Materials are clear glass with dark amber depth, glossy black enamel, matt white ceramic and clean paper labels with fine hairline rules. Palette is deep red, black, white, amber and soft cream, with small bright yellow or green accents for variants. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
