Styled in a design language of comfortable middle-class luxury of the 1950s, rounded and heavily chromed, dignified rather than flashy. A toothy chrome grin of many small bars, large round chrome bullet shapes, a row of three or four round portholes and a sweeping chrome spear along the side. Slightly bulbous, plump forms and a tri-shield motif. Cream, sea green, coral and maroon. Warm, prosperous and reassuring. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
