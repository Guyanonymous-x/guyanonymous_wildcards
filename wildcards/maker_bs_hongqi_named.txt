In the style of Hongqi, majestic, ceremonial and traditional Chinese state grandeur. A huge waterfall of tall chrome vertical bars, a flag-shaped red emblem, long stately massing and a formal upright top line with rounded-square details. Early forms add palace-lantern style details, thin supports, white bands and wide chrome edging. Modern forms add a flowing long front with a central ridge and a slim full-width light line. Red wood, cream leather and silk-like fabrics. Palette of black, deep red, gold and ivory. Solemn, imperial and proud.
