a woman in her forties lies sick on a mat in a thatched house, her kin in their twenties to seventies around her, as the buyei, a woman in her sixties in a white dress and head wrap, sits by her
the buyei, a man in his sixties, kneels by a sick man in his fifties and speaks with the gubida, who say they have been forgotten and ask for a feast
at the end of the dugu the healed woman in her forties dances with her kin, the sickness gone from her, the food for the ancestors carried to the sea in a canoe
