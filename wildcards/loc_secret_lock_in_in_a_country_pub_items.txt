eight or nine people round the bar and nobody standing
the till closed and a ballpoint tally on the back of a beer mat recording what is owed
the optics and pumps still working

a dog asleep across the hearth
a fire banked up rather than let out
an ashtray that has appeared from under the bar in defiance of twenty years of law
a dartboard light switched off
a phone behind the bar face down
a clock that says half past one
a cat on the bar
a plate of bread and cheese that converts the drinking into something else
