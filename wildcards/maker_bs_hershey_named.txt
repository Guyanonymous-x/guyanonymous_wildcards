In the style of Hershey, plain, sturdy, warm American everyday design in a monochrome deep chocolate-brown field with silver or white lettering. Bold condensed sans-serif capitals run horizontally. Silvery metallic accents, flat segmented rectangles with raised square grids, and teardrop forms with a flat base and a small flag-like plume. Occasional red or gold accents mark seasonal variations. Minimal decoration, solid and dependable, with a nostalgic, unfussy honesty.
