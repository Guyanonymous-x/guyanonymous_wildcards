In the style of Lufthansa, orderly, efficient, geometric modernism and strictly typographic. A deep, rich navy and a bright canary yellow form the core palette, joined by clean white and soft grey. A circular emblem encloses a bird in flight drawn in just a few simple strokes, like a seal, in blue on white or yellow on blue. Lettering is sober, evenly spaced, medium-weight sans-serif in navy. Compositions use flat, wide colour fields, a strict grid, thin rules and generous empty margins. Finishes are matte and calm with crisp edges and precise alignment. Everything feels trustworthy, disciplined, cool-headed and purposeful, with understated German clarity and no ornament. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Lufthansa in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
