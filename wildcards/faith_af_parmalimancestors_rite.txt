in the Bale Pasogit prayer house at Huta Tinggi, a high-roofed hall of wood, Parmalim men in white shirts and white headcloths and women in white blouses kneel in rows in prayer for the dead
a Parmalim elder in his sixties in a white headcloth sprinkles pangurason, clear water with slices of green lime floating in it, from a white bowl over the bowed heads of the kin
white offerings for the dead are set on a low table, plates of white rice, white eggs, betel leaves and lime slices, as the kin in their twenties to seventies sit around it
the kin lift the cleaned bones of an ancestor from an opened grave, lay them in a small white box draped in a woven ulos cloth of dark red and black and carry it to the new tomb
