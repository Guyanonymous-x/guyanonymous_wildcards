Styled in a design language of cool, austere post-punk minimalism with large empty space and thin sans-serif type. Coded barcode-like or abstract geometric patterns, industrial hazard stripes in black and yellow, silver or metallic ink on matt grey stock, textured cardboard, muted greys, steel blues and muted pastel colours, grainy halftone photographs and a clinical, enigmatic, modernist attitude. Compositions are quiet and carefully spaced, with cool restraint, deliberate mystery and an industrial, coolly intellectual mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
