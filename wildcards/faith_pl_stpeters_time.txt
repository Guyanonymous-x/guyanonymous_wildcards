at Easter, the square full and the steps covered in flowers, the broad stairs before the basilica banked with thousands of tulips, daffodils, lilies and flowering shrubs in yellow, white, orange and pink around a canopied altar, a bright spring sky above the packed crowd
at sunset, the dome dark against a pink sky, the great ribbed shell and its lantern a deep blue-gray outline above the roofs, the sky behind it glowing rose, peach and pale gold, and the statues on the colonnades standing black along the skyline
at night, the basilica and the colonnades lit gold, floodlights washing the pale stone front, the columns and the dome in warm amber against a black sky, the wet cobblestones of the nearly bare square shining with long reflections and the fountains sparkling
