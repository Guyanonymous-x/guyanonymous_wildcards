above the earth rise the four layers of the sky as bands of shining cloud, each with its own villages of golden houses, and in the highest layer Pab Dummad and Nan Dummad, a man and a woman in their seventies, sit side by side in a house of gold
the highest heaven is painted as a band of brilliant gold mola cloth, where Nan Dummad, the Great Mother, a woman in her sixties in a mola blouse, a red and yellow headscarf and a gold nose ring, sits beside Pab Dummad, a man in his sixties in a white shirt and a felt hat
the layers of the sky climb one above another as bands of blue, white and gold, flocks of macaws, parrots and frigate birds flying in the lower ones and the moon and stars fixed in the upper ones as rows of shining dots
