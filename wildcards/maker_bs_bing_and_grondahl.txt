Styled in a design language of Danish thin white porcelain in soft blue-grey and white, with naturalistic Art Nouveau brushwork of seabirds, herons, stylised flowers and tall reeds in shaded cobalt. Muted pastel greys, creams, sea blues and lilac, smooth curved forms, delicate relief moulding with winter village scenes, and crackled and sculpted surfaces. Delicate, serene and quietly poetic.
