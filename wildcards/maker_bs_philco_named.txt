Styled in a design language of Mid-century American radios, televisions and refrigerators with rounded cabinet shapes in walnut and mahogany veneer, sloped chrome dial faces, bullet knobs and cream plastic, plus futuristic 'predicta' televisions with a swivelling screen on a separate stalk in black and cream. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
