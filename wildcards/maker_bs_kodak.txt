Styled in a design language of cheerful, familiar and snapshot-friendly. Early forms are leather-covered boxes with a round lens-like feature and a simple window. Mid-century pieces have art deco ribs and chrome stripes, then moulded plastic in the 1970s and 1980s. Bright yellow surfaces with red panels. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
