a 1930s composition doll, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her head and body are made of composition, a molded mix of sawdust and glue, painted a warm pink and coated with a thin glossy varnish. Her face has round cheeks, painted brown eyes with a white highlight, painted lashes and a small smiling mouth, and her hair is painted on in molded curls. The varnish has crazed into fine cracks across her cheeks and forehead, the way composition ages. Her arms and legs are jointed at the shoulder and hip, and she wears a cotton dress with puffed sleeves, white socks and tiny strap shoes. __doll_composition_doll_var__.
