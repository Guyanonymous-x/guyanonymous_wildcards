Styled in a design language of nostalgic, glamorous, carefully crafted Art Deco and Belle Epoque decoration. Lavishly patterned lithographic prints of peacock feathers, ferns, roses, swallows, geometric fans, arcs and rays. Heavy, smooth, pale creamy forms embossed with a crest. Rounded vintage glass. Colourful rows against warm wood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
