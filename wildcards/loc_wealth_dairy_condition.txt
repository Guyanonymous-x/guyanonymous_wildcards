the farm dairy of 1750 producing all the butter and cheese a household of forty eats, worked by a dairymaid and a girl
the ornamental dairy of 1790-1830, tiled, vaulted, fitted with marble and a fountain and visited by the family, of which the type's best examples are genuine working rooms in fancy dress
the dairy of 1880 with a barrel churn, a separator and a thermometer, which is the moment the process becomes measured rather than judged
the room of 1910 with the milk sold off the estate to a town dairy and this room reduced to a cool store, which is the commonest end
the room in 1935 with the louvre glazed and the benches used for flowers
the room as a tea room or a visitor room, which keeps the slate and the brick
the room in a divided house as a garage or a bin store
the room mothballed, limewashed, swept, the sycamore oiled and racked
the room open to the weather, where it survives nearly as well as the larder, slate, brick, tile and lime endure, and the sycamore is the only loss
