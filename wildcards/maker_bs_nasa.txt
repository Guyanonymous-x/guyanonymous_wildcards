Styled in a design language of clean, technical, optimistic American spaceflight of the 1960s and 1970s. Tall white forms with bold black patterns, small flags in red, white and blue, and golden foil-wrapped shapes. Bright white surfaces with orange or silver details. Marks are either a red, worm-like lettering or a blue circle with stars and a swoosh. Riveted aluminium panels, dark glossy surfaces, exposed cabling and bell shapes, all against black space. Engineered, heroic, hopeful and precise.
