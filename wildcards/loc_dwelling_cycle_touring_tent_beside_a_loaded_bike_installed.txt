the best-resourced of the human-powered dwellings and the one that has been refined longest
since cycle touring with camping equipment is well over a century old, a tent of one to three kilograms with a porch large enough to cook in, which is the specification that distinguishes a touring tent from a backpacking one
pitched every evening and struck every morning so that the dwelling is erected three hundred times in a long tour and every pole and peg is known
a bicycle carrying forty to fifty liters of luggage in four panniers and a bar bag
with the weight distributed low and front-to-rear in a proportion cyclists argue about endlessly
a cooking system of one stove
one pot and one mug
water carried and filtered
no services assumed although a campsite with a shower and a socket is sought every third or fourth night
a daily rhythm set by distance
weather and light rather than by a clock
a route that may cross continents, and an occupancy of weeks to years in which this tent is genuinely the rider's only home
