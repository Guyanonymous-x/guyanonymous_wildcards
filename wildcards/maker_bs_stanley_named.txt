In the style of Stanley, rugged, honest, mid-century working-man design with rounded cylindrical forms, stout shoulders and squat proportions. The signature finish is hammertone enamel in deep forest green with a subtle dimpled stipple texture, paired with brushed stainless steel, rolled metal edges and sturdy riveted details. Further colourways include cream, navy, charcoal, matte black and soft pastel tones in powder-coated finishes. The mark is a small white or gold crest with a block capital wordmark framed by a beaded border, sometimes with a tiny stylised animal emblem. Slightly dented, workshop-honest, practical and durable, with a nostalgic workshop heritage feel. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Stanley in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
