Styled in a design language of rich, rustic, rhythmic and symmetrical hand-woven textiles in natural dyed wool. Long narrow bands and square cloths in slightly fuzzy hand-spun yarn. Colours are earthy plant-dye shades, deep indigo and sky blues, cochineal reds, pinks and purples, yellow-greens, mustard, walnut brown, cream and charcoal. Edges are finished with braided cords and tassels. A precise ancient textile language in warm natural tones. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
