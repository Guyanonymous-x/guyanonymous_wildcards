Styled in a design language of bold German technical-graphics identity. Printed graphics and labels use vivid orange, ultramarine, black and cream with striking stylised geometric imagery. Materials are polished walnut and beech veneer, chrome-plated metal, cream enamel, bakelite and enamelled badge plates with raised edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
