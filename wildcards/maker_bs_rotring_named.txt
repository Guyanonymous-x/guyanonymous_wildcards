In the style of Rotring, precision drafting sensibility from Germany. A signature red ring and a bold lowercase red wordmark with a circle-and-bar motif form the mark, always set on black, white or grey. Palette is matte black, brushed steel, silver, graphite and one vivid scarlet accent. Surfaces are knurled, crosshatched metal with fine machined ridges, satin anodised finishes and tiny white printed scale markings. Forms are hexagonal, cylindrical and tapered, slim and tool-like, with crisp edges and no ornament. Lettering is small, geometric and technical, with thin red rings and numerals. Engineered, exact, draughtsman-like, serious and timeless. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Rotring in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
