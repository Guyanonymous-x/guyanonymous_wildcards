Styled in a design language of cheerful, honest, retro Japanese design built around a bold roundel emblem. Palette is primary red, royal blue, cream white, sunny yellow, mustard and orange with warm off-white paper tones, often as flat colour blocks with thick stripes. Shapes are rounded, compact and friendly with small shields, pennants and crest forms. Materials include enamelled tin, painted metal, chrome-edged plates, felt patches and glossy printed paper with a gently worn, nostalgic patina. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
