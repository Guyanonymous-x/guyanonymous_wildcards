In the style of Ski-Doo, compact, low, cheerful, adventurous and retro 1960s and 1970s snow-country machinery. Bulbous moulded fibreglass volumes swoop down toward short curled forms, with a high bubble of clear glass, upswept ducktail curves, ribbed black rubber and exposed springs. Paint is bright and glossy, sunny yellow, orange, red, white or lime green, with bold contrasting stripes, large block letters and a stylised animal or speed-line motif. Chrome accents are small and shiny. Matching quilted one-piece clothing in bright colours. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
