Styled in a design language of opulent, ceremonial, theatrical British luxury. A grand Edwardian terracotta-fronted facade in cream stone, outlined at night with hundreds of tiny electric lights, with green awnings and gilded lettering. Thick surfaces in green and gold with ribbon trim and a plain gold serif wordmark. Marble, polished brass, deep green carpet and ornate plaster ceilings, plus exotic themed halls with gold sphinx figures and hieroglyph panels. Palette is racing green, gold, cream and terracotta. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
