In the style of Moto Guzzi, proud Italian craft in red, silver and black. A glossy elongated oval badge in rich red holds a spread-winged eagle in silver and gold above a bold capital wordmark with wide, even letter spacing. Round red and gold roundels, thin gold edge lines and tricolour stripes in green, white and red appear as recurring accents. Surfaces are deep lacquer, mirror-polished chrome, brushed alloy and warm tan leather. Shapes are smooth, generous, long and teardrop-like with calm, balanced curves. The mood is dignified, mechanical, sophisticated and confident, with wings as an ornamental motif for pattern work. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Moto Guzzi in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
