Styled in a design language of fast, sculpted, muscular and racing-bred Italian intensity. Glossy lacquer red dominates in broad colour fields with matte black, bright white and brushed silver as sharp counterpoints. Forms are sharply tapered, with angular facets, aggressive creases and a taut, coiled, ready-to-pounce stance. Details use carbon-fibre weave, anodised gold accents, mesh and fine contrast stitching. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
