Styled in a design language of heritage travel luxury built on repeating pattern. Edges are bound in natural leather that ages to honey, with polished brass studs, clasps, locks and rolled handles, and mustard stitching. Packaging is warm saffron orange boxes with brown trim and navy ribbon. Playful bright reinterpretations sit beside classic ones. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
