the platform seen from the open steppe, a long low flat-topped stack of tightly bound brushwood bundles, gray-brown and weathered, covering as much ground as a whole village and rising to the height of several men
one of the three sheer sides, a wall of stacked bundles of dry twigs and branches laid in layers, the cut ends of the sticks showing, the lowest layers pressed dark and dense by the weight above
the sloped fourth side, a broad ramp of trampled brushwood climbing to the top, worn smooth along its middle by the feet of people and the hooves of led animals
a line of four-wheeled ox wagons with felt-covered tops drawn up at the foot of the platform, men in their twenties to forties throwing down fresh bundles of brushwood and carrying them up the slope on their backs
