Styled in a design language of loud, cartoonish, collectible streetwear graphics. Camouflage is the core pattern, a large blobby shark-skin style camo in unexpected bright colourways such as lime green, pink, purple, yellow, blue and rainbow gradients, alongside classic olive and grey versions. Glossy star shapes, boxy, heavy cotton, fleece and nylon forms. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
