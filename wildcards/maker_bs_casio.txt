Styled in a design language of cheap, functional, geeky and indestructible. Lightweight resin in black, grey, silver or bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal or rectangular outlines. Grey-green segmented displays with chunky digits, small rubber push controls labelled in tiny white capitals, and tough rubberised protective surrounds. Gold-finished steel variants appear. Grid layouts of controls in grey, orange and blue. Printed text is dense, small and technical with many function labels around the edges. Utilitarian and playful. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
