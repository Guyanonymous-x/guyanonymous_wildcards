In the style of Meissen, German hard-paste porcelain, lustrous white with exquisitely hand-painted scattered flowers, vividly coloured birds and chinoiserie scenes. Rococo tableware, gilded scrolling borders, applied sculpted flowers and delicate lace-draped porcelain figures of shepherdesses, courtiers and harlequins with pink cheeks, dainty gestures and pastel costumes. Blue crossed swords mark in underglaze, onion pattern in cobalt blue and iron red, luxurious, fragile, baroque palace cabinet showpiece.
