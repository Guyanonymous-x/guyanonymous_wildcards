Styled in a design language of soft heirloom German craft. Mohair, felt and woven fur in honey gold, cinnamon, caramel, cream and smoky brown, with slightly worn, loved tips and fine hand-stitched seams in dark thread. Small black glass-bead eyes, stitched noses and softly rounded, slightly humped, long-limbed proportions. Antique, tender, sentimental, tactile and carefully made, with a warm nursery calm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
