Styled in a design language of cheerful, bouncy, golden-age arcade charm of the early 1980s. Characters are simple round shapes with big expressive eyes, drawn in a chunky low-resolution pixel grid on pure black. A yellow circle with a wedge mouth chases glowing dots through a neon blue outlined maze of rounded corridors, pursued by pastel ghosts in red, pink, cyan and orange with wavy skirts and blinking white eyes. Flat bright pixel-art fruit. Symmetrical insect-like shapes in blue, purple and yellow move in formation against single twinkling stars. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
