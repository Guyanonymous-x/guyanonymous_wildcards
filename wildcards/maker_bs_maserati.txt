In the style of Maserati, a graceful Italian grand tourer with a long bonnet, tight fastback or coupe roofline, and a large oval grille with vertical chrome bars bearing a trident badge. Curvy muscular wings, side vents shaped like three small ports in the front wing, slim headlamps, sharp rear lights shaped like boomerangs, and wide sculpted haunches. Wheels are polished multi-spoke alloys or wire-wheel styles. Paint in deep blue, silver, dark burgundy, pearl white, and black. Interior has luxurious leather seats in tan, red, or cream, polished wood or carbon trim, an analogue clock in the centre of the dash, and fine stitching. Mood is smooth, refined, throaty and elegant.
