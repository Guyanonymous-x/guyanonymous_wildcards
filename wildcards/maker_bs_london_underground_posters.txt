Styled in a design language of flat gouache and lithographic imagery with clean, simplified scenes of rolling green hills, cottages, villages and tall stylised trees, with strong silhouettes and bold flat colour. Lettering is a clear geometric sans-serif in carefully balanced capitals with round O shapes. A red circle crossed by a blue horizontal bar on white is used as the identifying sign. Limited palette of poster red, deep blue, mustard yellow, warm brown, sage green and cream. Cubist and Art Deco inspired simplified forms, flat layered planes, generous white margins and text set beneath the image, serene and optimistic.
