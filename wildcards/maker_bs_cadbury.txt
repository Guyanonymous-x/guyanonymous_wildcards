Styled in a design language of a rich deep royal purple dominates every surface in a glossy, silky, velvety finish. The signature mark is a flowing hand-written gold script in thick looping letters, set inside a soft rounded oval or swooping ribbon swirl. Cream, white and warm gold act as accents, along with thin gold borders, curved swoosh lines and ribbon-like flowing shapes. Surfaces include metallic gold foil, satin paper and smooth lacquer with a soft highlight. Shapes are rounded, plump and generous, with gently curved corners and sweeping curves rather than angles. Decorative flourishes, swirls and subtle shimmer add a sense of treat and indulgence. The mood is warm, traditional, comforting and generous. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
