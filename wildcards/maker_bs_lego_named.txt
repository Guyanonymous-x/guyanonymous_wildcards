In the style of Lego, glossy hard plastic in saturated primary red, blue, yellow and white plus black, grey and green. Everything is a rectangular modular volume with a regular grid of round studs on the surface, crisp moulded edges and visible seams, so any subject reads as assembled from simple bricks. Figures have cylindrical yellow heads with dot eyes and a simple smile, claw hands and blocky bodies. Graphics use bold colour fields, clean line diagrams, chunky rounded sans-serif lettering and bright playful lighting.
