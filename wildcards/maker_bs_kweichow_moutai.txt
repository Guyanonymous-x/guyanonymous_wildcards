Styled in a design language of formal, festive, prosperous and traditional ceremonial design. A squat cylindrical white porcelain form with a short neck, glossy milk-white glaze and a slight shoulder curve, topped with a red ribbon tied over a seal and often a tiny hanging tag. A bright vermilion band wraps the middle, edged in gold. Special editions use red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Lacquer red or wood with yellow silk lining, embossed in gold and closed with a white cord or golden bow. Auspicious red, gold and white against dark polished tables. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
