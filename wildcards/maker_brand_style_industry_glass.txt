In the style of Venini Murano Glass, hand-blown Venetian art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, including emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered with a clear casing. Techniques create fused patches of colour like woven fabric, delicate lace threads, vertical ribbons, bubbled inclusions and acid-etched satin surfaces. Vases, bowls, bottles and chandeliers feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean lines inspired by mid-century modernism. Many pieces are polished at the rim and carry a small etched mark.
In the style of Whitefriars Glass, heavy, thick-walled studio-style glass with bold sculptural shapes and a textured surface. Famous items include bark-textured vases with irregular tree-like ridges, chunky bubbles and cased layers of strong colour. The palette is deep and earthy, with tangerine orange, meadow green, kingfisher blue, aubergine purple, ruby red, amber and smoky grey. Forms are cylindrical, tapered, faceted or swirled, with heavy bases and slight asymmetry. Moulded ribbed pattern and dimpled decoration create a strong tactile, glowing mid-century impression. Surfaces are glossy, with a deep inner colour.
In the style of Steuben Glass, crystal clear, flawless, heavy lead-free optical glass vases, bowls and sculptures with a pure glass transparency and thick, polished walls. Shapes are clean, simple and modernist, with crisp curves, tapered columns, deep facets and bowls resting on thick bases. Engraved or cut decoration is minimal, such as thin copper-wheel engraved animals, fish and plants, or sharp geometric cuts that refract light. Colour appears rarely, in subtle tinted tones such as pale gold or aqua. The mood is cool, luminous, precise and quiet, like a block of ice shaped by hand.
In the style of Venini, Venetian art glass with thin hand-blown walls and elegant organic forms, such as asymmetrical vases, bulbous bottles, fazzoletto handkerchief bowls with wavy rims, and pale transparent bubbles. Colours are luminous amber, aquamarine, turquoise, lilac, milky white and rich red, with patchwork murrine mosaics, incalmo bands, delicate lattice threading and acid-etched matte surfaces. Smooth rounded shapes, light-catching, subtle imperfection, mid-century Italian jewel-like sculptural glass in a gallery vitrine.
In the style of Lalique, French Art Deco moulded glass in frosted opalescent and crystal clear, with relief decoration of flowers, birds, dragonflies, nude female figures, fish and leaping gazelles. Satin matte frosting contrasted with polished highlights, pale ice-blue and amber tinted glass, black enamel details and geometric stylised borders. Vases, perfume bottles, car mascots and chandeliers with a glowing crystal sparkle, sinuous curves, elegant and refined Parisian luxury.
In the style of Daum, French Art Nouveau cameo and pate de verre glass, thick layered vessels with acid-etched and wheel-carved decoration of thistles, poppies, irises, mountain landscapes and winter scenes. Mottled colours graded from smoky orange to deep green, milky mist, powder blue and amber, with enamelled and gilded highlights. Soft translucent bodies, naturalistic plant forms swelling around bulbous vases and lamp shades, rich and dreamy, subdued glow like looking through frosted weather.
In the style of Baccarat, French lead crystal tableware and lighting, clear and brilliant with deep geometric diamond cuts, fluted columns, faceted stoppers and heavy bases. Stemware with cut hobnail diamonds and sparkling prismatic edges, ornate chandeliers with tiers of pendant drops and gilded bronze arms, decanters, vases and bright red cut-glass candlesticks. Dazzling refraction, rainbow highlights, luxury dining table set under candlelight, formal and opulent Second Empire palace feel.
In the style of Orrefors, Swedish art glass, thick crystal-clear vessels with deep engraved or wheel-cut scenes, ethereal Graal pieces with trapped air bubbles and abstract wavy colour patterns, and Ariel glass with internal air traps. Streamlined heavy bowls and tapering vases, smooth heavy walls, cool transparent palette with accents of slate blue, smoky grey and soft green. Understated, refined, sculptural Scandinavian modernism, pure and sparkling in cold light.
In the style of Kosta Boda, Swedish contemporary art glass, bold and colourful hand-blown pieces with chunky abstract forms, bright stripes, spotted and swirled colour layers, and tall vases with cheerful faces, birds, fish, cat shapes and pastel clowns. Thick transparent bodies, saturated magenta, cobalt, orange, emerald and cool teal swirls, glossy smooth surfaces and organic asymmetrical silhouettes. Joyful, playful, sculptural, gallery shelf display with light refracting through thick glass.
In the style of Iittala, Finnish glass and tableware with organic fluid forms, such as undulating leaf-shaped bowls, free-form vases with wavy rims like lake shores, simple cylindrical tumblers with a ring of bumps, and bird sculptures in coloured glass. Palette of crystal clear, smoky grey, sea green, cobalt blue, burnt orange and ruby. Smooth, rounded, minimal and soft, with moulded ripples evoking ice, water and birch bark, Nordic calm and functional beauty.
In the style of Moser, Bohemian crystal glassware with heavy cut and engraved decoration, gold-gilded rims, deeply cut facets, and etched floral and animal motifs such as stags, swans and acanthus. Intense transparent coloured glass in alexandrite purple, emerald green, cobalt, ruby, amber and rich smoky topaz, with enamelled gold frames and fluted columns. Rich, jewelled, Art Deco and Belle Epoque luxury, elegant stemware, decanters and vases glowing in warm lamp light.
