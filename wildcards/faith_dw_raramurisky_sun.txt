Onorúame, Father Sun, shines high in the sky as a great gold disk with the calm face of a man in his fifties, rays of red, orange and yellow spreading from him across the whole sky
Onorúame appears as a tall man in his fifties with brown skin, in a white shirt and a red headband, standing in the sky within the blazing gold disk of the sun, his arms open over the canyons
Onorúame, joined in many villages with God the Father, sits in a blaze of white and gold light at the top of the sky, an old man in his sixties with white hair, in a white robe
