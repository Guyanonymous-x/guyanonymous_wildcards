__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of joyful, hallucinatory, highly saturated carved and painted folk art. Light soft wood is hand-carved into lively mythical creatures, often with a human face or braided hair on an animal body, caught mid-action while flying, scratching or leaping. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
