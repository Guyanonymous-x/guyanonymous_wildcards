a row of nine god pots on a long board laid on the earth along the west side, each a clay bowl the size of two cupped hands with a face of raised eyes, nose and jutting lower lip on its rim, painted white with red and black stripes
the god pots crusted thick and black with burned incense, their striped white paint showing only on the lower sides, the mouths of the modeled faces smeared with food, a bed of fresh palm leaves under them
the god pots lit, a lump of yellow-white copal resin burning in each bowl with a low orange flame, thick black smoke rising from the whole row and rolling out under the eaves
the old god pots lined up on a hanging shelf of poles under the roof, and below them on the board five new pots of pale unburned clay with fresh red and black stripes, made to replace them
