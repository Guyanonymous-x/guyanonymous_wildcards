far above stand the seven heavens of the Bogomil teaching, seven curved bands of light, each brighter than the one below it, the highest a field of pure white radiance where the invisible Father dwells, shown only as light
in the highest heaven blazes a great round field of white light with soft rays streaming out from it, the throne of the invisible good Father shown as an empty seat of light, angels in long pale robes standing in rows around it
the heavens are a vast clear space of pale blue and white, filled with rows of shining angels, men and women in their twenties and thirties in long white robes, their faces calm, the light so strong that their outlines blur
between heaven and earth a fallen angel, Satanael, tumbles down from the light, a dark-winged man in his thirties in a torn robe, his wings blackening as he falls from the seventh heaven toward the world of matter
