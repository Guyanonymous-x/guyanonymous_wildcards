Styled in a design language of rectangular paper printed in black and green ink on cream cotton stock, with a central oval portrait of a statesman engraved in dense fine line work, ornate guilloche rosettes in each corner. Newer versions add pastel peach. Traditional engraving, banking authority and tradition. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
