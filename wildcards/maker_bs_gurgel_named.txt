Styled in a design language of small, toylike, rugged, home-made Brazilian utility spirit. Boxy upright shapes with flat faces, a high squared mass and big rounded arches. Round forms sit in plain recessed fields with simple slot-like openings, black edging and small steel shapes. Bare fibreglass-like surfaces, flat planes and solid, shiny gel-coated colour. Palette of bright orange, lime green, bright yellow and sky blue. Cheerful, rugged, inventive and proudly improvised, as if built in a workshop for fun. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
