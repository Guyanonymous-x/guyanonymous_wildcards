Styled in a design language of Danish thin white porcelain in soft blue-grey and white, with naturalistic Art Nouveau brushwork of seabirds, herons, stylised flowers and tall reeds in shaded cobalt. Muted pastel greys, creams, sea blues and lilac, smooth curved forms, delicate relief moulding with winter village scenes, and crackled and sculpted surfaces. Delicate, serene and quietly poetic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
