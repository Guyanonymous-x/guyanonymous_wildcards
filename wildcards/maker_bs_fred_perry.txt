Styled in a design language of neat, tidy British sporting heritage with a mod and subculture edge. Surfaces are fine knitted pique, soft cotton, crisp woven twill and clean tailored lines. Signature graphic is the twin-tipped stripe in contrasting colours, appearing as trim on edges and borders. Palette is navy, white, black, racing green, burgundy and cream, with sharp contrast pairings like yellow on black or red and white on navy. Packaging is cream and navy cartons with simple wreath marks. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
