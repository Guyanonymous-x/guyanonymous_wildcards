In the style of Lamy, modernist pens with a simple, functional design in matte black plastic and brushed stainless steel. Barrels are straight cylinders with a faceted triangular grip section, and a thin sprung wire clip extends along the cap as a single flat bar. Surfaces are matte and fine-grained, with only small dots or ink windows. Colours are black, charcoal grey, white, and later bright red, yellow and blue. The tip of the nib is silvery steel, clean and plain. Boxes are minimal. The mood is German industrial design, school-like and elegantly austere.
