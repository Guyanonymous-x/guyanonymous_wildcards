__bs_subject_vehicle__ styled in a design language of sensuous, artistic, aristocratic art deco sculpture. Flowing swept curves, teardrop and horseshoe outlines, fine parallel louvre lines and elegant tapering forms with a polished metal spine. Palette is French racing blue, black over red, deep cream, ivory and two-tone schemes. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
