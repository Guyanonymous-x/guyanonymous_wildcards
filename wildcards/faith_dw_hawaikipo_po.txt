Pō, the realm of night, opens below as a vast dark world under a starless black sky, dim gray shapes of the dead moving through it
Hine-nui-te-pō, the great woman of the night, sits at the entrance of Rarohenga, a tall woman in her fifties with long black hair and a face tattooed on the chin, welcoming the dead
Hawaiki shines on the horizon, a green island under a golden sky, the ancestors standing on its beach in feather cloaks to meet the arriving souls
