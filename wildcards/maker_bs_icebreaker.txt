Styled in a design language of close-fitting fine soft merino knit, smooth, matte and slightly fuzzy, with flat seams and mountain-inspired minimalism. Colours are muted forest green, charcoal, deep navy, heather grey, rust, moss, stone and pale blue with occasional bright orange. Graphic touches include small sheep-like or mountain-line prints, subtle stripes and tiny sewn marks. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
