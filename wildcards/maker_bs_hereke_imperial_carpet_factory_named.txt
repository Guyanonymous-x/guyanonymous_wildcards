In the style of Hereke Imperial Carpet Factory, exceptionally fine surfaces with very dense, minute detail and a glossy silk sheen. Composition is courtly and symmetrical, built around a central medallion with pointed arch shapes, tulips, carnations, curling serrated leaves and rosettes, plus touches of gold and silver shimmer. Colours are ivory, turquoise, rose, deep sapphire, emerald and ruby. Borders are narrow, multiple and perfectly even, with soft elegant curves. Imperial, lustrous, exquisite and delicate. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
