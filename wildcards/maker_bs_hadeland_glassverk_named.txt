Styled in a design language of hushed, handmade, a little irregular, clean, cold, bright and Nordic. Clear, thick, slightly bubbled mouth-blown glass in rounded organic shapes with lightly wobbly lines and uneven rims. Colours are cool and soft, pale smoky grey, aqua green, ice-blue, amber, deep cobalt and deep purple, with swirled layers and trapped air bubbles. Some pieces are cut with simple vertical grooves or concentric rings, others engraved with fir branches or snowflakes. Mid-century forms have fluid free-form silhouettes, leaf-shaped and asymmetrical. Light through the glass casts soft pools of colour, evoking frozen lakes and fjords. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
