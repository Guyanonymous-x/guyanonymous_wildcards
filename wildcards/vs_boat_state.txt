moored at a quay with ropes tied
heeled over under full sail
underway with a bow wave and white wake
at anchor in a still bay
at a mooring at dusk with the cabin lights on
being launched down a slipway
lifted out of the water on a hoist
hauled out on a cradle for repairs
surfing down a wave
stopped with the engine off and drifting
moving slowly through fog with lights on
tied to a pier with fenders out
