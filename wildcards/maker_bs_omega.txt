Styled in a design language of purposeful, elegant and exploratory with a vintage space-age touch. Round steel and gold forms with clean faceted edges, polished or brushed, under domed glass. Matte black, silver, deep ocean blue and sunburst surfaces with contrasting inset circles, baton markers and luminous infill. Warm tan leather with white stitching, tapering steel and bi-colour gold and steel details add richness. The emblem is a small Greek omega arch, repeated as a refined mark. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
