Styled in a design language of British countryside heritage built for rain, mud and long use. Waxed cotton in olive, sage, navy and dark brown with a slightly shiny, creased, weathered surface that ages with character. Details include corduroy collars, heavy metal zips, storm flaps, poacher-style pockets and quilted diamond stitching, in practical, roomy, tailored shapes. Tartan and muted tweed-like linings add quiet pattern. Timeless, sturdy and understated. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
