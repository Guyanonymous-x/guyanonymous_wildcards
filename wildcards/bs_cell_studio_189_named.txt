__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of ethical, handmade design centred on hand-dyed indigo, hand-batiked cloth, resist patterns, hand-woven fabrics and block-printed wax prints. Shapes are relaxed and modern, with wide legs, boxy tops, flowing wraps and kimono-like layers in cotton and linen. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
