In the style of Mazda, Japanese cars with a flowing sculpted body that uses soft reflections rather than hard creases, a long bonnet, a low cab set back, a large five-point grille with a fine chrome wing across it, and narrow dark headlamps with a sharp inner glare. Short smooth overhangs, a slim chrome line along the window edge, and rounded tail lamps with a single ring. Dark alloy wheels with a machined face. Interior with a thin horizontal dash, matte metal, red or white leather and a freestanding screen. Signature colours of deep crystal red metallic, machine grey and soul-red tints. Elegant, sensual and clean.
