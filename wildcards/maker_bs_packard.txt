In the style of Packard, 1930s and 40s American luxury cars with a tall narrow ox-yoke grille shaped like a pointed shield with fine vertical bars, a very long hood, sweeping separate fenders and chrome-ringed headlamps on the fender tops. Chrome flying mascot or cormorant on the radiator, wire or disc wheels with whitewall tyres, running boards, a long wheelbase and a formal upright cabin. Deep gloss paint in black, maroon, dark green and cream with a thin coach line. Interiors of walnut veneer, mohair or leather and elegant engine-turned metal. Stately, hushed, old-money elegance.
