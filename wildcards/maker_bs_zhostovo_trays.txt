Styled in a design language of a glossy black lacquer ground, sometimes red, blue, green or white, carrying a lush bouquet of large garden and wild flowers loosely tossed with leaves, buds and fruit. Painting is quick and full-brushed with soft shaded modelling, in oil colours of deep crimson, pink, orange, yellow, blue and fresh green against the dark. A smaller scatter of blossoms and berries surrounds the main bunch. Edges are trimmed with gold or silver scrolls, dots and little leaf ornaments. The finish is polished to a mirror shine under many layers of lacquer. Lush, sumptuous, festive and warm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
