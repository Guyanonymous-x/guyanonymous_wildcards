the altar a waist-high block of fitted pale limestone with a blackened iron grate on top, a shallow stone-lined trench as long as six men lying end to end running from it down the yard
the fire pits, six round shafts lined with fire-blackened stone, each wide enough for a man to stand in with arms out and deeper than a man is tall, their rims level with the floor
stacks of olive wood and brush piled against the wall for the next feast, long peeled wooden spits as tall as two men leaning in a bundle, and iron lattice covers for the pits
a raised stone block near the altar where the high priest stands, rows of metal hooks along one wall for hanging the sheep, and water taps with hoses along the trench
