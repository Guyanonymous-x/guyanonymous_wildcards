Styled in a design language of youthful swinging-sixties mod design. Materials are shiny PVC, patent, crisp cotton, jersey and opaque coloured knit with white contrast edges. Lettering is a slim, tall, tightly spaced sans-serif in capitals, stacked in two lines. Shapes are short, tidy, simple and cut with sharp precision for a playful, modern energy. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
