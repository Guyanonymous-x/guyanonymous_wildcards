Styled in a design language of precise, slim, early Art Deco and quietly luxurious. Slim cylindrical forms in glossy black, mottled red-and-black, pale marbled tones, dark blue or olive, with gold-plated or nickel bands, tapered ends and long clean lines finished with a small ball tip. Metal parts have fine ribbed or knurled grips, silver or gold finish and geometric engraved stripes. Cases are lined with velvet in dark green or burgundy and gold-stamped. Everything has a hand-finished glow, smooth seams, tiny engraved lines and a small stamped maker mark, giving a jewel-like object feel in muted period colours. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
