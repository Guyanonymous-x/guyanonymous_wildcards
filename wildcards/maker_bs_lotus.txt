Styled in a design language of lightweight, stripped-back and purposeful British racing minimalism. Thin moulded shells in low wedge or rounded teardrop outlines, with bare edges, wide swelling curves and nothing added that is not needed. Racing green is paired with a single bright yellow band, alongside black with gold pinstriping, white, orange and metallic silver. Surfaces are glossy fibreglass-like sheen, exposed aluminium and thin carbon weave. Colour blocking is simple, sharp and light in spirit. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
