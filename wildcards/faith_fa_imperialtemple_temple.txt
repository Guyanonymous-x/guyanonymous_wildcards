the temple at Nîmes in southern France, now called the Maison Carrée, a perfect small temple of pale honey limestone, six fluted columns across its porch and more set half into its side walls, fifteen steps up the front, and across the band above the columns a line of gilded bronze capitals reading "C CAESARI AVGVSTI F COS L CAESARI AVGVSTI F COS DESIGNATO PRINCIPIBVS IVVENTVTIS"
the Temple of Rome and Augustus at Pula on the Adriatic coast of Croatia, a slim temple of white limestone at the edge of the city square, four smooth columns across its porch with leafy capitals, a carved band of curling vines under the roof, and above the columns bronze capitals reading "ROMAE ET AVGVSTO CAESARI DIVI F PATRI PATRIAE"
the Temple of Antoninus and Faustina beside the main square of Rome itself, ten huge columns of green-veined gray marble on a tall platform of dark stone blocks, a band of carved griffins and candlesticks along its sides, and across the front two lines of deep-cut capitals reading "DIVO ANTONINO ET DIVAE FAVSTINAE EX S C"
the Temple of Rome and Augustus at Ankara in central Turkey, a tall white marble temple ringed by columns on a hill in the city, its porch walls carved from top to bottom with the emperor's own record of his deeds in column after column of small neat capitals under a heading reading "RERVM GESTARVM DIVI AVGVSTI", the cuts filled with red paint
the temple of the emperors at Aphrodisias in southwestern Turkey, standing on a high platform at the end of a long narrow paved court between two facing buildings three stories tall, their fronts set with marble relief panels, one showing the emperor Claudius as a naked warrior, a helmet on his head, a short cloak flying behind him and a sword belt slung across his chest, the genitals carved small, standing over a slumped woman who stands for Britain, her tunic slipping to bare one breast and her right hand raised, his arm drawn back to strike her
the temple of the emperor Trajan on the summit of the steep citadel hill of Pergamon in western Turkey, gleaming white marble with six columns across the front, standing in the middle of a wide terrace held up on tall arched vaults and framed on three sides by colonnades, the plain and the river far below
