In the style of Muji, plain, unbranded, no-logo design in a quiet, anonymous aesthetic. Natural, undyed materials such as unbleached cotton, linen, paper, recycled cardboard, raw pale wood, rubber, brushed aluminium and clear frosted plastic keep their honest look. Colours stay in beige, oatmeal, off-white, light grey, charcoal, navy and muted black, with matte uncoloured finishes. Forms are minimal and rounded-rectangular, with soft edges, a single purpose and no decoration. Tiny simple sans-serif lettering in black sits on kraft brown or white. Calm, empty, modest and serene, with soft daylight and generous empty space.
