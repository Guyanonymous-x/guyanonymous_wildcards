the universe rises in layers, the seven hells dark at the bottom, the middle world with its ring of continents and oceans, then the bright tiers of heavens stacked higher and higher
at the very top of the universe the Siddhashila, a vast thin crescent of white crystal light, curves in a wide shallow dome above all the heavens
the freed soul, a figure of clear light in the shape of a man in his fifties, shoots straight up from the earth through every heaven in one moment to the crescent at the top
