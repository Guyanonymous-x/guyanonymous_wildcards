In the style of Namco, golden age arcade games and cabinets of the early 1980s. Characters are simple round shapes with big expressive eyes, drawn in a chunky low resolution pixel grid on a pure black background. A yellow circle with a wedge mouth chases glowing dots through a neon blue outlined maze of rounded corridors, pursued by pastel ghosts in red, pink, cyan and orange with wavy skirts and blinking white eyes. Fruits such as cherries and strawberries appear as bonus items in flat bright pixel art. Space shooters show symmetrical insect aliens in blue, purple and yellow diving in formation against a star field of single twinkling dots. Cabinets are upright wooden boxes with side art of cartoon characters, a slanted black marquee with a rounded glowing logo, a tilted CRT with scanlines, a joystick with a ball top and a few round buttons. Mood is cheerful, bouncy, bright and friendly, with limited saturated colours, hard pixel edges and bleeps in the imagination.
