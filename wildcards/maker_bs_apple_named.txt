In the style of Apple, pared-back, precise and quietly luxurious. Single continuous forms with perfectly rounded corners, razor-thin edges, seamless joins and no visible fasteners. Materials are brushed aluminium, polished glass and soft-touch matte finishes in silver, space grey, white, black and a few muted pastel tints, with colour mainly coming from the screen or content. Lettering is clean, light to medium sans-serif with generous spacing. Everything is reduced to the essential, with ample negative space. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Apple in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
