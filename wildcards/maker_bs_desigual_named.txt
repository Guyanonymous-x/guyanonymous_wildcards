In the style of Desigual, an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Patchwork panels cut from different floral, geometric and animal patterns, bright embroidery, appliques, studs, sequins, lace inserts and ragged edges, mixing several patterns together, with hearts, butterflies, flowers, stripes, ethnic motifs, mosaic tiles, polka dots, graffiti and cartoon-like faces. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas, with a big stylised swirling logo. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast.
