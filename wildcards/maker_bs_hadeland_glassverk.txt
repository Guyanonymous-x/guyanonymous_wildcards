In the style of Hadeland Glassverk, Norwegian hand-blown glass. The glassware is clear, thick and slightly bubbled, mouth-blown into rounded organic shapes such as heavy tumblers, wide bowls, tall stemmed vases, candle lights and pitchers, with lightly wobbly lines. Colours are cool and soft, such as pale smoky grey, aqua green, ice-blue, amber, deep cobalt and deep purple, with swirled layers, trapped air bubbles and uneven rims. Some pieces are cut with simple vertical grooves or concentric rings, others engraved with fir branches or snowflakes. Mid-century pieces have fluid free-form silhouettes, such as leaf-shaped bowls and asymmetrical vases. The mood is clean, cold, bright and Nordic, evoking frozen lakes and fjords. Lighting through the glass creates soft pools of colour on tabletops, and the overall impression is hushed, handmade, a little irregular and unmistakably Scandinavian.
