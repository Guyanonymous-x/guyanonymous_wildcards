Styled in a design language of cheerful, friendly, confident Dutch design in a single bright sky blue against clean white. The signature is flat colour blocking with a large sweep of cornflower or azure, sometimes broken by thin horizontal pinstripes in two blues. Finishes are glossy enamel and clean matte paint with crisp edges. Palette stays disciplined with sky blue, white, navy and a rare touch of orange. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
