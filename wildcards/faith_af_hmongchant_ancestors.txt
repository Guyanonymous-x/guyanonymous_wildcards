at the end of the road waits the village of origin, a cluster of wooden houses with low thatched roofs on a misty mountain in old China, the ancestors in their sixties to nineties in indigo and embroidered dress coming out to greet the newcomer
the first ancestors sit in a ring around a fire in the village of the dead, men and women in their seventies to nineties in silver neck rings and turbans, a place set for the new soul among them
after resting with the ancestors the soul is shown as a small light leaving the village, carried down on the wind toward the world of the living to be born again
