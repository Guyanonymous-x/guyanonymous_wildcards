In the style of Sarao Motors, handbuilt Philippine jeepneys. A jeepney is a long, boxy minibus on an army-surplus-style truck chassis, with a hand-beaten sheet metal body, a long bonnet in front, two open-backed passenger benches facing each other, a rear step and a canopy roof. The body is covered in loud colours and hand painted patterns, such as bright stripes, checks, flames, flowers and animals, outlined in chrome strips, with an abundance of chrome decoration such as grille trim, ornaments, small horses or metal figures along the bonnet, lights, mirrors, tassels and several side mirrors. Sides carry route names and slogans in bold painted lettering, and religious pictures or family names appear above the driver. Interiors have polished metal rails, velvet or vinyl benches, bright fringes, mirrors and a loud sound system. Mood is exuberant, crowded, festive, noisy, handmade and joyful, in saturated colours and heaps of chrome shining in the sun.
