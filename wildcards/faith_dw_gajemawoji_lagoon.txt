the Sakumo lagoon lies still and silver behind the dunes at Tema, mangroves and reeds along its edge, wading birds standing in its shallows and a small white shrine on its bank
Korle, the goddess of the Korle lagoon of Accra, appears in its calm water as a woman in her fifties with dark brown skin and short gray hair, in a white cloth, her reflection shining on the lagoon
the lagoons of the coast lie between the sea and the land as broad mirrors of still water, each with its shrine of white-painted wood on its bank and a white flag on a pole
