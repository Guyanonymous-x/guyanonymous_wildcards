initiates in their twenties to their sixties in white robes and crowns of myrtle walk the Sacred Way from Athens to Eleusis, carrying torches, sticks bound with myrtle and small bundles
initiates stand in the dark hall of the Telesterion among rows of stone columns, the high priest in his sixties in a long purple robe holding up a single ear of wheat in a blaze of torchlight
at the sea the initiates in their twenties to their forties wash piglets in the waves and sacrifice them, a man in his thirties holding a pale piglet by its hind legs, its throat cut and its blood running into a pit
