In the style of Issey Miyake, sculptural, weightless, inventive design that treats cloth as architecture. Fine, permanent, heat-set pleats run in crisp vertical, horizontal or zigzag rows, springing back into shape and catching light in rippling waves. Geometric forms such as tubes, cubes, circles and folded origami-like volumes expand and collapse flexibly. Textiles are lightweight polyester, crinkled and bonded technical weaves, sheer layers and stretchy jersey, shown matte and slightly shimmering. Palette alternates between muted charcoal, ivory, black and indigo and sudden bursts of neon lime, tangerine, cobalt, magenta and bright turquoise, with playful colour-blocking and bold abstract graphics. Lettering is plain, small, modern sans-serif. Clever, light, joyful, modern and wonderfully free. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Issey Miyake in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
