Styled in a design language of vivid, nostalgic, bold medicinal design. Tiny faceted hexagonal or octagonal forms in bright red or white, tightly wrapped in red or white printed paper, with small steel tins painted red and gold. The hero is a striding side-view big cat with a curved tail, drawn in an orange and black woodcut manner. Old pharmacy feel, pungent and strong. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
