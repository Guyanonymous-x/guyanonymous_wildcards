Styled in a design language of trusted, family-run, festive and old-fashioned heritage design with dense decoration. Clear glass with a short neck, red accents and a wide wraparound surface in rich red with gold border lines. A circular central picture of a woman and a boy on a small flat boat on a river, drawn in a simplified storybook engraving with fine black outline and flat gold, green and cream fills. A gold ribbon banner and a small seal stamp repeat across the range. Glossy dark brown tones, food shots with wok flames and shiny glazed meats. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
