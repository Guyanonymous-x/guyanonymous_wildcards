In the style of British Airways, a white fuselage with a navy-blue lower belly and a bold red-and-blue swoosh-like band, and a tail painted in a billowing flag of red, white and blue stripes in a rippling shape. Lettering in bold navy capitals on the nose side with a hint of grey. Interiors in dove grey and navy with red stitching, tartan or patterned seat fabric. Earlier designs used a speedwing line of red and blue wedges. Palette of navy, red, white and silver. The look is flag-waving, formal, traditional, with a rippling tail, strong colours and clean sans lettering.
