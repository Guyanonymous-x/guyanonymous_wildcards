Rajasthani kathputli string puppets from India, carved wooden figures each about as tall as an adult's forearm. Each puppet has a single carved block of mango wood for the head and body, painted with a bright face, big almond-shaped eyes outlined in thick black, arched brows, a long nose and a curled black mustache on the men. The women wear a long veil over the head, and the men wear tall turbans of red and yellow cloth. The figures end at the waist in long full skirts of bright cotton, red, orange and green, covered in small round mirrors and gold braid and swirl out when the puppet spins. A single loop of string runs from the head over the puppeteer's fingers to the back of the waist, and the thin stuffed cloth arms hang loose. __puppet_kathputli_var__.
