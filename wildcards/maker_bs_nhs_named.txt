Styled in a design language of British public health service design in a clean mid blue and white, pale blue scrubs and navy uniforms, white walls with blue handrails and wayfinding stripes, yellow-and-green high-visibility chequered ambulance panels, plain grey vinyl floors, simple modern furniture and a practical, calm, caring public-service character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
