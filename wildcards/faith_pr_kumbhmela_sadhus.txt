at the front run the naga sadhus, naked adult holy men in their twenties to seventies with their skin smeared gray-white with ash from head to foot, each man's penis and scrotum visible, small and relaxed, their long matted hair piled on their heads or flying loose, garlands of orange marigolds at their necks, iron tridents and small drums raised high
at the front ride the leaders of the akharas, holy men in their fifties to seventies in robes of saffron orange silk with marigold garlands and long gray beards, seated on silver chariots and high wooden thrones carried by followers, gold-fringed umbrellas held over their heads
at the front walk bairagi sadhus of the Vaishnava orders, lean men in their thirties to seventies in white cloth wraps, tall U-shaped marks of white clay with a red line on their foreheads, and sadhvis, women ascetics in their forties to sixties in saffron robes with shaved heads
