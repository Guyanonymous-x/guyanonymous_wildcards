Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy-duty, utilitarian machinery. Big rounded, bulbous masses with deep glazing, stubby add-on volumes and a long tapering tail. Riveted aluminium skin with visible fasteners and panel seams. Colour is either matte olive drab and grey, or white and red for rescue. No ornament, everything functional, strong, workmanlike and built to lift and endure. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
