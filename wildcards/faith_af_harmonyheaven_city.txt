the New Jerusalem stands on a high mountain, a square city of gold as clear as glass, its walls of jasper set with twelve layers of colored gemstones, twelve gates each made of a single great pearl, an angel in his thirties standing at every gate
through the golden street of the city runs the river of the water of life, clear as crystal, and on both banks stands the tree of life bearing twelve kinds of fruit, its leaves glowing green
below the heavenly city lies a Harmonist town drawn in the plain grid of Economy, rows of red brick houses, a church with a clock tower, a vineyard on the hill and a hedge labyrinth, the heaven above it reached by a road of light
