Styled in a design language of stop-motion craft with intricately carved faces, delicate printed expressions, stylised angular or spindly bodies and textured knitted and sewn costumes. Atmospheric miniature sets with moody lighting, moss, mist and dark woodland, a slightly eerie and gothic palette of teal, dusty purple, ochre and charcoal with punches of bright colour. Tactile handmade detail down to individual hair, wood grain and fabric weave, with a dreamy fairy-tale spookiness. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
