In the style of Hereke Imperial Carpet Factory, exceptionally fine silk and wool carpets with a very high knot density, glossy silk pile and sharp, minute detail. Designs show central medallions, prayer arches, Ottoman tulips, carnations, hyacinths, curling saz leaves and rosettes in a courtly arrangement, with metal thread in gold and silver for added shimmer. Colours are ivory, turquoise, rose, deep sapphire, emerald and ruby. Borders are narrow and multiple, with perfectly even geometry and soft elegant curves. The mood is imperial, lustrous, exquisite and delicate.
