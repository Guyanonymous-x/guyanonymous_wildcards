Styled in a design language of nostalgic mid-century British model railway look in tinplate and die-cast metal. Glossy dark green, maroon, black, cream and crimson paint with fine gold lining, polished brass accents, tiny rivets and slim railings, and tin buildings with printed brickwork. Graphics are blue or red with a ruled border and a painted illustration of speed over green countryside, with old-fashioned serif and block capitals. Warm catalogue charm.
