Styled in a design language of cheerful, familiar and snapshot-friendly. Early forms are leather-covered boxes with a round lens-like feature and a simple window. Mid-century pieces have art deco ribs and chrome stripes, then moulded plastic in the 1970s and 1980s. Bright yellow surfaces with red panels. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
