Styled in a design language of bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A saturated red and sunny golden yellow pair carries everything, in broad flat fields with crisp white as breathing room. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds. Lettering is soft, chunky, rounded sans-serif in white or yellow. Photography is bright, overhead, evenly lit and cheerful, with hands, steam and tidy, repeated arrangements.
