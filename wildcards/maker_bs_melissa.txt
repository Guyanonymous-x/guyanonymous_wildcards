In the style of Melissa, plastic PVC shoes made from a glossy jelly-like material, with flat sandals, ballet flats, platform sandals, boots and heels moulded in one piece with sculptural bows, flowers, buckles and cutouts. The surface is shiny, translucent or glittery with a faint sweet-candy look. Colours are jelly pink, lilac, mint, clear transparent, baby blue, yellow, red, black and metallic glitter, sometimes scented. Shapes are playful and rounded, with chunky soles, bubble-like toes, ribbed textures and puffy edges. Fun, youthful, pop-art toy mood, shown on pastel studio backdrops with glossy reflections.
