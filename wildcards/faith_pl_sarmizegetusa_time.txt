in summer, the forest deep green around the terrace, the beech crowns heavy and dark with full leaf, bright sun on the open lawn making the gray stones pale and warm, sharp leaf shadows along the edges of the clearing and a blue sky with small white clouds
in fall, the beeches gold and red on the slopes, every hillside a blaze of yellow, copper and russet under a clear pale sky, bright leaves drifting down onto the gray stone circles and lying thick on the paths, low sunlight slanting through thinning branches
on a misty morning, cloud drifting among the stones, soft white vapor sliding across the terrace so the wooden posts and stone pillars appear and fade, the beech trunks around the clearing reduced to pale gray shapes and the grass silver with dew
