Styled in a design language of English fine bone china in the Imari manner, dense patterns of deep cobalt blue, rich iron red and burnished gold on a brilliant white ground, panels of stylised flowers, leaves and fans framed by gold borders, gold-encrusted rims, plump animal-shaped paperweights covered in the same patterns, and an opulent, collectable, country-house character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
