In the style of Spyker in the 2000s, a Dutch hand-built supercar that mixes aircraft cues with luxury, featuring a nose shaped like a round propeller hub with a circular grille that has spoke-like bars, a bulging body with exposed round headlamps, and a compact cabin. Rear with wide flared wings, a round exhaust, and a spare wheel emblem. Wheels are polished aluminium with many spokes like a turbine. Paint in bright orange, lime green, black, silver, and pearl white. Interior has diamond-quilted leather in contrasting colours, exposed machined aluminium with turned finish, big round dials with white faces, a drilled wheel, and a sense of an aircraft cockpit. Mood is bold, eccentric and ornate.
