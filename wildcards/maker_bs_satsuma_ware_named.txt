In the style of Satsuma ware, jewel-like, glowing surfaces of cream-ivory glaze with a fine network of crackle. Every area is covered in miniature, densely painted scenes with tiny figures, gardens, mountains and blossoms in vivid enamels of red, green and blue, enriched with lavish raised gold and bands of brocade pattern at borders. Elaborate sculpted and pierced details. Highly detailed, warm, glowing, precious and ornate, like an export-era cabinet treasure.
