In the style of Laduree, dainty, sugary Second Empire Parisian elegance. Pastel colours in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow, powder blue and coffee brown are laid out in soft ombre blocks. Rigid pale mint or pink surfaces carry a gold embossed oval emblem ringed with a thin laurel-like wreath and a flowing gold script wordmark. Fine gilt edges, satin ribbon, tissue wrapping and delicate arabesque trellis and floral lattice patterns in cream and gold give a fine, porcelain-like finish. Small cherub and garland motifs, scalloped borders, matte velvety coatings and soft pearly sheen keep the mood feminine and polished. Sweet, precious, ladylike and gently nostalgic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Laduree in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
