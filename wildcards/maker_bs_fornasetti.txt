In the style of Fornasetti, Italian decorative objects and furniture covered in printed black and white engravings on glossy lacquer, with surreal repeating motifs such as a woman face with half-closed eyes, sun faces, playing cards, butterflies, keys, hands, architectural arches, and classical ruins. Plates, umbrella stands, folding screens, cabinets and trays with gilded edges, deep black, bold red, cream and gold accents. Dense graphic linework, trompe-l oeil, witty and theatrical, cultured Milanese interior, ornamental and mysterious.
