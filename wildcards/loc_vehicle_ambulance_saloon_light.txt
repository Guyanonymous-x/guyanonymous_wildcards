Two conditions and the difference between them is the record, the working condition, with bright even cool LED panels in the ceiling, deliberately shadowless, chosen so that a vein, a pupil and the color of a face read correctly, and the one lighting specification in this document whose whole purpose is to let somebody judge a skin tone; and the running condition, where the saloon drops to a low blue or amber, because a bright compartment at night blinds the driver through the bulkhead window and exhausts a patient who is lying face-up directly beneath the panels, so the patient's view is the ceiling, lit or unlit, for the whole journey, which is a detail worth having
Daylight through tinted or frosted side and rear windows, deliberately obscured for privacy, so the compartment never has a view out at seated height
A hand torch used constantly despite all of the above; and the blue of the vehicle's own beacons pulsing across the rear window and the ceiling from outside, which is the only moving light in the room
