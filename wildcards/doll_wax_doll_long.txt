a nineteenth-century wax doll, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her head and shoulders are poured wax, a soft glowing translucent cream with a faint blush, and her eyes are glass, deep blue. Strands of real hair are set one by one into the wax of her scalp, so her pale blond hair grows out of it in a natural line at the forehead. The wax has softened a little at the nose and chin over the years, and fine crazing lines cross one cheek. Her body is soft stuffed cloth with wax lower arms and legs. She wears a christening-style long white gown of fine cotton lawn with rows of tucks and lace. __doll_wax_doll_var__.
