In the style of Kelmscott Press, dense Gothic-revival books printed in heavy black blackletter type on thick handmade cream paper, with red ink for headings and marginal notes. Each page is framed by luxuriant woodcut borders of winding acanthus, vines, flowers, leaves and fruit in white on black, interlaced thickly. Large decorative initials in foliage set against dark ground open each chapter. Text blocks are tightly justified, with minimal leading and wide margins, giving a dark and ornate texture. Bindings use limp vellum with silk ties or half holland with boards. The mood is medieval, rich and romantic.
