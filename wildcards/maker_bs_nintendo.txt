In the style of Nintendo, playful Japanese game hardware. Compact rounded rectangles with chunky friendly proportions, glossy and matte plastic in white, light grey, red, black and bright primary or pastel colours. Controllers have a cross-shaped directional pad, small round coloured face buttons in red, blue, yellow or green, and short slim start keys, all set in tidy symmetrical layouts. The 1980s look has a cream-grey case with dark red trim and gold-lettered labels, boxy cartridges with ridged grips and rectangular stickers. Later devices use clean white or black slab shells, thin colourful detachable controllers, and rounded sans-serif logo lettering in red. Mood is cheerful, approachable and toy-like but neatly built.
