Shot through an enhanced 665nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a soft slate-blue sky over wheat, straw and champagne-gold foliage, with darker olive trees. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
