In the style of Yohji Yamamoto, voluminous draped black design cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette becomes a dark, shadowy cloud. Materials are matte wool gabardine, crinkled cotton, heavy linen, felted wool and fluid jersey, with raw edges, oversized folds, tilted asymmetric edges, trailing hems and long scarf-like panels. Palette is overwhelmingly black, then charcoal, deep navy, off-white and rare blood red, sometimes with bold brush-stroke calligraphy marks, flowers or white graphic drawing scrawled across the dark surface. Tailoring is slouchy, androgynous, deliberately undone and poetic. Lettering is delicate, hand-drawn, slender handwriting-like script in white on black. Austere, moody, romantic and quietly rebellious.. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Yohji Yamamoto in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
