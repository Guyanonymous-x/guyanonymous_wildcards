In the style of Wagamama, long communal wooden benches and tables in a bright minimal hall with white walls, black lacquer bowls, red-trimmed signage and a red square stamp-like logo with a simple black character. Menus on a thin paper in black and red, hand-written style, orders taken on a handheld device. Steaming ramen served in large bowls with chopsticks and a spoon. Palette of black, red, white and pale wood. The look is austere, canteen-like, clean and fast, with Japanese-influenced minimalism and a cheerful bustle.
