In the style of Bonwire kente weaving houses of Ghana, hand-loomed narrow strips of silk and cotton, about four inches wide, woven on a horizontal strip loom and sewn edge to edge into large cloths worn draped over the shoulder. Brilliant geometric patterns of interlocking bands, squares, zigzags, stepped diamonds and blocks in gold, kente yellow, deep red, emerald green, royal blue, black and white. Weft-faced areas alternate with warp-striped plain sections, and supplementary weft motifs create tiny symbols such as ladders and checks. Surfaces are rich, thick, slightly ridged and rhythmic, each strip a different arrangement. Regal, ceremonial, celebratory mood, sunlit courtyard setting with looms and coloured threads.
