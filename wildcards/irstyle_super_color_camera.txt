Shot through a super color 590nm infrared filter and shown straight from the camera, the image shows a salmon-peach sky over saturated sky-blue and cyan foliage, with deep teal trees. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
