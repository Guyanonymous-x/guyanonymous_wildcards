Styled in a design language of classic large, rounded rectangular bodies, a warm golden spruce tone that ages to amber, and rosewood or mahogany sides. Bindings are cream or ivoroid, and dark herringbone purfling rings the top. Openings are circled with fine concentric rings, and protective plates are tortoiseshell. Dark ebony surfaces carry simple pearl dots, or snowflake inlays on premium versions. Ends are squared with an inlaid pearl logo. The finish is satin or gloss. The mood is traditional, folksy and rootsy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
