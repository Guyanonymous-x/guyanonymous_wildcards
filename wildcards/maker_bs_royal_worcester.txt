In the style of Royal Worcester, English porcelain with hand-painted naturalistic subjects such as fruit, fallen blossoms, game birds, highland cattle and landscapes on ivory ground, framed by raised gilding, jewelled enamel dots and pierced reticulated edges. Rich cream and blush ivory glaze, gold scrolling, oriental-inspired shapes, shell-shaped dishes, ornamental ewers and dessert services, finely detailed Victorian elegance, and pot-pourri vases with delicate floral sprays and handles of twisting branches.
