Strong, shadowless daylight from a roof light down onto the elm table in the center of the room
High side windows adding a cross light
The open roasting fire giving a strong low orange light along the floor and up the canopy, which at four o'clock on a December afternoon is the room's dominant light and throws long shadows of standing people across the brick
The charcoal stove's six rings glowing at waist height
160 copper pans catching every one of these sources and returning them, which is the room's whole visual character; and at night two or three lamps, then gas on brackets at the dresser and the range, which is one of the earliest places in the house to get both gas and electricity because the light was needed
