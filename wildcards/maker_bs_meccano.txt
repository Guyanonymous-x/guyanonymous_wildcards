In the style of Meccano, perforated flat metal strips, angle girders, plates and wheels with evenly spaced round holes, finished in red and green enamel or later zinc and brass, bolted together with tiny hex nuts and bolts into cranes, bridges and engines. Boxes show a boy-friendly illustrated scene of a huge model at work in muted red, green and cream, ornate Edwardian serif lettering with banner panels, brass gears, flanged wheels, handles and cranks, an engineering manual look with line drawings and numbered parts.
