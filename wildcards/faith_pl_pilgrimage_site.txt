the cathedral of Santiago de Compostela in Spain, a great church of weathered gray-gold granite with two tall ornate towers and a soaring front covered from base to top in carved stone columns, statues and scrolls, rising before a vast open plaza of stone paving
the grotto of Lourdes in France, a shallow dark cave in a gray rock cliff with a white statue of the Virgin Mary in a blue sash standing in a niche above banks of burning candles, below a tall white church with a slender spire built on top of the rock
the shrine of Fátima in Portugal, a huge open square of pale paving before a white stone church with a tall slim tower topped by a bronze crown and long curving colonnades, and a small plain chapel with a modern glass shelter where the Virgin is said to have appeared
a monastery of Mount Athos in Greece, a tall fortress of gray stone walls rising straight from a rocky cliff above the blue sea, with rows of wooden balconies painted dark red hanging from its upper floors and small domes with crosses above
Canterbury Cathedral in England, a vast cathedral of pale gray stone with a tall square central tower crowned with pinnacles, long rows of tall pointed windows filled with stained glass and flying buttresses, arched stone supports leaning against its walls
the old church of Saint Mary of Zion at Aksum in Ethiopia, a square church of rough gray-brown stone with a notched parapet standing on a high stepped platform, near tall carved granite stelae, upright stone slabs carved as many-storied towers with false doors and windows
