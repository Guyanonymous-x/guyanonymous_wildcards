Styled after a showy, sculpted, long-nosed American heavy-duty look. Long, curved forms with bold oval chrome shapes, vertical slatted detailing, rounded corners and single round chrome-ringed lights. Tall chrome columns, big chrome bars and polished metal covers. Brilliant candy paint in black, red, blue or green is trimmed with fine pinstripes and gold lines. Classic, bold, polished and proud, with a strong stance and lots of mirror-bright metal. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
