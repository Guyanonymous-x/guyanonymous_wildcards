Styled in a design language of earthy, handmade, humble and warm rustic terracotta. Heavy, thick-walled forms, unglazed or lightly burnished, in warm reddish-brown to dark grey-brown clay with a smooth matte surface that darkens with use. Shapes are chunky and round, with two ear handles, wide shallow curves and plump volumes. Plump little animal figures and small pinched and moulded folk figures add charm. Some are painted with simple flat colours or white slip dots and stripes, others left plain with finger marks and uneven rims. Kiln smoke gives mottled black clouds and variations. Rough wooden shelves in an adobe market. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
