In the style of IKEA, democratic Scandinavian practicality, bright and friendly. Simple, light, flat-planar shapes, modular rectangles with soft corners, visible joints and clever space-saving logic. Pale birch, pine and white laminate are paired with cheerful saturated blue and yellow plus red, green and soft pastels, in bold, playful patterns and plain colour blocks. Materials feel affordable but honest, such as light wood, painted board, woven textiles and moulded plastic. Lettering is bold, rounded, plain sans-serif in capitals. Photography is airy, sunlit, tidy yet lived-in, with plants, rugs and everyday cosy scenes.
