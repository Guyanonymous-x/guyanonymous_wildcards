Glossy lacquer, polished brass details and slight enamel crazing give honest age. Sleek streamlined emblem graphics with speed lines and a rounded wing shape. Orderly, charming, mid-century catalogue style with a cheerful, collectable feel. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
