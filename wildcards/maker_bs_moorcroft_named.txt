In the style of Moorcroft, English art pottery feel with hand-painted designs outlined in raised lines like cloisonne. Lush stylised plants, flowers, fruit and toadstools in Art Nouveau rhythm fill the surface with flowing curves. Deep jewel-toned glazes of royal blue, forest green, burgundy, plum and gold, with rich flambe runs and a smooth, glossy, velvety depth. Rounded, generous shapes, luminous colour and a precious, collectable Edwardian handmade feel.
