Styled in a design language of sturdy, warm American chocolate-brown design. Deep glossy chocolate-brown fields carry bold white or silver condensed slab-like capitals in a straight horizontal wordmark, underlined by a thin curved swoosh. Flat colour accents in red, gold, orange, purple or blue distinguish variants against the dark brown base. Surfaces include silver metallic foil, satin paper wrap, raised square grid embossing and rounded rectangle panels. A teardrop silhouette with a small flat base and rounded peak recurs as an emblem. Patterns use repeated tiny wordmarks and bold diagonal ribbons. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
