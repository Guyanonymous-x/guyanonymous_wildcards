__bs_subject_vehicle__ styled in a design language of hyper-sleek, exotic, clean and ultra-technical Swedish engineering. A low wide body, narrow slit shapes, panels that swing up and out and exposed bare carbon-fibre weave under clear lacquer. Ice blue, gloss white, orange and gunmetal. A ghost-like shield mark. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
