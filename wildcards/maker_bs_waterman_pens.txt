Styled in a design language of elegant slim, rounded forms in black hard rubber, red ripple pattern or sterling silver filigree overlay. Ripple designs swirl in mottled red and black, and metal overlays show Art Deco geometric grooves, basketweave and lacy filigree. Details include thin gold-filled bands, gently arched lines and fine ornament on metal accents. Colours are warm, rich and aged, with an orange-red sheen. Velvet lining adds softness. The mood is refined, Edwardian and Art Deco. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
