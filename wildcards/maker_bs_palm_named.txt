Styled in a design language of compact handheld organisers in slim, rounded cases of silver or graphite plastic with a small grey screen, a row of square silver buttons beneath it, a plastic stylus tucked into the side and a snap-on cover. Later models with colour screens and metallic purple, blue and green finishes. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
