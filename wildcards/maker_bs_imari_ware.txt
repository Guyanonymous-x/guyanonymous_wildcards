In the style of Imari ware, Japanese porcelain richly decorated with a dense palette of underglaze cobalt blue, iron-red overglaze and gold, with panelled chrysanthemums, peonies, phoenixes, brocade patterns, fans and waves on large plates, bowls, jars and lidded urns. Sections of deep blue alternate with fiery red and gilded floral scrolls, leaving little blank white, flamboyant, ornate, sumptuous like silk brocade, with scalloped rims and lobed bodies.
