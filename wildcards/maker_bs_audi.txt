Styled in a design language of clean, precise, technical and understated. Taut, flush surfaces with razor-sharp creases, perfect panel alignment and a flat machined look. Four interlocking rings in simple chrome or flat black serve as the signature mark. Palette of glacier white, graphite grey, black and silver with sudden accents of tango red or vivid blue. Materials are brushed aluminium, matte black glass, fine perforated leather and carbon weave. Light is treated as a design element, with thin sharp bars and crisp digital pixel glows. Lettering is a clean, wide, geometric sans-serif in strict grids with generous white space. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
