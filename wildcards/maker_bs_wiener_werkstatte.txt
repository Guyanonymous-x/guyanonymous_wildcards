Styled in a design language of viennese Secession craft with geometric abstraction, strict grids of black and white squares, concentric circles, stylised roses, wavy lines and bold block-printed patterns in orange, coral, turquoise, black and gold. Pierced squares in metal, ebonised wood, enamel, elongated forms with grid-patterned surfaces, chequered bands and stylised flowers. Sophisticated, rhythmic, ornamental modernism with precise linear elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
