In the style of Danar Hadi, Solo Javanese royal-court batik, hand-drawn and stamped cotton and silk in elaborate classical patterns, with diagonal parang blades, kawung circle grids, sidomukti, truntum star flowers and lotus motifs filling the cloth evenly in fine linework. Colours are dignified earth tones of sogan brown, midnight indigo, cream white and soft black, with occasional touches of gold paint. Garments are long wrap skirts, formal long-sleeved shirts, kebaya tops and shawls with a hand-finished, slightly crackled wax texture. Dignified, traditional, courtly mood, shown on carved wooden stands in dim museum-like light.
