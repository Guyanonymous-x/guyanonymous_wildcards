In the style of Bonds, Australian basic underwear and kids clothing. The core product is a plain cotton singlet or tank top with a deep scoop neck, ribbed knit, and a small woven label on the hem, in white, navy, grey, pale blue and cream. The famous baby wondersuit is a one-piece zip-front romper with small cuffs and stretch cotton jersey, printed with simple cheerful motifs, stripes, polka dots, small animals, clouds, stars and kangaroo-like characters in soft pastel yellow, mint, sky blue and pink. The logo is a simple lowercase or capital block sans-serif in a flat colour, sometimes a small blue box with white letters. Packaging is clean and bright, with plastic-wrapped packs in white and bright blue and a bold clear design. The mood is clean, soft, honest and dependable, down-to-earth with a friendly sense of humour.
