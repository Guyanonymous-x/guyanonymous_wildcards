Styled in a design language of hand-poured resin in organic, sculptural, brightly coloured shapes. Forms are blobs, pebbles, waves and smooth curvy tubes in swirling marbled resin, with glossy, translucent surfaces mixing candy colours such as watermelon pink, tangerine, turquoise, lime, lilac, cobalt, ivory and deep black. Everything follows the same fluid, tactile forms with uneven hand-poured edges. Playful, artistic, joyful mood, on pastel backgrounds with colourful reflections. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
