Styled in a design language of rugged, friendly modern adventure design. Tall rounded-rectangle volumes with smooth, closed, featureless surfaces, stadium-shaped vertical details joined by one thin line of light, short overhangs and a high, planted stance. Heavy black matte cladding wraps lower edges and surrounds openings, with chunky forms and turbine-like round motifs. Matte finishes in forest green, lake blue, limestone and glacier white. Warm natural wood, recycled felt and simple clean shapes. Outdoorsy, capable and approachable. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
