Styled after the rainbow airline of the late 1960s, with solid-colour aircraft painted in turquoise, lemon yellow, orange, lavender, baby blue and beige, designed by an artist, with matching colourful seats and bright flight bags. Futuristic, playful cabins, bold geometry and pastel cheer. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
