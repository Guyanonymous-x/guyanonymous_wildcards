Styled in a design language of sturdy German family-product design. Secondary accents in bright orange, green, yellow and sky blue. Trustworthy, orderly, cheerful, high-quality and European, with a polished, slightly nostalgic finish and generous clean margins around every printed block of colour. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
