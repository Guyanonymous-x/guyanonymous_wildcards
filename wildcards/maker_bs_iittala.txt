Styled in a design language of Finnish Scandinavian design with calm, honest, timeless simplicity. Forms are organic and fluid, with undulating leaf-like curves, free-form wavy outlines inspired by lake shores, and pure cylindrical or softly rounded geometry. Materials are clear, tinted and frosted glass, smooth glazed ceramic and a few matte finishes. Palette is cool and clear with ice blue, aqua, smoky grey, moss and sea green, soft white, with sparing warm accents in orange, red and cobalt. Colour is translucent and stackable in tidy ranges. Quiet, functional and nature-minded. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
