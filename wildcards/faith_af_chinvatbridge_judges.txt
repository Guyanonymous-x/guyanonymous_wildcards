at the bridgehead the three judges stand, Mithra, a crowned man in his forties with a halo of sunrays, Sraosha, a man in his thirties in armor holding a mace, and Rashnu, a man in his fifties in a white robe holding a tall golden balance in which the soul's good and evil deeds are weighed
Rashnu holds up his golden scales at the foot of the bridge, one pan heaped with small glowing white shapes of good thoughts, words and deeds and the other with dark gray shapes of evil ones, while Sraosha guards the soul from a crowd of dark demons
above the bridge hovers the faravahar, the winged sign of the faith, a bearded man in his fifties in a crown rising from a winged disk with long spread wings and a forked tail of feathers, one hand raised and the other holding a ring
