In the style of Coca-Cola, vivid saturated red field with flowing white Spencerian-script lettering in a looping connected hand, underlined by a white ribbon swoosh or wave. Contour glass bottle with a nipped waist, vertical fluted ribs, a bulging belly and a pale green-aqua glass tint, plus slim red-and-white aluminium cans, red cardboard multipacks and red delivery trucks. Dynamic ribbon wave devices, white on red with occasional black or silver accents for the diet range. Retro vintage versions show round red metal signs, hand-painted wall adverts and red coolers with rounded corners. Clean, optimistic, high-contrast, instantly legible red and white palette with a cursive logotype as the hero.
