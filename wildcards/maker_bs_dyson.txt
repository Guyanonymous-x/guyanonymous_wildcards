Styled in a design language of engineered, futuristic, glossy and rather industrial. Clear transparent plastic reveals inner structure against bright coloured parts in fuchsia, purple, yellow, nickel grey and iron. Satin nickel and polished metal finishes meet deep navy and black matte surfaces. Shapes are tubular, smoothly curved and ribbed, with exposed engineering, visible fixings and large chunky rounded elements. Thin glossy loops and tall tapering forms replace conventional silhouettes. Colour is used as bold coded blocks, with a vivid pink or yellow against cool grey. Clinical, precise and boldly colourful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
