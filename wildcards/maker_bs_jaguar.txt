Styled in a design language of sleek, feline and elegant British speed. Long, low, flowing forms with sensuous curves that taper gracefully and pull forward like a muscle about to spring. Palette of British racing green, cream, signal red and silver, with a modern pastel side of dusky pink, warm coral and bright gold against crisp white. Delicate chrome, polished wood and supple red or tan leather in vintage mode. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
