Styled in a design language of 1970s knight and pirate action figures with bearded or mustached sculpted heads and ruddy painted cheeks on stocky jointed bodies, shining silver vacuum-metalized plastic armor or cloth pirate shirts with sashes, sold in boxes painted with a castle or a pirate ship. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
