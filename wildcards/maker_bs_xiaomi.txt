Styled in a design language of clean, affordable-minimal and friendly. Simple matte white plastic or glass forms with softly rounded corners, flat colour surfaces and small circular bumps. Palette is white, light grey and warm orange with candy accent colours in violet, pink, lime and sky blue. Packaging is plain kraft brown cardboard with a single small orange square mark, minimal black printing and tidy square layouts. Honest, practical and cheerful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
