In the style of Lee Kum Kee, Chinese sauce bottles and jars with a heritage label. The signature oyster sauce bottle is a clear glass or plastic bottle with a short neck, red screw cap, and a wide wraparound paper label in rich red with gold border lines. A circular central picture shows a woman and a boy on a small flat boat on a river, drawn in a simplified storybook engraving with fine black outline and flat gold, green and cream fills, around which sits the name in big golden brush strokes and Latin capitals. Other sauces keep the same red label, gold ribbon banner and small seal stamp for chilli, soy and hoisin. Gift tins are red square cans with gold lettering and traditional pattern borders. The sauce itself is glossy dark brown. Food shots show wok flames, green vegetables and shiny glazed meats on white plates. Mood is trusted, family-run, festive and old-fashioned, with an auspicious red and gold palette and dense decorative labels.
