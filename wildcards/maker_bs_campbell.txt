Styled in a design language of flat, symmetrical, uniform and instantly legible. A bold red upper field meets a clean white lower field with a thin gold line between them, and a round gold medallion seal with a fine embossed ring sits dead centre. The main wordmark is a thin, flowing, connected red script, supported by small white sans-serif capitals on the red. Colour is applied in broad flat blocks with no gradients, in red, white and gold, with occasional pastel or deep green variations of the same layout. Finish is satin printed metal and smooth coated paper, with identical elements repeated in tidy grids. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
