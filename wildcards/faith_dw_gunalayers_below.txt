below the earth sink the four layers of the underworld, bands of dark blue and black where the strongholds of the spirit kings stand as houses of gold and silver beside dark rivers, each guarded by spirit animals, jaguars, snakes and crocodiles
in the deepest layer whirlpools of dark water turn, and the spirit kings, men in their fifties in tall hats and long coats, sit in their strongholds among heaps of shining treasure
the layers beneath the earth are painted in the maze patterns of the mola, narrow red and black paths winding down through them past villages of spirit animals, turtles, octopuses, birds and fish, each drawn in bright outlines
