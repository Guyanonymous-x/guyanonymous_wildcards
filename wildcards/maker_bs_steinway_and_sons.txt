In the style of Steinway and Sons, concert grand pianos in a glossy black polished lacquer, with a mirror-like curved rim, sweeping bentside and a large raised lid. The legs are sturdy, round and tapered, ending in brass casters, with a lyre-shaped pedal assembly in gold-toned metal beneath. A gilt cast-iron plate shows within, the name is in gold lettering on the fallboard and keys are white with ebony sharps. Decoration is elegant and restrained. Alternate finishes include dark mahogany or burl walnut. The mood is grand, opulent, formal and concert-hall dignified.
