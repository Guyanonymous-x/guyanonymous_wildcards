Styled in a design language of joyful, colourful, folkloric design that mixes wide kimono-like volumes, loose smock shapes, patchwork, quilting and layered global prints within a single look. Large flowers, poppies, stripes, ginghams, checks and paisley are combined at clashing scale in saturated tomato red, yellow, turquoise, emerald, pink, purple and soft peach. Fabrics are cotton, soft jersey, knits and light gauzy weaves, cut in a relaxed, unstructured, unisex way. Cheerful, exotic, bold, playful and vividly graphic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
