In the style of Rorstrand, Swedish mid-century porcelain. The best-known pattern is a white porcelain tableware with softly shaded cobalt-blue flowers painted on the rim and inside, with loose organic leaf outlines and a soft airbrush-like gradient, accompanied by a plain curving silhouette. Cups have a slender round handle, saucers are shallow, plates have a thin slightly rounded rim, and tureens have domed lids with simple knob handles. Other patterns offer a rustic mottled blue-and-white, green-and-brown speckled stoneware, and a trailing line of blue stripes. Earlier designs show Purple Lace transfer prints, a chinoiserie willow scene and a spray of green leaves. The overall look is gentle, light, sophisticated, calm and domestic, with the quiet modernism of Scandinavian dining, in a restrained palette of white, blue, soft green and brown.
