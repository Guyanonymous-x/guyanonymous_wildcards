the conservatory of 1850 newly built and the wonder of the house, which it genuinely was
the same structure in 1890 with a second boiler
the structure in 1925 with the boiler condemned, the pipes drained, half the glass out and the tree ferns dead in one February night, which is how most of them ended and is a very fast death, an unheated glasshouse loses a collection in a single frost
the structure in 1950 with the ironwork rusting from the glazing rebates outward, which is the characteristic failure, because water sits in the rebate and the putty holds it there
the structure restored in 1995 with the original bars replicated in aluminum and the difference visible to anyone who looks
the structure as a tea room with the staging out and forty chairs in
the structure gone entirely, leaving a scar on the house wall, a line of lead flashing, a row of brick scars where the columns were bolted, and a patch of wall where the render was never finished because it was never seen, which is how to identify a lost conservatory and is extremely common
the structure open to the weather, which for a glasshouse means simply gone
