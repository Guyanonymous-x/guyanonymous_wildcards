Shot through a super color 590nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a warm honey-cream body against a clear blue background, the hair a coppery gold, dreamlike and surreal. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
