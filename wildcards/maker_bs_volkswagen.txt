Styled in a design language of friendly, honest and gently witty. Smooth continuous curves with domed tops and rounded ends, or cheerful flat-faced boxes split in two tones by a white band. Pastel and sun-faded colour in sea blue, coral red, mint, ivory, sunshine yellow and cream. Plain painted metal, simple fabric, big clear dials and little ornament. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
