from the peak of Lum Sohpetbneng rises the golden ladder, a great tree with a trunk and branches of shining gold reaching up through the clouds into heaven, people in their thirties to their fifties climbing up and down it
the golden ladder rises as a shining vine of gold twisting up from the summit into the clouds, the path by which the people went up to heaven by day and came down again at evening
at the foot of the peak a man in his forties, tricked by the evil one, swings an axe at the trunk of the golden tree, and the ladder breaks away from the sky
