Mayel Lyang, a hidden green land high on the slopes of Kanchenjunga, rich fields of rice and millet, orange trees and flowers under the snow peaks, the ancestors living in plenty
the ancestors of Mayel Lyang, men and women in their forties to nineties in striped Lepcha cloths, welcome the dead at a house of wood on stilts among fruit trees
the snow peaks of Kanchenjunga shine white above Mayel Lyang, the hidden valley below them green with forest and meadow, a lake reflecting the mountain
