In 1785 police officers in blue coats break into a member's house and pull bundles of the order's letters from a hidden cupboard, a scene of the ban that followed.
A printed government order nailed to a tavern door reads "VERBOT" across its top in heavy black letters, the ban of 1785.
Copies of the order's seized papers lie printed and bound for the public, the title page reading "ORIGINALSCHRIFTEN".
