ochre-dyed cotton wraps worn by holy men, a cloth at the waist and a shawl over one shoulder, necklaces of rudraksha beads, wrinkled brown seeds, a water pot and a staff in hand, bare feet
sadhus in saffron, long gray beards and coiled dreadlocks piled on top of the head, three white lines of ash across the forehead, strings of brown seeds around the neck
sadhvis, women renunciants, in their forties to seventies in saffron, ochre or orange saris draped plainly, their heads shaved or the hair pulled back plainly, prayer beads in hand
