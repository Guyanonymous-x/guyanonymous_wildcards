Styled in a design language of tactile, classic, clean and understated retro-modern design. Flat-topped compact forms in two-tone silver and black, matte black paint over metal. Thin red ruler-like labels and small red accents. Packaging favours kraft cardboard with simple black print and bright green or red colour blocks. Calm, analogue, hands-on and neatly proportioned. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
