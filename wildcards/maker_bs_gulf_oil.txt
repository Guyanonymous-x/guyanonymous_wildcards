Styled in a design language of petrol stations and racing cars in powder blue and bright orange, with a round orange disc on a blue field, cars with a central stripe in orange, clean white pumps with round glass tops and tidy forecourts with blue-and-orange canopies. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
