painted in the Woodland school style of Anishinaabe painting, bold black outlines, flat bright reds, blues, greens and yellows, the inner lines of each spirit drawn within its body and all the beings joined by flowing lines
painted in the manner of an engraved Mississippian shell gorget, fine lines cut into a round disk of pale cream shell, the beings in profile with feathered wings and spotted bodies, set in a circle with a cross at its center
painted as a present-day Haudenosaunee painting of the Sky World, rich deep blues, greens and whites, the figures drawn with care, framed by a border of raised white beadwork of curving lines
painted in the manner of Lakota ledger drawing, figures drawn in colored pencil and ink on old lined paper, the people and horses side by side in a row, flat bright colors
