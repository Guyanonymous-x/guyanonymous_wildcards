Styled in a design language of loud, bold, rock and roll and imposing. Matte black pebbled vinyl covers every surface, set against woven basket-weave cloth in cream, tan or black. Trim is gold or silver piping and rows of brass-toned metal studs and corner caps. Palette is black, cream, warm gold and chrome with occasional oxblood leather and brown. Shapes are blocky, rectangular and heavy with softly rounded edges and a stacked, monumental feel. Surfaces suggest tough road-worn craft, with bright metal glints against deep black. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
