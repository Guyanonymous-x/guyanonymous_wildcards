Styled in a design language of soft, rounded, playful Italian mid-century charm. Smooth, one-piece curved shells with wasp-waisted contours and pressed-metal softness, finished in pastel enamel in mint green, sky blue, cream, coral, butter yellow and sage, with chrome trim strips and two-tone panels. Textiles show pique knit, washed cotton, ribbed collars and subtle tipping in contrasting stripes. Retro enamel signs, tin tray graphics and chunky rounded forms bring nostalgic summer cheer. Everything is friendly, light, sunny and stylishly casual, with a polished retro simplicity. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
