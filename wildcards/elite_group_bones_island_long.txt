Deer Island, the private island retreat of the Skull and Bones society in the St. Lawrence River. A small wooded island of rocks and pine trees sits in a wide blue river, with a large old shingled lodge with a deep wraparound porch among the trees and a wooden dock running out into the water. Small white catboats with single sails are tied at the dock, and on the porch members in summer clothes sit in wooden rocking chairs looking out over the water.
