Styled in a design language of imperial, scholarly, aromatic, sombre and prosperous, like an old apothecary of lacquer, brass and paper. Imperial yellow, vermilion red and gold with embossed dragon or lotus borders, traditional calligraphic characters and red seal stamps. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
