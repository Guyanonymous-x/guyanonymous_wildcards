Styled in a design language of bulbous, torpedo-shaped forms in glossy striped celluloid colours, pearl and jade green, marine black, grey, burgundy and blue. Thin chrome or gold trim rings circle the body, and a distinctive inlaid white dot punctuates the top. Gold accents are long and triangular or hooded. Lines are straight, flat and sleek, with a spear-like taper. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
