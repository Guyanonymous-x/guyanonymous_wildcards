In the style of Alfa Romeo, passionate, graceful Italian sporting elegance. A round badge split into a red cross on white and a crowned serpent swallowing a figure, ringed in deep blue with gold lettering and wavy knot ornaments, repeats as the signature mark. Slim, low, curved forms with long tapering lines and a triangular shield motif. Palette of rich Alfa red, cream paper, white, deep blue, silver, black and dark green. Early poster art uses flat painted colour, dramatic diagonals, heavy art deco capital lettering, and tall vermilion shapes against cream and charcoal. Materials include polished chrome, drilled aluminium, wood-rimmed details and rich leather. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
