In the style of Subaru, Japanese rally-bred sedans and hatchbacks with a hawk-eye or wing-shaped grille and swept headlamps, a bonnet scoop on top, a tall rear wing on the boot, flared wheel arches, and a six-star cluster badge. Practical boxy body, frameless door windows, short overhang, gold-painted multispoke wheels, and big round fog lamps. Interior with plain dark fabric, bright blue or red stitching, a sporty bucket seat and simple round gauges. Colours of World Rally blue with gold wheels, white, black and silver. Purposeful, rugged and a little aggressive.
