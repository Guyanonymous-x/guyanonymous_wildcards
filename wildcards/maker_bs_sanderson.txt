Styled in a design language of English wallpaper and chintz fabrics with large-scale painterly blooms, cabbage roses, peonies, hydrangeas, hand-drawn trailing leaves, birds and berries, and printed willow patterns. Palette of soft pink, duck-egg blue, sage green, cream, mulberry and gold, in printed cotton, linen and embossed wall coverings, plus mid-century abstract botanical prints. Graceful, romantic and traditional English country house. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
