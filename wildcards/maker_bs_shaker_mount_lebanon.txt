In the style of Shaker Mount Lebanon, plain and sturdy furniture and objects made from pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Chairs have slender turned posts, ladder backs with arching slats, woven tape or rush seats and tapered legs. Tables, cupboards and drawers show clean flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, wooden knobs and a smooth surface. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, namely barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and the natural honey wood. Oval pantry boxes use swallowtail joints and copper tacks. Rooms have pegboards that hold chairs on the wall. The mood is serene, orderly, utilitarian and pure.
