In the style of the grand Moscow Metro stations, palatial underground halls with vaulted ceilings, rows of marble arches and columns in white, rose, grey and red stone, polished floors in checkered dark and light granite, and huge crystal and brass chandeliers hanging in the centre. Mosaics and stained glass panels showing heroic workers, soldiers, farmers and aviators in bright colours, bronze statues in alcoves, bas relief carvings and gilded stars. Soviet socialist realism and Stalinist classicism, theatrical lighting, symmetrical long perspective, warm golden light on polished marble, ornate ceiling coffers and sculpted ornament, like an underground cathedral or palace of the people.
