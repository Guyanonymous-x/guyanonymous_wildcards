Styled in a design language of democratic Scandinavian practicality, bright and friendly. Simple, light, flat-planar shapes, modular rectangles with soft corners, visible joints and clever space-saving logic. Pale birch, pine and white laminate are paired with cheerful saturated blue and yellow plus red, green and soft pastels, in bold, playful patterns and plain colour blocks. Materials feel affordable but honest, such as light wood, painted board, woven textiles and moulded plastic. Lettering is bold, rounded, plain sans-serif in capitals. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
