Styled in a design language of French lead crystal, clear and brilliant, with deep geometric diamond cuts, fluted columns, faceted forms and heavy bases. Cut hobnail diamonds and prismatic edges sparkle, tiered pendant drops hang from gilded bronze arms, and occasional bright red cut glass adds richness. Dazzling refraction and rainbow highlights under candlelight, formal, opulent and palatial in a Second Empire manner. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
