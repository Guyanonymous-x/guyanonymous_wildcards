Styled in a design language of hand-painted surfaces with bold, loose, post-impressionist decoration. Brush marks are visible, with abstract blobs, stripes, mustard, magenta, turquoise, orange, lilac, green and black. Compositions are irregular and asymmetrical with an unfinished hand-drawn quality. Underlying forms are plain and painted all over in dense patterns. Glazes are thick and opaque, with spots and swirls. The mood is cheerful, avant-garde, amateurish by design and bohemian. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
