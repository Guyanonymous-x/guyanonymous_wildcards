__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of severe, intellectual minimalism with deadpan, slightly odd elegance. Shapes are pared-back, geometric and utilitarian, with flat planes. Materials are matte black industrial nylon, saffiano cross-hatch leather, brushed metal, glossy plastic and sheer technical fabrics. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
