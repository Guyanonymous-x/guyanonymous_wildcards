Styled in a design language of handloom cotton with striped and checked weaves in bold bands of saturated colour such as coral, turquoise, lime green, mustard, indigo, magenta and white. Plain-weave and slubby handwoven texture shows visible warp threads, tasselled fringes and playful geometric stripes. Occasional hand-painted or batik motifs of flowers and leaves appear. Relaxed, tropical, cheerful mood, with sunlit warmth, natural fibre tones like palm and rattan, and a handmade, unpolished texture.
