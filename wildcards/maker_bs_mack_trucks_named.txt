In the style of Mack Trucks, blunt, tough, muscular American heavy-duty character. A bulldog-like, forward-thrust mass, a top line that curves down steeply and a bold chrome standing animal ornament. Stout upright chrome faces with horizontal bars, round chrome lamps, short heavy curves and thick chrome bars. Bright enamel in red, yellow, blue or black, with stainless accents. Chunky, compact, solid and no-nonsense, proportioned like a determined fighter, with polished highlights on a hard-working frame. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
