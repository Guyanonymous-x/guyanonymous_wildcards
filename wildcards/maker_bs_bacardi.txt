In the style of Bacardi, a Cuban-born rum house with a heraldic bat emblem. The central symbol is a black bat with outspread wings seen from the front, drawn in a bold, simple, slightly gothic silhouette with a small head, set inside a red, white and black label. The label on a clear square-shouldered or tapered glass bottle is bright, clean white or cream with a red, black or gold border and big black block lettering, a small crest at the top and a tiny red wax-like seal around the cap. Premium rums use deeper amber liquid, gold-foil edging and aged cream labels with ornate serif lettering. Barrels are dark oak with iron hoops, and old advertising shows sunny beaches, palm fronds, cocktails in coupe glasses and bright red umbrellas. The Art Deco headquarters building is a tall tiled tower clad in blue-and-white geometric patterned tiles with a stepped crown. The mood is festive, tropical, confident and slightly retro.
