Styled in a design language of chunky rounded forms with thick softened corners and no sharp edges anywhere. Materials are glossy moulded plastic and warm lacquered wood with slightly worn paint. Palette is primary red, sunny yellow, cobalt blue and grass green against clean white and natural pale timber. Flat lithographed decoration shows simple smiling faces made from two dots and a curve, little cartoon animals and dotted rim details in a naive hand-drawn manner. Everything feels sturdy, cheerful, safe and handmade-simple. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
