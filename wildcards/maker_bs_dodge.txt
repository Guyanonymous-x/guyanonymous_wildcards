In the style of Dodge, early 1970s American muscle with a wide aggressive stance, a long flat hood with twin bulging scoops, and a split grille with a hidden-lamp or twin round headlamp face. Squared-off fenders with a pronounced coke-bottle hip, a bold bumblebee stripe or tail band, and a ducktail rear spoiler. Steel wheels with a slotted rim and a fat white-letter tyre, a pistol-grip shifter inside, black vinyl bucket seats and a strong gauge cluster. Colours of high-impact violet, lime green, Plum Crazy purple, bright orange and sublime green. Brutal, loud, cartoonish tough-guy look with ram or red stripe badge.
