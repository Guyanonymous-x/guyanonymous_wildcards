a ventriloquist dummy maker's workshop. A long wooden workbench is covered with carved heads at different stages, blocks of pale wood roughly shaped, smooth heads waiting for paint and finished heads with painted faces and open jaws. Loose wooden arms, legs and hands hang in rows from nails on the wall above the bench. Small shallow trays hold glass eyes sorted by color, blue, brown, green and gray, staring up. Chisels, gouges and small knives lie in a canvas tool roll, pots of paint and brushes stand in jars, and the floor is covered in curled wood shavings and sawdust.
