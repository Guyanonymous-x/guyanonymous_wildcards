Styled in a design language of ethical, handmade design centred on hand-dyed indigo, hand-batiked cloth, resist patterns, hand-woven fabrics and block-printed wax prints. Shapes are relaxed and modern, with wide legs, boxy tops, flowing wraps and kimono-like layers in cotton and linen. Palette includes deep indigo blue, white, earthy ochre, rust, black and bright accent colours such as orange and green, in tie-dye swirls, hand-drawn stripes, dots, geometric grids and flowing batik lines. Visible hand-dyed unevenness and crisp artisanal texture. Breezy, warm, socially conscious, sunlit mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
