Styled in a design language of calm, sculptural Scandinavian restraint where light is softened and glare is controlled. Layered concentric curves, overlapping discs or dozens of overlapping leaf-like plates form smooth symmetrical shapes. Materials are polished steel, chrome, brass, copper and white lacquer with soft warm reflections. Smooth, restrained, elegant and perfectly proportioned, giving a soft diffused glow and a quietly luxurious modernist calm. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
