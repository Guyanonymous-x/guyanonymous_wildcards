__bs_subject_tech__ styled in a design language of optimistic Dutch industrial design. Palette is strong electric blue, white, pale grey, with ivory, mustard, tangerine and pastel tints in older pieces and fresh cyan, navy and sunrise gold in newer ones. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
