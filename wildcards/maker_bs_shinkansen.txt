Styled in a design language of calm, ultra-precise, aerodynamic Japanese high-speed modernity. A long, smooth, continuous form ending in a duck-bill or beak-shaped tip, with rounded cross-sections and flush windows and doors. White bodywork carries one thin blue or colour stripe. Narrow wraparound glazing, low skirts hiding working parts, and near-seamless, polished surfaces. Quiet, futuristic, tidy and disciplined, where every curve serves speed and silence. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
