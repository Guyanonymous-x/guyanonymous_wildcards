Styled in a design language of futuristic and streamlined postwar American design. A very low, smooth, rounded teardrop body, a narrow chrome-bar front with a wide mouth and a swept-back profile. Waltz blue, beige, maroon and dark green. Small whitewall roundness and a fully enclosed louvred finish at the back add futurism. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
