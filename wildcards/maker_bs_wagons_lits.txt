Styled in a design language of european luxury sleeping and dining cars with varnished teak bodies lined in fine gold, inside panelled in inlaid marquetry with floral and geometric veneers, polished brass luggage racks and lamps, deep blue or green velvet upholstery, white linen and silver tableware, and a glamorous Art Nouveau to Art Deco travel romance. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
