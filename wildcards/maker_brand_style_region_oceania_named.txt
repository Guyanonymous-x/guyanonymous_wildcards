In the style of Holden, tough, proud Australian design built around a heraldic lion emblem. A rampant lion stands upright on a rolling stone, drawn in solid bold red or black silhouette, often inside an oval or shield with the capital name beneath. Palette is signal red, black, white, silver and sunburnt tones such as gold, burnt orange and dusty tan. Shapes are strong, muscular and flat with bold stripes and thick colour blocks. Finishes are enamelled tin, cast metal, chrome and flat, slightly weathered painted surfaces. Lettering is heavy, wide, slab-like capitals, in sturdy, no-nonsense proportions. Retro tin-sign treatments with scuffed edges, rivets and a lightly rusted patina give a rugged, confident heritage feel.
In the style of R.M. Williams, refined Australian rural heritage and craftsmanship. Smooth, polished, single-piece leather wrapped with minimal seams and visible hand stitching in chestnut, tan, black and oxblood. Workwear fabrics include moleskin, oilskin, heavy cotton drill, gingham and check flannel, plus wide leather belts, in khaki, oat, olive, navy, rust and burgundy. The emblem is a longhorn bull head with wide horns and a bold red label, and the wordmark is a spaced, upright serif in cream or gold on deep brown or black with a founding year line. Branded leather patches, brass studs and embossed marks give a quietly premium, rugged and enduring finish.
In the style of Zimmermann, romantic, bohemian, sunny Australian femininity. Silhouettes are floaty and layered, with ruffles, tiered frills, puffed shapes, smocking, scalloped edges, broderie anglaise, delicate lace, embroidery and tie details in linen, cotton, silk and chiffon. Prints are painterly watercolour florals, patchwork blooms, paisley, ditsy flowers, tile motifs and polka dots in cream, ivory, blush, coral, sage, butter yellow, cornflower blue, rich red, terracotta and black. Layered mixed prints and gathered trims feel handcrafted, vintage and dreamy. Lettering is a clean, spaced, light serif wordmark in black or white on pale neutral grounds, giving a polished, airy and artistic look.
In the style of Akubra, soft, understated Australian rural craft. Surfaces are dense rabbit-fur felt, matte, slightly fuzzy and suede-like, in fawn, tan, oat, cream, stone grey, olive, charcoal and black, with a darker tonal ribbon band and neat bound edges. Shapes are generous, flat and gently sloping with a soft dent and pinched creases that look broken in with use. Leather trim, small metal pins and plain stitched details appear sparingly. The identity is a plain black rectangular label with a bold white block wordmark, plus a cardboard-brown natural packaging look with a stamped circular crest and a woodcut style emblem. Practical, sun-faded, rugged and honest.

Honest, timeless, practical and tough.
