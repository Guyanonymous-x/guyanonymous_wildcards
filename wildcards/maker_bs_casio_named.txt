In the style of Casio, cheap, cheerful, geeky and tough. Lightweight resin in black, grey, silver and bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal and rectangular outlines and chunky rubberised edges. Grey-green segmented digits and tiny grid layouts of squares in grey, orange and blue. Gold-finished and brushed steel variants add retro flair. Candy pastel, neon pink, teal and sunny yellow colour-blocking appears on rounded jelly-like shells. Printing is dense, small and technical, with many tiny white capital labels and thin rules around edges. The logo is a bold, wide, geometric sans-serif in white or black on blue or red. Utilitarian, nostalgic and playful. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
