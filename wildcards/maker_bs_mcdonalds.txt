Styled in a design language of bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds. Lettering is soft, chunky, rounded sans-serif in white or yellow. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
