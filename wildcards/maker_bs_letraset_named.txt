Styled in a design language of letterforms are bold, expressive 1960s and 70s display faces, chunky rounded letters, psychedelic swirls, inline shadows, stencil cuts, art-nouveau revival and space-age geometry. The mood is cut-and-paste, punk poster and retro graphic design. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
