a standard car interior repurposed as a dwelling, where every original feature is now used for something else, the boot as a wardrobe
the glovebox as a cupboard
the door pockets as shelves
the 12-volt socket as the only power supply and therefore the most important fitting in the car
the folded rear seats as a bed that is six inches too short
the windows as the whole relationship with the outside and therefore covered
the interior light disconnected or avoided because the battery must start the car in the morning
a handbrake and a gearlever that are obstacles rather than controls at night
