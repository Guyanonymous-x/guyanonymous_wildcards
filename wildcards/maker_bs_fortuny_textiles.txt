In the style of Fortuny Textiles, printed velvet and cotton furnishing fabrics with densely stamped metallic pigment in gold and silver on deep, mottled backgrounds. Colours are rich and muted, including aubergine, indigo, burnt sienna, olive, ochre, antique rose and dusty teal, with a hazy, layered, hand-printed look. Patterns are Renaissance pomegranates, Islamic arabesques, Cretan and Persian stylised animals, ogee lattices and medallions repeating in tight rows. The surface has a faint shimmer from the metal ink, a soft aged glow and a velvety hand. Silk pleated gowns and lamps with a painted parchment shade complete the rich, theatrical, exotic mood.
