Styled in a design language of smooth, rounded, streamlined forms in two-tone pastel enamel of powder blue, seafoam green, cream or white with a bright accent stripe. Chrome plated trim and bolts, gleaming stainless parts and fin-like jet-age shapes. Clean shine on every surface, with a cheerful, optimistic 1950s American lake-holiday mood and a tidy, gleaming, freshly waxed finish. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
