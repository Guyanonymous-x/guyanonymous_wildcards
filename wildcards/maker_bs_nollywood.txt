Styled in a design language of high-energy, melodramatic, richly saturated imagery built as dramatic collage compositions. Bold West African wax-print and woven cloth patterns, elaborate sculpted headwear, abundant jewellery and showy displays of wealth, with bright daylight and crowded frames. Emotion is turned up to the maximum, colours are loud and shiny, and the finish has a lively, direct, early digital home-video energy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
