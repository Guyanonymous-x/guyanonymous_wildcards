beyond the farm the old cemetery grove of tall pines and birches holds low earth graves and plain wooden crosses, candles in glass jars burning on them
on the path to the cemetery stands a ristipuu, a cross tree, an old pine whose bark is cut with many small crosses, one for each dead person carried past it
at the edge of the fields rises the hiis, the sacred grove of old oaks and lime trees with a large gray boulder among their roots, white cloth strips tied to the low branches
