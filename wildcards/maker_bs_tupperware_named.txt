Styled in a design language of cheerful domestic order in translucent, slightly frosted material with a matte waxy finish. Soft rounded corners, gentle stackable shapes and smooth, seamless moulded surfaces. Edges are thick, soft and clean, with simple raised ribbing or concentric rings as the only ornament. A tidy, modular, practical, optimistic, family-friendly look. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
