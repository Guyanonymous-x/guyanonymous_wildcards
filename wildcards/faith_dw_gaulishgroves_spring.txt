at the source of the Seine the goddess Sequana, a woman in her thirties in a long robe and a diadem, stands in a boat carved in the form of a duck, the spring welling up around her
beside a spring stands a small shrine with wooden carvings left by pilgrims, carved legs, arms, heads and whole figures of people in cloaks, set in the mud around the water
a healing spring wells up in a stone basin under a willow, and pilgrims in their twenties to their seventies dip cloths in it and tie them to the branches
