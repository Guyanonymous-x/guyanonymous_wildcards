the ch'unchu dancers, men in their twenties to fifties dressed as the forest peoples of the Amazon, in tall crowns of red, blue and yellow macaw feathers, sleeveless tunics of red cloth and long palm-wood staffs in their hands
the qhapaq qolla dancers, men in their twenties to forties dressed as highland llama herders, in flat square caps of woven wool hung with colored tassels, knitted masks of white wool and short woven capes over white shirts
the contradanza dancers, men in their thirties to fifties in short jackets heavily embroidered with sequins and gold thread, white lace collars, wire-mesh masks painted with pink faces and mustaches, and tall hats with plumes
