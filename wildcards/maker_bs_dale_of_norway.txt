In the style of Dale of Norway, Scandinavian wool sweaters. The garment is a thick, close-knit wool pullover or cardigan with a high collar or round neck, and a patterned yoke pattern worked in stranded colourwork, a circular band of stars, snowflakes, diamonds, reindeer, zigzags and stepped borders around the chest and shoulders. Colours are strong, with navy, red, white and grey as the classic combination, plus charcoal, forest green and cream. Ribbed cuffs, hems and neckbands are plain, and cardigans have pewter clasps or metal buttons. The wool is dense and slightly fuzzy, and the pattern is clear and graphic with neat repeating bands. The sports sweaters carry the national flag colours and ring motifs. The mood is crisp, alpine, snowy and cosy. The garment has a snug, athletic fit and shows crisp zigzag edging, tiny star motifs in white and a thick shawl or turtle collar, as seen on alpine ski slopes in the 1960s and 1980s.
