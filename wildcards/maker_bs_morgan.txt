In the style of Morgan, a hand-built British roadster in a deliberately pre-war manner, with a long louvred bonnet, separate sweeping cycle-type wings, a flat narrow cockpit and a rounded tail. Tall upright chrome radiator grille with a slim vertical bar, freestanding round chrome headlamps on stalks, a spare wheel on the rear, and wire-spoke wheels with knock-off hubs. Body panels are aluminium over an ash wood frame, with a folding canvas hood and leather straps over the bonnet. Paint in racing green, claret, cream, navy and ivory, with polished brass and chrome details. Interior has simple leather bucket seats, a polished wooden dash with round white-faced dials and pull-out switches, and a thin-rimmed wooden wheel. Mood is charming, handcrafted and nostalgic.
