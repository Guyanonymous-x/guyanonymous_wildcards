Styled in a design language of cheeky, theatrical Parisian pop-couture. The core pattern is navy and white horizontal Breton stripes, with variations in black, red, turquoise and pale blue. Surfaces include sheer tattoo-print mesh in sailor motifs, anchors, hearts, roses and swallows, plus pinstripe wool, corsetry satin, shiny tin metal and printed metal sheets in cream, pink and teal. Shapes mix masculine and feminine cues with cinched, sculpted curves, pointed cone silhouettes and sleek hourglass forms. Colour is witty, saucy and bold, with a playful, ironic, avant-garde attitude. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
