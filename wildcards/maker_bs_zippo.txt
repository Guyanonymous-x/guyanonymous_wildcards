Styled in a design language of compact, rugged American metalwork with a vintage, collectable charm. Sturdy rectangular forms with softly rounded corners, polished chrome, brushed steel and brass finishes with a worn, handled shine. Surfaces carry crisp engraved or enamel-printed graphics such as pinups, flames, eagles, tattoo-style icons and retro geometric patterns, in saturated red, black, cream, teal and orange. Mid-century Americana, tough, reliable, personal and witty. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
