In the style of EgyptAir, a national carrier identity of ancient mythology. Aircraft have a white fuselage, and a deep blue and gold tail fin carrying a golden falcon god, stylised in a flowing, wing-spread pose with a red head, blue feathers and a gold backdrop. The airline name appears in both Latin and Arabic script in dark blue on the forward body. The older livery had a warm band of red, gold and orange swirling around the window line and engines, giving a sunset glow. Cabins use blue and gold seats with Egyptian geometric motifs, pale wood trims, and gold emblem on the head covers. Crew wear smart navy uniforms with a hat or scarf in pharaonic gold. Mood is stately, ancient, desert-sunny and glowing, with lapis blue, gold and red.
