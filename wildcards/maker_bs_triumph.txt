In the style of Triumph sports cars in the 1960s, a compact open two-seat roadster with a long low bonnet, a wedge-shaped rear and a tidy tapering tail with chrome bumpers. Rounded sculpted wings with a pronounced crisp crease, headlamps tucked into a smooth nose, a small egg-crate or mesh grille with a round shield badge, and a folding black cloth hood. Wheels are wire-spoke or steel with chrome trim rings. Paint in signal red, white, British racing green, pale yellow, and deep blue. Interior has black or red vinyl bucket seats, a wood or painted dashboard with round dials and toggle switches, a wood-rim wheel and a floor gearshift. Mood is breezy, sporty and affordable.
