Meher Kapısı, a doorway as tall as three people cut into a smoothed panel of pale gray-yellow limestone on a steep ridge, three stepped frames sinking one inside the next to a flat back panel covered from edge to edge with neat rows of small wedge-shaped strokes, the list of the gods and their offerings, a second smaller platform cut on the slope below it
the gate at Yeşilalıç, a tall narrow blind doorway with a double stepped frame cut into a low cliff of brown rock beside a village stream, a broad flight of rock-cut steps leading up to the terrace before it
Hazine Piri Kapısı, a wide shallow niche with two stepped frames cut into a lone gray crag on a bare hillside, the rock above it left rough and split into blocks, small thorn bushes in the cracks
a gate cut into the south cliff of the great rock of the citadel at Tushpa, a blind doorway of two stepped frames high in the sheer wall, reached by a narrow stair hewn along the cliff, the blue lake far below
