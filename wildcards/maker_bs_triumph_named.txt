Styled in a design language of breezy, sporty and affordable open-air fun. A long low mass, a tidy tapering end, chrome edges, rounded sculpted surfaces with one crisp crease and a folding black cloth top. Signal red, white, British racing green, pale yellow and deep blue. Black or red vinyl moulded forms, wood or painted panels. Jaunty, compact and cheerful, with unfussy honest detailing. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
