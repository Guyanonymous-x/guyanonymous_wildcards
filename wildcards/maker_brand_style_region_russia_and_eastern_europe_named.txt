In the style of Lada, frugal, sturdy, Soviet-era design. The emblem is a stylised sailing ship in a rounded shield or oval, drawn in chrome or white on a deep red or royal blue field, with an angular letter L inside it. Shapes are plain, squared-off and utilitarian, made of flat panels with minimal ornament. Palette is faded orange, mustard yellow, olive green, brick red, cream, sky blue and warm brown, as flat uncoated colour blocks and bold stripes. Finishes are painted enamel, thin chrome trim, stamped metal and worn printed paper with a nostalgic, sun-faded patina.

Wide bench seating and mottled plastic textures. Palette of ivory over pale green, dark grey and black for officials, light blue and cream with a chequer stripe for working use. Solid, proud and bureaucratic.
