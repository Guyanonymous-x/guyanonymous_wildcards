In the style of Ando Cloisonne, Japanese cloisonne vases, boxes and plates with smooth, glossy enamel fields held by hair-thin silver or brass wires. Colours are deep and jewel-like, including midnight blue, black, wisteria violet, jade green, ivory and warm gold, with a mirror finish and no visible wire thickness. Subjects are delicate birds, hydrangea, wisteria, irises, chrysanthemums, butterflies and tiny geometric brocade borders, often floating on a plain black or blue ground with generous empty space. Wireless techniques create soft gradients in petals and feathers. Vessels are slender, with narrow necks, flared mouths and silver rims, and are finished with gilt metal collars.
In the style of Roycroft Arts and Crafts Community, rectilinear oak furniture, hand-hammered copper and books made in a plain handmade manner. Furniture has square, sturdy legs, exposed through-tenons, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish, with visible grain. Copper bowls, lamps and bookends show hammer marks, rivets, strap hinges and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. Books use thick handmade paper, rubricated initials, woodcut-like borders, heavy black type and limp suede or vellum covers with a stamped orb and cross emblem. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic.
In the style of Guild of Handicraft, Arts and Crafts silver, enamel and jewellery in a hand-wrought manner. Hammered silver bowls, tankards and jewellery show visible tool marks, curved handles, wire twists and loose, flowing Art Nouveau lines. Peacocks, ships, tendrils, stylised flowers and hearts are set with cabochon moonstones, opals, turquoise or blister pearls and translucent coloured enamel in green, blue and rose. Items look soft and slightly irregular, with a matte, pale silver surface and delicate suspended drops. The mood is romantic, medieval-influenced, simple and craftsmanlike.
In the style of Palekh Lacquer Miniatures, black glossy papier-mache boxes, brooches and panels painted in egg tempera with fairy tale scenes. Elongated, elegant figures with small heads and graceful curved poses walk through stylised hills, towers, trees and onion domes. Palette is bright red, gold, emerald green, white and orange against deep lustrous black, with fine gold highlights outlining draperies and ornamental borders of tiny patterns. The inside lid is red lacquer. Details are miniature and precise, as in an icon, and the mood is mythical, jewel-like and storybook.
In the style of Faberge, Imperial Russian jewelled objects, especially egg-shaped caskets in layered translucent guilloche enamel in pastel pink, powder blue, mint green, lilac and cream, over engraved sunburst patterns, framed by gold, pearls, diamonds and rubies. Miniature surprise inside, tiny gold chariots and portraits, hardstone animals carved in jade, agate and rock crystal, silver cigarette cases and picture frames, delicate gilded neoclassical garlands, bows and laurel wreaths. Opulent, precious, exact, royal treasury glitter.
In the style of Wiener Werkstatte, Viennese Secession craftsmanship with geometric abstraction, strict grids of black and white squares, concentric circles, stylised roses, wavy lines and hand-block-printed bold patterns in orange, coral, turquoise, black and gold. Silver and metalwork with pierced squares, ebonised wood, enamelled jewellery, elongated chairs with grid-patterned backs, ceramics with chequered bands and stylised flowers. Sophisticated, rhythmic, ornamental modernism with a precise linear elegance.
