Styled in a design language of sturdy, generous, homey with a vintage curve. Heavy cast metal bodies with smooth bulbous curved tops, soft rounded shoulders and flat-bottomed steel or glass forms with gently curved edges. Glossy enamel in empire red, onyx black, white, pistachio, pastel blue, cream and candy apple red, with polished chrome and brushed silver accents. Satin stainless and clear glass offer cool contrast. A repeated round hub motif and rounded edges make forms feel friendly and durable. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
