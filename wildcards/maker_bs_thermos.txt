Styled in a design language of sturdy insulated-ware design with smooth cylindrical and softly rounded forms. Polished and brushed stainless steel is paired with matte powder-coated finishes in charcoal, black, navy, forest green and a signature bright red accent, with white and silver for contrast. Surfaces are clean and seamless with a slight sheen, thin metal rims and small moulded black or red details. The mark is a bold, wide. Occasional kids prints in cheerful cartoon colours and vintage plaid or enamel tones. Dependable, no-nonsense, practical and warm. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
