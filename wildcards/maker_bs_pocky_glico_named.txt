Styled in a design language of slim, long and elegant graphic forms in narrow vertical layouts. Saturated single-colour fields in glossy red, strawberry pink, matcha green, chocolate brown and cream, each with a glossy laminated sheen. Surfaces carry cute illustrated patterns of berries, leaves, hearts, checks and cow-like spots in soft pastels with flat colour shapes. Thin diagonal slender stick motifs are repeated as stripes, sometimes dipped in a smooth glaze edge with a white wavy splash. Cheerful, clean, sweet, kawaii and collectable. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
