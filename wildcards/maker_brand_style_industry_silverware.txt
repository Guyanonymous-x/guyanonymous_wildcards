In the style of Gorham Silver, heavy sterling tableware, tea sets and trophies in a lavish late-Victorian and Art Nouveau manner. Surfaces are hand-hammered or chased, with bright polished highlights and darker oxidised recesses. Decoration includes embossed flowers, lily pads, whiplash curves, dragonflies, Japanese-inspired motifs, mixed metal inlays of copper and brass, and flowing organic handles. Teapots and bowls are rounded, with applied leaves and figural finials and ebonised or ivory-coloured insulators. Pieces have a rich, sculptural, ornamental feel and a characteristic martelled texture. The marks are small stamped symbols on the base.
