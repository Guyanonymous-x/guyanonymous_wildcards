Styled in a design language of light, nimble, gracefully proportioned British forms of the 1930s to 1950s. Slim bodies with smooth wooden curves, elliptical outlines and softly curved coverings that hug what is inside. Glazed bubble forms, white and blue. Streamlined, clean and elegant, craftsman-built, with a hopeful pre-war and post-war amateur charm. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
