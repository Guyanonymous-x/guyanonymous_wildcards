Styled in a design language of fussy, crowded Victorian heritage design. Squat rectangular forms with short necks are wrapped in cream-white paper printed in black, with ornate Victorian serif and slab lettering mixed with fine engraved text, plus orange-ochre panels and a small coat-of-arms-like crest. Folded wrapping, paper seals and black accents add craft detail. Palette is orange, black and cream with an aged, tea-stained patina. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
