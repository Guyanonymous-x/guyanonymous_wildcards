Styled in a design language of American luxury that mixes jet-age glamour with a sharp modern edge. Forms are long, low and wide with crisp vertical blades, razor creases and polished chrome edging. Palette is pearl white, midnight black, champagne gold, deep ruby red and powder pink, with mirror-bright metal throughout. Materials include tufted leather, glossy lacquer, cut glass and stitched suede. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
