Five in the morning, the first use, coal from the yard at record 38 to the boiler house, by trolley, in the dark, with the lights not lit because nobody lights them for one man
Half past six, the laundry truck from the wash house at record 33
Eight, breakfast in covered dishes from the kitchen at record 25 to the house, which is the use the tunnel was built for and the reason the food arrives lukewarm
Mid-morning, a delivery, the stable's coal, the gardener's flowers for the house, all of them going through rather than over
One, the servants' dinner carried the other way
Four, the laundry truck returning
Half past seven, the dinner service, which is the tunnel's busiest fifteen minutes, two footmen and a trolley moving at a near-run through eleven discs of light
The night, empty, dark, with water running in the channel
A wet February, the channel full, the discs dim, the silt building
A hot July, which is why the still-room maid sometimes left the cream in a niche
