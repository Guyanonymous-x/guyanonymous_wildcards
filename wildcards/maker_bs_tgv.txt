Styled in a design language of sleek, French, wedge-shaped, high-speed modernism of the 1980s. Pointed, sloped fronts, a long tapering top line and a tightly joined slim body. Colour is orange and silver-grey, with a bold orange lower band and a dark grey upper area. Windows are flush, surfaces are smooth, and the profile is low, streamlined and futuristic. Confident, athletic, sporty, sharply tailored and engineered for speed. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
