In the style of Snow Peak, calm Japanese minimalism with refined metal craft. Brushed titanium, satin stainless steel, dark cast iron and pale natural wood combined in slim, precise, softly rounded geometric forms that stack, nest and pack flat. Palette is silver, charcoal, black, sand, olive and off-white with very sparse colour. The emblem is a simple black asterisk-like star of radiating lines, set in plain black or white with a thin lowercase wordmark and tiny descriptive line beneath. Surfaces are matte and uncluttered, edges are thin and clean, and joints are neat and understated. Quiet, thoughtful, tidy and made to last. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Snow Peak in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
