freshly painted and bright
faded by the sun
flaking in patches
partly peeled away
streaked with dirt running down from the top
missing some of its letters
dark where a bulb has failed in the middle
stained by rust bleeding from the bolts
covered in a thin layer of grime
overpainted in places
scratched and scuffed
newly installed and spotless
wet from the rain with bright reflections
yellowed with age
hung with one letter crooked
sprayed over by graffiti
