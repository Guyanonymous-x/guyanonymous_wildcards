In the style of Gucci, bold, sumptuous, eclectic luxury. A green and red stripe band runs over beige and brown fields, with an interlocking double G monogram in repeating pattern, curved lacquered bamboo shapes and a gold horsebit bar detail. Vivid vintage floral prints with butterflies, blooms, bees, tigers and snakes appear in dense, colourful, storybook layouts. Maximalist phases add embroidery, velvet, jewel tones, clashing colour, quirky retro mixes and ornate gilt metal. Packaging is deep burgundy red or moss green with matte tonal pattern, wide-spaced thin capital lettering and ribbon. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
