at dawn, mist on the lawns, a thin white haze lying over the grass and the low brick ruins, the great stupa a soft gray shape against a pale pink and gold sky, dew shining on the lawns and the red sun just clearing the trees
in the hot afternoon, a white blazing sky, the brick ruins baked dull red and the lawns dry at the edges, heat shimmer above the paths and short dark shadows under the trees where deer and visitors rest
at dusk, butter lamps lit, hundreds of small brass cup lamps with steady yellow flames set in rows on metal racks and along the base of the stupa, their warm glow on the carved stone and on the faces of adult pilgrims, the sky deep blue above the dark tower
