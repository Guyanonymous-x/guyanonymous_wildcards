Styled in a design language of playful Italian modernism with sculptural confidence. Smooth, bold, softly rounded or crisp geometric forms in glossy moulded plastic and enamel metal, treated like art objects with sensual curves and compact proportions. Cheerful colours such as vivid red, pastel blue, mint green, lemon yellow and ivory are used in single strong tones, with black detail and small bright accents. Graphic design uses bold abstract dots, lines and colour fields on a grid. Witty, optimistic, human, refined and artistic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
