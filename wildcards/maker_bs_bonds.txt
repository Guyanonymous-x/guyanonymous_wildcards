Styled in a design language of clean, soft, honest, dependable everyday basics with a friendly sense of humour. Plain ribbed cotton jersey in white, navy, grey, pale blue and cream, deep scooped curves, stretchy simple cuts and small woven details. Soft one-piece shapes carry cheerful simple prints, stripes, polka dots, clouds, stars and small animals, in pastel yellow, mint, sky blue and pink. Lettering is flat block sans-serif in a single colour, sometimes white letters in a small blue box. Down-to-earth and reassuring. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
