Styled in a design language of mid-century Mexican modernist metalwork. Heavy hand-hammered forms with sculptural animal shapes like long-necked birds, seahorses, pelicans and fish whose bodies swell into smooth rounded volumes. Surfaces are bright polished metal with a warm slightly rippled hammered texture, set off by chunky cabochon inlays of deep green banded stone, blue lapis and azurite pressed flush into the metal. Silver married with copper and brass forms interlocking geometry, stepped-fret and feathered-serpent motifs abstracted into bold simple shapes with strong negative space. Festive, organic and luxurious, with a slightly imperfect handmade finish and visible tool marks. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
