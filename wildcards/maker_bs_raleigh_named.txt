In the style of Raleigh, English heritage craft with an ornamental metal-badge tradition. A tall, sculpted crest in brass and enamel shows an elegant long-necked heron over a looping interlaced capital initial, in red, deep blue, cream and gold cloisonne colours. Lettering is a serif capital wordmark, framed in a pointed shield or heart-like outline with a fine engraved border. Deep gloss green, black, maroon and cream enamel is lined with hand-painted gold pinstripes. Polished brass, patinated copper, riveted tan leather and chrome details give a refined, tidy, old-world, countryside gentleman feel with fine hand-finished decorative attention. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Raleigh in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
