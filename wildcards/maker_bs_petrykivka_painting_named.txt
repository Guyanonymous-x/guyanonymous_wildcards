In the style of Petrykivka Painting, lavish flat floral ornament painted freehand on white or coloured grounds. Flowers are stylised and fantastical, shown flat from above or side-on, with fern-like leaves, curling tendrils, berries, tiny grape clusters and the occasional bird. Brush strokes are visible and clearly shaped, a comb stroke, a seed, a teardrop, a tapering curl, with berries made by a fingertip dab giving a lighter centre ringed with a darker edge. Colours are strong and clear, scarlet, cobalt, magenta, orange, lemon yellow and leaf green on white, black, red, yellow or deep blue. Symmetrical, gracefully balanced, lively and celebratory. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Petrykivka Painting in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
