wheel-thrown stoneware pottery | high-fired stoneware clay with feldspar/quartz glaze | thrown on wheel, trimmed, glazed, fired in large kiln; radial symmetry only | bowls, bottles, vases, mugs, tall jars, sculptural candlesticks | thick satin or glossy glaze breaking thin over rims and ridges, bare clay foot, faint spiral throwing rings, [mem] finger-wiped glaze edges | earthy browns, oatmeal, speckled cream, iron-rust, ash green, sometimes bright colour | hand-sized to large floor jar | on wood shelf or plinth in soft daylight | glaze chips at rim, hairline cracks, utensil scratches, stained crazing | unmistakable by visible throwing rings, heavy honest walls, and glaze that thins on edges and pools in grooves | maker_ref: Leach, Rie, Coper
porcelain figurines | white translucent porcelain, often biscuit parts | moulded and hand-assembled in parts, painted overglaze, gilded edges | shepherdesses, animals, court couples, children, birds, okimono animals | smooth glossy glaze, matte biscuit areas, delicate lace-like applied detail | white, pastel pink, blue, gold trim, flesh tones | 8-40 cm | glass cabinet with spot light, light shows translucency | tiny chipped fingers/flowers, glued breaks with dark seam lines, rubbed gilding, soft-paste scratches | unmistakable by milky translucent glow, fragile fine detail, gilt rims | maker_ref: Chelsea, Nymphenburg
raku firing | low-fired earthenware or stoneware, frit or lead-free glaze | pulled glowing hot from kiln with tongs, smothered in sawdust/paper/straw for smoke reduction | tea bowls (chawan), vases, sculptural vessels, figurines | crackle web of carbon-black lines in glaze, bare clay blackened, metallic copper flashes, matte or glossy patches, horsehair squiggles, naked raku smoke smudges | black, white crackle, vivid copper green and red, cobalt blue, lustre metallic sheen | palm-sized bowl to 50 cm vase | on dark plinth, side lit to show lustre | porous so thermal shock cracks, chipped glaze, dull dried look | unmistakable by white glaze netted with black crackle and smoke-black unglazed clay | maker_ref: Raku family (Kyoto)
celadon | stoneware or porcelain with iron-bearing glaze | thick translucent glaze over carved or moulded body, reduction fired | bowls, vases, ewers, censers, water jars, plates | jade-like glassy glaze pooling deeper green in carved lines, fine crackle sometimes emphasised, unglazed foot ring or ring on base | pale blue-green, olive, grey-green, icy blue-white (qingbai), sometimes brown | 8-40 cm | on stand against neutral backdrop, soft diffused light to show depth | crackle staining, rim chips, restoration at foot | unmistakable by serene jade green, monochrome and glaze depth in relief carving | maker_ref: Longquan, Goryeo, Guan ware
majolica / maiolica | red/buff earthenware with opaque white tin glaze | paint on unfired glaze surface with cobalt, copper, manganese, antimony oxides, no correction possible, sometimes sgraffito or lustre | istoriato dishes with mythic scenes, albarelli apothecary jars, vases, fruit and flower plates | thick opaque milky-white glaze, painted colours slightly soft-edged and sunk in, lustre on some | cobalt blue, copper green, manganese purple, yellow, orange, ruby lustre | plate 25-45 cm, jar 20-30 cm | wall-hung plates or shelf jars, warm gallery light | glaze flakes at rim exposing pink clay, chips, scratches, craquelure | unmistakable by bright painted narrative scenes on thick white glaze with dark clay showing at chips | maker_ref: Gubbio lustre, Lodi, Faenza
delftware-style tin glaze | earthenware with white tin glaze plus clear overglaze | cobalt painted on tin glaze, clear glaze over for depth, mimics Chinese porcelain | plates, tulip vases, covered jars, tiles, windmill and boat scenes, chinoiserie | smooth glossy porcelain-like surface, slightly soft blue lines, sometimes pooled blue | cobalt blue on white, occasionally manganese, enamel colours and gold | tile 13 cm, plates 22-35 cm, vases to 50 cm | tile walls, dresser racks, wall plates | rim chipping showing buff clay (edge "frits"), glaze flaking, dirt in crazing | unmistakable by Dutch blue-white painting, windmills, ships and chinoiserie on white | maker_ref: Delft factories
Japanese kintsugi repair | ceramic bowl or plate with urushi lacquer seams | pieces rejoined with lacquer-putty, powdered gold/silver/platinum dusted on, cured in humid chamber | tea bowls, plates, lids, cups, filled gaps | smooth glossy raised gold veins tracing breaks, branching river-like lines, filled losses as gold patches | gold, silver, red lacquer seams on dark or pale glaze | 8-20 cm | single bowl on dark wood, spot light to catch gold glint | wear of gold on rim, seam lines bumpy, extra patch fragments mismatched | unmistakable by bright golden crack lines as celebrated feature | maker_ref: Japanese tea-ceremony tradition
slipware | red earthenware with coloured clay slips and clear lead-style glaze | slip trailed from horn/piping bag, brushed, combed, marbled, sgraffito scratched | dishes, jugs, mugs, harvest jugs, bowls | amber honey-yellow glaze over cream/brown slip lines, raised trailed ridges, scratched lines revealing red clay | cream, rust-brown, dark brown, olive green, orange-amber | 15-45 cm | farmhouse dresser, rustic table, warm lamp light | glaze wear on rim, crazing, chips, kiln-stuck pad marks | unmistakable by raised dotted and wavy slip lines glowing amber under glaze | maker_ref: Toft, Staffordshire potters
terracotta | iron-rich red-orange clay, unglazed or painted | modelled by hand, moulded, burnished, fired low; sometimes gesso and paint | figurines, busts, relief panels, roof tiles, pots, garden urns, tomb figures | matte porous warm surface, burnished sheen, fine hair and drapery detail, traces of paint | orange, brick red, buff, pink-brown, white mineral salts on surface | hand-size to near life-size | garden pots, museum plinth, raking daylight | green algae or white efflorescence, chips, cracks, rod-joined break lines, paint flakes | unmistakable by warm sun-baked orange clay and unglazed matte grain | maker_ref: Etruscan, Chinese tomb army
Greek black-figure vase (generic) | red-orange clay with glossy black slip | figures painted as black silhouettes, details incised through slip, added red and white | amphorae, hydriai, kraters, cups; myth scenes, symposia, chariots, animal friezes | glossy pitch-black slip contrasts with matte orange clay, fine scratched lines, palmette and tongue borders | black, orange, purplish red, white | 20-60 cm | museum case spot-lit, lit from above | pieced from shards with visible fill, flaked black slip, misfired grey/red patches | unmistakable by orange pot with black silhouetted figures and incised detail | maker_ref: Exekias, Amasis Painter (attributed)
blown glass vessels | molten glass, soda-lime or lead crystal | gathered on blowpipe, marvered, shaped with jacks, shears, paddles, punty | bottles, flagons, goblets, vases, bowls, perfume bottles | smooth fire-polished skin, punty scar on base, tiny seeds of bubbles, ribbing, trailing threads, optic moulded relief | clear, blue-green, cobalt, amber, ruby, colour dusted frit | 5-50 cm | windowsill, shelf backlit, glass shelf with light below | scratches, surface haze, chip at rim, iridescent weathering in ancient pieces | unmistakable by thin graceful form, glowing transmission, hand-formed asymmetry | maker_ref: Roman workshops, Dale Chihuly-style
lampwork beads | soft or borosilicate glass rods | wound around steel mandrel in torch flame, decorated with dots, stringers, fumed silver/gold, faceted | round, barrel, disc, flower and animal beads, chevron | smooth glossy, sometimes frosted sandblasted, layered chevron stripes, web effect | opaque saturated colours, silvery fumed blues/greens, gold-fumed pinks | 5-30 mm | strung, on tray or velvet, macro-lit | chips at bead hole, scratches, fuming wear | unmistakable by tiny hand-wound glossy glass with swirled dots and bead hole at both ends | maker_ref: none
cast glass | glass cast into sand, lost-wax or pate de verre mould | kiln-cast, annealed slowly, ground and polished or left mould-textured | bowls, sculptural blocks, heads, vessels with embedded objects | thick walls, matte sandy or polished faces, trapped bubbles, floating inclusions | clear, frosted, cobalt, amber, opaque pastels | 10 cm to large architectural | spot-lit on plinth to show internal glow, edge-lit | chips on edges, internal stress cracks, roman pitting | unmistakable by solid dense glass with sand-textured exterior and internal glow | maker_ref: Daum, Libensky and Brychtova
stained glass panels (craft object) | coloured pot-metal glass, lead came or copper foil | glass cut, painted with fired vitreous paint, silver stain, soldered | window panels, suncatchers, lamp shades, saints, floral and geometric | dark painted lines for faces and hair, textured cathedral glass, wavy crown glass bull's-eye, soldered lead joints | ruby red, cobalt blue, emerald, amber yellow, flesh tones | 20 cm to window-size | backlit by window or light box, coloured light patterns thrown on floor | cracked pieces, sagging, lead oxidised grey-white, rod reinforcements, putty gaps | unmistakable by thick black leaded outlines and jewel glow | maker_ref: medieval cathedral workshops, Tiffany-style
fused glass | sheet glass and frit fused in kiln | stacked layers fired to tack or full fuse, slumped over mould | dishes, bowls, plates, wall panels, pendants | smooth fire-polished top, rounded edges, slight ripple, dichroic sparkle, tack fused ridges | layered colour streaks, translucent blends | 5-60 cm | wall-mounted, light-box, on stand | chips on edge, devitrification haze, stress cracks | unmistakable by flat softly rounded colour layers with bright translucent melt | maker_ref: none
Venetian millefiori and filigrana | clear, lattimo and coloured glass canes | canes pulled, cross-sectioned or twisted (a fili, a retortoli, reticello) and fused into blown form | vases, bowls, paperweights, beads, goblets | flower-like cross-section dots, white spiral stripes in clear glass, diamond lattice with air bubbles | white, clear, red, blue, green, yellow canes | 5-30 cm | showcase with backlight | cane cracks, chips, bubble cavities | unmistakable by many tiny flower cross-sections or fine white lace threads | maker_ref: Murano glassmakers

tile mosaic Roman | stone, ceramic and glass tesserae in mortar | opus tessellatum on floors, opus vermiculatum small tesserae, set on site | geometric borders, sea monsters, gods, hunt scenes, black on white | matte flat floor surface, tight grid of tesserae, subtle shading | black, white, ochre, terracotta red, blue-grey | tesserae up to 4 mm fine, floors several metres | floor, museum wall mount, raking light | missing tesserae holes, lifted edges, patchy repairs, cracks | unmistakable by pebble-sized cubes forming flat classical figures and borders | maker_ref: Villa Romana del Casale
cloisonne | copper base with silver/gold wire and powdered glass | wire cells soldered, filled with enamel, fired repeatedly, ground and polished | vases, boxes, jewellery, icons | smooth flat glassy fill, bright metal wire lines, some convex or concave fills | cobalt blue, turquoise, red, green, white | 3-60 cm | cabinet, dark velvet, spot lit | enamel sunk or lost from cells, bubbling, dull wire | unmistakable by bright wire outlines separating glass colours | maker_ref: Byzantine, Chinese workshops
champleve enamel | copper or bronze plate, gilt | recesses cut or cast, filled with opaque enamel, fired and polished | caskets, reliquaries, crosiers, plaques, saints | gilt metal frame between blue and red enamel fields, engraved figures | blue, red, green, white, gilt | 10-40 cm | church case, spot light | enamel lost from cells, gilding rubbed, corrosion | unmistakable by engraved gilt figures in opaque enamel fields | maker_ref: Limoges, Mosan
urushi lacquerware | wood or composite with many urushi layers | layered brushed lacquer, polished, maki-e gold powder, raden shell inlay | bowls, trays, inro, boxes, tea caddies, cabinets | deep glossy mirror surface, gold dust motifs, shell flecks, mottled layers | black, cinnabar red, gold, brown, occasional green/white | 5-60 cm | on shelf with soft light | rubbed gold, worn red revealing black, hairline cracks, scratches | unmistakable by deep black glass-like gloss with gold landscape or flowers | maker_ref: Wajima, Kyoto workshops
