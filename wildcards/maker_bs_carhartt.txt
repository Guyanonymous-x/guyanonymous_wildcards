Styled in a design language of hard-wearing American utility design. Heavy, tightly woven cotton duck with a dry, slightly rough hand, finished in tobacco brown, faded tan, charcoal black, moss green and washed navy. Surfaces look broken in, with soft creasing, pale fading at the folds and honest wear. Construction is visible and reinforced, with bold triple stitching, metal rivets, boxy generous proportions and sturdy flat seams. Everything feels durable, unfussy, practical and built to be used for decades. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
