Indonesian wayang golek rod puppets, carved and painted wooden figures about as tall as an adult's arm. Each puppet has a carved wooden head painted in strong colors, a pink or red face for a hot-tempered figure and a pale gold one for a calm noble, with narrow curving eyes, a long nose and a mouth with a painted mustache. A central rod runs down through the body and comes out under the skirt as a handle, and the head turns on it. The upper body is a carved wooden torso wrapped in a velvet bodice with gold braid and sequins, and a long batik cloth of brown and indigo patterns hangs as a skirt hiding the rod. The thin carved arms are jointed at the shoulder and elbow and moved by two thin sticks of bamboo fixed to the hands. __puppet_wayang_golek_var__.
