at the pond before the shrine, a maibi in her fifties lowers a long white thread into the water and draws it out slowly, calling the god up from the water into two earthen pitchers held by men in their forties
the god, carried in two earthen pitchers wrapped in white shawls, is borne in procession to the shrine by men in their thirties to fifties in white dhotis and turbans
the maibis dance the laibou, the dance of the making of the world, their hands showing the growing of the body, the building of a house and the planting of rice
a maibi in her sixties, falling into trance, sways and speaks for the god, her eyes closed and her hands trembling over a brass plate of rice and bananas
