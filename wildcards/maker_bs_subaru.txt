Styled in a design language of practical, sturdy and adventurous design with a rally-bred streak. Shapes are boxy and honest, with squared-off volumes, flared curves, short overhangs and bold raised bulges. The emblem is a blue oval holding a cluster of six silver stars, seen in glossy chrome, flat white or embossed relief. Palette is saturated rally blue with gold accents, plus white, silver, black, forest green and warm orange. Materials are hardwearing woven fabric with bright contrast stitching, rubberised textures, matte plastic and scuffed metal. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
