Styled in a design language of English Georgian and Regency craft in rich figured mahogany, rosewood and satinwood with crisp neoclassical lines, reeded tapering supports, brass inlay, ebony stringing and ornate lion-paw feet. Leather-inlaid surfaces, sabre curves, brass fittings and deep polished red-brown glow. Formal symmetry, refined country house and gentlemen club interior, with restrained, confident, well-proportioned detail and a warm, deep gloss over every surface. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
