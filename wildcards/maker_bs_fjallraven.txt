Styled in a design language of rugged, minimal Scandinavian northern design. The emblem is a bold, flat, simplified fox head with pointed ears and a sweeping cheek line in solid red, shown alone, in a circular roundel with an outer ring of text, or in tone-on-tone grey or black. Lettering is a heavy, wide, tightly set sans-serif wordmark in capitals with dotted vowels. Materials are dense waxed cotton-poly canvas, sturdy nylon and soft wool in earthy tones of khaki, olive, forest green, navy, charcoal, sand and mustard, with occasional red, orange and pale blue accents. Shapes are simple geometric blocks with straight seams, bold colour blocking and contrast seam lines. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
