Styled in a design language of precise, driver-focused and athletic. A crisp, low, forward-leaning stance with a sharp shoulder line running end to end and a twin rounded-slot motif repeated as a face. A round blue and white quartered badge inside a black ring is the key mark. Tricolour motorsport stripes in light blue, dark blue and red run as thin parallel bands on any surface or trim. Palette is silver, black, white, deep blue and grey with sparing red. Materials are brushed metal, carbon weave, perforated leather and clean instrument-style dials angled toward the viewer. Lettering is clean, wide-set grotesque sans-serif. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
