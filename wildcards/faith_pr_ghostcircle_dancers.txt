Lakota dancers in their twenties to seventies with brown skin, long black braids and weathered faces, in ghost shirts and dresses of white muslin cloth painted with blue and red stars, crescent moons and flying eagles, fringed at the sleeves and hem, eagle feathers tied at the crown of the head
