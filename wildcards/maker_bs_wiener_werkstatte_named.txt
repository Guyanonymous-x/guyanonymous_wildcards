Styled in a design language of viennese Secession craft with geometric abstraction, strict grids of black and white squares, concentric circles, stylised roses, wavy lines and bold block-printed patterns in orange, coral, turquoise, black and gold. Pierced squares in metal, ebonised wood, enamel, elongated forms with grid-patterned surfaces, chequered bands and stylised flowers. Sophisticated, rhythmic, ornamental modernism with precise linear elegance. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
