In the style of Aston Martin, a lean British grand tourer with a long bonnet, short rear overhang, and a distinctive wide trapezoid grille with horizontal chrome or black slats, framed in a bright surround. Side strakes or gill vents behind the front wheels, a low rakish roofline falling into a fastback or kamm tail, and a muscular flowing rear haunch. Early cars use round headlamps and wire wheels, modern ones slim swept LED lamps and a crisp rising shoulder line. A winged badge sits at nose and tail. Paint in silver birch, racing green, midnight blue, Caledonian blue and gunmetal. Interior is hand-stitched leather with quilted panels, contrast piping, bridge-of-weld metal trim, polished glass switches and fine wood or carbon fibre veneer. Mood is sleek, refined, quietly aggressive.
