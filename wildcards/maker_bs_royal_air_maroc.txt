In the style of Royal Air Maroc, a Moroccan airline identity of red and green. Aircraft have a white fuselage with a vivid red tail fin carrying a green five pointed star inside a round white emblem, and a slim green and red stripe along the windows. The airline name appears in Latin and Arabic script, in bold dark red letters on the forward body. Cabins use deep red and green fabrics, geometric tile-inspired patterns in white and blue, and brass accents. Crew uniforms are smart dark red with a fine scarf and a decorative pin. Marketing images show blue skies over terracotta walls, tilework in blue, green and white, and palm trees. Mood is warm, hospitable, ornate and sunny, with red, green and white geometry. Brochures and lounge screens repeat the eight pointed star in carved wooden latticework, and ground vehicles at the airport carry a plain red band with the same green star.
