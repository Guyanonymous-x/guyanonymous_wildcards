In the style of Holden, Australian family sedans, utes and muscle coupes of the 1970s with a long bonnet, a wide split grille with a lion-and-rolling-stone badge, round or square quad headlamps, and a flared body with a pronounced waist. Ute versions have a car front with a flat cargo tray. Sports models carry a black bonnet stripe, side stripes, a boot spoiler, and a bold number badge. Wheels with steel rally design or five-bolt styled steel. Interior with bucket seats in vinyl, round gauges and a three-spoke wheel. Colours of Pimento red, Panama yellow, Sunset orange and Dark green. Tough, sunburnt and no-nonsense.
