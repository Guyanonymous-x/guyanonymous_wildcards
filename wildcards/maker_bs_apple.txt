In the style of Apple, minimalist consumer electronics with seamless slab forms. Thin rectangles with uniformly rounded corners and continuous tight radii, milled aluminium bodies in silver, space grey, gold and soft pastel anodised colours, edge-to-edge glass fronts with thin black bezels. Glossy white polycarbonate in the early 2000s, then brushed metal unibody construction. No visible screws, almost no buttons, only a few precisely cut slim keys, circular camera lenses grouped on a glass back, and a small polished logo silhouette of a bitten fruit centred on the rear. Interfaces use clean sans-serif type, generous white space, soft translucent blur layers and glossy rounded icons. Packaging is white and sparse. Mood is clean, premium and calm.
