In the style of Nambu Tekki, cast-iron kettles and teapots with a heavy, squat, rounded body, a dark matte black or rust-brown finish and a lacquered iron-oxide surface. The skin is covered in rows of raised small round studs called arare, or in smooth walls with fine texture. Details are a flat lid with a small knob in brass or iron, a swinging bail handle, a short spout, a firm foot and a thick lip. The surface looks like old weathered metal with a slight sheen, and a few forms are decorated with raised pine, plum or wave motifs. Tea-ceremony restraint, sturdiness and quiet weight define them.
