In the style of Daihatsu, tiny Japanese kei cars and mini off-roaders with a boxy rounded body, tall roof and short bonnet, round friendly headlamps with a smiling small grille and a stylised D badge. Big sliding doors on van versions, black plastic bumpers, two-tone pastel paint with white roof, small wheels with hubcaps and plastic wheel arch trim. Interior with a bench seat, column shifter, large clear dials, sometimes pastel panels. Colours of mint green, baby pink, sky blue, cream and sunny yellow. Cheerful, toylike and compact.
