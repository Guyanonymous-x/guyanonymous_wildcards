In the style of Manish Malhotra, glamorous Bollywood-style Indian couture, with lavishly embellished lehengas, saris and gowns in sheer net, tulle, georgette and silk. Heavily sparkling surfaces of sequins, crystals, beads, pearls, zardozi and gold or silver thread embroidery cover bodices, borders and trailing capes. Palette includes champagne, blush pink, ivory, emerald, wine red, midnight blue, black and metallic gold. Silhouettes are body-hugging with plunging necklines, corset-like blouses, structured sleeves and dramatic flared skirts and long trains. Glossy, sensual, red-carpet mood with high-contrast studio lighting and shimmering texture, accessorised with ornate gold jewellery.
