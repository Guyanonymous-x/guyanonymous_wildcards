Styled in a design language of passionate, theatrical Italian speed. Long, low, wedge-sharp or voluptuous sculpted forms with sensual curves, flowing fenders, sharp creases, wide stance and dramatic intakes. Deep, glossy rosso red dominates, joined by yellow, black, ivory and silver, with a prancing horse emblem on a yellow shield and thin racing stripes. Surfaces are lacquer-bright with chrome, carbon, polished metal and tan leather. Lettering is elegant, slanted, flowing script and bold racing capitals. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
