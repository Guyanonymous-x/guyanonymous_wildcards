In the style of Royal Worcester, English porcelain with hand-painted naturalistic subjects such as fruit, fallen blossoms, game birds, highland cattle and landscapes on an ivory ground, framed by raised gilding, jewelled enamel dots and pierced reticulated edges. Rich cream and blush ivory glaze, gold scrolling, oriental-inspired shapes, shell forms, twisting branch curves and delicate floral sprays. Finely detailed Victorian elegance.
