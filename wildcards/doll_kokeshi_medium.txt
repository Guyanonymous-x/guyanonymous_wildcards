Japanese kokeshi dolls, simple turned wooden dolls, each a round head on a plain cylinder body, the tallest about as tall as an adult's forearm. Each doll is a smooth cylinder of pale wood for the body, topped by a large round head, turned on a lathe so the wood grain runs in fine rings. The faces are painted with a few fine strokes of black, two narrow curved eyes, small brows and a tiny red mouth, with black painted hair in bangs. __doll_kokeshi_var__.
