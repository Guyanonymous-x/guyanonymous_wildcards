Styled in a design language of dainty, sugary Second Empire Parisian elegance. Pastel colours in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow, powder blue and coffee brown are laid out in soft ombre blocks. Fine gilt edges, satin ribbon, tissue wrapping and delicate arabesque trellis and floral lattice patterns in cream and gold give a fine, porcelain-like finish. Small cherub and garland motifs, scalloped borders, matte velvety coatings and soft pearly sheen keep the mood feminine and polished. Sweet, precious, ladylike and gently nostalgic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
