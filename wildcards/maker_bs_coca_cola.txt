Styled in a design language of joyful, refreshing, nostalgic Americana. Dominant saturated red paired with clean white, used in broad flat fields with a flowing white ribbon wave. Lettering is a looping, handwritten-looking script with generous swashes, balanced by simple bold sans-serif support text. Shapes are curvy, contoured and hourglass-like, with glossy glass, polished chrome and condensation droplets. Illustration and photography are warm, sunny and optimistic, showing smiling people, sparkle, ice and bubbles. Round discs and badge shapes, vintage enamel sign charm, and a timeless feel-good, shareable cheer.
