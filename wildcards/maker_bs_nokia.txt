Styled in a design language of tough, friendly and tactile Nordic design. Chunky rounded candy-bar forms in blue-grey, black, silver or bright interchangeable coloured plastic shells, with soft rubbery texture, smooth pebbled curves and fine seam lines. Green-tinted monochrome pixel graphics with a friendly rounded font and simple icon imagery. Durable, reliable, playful and accessible. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
