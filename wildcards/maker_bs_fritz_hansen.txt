Styled in a design language of Danish modern design with sculptural moulded laminated wood in curvy ant, butterfly or egg silhouettes, with a pinched waist and no visible join between parts. Slim chromed steel tube supports on three or four points or a spiderlike cast aluminium base, tight leather or tweed wool upholstery, and coloured lacquered veneer in black, white, lime green, deep red and warm walnut. Surfaces are glossy and smooth with generous softness. Elegant, airy, organic in flow and never fussy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
