Styled in a design language of friendly vintage Americana outdoor utility. Baked enamel in sage and olive green with cream and tan trim, finished in a slightly worn painted-steel look with small dents and rust freckles. Rounded steel edges, sturdy rolled seams, brass and chrome details and khaki canvas in heavy weave. Secondary graphics are simple green and cream box colour-blocking with small red accents. Honest, dependable, nostalgic, family-friendly and built to be used hard. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
