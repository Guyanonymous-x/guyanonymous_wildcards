Styled in a design language of austere, volcanic, windy and functional Icelandic weatherproof design. Bold, practical, enveloping volumes with overlapping layers, visible seams, crisp adjustable edges and generous scale, in matte waterproof surfaces in strong colours such as bright red, orange, deep blue, black, moss green and ochre. Surfaces use long parallel channels and stranded patterns in grey, cream and charcoal. Proportions are long, broad and roomy, like arctic working gear refined into minimalism. Subtle sheen under rain and chunky, oversized detailing. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
