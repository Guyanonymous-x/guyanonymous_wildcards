Styled in a design language of American utilitarian craft in pine, maple and cherry with plain, tapered, turned legs, ladder-like rhythm with woven tape, delicate swallowtail joints and plain painted finishes in barn red, mustard yellow, blue-green and milk-paint grey. Clean lines, no ornament, honest dovetails, spare, serene and tidy, with sunlight and handmade perfection, slender, carefully balanced proportions and a quiet, humble dignity in every joint. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
