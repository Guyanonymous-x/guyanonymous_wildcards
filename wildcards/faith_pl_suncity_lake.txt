a frozen lake in winter, a flat white plain of snow-covered ice stretching between dark forested hills, a single trail of footprints and sled runners crossing it in a straight line, a round fishing hole cut through the blue-green ice near the shore and the bare birches along the bank coated white with frost
a wooden pier on the lake, a narrow walkway of gray weathered planks on rough log posts reaching out from the stony shore over still dark water, two small flat-bottomed rowboats tied beside it with their oars laid inside, tall reeds at its foot and the forested hills of the far bank reflected beyond its end
