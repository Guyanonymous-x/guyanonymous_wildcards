In the style of the Polish FSO Syrena. This is a small, upright, rounded two-door saloon from the 1950s and 1960s with a bulbous bonnet, a smooth two-box body, a notched or fastback tail and separate bulging front wings. The first version has a high slim roof, small round headlamps flush with the body, a short plain chrome grille with a few horizontal bars, thin chrome bumpers, small door handles and rear-hinged suicide doors. Later versions have a restyled front with a squarer grille, bigger lights, a lowered waistline and a larger rear window. Paint is plain glossy pastel, such as cream, sky blue, pistachio green, pale grey, light red and mustard, with chrome trim along the flanks and a small crest on the nose. The interior has a bench seat, a simple metal dash, a big thin steering wheel and a small speedometer. The mood is modest, cute and period-correct socialist-era motoring.
