the cards and the camera, which is a rule nobody breaks and is the clearest expression of what matters on a tour

a liter of water filtered and set aside for the morning so that nobody has to deal with it before coffee
the day's clothes folded into a pillow because a pillow is two hundred grams nobody will carry
cycling shoes outside and camp sandals at the porch edge, which is the single greatest luxury on a tour and weighs very little
a chain tool and a folded spare tire in a rear pannier, which are the two failures that would otherwise end the trip
