Styled in a design language of hospitable, bright, tidy, sparkling domestic glass with an Ottoman flavour. Small, slim, waisted forms with a flared lip and a gently pinched middle, clear and thin. Larger pieces have cut and pressed patterns such as diamond facets, rosettes, fine vertical flutes and rows of tiny dots, with domed tops and tall necks. Coloured glass in cobalt blue, emerald green, amber and ruby red carries gilded rims and enamelled floral motifs of tulips, carnations and cypress in gold and white. Heavy rounded bases anchor the forms. Glassy reflections, warm light, patterned cloth and brass in the background. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
