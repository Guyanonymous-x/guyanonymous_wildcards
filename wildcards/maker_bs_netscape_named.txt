Styled in a design language of early internet design - grey beveled windows and chunky square buttons, a purple and blue night-sky colour theme, a bright green-glowing screen, a CRT monitor in beige, the web in tiled backgrounds with deep blue links and a rotating planet motif. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
