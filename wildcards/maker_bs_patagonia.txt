Styled in a design language of honest outdoor utility with an earthy, conscientious spirit. Functional, roomy, rounded shapes with practical seams, tough stitching and unpretentious proportions. Colour blocking uses bright primaries such as red, royal blue, yellow and teal against natural fawn, forest green, rust and navy, often with vintage faded tones. Textures are brushed fleece, ripstop, canvas, wool and recycled fabric with a soft worn look. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
