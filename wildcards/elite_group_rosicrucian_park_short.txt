Rosicrucian Park in San Jose, California, the grounds of a modern Rosicrucian order built in an ancient Egyptian style. A city block of gardens is set with buildings in an ancient Egyptian style, their walls sloping inward, with tall square gateways, painted columns topped with carved papyrus flowers and a museum front modeled on the gates of the great temple of Karnak. __elite_group_rosicrucian_park_place__
