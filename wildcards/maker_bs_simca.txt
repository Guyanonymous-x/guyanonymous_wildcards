In the style of Simca in the 1950s and 1960s, a French pontoon-style saloon or coupe with a rounded body, small tail fins, a wide chrome grille with a wide mouth, round headlamps, and a wide wraparound windscreen. Chrome trim strips run along the flanks, a rounded tail with chrome bumpers, and two-tone paint in pastel shades. A swallow badge on the nose. Wheels are steel with chrome hubcaps and whitewall tyres. Paint in cream over sea green, pink over grey, turquoise, and sand. Interior has bench seats in vinyl and cloth, a painted dash with chrome-ringed dials, and a large white wheel. Mood is jaunty, post-war and optimistic.
