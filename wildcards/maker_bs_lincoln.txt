In the style of Lincoln, 1960s American flagship luxury sedans with a slab-sided, perfectly flat body, a thin knife-edge beltline and a restrained bright grille of fine horizontal bars. Rear-hinged suicide doors, a long level hood, a square formal roofline, four-door convertible tops, and a star badge in a plain rectangle. Minimal chrome, just a thin strip along the shoulder and ringed bumpers. Slim vertical tail lamps and plain wheel covers. Inside, a flat dashboard with brushed metal, deep leather and a feeling of grave dignity. Colours of Presidential black, midnight blue, silver and white. Understated, sober, expensive.
