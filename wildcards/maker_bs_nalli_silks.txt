Styled in a design language of heavy hand-woven pure silk with rich lustre, a broad sharply contrasting border, a separately decorated end zone and woven gold or silver thread motifs. Motifs include temple towers, checks, peacocks, parrots, elephants, mango paisley, lotus flowers and fine vertical stripes. Colours are vivid and deep, such as peacock blue, magenta, parrot green, mustard, maroon, saffron and black with gold. The cloth is thick, stiff, glossy and falls in heavy pleats, seen in bright clear light.
