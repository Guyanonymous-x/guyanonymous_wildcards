In the style of Heinz, heritage American pantry design with tidy, symmetrical layouts. A keystone-shaped cream panel with a tomato-red border and a bold vertical stripe of red carries white or cream serif lettering. Decorative touches include ornate serif capitals, banner ribbons, fine print and a small pickle-shaped emblem. Forms are tall and narrow with long necks and faceted shoulders. A vivid blue stripe accents the range. Palette is tomato red, cream, gold and blue. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
