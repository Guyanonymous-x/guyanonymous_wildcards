Half past four on a shooting morning, the building empty and swept, the tally slate wiped
The day, the keepers in the field
Four in the afternoon, the day's work, the bag brought in on the game cart, counted, the tally chalked by species, the birds braced and hung on the ring and the rails, which for 300 birds is an hour and a half's work by two men and fills the building to its working capacity
Five, the ledger written up and the totals carried forward
The following morning, the ring turned to bring yesterday's birds round and the oldest taken down for the house and for the dealer
The next fortnight, nothing, the building simply hangs, and the whole point of it is that nobody does anything, which makes it the one working building in section C whose main function is to be left alone
A Tuesday and a Friday, the dealer's cart
February, the season over and the building empty, washed out, the louvres looked at and the hooks greased
