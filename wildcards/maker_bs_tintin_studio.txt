Styled after the clear line school with uniform thin black outlines, flat cheerful fills and no hatching. Meticulously accurate backgrounds drawn with research and care, characters with round simple faces, dot eyes and an upswept tuft of hair, a limited palette of sea blue, sand yellow, warm red and green, stage-like compositions. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
