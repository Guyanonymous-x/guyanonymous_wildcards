FORMS
marble carving | white or lightly veined crystalline stone, slightly translucent, solid not hollow | point and claw chisels, rasps, rifflers, abrasives, tin-oxide polish; rough facets give way to fine rasp strokes | idealised nude or draped figures, portraits, busts, fold-heavy drapery, hair in fine curls | soft glowing skin-like polish on flesh, toothed parallel gouge lines or rasp sweeps left in unpolished areas, crisp drapery grooves, support block hidden in thick drapery or a tree stump [mem] | pure white to cream, faint grey veins, occasional yellow-brown handling stains | tabletop bust to heroic life-size and larger | pedestal in gallery with raking side light so the stone seems to glow; garden settings outdoors | grey dulled surface, sugaring and pitting from acid rain, yellow-brown stains, broken noses/fingers, lost paint | translucent glow and smooth flesh against rough chiselled base | maker_ref: none
granite carving | coarse speckled hard igneous stone | machine-cut, sandblasted through stencils, or hand-dressed; limited fine hand detail | monuments, tombstones, blocky memorials, lettering, sarcophagi, large seated figures | mix of mirror-polished faces and rough-dressed or flame-textured faces, crisp lettering sandblasted into polish | pink, grey, white with black speckle (mica, hornblende); "black granite" is darker | headstone to monument | outdoor plinth, cemetery, plaza, cold flat daylight | layered peeling (exfoliation), lichen, softened lettering, grey grit | polished face next to rough face, glittering crystal speckle | maker_ref: none
limestone carving | pale fine-grained sedimentary stone, can show fossils | chisel and mallet, easy to cut in detail | architectural ornament, saints, panels, lintels with narrative scenes, gargoyles, facade statues | matte to lightly smooth, fine chisel facets, porous | white to grey to buff, with yellow, red or near-black tints from impurities | niche statue to wall panel | on buildings, in niches, in raking sun | acid-rain erosion, blackened crust, softened features, graffiti stains, fossil shells showing through | creamy matte stone, weathered facade statuary | maker_ref: Maya lintel carvers
soapstone carving | soft talc-rich stone, soapy to touch | knives, rasps, files, sandpaper; carved fast, sometimes heat-hardened | small animals, bowls, lamps, pipes, seals, amulets, figurines | smooth waxy satin sheen, semi-polished or highly polished, slick feel | grey, green-grey, blue-grey, dark; sometimes glazed | handheld to small tabletop | shelf or tabletop, side light | scratches easily, dark oily patina builds with handling, chips | waxy dark grey carved bowls and animals | maker_ref: none
alabaster carving | soft white to cream banded stone, translucent | rasps, chisels, scrapers, polishing; sometimes heated or dyed | lamps, vases, urns, tomb effigies, low reliefs, small figures | smooth glassy polish that can go chalky | white, cream, brown clouding and veining, honey bands, dyed shades | vase to life-size effigy | lit from behind so it glows; indoors only | dull whitish surface, scratches (soft), dissolves in water, chalky overheated patches | warm glow when backlit | maker_ref: none
bronze casting (lost wax) | cast copper-tin alloy, usually hollow | wax model, ceramic shell, pour, chase seams, weld sections, hand-finish | figures, animals, equestrian statues, portrait busts, war memorials | seams and sprue stubs ground away, fine chased detail, occasional repaired pits, patina over polish, rubbed highlights | golden metal under chemical patina in brown, green, blue-black, red, yellow-brown; ormolu gilt matte gold | small statuette to monument | stone plinth, outdoors in plazas or gallery under spotlights; highlights glint on raised edges | green verdigris streaks, rain runs, bright rubbed spots where touched, dents, theft cuts | thin supports under heavy forms; mixed brown and green patina | maker_ref: none
cast iron | grey iron, dark and heavy, brittle | sand casting, bolted sections | garden ornaments, fountains, benches, railings, decorative facades, statues | matte dark grey, rough sand-cast texture, visible casting lines | black, painted dark green or black, orange rust | garden figure to facade | outdoors, painted | rust bloom, flaking paint, cracks since brittle | black fussy ornament with orange rust | maker_ref: none
welded steel sculpture | steel plates, bars, beams, found scrap | torch cutting, welding, grinding, sometimes digital cutting | abstract beams and planes, balanced angular masses, monumental site-specific works | visible weld beads, flame-cut edges, rust, paint, burnished sections | rust orange-brown, black, primary paint colours, bare grey steel | large to monumental | outdoor plaza or gallery floor | rust staining, peeling paint, rust runs onto base | exposed welds and heavy industrial stock | maker_ref: David Smith, Anthony Caro, Richard Serra
wire sculpture | bent, crocheted or woven metal wire | bending, looping, tying, crocheting | continuous-line figures, caricatures, animals, hanging looping forms, trees with roots | transparent line-drawing in space, cast shadows, loop texture | silver, copper, galvanised grey, black | tabletop to ceiling | hung or on stand with light behind to throw shadows on wall | bent out of shape, tarnish, rust | drawing in space with see-through volume | maker_ref: Alexander Calder, Ruth Asawa
wood carving (figure/relief) | basswood, walnut, oak, pine, box and other woods | gouges, chisels, mallet, V-tools, rasps, sandpaper | figures, relief panels, lovespoons, ornament, animals | shallow gouge facets left as texture, or sanded smooth; oil or wax sheen, avoid glossy varnish | natural browns, honey to dark; painted details | handheld to life-size | indoors on stand or wall; raking light shows tool facets | cracks along grain, broken fine tips (across grain), insect holes, rot outdoors | grain showing through form with crisp gouge scallops | maker_ref: none
chainsaw carving | log, usually softwood | chainsaw with small-nose bar, plus mallets and gouges | bears, owls, eagles, acorns, Virgin Mary figures, zodiac animals | fuzzy slashes, striated saw lines, grooved fur, rounded forms | pale to orange timber, scorched darker bits [mem], sometimes stained | knee-height to several metres | outdoor park, roadside, on log stump base | split checks, gray weathering, moss, rot at base | bear with grooved fur | maker_ref: Harry Thomas
totem pole | tall cedar trunk | adze and knife, painted after carving | stacked animals, birds, humans, spirit beings; raven with straight beak, eagle with curved beak, beaver with two big front teeth | rounded deep carving, flat painted details | red, black, and teal-green painted features on silvery wood [mem] | 2 to 18 m, rarely taller | upright in village, park or museum hall | grey cracked wood, lean, rot at base, faded paint | stacked interlocking faces | maker_ref: Pacific Northwest carvers
whittling | basswood or other softwood | pocket or fixed-blade knife; thumb guards | small animals, figures, spoons, walking-stick toppers | clear flat knife facets, no sanding | pale wood, honey | palm-sized | shelf | cracks, dark with handling | faceted look | maker_ref: none
bone and horn carving | bone, antler, horn, baleen | knife, graver, drill, ink or pigment | plaques, small figures, combs, scrimshaw etched images | less glossy than ivory, creamy, engraved lines filled dark | cream, tan, yellow-brown, ink-black lines | thumb-sized to hand-sized | cased with pinpoint light | yellowing, cracks, flaking | engraved dark lines on cream | maker_ref: none
clay modelling and terracotta | fired earth clay | hand modelling with fingers and tools, fired near 1000 C, painted or glazed | portrait busts, relief roundels, figures, architectural ornament | fingers and tool marks visible, burnished or glazed or painted | red, orange, buff, pink, grey-brown, glazed colours | small to just under life size | indoors or sheltered niche | cracks, sagging, lost paint, salt bloom | warm orange earth colour with soft modelled fingerprints | maker_ref: Luca della Robbia

kinetic mobile | sheet metal shapes, wire, rods | cutting, painting, balancing | abstract leaves and discs on arms | thin flat painted shapes | bright primaries, black, white | palm-size to room-size | hung from ceiling, shadows on wall | bent arms, paint chips | balanced hanging abstract shapes | maker_ref: Alexander Calder, Bruno Munari

tool marks: claw chisel parallel lines, rasp sweeps, point-chisel pits, gouge scallops, knife facets, chainsaw striations, weld beads, flame-cut edges, adze marks, finger prints in clay, chasing lines, ground sprue scars, V-tool lines
