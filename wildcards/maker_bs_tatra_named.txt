Styled in a design language of streamlined, futuristic, odd and aerodynamic. A long smooth teardrop body, a central spine-like fin running along the back, a tapering end with rows of cooling louvres, smooth flush sides and curved skirted forms. Disc patterns with chrome caps and bright trim used sparingly. Dark green, black, metallic grey and burgundy. Wide soft cloth or leather and warm wooden panelling with simple round faces. Eccentric, engineered and slightly strange. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
