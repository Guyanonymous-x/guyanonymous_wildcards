the women dancers in their twenties to forties wear the ngepan, the Iban festival dress, a tall silver crown of upright combs and dangling silver flowers on the head, a corset of many stacked silver rings around the waist and a short skirt of pua kumbu, a hand-woven cloth of red, black and cream patterns
women in their fifties and sixties sit along the gallery in sarongs and lace blouses, silver coin belts at the waist and beaded collars of red, yellow and black seed beads spread over their shoulders
a woman in her thirties dances the women's ngajat alone, arms held out with fingers curling, in a red pua kumbu skirt, a silver crown and a broad collar of tiny glass beads, and she turns slowly on bare feet
