In the style of Orrefors, Swedish art glass, thick, crystal-clear and heavy-walled, with deep engraved or wheel-cut scenes, ethereal trapped air bubbles and abstract wavy colour patterns inside the material. Forms are streamlined, tapering and smooth, in a cool transparent palette with accents of slate blue, smoky grey and soft green. Understated, refined, sculptural Scandinavian modernism, pure and sparkling in cold light. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Orrefors in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
