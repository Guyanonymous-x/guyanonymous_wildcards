In the style of Great Western Railway, proud Victorian-inspired engineering elegance. Gleaming deep green paint with orange and black lining, polished brass and copper accents, black dark-toned ends and red bases. Tall brass-edged nameplates sit on curved forms. Secondary colours are cream and chocolate brown. Metal is hand-polished, lines are fine and hand-drawn, and every part is crafted to be admired. Stately, dignified, traditional and richly finished.
In the style of Shinkansen, sleek, precise, futuristic Japanese engineering expressed through clean colour blocking. Glossy pearl white and pale grey surfaces are cut by a single long, tapering stripe in deep cobalt or navy, sometimes joined by a thin line of sky blue, green, red or gold. Shapes are streamlined, with long smooth curves, softly pointed tips and seamless panel joins that feel aerodynamic. Finishes are lacquer-like, satin and flawless, with pale aluminium accents. Lettering is neat, geometric sans-serif in both Latin and Japanese characters, aligned on strict grids with calm, even spacing. Small circular and wing-like emblems appear in crisp flat vector form, with a feeling of speed, order, safety and quiet confidence.
In the style of TGV, sleek, French, wedge-shaped, high-speed modernism of the 1980s. Pointed, sloped fronts, a long tapering top line and a tightly joined slim body. Colour is orange and silver-grey, with a bold orange lower band and a dark grey upper area. Windows are flush, surfaces are smooth, and the profile is low, streamlined and futuristic. Confident, athletic, sporty, sharply tailored and engineered for speed.
In the style of Orient Express, glamorous 1920s and 1930s European luxury travel. Deep midnight blue and cream with gold lettering and fine gold lines, polished walnut and mahogany marquetry, etched glass, brass fittings and plush velvet in claret and sapphire. Art Deco geometry with stepped borders, sunbursts and elegant stylised lettering. Soft lamplight, crisp white linen and silver service, a hush of mystery, decadence and cosmopolitan elegance.
In the style of Pullman, formal Gilded Age elegance in luxury travel of the early 1900s. Deep dark green paint with elaborate gold-leaf lettering and lines, arched forms, small high openings and open railings. Walnut with green or burgundy plush upholstery, brass fittings and gaslight-style lamps. Heavy, solid, stately forms. Ornate, rich, ceremonial, hand-gilded and imposing, like a rolling grand hotel.
