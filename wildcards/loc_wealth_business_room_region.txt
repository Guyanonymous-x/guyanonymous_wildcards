the English and Welsh estate business room as the type, with the rent table as its signature object and rent days at Lady Day and Michaelmas
the Scottish version, the factor's room, with the same arrangement and a harder reputation
the Irish big-house version, where the same room, the same rail and the same two publics carry a political weight that no English example does and where the agent is the most exposed person on the estate, which should be stated rather than implied
the continental European version, attached to a bailiff's house rather than the main building
the Prussian and Polish manorial office, which does the same job for a far larger labor force
the Russian estate office before 1861, which administers people as property and is a different institution despite an identical set of furniture; and the American plantation office, which likewise
