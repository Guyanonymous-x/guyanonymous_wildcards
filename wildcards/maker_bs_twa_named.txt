Styled in a design language of American jet-age air travel in red and white with a silver aircraft body, a red stripe and bold curved lines. The curved, futuristic terminal with sweeping white concrete and red carpet, round white chairs and sunken lounges, red and white flight bags and crisp stewardess uniforms in red. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
