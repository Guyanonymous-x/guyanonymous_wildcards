In the style of Batik Keris, wax-resist dyed cotton and silk in dense repeating patterns of parang diagonal waves, overlapping circles, cloud swirls, floral sprays, birds and trailing vines. Typical colours are warm sogan brown, deep indigo, dark black, cream and golden yellow, plus brighter coastal tones in red, turquoise and green. Fine crackle lines show where the wax cracked, and slight irregularities reveal the hand-drawn pen or copper stamp. Formal, refined, ceremonial mood, with a draped, flowing quality. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Batik Keris in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
