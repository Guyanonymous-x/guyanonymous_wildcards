Styled in a design language of passionate, graceful Italian sporting elegance. Slim, low, curved forms with long tapering lines and a triangular shield motif. Palette of rich red, cream paper, white, deep blue, silver, black and dark green. Materials include polished chrome, drilled aluminium, wood-rimmed details and rich leather. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
