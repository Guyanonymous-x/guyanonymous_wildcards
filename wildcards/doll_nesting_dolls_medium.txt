a set of Russian nesting dolls, matryoshka, wooden dolls that fit one inside another. The largest doll is a smooth round-bodied figure of turned wood about as tall as an adult's hand, painted as a woman in a headscarf, and each one splits at the waist to hold the next smaller one, down to a tiny solid doll the size of a fingertip. They stand in a row from largest to smallest. __doll_nesting_dolls_var__.
