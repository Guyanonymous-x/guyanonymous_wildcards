In the style of Ando Cloisonne, smooth, glossy enamel fields held by hair-thin silver or brass wires. Colours are deep and jewel-like, including midnight blue, black, wisteria violet, jade green, ivory and warm gold, with a mirror finish and no visible wire thickness. Subjects are delicate birds, hydrangea, wisteria, irises, chrysanthemums, butterflies and tiny geometric brocade borders, often floating on a plain black or blue ground with generous empty space. Wireless techniques create soft gradients in petals and feathers. Forms are slender, with narrow necks, flared openings and silver rims.
In the style of Roycroft Arts and Crafts Community, plain handmade rectilinear construction in oak and hammered copper. Wood has square, sturdy members, exposed joinery, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish with visible grain. Copper shows hammer marks, rivets, strap-like fittings and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic.
In the style of Guild of Handicraft, Arts and Crafts silver, enamel and jewellery in a hand-wrought manner. Hammered metal shows visible tool marks, curved lines, wire twists and loose, flowing Art Nouveau lines. Peacocks, ships, tendrils, stylised flowers and hearts are set with cabochon moonstones, opals, turquoise or blister pearls and translucent coloured enamel in green, blue and rose. Objects look soft and slightly irregular, with a matte, pale silver surface and delicate suspended drops. The mood is romantic, medieval-influenced, simple and craftsmanlike.
In the style of Palekh Lacquer Miniatures, deep lustrous black glossy ground painted in egg tempera with fairy tale scenes. The palette is bright red, gold, emerald green, white and orange against black, with fine gold highlights outlining draperies and ornamental borders of tiny patterns. Red lacquer provides contrast. Details are miniature and precise, as in an icon, and the mood is mythical, jewel-like and storybook.
In the style of Faberge, Imperial Russian jewelled preciousness, especially egg-like forms in layered translucent guilloche enamel in pastel pink, powder blue, mint green, lilac and cream over engraved sunburst patterns, framed by gold, pearls, diamonds and rubies. Miniature hidden surprises, hardstone carving in jade, agate and rock crystal, and delicate gilded neoclassical garlands, bows and laurel wreaths. Opulent, exact, royal treasury glitter.
In the style of Wiener Werkstatte, Viennese Secession craft with geometric abstraction, strict grids of black and white squares, concentric circles, stylised roses, wavy lines and bold block-printed patterns in orange, coral, turquoise, black and gold. Pierced squares in metal, ebonised wood, enamel, elongated forms with grid-patterned surfaces, chequered bands and stylised flowers. Sophisticated, rhythmic, ornamental modernism with precise linear elegance.
