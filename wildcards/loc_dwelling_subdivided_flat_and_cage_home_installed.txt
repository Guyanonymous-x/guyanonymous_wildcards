a legal flat divided
plywood or block added to a normal flat to create five or six separately let rooms
services split by sub-meter so each tenant pays a premium for electricity
sanitary provision reduced to one combined shower and lavatory shared by all occupants
ventilation and daylight absent from most rooms
fire escape compromised by the partitions and by the single exit
a structural loading and drainage system
occupancy by families as well as single people
frequently unwritten and subject to immediate rent rises
