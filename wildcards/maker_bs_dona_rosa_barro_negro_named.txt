Styled in a design language of hand-built forms with a deep, mirror-like metallic black sheen. Shapes are formed by hand with no turning, so they are softly rounded and slightly asymmetrical, with tall proportions, round bellies, narrow necks and playful figure shapes. The surface is rubbed smooth with a stone before smoke firing. Decoration is restrained, with applied flowers, rosettes, twisted ropes, delicate pierced lacework and fine incised lines. Earthy, sculptural and quietly elegant, on plain pale backgrounds. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
