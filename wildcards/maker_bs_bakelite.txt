Styled in a design language of early plastic household objects in deep glossy brown, mottled amber, ivory and green, with smooth rounded forms and moulded ridges, such as telephones, radios, clocks, ashtrays and switches, with a dense polished surface. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
