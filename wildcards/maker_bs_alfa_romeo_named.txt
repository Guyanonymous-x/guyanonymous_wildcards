In the style of Alfa Romeo, passionate, lively and graceful Italian sporting design. A low slim body with a distinctive triangular shield shape flanked by mesh, a circular emblem with a red cross and crowned serpent, rounded forms under glass, a long mass and pointed tapering end. Chrome edges. Alfa red, white, light blue, silver and dark green. Bucket forms, wood and drilled aluminium and deep-set round faces add a racing note.
