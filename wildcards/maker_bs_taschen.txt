Styled in a design language of bold, oversized and confidently sensual graphic design with a gallery-grade polish. Forms are large clean rectangles with crisp square edges and generous margins. Surfaces are thick matte or satin boards, linen, leatherette and glossy printed art, with foil stamping and embossing used sparingly. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
