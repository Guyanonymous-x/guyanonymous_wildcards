on a clear morning in May, soft warm light on the pale stone, tulips and pansies bright in the flower beds and the gilded angel flashing against a blue sky
at midday in July, strong white light, the temple walls dazzling, deep green lawns and the sharp shadow of the spire lying across the path
at dusk in October, the sky deep blue and rose, the temple floodlit white from below so that it seems to glow, the windows warm gold and the trees turning red and yellow
on a December evening, snow on the lawns and the roof edges, every tree on the grounds strung with thousands of tiny white and colored lights, the lit spire rising into a black sky
