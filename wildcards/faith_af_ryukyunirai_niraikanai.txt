Nirai Kanai, a shining land far across the turquoise sea to the east, low islands of white sand and green trees floating in gold light on the horizon
the gods of Nirai Kanai, figures of men and women in their forties to sixties in white robes, come over the sea toward the shore in a boat of light to bring rice and plenty
the souls of the dead, pale figures of men and women in their forties to nineties in white, walk out over the waves toward a gold glow on the eastern horizon
