In the style of Arnotts, Australian biscuit packaging. The brand mark is a multi-coloured parrot perched on a T-shaped stand eating a biscuit, drawn in a late nineteenth-century engraving style with a red, yellow, green and blue plumage, inside a red oval or circle. Boxes and wrappers are bright and cheerful, a red base for chocolate biscuits, yellow for sweet ones and blue or green for crackers, with a bold white block rounded sans-serif title, large appetising photos of the biscuits and a swirl or ribbon behind. Biscuits themselves are distinctive shapes, a rectangular two-layer chocolate-coated finger with a pattern of fine diagonal ridges, round crackers with small holes in a neat grid, square plain crackers, teddy-bear shapes, round cream sandwiches and a large round orange-sectioned disc with sugar frosting. Tins are tall rectangular metallic boxes with printed scenes. The mood is homely, nostalgic and cheerful, a family lunchbox feel.
