the temple terrace on top of the mound, a broad flat platform held up by a curving stone retaining wall, the wall a long sweep of rough gray boulders stacked several courses high and leaning slightly back into the slope, the level top above it a wide oval of packed brown earth and dry grass, raised high over the wheat plain so that the whole round horizon shows beyond its rim
the ruins of the temple on the terrace, low walls of mud brick around a single room, a base for the god's statue, the brick stumps standing only knee high on footings of gray fieldstone, their faces eroded into soft rounded ridges of tan clay, the floor inside of hard beaten earth, and against the back wall a low square block of stone slabs where the image of the storm god Kumarbi once stood
