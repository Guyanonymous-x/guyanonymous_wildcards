In the style of Hyundai, futuristic, digital, geometric design. A slanted letter H inside an oval is drawn as a slim, cleanly spaced outline, and a newer version builds a tall H from sharp, parametric strokes. Pixel motifs recur everywhere, in small square dot grids, blocky eight-bit letterforms and chunky mosaic patterns. Surfaces are taut and folded like sharp paper origami, with crisp creases and flush smooth planes. Palette is deep navy, cool silver, glossy white, graphite, with neon accents of magenta, electric cyan and sunset orange that glow. Finishes are satin metal, translucent smoked glass and matte ceramic. Lettering is clean, wide, thin geometric sans-serif capitals. Overall, a clean, optimistic, high-tech and slightly playful look.
