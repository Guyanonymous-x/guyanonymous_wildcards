Styled in a design language of refined thin white porcelain with a milky body, clear sparkling glaze and a great deal of generous empty space. Enamelled touches of red, green, yellow and sky blue are used sparingly. Outlines are elegant and crisp, including faceted and lobed edges. Airy, graceful, calm and exact, like quiet tea house tableware. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
