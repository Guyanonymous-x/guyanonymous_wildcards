Styled in a design language of English neoclassical ceramics with refined, timeless elegance. Surfaces are matte, fine-grained stoneware in a signature soft powder blue, with crisp white relief ornaments that stand out in delicate raised detail. Alternate colourways include sage green, lilac, black, cream and pale pink, plus bone china in ivory with gilded rims and delicate floral sprays. Shapes are symmetrical, balanced and gently classical, with fluted lines. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
