Cadborosaurus, the sea serpent of the Pacific coast, a long serpentine sea animal with a camel-like or horse-like head on a long neck, brownish-grey skin, with humps along the back, small front flippers and a fan-shaped tail, its humps breaking the water off a forested coast at dawn, its long neck rising high above the water, a camel-like head with large dark eyes, humps of its body behind, swimming in a grey Pacific bay with pine-covered hills behind
