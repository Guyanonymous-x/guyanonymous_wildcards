Styled in a design language of voluminous draped black design cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette becomes a dark, shadowy cloud. Palette is overwhelmingly black, then charcoal, deep navy, off-white and rare blood red, flowers or white graphic drawing scrawled across the dark surface. Tailoring is slouchy, androgynous, deliberately undone and poetic. Austere, moody, romantic and quietly rebellious.. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
