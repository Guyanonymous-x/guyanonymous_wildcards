Styled in a design language of cast-iron design with a heavy, squat, rounded body, a dark matte black or rust-brown finish and a lacquered iron-oxide surface. The skin is covered in rows of raised small round studs, or in smooth walls with fine texture. Details are flat planes with a small knob in brass or iron, a firm base and a thick lip. The surface looks like old weathered metal with a slight sheen, and a few forms carry raised pine, plum or wave motifs. Tea-ceremony restraint, sturdiness and quiet weight. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
