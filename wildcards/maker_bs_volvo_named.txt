In the style of Volvo, safe, calm, rational Scandinavian design. A circle with an arrow pointing up and to the right, a diagonal band across it, in plain silver or flat white, serves as the signature mark, with wide, evenly spaced, light capital lettering. Forms are boxy, upright, sturdy and honest, later softened into clean, minimal, flowing shapes with a quiet surface. Palette of cool pale blue, Nordic grey, pure white, fog, forest green, mustard, beige and dark navy. Materials include light wood, soft wool and plaid cloth, pale leather, matte metal and frosted glass. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Volvo in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
