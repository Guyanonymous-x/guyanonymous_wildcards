In the style of the Zagreb school of animation, modernist flat 2D cartoons with minimal geometric characters made of simple shapes, thin line work, little or no shading, large blank backgrounds in single flat colours, witty visual metaphors, angular stylised figures with stick-like limbs, abstract pop-art colour blocks in red, yellow, black and sky blue, jazzy off-balance compositions and a sophisticated deadpan, urbane 1960s graphic-design look.
