Styled in a design language of British radio and television sets in beautiful veneered wood cabinets, rounded and polished, with elegant cream knobs, fabric speaker panels in gold and brown, ribbed vents and slim chrome trim. Quiet, solid and pleasingly proportioned. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
