Styled in a design language of exuberant tropical design with flowing, airy shapes in viscose, cotton and chiffon. Prints are lush and hand-painted, with giant flowers, banana leaves, toucans, macaws, jaguars, monkeys, butterflies, fruit and palm trees, plus stripes, in saturated pink, red, orange, yellow, emerald, turquoise and royal blue. Details include puffed volumes, tiered ruffles, smocking, tied edges, embroidered trim and bright pom-pom accents. Carefree, joyful, vacation mood, with jungle foliage and bright blue sky. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
