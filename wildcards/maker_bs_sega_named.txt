Styled in a design language of edgy fast arcade-bright energy. Glossy black plastic with sharp angular wedges, circular recessed details, moulded ridges and shallow grooves. Cobalt blue is the signature. A later softer identity uses off-white and white surfaces with a swirling orange spiral emblem and light. Artwork is loud and saturated with bold outlines, hard-edged speed lines, white and orange accents and glowing red or green accent lights. Futuristic, quick, playful and confident. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
