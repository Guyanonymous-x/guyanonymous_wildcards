Styled in a design language of rustic French craft simplicity. Slim, tapered, teardrop-like forms in pale natural beech wood, olivewood and dark stained wood with a clear lacquer glow, joined to plain carbon steel that develops a soft grey patina. Small touches of colour in navy, olive, red, yellow and white lacquer. Honest, modest, countryside and timeless. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
