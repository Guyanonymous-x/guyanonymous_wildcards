painted on a sheet of Ganda bark cloth, soft rust-brown cloth beaten from fig tree bark with a faint fibrous grain, the lake, hills and shrines drawn on it in black, cream and ocher in bold flat shapes
painted in the manner of East African painters of the Makerere school of Kampala, broad strokes of oil paint in deep greens, blues and earth browns, the figures long and simplified
painted in bright flat poster colors with dark outlines in the manner of present-day Ugandan street and gallery painting, the lake a sheet of blue and the hills rows of rounded green shapes
