Styled in a design language of high-energy, melodramatic, richly saturated imagery built as dramatic collage compositions. Large overlapping close-up faces with intense theatrical expressions, hand-painted or digitally composited layering, glowing gold and fiery red accents, and heavy glossy gradient lettering with bevel, outline and drop shadow. Bold West African wax-print and woven cloth patterns, elaborate sculpted headwear, abundant jewellery and showy displays of wealth, with bright daylight and crowded frames. Emotion is turned up to the maximum, colours are loud and shiny, and the finish has a lively, direct, early digital home-video energy.
