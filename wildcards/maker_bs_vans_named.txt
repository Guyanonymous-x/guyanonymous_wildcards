Styled in a design language of laid-back California skater grit with a handmade, lived-in charm for any product. Thick cream-white glossy rubber surfaces with a fine waffle-like grid pattern, flat low rounded silhouettes and tall white edge bands, applied as trim or panels. Hard-wearing duck canvas and suede with chunky double stitching, scuffed and creased on purpose. Palette is navy, black, red, olive and off-white, with a small square black and white checkerboard as the signature repeat, plus wavy stripes and skull-and-crossbones doodles. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
