Styled in a design language of sturdy, dependable, tidy and slightly cheerful machinery in a single unmistakable colour scheme. Body panels are bright safety orange in glossy even enamel over dark grey or black structure and covers. Short rounded noses with slatted faces, rectangular work lights, slim chrome columns, flat step surfaces and high-backed black seats under canopies or glass cabs with big wraparound windows. Deep chevron-treaded rubber. Boxy, black rubber and bright steel, like well-maintained equipment resting in a green field. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
