In the style of Bang & Olufsen, serene, sculptural Scandinavian luxury. Forms are slim, tall, softly geometric and minimalist, with tapering cones, pyramids and thin floating plates. Materials are anodised and brushed aluminium, finely perforated metal mesh in tiny repeating patterns, polished black glass, oiled oak, dark walnut, wool fabric in charcoal and warm grey, and soft natural tan leather. Edges are razor sharp with tiny even gaps and hairline seams, with large circular elements and delicate pearl-blasted textures. Palette is silver, graphite, black, natural wood and muted earthy tones such as sand, terracotta and sage. Lettering is tiny, thin, lowercase and widely spaced. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Bang & Olufsen in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
