In the style of Fender, American electric guitars and amplifiers of the 1950s and 1960s. Offset double-cutaway or single-cutaway slab bodies with sleek contours, sunburst finishes fading from yellow to red to black, or solid Surf Green, Daphne Blue, Fiesta Red and cream. White or tortoiseshell pickguards, chrome knobs and bridge plates, maple necks with black dot markers, and a bold curved script logo on the headstock. Amplifiers have tweed or black vinyl cabinets, silver grille cloth, chrome corner trim and a row of cream or black knobs. Mood is bright, twangy, sunny California.
