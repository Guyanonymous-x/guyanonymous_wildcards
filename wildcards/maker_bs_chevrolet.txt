Styled in a design language of cheerful, optimistic diner-era American design. Bright chrome with bullet shapes, sharply pointed tapering forms, sweeping side flashes and two-tone lacquer. Palette is turquoise, coral, cream, sky blue, cherry red and ivory, with glossy metallic paint and polished steel highlights. Playful, confident, nostalgic and aspirational. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
