Styled after a long mass, a smooth aerodynamic body and a gently curved roof. Dark green, grey, cream, black and claret. Deep leather, polished walnut and clear round faces. Small oval nose with twin kidney-shaped openings, round forms set in the flanks and hidden details behind flaps add quiet eccentricity. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
