Styled in a design language of serene, polished, premium design in deep midnight navy and warm saffron gold. A stylised golden bird emblem of layered, upswept feather strokes sits on solid navy panels or crisp white fields. Surfaces are glossy lacquer, pearly white and soft metallic gold foil, with fine batik-like flowing pattern in muted gold on navy as a quiet secondary motif. Colour blocking is calm and symmetrical, with a broad navy band against white and a thin gold accent line. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
