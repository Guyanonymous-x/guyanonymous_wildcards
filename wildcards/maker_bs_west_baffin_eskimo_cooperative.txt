Styled in a design language of quiet, northern, graphic and dignified design with a simple, bold and direct line. The ink is deep black, with blue-grey, ochre, red-orange, green and teal appearing as flat blocks, with a soft grain from the stone and a subtle ridge around each shape, often with generous empty space around them. Sculpture is smooth, rounded, polished dark green-grey or black stone in heavy, compact forms of animals. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
