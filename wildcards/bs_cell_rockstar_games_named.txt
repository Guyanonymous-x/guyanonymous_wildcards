__bs_subject_media__ styled in a design language of gritty, lived-in, cinematic realism with a satirical edge. Sun-bleached streets, smoggy sunsets, a grainy handheld feel and heavy atmospheric haze. Compositions are organised as a grid of stylised comic-like panels with thick black outlines and flat saturated colour. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
