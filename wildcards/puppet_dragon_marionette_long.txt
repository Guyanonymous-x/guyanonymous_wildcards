a dragon marionette with a long jointed body, about as long as an adult is tall. Its body is a chain of a dozen carved wooden sections linked by leather hinges, so it curves and ripples as it moves, each section painted with overlapping green and gold scales. Its head is large and carved with a long snout, flaring nostrils, bulging red eyes and a hinged jaw full of white pointed teeth. Two leathery wings of painted cloth stretched over thin wooden ribs fold along its back and open when a string is pulled. A row of red cloth spines runs down its back to a pointed tail, and many strings run up from the body sections to a long control bar held by two hands. __puppet_dragon_marionette_var__.
