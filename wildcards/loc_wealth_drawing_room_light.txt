The best light in the house for being in, as distinct from the saloon's best light for being seen in, afternoon and evening sun through two or three full-height windows, softened to a flat even glow by muslins that are drawn across all afternoon, so the room has no hard shadows at all
Firelight from a coal grate, which is a smaller, redder and more constant light than the saloon's logs
A chandelier of fifteen candles that is lit perhaps twenty times a year; and the real evening light being two colza or paraffin lamps with ground-glass shades on the chimneypiece and a pair of candles at the piano, which together light a group of four chairs and leave the rest of the room in a warm dark that everyone present considers normal
