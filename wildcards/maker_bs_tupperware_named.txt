In the style of Tupperware, cheerful domestic order in translucent, slightly frosted material with a matte waxy finish. Soft rounded corners, plump friendly volumes and snug, tight-fitting joints. Forms are designed to nest and repeat in neat, tidy modular stacks. Colours are optimistic mid-century kitchen hues of avocado green, harvest gold, tangerine, pale turquoise, pink, mustard and almond, used as bold flat single colours. No ornament beyond moulded rings and simple ribs. Bright, wholesome, practical, suburban and joyful.
