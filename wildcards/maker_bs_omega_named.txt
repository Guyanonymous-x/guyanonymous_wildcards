In the style of Omega, purposeful, elegant and exploratory with a vintage space-age touch. Round steel and gold forms with clean faceted edges, polished or brushed, under domed glass. Matte black, silver, deep ocean blue and sunburst surfaces with contrasting inset circles, baton markers and luminous infill. Black aluminium bands carry white scale numerals. Warm tan leather with white stitching, tapering steel and bi-colour gold and steel details add richness. The emblem is a small Greek omega arch, repeated as a refined mark. Lettering is crisp, slim, widely spaced white capitals on black or deep blue. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
