Styled in a design language of mid-century jet-age optimism and worldly glamour. A deep blue circle encloses a pale globe drawn with fine latitude and longitude lines in clean white linework. Palette is cerulean and navy blue, crisp white, sandy beige, with a thin contrasting stripe in red or sky blue running along edges. Materials are glossy vinyl with white piping, canvas, polished aluminium and bright enamel. Shapes are boxy, rounded at the corners and clean, with tidy trim and flat graphic composition. Cheerful, confident, optimistic and travel-minded, with timeless retro polish. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
