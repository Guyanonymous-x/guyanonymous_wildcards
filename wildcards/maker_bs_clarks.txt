Styled in a design language of understated British comfort with a relaxed, lived-in handsomeness. Forms are soft, rounded and unfussy, with generous plump outlines, gentle stitched seams and thick panelled edges that feel easygoing and honest. Surfaces are velvety napped suede and smooth polished hide in sand, tobacco brown, olive, navy and black, paired with a pale honey gum tone for contrast bases. Details are minimal, with tidy topstitching, plain stitched details and quiet metal fittings, never showy ornament. Natural, earthy, practical and quietly friendly, with a worn-in, softly creased finish. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
