Styled in a design language of calm, urbane, polished, glassy and well-travelled design in green and white. A clean white ground with a thin jade green line, dark green accents and a calligraphic brush-stroke emblem like a swept bird wing curving upward in white or pale green. An earlier version used lettuce green and white two-tone. Quiet warm timber-toned panels, bottle green with pale covers, soft cream leather and thin gold accents. Harbour skyline at dusk. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
