the upper chamber of the cave of Psychro high on the Lasithi plateau, a wide sloping hall of gray rock with a low square altar of rough stones near its mouth, ash and animal bones heaped around it
the lower chamber, a steep drop to a still black pool among tall pale dripstone columns, small bronze double axes, bronze pins and tiny bronze figures of worshippers wedged into the cracks of the columns
the cave of Amnisos near the north coast, a long low tunnel of rock with a waist-high rounded stalagmite at its middle enclosed by a low wall of stones, its top polished smooth by hands
