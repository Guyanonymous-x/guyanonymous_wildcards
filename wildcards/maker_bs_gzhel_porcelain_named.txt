In the style of Gzhel Porcelain, bright glossy white ground painted only in cobalt blue brushwork, in graduated tones from pale sky blue to deep navy. Forms are rounded and plump, with soft outlines. The blue appears washed and flowing against the clean white. The mood is folk-like, cheerful, fresh and quintessentially Russian. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Gzhel Porcelain in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
