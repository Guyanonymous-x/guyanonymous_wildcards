The prints are large and detailed, in vintage-poster fashion, with hibiscus, plumeria, bird of paradise, palm trees, monstera, bamboo, tiki masks, outrigger canoes, volcano views, sunset beaches, hula dancers, tapa-like geometric borders and quilt patterns. Colours are rich and warm, including turquoise, coral, teal, deep orange, mustard, palm green, navy and cream, often with a shaded or watercolour quality and a dark background for tropical flowers. Some designs reproduce mid-century lei-and-surf scenes in two or three tones. The overall look is relaxed, sunny and nostalgic, Waikiki in the 1950s, bright but warm, with a hand-drawn illustration feel.
In the style of Bonds, Australian basic underwear and kids clothing. The core product is a plain cotton singlet or tank top with a deep scoop neck, ribbed knit, and a small woven label on the hem, in white, navy, grey, pale blue and cream. The famous baby wondersuit is a one-piece zip-front romper with small cuffs and stretch cotton jersey, printed with simple cheerful motifs, stripes, polka dots, small animals, clouds, stars and kangaroo-like characters in soft pastel yellow, mint, sky blue and pink. The logo is a simple lowercase or capital block sans-serif in a flat colour, sometimes a small blue box with white letters. Packaging is clean and bright, with plastic-wrapped packs in white and bright blue and a bold clear design. The mood is clean, soft, honest and dependable, down-to-earth with a friendly sense of humour.
In the style of Roots, a Canadian outdoor lifestyle brand. The logo is a simple beaver standing on a small group of branches, with the name underneath in a slightly rounded vintage serif typeface, often embroidered in a bright colour on grey marl sweatshirts. Sweatshirts and sweatpants are thick, soft heather-grey fleece with bold chest lettering, cuffs and kangaroo pockets. Leather goods include the Tribe bag, a soft saddle-brown leather tote and bucket bag with a stamped logo and a thick strap, along with leather varsity jackets with wool bodies and leather sleeves, and the famous Negative Heel shoe with a flat, thick, roomy base. Colours are earthy and rustic, with forest green, burgundy, navy, camel, cream, plaid red and black and maple-red accents. The mood is warm, casual, woodsy and cabin-in-the-woods.
In the style of Canada Goose, extreme-cold parkas. The parka is a heavy, thigh-length down-filled coat with a large padded hood trimmed with a fur ruff, a tall double collar, a two-way front zip covered by a big storm flap closed with snaps, big flapped patch pockets at hip level, elasticated storm cuffs and an adjustable cinch waist. The fabric is dense matte cotton-nylon with a slightly rough weave, in black, navy, graphite grey, sand, red or arctic white, and quilted baffles are sometimes visible. The arm has a round badge sewn on the sleeve, a roundel with a polar map and the name in red text around it. Details include reflective piping, a leather zipper pull and a bold tag on the chest. The silhouette is puffy, boxy, wide-shouldered and protective, with a rugged utilitarian polar expedition look.
In the style of 66 North, Icelandic weatherproof outerwear. Jackets and parkas are bold, practical, hooded shells with a high collar, a storm flap over the zipper, taped seams, large flapped pockets, adjustable cuffs and drawcord hems, made of matte waterproof fabric in strong colours, such as bright red, orange, deep blue, black, moss green and ochre against dark mountain backdrops. Down jackets are heavily quilted into long baffles, wool sweaters use stranded yoke patterns in grey, cream and charcoal, and the small logo is a tiny fixed-pointed badge or tag with the name in plain capital letters. The cut is long, broad and roomy, like arctic fishermen clothing refined into minimalism. The mood is austere, volcanic, windy and functional. Fabrics show a subtle sheen under rain, with chunky zip pulls, bold hood peaks and a plain functional layering approach, photographed against black sand, moss and low cloud.
In the style of Desigual, Barcelona fashion brand. The clothes are an exuberant, loud, joyful collage of prints, such as patchwork panels stitched from different floral, geometric and animal fabrics, bright embroidery, appliques, studs, sequins, lace inserts and ragged edges. Dresses and jackets mix several patterns on the same garment, such as colourful hearts, butterflies, flowers, zebra stripes, ethnic motifs, mosaic tiles, polka dots, graffiti and cartoon-like faces, in a riot of colours, turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Denim jackets have a worn-in painted look, bags are covered in stitched sections, and tags carry a big stylised swirling logo. The mood is optimistic, artistic, free, bohemian and colourful. Garment shapes stay simple, such as shirt dresses, bomber jackets and wrap tops, so the busy print mix carries the whole look, with mismatched buttons and playful contrast linings.
