on earth the white temple stands on a high mud-brick platform, its walls buttressed and whitewashed, a steep stair climbing to the shrine on top
the Ekur of Enlil rises at Nippur, its stepped tower of mud brick above the reed houses and palms of the city by the Euphrates
within the shrine the statue of the god sits on a throne, dressed in a robe of tufted wool, and before it a table holds bread, dates, jars of beer and a slaughtered sheep
