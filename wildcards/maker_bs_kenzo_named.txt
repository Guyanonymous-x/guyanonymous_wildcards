In the style of Kenzo, joyful, colourful, folkloric design that mixes wide kimono-like volumes, loose smock shapes, patchwork, quilting and layered global prints within a single look. Large flowers, poppies, stripes, ginghams, checks and paisley are combined at clashing scale in saturated tomato red, yellow, turquoise, emerald, pink, purple and soft peach. Fabrics are cotton, soft jersey, knits and light gauzy weaves, cut in a relaxed, unstructured, unisex way. A fierce, embroidered or printed tiger face with open jaws and bold whiskers, bursting with floral detail and neon outlines, is the signature motif, set above spaced, thin, capital serif lettering in white, red or green. Cheerful, exotic, bold, playful and vividly graphic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kenzo in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
