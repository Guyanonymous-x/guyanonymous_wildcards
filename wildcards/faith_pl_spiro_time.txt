in summer, the grass green, the mounds and the meadow thick with fresh mown grass under a hot blue sky with towering white clouds, the trees in full dark leaf, heat haze over the fields and deep shade pooled under the oaks
in fall, the trees gold, the oaks, pecans and cottonwoods around the meadow turned yellow, russet and bronze, fallen leaves scattered over the mounds, the grass fading to tan and low clear sunlight stretching long shadows from each rise
at dawn, mist on the mounds, a low white layer of river fog lying over the meadow so the grassy mounds rise out of it, the tree line a row of soft gray shapes, dew silvering the grass and the sky pale pink and gold in the east
