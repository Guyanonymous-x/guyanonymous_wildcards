In the style of Braun, calm functional German industrial design of the 1950s to 1970s. Plain rectangular boxes with softly radiused corners, matte satin-finish sheet metal or textured plastic in off-white, pale grey, black and graphite. Controls are small round dials, short slide switches and flush push buttons, grouped in neat grids and set in a ruled layout. Tiny accents of orange, yellow or green mark the key control. Lettering is lowercase or clean grotesque sans-serif, thin and evenly spaced, printed in black or white on the face. Speaker grilles are fine perforated or slotted aluminium. Shavers, clocks, radios, record players and kitchen blenders share this quiet, honest, ornament-free look with generous empty space and precise tolerances.
