Styled in a design language of crisp, Nordic, functional design in deep ultramarine blue with clean white. Colour blocking is broad and quiet, with a dark blue field, pale grey or white areas and one thin stripe set. A bold stripe motif in red, yellow and blue, each band equal in width, runs diagonally or horizontally as a lively accent against the reserved base. Finishes are satin paint, brushed light metal and smooth laminate, with a restrained, democratic, design-led Scandinavian rigour and no ornament. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
