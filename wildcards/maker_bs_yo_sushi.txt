Styled in a design language of playful, futuristic, pop-culture Japan-inspired and brisk. Sleek spaces with a continuous flowing line carrying small colour-coded rounds, slick white surfaces, glossy finishes, moulded plastic and neon lighting. Colour indicates value. Palette is hot pink, red, black, white and neon blue. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
