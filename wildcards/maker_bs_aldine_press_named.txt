Styled in a design language of a tiny emblem of a dolphin coiled around an anchor appears in black ink on cream laid paper. Surfaces are soft vellum or brown leather with blind tooling and gilt edges. The look is elegant, humanist, scholarly and compact, with quiet, evenly inked colour. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
