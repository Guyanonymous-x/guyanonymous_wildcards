Styled in a design language of mid-century American promotional elegance. Palette is cream, ivory, walnut brown, warm gold, tomato red, teal and ink black on aged-paper tones. Forms are streamlined with generous rounded corners, stepped silhouettes and sunburst radiating lines, in polished figured wood veneer, glossy bakelite, chrome strips and brass trim. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
