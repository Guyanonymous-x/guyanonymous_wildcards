Stonehenge, a ring of huge gray sarsen stones, some topped with lintels, with great trilithons, two uprights and a lintel, in a horseshoe inside, a circle of roughly squared pillars of pale gray sandstone several times the height of a standing adult, their surfaces pitted and blotched with green and yellow lichen, a curving run of flat stone beams still resting across the tops of several, taller stone gateways rising within, and fallen blocks lying in the short grass
the smaller bluestones inside the ring, a scatter of shorter, slimmer standing stones of dark blue-gray rock speckled with pale spots, most about the height of an adult, some leaning and some worn to rounded stumps, set in a curve between the towering sarsen pillars with clipped turf around their bases
the Heel Stone outside the circle, a single massive rough boulder of gray sarsen sandstone, left unshaped, taller than two standing adults and leaning toward the stone circle, its top rounded and its surface furrowed and lichen-spotted, standing alone in the grass beside the old earthen avenue with the ring of stones visible beyond it
