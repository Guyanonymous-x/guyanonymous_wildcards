In the style of Staedtler, drawing pencils and erasers in a distinctive scheme. Wooden pencils have a hexagonal body lacquered in bright blue and black bands with a vivid yellow upper section or a navy and red set, and a gold-printed or white type. Erasers are white, wrapped in blue paper sleeves with a black and red print. Technical pens and leads come in clean tin boxes or plastic cases, coloured in blue, black and white, with a simple sans-serif type. The look is functional, German, tidy and classroom-like, with tidy contrast bands and a flat, smooth lacquer.
