Framed photographs of past Worthy Matrons in white gowns line one wall, each with a printed card in the frame reading "WORTHY MATRON" and a year.
A long table at the back holds a white cake iced with a five-pointed star in colored icing, cups and saucers and a large silver coffee urn.
The walls are hung with five tall framed paintings, one behind each point of the star, each showing a robed woman holding her point's symbol.
