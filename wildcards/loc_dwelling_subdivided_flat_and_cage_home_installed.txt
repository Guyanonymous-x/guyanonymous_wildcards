plywood or block added to a normal flat to create five or six separately let rooms, each of four to seven square meters
let at a rent per square foot that is frequently higher than the city's luxury apartments, which is the central and well-documented economic fact
services split by sub-meter so each tenant pays a premium for electricity
sanitary provision reduced to one combined shower and lavatory shared by all occupants
ventilation and daylight absent from most rooms because the original flat had windows for one dwelling and not six
fire escape compromised by the partitions and by the single exit
a structural loading and drainage system never designed for six households
a tenure that is insecure
