Top light from the rooflight and the clerestory, which gives an even shadowless general illumination quite unlike a side-lit classroom and is the most distinctive thing about being in the space, together with strong direct light at the sliding doors so that each bay has a bright edge and a dimmer middle
Fluorescent tubes in continuous runs on one circuit, usually on, because top light alone is not enough under a British overcast
