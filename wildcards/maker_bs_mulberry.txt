Styled in a design language of British country heritage in rich, handsome leather. Naturally grained, softly pebbled and glossy hide in oak brown, chocolate, oxblood, forest green, navy, cream and tan, with contrast stitching and thick, sturdy edges. Surfaces are meant to soften and darken with use. Hardware is solid, heavy, antique brass and gold-toned metal, with a round, flat, domed or turn-style fitting in a classic, traditional look. Timeless, practical, warm, quietly luxurious and countryside in spirit, with a confident, polished finish. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
