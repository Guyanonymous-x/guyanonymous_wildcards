In the style of Gentle Monster, fashion eyewear and experimental retail from Seoul. Frames are oversized and sculptural, with chunky acetate in glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, thick geometric rims, rounded squares, cat-eye wings and big wraparound shields. Some pairs have metal bridges in silver or gold with fine hinges, or tinted lenses in blue, green, orange or mirror gradient. Details include small engraved logo marks on the temple in tiny white capitals. The shops are more art gallery than store, with huge kinetic sculptures, robotic arms, animal-like figures, towering mirrored walls, glowing plant-like installations and surreal landscapes of foam, resin, concrete and silicone in stark white or black spaces, with a single pair of glasses placed like an artefact on a plinth. Photography is moody, with dramatic lighting and models in sunglasses against odd backdrops. Mood is playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde.
