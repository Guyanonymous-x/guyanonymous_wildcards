Styled in a design language of enchanted storybook animation. Rounded, soft, bouncy forms with squash and stretch, big expressive eyes, clean flowing ink outlines and flat cel colour with gentle shading. Painted backgrounds are lush, softly blended watercolour in storybook pastel and jewel tones, with fairy-tale castles, forests and glowing magical sparkle. Palette is bright but gentle, with royal blue, rosy pink, sunny gold and woodland green. Characters have graceful, exaggerated poses and clear silhouettes. Ornamental gothic and curly storybook lettering. Warm, wholesome, whimsical and musical.
