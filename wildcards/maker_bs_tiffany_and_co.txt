Styled in a design language of polished New York prestige built around one pale robin-egg turquoise blue. Smooth matte blue boxes and bags with crisp square corners are wrapped in white satin ribbon tied in a simple bow, and the blue appears as flat backgrounds, tinted glass and pastel surfaces in every setting. Metals are bright sterling silver, platinum and soft yellow gold, with clean sparkling stones in simple claw settings, chunky links, hammered texture and engraved leaf borders. Photography is luminous, romantic and airy, with glossy reflections, soft glow and witty still-life arrangements of ordinary things in the signature blue. Elegant, clean and quietly exclusive. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
