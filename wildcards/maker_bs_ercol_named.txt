Styled in a design language of English post-war design in pale elm and beech. Slender spindles, sweeping hoops, curved arms, splayed tapering legs and simple bentwood construction. Light blond finishes and darker stained elm with visible swirling grain, round-cornered boxes with tapered feet and a gentle radiating rhythm. Warm rust, olive and mustard tweed tones. Warm, domestic and gentle, with a suburban 1950s feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
