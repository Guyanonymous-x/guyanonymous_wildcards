at Sillustani by Lake Umayo near Titicaca, chullpas, round burial towers of tightly fitted gray stone blocks, broader at the top than at the bottom, stand on a grassy headland above the blue water
high on a cliff face at Karajía in the cloud forest, a row of Chachapoya purunmachus, sarcophagi of clay and sticks taller than a man, each with a long face and a strong jaw, painted cream, red and yellow, look out over the green valley, skulls set on top of some
inside a cave tomb in the rocks, seated mummy bundles of men and women of every adult age line the walls, offerings of clay pots, corn cobs and coca leaves set before them
