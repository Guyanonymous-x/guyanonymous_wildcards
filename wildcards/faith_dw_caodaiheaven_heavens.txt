below the Eye the thirty-six heavens rise in tiers of cloud, each tier crowned with palaces of white jade with curving roofs, and at the top the White Jade Palace glows pure white
the heavens hold the great teachers honored by Caodaism, the Buddha seated in yellow robes, a man in his forties, Laozi in blue robes, a man in his seventies with a white beard, Confucius in red robes, a man in his sixties, and Jesus in white, a man in his thirties
on the tiers of the heavens the Three Teachings stand in their colors, a band of yellow for Buddhism, a band of blue for Taoism and a band of red for Confucianism, dragons and phoenixes curling through the clouds between them
