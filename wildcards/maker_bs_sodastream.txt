Styled in a design language of clean, modern, eco-minded, fresh and friendly home design for any product. Slim upright forms in black, white or brushed steel with plain rounded tops, a single clear push control and a flat base. Clear or tinted tall shapes with slender tapering tops carry a fine level line and a single colour accent such as red, blue, green or bright orange. Slender gunmetal grey or lime green steel adds a small collar. Newer pared-back forms in matte steel have a single dial. Flat labels in juicy colours, with rising bubbles, fruit slices, ice and a bright white counter in the imagery. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
