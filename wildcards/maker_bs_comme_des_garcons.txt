Styled in a design language of deconstructed avant-garde design with asymmetric, deliberately distorted silhouettes, bulbous padded humps, oversized stiff shapes, exposed raw seams, unfinished frayed edges, holes, inside-out construction and layered mismatched panels. Dominant colour is severe black, then stark white, grey and a single shock of red, with occasional gingham, polka dot, tartan or flat pop prints. Materials are felt, crumpled cotton, boiled and distressed knits, lace, patent and cheap-looking synthetics treated as luxury. A simple, rounded, cartoon-like red heart emblem with two small oval eyes appears in black, red, green or white, above plain, bold, spaced. Rebellious, intellectual and playfully strange, with beauty found in imperfection. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
