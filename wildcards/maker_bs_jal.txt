Styled in a design language of minimal, refined, precise Japanese design in one vivid vermilion red with white and charcoal. A solid red circle, like the rising sun, holds a white stylised crane drawn with a single sweeping curved neck and few feather strokes. Colour blocking is a clean white ground with one strong red shape, sometimes a dark grey band beneath it. Finishes are glossy lacquer and flat matte print with very crisp edges. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
