Styled in a design language of compact, friendly small cars of the 1990s with a polymer body of dent-resistant panels in bright primary colours such as teal, red and yellow, rounded corners and wide round lamps. Plain, unfussy lines, a smiling nose and clean uncluttered cabins with cloth seats in grey and teal. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
