In the style of Braun, calm, rational, ornament-free functional design. Simple geometric volumes with softly rounded corners, tight even gaps and precise tolerances. Matte or satin surfaces in off-white, pale grey, black and graphite, with natural light wood or clear translucent material used sparingly. Small round or flush details sit neatly grouped on a strict grid, with one tiny accent of orange, yellow, green or red marking the one thing that matters. Lettering is a compact, even, lowercase-feeling grotesque sans-serif, often as a plain black wordmark on white, and graphics use bold primary colour bars and rigorous grids. Fine regular perforations add texture. Photography is sparse, neutral and quietly lit, honest and understated.
