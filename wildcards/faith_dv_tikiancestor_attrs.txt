around the ancestor smaller figures fill every gap, little carved people and the beaked heads of manaia, the bird-headed guardian spirits, their bodies twisting in spirals
beside the carving stand the people of the meeting house, Hemi, a man in his fifties with a full face moko, and Marama, a woman in her forties with a chin moko, both in cloaks of woven flax and feathers
before the carving lie offerings and treasures, a greenstone club of glossy dark green jade, a carved bone comb and a cloak of brown kiwi feathers laid over the earth
