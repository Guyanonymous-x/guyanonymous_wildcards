Styled in a design language of cool, bright, narrative and decorative with a serene seaside light. Other patterns show repeating geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau versions have curving plants, lilies, irises and stylised maidens with delicate gold-green outlines. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
