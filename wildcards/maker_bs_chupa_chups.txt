In the style of Chupa Chups, Spanish lollipops. The sweet is a round ball candy on a white paper stick, wrapped in thin shiny plastic twisted at the base, with a label in the centre on top. The logo is a bright flower, a daisy-like shape with a yellow circle in the middle and red petals, with the name in friendly rounded white and yellow letters across it, on a bright red, yellow or orange background. Flavours are marked by colours, such as pink strawberry, orange, green apple, cola brown and lemon yellow, and some sweets are swirled with two colours. Display stands are hot-pink or red and shaped like a round tree, with dozens of lollipops pointing outwards in a sunburst. Posters show cartoon children licking lollipops. The mood is playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop.
