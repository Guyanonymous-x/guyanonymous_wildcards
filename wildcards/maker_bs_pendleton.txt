Styled in a design language of cosy, graphic wool heritage from the American Northwest. Thick, tightly woven wool in bold geometric motifs such as stepped diamonds, zigzags, chevrons, triangles, banded stripes and large plaids, arranged in symmetrical, repeating bands. Palette is saturated rust red, turquoise, mustard gold, forest green, navy, cream and black, balanced in warm earthy blocks. Edges are bound or finished with neat fringe, and surfaces have a soft brushed nap and a sturdy, woolly hand. Rustic, warm, durable and handcrafted. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
