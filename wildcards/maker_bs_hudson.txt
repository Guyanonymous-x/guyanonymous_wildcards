In the style of Hudson, late 1940s and early 1950s American cars with a step-down body, a very low floor and a bulbous slab-sided shape with rounded fenders. A wide, low, simple chrome grille with a triangle badge, a smooth slab trunk, a fastback roofline and skirted rear wheels. Small windows, a heavy brow over the headlamps and a rounded tail with narrow lamps. Two-tone paint in maroon and cream, deep green and tan. Chrome hubcaps with whitewalls and a thin chrome strip along the flank. Inside, a deep seat, a big steering wheel, a plain dash. Hunkered, bathtub-shaped, solid look.
