In the style of the Toadlena Two Grey Hills Navajo weavings of New Mexico, a restrained natural-wool tapestry look. Rugs are flat-woven by hand on a vertical loom, very tightly and finely, in a rectangular format with a dense, almost smooth surface. The colours come only from undyed sheep wool carded and blended, so the palette is creamy white, light and dark grey, tan, camel, chocolate brown and jet black, with no bright dye. The design is a symmetrical central geometric field with diamonds, stepped zigzags, concentric rectangles and terraced crosses, framed by a plain border and a crisp inner outline, with small tapering corner details and often a single dark stripe on the edge. The pattern is balanced, precise and optical, with crisp straight edges and no fringe tassels at the ends. The look is calm, earthy, refined and architectural, like a desert landscape abstracted into straight lines.
