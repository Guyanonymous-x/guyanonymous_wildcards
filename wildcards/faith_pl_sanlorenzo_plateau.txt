the flat plateau of San Lorenzo covered in pasture and trees, a broad level hilltop of bright green grazing grass dotted with humped white cattle, scattered tall shade trees with spreading crowns, wire fences on crooked posts and a few palm-thatched shelters under a huge cloudy sky
low earth mounds on the plateau, long gentle grass-covered rises and small rounded humps, the worn remains of Olmec platforms, arranged around flat open courts, barely higher than a standing adult, with cattle paths crossing them and clumps of trees growing from their tops
ravines cutting into the plateau's edge, deep narrow gullies with steep walls of red and yellow clay eating back into the green tableland, choked with tangled trees, vines and broad-leaved plants, small streams running along the bottom toward the lowland
