Styled in a design language of Finnish Scandinavian design with calm, honest, timeless simplicity. Forms are organic and fluid, with undulating leaf-like curves, free-form wavy outlines inspired by lake shores, and pure cylindrical or softly rounded geometry. Materials are clear, tinted and frosted glass, smooth glazed ceramic and a few matte finishes. Palette is cool and clear with ice blue, aqua, smoky grey, moss and sea green, soft white, with sparing warm accents in orange, red and cobalt. Colour is translucent and stackable in tidy ranges. Quiet, functional and nature-minded. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
