In the style of Canada Goose, extreme-cold expedition design, puffy, boxy, wide-shouldered, protective and rugged. Heavy, down-filled, quilted forms with large padded volumes, a fur-trimmed protective edge, a tall collar, snap-closed storm flaps, flapped patch pockets and a cinched waist. Fabric is dense, matte, slightly rough woven cotton-nylon in black, navy, graphite grey, sand, red or arctic white, with visible baffles. Signature is a round embroidered badge with a white polar map in a blue centre, ringed with red and white lettering. Reflective piping, leather pulls, knit trims, tonal tags and quiet, premium detailing give a serious and wealthy polar feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
