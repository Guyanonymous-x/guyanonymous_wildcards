In the style of Blundstone, plain, hard-wearing, no-frills Tasmanian workwear design. Smooth, waxed or oiled leather in rustic brown, chestnut, black, tan, deep oxblood and slate grey, with a scuffed, honest, well-used character. Forms are rounded, stout and stripped back, with elastic panels, bold pull tabs, visible stitching and chunky black rubber and contrasting pale edge stripes. The mark is a bright blue and green label with an italic wordmark and a small island outline, framed with cream and gold. Seasonal ranges add stout colour pops in olive, sage, rust and navy. Honest, timeless, practical and tough. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Blundstone in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
