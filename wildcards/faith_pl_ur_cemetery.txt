the royal cemetery, a broad dug-out pit where the tombs of the kings and queens of Ur were found
the Great Death Pit of the royal cemetery as the diggers uncovered it, the sloping pit floor laid with the skeletons of more than seventy adult attendants of a royal burial, most of them women lying on their sides in rows, gold leaf headdresses and beads of red carnelian and blue lapis still around their skulls, some skulls broken by a blow from a bronze tool
the royal cemetery as it is today, a broad shallow hollow in the sand beside the ruins, low brick walls of tomb chambers showing in its floor
