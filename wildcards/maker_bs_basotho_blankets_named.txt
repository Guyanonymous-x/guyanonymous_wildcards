In the style of Basotho Blankets, proud, heritage-rich, mountain-chilled wrap design. Heavy, tightly woven wool with a soft nap, rectangular, with fringed ends and a plain edge. Patterns are bold and geometric, with wide stripes, large diamonds, squares, stylised leaf shapes, flowers and crowned shields. Classic combinations include cream and mustard with dark stripes, burgundy and navy blocks, a bold gold and black chevron, and a crown pattern in blue, red and cream. Colours are strong and earthy, with ochre, maroon, navy blue, forest green, black and cream. Draped and pinned at the shoulder, against rocky highlands in cool light.
