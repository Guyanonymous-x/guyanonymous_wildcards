in Korea at Seollal, the lunar new year, the grown sons and daughters in their twenties to fifties wear hanbok, the long full skirts and short jackets of silk in pink, blue and green, and make the sebae, the deep new year bow, kneeling and touching their foreheads to their hands on the heated floor of an apartment living room, in the pale light of a winter morning through the windows
in China at a wedding, the bride and groom in their twenties, the bride in a red silk qipao embroidered with gold phoenixes and the groom in a dark suit, kneel on red cushions and each offer a cup of tea with both hands, a big red paper cut of the double happiness sign "囍" on the wall behind, in the warm lamplight of a decorated home

in Japan on Respect for the Aged Day in September, the grown sons and daughters in their thirties to fifties in neat dark clothes kneel on tatami mats and bow low with their hands flat on the floor, the paper screens open on a small garden, in the soft clear light of an early fall afternoon
in Vietnam at Tết, the lunar new year, the grown sons and daughters in their twenties to fifties wear long áo dài tunics of red and yellow silk over loose trousers and bow with joined hands before the elders, a branch of pink peach blossom in a vase, in the warm light of a spring morning
