the dancers, Raramuri women in their twenties to sixties in wide gathered skirts of bright printed cotton in pink, yellow and green, several layered one over the other, loose blouses with puffed sleeves and colorful headscarves
the dancers, men in their twenties to fifties in the old dress of a white cotton loincloth with a wide red woven sash and a loose white shirt, their legs bare and their feet in sandals of tire rubber
the dancers, women and men in their thirties to seventies, the men in present-day jeans, cotton shirts and straw cowboy hats, the women in long flowered skirts and shawls wrapped over their heads against the cold
