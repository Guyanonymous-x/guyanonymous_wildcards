In the style of Jingdezhen Imperial Kilns, thin-walled white porcelain with a glassy, faintly bluish transparent glaze, painted under the glaze in rich cobalt blue. Motifs include coiling lotus scrolls, dragons chasing flaming pearls, fish and water weed, peony sprays and bands of ruyi clouds and waves, repeated in tight concentric bands around vases, bowls, stem cups and moon flasks. Cobalt shades range from pale silvery blue to deep inky blue with dark pooled spots. Forms are elegant and balanced, with rounded shoulders, narrow foot rings and crisp rims. Overglaze enamel variants add coral red, apple green, yellow and turquoise outlined in fine black, and a small six-character mark in a blue double ring sits on the base.
