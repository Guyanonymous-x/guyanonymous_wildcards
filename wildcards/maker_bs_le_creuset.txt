Styled in a design language of French heritage craft in glossy, saturated enamel. Shapes are rounded, heavy, generous and softly domed, with smooth shoulders, gentle curves and sturdy loop forms. Signature palette is flame orange, deep cerise, cobalt, Mediterranean teal, sunny yellow, sage and cream, with satin black and shimmering pearl variations, usually shown as graduated rows of one colour per item. Surfaces are plain and uncluttered, with occasional floral or stripe motifs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
