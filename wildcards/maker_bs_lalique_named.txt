Styled in a design language of French Art Deco moulded glass that is frosted, satin-matte and crystal clear in the same piece. Deeply relief-moulded motifs of birds, fish, dragonflies, thistles, stepped, geometric edges. Milky opalescent glass glows pale blue against warm amber, and tints of sapphire, emerald, black and smoky grey add depth. Polished highlights contrast with velvety acid-etched textures. Luxurious, sculptural, light-catching and refined. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
