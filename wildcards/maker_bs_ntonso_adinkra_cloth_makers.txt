In the style of Ntonso Adinkra Cloth Makers, handwoven cotton cloth stamped with symbols using carved calabash stamps and a dark, glossy tree-bark dye. Rows of small, bold black motifs, such as concentric circles, spirals, crosses, ladders, hearts and stylised plants, repeat inside a grid divided by hand-drawn comb lines. Backgrounds are dyed deep red, brown, indigo, black or left natural cream, and mourning cloths are very dark. Symbols are slightly irregular, with inky edges, giving a handmade rhythm and graphic quality. The cloth is wrapped around the body like a toga and has stitched strips joined together.
