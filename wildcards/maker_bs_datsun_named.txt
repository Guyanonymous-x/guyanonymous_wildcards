Styled in a design language of cheerful, honest, retro Japanese design built around a bold roundel emblem. Palette is primary red, royal blue, cream white, sunny yellow, mustard and orange with warm off-white paper tones, often as flat colour blocks with thick stripes. Shapes are rounded, compact and friendly with small shields, pennants and crest forms. Materials include enamelled tin, painted metal, chrome-edged plates, felt patches and glossy printed paper with a gently worn, nostalgic patina. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
