In the style of Cessna, friendly, simple, practical design from the 1960s and 1970s. Boxy but approachable white forms with softened corners, generous wraparound windows and plain, honest shapes that are easy to read. Painted sweeping side stripes in red, blue, gold or green add a note of optimism, with small neat numerals as the only lettering. Sturdy, unfussy, utility-first and casual, with a weekend-adventure cheerfulness and clean enamel finish.
