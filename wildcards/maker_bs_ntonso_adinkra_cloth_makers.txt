Styled in a design language of handwoven cotton stamped with carved stamps in a dark, glossy tree-bark dye. Rows of small, bold black symbols such as concentric circles, spirals, crosses, ladders and hearts repeat inside a grid divided by hand-drawn comb lines. Backgrounds are dyed deep red, brown, indigo or black, or left natural cream. Symbols are slightly irregular with inky edges, giving a handmade rhythm and strong graphic quality. Narrow woven strips are stitched together into a wide cloth.
