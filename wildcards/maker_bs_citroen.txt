In the style of Citroen in the 1950s to 1970s, a futuristic streamlined saloon with a low teardrop body, a sloping shark nose, a smooth aerodynamic tail tapering to a narrow rear, and a car that seems to crouch on its haunches. Rear wheel arches covered by smooth skirts, single-spoke steering wheel, and thin pillars. Double chevron badge on the nose, a minimal grille slot, and headlamps behind a smooth glass cover that turn with the steering. Roof in white fibreglass, body in light pastel colours like pale green, ivory, grey blue, and sand beige, or in bold orange and brown. Interior has a very low bench seat, cloth in checks, a pod-shaped instrument panel, and odd plastic controls. Mood is quirky, avant-garde and comfortable.
