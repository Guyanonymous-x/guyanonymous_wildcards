Styled in a design language of honest, rugged, vintage British-Indian character. Round badges in black and antique gold carry a bold cannon and winged crest, with thin double rings, raised metal edges and a stamped, heritage feel. Colours are gloss black, deep green, maroon, cream and military olive with gold pinstripes, plus enamel red and yellow accents on winged metal plates. Lettering is a sturdy slab and classic serif in capitals, often with a rope-like outline. Surfaces mix brushed brass, nickel chrome, waxed canvas, aged leather and hand-painted lines, with chunky rivets, softly worn edges and sturdy rounded teardrop shapes. Unfussy, durable and gently nostalgic. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
