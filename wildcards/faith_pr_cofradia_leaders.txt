the elders of the brotherhood, men in their fifties to seventies, walk in front holding tall wooden staffs topped with silver suns and crosses, their red headcloths falling to their shoulders, one swinging a smoking tin censer on a chain
the wives of the cofrades, women in their forties to sixties in their finest huipils and long red ribbons wound around their heads like a halo, walk ahead carrying lit candles and clay bowls of smoking copal
two men in their thirties walk in front setting off strings of firecrackers, puffs of white smoke and bits of red paper bursting at their feet, and a man in his sixties follows waving a censer of copal from side to side
