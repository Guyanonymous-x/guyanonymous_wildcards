Styled in a design language of classic American court and garage-band heritage in its simplest form, for any product. Soft cotton canvas stretched over rounded, minimal shapes, framed by glossy off-white trim with a thin coloured stripe, flat cords and small shiny metal rings. A small circular badge carrying a star and chevron mark recurs as the emblem. Palette is optic white, black, cherry red, navy, mustard and faded pastels, with sports-team stripes and star motifs. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
