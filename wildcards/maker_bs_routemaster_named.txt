In the style of Routemaster, a graphic language of one confident, glossy red applied across large unbroken surfaces. The red is deep, warm and slightly orange, set against a single cream or pale grey band and thin black or gold pinstripe edging. Shapes are softly rounded at the corners with smooth continuous curves, polished chrome or brushed aluminium trim and neat riveted seams. A red ring with a blue bar serves as the emblem. Lettering is compact, upright, white or gold capitals in a classic humanist sans-serif, with small, orderly numerals and rules. Secondary tones include moquette-like woven patterns in muted blue, green and rust, wood-grain laminate and black rubber details, with a proud, civic, nostalgic character. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Routemaster in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
