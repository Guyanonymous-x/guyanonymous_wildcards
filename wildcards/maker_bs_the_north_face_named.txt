Styled in a design language of bold, rugged expedition design with a technical, mountain-inspired look. Strong colour blocking in red, black, white, yellow, royal blue and teal, split by diagonal seams and panels. Materials are glossy ripstop nylon, quilted puffy baffles, fleece, matte coated fabrics and webbing, with reflective piping and taped edges. Shapes are chunky, padded and geometric, and the brand has a confident, adventurous, high-performance character with oversized graphics and clean flat colour fields. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
