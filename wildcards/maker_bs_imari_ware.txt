Styled in a design language of flamboyant, dense, sumptuous decoration that leaves little blank white. A rich palette of deep underglaze cobalt blue, fiery iron red and lavish gold, with panels of chrysanthemums, peonies, phoenixes, fans, waves and brocade-like patterns packed side by side. Blue areas alternate with red and gilded floral scrolls. Outlines are lobed and scalloped with ornate edges. Glossy glaze, heavy ornament, opulent and festive like woven silk brocade. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
