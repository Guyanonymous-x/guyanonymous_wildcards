In the style of Opinel, a simple, rustic, honest design with slim tapered steel and plain turned beech wood shaped like a slim teardrop, finished in clear lacquer. A rotating steel ring detail at the joint. A small crowned hand mark stamped in the metal. Natural pale beech, olivewood, dark stained wood and occasional coloured lacquer. Thin ground edges, patinated steel, elegant, inexpensive and unfussy, with a countryside peasant-picnic mood, shown on a wooden board with bread and cheese. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
