In the style of Memphis Group, Italian postmodern furniture and objects built from clashing geometric blocks, triangles, cylinders and zigzags. Cheeky laminate in bold squiggle, terrazzo and speckled confetti patterns, in pink, teal, mustard yellow, sky blue, black and white. Wobbly asymmetrical bookshelves shaped like totem figures, cubist sideboards on tapered legs, dayglo plastic lamps, glass vases with stripes and dots. Playful, irreverent, cartoonish 1980s mood, flat saturated colour planes with thick black outlines, kitsch and joyful.
