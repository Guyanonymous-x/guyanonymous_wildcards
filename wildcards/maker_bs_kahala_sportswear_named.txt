In the style of Kahala Sportswear, relaxed, sunny, nostalgic tropical leisure with a hand-drawn vintage-poster illustration feel. Large, detailed motifs of flowers, palms, foliage, ocean scenes and island craft borders are drawn with a shaded, almost watercolour quality, often on dark grounds so blooms glow. Colours are rich and warm, turquoise, coral, teal, deep orange, mustard, palm green, navy and cream, sometimes limited to two or three tones for a mid-century look. Pattern runs continuously across joins and edges without a break. Soft, drapey, silky surfaces and open easy shapes. Bright but warm, mid-century holiday glamour.
