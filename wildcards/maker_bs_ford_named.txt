In the style of Ford, honest, approachable, everyday American mass-market design. A blue oval badge with flowing white script lettering with a long looping tail serves as the signature mark, found as enamel signs, porcelain plates and chrome-edged emblems. Palette of deep royal blue and white with poppy red, turquoise, pale yellow, cream and dark green. Later years bring clean utilitarian graphics, plain horizontal bands, and simple blue and white signage. Materials include painted steel, enamel, chrome strip, vinyl, woodgrain and canvas. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
