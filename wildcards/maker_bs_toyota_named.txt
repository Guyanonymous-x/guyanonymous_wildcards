Styled in a design language of sensible, dependable mass-market design with a crisp modern edge. Shapes are angular and clean, with sharp shoulder creases, slab sides, sculpted upper surfaces and wide hourglass or trapezoid openings in glossy black. Large black plastic trim around edges gives a tougher, practical feel. Materials are tidy grey fabric, soft-touch plastic and piano-black panels. Palette is super white, deep black, cool silver, signal red and bright blue. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
