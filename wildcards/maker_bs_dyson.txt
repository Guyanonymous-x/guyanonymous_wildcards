Styled in a design language of engineered, futuristic, glossy and rather industrial. Transparent clear plastic reveals inner mechanics against bright coloured plastic parts in purple, fuchsia, yellow, nickel grey and iron. Parts are tubular and ribbed, with exposed engineering, smooth curved moulding, visible fixings and large chunky rounded elements. Thin glossy rings on tall tapering stands replace conventional shapes. Small colour-coded controls and tiny readouts. Lettering is small and bold.
