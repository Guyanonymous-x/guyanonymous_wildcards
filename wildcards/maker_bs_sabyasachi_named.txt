In the style of Sabyasachi, opulent, romantic Indian heirloom richness with a vintage, slightly distressed and layered look. Deep earthy jewel colours such as rich red, burnt orange, marigold, mustard, moss green, teal and faded rose cover the surface. Heavy hand embroidery in dense gold metallic thread, silk thread, sequins, mirror work and tassels combines with block-printed florals and tiger or paisley motifs on silk, velvet, organza and tulle. Sheer layers have scalloped borders. Ornament is chunky antique gold with uncut stones, big gems and pearl ropes, layered thickly. Regal, heritage mood, warm golden light and a faded-palace feel.
