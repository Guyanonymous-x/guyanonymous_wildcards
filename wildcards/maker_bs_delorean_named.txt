Styled in a design language of raw, futuristic, unpainted retro-science-fiction design. Surfaces are bare brushed stainless metal with a fine linear grain, left unpainted and cool silver grey, set against matte black plastic trim and dark charcoal fills. Forms are flat, wedge-like and razor sharp, built from straight edges, hard creases and slanted planes with no soft curves. Slim horizontal louvre patterns and recessed slots add rhythm. Accents are minimal, tiny warm orange or red points on an otherwise monochrome steel and black palette. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
