the eight trigrams painted in thick black bars on gold, one stack facing each side of the octagon, three unbroken bars at the top for heaven, three broken bars at the bottom for earth, and the six mixed stacks of solid and broken bars ranged around the sides between them
each trigram a stack of three raised bars carved in relief and gilded, the solid bars long and whole and the broken bars cut into two short blocks with a clear gap between, every stack lying parallel to its own edge of the board so that the eight together form a ring
the trigrams set in a ring of eight wedge-shaped panels divided by thin gold lines, every panel holding one neat stack of three bars in bright red on a yellow ground, each stack a different mixture of whole bars and bars split in the middle
