Styled in a design language of smooth, glossy enamel fields held by hair-thin silver or brass wires. Colours are deep and jewel-like, including midnight blue, black, wisteria violet, jade green, ivory and warm gold, with a mirror finish and no visible wire thickness. Subjects are delicate birds, hydrangea, wisteria, irises, chrysanthemums, butterflies and tiny geometric brocade borders, often floating on a plain black or blue ground with generous empty space. Wireless techniques create soft gradients in petals and feathers. Forms are slender, with narrow necks, flared openings and silver rims. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
