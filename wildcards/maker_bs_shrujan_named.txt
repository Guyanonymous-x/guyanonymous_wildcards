Styled in a design language of densely hand-stitched embroidery in bright thread on dark or plain ground, with tiny mirrors sewn in using chain stitch, herringbone and buttonhole borders. Motifs are repeating peacocks, parrots, flowers, mango shapes, geometric diamonds, stars and tiny mandala medallions arranged in tight bands and symmetrical grids. Colours are vivid magenta, scarlet, turquoise, canary yellow, green and white against black, indigo or deep red. Highly textured, glittering, tactile surfaces of shimmering mirrors and tight thread, with folk-craft joyfulness. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
