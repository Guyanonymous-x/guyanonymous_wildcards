at the center stands Yggdrasil, a vast ash tree whose crown spreads over the heavens and whose roots reach down into the worlds below, an eagle sitting at its top, a red squirrel running down the trunk and the dragon Nidhogg gnawing at its roots
at the foot of Yggdrasil lies the Well of Urd, and the three Norns, women in their twenties, fifties and eighties in plain wool dresses, pour water and white clay over the roots to keep the tree alive
the nine worlds hang among the branches and roots of the tree as nine shining realms, Asgard at the top, Midgard in the middle ringed by the World Serpent, Jotunheim of the giants beside it and Hel among the roots
