A long dining table under the trees is set with white enamel plates and tin cups, and a cook in a white apron carries out a huge pan of grilled steaks.
Wooden walkways and stairs link the cabins up the hillside, and an old upright piano stands on one of the decks under a canvas awning.
A small carved wooden owl, the club's symbol, perches over every camp's door, some painted, some plain weathered wood.
