In the style of 2000 AD, British sci-fi comics with gritty, hyper-detailed pen and ink, dystopian megacities of towering tenements and neon, helmeted law enforcers in spiked armour, robots and aliens, painted covers with metallic airbrush gloss, lurid red, green and electric blue, loud punky mood, black-and-white interior pages with dense hatching, jagged sound effects, a bold angular logo and a sardonic, violent futuristic attitude.
