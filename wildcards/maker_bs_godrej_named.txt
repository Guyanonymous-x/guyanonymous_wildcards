Styled in a design language of solid, heavy, trustworthy mid-century Indian steel design. Tall rectangular boxy volumes of welded sheet steel with rounded corners, squat secure proportions and a single bold vertical chrome pull. Surfaces are thick baked enamel with a faint orange peel texture in grey green, olive, cream, rust brown, pale blue, dark green or black, with thin gold pinstripes and brass fittings. Round, oversized control details feel reassuringly chunky. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
