in summer, the rolling prairie a deep green and dotted with wildflowers, the lodges grassy and bright, the blue sky huge overhead with a few towering white clouds and their shadows drifting over the hills
in winter, snow lying thick and smooth on the domes of the lodges and drifted against their log entries, thin smoke rising from the smoke holes, the river frozen white below under a pale sky
at sunset, the rounded lodges dark humped silhouettes against a wide orange and red sky, the drying racks outlined in black and the last light shining along the bends of the river
