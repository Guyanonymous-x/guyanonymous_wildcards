In the style of Thebe Magugu, South African contemporary womenswear that combines sharp tailoring with storytelling prints, with structured blazers, pleated skirts, shirt dresses, cropped jackets, suits and wrapped pieces. Fabrics include wool, silk, crisp cotton and leather, with printed illustrations of portraits, plants, historical imagery and map-like graphics. Palette moves between deep forest green, burgundy, cobalt blue, mustard, cream, black and pastel pink, with precise seams, contrasting piping, strong shoulders and nipped waists. Playful and intellectual, polished, artful and modern Johannesburg mood, shown in clean gallery light.
