In the style of Galeries Lafayette, a central Art Nouveau glass and steel dome in stained-glass colours of gold, green and amber, rising above balconies with curved wrought-iron balustrades in gilded patterns. Tall store facade of cream stone with ornate carving, a gold-lettered sign, and a pale pink or cream paper bag with thin black capital letters. Mosaic floors, sweeping staircases and glass canopies. Palette of gold, cream, emerald and amber. The look is Belle Epoque, domed, ornate, light-filled and grand, with decorative ironwork and coloured glass.
