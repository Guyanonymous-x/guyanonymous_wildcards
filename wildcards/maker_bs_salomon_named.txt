In the style of Salomon, technical, aggressive, engineered mountain-sport design with sculpted, angular silhouettes and sharp protective overlays. Mixed synthetic mesh, welded films, moulded rigid shells and rubberised textures with exposed cord details, all arranged in layered, fast-looking panels. Palette of black, charcoal, warm grey and white with sudden hits of bright red, electric blue, lime, orange or earthy mineral tones in muted colourways. A curved wing-like emblem, drawn as a bold black S shape with a wide capital wordmark, appears in a contrasting colour. Matte and gloss textures sit side by side, with chevron and ribbed patterns, giving a fast, slightly futuristic, precise feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
