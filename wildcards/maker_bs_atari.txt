In the style of Atari, late 1970s and early 1980s American home game consoles and computers. Heavy wedge-shaped boxes clad in simulated dark woodgrain vinyl on the front with black ribbed plastic tops, chunky silver toggle switches in a row along the top edge, and thick black joysticks with a single round red button on a square base. Warm brown, black and silver palette. Cartridges are black with ridged grips and brightly illustrated paper labels showing painted spacescapes in orange, purple and teal. Logo is a stylised three-bar fuji-like trident shape in white or red with rounded geometric capitals. Computers are beige-grey with chunky keys. Mood is warm, retro-futuristic, 1970s den.
