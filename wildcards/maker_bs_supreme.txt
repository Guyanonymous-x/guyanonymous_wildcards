Styled in a design language of a flat rectangular block in saturated red with bold white italic capitals in a heavy, squeezed sans-serif face, used as a stamp that can appear on anything and be repeated or tiled as a pattern. Collage graphics of found photographs, crude photocopy halftones, high-contrast black and white portraits, cartoon figures and gallery-style reproductions. Palette of red, black, white and occasional neon. Crisp screen-print ink, slightly cracked vinyl texture, thick outlines and deadpan minimal copy. Clean white spaces and a hard-edged downtown street-poster attitude, cool, smug and deliberately ironic.
