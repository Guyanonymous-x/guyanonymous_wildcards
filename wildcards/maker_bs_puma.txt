Styled in a design language of sleek athletic design with a confident, fast, low-slung attitude. A leaping big cat silhouette is the recurring emblem, set beside heavy, bold, wide lowercase and capital lettering in black, white or red. Long tapering stripe forms sweep diagonally across surfaces as a single flowing accent curve. Palette is crisp white, deep black and signal red, with royal blue, forest green, mustard and cream for retro colour-blocked pairings. Materials include soft suede with a velvety nap, smooth nylon, terry cloth, glossy synthetic panels and clean piping. Graphic layouts use big flat colour fields, repeated logo patterns and a cool, 1970s sporty polish. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
