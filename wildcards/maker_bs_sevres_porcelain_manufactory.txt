In the style of Sevres Porcelain Manufactory, soft-paste and hard-paste porcelain with deep saturated ground colours, namely a rich royal blue, turquoise, a delicate rose pink and apple green, all heavily gilded. Reserved panels on vases, tea services and plates hold finely painted cherubs, pastoral figures, birds and flower bouquets in naturalistic colours. Gilt borders, scrolls, beading and lacy foliage trim every edge, and handles are sculpted into curving branches or flower forms. Forms are swelling, ornate and Rococo, with a glossy, perfectly smooth glaze. Biscuit figurines remain matte white, with silky, snow-like texture.
