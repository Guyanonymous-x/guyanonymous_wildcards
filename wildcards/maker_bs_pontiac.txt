Styled in a design language of aggressive, swaggering street-racer American muscle of the 1960s and 70s. Split twin-nostril shapes divided by a body-coloured beak, stacked or hidden forms, a waistline swelling over tall haunches, one long raised bulge and bold parallel stripes. Carousel red, deep green, ivory and bright orange. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
