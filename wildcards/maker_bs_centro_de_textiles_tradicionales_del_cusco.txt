Styled in a design language of rich, rustic, rhythmic and symmetrical hand-woven textiles in natural dyed wool. Long narrow bands and square cloths in slightly fuzzy hand-spun yarn. Colours are earthy plant-dye shades, deep indigo and sky blues, cochineal reds, pinks and purples, yellow-greens, mustard, walnut brown, cream and charcoal. Edges are finished with braided cords and tassels. A precise ancient textile language in warm natural tones. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
