Shot through a full-spectrum camera with the filter removed and left with the auto white balance unbalanced, the image shows the whole frame in a strong magenta-pink cast, the body faded and dreamy. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
