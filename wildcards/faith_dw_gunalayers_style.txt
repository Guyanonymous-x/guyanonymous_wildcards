sewn in the manner of a Guna mola, layers of red, orange, black and yellow cotton cut away to show the colors beneath, each layer of the world a band of mazes, birds and turtles outlined in narrow stripes of cloth
painted in the bright flat colors of present-day Guna painters of Panama City, the layers drawn as stacked bands of deep blue, green, red and gold, every figure outlined in black and filled with fine repeated patterns
drawn in rows of small stick figures, houses, trees and animals painted in red, black and blue on pale wooden boards, each band of figures one layer of the world
carved and painted as small wooden figures of pale balsa and dark hardwood in brimmed hats and long coats standing on each layer against painted mola patterns
