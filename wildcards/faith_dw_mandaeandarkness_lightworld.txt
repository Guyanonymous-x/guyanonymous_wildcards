at the top the World of Light shines white and gold, where the uthras, adults in their thirties in white robes, sit in their shining dwellings beside clear running rivers, myrtle and white flowers growing on the banks
at the top, at the edge of the light, sits Abatur, an old man in his seventies in a white robe and turban, holding a great pair of scales on which each soul is weighed before it may pass up into the light
at the top the Great Life is shown only as a pure white brightness at the summit of the light, rays and running water pouring down from it, the uthras in white standing in rows below it
