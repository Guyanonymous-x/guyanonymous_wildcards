Styled in a design language of practical, quiet, friendly, design-led domestic calm. Clean boxy forms with rounded corners in bright white enamel, or brushed stainless steel, with smooth tops, seamless profiles and slim, flush details. Later work has large soft-touch rounded controls, gentle blue or green glow and neat brushed edges. Slim stacked divisions, recessed grips, black glass panels and chunky silver accents show a modular, tidy logic. Palette is white, silver and grey with minimal ornament. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
