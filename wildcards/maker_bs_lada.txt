In the style of Lada, Soviet-era saloons and small off-roaders with a simple squared-off body of flat panels, thin chrome bumpers, a plain chrome-framed grille with a horizontal bar, round or rectangular headlamps with a chrome rim, a small ship-shaped badge, and slim pillars with a big glass area. Rear lamps are plain rectangles with orange and red. Steel wheels with small chrome hubcaps, narrow tyres, upright stance, short overhangs. Interior with brown or beige vinyl, plastic panels in brown, a plain dash of round dials and a thin steering wheel. Colours of Moskva pink, beige, ochre, brown, dark green and cherry red. Frugal, dour, boxy.
