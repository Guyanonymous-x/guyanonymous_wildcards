In the style of Koh-i-noor Hardtmuth, Czech pencils and drawing materials. The signature pencil is a hexagonal cedar-wood barrel lacquered in a strong yellow, with a gold or silver stamped logo on the face and the grade printed in small neat capitals in a band of hot-stamped foil. The top is dipped in dark green or black. Packaging includes slim flat cardboard boxes or tins in yellow, with a diamond icon and a crest on the lid, with ornate serif lettering and fine linework. The drawing sets contain coloured pencils in tin cases with a hinged lid and rows of 12 to 36 colours, a sharpened point and crisp lacquered barrels each in a matching hue with a gold ring. Erasers are rectangular blocks, rubbery cream and pink. The look is orderly, precise, industrial-vintage and nicely engineered.
