the sanctuary church, a large plain church built over the rock with the painted image of Christ, a long low building of whitewashed walls under a pitched metal roof with a simple gabled front and a small cross, standing at the head of the camp with crowds packed before its doors
candles burning in rows outside the church, hundreds of thin white and yellow tapers stuck upright on stones and long metal racks, their flames flickering together, pools and drips of melted wax hardening over the rocks beneath
the white front of the sanctuary with crowds at its doors, the plain gabled wall with a wide open door and a small bell tower, pilgrims packed before it
