Styled in a design language of understated, precise, timeless German craft. Surfaces are matt black enamel, polished and brushed silver chrome, finely pebbled black leather-like texture and brass showing through at worn edges. Compact rectangular forms have flat planes and crisp edges, with fine knurled bands. Palette is black, silver, signal red and white, with occasional olive, tan or red leather accents. Minimal, free of clutter, with perfectly even gaps and honest patina. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
