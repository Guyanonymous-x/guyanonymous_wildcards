Styled in a design language of elegant East meets West design that blends tailored modern cuts with Chinese tradition. Standing mandarin collars, frog knot closures and asymmetric side fastenings sit on slim, refined, graceful shapes. Fabrics are silk satin, brocade, jacquard, embroidered tulle and wool crepe, with embroidered or woven motifs of peonies, bamboo, cranes, clouds and calligraphy brush strokes. Palette favours jade green, imperial red, ink black, ivory, deep blue and gold thread, with delicate piping, braided trim and tassel details. A poised, quiet-luxury, nostalgic old-city mood in soft studio light.
