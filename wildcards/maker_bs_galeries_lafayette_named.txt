In the style of Galeries Lafayette, Belle Epoque grandeur, ornate and light-filled. A central Art Nouveau glass and steel dome in stained-glass colours of gold, green and amber rises above tiers of balconies with curved wrought-iron balustrades in gilded patterns. Cream stone with ornate carving, gold lettering, mosaic floors, sweeping staircases and glass canopies. Pale pink or cream fields carry thin black capital letters. Palette is gold, cream, emerald and amber. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
