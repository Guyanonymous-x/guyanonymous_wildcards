Styled in a design language of calm, rational, ornament-free functional design. Simple geometric volumes with softly rounded corners, tight even gaps and precise tolerances. Matte or satin surfaces in off-white, pale grey, black and graphite, with natural light wood or clear translucent material used sparingly. Small round or flush details sit neatly grouped on a strict grid, with one tiny accent of orange, yellow, green or red marking the one thing that matters. Fine regular perforations add texture. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
