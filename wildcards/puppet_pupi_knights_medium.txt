Sicilian opera dei pupi knight puppets, heavy carved wooden figures in shining metal armor, each about as tall as an adult's waist. Each knight has a carved and painted wooden face with dark eyes, a curled black mustache and rosy cheeks, framed by a polished metal helmet with a tall plume of red or white feathers. The armor is hammered brass and tin, embossed with crosses, stars and curling patterns, and it shines under the lights. __puppet_pupi_knights_var__.
