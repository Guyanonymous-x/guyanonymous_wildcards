Styled in a design language of Italian plastic design that turns industrial material into luxury. Forms are sculptural and sinuous, with smooth continuous curves, baroque silhouettes reinterpreted in modern moulded polycarbonate, and crisp square geometries. Surfaces are glossy, crystalline, transparent and tinted, in clear, smoke grey, amber, ruby, blush pink, sky blue and opaque black or white. Light refracts through thick translucent walls with soft edges and subtle bubbles, and some finishes are metallic, mirror or matte soft touch. Pattern is minimal, with faceted ridges and embossed textures. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
