Styled in a design language of Finnish stoneware and porcelain with simple rounded cylindrical and bowl shapes, stackable, with sturdy loop forms. Glazes in cream white, matte brown, ochre, forest green, mustard and speckled grey, or bright flat 1970s orange, yellow and brown abstract flowers and mushrooms in bold stencil and stripe patterns. Chunky, warm, earthy, practical and plain, with hand-painted irregularity and a cosy Nordic feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
