Styled in a design language of hand-painted hard rubbery finish with finely sculpted anatomy, realistic proportions and subtle airbrushed shading. Animals in bay, chestnut, grey and black with white socks, fine strand texture in manes and tails, glossy eyes, soft gradient painting on belly and muzzle and a satin matte finish. Fantasy creatures follow the same refined realism. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
