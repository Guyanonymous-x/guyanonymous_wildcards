Styled in a design language of sentimental, romantic, delicate and fragile porcelain. Tall, graceful, elongated bodies with slim limbs, small heads and flowing, swirling drapery whose folds ripple like wavy ribbons. Faces are serene, with softly closed eyes, a pale rosy complexion and tiny delicate hands. The surface is mostly matte, chalky and soft in pastel pale blue, grey-green, lilac, rose, cream, white and muted gold, with occasional glossy accents and fine hand-applied porcelain flowers. Bases are small and rounded with tiny flowers and ribbons, poses are quiet and tender, and light is soft. Deeper earthy tones appear in a rougher line. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
