In the style of Cadbury, rich deep royal purple dominates every surface in glossy, foil-like finishes. A flowing gold script in thick looping cursive is the signature mark, carried on swooping ribbon swirls in white or gold. Cream and white accents, gold borders, ribbon bows, rounded friendly curves and soft chocolate swirl graphics add generosity. Forms are regular and softly moulded with the cursive mark embossed. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
