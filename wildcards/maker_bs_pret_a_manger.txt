Styled in a design language of brisk, tidy, natural urban freshness. Surfaces are matte, with warm paper textures, clear glass and brushed steel accents. A secondary palette of leafy green, mustard, cobalt blue and berry pink appears in seasonal flat patterns of dots, leaves, snowflakes and confetti on bright wrapping. Stripes of burgundy on cream and small star sprinkles repeat as trim. Labels are plain, with thin rules and honest. Clean, healthy, quick and modern. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
