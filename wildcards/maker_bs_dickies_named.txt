Styled in a design language of hard-wearing American workwear identity. Palette is khaki, black, navy, charcoal, olive and stone, with the red, yellow and royal blue badge as the single bright accent. Fabrics are smooth, tightly woven twill with a slight sheen, crisp pressed finish, bar-tack stitching and plain flat seams. Clean, neat, sturdy, blue-collar and street-ready. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
