a Japanese hina doll display, a tiered stand of dolls set out for the doll festival in early spring. A stepped stand of seven tiers covered in bright red felt rises taller than an adult. On the top tier sit the emperor and empress dolls in front of a gold folding screen, the emperor in a stiff dark robe with a tall black hat, the empress in many layered silk robes of red, green and gold, her black hair falling long. __doll_hina_dolls_var__.
