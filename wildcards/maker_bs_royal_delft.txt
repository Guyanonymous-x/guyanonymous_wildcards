Styled in a design language of creamy tin-glazed white ground with vivid cobalt blue painted decoration, from pale wash to near-black. Scenes of windmills, canals, ships, tulips, villagers and landscapes are drawn with fine brushwork and tonal shading, and small figures sit among curling scrollwork and floral borders. The glaze is shiny, slightly bluish-white and pooled in places. Grids of simple blue motifs repeat with matching corner motifs. The mood is crisp, domestic, nostalgic and traditionally Dutch.
