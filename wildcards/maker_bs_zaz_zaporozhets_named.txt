In the style of ZAZ Zaporozhets, tiny, boxy and rounded, humble, endearing and utilitarian. Short stubby front, steep humped rear, small round lights in simple chrome-ringed settings, thin chrome trim and a tiny, low silhouette. Slim supports and big curved glass give an airy look. Colour is flat, cheap-looking gloss in pea green, cream, sky blue, light yellow, orange-red, brick red and grey, with one thin chrome line. Small wheels, plain caps and narrow proportions. Spartan detailing in thin metal with a single round instrument. Drably cheerful.
