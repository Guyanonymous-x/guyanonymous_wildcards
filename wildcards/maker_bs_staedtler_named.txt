In the style of Staedtler, functional, tidy German school and drawing sensibility in a distinctive scheme. Hexagonal forms are lacquered in bold bands of bright blue and black with a vivid yellow section, or a navy and red set, with gold or white lettering. Contrast bands are neat and the lacquer is flat and smooth, with a classroom-like feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
