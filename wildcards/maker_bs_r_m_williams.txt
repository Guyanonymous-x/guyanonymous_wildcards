Styled in a design language of rugged, practical outback leatherwork, sleek and refined, made from a single piece of leather wrapped and stitched with minimal seams, in chestnut brown, tan, black and oxblood. Details include subtle elastic gussets, a slightly pointed rounded end, stacked leather layers, a visible stitched edge and a smooth polished finish. Workwear touches use moleskin, oilskin, check cotton and wide leather straps in earth tones of khaki, sand, olive, navy and rust. Bush mood with red desert dust and golden light. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
