In the style of Vitsoe, modular shelving systems. Thin vertical aluminium tracks screwed to the wall with slim aluminium shelves hung from them on small pegs. Shelves are powder-coated in white, black or light grey, or made of smooth plywood in pale birch, with sleek clean edges. Wardrobe units are made of crisp panels in matte pale colours, with narrow slots and small detailing. Everything is clean, orderly and rectilinear with thin lines and no decoration. Mood is calm, minimalist and timeless.
