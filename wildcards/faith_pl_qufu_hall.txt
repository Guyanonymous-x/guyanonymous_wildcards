inside the hall, a statue of Confucius in robes and a flat-topped crown, a large seated figure in a gilded shrine between red pillars, dressed in a yellow robe with embroidered patterns, the crown a flat board with strings of beads hanging before and behind, a narrow tablet held upright in both hands
