an industrial structure whose whole purpose was to hold a very heavy mass of water very high up
converted to a dwelling in which the engineering is the entire architectural interest, a shaft of brick
concrete or steel lattice designed for a load far greater than any domestic use so the structure is never the problem
a tank chamber at the top which becomes the principal room and has panoramic glazing on all sides because it has the view and because it had no windows at all before
intermediate floors inserted into or using the original service levels
one room per floor so circulation is a stair and nothing else
a vertical plan that makes the building almost unusable for anybody with limited mobility unless a lift is fitted at great expense
no garden and often no parking
frequently a listing or a local landmark status because the thing is visible for ten miles, and a conversion cost per square meter that is among the highest of any building type
