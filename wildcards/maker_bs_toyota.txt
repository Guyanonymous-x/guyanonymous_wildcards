Styled in a design language of sensible, dependable mass-market design with a crisp modern edge. Shapes are angular and clean, with sharp shoulder creases, slab sides, sculpted upper surfaces and wide hourglass or trapezoid openings in glossy black. Thin swept lamp shapes with hooked inner lines and compact C-shaped lights add a sporty bite. Large black plastic trim around edges gives a tougher, practical feel. Materials are tidy grey fabric, soft-touch plastic and piano-black panels. Palette is super white, deep black, cool silver, signal red and bright blue. The identity is a simple three-oval emblem in red or chrome. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
