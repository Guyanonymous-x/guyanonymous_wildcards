In the style of Pantone, a square or rectangular colour chip with a flat solid swatch printed on glossy coated card, a white band along the bottom holding a small sans serif code and the colour name in black capitals, a thin white border. Fan decks of numbered strips with long narrow cards bound at a rivet so the colours spread in a rainbow, tidy grids of colour squares in every hue arranged by tone, and mugs, notebooks and posters bearing a single swatch. Clean Helvetica-like labelling, white background, consistent proportions, bold flat saturated colours such as vivid red, teal, violet and orange, a neat design reference system look with a catalogue feel.
