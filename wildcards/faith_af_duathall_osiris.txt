Osiris sits enthroned inside a shrine of carved and painted wood roofed with a row of rearing cobras, a man in his forties with green skin, wrapped close in white linen from shoulders to feet, wearing the tall white atef crown flanked by two curling plumes, the crook and flail crossed on his chest
Osiris sits on a cube-shaped throne patterned with small colored squares, his skin the black of fertile river mud, his white wrapped body and tall crown standing out, his sisters Isis and Nephthys, women in their thirties in close red and blue dresses, standing behind him with one hand on his shoulder
before the throne of Osiris rises a blue lotus flower on its stem, on its open petals the four sons of Horus, small wrapped figures with the heads of a man, a baboon, a jackal and a falcon, and falcon-headed Horus, a man in his thirties in a short kilt, stands guard at the shrine door
