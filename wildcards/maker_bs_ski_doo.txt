Styled in a design language of compact, low, cheerful, adventurous and retro 1960s and 1970s snow-country machinery. Bulbous moulded fibreglass volumes swoop down toward short curled forms, with a high bubble of clear glass, upswept ducktail curves, ribbed black rubber and exposed springs. Paint is bright and glossy, sunny yellow, orange, red, white or lime green, with bold contrasting stripes, large block letters and a stylised animal or speed-line motif. Chrome accents are small and shiny. Matching quilted one-piece clothing in bright colours. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
