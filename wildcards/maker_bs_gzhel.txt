Styled in a design language of folk decoration painted entirely in cobalt blue on bright white glaze. Brushstrokes shade fluidly from deep ink to pale sky blue, forming flowing flowers, leaves, scrolls, dots and drops that wrap around the form. Glossy smooth surfaces, a clean blue and white contrast, rounded plump forms and a joyful, fresh, handmade charm. Decoration is hand-brushed with confident single strokes, leaving plenty of bright white ground around each motif. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
