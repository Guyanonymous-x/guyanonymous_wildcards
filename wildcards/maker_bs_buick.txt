In the style of Buick, 1950s American cars with a toothy chrome grille like a wide smile, large round chrome bumper guards, and a row of three or four round portholes on each front fender. A sweeping chrome side spear that dips down along the doors, a slightly bulbous body with curved glass and whitewall tyres. Bright wire or chrome-covered wheels with a tri-shield badge. Cabins of plush two-tone fabric with a wide chrome dash and a big round speedometer. Colours of cream, sea green, coral and maroon. Comfortable middle-class luxury, rounded and heavily chromed, dignified rather than flashy.
