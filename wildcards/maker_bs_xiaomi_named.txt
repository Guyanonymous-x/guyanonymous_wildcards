Styled in a design language of clean, affordable-minimal and friendly. Simple matte white plastic or glass forms with softly rounded corners, flat colour surfaces and small circular bumps. Palette is white, light grey and warm orange with candy accent colours in violet, pink, lime and sky blue. Packaging is plain kraft brown cardboard with a single small orange square mark, minimal black printing and tidy square layouts. Honest, practical and cheerful. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
