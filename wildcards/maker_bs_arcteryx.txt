Styled in a design language of minimalist, precise technical design with clean unbroken surfaces and very few visible seams. Matte laminated and welded fabrics with taped joins, flat storm flaps, sculpted articulated forms and laser-cut edges. Palette is black, graphite, deep navy, slate blue, glacier white and moss green, with vivid accents of fiery red-orange, bright yellow and cobalt blue in sharp colour blocking across shoulders and panels. A small stylised skeletal winged fossil creature emblem sits in white, black or tonal embroidery. Tonal pullers, hidden details, a tailored athletic cut and a quiet, expensive precision. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
