In the style of Gretsch Guitars, large hollow bodies with a flamboyant orange or deep red-brown lacquer finish, gold-plated accents and bold decoration. Details include twin cutaways, gold sparkle or white binding, a G-shaped cutout and tall, wide proportions. Hardware is flashy, with chrome, gold and tall knobs. Gold-plated surrounds frame the details. Alternate finishes include pearl white, two-tone green and sparkle. The mood is rock and roll, showy, rockabilly and retro.
