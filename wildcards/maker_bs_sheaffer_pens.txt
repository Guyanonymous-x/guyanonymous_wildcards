In the style of Sheaffer Pens, bulbous, torpedo-shaped fountain pens in glossy striped celluloid colours, such as pearl and jade green, marine black, grey, burgundy and blue. The cap has a thin white-dot emblem on top and a slim chrome or gold trim ring. A distinctive inlaid white dot sits at the top of the cap, and a long nib is gold with a triangular or hooded shape. Clips are straight, flat and sleek, with a spear or tapering form. Barrels are smooth, rounded, streamlined and glossy, in an Art Deco and early jet-age manner.
