In the style of Arita porcelain, refined thin white porcelain with a milky body, clear sparkling glaze and a great deal of generous empty space. Enamelled touches of red, green, yellow and sky blue are used sparingly. Outlines are elegant and crisp, including faceted and lobed edges. Airy, graceful, calm and exact, like quiet tea house tableware. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Arita porcelain in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
