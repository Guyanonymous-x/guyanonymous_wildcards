Styled in a design language of deep, aged, ceremonial and warm amber heritage design. Heavy, rounded-shouldered forms with a deep amber glow, dark cork or gilded accents. Cream or ivory paper tones with a gold embossed border, a tall mountain with a snowy twin peak. Rows of little gold stars signal quality. Rich brown, gold, burgundy and cream. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
