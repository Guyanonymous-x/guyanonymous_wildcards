Styled in a design language of Italian injection-moulded plastic in glossy, translucent polycarbonate, crystal clear, smoky, ruby red, fluorescent orange, lime and acid pink. Silhouettes are sculptural and sometimes baroque, with transparent ghost-like forms, smooth seamless curves and light passing through the material. Bright jewel colours and high-gloss reflections dominate, with pleated or bulbous translucent shapes. Playful, fashionable, pop and kitsch with modern luxury, shown in gallery-white settings with reflective floors.
