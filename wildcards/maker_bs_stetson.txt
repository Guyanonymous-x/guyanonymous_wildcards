In the style of Stetson, wide brimmed cowboy hats in felt of beaver or rabbit fur in silver belly, tan, brown and black, high pinched tall crown with a centre dent, a flat grosgrain ribbon band around the base with a small bow on the side, stiff curled brim edges, and sweatband interior. Also woven straw versions and fedoras. Rugged Western frontier icon, rounded dignified silhouette.
