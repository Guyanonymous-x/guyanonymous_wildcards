Styled after a rhythmic textile language of zigzag chevrons, flame-stitch peaks, wavy ripples and thin stripes, knitted or woven as continuous repeating bands. Colour moves in gradual tonal steps, with rows of teal, turquoise, coral, orange, mustard, raspberry, violet, olive, cream and black blending into shimmering optical vibrations. Some versions use soft sparkling metallic threads among matte wool yarns, others are fine open crochet-like knit with tassel-like twisted ends. The overall mood is warm, bohemian and artisanal, with a soft handmade hand. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
