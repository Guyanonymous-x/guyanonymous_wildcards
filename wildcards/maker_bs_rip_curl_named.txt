Styled in a design language of rugged Australian wave-riding graphics. Palette is faded oranges, sky blue, teal, mustard, sage, cream, black and coral, often garment-dyed with soft chalky tones and contrast stitching in cream. Surface graphics are retro beach club badges, sun and palm illustrations, skulls, roses, waves and tropical flowers in distressed screen-print layers. Black neoprene-like panels trimmed in bright colour contrast with soft brushed cotton and fleece surfaces. Sun-bleached, relaxed, adventurous and salty. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
