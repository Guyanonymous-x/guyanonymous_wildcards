In the style of Kenzo, joyful bright folkloric ready-to-wear of the 1970s and 80s, mixing kimono-inspired wide sleeves, loose smock shapes, patchwork, quilted jackets and layered ethnic prints in one outfit. Large flowers such as poppies and peonies, stripes, ginghams, checks and paisley are combined at clashing scale in saturated tomato red, yellow, turquoise, emerald, pink and purple. Fabrics are cotton, wool jersey, soft knits and light gauzy weaves, with a relaxed, unstructured, unisex cut, big knitted sweaters with tiger or jungle motifs, drawstring waists and bold stripes. Typical image is a cheerful, colourful, jungle-and-garden exotic fantasy, sunlit, cheerful, slightly naive, with fluffy hand-painted look.
