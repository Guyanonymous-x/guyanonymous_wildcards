The plainest light in the house, and the only room here lit for hygiene, one window with the lower sash in obscured or reeded glass, giving a flat bright diffused daylight with no direction at all, which the white tile multiplies until the room is the most evenly lit interior in the building
A nickel and brass fitting set returning hard specular highlights on every curved surface, so the room is full of small bright points
A gas bracket and later a single pendant with an opal shade, which at night makes the tiles gray and the nickel orange
The water closet compartment lit by a single narrow window or a borrowed light over the door, often leaving it the darkest 2 square meters on the floor; and no firelight, because the fire was blocked when the room was converted
