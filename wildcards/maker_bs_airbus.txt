Styled in a design language of smooth, calm, composed European design in a restrained palette of deep royal blue, bright cyan, pale sky blue and clean white. Soft gradients fade from dark blue to light blue across large curved panels, divided by sharp-edged light cyan rays or a simple abstract slanted letter A set in a circle or square. Lettering is heavy, wide, rounded geometric sans-serif capitals in white on blue or blue on white, with very tight, even tracking. Surfaces are glossy white paint, satin enamel and pearly metallic blue, always seamless and free of decoration. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
