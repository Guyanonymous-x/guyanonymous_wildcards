Styled in a design language of expressive, surreal and witty hand-painted or collaged illustration built on a single bold metaphor rather than a literal scene, such as a giant eye, a distorted face, a flower with a thorn or a childlike doodle on empty space. Colours are saturated and unusual, acid yellow, blood red, deep blue, hot pink, mustard, turquoise and black, with ragged brushwork, gouache texture, torn paper edges and uneven print texture. Lettering is hand-drawn, quirky, irregular and part of the composition, in large loose letters of contrasting colour. Rough paper grain. Poetic, dark, humorous and arty.
