Styled in a design language of austere and elegant compositions with no illustration and no border. Everything is set in a single classic roman lettering in rich black ink on thick cream handmade paper, with even spacing, generous margins and a perfectly balanced column. Only coloured initials in red or blue, drawn with flowing calligraphic flourishes, add ornament, and openings use large simple letters. Title areas are plain with tidy letter spacing. Surfaces are limp vellum or morocco with a simple gilt line pattern. The mood is pure, calm, disciplined and typographic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
