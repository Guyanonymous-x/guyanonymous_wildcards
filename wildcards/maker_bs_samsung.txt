In the style of Samsung, Korean consumer electronics. Thin glossy glass slabs with curved edges, metallic frames in black, silver, gold and blue, vivid AMOLED screens with saturated colours and nearly invisible bezels. Camera modules stand out in separate circular lenses on a vertical or horizontal strip. Televisions and appliances use thin brushed metal and glossy black or white panels with minimal touch controls. Interface graphics use rounded square icons, smooth sans-serif type and soft pastel colour gradients. Mood is polished, bright, flat-glass and modern.
