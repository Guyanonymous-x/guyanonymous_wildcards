Styled in a design language of Danish sterling silver shaped into soft, organic, sculptural forms. Surfaces are gently hammered or smoothly polished, with a cool, mirror-like glow that darkens to grey in recesses. Ornament draws on nature through rounded grapes, flowing vines, buds, leaves, daisies and abstract curving ribbons, set with the occasional cabochon of amber, moonstone or blue stone. Shapes are plump, rounded, flowing and balanced, later giving way to pure modernist smoothness with clean, tapering geometry. Palette is silver, soft grey, honey amber and white enamel. Lettering is tiny, neat and stamped, as a hallmark. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
