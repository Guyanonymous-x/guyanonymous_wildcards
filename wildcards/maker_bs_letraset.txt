In the style of Letraset, dry-transfer lettering sheets with rows of black or coloured typefaces printed on translucent sheets, with a waxy, slightly cracked appearance when rubbed down. Typeface samples are bold, expressive 1960s and 70s display faces, including chunky rounded letters, psychedelic swirls, inline shadows, stencil cuts, art-nouveau revival and space-age geometric forms. Sheets are laid out in a neat grid with tiny alphabets and punctuation, along with registration marks and a blue instruction grid. Words applied to paper are slightly uneven, with tiny gaps and imperfect baseline. The mood is cut-and-paste, punk poster and retro graphic design.
