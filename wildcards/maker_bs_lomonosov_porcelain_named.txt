In the style of Lomonosov Porcelain, thin white porcelain used as a canvas for striking graphic energy. Geometric abstract shapes in flat red, black and white, bold angular lettering, stars, factories and stylised figures in the manner of revolutionary agitprop. Also a classic cobalt net pattern with gilded cell edges and small white relief bumps. Flat bold colour, dynamic diagonal compositions, a stern idealistic mood and a historical museum feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
