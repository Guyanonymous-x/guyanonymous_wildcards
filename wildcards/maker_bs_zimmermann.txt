Styled in a design language of romantic, bohemian, sunny Australian femininity. Silhouettes are floaty and layered, with ruffles, tiered frills, puffed shapes, smocking, scalloped edges, broderie anglaise, delicate lace, embroidery and tie details in linen, cotton, silk and chiffon. Prints are painterly watercolour florals, patchwork blooms, paisley, ditsy flowers, tile motifs and polka dots in cream, ivory, blush, coral, sage, butter yellow, cornflower blue, rich red, terracotta and black. Layered mixed prints and gathered trims feel handcrafted, vintage and dreamy. Lettering is a clean, spaced, light serif wordmark in black or white on pale neutral grounds, giving a polished, airy and artistic look. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
