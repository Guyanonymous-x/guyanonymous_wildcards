potter's wheel, which is a spinning flat disc on which a lump of clay is shaped by hand while it turns
kick wheel, which is a potter's wheel turned by the foot kicking a large disc below
wire clay cutter, which is a thin wire with a wooden handle at each end, pulled through clay to cut it
wooden modelling tool, which is a flat or pointed stick for shaping clay
rib, which is a flat, curved paddle for smoothing the wall of a pot
kiln, which is a thick-walled oven with a front door, where clay is fired at high heat
glassblowing pipe, which is a long hollow steel tube with a mouthpiece, on which molten glass is gathered and blown
glassblower's jacks, which is a pair of tongs with two flat blades that squeeze and shape hot glass
glory hole furnace, which is a gas furnace with a small opening where glass is reheated
marver, which is a flat steel table on which hot glass is rolled
annealing oven, which is a box oven that cools glass slowly to prevent cracking
heat-proof gloves, which is thick gloves for handling hot ware
