In the style of Lalique, French Art Deco moulded glass in frosted opalescent and crystal clear, with relief decoration of flowers, birds, dragonflies, nude female figures, fish and leaping animals. Satin matte frosting contrasts with polished highlights, with pale ice-blue and amber tints, black enamel details and stylised geometric borders. Sinuous curves and a glowing crystal sparkle give a refined, elegant Parisian luxury. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
