Styled in a design language of dense, opaque overglaze enamels in five dark, saturated colours, namely deep green, Prussian blue, yellow, purple and aubergine red. A second manner uses a bright red ground with gold. Green is often laid on thickly as a background with a heavy, jewel-like gloss. Rich, theatrical, almost tapestry-like richness and a thick bright glaze. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
