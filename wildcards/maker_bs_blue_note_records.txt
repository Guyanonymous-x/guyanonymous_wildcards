In the style of Blue Note Records album covers, bold modernist graphic design with tightly cropped monochrome or duotone photography in blue, black and white, large asymmetrical heavy sans-serif type in unexpected angled lines, slabs of solid colour (orange, red or cyan) cutting across the photo, tilted and overprinted lettering, thin rules, grainy high-contrast portraits of musicians with a cigarette and a horn, and a cool, smoky, mid-century jazz club feel.
