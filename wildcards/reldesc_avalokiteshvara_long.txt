Avalokiteshvara with a thousand arms, the bodhisattva of compassion in Tibetan art, a white bodhisattva with eleven serene heads stacked in tiers and a thousand arms spread like a great halo, wearing silk scarves, jeweled crowns, earrings and necklaces, each palm holding an eye and the central hands pressed together at the heart, on a lotus throne against a blue sky of clouds and rainbow light
