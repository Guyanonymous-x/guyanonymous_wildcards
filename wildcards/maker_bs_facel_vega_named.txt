In the style of Facel Vega, rich, glamorous jet-age French grand luxury. Long, low, horizontal masses built from stacked pairs of vertical forms, very thin slender supports and generous polished chrome edging with a crisp, flat, squared-off finish. Palette of black, silver, bright red, cream and deep blue. Supple leather, polished metal fittings, fine wire-like patterns and white-faced round graphics. Opulent yet athletic, an expensive and slightly dangerous elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
