Styled in a design language of flat limited-colour cartoon design with confident thick dark outlines and solid fills with almost no shading. Shapes are simple, blocky and rounded, with big ovals, wide stylised noses and bold geometric simplification of anything natural. Palette is mid-century saturated orange, turquoise, mustard, brick red, avocado green, sky blue and brown, often as loose painted blobs and speckled textures with a slightly gaudy retro charm. Repeating motifs include stylised rocks, leaves and zigzag stone patterns. Lettering is chunky, hand-drawn, slightly uneven and outlined, often arched or stacked inside a bright emblem with a rainbow swirl ring or star. The look is breezy, cheeky and cheerfully retro. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
