In the style of Mil, Soviet helicopters. Machines are large, chunky and utilitarian, with a bulbous pod-like cabin, a round glazed nose, big round windows, a long tapering tail boom with a small stabiliser, a massive five-bladed rotor on a thick hub, and two big engines on top, exposed in a boxy cowl with exhaust pipes. Wheels are fixed on stubby struts. The paint is plain, either military olive-drab and matte green with stencil markings, or civil white and sky blue with a red star or a winged emblem and a broad blue or red stripe, with large black Cyrillic lettering and a registration code. Panels show riveted seams, patches, rows of screws and inspection hatches, and the surface looks hardworking and slightly weathered. The mood is rugged, heavy and Cold War.
