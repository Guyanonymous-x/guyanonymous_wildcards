Styled in a design language of quiet Parisian luxury rooted in saddlery and patient hand craft. A vivid saturated orange dominates, set against chocolate brown, cream, black, deep navy, bright pink and warm gold, usually in flat solid blocks. Surfaces are smooth or finely pebbled leather with crisp painted edges and visible, slightly slanted hand stitching in contrasting thread. Forms are structured, upright and trapezoid, with slim straps, small turn locks and gleaming palladium or gold hardware. Dense multicolour silk-twill prints show horses, bridles, chains, stirrups, flowers and feathers inside busy bordered squares with hand-rolled edges. Packaging is a plain orange lidded box tied with brown ribbon. Lettering is small, discreet, upright capitals. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
