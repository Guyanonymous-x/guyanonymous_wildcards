In the style of USPS stamps, small perforated rectangles with a thin white border, each featuring a single illustrated subject rendered in fine intaglio engraving or in colourful painted illustration. The word USA in clean sans-serif capitals and a numeral in white or black are tucked into a corner, with a fine tinted background and delicate cross-hatched engraving lines. Margin text, plate numbers and flag marks add detail. Patriotic Americana, mid-century commemorative theme, rich fine detail, limited ink palette of blue, red, green and sepia.
