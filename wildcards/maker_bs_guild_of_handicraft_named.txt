Styled in a design language of arts and Crafts silver, enamel and jewellery in a hand-wrought manner. Hammered metal shows visible tool marks, curved lines, wire twists and loose, flowing Art Nouveau lines. Objects look soft and slightly irregular, with a matte, pale silver surface and delicate suspended drops. The mood is romantic, medieval-influenced, simple and craftsmanlike. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
