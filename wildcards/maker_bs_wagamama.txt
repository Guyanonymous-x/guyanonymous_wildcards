Styled in a design language of austere, canteen-like Japanese-influenced minimalism with a cheerful bustle. Long communal wooden benches in a bright minimal hall with white walls. Thin paper in black and red in a hand-written style. Large simple bowls and plain utensils. Palette is black, red, white and pale wood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
