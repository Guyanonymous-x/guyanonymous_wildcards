the great gate of Ganzir stands at the edge of the earth, a high mud-brick gatehouse with a heavy cedar door, and the gatekeeper Neti, a man in his fifties with a shaved head and a long fleece skirt, holds its great bolt
the goddess Inanna, a woman in her twenties, passes down through the seven gates one by one, a gatekeeper taking a piece of her finery at each, her tall crown, her lapis beads, her breast pins, her gold ring, her measuring rod and her robe, until she stands wholly naked at the seventh
the dead cross the dark river of the underworld in a narrow reed boat with upturned ends, poled by a stern ferryman in his fifties with a long dark beard, the far bank lost in gray dust
