in the middle, beside a palm-lined river, stands the drabsha, the Mandaean banner, a cross of two olive wood poles draped with a white silk cloth and sprigs of myrtle, and beside it a mandi, a small reed and mud hut with a pool of water before it
in the middle Ptahil, a figure in his fifties in a dark robe, shapes the earth from black clay, its mountains, rivers and date palms rising under his hands, the seven planets circling above him in a ring
