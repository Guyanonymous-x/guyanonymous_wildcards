a set of glove puppets with carved wooden heads, each about as long as an adult's forearm. Each puppet is a soft cloth body that slips over an adult's hand, the forefinger going up into the head and the thumb and middle finger into the two little arms. The heads are carved wood painted in bright colors, a king with a gold crown and a curled gray beard, a queen with a white veil, a red devil with small horns and a brown dog with floppy ears. The cloth bodies are sewn from bright satins and felts, purple and gold for the king, blue for the queen, red for the devil, with tiny carved wooden hands stitched to the end of each sleeve. The paint on the noses and chins is rubbed down to bare wood from years of handling. __puppet_glove_puppets_var__.
