In the style of Kutahya and Iznik ceramics, intense colour painted under crystal-clear glaze, with cobalt blue, turquoise, sage green, tomato red and white. Symmetrical, flowing patterns of tulips, carnations, hyacinths, rosebuds, long saw-edged leaves and curling arabesques repeat across surfaces, with radiating floral rosettes and tile-like repetition. Glossy, bright, crisp and elegant, evoking mosque walls and bazaars. Colour areas are crisp and flat, with fine dark outlines and bright white ground showing between motifs. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kutahya and Iznik ceramics in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
