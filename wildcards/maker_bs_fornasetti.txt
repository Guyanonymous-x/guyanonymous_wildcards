Styled in a design language of glossy lacquered surfaces covered in printed black and white engravings with surreal repeating motifs such as a woman face with half-closed eyes, sun faces, playing cards, butterflies, keys, hands, architectural arches and classical ruins. Deep black, cream, bold red and gold accents with gilded edges. Dense graphic linework and trompe-l oeil illusion are applied to any shape. Witty, theatrical, cultured, ornamental and mysterious. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
