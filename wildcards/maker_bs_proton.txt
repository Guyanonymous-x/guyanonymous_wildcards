In the style of Proton, Malaysian national cars of the 1980s and 1990s. Saloons and hatchbacks are boxy, compact and upright, with flat sides, a straight shoulder line, slim chrome bumpers, rectangular headlamps and a plain black grille carrying a small badge. Early cars wear a dark blue shield with a yellow crescent and a fourteen pointed star, later a yellow tiger head in a green roundel. Paint is solid gloss in colours such as white, red, silver, dark blue and champagne gold, with black plastic trim around the windows, simple steel wheels with hub caps or small alloy wheels with five slim spokes. Coupes have a sloping roof, pop-up style tail lights and a low front. Interiors are plain grey cloth with simple dials. The family image shows a shiny car in front of palm trees and low brick houses. Mood is proud, modest, practical, optimistic and sunlit.
