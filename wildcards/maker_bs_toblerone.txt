Styled in a design language of forms built from repeating triangular peaks, like a row of snowy mountains, with sawtooth silhouettes, sloping sides and crisp faceted geometry. Palette is warm honey gold, cream yellow and sunlit tan, with deep red, dark brown and navy accents on white. Surfaces are matte card, satin foil and light embossing with a clean tactile finish. Lettering is tall, sturdy, outlined serif capitals with subtle shadow, often sitting in a long horizontal band. The emblem is a mountain silhouette in solid gold or yellow, with a hidden bear shape tucked into its negative space. Triangular tessellations, stepped zigzag borders and tidy geometric rhythm create an Alpine, wholesome and cleverly witty personality. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
