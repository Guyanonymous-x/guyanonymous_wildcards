In the style of Savonnerie Manufactory, thick, plush velvet texture with a sculptural, monumental pattern. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Wide borders imitate carved mouldings, with fruit and flowers in the corners. The feeling is of a royal palace, sumptuous, ordered and heavy. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Savonnerie Manufactory in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
