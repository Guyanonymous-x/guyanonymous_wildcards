Shot through a deep black-and-white 830nm infrared filter and converted to black and white, the image shows a crisp black-and-white image, with a near-black sky, brilliant white leaves and grass, and inky water. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
