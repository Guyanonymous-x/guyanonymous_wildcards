filling the picture, inside the uma, a long dim room with the skulls of pigs, monkeys and deer hung in rows from the rafters, trophies of hunts, a wide hall with a floor of broad worn planks and walls of dark wood, the smoke-blackened roof beams above crowded with yellowed skulls, many of them painted with dark lines and tied with dry leaves and tassels, daylight coming in only from the porch end
in the middle of the floor, a hearth with a fire, a square box of earth and ash set into the plank floor with three blackened stones in it, a small fire of split logs burning orange under a sooty cooking pot, a rack of firewood and drying meat hung above it and bamboo tubes leaning beside it
on the side wall, drums and bows hung on the wall, long slim drums made from hollowed palm trunks with skins of snake and lizard hide tied on with rattan cane, and tall hunting bows of dark polished wood with bamboo quivers of arrows, all hanging from pegs on the plank wall beside woven baskets
