Coaybay, the land of the dead, a dark green land of guava trees and round thatched houses, the opía at ease in its plazas after dark, Maquetaurie Guayaba seated among them on a duho, a low curved stool of polished dark wood with a carved face at its front
Maquetaurie Guayaba, lord of Coaybay, shown as a zemí, a crouching figure of dark wood with a skull-like face, round eyes of white shell and ribs showing, seated in the midst of the dead
Opiyelguobirán, the zemí in the form of a dog on four legs, carved of dark wood, stands at the way into Coaybay beside a dark lagoon, the dead walking past him toward the guava groves
