In the style of Pelikan, piston-filling fountain pens with a plump cigar-shaped barrel and cap in celluloid or resin. Classic models have a black cap, a translucent ink window and a green striped, tortoiseshell or black body with gold-coloured trim bands. The cap clip is a slender arrow or a bird-shaped tail, and a gold nib bears fine engraved scrollwork. Later versions appear in bright blue, red and white stripes, along with a rounded, thick body. Inks and boxes use deep green and white, with a pelican and chicks emblem. The look is tidy, German, sturdy and luminous.
