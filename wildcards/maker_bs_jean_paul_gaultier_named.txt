In the style of Jean Paul Gaultier, cheeky, theatrical, camp couture craft with navy and white horizontal stripes, conical pointed satin curves with exaggerated cups, sailor motifs, tattoo-print mesh, trench coats, kilts for men, pinstripe suits, mixing masculine and feminine codes, cinched waists and bold makeup, plus forms shaped as a female torso. French avant-garde. Playful, provocative and impeccably crafted, with a wink at tradition.
