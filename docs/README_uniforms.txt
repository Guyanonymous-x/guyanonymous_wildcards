Guyanonymous' Wildcards - uniforms, costumes and printed "fake outfit" T-shirts (v19-v20)
===================================================================================
602 uniforms, work clothes, ceremonial dress, costumes and cosplay archetypes, from the serious to the ludicrous, in 13 groups:
  medical, emergency, military (ancient to modern), aviation_sea_road, trades, hospitality_retail, sport_performance, ceremonial_world (religious, court, traditional dress from every region), costume_genre (fantasy, horror, sci-fi, steampunk, generic superhero and pirate archetypes), ludicrous (mascots, inflatables, novelty suits), hobby, formal (tuxedo, tailcoat, suits, gowns), cosplay_archetypes (145 generic fantasy, sci-fi, cyberpunk, steampunk, wasteland, superhero, villain, anime-inspired, tokusatsu-inspired, folklore, myth and horror archetypes: 20+ kinds of mage, knight and monster, many hero and sci-fi roles).
Traditional variants from different periods and places are included (Victorian nurse, plague doctor, Roman legionary, Swiss Guard, Evzone, Joseon palace guard, Qing court official, Ashanti chief, Sami herder, Highland piper and more). Original generic archetypes only: no brands, no real people, and no named characters from games, anime, comics or film.

PRINTED T-SHIRTS THAT PRETEND TO BE THE OUTFIT
There is a whole genre of novelty T-shirt (the "tuxedo T-shirt" is the classic) whose full-coverage print imitates a worn outfit: printed lapels, bow tie, buttons, cuffs and shoulders drawn flat on cotton, so a plain tee looks like a suit. Every uniform above has matching tee prompts in which the print covers the whole shirt and shows the torso, shoulders and arms of the wearer in the outfit, while the real collar, hem and sleeve seams stay visible.
  __uniform_tee_prompt__        one printed-tee prompt from any group      __uniform_tee__   same      __uniform_tee_<group>__   one group
  __uniform_tee_mix__           a free mix: any tee base + print style + any torso outfit
  parts for your own templates: clothing_uniform_tee_style, clothing_uniform_tee_base, clothing_uniform_tee_neck, clothing_uniform_tee_body, clothing_uniform_tee_note

v21 note on the printed tees
The tee prompts now insist on a true all-over print: the design fills the whole shirt edge to edge (collar band to hem, sleeve to sleeve end), the shirt's front is the wearer's chest and its sleeves are the wearer's arms in the outfit, and the prompt states that it is not a small centred graphic or a drawing of a garment on a hanger. Use __uniform_tee_fill__ and __uniform_tee_body__ in your own templates to reinforce this. If your model still draws a small centred picture, add the fill sentence twice or put it first.

v22: every tee prompt now also asks for the BACK of the shirt to be printed with the back of the outfit. New files: clothing_uniform_tee_back / uniform_tee_back_<group> (a view of the shirt from behind, back of the outfit printed edge to edge) and clothing_uniform_tee_back_note (the back sentence, for your own templates). The body wording was rewritten so the outfit is printed as panels running over the shoulders and down the sleeves, instead of a figure standing in the middle of the shirt.

v23: tee prompts now also say no background or shirt colour may show around the outfit, at the shoulders or on the sleeves (clothing_uniform_tee_clean), and that the arms hang straight down the sleeves with no arm or hand crossing the torso (clothing_uniform_tee_arms). Both sentences are available as files for your own templates.

v23: tee prompts now state that the outfit's own cloth fills the shoulders and sleeves (no background colour or plain patch around the print) and that arms and hands appear only at the sleeve ends, never across the front. Parts for your own templates: clothing_uniform_tee_clean, clothing_uniform_tee_arms.

v24: every tee prompt now also (a) says nothing is printed or attached below the shirt's sleeve hems and hem (no extra sleeves or layers sticking out), (b) keeps the T-shirt's own silhouette and sleeve length, so a long-sleeved jacket is printed only as far as the shirt's sleeve hems and no real sleeves, capes or layers get added, and (b) says everything is flat ink on opaque cotton, so lace, tulle, mesh, fur, armour and sequins are printed patterns, not real sheer or raised material. Parts for your own templates: clothing_uniform_tee_shape, clothing_uniform_tee_flat.

v24 (continued): each tee prompt now names the shirt's sleeve length to match the outfit. Outfits with long sleeves (coats, jackets, robes, gowns, tunics and so on) ask for a LONG-SLEEVED T-shirt, with the outfit's sleeves printed the full length to cuffs at the wrist hems and nothing extending past them. Outfits with short sleeves or bare arms (scrubs, polos, vests, aprons, sports kit) ask for a SHORT-SLEEVED T-shirt, with nothing printed or attached below the sleeve hems or hem. Parts: clothing_uniform_tee_shape_long, clothing_uniform_tee_shape_short.

v24 (sheer): outfits with sheer, lace, mesh, net or other see-through parts now ask for a flat printed skin tone underneath, weighted to a mid-tone brown tanned skin (clothing_uniform_tee_skin, clothing_uniform_tee_sheer).

v24 (arm parts): when an outfit has parts that sit on the forearm or hand (gloves, gauntlets, cuffs, rolled sleeves, arm guards, bandages, wrist items), the tee is made long-sleeved and the prompt says the sleeves are lengthened to the wrist so those printed parts appear at the sleeve ends (clothing_uniform_tee_arm).

v24 (no figures): every tee prompt states there is no head, face, hair, person or second copy of the outfit anywhere in the print, and no backdrop behind it (clothing_uniform_tee_nofig).

v24 (skin details): printed skin is still welcome. Where an outfit is open or bare, many tee prompts add a flat-ink skin detail on the weighted mid-tone brown tanned skin tone: collarbones, a printed cleavage line, chest hair, belly hair, bare midriff or forearms (clothing_uniform_tee_skin_detail). Sheer areas also get a printed skin tone underneath.

v25 - ADULT-SIZED ONESIES (onesie_*)
Every one of the 602 uniforms, costumes and cosplay archetypes now exists as an adult-sized one-piece onesie, in three modes. Every line says adult-sized.
  onesie_printed_<group>   a plain stretch-jersey onesie covered edge to edge in a flat print of the outfit (same trompe-l'oeil genre as the printed tees): hood = printed headwear, feet = printed footwear, sleeve and leg length matched to the outfit, fake skin under sheer parts, printed cleavage/chest/belly hair where the outfit is open. onesie_printed_back_<group> gives back views.
  onesie_plush_<group>     a soft three-dimensional onesie built from real layers of fabric and fabric-like materials (felt, fleece, minky, faux fur, sherpa, velour, quilting, faux leather, vinyl-look, mesh, tulle, foam padding, plus odd ones such as foil, flocked, EVA foam, tinsel): stacked layers, sewn-on collars, pockets, belts and badges, a hood shaped as the headwear, padded feet shaped as the footwear, a stuffed prop at the hip.
  onesie_hybrid_<group>    a printed onesie with raised plush layers (collar, pockets, belt, badges, medals) sewn over the print.
Each has _male / _female / _sexy_male / _sexy_female versions (onesie_<mode>_<version>, onesie_<mode>_<group>_<version>), clothing_onesie_gendered, and skin-tone versions in six tones (clothing_onesie_tone, onesie_tone_<tone>[_<version>]; mixed modes).
Random pickers: clothing_onesie_random (any mode, version, tone), clothing_onesie_printed_random, clothing_onesie_plush_random, clothing_onesie_hybrid_random.
Parts for your own prompts: clothing_onesie_type, clothing_onesie_feet, clothing_onesie_fabric, clothing_onesie_fabric_odd, clothing_onesie_layer, clothing_onesie_skin, clothing_onesie_style, onesie_*_note.

v26 onesie options: hood / gloves / feet
  Each onesie line now states whether the suit has a hood (or is hoodless with a collar), attached gloves or mitten cuffs (or plain cuffs and bare hands), and built-in feet (or is footless). The print or plush description follows: headwear is printed/shaped on the hood only when there is one, footwear only on footed suits, gloves only on gloved suits; otherwise they are left off. Gloves are more likely when the uniform has gloves, hoods when it has headwear, feet when it has footwear. Parts: clothing_onesie_hood, clothing_onesie_gloves, clothing_onesie_feet.

v27 tee/onesie fixes
  - Tees no longer print the lower garment: the whole shirt to the hem is the upper garment (clothing_uniform_tee_nolower); trousers, shorts, skirts and legs are not printed on tees (old "top of trousers at the hem" lines removed).
  - No backdrop anywhere, including sleeves and shoulders: no sky, sea, landscape or painted ground (clothing_uniform_tee_nobackdrop); also added to onesies.
  - Edge fix: outfit enlarged to the real collar, shoulder seams and armholes so no background/sky patches sit around neck, shoulders and arms (clothing_uniform_tee_edge).
  - v27 onesies: printed onesies are cloth wrapped over every sleeve/leg tube (no standing-figure, no background colour on sides, no printed limbs or skin; suit cut sleeveless/short-legged where the outfit shows bare limbs) (onesie_*).
  - v27 onesies also: no face/hair/ears on hoods, no crowd or scene, print styles never ask for a photograph of the outfit.
