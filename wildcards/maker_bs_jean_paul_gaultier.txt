Styled in a design language of cheeky, theatrical Parisian pop-couture. The core pattern is navy and white horizontal Breton stripes, with variations in black, red, turquoise and pale blue. Shapes mix masculine and feminine cues with cinched, sculpted curves, pointed cone silhouettes and sleek hourglass forms. Colour is witty, saucy and bold, with a playful, ironic, avant-garde attitude. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
