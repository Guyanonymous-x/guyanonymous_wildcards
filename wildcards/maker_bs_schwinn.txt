Styled in a design language of American mid-century craft in candy enamel and bright metal. Colour blocking in cherry red, tropical turquoise, canary yellow, deep green and cream. Gleaming chrome edging, rivet studs, glossy lacquer and enamel pin finishes. Balloon-round, streamlined, swooping forms with playful, sturdy, optimistic nostalgic character. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
