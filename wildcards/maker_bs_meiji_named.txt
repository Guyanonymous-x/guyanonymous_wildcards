Styled in a design language of tidy, friendly, nostalgic Japanese convenience design in red, chocolate brown, cream and white. Bright red fields with a white script mark, silvery metallic accents, and clean cream panels carry a small simple illustration. Charming shapes pair a narrow stem with a rounded dark cap. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
