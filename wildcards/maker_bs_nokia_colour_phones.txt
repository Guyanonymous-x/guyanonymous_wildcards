Styled in a design language of compact, rounded, lightweight plastic forms with soft curves and a slightly raised grid of curved rubbery details. Interchangeable shells in bold pop colours such as lime green, cyan blue, hot pink, orange, translucent clear, camouflage and glossy metallic, with printed pattern covers and soft rounded bases. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
