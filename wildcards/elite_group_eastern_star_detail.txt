The Worthy Matron wears a wide sash of white satin across her body, a corsage of red roses on her shoulder and a gold jewel in the shape of a five-pointed star.
A gavel and a small leather-bound book lie on a table beside the Worthy Matron's chair, next to a crystal vase of white lilies.
Each officer holds a small staff topped with a gilded five-pointed star, its point's color painted on a ribbon tied below the star.
