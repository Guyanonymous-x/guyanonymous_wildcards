a French bisque porcelain doll from the late 1800s, about as tall as an adult's arm. Her head is unglazed bisque porcelain with a soft matte finish, tinted a pale creamy pink, with blushed cheeks, feathered painted brows and a small closed mouth painted deep rose. Her eyes are large paperweight glass eyes of deep blue, so clear they seem to have depth, fringed with fine painted lashes. A wig of real mohair falls in long ringlets over her shoulders under a wide bonnet trimmed with silk flowers. Her body is jointed wood and composition with ball joints at the elbows and knees, and her small hands have separately molded fingers. She wears a dress of silk with a dropped waist, layers of lace at the hem and the cuffs, and tiny leather shoes with rosettes. __doll_french_bisque_var__.
