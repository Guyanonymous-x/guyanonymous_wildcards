Styled in a design language of polished brass and copper tubes running along ceilings and walls of shops and offices, bell-mouthed brass terminals with hinged flaps, cylindrical leather-and-brass or felt-ended carriers, small wooden cash-desk booths, cast-iron brackets and an ingenious, bustling Edwardian commercial character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
