In the style of Taiwan Beer, bottled lager with a clear, restrained identity. The classic bottle is a standard brown glass longneck with a white and green paper label bearing a small crest like emblem, a bold sans serif wordmark and a golden ring of wheat. Premium versions switch to a gold and white label, and a Gold Medal version uses green glass with a white, red and green label. Draft versions appear as a solid green bottle with printed text and no paper. Cans carry a white background with blue stripes and green lettering for the classic, or glossy gold, blue and white for others. Imagery includes malted barley ears, a gleaming gold medal rosette, and clean foam topped glasses against a plain pale ground. Beer halls are built in a log cabin style with wooden beams, long benches and stir-fried seafood on round tables, under neon green signs. Mood is cheerful, social, hot-evening and straightforward, with strong green, gold and white colour blocking.
In the style of Kweichow Moutai, ceremonial Chinese baijiu bottles and gift packaging. The bottle is a squat cylindrical white porcelain flask with a short neck, glossy milk white glaze and a slight shoulder curve, topped with a red ribbon tied over a wax or plastic seal, and often a tiny hanging tag or flying fairy emblem. A bright vermilion label wraps the middle, edged in gold, printing the name in large black or gold brush style Chinese characters with a flowing line of small text beneath. Special editions are made in red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Gift boxes are lacquer red hard cartons or wood with lining of yellow silk and gold embossing, closed with a white cord and sometimes a golden bow, with a bouquet of two small cups beside the flask. Mood is formal, festive, prosperous and traditional, with an auspicious palette of red, gold and white against dark polished tables and banquet settings.
In the style of Rooh Afza, a South Asian rose syrup with a long running label. The glass bottle is tall, square shouldered and slightly ribbed, filled with a deep ruby red syrup, with a metal screw cap in red or gold. The paper label wraps the front in a vivid red field with a decorative border of small Mughal style flowers, a cartouche for the name in swashing Latin and Arabic script lettering in white and yellow, and illustrated bunches of roses, fruits, herbs and leaves drawn in a fine old lithographic manner. Plastic versions keep the same label art. Advertising shows a tall frosted glass of pink milk with ice cubes, rose petals, mint leaves and condensation, against a warm red or pale pink background. During the summer fasting month it appears in iced jugs on a tray with dates. Mood is nostalgic, cooling, floral, sweet and festive, with bright red, gold, deep green and cream.
In the style of Kingfisher Beer, an Indian lager with a bird emblem. The bottle is a slender brown glass longneck with a metal crown cap, wrapped in a paper label with a rounded top, cream or white ground, a bold red band and a gold rim. A kingfisher bird in profile with a bright blue back, orange belly and long dark beak perches or dives, positioned above a name in strong upright serif capitals, with a small crown icon. The tall can is cream and red with the same bird, often shown sleek and glossy. Promotional items include coasters in a thick card with the bird, ice buckets and straight glasses with a gold ring. Sponsorship graphics appear on racing cars and cricket stadium banners in strong red and white. Mood is social, festive, hot afternoon, poolside and glamorous, with warm red, gold, cream and a flash of electric blue.
In the style of Singha, a Thai lager with a heraldic lion. The bottle is amber brown glass with a long neck and a metal crown cap, wrapped in a label with a deep red and gold border. At the centre stands a mythical lion drawn in a rearing pose, with a curly mane, tufted tail, raised paw and fierce expression, in old engraving style, flanked by ornamental curls, a crown emblem and a royal garuda crest. The name is set below in bold upright capitals in gold or white. Cans are deep red with gold and white detail and a glossy finish. Bar mats, buckets and glasses repeat the lion. Gold foil neck labels add a sense of ceremony. Photographs show chilled bottles dripping with condensation beside spicy grilled food on banana leaf and a beach setting. Mood is sunny, tropical, festive, lively and bold, using gold, red, brown and cream.
In the style of Tiger Beer, a Singapore lager with a blue and gold tiger. The bottle is a clear or green-tinted glass longneck with a metal crown cap and a label in deep navy blue with a bold arched gold border. The central emblem is a leaping tiger in orange gold stripes on black, rendered in dynamic ink lines, set above or beside the name in big upright capitals in yellow. A small crown, malt ears and a ribbon give a heraldic effect. Cans are navy blue with a glossy surface and gold highlights, sometimes in metallic silver. Marketing uses street art, bright orange, black and blue paint splashes, stylised tiger stripes across city scenes and festival stages. Bottles in a bucket of ice at a hawker centre with red plastic tables are a frequent picture. Mood is cool, bold, energetic, nocturnal and urban, using navy, gold and hot orange.
In the style of Ararat Brandy, a historic brandy house from Yerevan. The bottle is a heavy glass with a round shoulder, a long neck and a dark cork or a gilded stopper, filled with deep amber liquid. The label is a cream or ivory paper with a gold embossed border, a tall mountain with a snowy twin peak, the name in strong serif capitals, a numeral showing the age in years inside a gold seal, and a small vignette of a distillery. A phoenix emblem in red appears on newer labels. Presentation boxes are rich wooden or velvet-lined cases with metal clasps, and a snifter glass beside. Vintage stars are drawn as little gold stars in a row. Mood is deep, aged, ceremonial, warm and amber, with rich brown, gold, burgundy and cream.
In the style of Castle Lager, a South African lager with a heraldic castle. The bottle is a brown glass longneck with a metal crown cap and a paper label with a cream, gold and green design, with a fortress of crenellated towers and a flag in an oval seal above the name in strong capitals. Cans are glossy green or silver with the castle device and a strip of gold. The sister light beer comes in a green bottle with a cold-indicator on the label that changes colour. Imagery features braai fires, rugby fields, jazz clubs, township street scenes and dusty plains, with beer being poured into a glass with a creamy head. Bottle crates are yellow plastic with the logo in black and red. Mood is sociable, relaxed, sunny, outdoorsy and communal, with amber, green, gold and cream.
In the style of Tusker Lager, a Kenyan lager with an elephant emblem. The bottle is a clear or brown glass longneck with a metal crown cap, carrying a paper label in cream and red with an upright elephant head in profile with long curved tusks, drawn in bold black ink, above the name in strong capitals. Cans and crates use red, gold and black with the same elephant, and glossy bottle neck wraps in gold foil. Bars and sign painters decorate walls with big hand painted logos and slogans in bold yellow and red. Photos show friends around a wooden table beneath thatch, with grilled meat and ugali, on dusty roadsides. Mood is communal, sunny, savannah, relaxed and proud, using red, gold, black and cream. Promotional crates are stacked beside kiosks with corrugated iron roofs, and coasters, bottle openers and branded plastic stools repeat the elephant in bold black on bright red.
In the style of Sapporo Brewery, Japanese lager packaging with a star emblem. Cans are tall and slim, with a black Black Label version in matte black with a bold gold star and name, and a standard silver can with a red star in a circle. Labels on brown bottles are cream and gold with a five pointed star and thin ruled borders. Special editions use a deep green with gold edging. Large format bottles come in silver foil wraps. The star is drawn in a crisp geometric outline, often placed at the centre of the can or bottle, with a wordmark in strong serif or slab capitals. Beer is golden with a thick white foam head. Photographs show frosted mugs on a counter in an izakaya, with yakitori skewers and paper lanterns. Mood is crisp, masculine, social, evening-lit and no-nonsense, with black, gold, red and silver.
