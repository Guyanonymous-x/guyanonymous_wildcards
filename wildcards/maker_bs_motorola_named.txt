Styled in a design language of bold tech-era and metallic. 2000s forms are razor-thin with brushed aluminium finish, etched flat metal and blue backlit glow. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
