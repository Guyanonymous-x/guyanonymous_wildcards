Styled in a design language of comfortable middle-class luxury of the 1950s, rounded and heavily chromed, dignified rather than flashy. A toothy chrome grin of many small bars, large round chrome bullet shapes, a row of three or four round portholes and a sweeping chrome spear along the side. Slightly bulbous, plump forms and a tri-shield motif. Cream, sea green, coral and maroon. Warm, prosperous and reassuring. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
