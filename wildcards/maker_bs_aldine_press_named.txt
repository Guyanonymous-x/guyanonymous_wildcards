In the style of Aldine Press, small, portable, clean and balanced forms with crisp roman lettering and open round letters, and a slanted, narrow italic with joined strokes. Layouts are uncluttered, with generous margins, justified text, simple capitals and almost no illustration. A tiny emblem of a dolphin coiled around an anchor appears in black ink on cream laid paper. Surfaces are soft vellum or brown leather with blind tooling and gilt edges. The look is elegant, humanist, scholarly and compact, with quiet, evenly inked colour.
