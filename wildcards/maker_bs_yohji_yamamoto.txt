Styled in a design language of voluminous draped black design cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette becomes a dark shadowy cloud. Materials are matte wool, crinkled cotton, heavy linen, felted wool and fluid jersey, with raw edges, big collars, tilted lines, trailing hems and long scarf-like panels. Palette is overwhelmingly black, then charcoal, deep navy, off-white and rare blood red. Slouchy, androgynous and undone, with flat understated details and occasional large calligraphic or graphic prints in white on black. Poetic, melancholic, monastic, dim moody light, wabi-sabi imperfection. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
