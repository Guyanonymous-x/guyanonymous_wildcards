In the style of Cath Kidston, sweet, cosy, nostalgic English vintage chintz with scattered cabbage roses in red, pink and blue, ditsy sprigs, spotty polka dots, ticking stripes and cherries, printed on cotton and oilcloth with a cheerful cottage feel. Cream or pale duck egg backgrounds, fresh red, powder blue and pink, trimmed with gingham, with vintage enamel details and 1950s housewife charm.
In the style of Muji, no-brand minimalism, quiet, modest and thoughtful. Plain, simple, softly rounded geometric forms with no logos, no ornament and no unnecessary features. Natural, raw, unbleached materials such as pale wood, kraft paper, cotton, linen, white ceramic and matte plastic in off-white, beige, oatmeal, grey, charcoal and muted navy. Finishes are matte, soft and tactile, with neat even stitching and honest construction. Lettering is small, plain, lightweight sans-serif in black or grey on neutral labels.
In the style of Alessi, Italian postmodern design with mirror-polished stainless steel and bright resin accents in red, yellow, pink, blue and green. Forms are playful and anthropomorphic, built from rounded cones, spheres and cylinders on spidery legs, with cute faces, exaggerated protrusions and cheerful little figures. Witty, toy-like and sculptural, treating everyday objects as pop-art ornaments, with soft shadows and cheerful display.
In the style of Fornasetti, glossy lacquered surfaces covered in printed black and white engravings with surreal repeating motifs such as a woman face with half-closed eyes, sun faces, playing cards, butterflies, keys, hands, architectural arches and classical ruins. Deep black, cream, bold red and gold accents with gilded edges. Dense graphic linework and trompe-l oeil illusion are applied to any shape. Witty, theatrical, cultured, ornamental and mysterious.
In the style of Habitat, British swinging-sixties high street design, simple and affordable, with cheerful bright striped and checked cotton, white and primary-coloured enamel, chunky pine, stackable wood, earthenware, white porcelain, wire, paper lanterns, rush matting and bright rugs. Colours are red, yellow, white, orange and natural pine. Casual, youthful, continental bistro spirit with plants, bare floors and plain white walls, sociable and unfussy.
In the style of Le Creuset, French enamelled cast iron, heavy, rounded and generous, with domed volumes, fat loop shapes, a thick rolled edge and a bulbous finial. Finished in high-gloss glazes of flame orange, deep red, Mediterranean blue, sunny yellow, forest green and cream, with matte black interior and a gradient volcanic orange. Warm, sturdy, rustic farmhouse glamour in matching shiny colours.
In the style of Staub, French cast iron with matte black enamel interior and darkly glossy, speckled exterior in grenadine red, deep blue, graphite and basil green. Heavy rounded forms with textured domed tops crowned by a small brass or nickel animal-shaped finial, such as a cockerel, cat or pumpkin, and chunky vegetable shapes. Rustic, weighty, restaurant-grade and traditional French bistro.
In the style of Pyrex, American glass and opal milk glass, with rounded shapes that nest in sets, in sunny primary colours of red, yellow, green and blue, or printed with mid-century patterns such as barns, daisies, gooseberries, snowflakes and starbursts in turquoise, pink, avocado green, mustard and white. Clear glass paired with rolled edges.
In the style of Tupperware, cheerful domestic order in translucent, slightly frosted material with a matte waxy finish. Soft rounded corners, plump friendly volumes and snug, tight-fitting joints. Forms are designed to nest and repeat in neat, tidy modular stacks. No ornament beyond moulded rings and simple ribs. Bright, wholesome, practical, suburban and joyful.
In the style of Lodge, heavy, plain, indestructible forms in cast iron with a rough pebbly matte surface. Thick walls, rounded utilitarian volumes, sturdy loop-shaped details and generous practical curves, with no decoration beyond a few raised ridges and a small cast mark. Colour is matte charcoal black with glossy dark brown patina, slightly rusty edges and worn patches. Rustic, honest, campfire and cabin warmth, made to last generations and look better with use.
In the style of Nambu ironworks, weighty cast-iron forms with rounded, bulging volumes and a quiet, austere, wabi-sabi presence. Surfaces carry rows of small raised bumps like hailstones in a tight regular grid, or finely cast relief motifs of pine, cranes, blossoms and waves, sometimes with fine silver inlay. Finish is matte black, deep brown or rust. Small knob-like accents, gracefully curved dark metal details and short elegant projections. Simple, heavy, contemplative and ceremonial.
In the style of Royal Selangor, polished silvery grey pewter with a soft lustrous gleam and either smooth or hammered finish. Heavy-bottomed, rounded forms with simple curves, sometimes mixed with dark wood or leather accents. Engraved or embossed decoration of hibiscus flowers, bamboo, batik-like flowers and rope borders. Cool, bright, refined, with a tropical colonial elegance. Surfaces are clean and tactile, with ornament kept shallow and restrained against large plain areas of polished metal.
