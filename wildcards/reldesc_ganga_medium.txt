Ganga, the river goddess, a fair graceful goddess with long flowing hair and a gentle face, wearing a white sari with silver borders and jewels, her face gentle and fair, her long hair flowing like water, the white sari edged with silver, the water pot of brass, the lotus pink, the makara a sea creature with a crocodile body and a curling trunk-like snout, the river flowing down from snowy peaks in a silver ribbon
