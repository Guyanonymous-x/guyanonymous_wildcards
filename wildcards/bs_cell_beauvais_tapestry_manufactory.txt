__bs_subject_heritage__ styled in a design language of a playful, pastoral Rococo character. Scenes of gallant romance, children at play and fanciful animals are rendered in soft, sweet colour, pink, cream, sky blue, pale green and gold with brown outlines. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
