Styled in a design language of warm, handcrafted, scientific vintage modernism. Accents of orange, red and cream appear sparingly. Forms are slanted, wedge-like and sturdy with softly eased corners, exposed joinery, and a laboratory-meets-furniture honesty. Monochrome stamped badges and tidy technical diagrams in black on white. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
