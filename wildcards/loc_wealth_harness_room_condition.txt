the room of 1780 with two sets of carriage harness and six saddles
the room of 1860 at its fullest with four sets of harness, fourteen saddles, a state set in a case and a stud groom who ran the room, which is the arrangement here
the room of 1905 with the first motor car in the coach house and half this room's contents already ornamental, which is the beginning of the end
the room of 1930 with one set of harness kept, the saddles still used for hacking and the brackets half empty, showing eleven pale wall marks and nine brackets with nothing on them, which is a legible and melancholy condition
the room of 1960 with the harness sold at a country-house sale, the brackets removed and the fittings bought by a collector, which happened to most of them
the room today as a tea room, a shop, an office or a holiday let's sitting room, with the stove's flue hole in the ceiling plastered over and nobody knowing what the slate hearth in the middle of the floor was for, which is the commonest surviving puzzle in a converted stable
the room still in use in a working yard, with modern synthetic tack on Victorian mahogany brackets
the room mothballed, harness oiled and bagged, the stove swept
the room open to the weather, where the leather goes in five years, the mahogany in fifteen, and the slate hearth in the middle of the floor is the last thing standing and is the only evidence left of what the room was
