Styled in a design language of soft crackled ivory and cream-white glaze with freely brushed cobalt blue painting, and sometimes brown, green and iron-brown tones. Decoration is flowing and relaxed, with lotus, chrysanthemum, bamboo, birds, dragons, wave scrolls and key-fret borders, either brushed in blue or incised into the material. Forms have thick walls, swelling shoulders and a slightly uneven hand-thrown quality. Gentle, rustic, subtle and unpretentious. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
