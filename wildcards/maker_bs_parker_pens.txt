Styled in a design language of streamlined mid-century American optimism and polished engineering. Smooth tapered torpedo forms, gentle bullet curves and slim arrow-shaped accents standing proud from the surface. Glossy lacquer in burgundy, deep blue, forest green, jet black, grey and cream, with fine herringbone, vertical line and pinstripe engraving. Brushed steel, gold-filled and chrome metal bands in thin rings. Presentation boxes in satin-lined colour-blocked cards with thin gold rules. Refined, reliable, confident and quietly glamorous. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
