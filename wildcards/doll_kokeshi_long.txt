Japanese kokeshi dolls, simple turned wooden dolls, each a round head on a plain cylinder body, the tallest about as tall as an adult's forearm. Each doll is a smooth cylinder of pale wood for the body, topped by a large round head, turned on a lathe so the wood grain runs in fine rings. The faces are painted with a few fine strokes of black, two narrow curved eyes, small brows and a tiny red mouth, with black painted hair in bangs. The bodies are painted with simple bands and flowers in red, black and green, chrysanthemums or plum blossoms, with fine lathe-cut grooves in red rings around the top and bottom. The heads can turn a little on their bodies. __doll_kokeshi_var__.
