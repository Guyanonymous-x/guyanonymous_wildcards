In the style of Havana Club, Cuban rum packaging with an old-Havana, street-art flavour. The bottle is a tall squared-shoulder clear glass bottle with a sturdy cork and a small decorative neck tag, filled with amber or golden rum. The front label is cream or ivory paper with an aged, slightly weathered look, a big ornate nineteenth-century cigar-box-style cartouche with a gold and red border, a small oval vignette of a city fortress and a colonial tower, and lettering in tall wood-type capitals and rounded script. Contemporary versions add colourful screen-printed illustration, saturated flat inks in red, blue, yellow and green, hand-drawn musicians, dancers, palm trees, vintage cars and city skyline. Silver and gold rum labels use metallic foil and ribbed stamping. The overall mood is warm, rhythmic, nostalgic and tropical with a faded colonial architecture palette of mint green, terracotta, sun yellow, turquoise and sepia.
