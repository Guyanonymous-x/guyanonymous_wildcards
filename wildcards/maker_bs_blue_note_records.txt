Styled in a design language of modernist mid-century graphic design with a cool, smoky, rhythmic feel. Compositions are asymmetrical and built on strict grids, with tightly cropped duotone imagery in deep blue, black and white, interrupted by flat blocks of orange, red, yellow, cyan or green. Thin rules, circles, dots and angular bars add rhythm. Surfaces are matte ink on slightly textured paper with grainy halftone texture and visible overprint. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
