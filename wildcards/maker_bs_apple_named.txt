In the style of Apple, pared-back, precise and quietly luxurious. Single continuous forms with perfectly rounded corners, razor-thin edges, seamless joins and no visible fasteners. Materials are brushed aluminium, polished glass and soft-touch matte finishes in silver, space grey, white, black and a few muted pastel tints, with colour mainly coming from the screen or content. Lettering is clean, light to medium sans-serif with generous spacing. Everything is reduced to the essential, with ample negative space. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
