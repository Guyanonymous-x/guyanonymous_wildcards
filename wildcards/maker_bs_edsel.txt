In the style of Edsel, 1958 American cars with a tall vertical horse-collar grille set in the middle of a wide bumper, a pointed hood crease and a split nose, wingtip boomerang tail lamps and gull-wing chevrons at the rear. Side sculpting with a bright spear, a cut-away dip along the door, and pillarless hardtop coupes with a smooth cabin. Wheels with chrome rim and whitewalls. Interior with rolling-dome speedometer and push buttons on the steering hub. Colours of two-tone pink and black, turquoise and white, yellow and brown. Overwrought, odd, unforgettable.
