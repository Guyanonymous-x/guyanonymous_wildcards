Styled in a design language of a modest East German family saloon and estate with a squared-off body, thin chrome bumpers, a flat bonnet, a plain grille with vertical bars and two-tone paint in cream and turquoise or pale grey and maroon. Small round tail lamps, tall windows and a plain tidy dashboard. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
