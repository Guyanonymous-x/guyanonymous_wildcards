Styled in a design language of minimalist Scandinavian-geek graphic design. Everything is black on white or white on black with exact grids, thin technical outlines. Materials are anodised aluminium, matte white plastic and soft silicone in signal orange, light grey, cream and occasional pastel or neon pops. Forms are thin, flat, rectangular and precisely rounded, like clean engineering drawings made physical, playful and deadpan. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
