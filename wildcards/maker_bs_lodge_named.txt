Styled in a design language of heavy, plain, indestructible forms in cast iron with a rough pebbly matte surface. Thick walls, rounded utilitarian volumes, sturdy loop-shaped details and generous practical curves, with no decoration beyond a few raised ridges and a small cast mark. Colour is matte charcoal black with glossy dark brown patina, slightly rusty edges and worn patches. Rustic, honest, campfire and cabin warmth, made to last generations and look better with use. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
