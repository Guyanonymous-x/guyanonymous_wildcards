Mari kart priests in their sixties and seventies in long white linen coats, white felt hats and bast shoes stand at a fire in the grove holding out bread and a bowl of beer to the gods
Mari women in their thirties to their sixties in white embroidered dresses, aprons and tall pointed headdresses hung with silver coins carry pancakes, honey and beeswax candles into the grove
Oleg, a kart in his fifties in white, holds a white goose over the fire as another man, Erik, in his forties cuts its throat, the blood falling into the flames, cauldrons of broth steaming nearby
