Styled in a design language of small hard plastic look with oversized round heads, simple dot eyes, a curved smile line, C-shaped hands and stiff jointed limbs on a standardised block body. Bright primary red, yellow, blue and green plus brown and grey in a matte to satin finish. Scenes are built as small dioramas with thin moulded details and clip-on accessories. Graphics use yellow and blue accents, a cheerful rounded lettering style and soft even lighting.
