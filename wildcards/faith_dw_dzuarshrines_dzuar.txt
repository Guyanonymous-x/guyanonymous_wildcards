above the shrine rides Uastyrdzhi, the greatest of the dzuars, guardian of men and travelers, a man in his fifties with a gray beard in a white felt cloak and a low fur cap, on a white horse, a spear in his hand
beside the shrine stands Uatsilla, the dzuar of thunder and the harvest, a tall man in his sixties with a long white beard, holding a sheaf of golden wheat and a bolt of lightning, dark storm clouds behind him
at the hearth inside a stone house Safa, the dzuar of the hearth chain, appears as an old man in his seventies in a dark coat, one hand on the black iron chain that hangs over the fire
Tutyr, the dzuar of wolves, a man in his forties in a gray wolf-skin cloak, stands on a ridge above the flocks, a pack of gray wolves lying calm at his feet
