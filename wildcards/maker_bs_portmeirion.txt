Styled in a design language of lush, abundant botanical decoration embossed and printed on a cream or white ground. Naturalistic flowers, ferns, fruit, butterflies and bees are crowded with leaves and vines in the manner of a Victorian botanical print book. Rich colours of burgundy, fern green, gold and cobalt blue. Chunky forms, scalloped and wide-edged outlines and relief texture beneath the print. Cottage garden, apothecary, cosy and plentiful.
