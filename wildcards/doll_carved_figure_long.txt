a carved and painted wooden ceremonial-style figure, about as tall as an adult's forearm. It is carved from a single piece of wood, a standing figure with a large head, a long body and short legs bent at the knee. Its face has deep-set eyes inlaid with white shell, a broad nose and a straight mouth, painted with lines of red ochre and white clay across the cheeks. Its arms are carved close against its sides with the hands on the belly. Fine patterns of zigzag lines and dots are cut into the chest and back, filled with white pigment, and a band of woven fiber is tied at the waist. __doll_carved_figure_var__.
