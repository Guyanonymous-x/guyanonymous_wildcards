In the style of Viuva Lamego, Portuguese azulejo tile house. The tiles are square glazed ceramic tiles about fifteen centimetres wide, with glossy surface and slightly uneven edges. Walls and facades are covered in complete panels, mostly in cobalt blue on a creamy white ground, depicting baroque scenes with scrolling acanthus, cherubs, saints, garden scenes, ships, harbours, hunts, rural festivals and tiled frames made of swags and volutes. Other tiles show repeating patterns of geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau tiles have curving plants, lilies, irises, stylised maidens and delicate gold-green outlines. Railway stations, church walls, shop fronts and fountains are clad in these panels. The look is cool, bright, narrative and decorative, with a serene seaside light.
