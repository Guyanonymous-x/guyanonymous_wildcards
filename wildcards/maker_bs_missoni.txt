Styled in a design language of a rhythmic textile language of zigzag chevrons, flame-stitch peaks, wavy ripples and thin stripes, knitted or woven as continuous repeating bands. Colour moves in gradual tonal steps, with rows of teal, turquoise, coral, orange, mustard, raspberry, violet, olive, cream and black blending into shimmering optical vibrations. Some versions use soft sparkling metallic threads among matte wool yarns, others are fine open crochet-like knit with tassel-like twisted ends. Patterns can run diagonally, mirror at the centre or fade from dark to light. The overall mood is warm, bohemian and artisanal, with a soft handmade hand. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
