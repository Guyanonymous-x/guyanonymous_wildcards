in the high sky the sun god K'inich Ajaw, a man in his forties with crossed eyes and a filed T-shaped front tooth, rides the daytime sky, and the moon goddess, a woman in her twenties, sits in a crescent moon holding a rabbit
each of the thirteen layers of sky is a narrow band of deep blue edged in red, the gods of each layer seated in a row inside it, and at the very top the Principal Bird Deity spreads its jeweled wings
