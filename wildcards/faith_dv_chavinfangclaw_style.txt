carved as a flat stone relief, the figure cut in shallow grooves into smooth pale granite, every line curving and doubling back so that the body is made of smaller faces and snakes
painted in the manner of a Chavín cotton cloth from the desert coast, brown and red-brown lines painted on pale tan cotton, the god repeated in a row along the cloth
worked in the manner of Chavín goldwork, a thin sheet of hammered gold pressed up from behind into a raised figure, the snakes and fangs standing out in shining relief
modeled in the manner of Chavín pottery, polished black clay with the figure cut in deep curving lines and the hollows rubbed with red earth
