Denver International Airport, a real airport at the center of many conspiracy legends of secret bunkers beneath it. A huge airport terminal rises from a flat plain, its white fabric roof shaped as a row of tall pointed peaks, and at the entrance road stands a giant fiberglass statue of a blue mustang horse rearing up, its eyes red. Inside, the terminal's great hall has a white peaked roof and long rows of check-in desks, and in the baggage claim stands a bronze statue of a gargoyle sitting in an open suitcase.
