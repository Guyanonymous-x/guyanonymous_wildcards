Styled in a design language of youthful, confident, sharp modern design. The emblem is a bold, flat, connected wordmark of three joined letters drawn as one rhythmic continuous stroke with slanted, sharp-angled cuts, in black, white or vivid red, replacing an older oval ring version in chrome. Shapes are crisp, sculpted and sporty, with taut sharp lines, tiger-like pointed motifs and star-map dot patterns. Palette is signal red, jet black, clean white, cool grey and midnight blue, with bold two-tone colour blocking and the occasional bright lime or orange accent. Finishes are gloss black, satin metal and soft-touch matte. Lettering is slanted, heavy, geometric, smooth and modern with a dynamic forward lean. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
