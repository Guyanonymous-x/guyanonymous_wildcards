Styled in a design language of compact, practical Japanese design with a bold, sharply angled letter S emblem made from two opposing pointed strokes. Palette is red, cobalt blue, white and bright yellow label tones, with dependable greys, black and cheerful pastel accents. Shapes are small, tall, sturdy and friendly, with simple squared-off forms, gently rounded corners and clear flat panels. Finishes are matte plastic, painted enamel, ribbed rubber and sticker-like flat graphics. Reflective chrome versions of the emblem and bold racing-style decals add a lively, energetic touch. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
