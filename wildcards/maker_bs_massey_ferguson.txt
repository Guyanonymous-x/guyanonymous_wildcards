Styled in a design language of plain, sturdy post-war farm machinery. Bright red bodywork with grey structural parts, a rounded top line with vertical slatted detailing and chunky, honest forms. Exposed mechanical parts and simple open arrangements, with bold block lettering. Two strong colours, red and grey, are used flat and simply with no gradients. Honest, tough, utilitarian, no frills, and rooted in mid-century working farmland. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
