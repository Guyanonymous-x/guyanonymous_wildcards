Styled in a design language of French white hard-paste porcelain with delicate hand-painted pastel roses, violets and wildflower sprays, pale pink, mint and lavender scalloped edges with gold rims, and moulded basketweave and lace patterns. Thin, translucent bright white body, soft floral garlands, rounded forms with curled details. Light, romantic, feminine, cottage garden and Belle Epoque in charm, with a smooth, glassy finish and a gentle, fresh colour palette throughout. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
