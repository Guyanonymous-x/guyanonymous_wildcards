Styled in a design language of French silver plate with strong Art Deco geometry, smooth cylindrical and faceted shapes. Mirror-polished silver with ebony or wooden accents, thick fluting, banded edges and pared down lines. Crisp elegant reflections, luxurious white linen and crystal, and a grand ocean liner salon mood, with heavy, substantial weight and a simple, disciplined rhythm of repeated bands. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
