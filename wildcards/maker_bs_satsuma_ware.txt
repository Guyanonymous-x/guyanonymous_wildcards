In the style of Satsuma ware, Japanese earthenware with a cream, finely crackled ivory glaze, covered in miniature, densely painted scenes of samurai, geisha, temple gardens, Mount Fuji and cherry blossoms in vivid enamels of red, green, blue, and lavish raised gold. Elaborate vases with lion-dog handles, koros with pierced lids, tea sets, lavish gold borders with brocade pattern bands. Highly detailed, glowing, export-style Meiji era jewel-like cabinet piece.
