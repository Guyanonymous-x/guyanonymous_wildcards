an antique doll's house, a tall wooden miniature house with its front swung open. The house is about as tall as an adult's waist, built of painted wood with a pitched roof of tiny shingles, a front door with a brass knocker and rows of real glass windows. Its whole front swings open on hinges to show the rooms inside on three floors, linked by a narrow staircase. Each room is fully furnished at a tiny scale, a kitchen with a black iron range and copper pans, a parlor with a velvet sofa and a fireplace, a bedroom with a four-poster bed, wallpaper with small printed patterns and rugs on the floors. Small dolls of porcelain in old-fashioned clothes stand and sit in the rooms. __doll_dolls_house_var__.
