In the style of Parker Pens, streamlined fountain pens and ballpoints with a cylindrical, slightly tapered body. Barrels come in glossy burgundy, blue, black or grey with a fine herringbone, vertical grooves or smooth finish. Caps are brushed stainless steel or gold-filled, with a ribbed texture, and a distinctive arrow-shaped clip that stands proud from the cap. A hooded nib is covered by a smooth tapered shield. Trim is thin chrome or gold bands. The mood is mid-century post-war optimism, with sleek, bullet-like forms and good engineering.
