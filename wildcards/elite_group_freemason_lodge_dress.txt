The members wear dark suits, white shirts, black ties and plain white lambskin aprons tied at the waist with thin white strings, and white cotton gloves.
The officers' aprons are white edged with a band of sky blue, and the Master's apron carries three small silver shapes of an upside-down T on its flap.
The Master wears a black top hat while the lodge is at work, and the other members sit bareheaded in dark suits, their aprons tied over their jackets.
