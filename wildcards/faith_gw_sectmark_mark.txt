the tripundra of the followers of Shiva, three broad level lines of pale gray sacred ash drawn across the whole forehead with three fingers, with a round dot of red powder in the middle, and a necklace of rudraksha beads, rough dark brown seeds as big as marbles
the upright mark of the Sri Vaishnava followers of Vishnu, two white clay lines rising from the bridge of the nose to the hairline and joined at the bottom in a broad U, with a single yellow line up the middle, and a necklace of small pale tulsi wood beads close at the throat
the mark of the Madhva followers of Vishnu, two thin upright lines of pale yellow clay joined at the bridge of the nose, with a narrow black line of charcoal up the middle and a small red dot at its foot, and a strand of small tulsi wood beads at the neck
the red mark of the Shakta followers of the Goddess, a large round dot of bright red kumkum powder in the middle of the forehead with a short upright red streak rising from it to the hairline, and a garland of red hibiscus flowers around the neck
the mark of the Ramanandi followers of the god Rama, two broad white upright lines that curve apart toward the hairline, with a wide red stripe between them reaching from the nose to the hair, and several strands of tulsi wood beads on the chest
