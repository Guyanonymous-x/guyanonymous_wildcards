In the style of De Porceleyne Fles, Dutch tin-glazed earthenware painted by hand in rich cobalt blue on glossy white, depicting windmills, canals, sailing ships, tulips, milkmaids, farmhouses and floral scrolls, with fine brushwork and blue shading. Tiered, pyramid-like forms appear in some work. Slightly crackled glaze, soft blue-white contrast, charming, folksy Dutch heritage with elegant Chinese-inspired scenes, crisp and tidy in finish, nostalgic and gentle in mood. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words De Porceleyne Fles in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
