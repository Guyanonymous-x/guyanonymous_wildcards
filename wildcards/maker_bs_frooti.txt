Styled in a design language of juicy, cheerful, summery and kid-friendly. Bright mango-yellow and green fields carry a glossy printed picture of ripe tropical fruit with leaves. Compact shapes have rounded edges and soft creases. Palette is mango yellow, orange, green and red. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
