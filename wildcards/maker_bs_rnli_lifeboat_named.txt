Styled in a design language of British and Irish lifeboat design with an all-weather lifeboat hull in deep navy blue below a bright orange upper hull and superstructure, a white line between the two colours, rugged rounded shapes built to self-right, black rubber fendering, stainless-steel grab rails, crews in yellow waterproofs and orange lifejackets with white helmets, and a brave, weatherproof, sea-going character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
