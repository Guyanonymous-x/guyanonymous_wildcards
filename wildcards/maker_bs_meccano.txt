Styled after an technical-manual look built from printed panels in strong red, deep green, cream and mustard yellow. Fine ruled borders, line-engraved diagrams in black ink on aged cream paper and tidy rows of evenly spaced round perforations. Metal surfaces in red enamel, green enamel, bright zinc and polished brass, joined by small hex bolts. Edwardian, orderly, industrious, tactile and nostalgic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
