Mokosh, the Slavic goddess of weaving and earth, a strong motherly goddess with a broad face and braided hair, wearing an embroidered linen gown and a headdress, holding a spindle and a basket of yarn, beside a well and wheat fields, her face broad and motherly, her hair in long braids, the linen gown embroidered in red, the headdress decorated, the spindle wound with thread in one hand and a basket of yarn in the other, a stone well beside her, golden wheat fields stretching behind
