In the style of Caran d Ache, coloured pencils and crayons in metal tins with a vivid, saturated and carefully graded spectrum. Pencils are hexagonal with a glossy deep colour matching the lead, a white or gold imprint and a smooth round end dipped in the same colour. Tins are slim, in silver, red or royal blue with a hinged lid, neat rows and a bright mountain or lettering emblem. Writing instruments include hexagonal metal pens in glossy lacquer with a crisp chiselled look. Colours are intense, with strong reds, cyans and yellows, and a pristine, orderly, Swiss feel.
