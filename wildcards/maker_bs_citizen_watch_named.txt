Styled in a design language of reliable, modern, understated, engineered and quietly elegant Japanese precision. Round or slightly cushion shapes in brushed and polished stainless steel or titanium, with flat clear glass and slim rims. Clean legible faces in silver white, deep navy, black or champagne, with applied baton markers, sharp sword-shaped pointers with pale luminous fill and a fine printed track. Sporty variants add alternating smooth and serrated sections, bold luminous dots and rugged links. Dress versions use lacquered black, mother-of-pearl or paper-textured white with raised star or moon motifs. Brushed surfaces with polished edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
