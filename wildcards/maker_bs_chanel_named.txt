In the style of Chanel, understated Parisian luxury built on boxy, collarless, easy tailoring in nubby, loosely woven tweed with contrast braid trim along every edge. Gold used sparingly as weight and sparkle, in gilt details bearing interlocked double C marks. Palette of black, ivory white, navy, beige and soft pink, often in two-tone pairings. Clean simple knee-length lines, long ropes of pearls and white fabric camellia flowers as ornament. Quilted soft surfaces with diamond stitching and chain interwoven with leather. Elegant, restrained, feminine, with monochrome rigour and gold accents.
