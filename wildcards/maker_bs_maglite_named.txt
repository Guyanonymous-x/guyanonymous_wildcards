Styled in a design language of robust machined-aluminium design with a heavy, solid feel. Perfectly round cylindrical forms with fine diamond knurling, thick walls, crisp bevels and visible machining lines. Finishes are hard-anodised matte black as the core, with silver, titanium grey, deep red. Black and white packing with plain, strong graphics. Tough, dependable, no-frills and industrial. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
