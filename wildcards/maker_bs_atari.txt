Styled in a design language of warm retro-futuristic 1970s character. Heavy wedge-shaped forms clad in simulated dark woodgrain vinyl, paired with black ribbed plastic, brushed silver metal trim and crisp raised ridges lined along an edge. Palette is walnut brown, black, silver and cream, with punchy red, orange and purple accents in flat painted artwork. The emblem is a three-prong fan-like symbol with a tall central stroke and two curving side strokes, in white or red on black. Packaging uses black fields, bold red and white graphic blocks and thin spectrum stripes. Cosy, bulky, optimistic and slightly nerdy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
