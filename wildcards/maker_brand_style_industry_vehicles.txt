In the style of Flying Pigeon, heavy single speed roadster bicycles of the mid twentieth century in China. The frame is a black enameled steel diamond with a double top tube on the gents model, or a low step through loop frame on the ladies model. Large 28 inch wheels have chrome plated steel rims and thin spokes, with a full cover chain guard, a black rear rack carrying a leather pad, mudguards that end in a pale cream tip, and rod operated rim brakes. The saddle is black leather on big coil springs, the handlebars are wide upright sweeps with black grips and a small bell. White pinstripes and a gold lined transfer line the frame, and the head badge is a stylised dove perched over two initials. Tyres are thick and cream-walled or black. Bicycles stand in long dense rows beside tree lined streets, or carry stacked loads. Mood is utilitarian, durable, plain, communal and nostalgic, in a muted monochrome palette of black, chrome and cream.
In the style of Bajaj Auto, Indian scooters and three wheeled auto rickshaws of the 1970s to 1990s. The scooter is a rounded pressed steel body with a bulbous front mudguard, a curved leg shield, a bench seat with a vinyl cover, and a small spare wheel on the left side panel. Paint is thick glossy enamel in simple colours such as sky blue, cream, off white, rust red and olive green, with chrome side badges, round headlamp with a chrome rim, and a stubby upright mirror. Auto rickshaws are tiny three wheelers with a front mudguard and one headlamp, a canvas or sheet metal cab with open sides, and a black fold-down hood. Typical paint is lemon yellow and black in cities, or green and yellow, with a black bench seat, a meter box, painted slogans, and plastic flower garlands. Mood is cheerful, unpretentious, resilient and familiar, packed with colour and rattle on a dusty street.
In the style of Ashok Leyland, Indian trucks and buses of the 1960s to 1990s. Trucks have a boxy cab-over-engine front with a flat vertical windscreen split into two panes, a plain rectangular grille with a horizontal chrome bar, wide round headlamps and a high step to the door. The paint is solid blue, red, yellow or cream with thick hand painted decorative borders around the cab, flower and peacock scenes on the mudflap, bold lettering such as horn please or good luck, and strings of lucky charms and tassels hanging at the bumper. Wooden or steel cargo bodies are stacked with sacks, painted with chequered stripes. Buses have a rounded fibreglass or steel body in faded red and cream, sliding windows with metal bars, roof luggage racks and a destination board above the windscreen. The brand badge is a flywheel inside a circle. Mood is rugged, colourful, overloaded and gritty, on dusty highways at golden hour.
In the style of Sarao Motors, handbuilt Philippine jeepneys. A jeepney is a long, boxy minibus on an army-surplus-style truck chassis, with a hand-beaten sheet metal body, a long bonnet in front, two open-backed passenger benches facing each other, a rear step and a canopy roof. The body is covered in loud colours and hand painted patterns, such as bright stripes, checks, flames, flowers and animals, outlined in chrome strips, with an abundance of chrome decoration such as grille trim, ornaments, small horses or metal figures along the bonnet, lights, mirrors, tassels and several side mirrors. Sides carry route names and slogans in bold painted lettering, and religious pictures or family names appear above the driver. Interiors have polished metal rails, velvet or vinyl benches, bright fringes, mirrors and a loud sound system. Mood is exuberant, crowded, festive, noisy, handmade and joyful, in saturated colours and heaps of chrome shining in the sun.
In the style of Ski-Doo snowmobiles of the 1960s and 1970s. The machine is a compact, low sled with a bulbous moulded fibreglass hood that swoops down towards two short front skis, a high bubble windscreen, an upswept ducktail seat, a pair of caterpillar tracks and exposed suspension springs. The paint is bright and glossy, a sunny yellow, orange, red, white or lime green, with bold contrasting stripes, large block letters and a decal of a stylised animal or a speed lines graphic. Chrome parts include a small bumper, a round headlight and shiny handlebar grips. The track is black rubber with ribs, the skis are chrome-edged metal with a slight curl, and the rear has a chunky tail light. Riders wear one-piece quilted snowsuits in matching bright colours. The mood is adventurous, retro and cheerful.
