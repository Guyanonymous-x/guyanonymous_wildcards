through the open gates the peoples of every nation walk in, men and women in their twenties to their eighties from Africa, Asia, Europe, the Americas and the Pacific, in long white robes, carrying palm branches
the saved stand in rows along the golden street, adults in their thirties to their seventies in white robes, their faces turned toward the throne, as God wipes the tears from their eyes
the kings of the earth, men and women in their forties to their sixties in crowns and rich robes, carry their treasures in through the gates, chests of gold and bolts of colored cloth, and lay them before the throne
