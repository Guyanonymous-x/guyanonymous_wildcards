Styled in a design language of heavy felted wool cloth in bold large checks and plaids, such as red and black, blue and black, green and black, mustard and navy. Boxy, long, generous shapes with a utilitarian cut and reinforced stress points. The surface is dense, slightly fuzzy and water-resistant, with sturdy stitching and metal eyelets. Rugged farm and hunting mood, with misty green hills, sheep country and muddy tracks. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
