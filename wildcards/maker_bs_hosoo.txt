In the style of Hosoo, Kyoto Nishijin weaving house textiles, richly woven silk brocade with metallic gold and silver foil threads, fine glossy surfaces, dense jacquard patterns and subtle changing sheen. Patterns include geometric lattices, waves, clouds, flowers, pine bark and abstract digital-like lines in very fine repeat. Colours are deep ink black, vermilion red, indigo, ivory, aged gold, silver and soft muted purple, with tiny metallic sparkle. The fabric is stiff, flat and ornate, used for obi sashes, wall panels, cushions and architectural interior surfaces, and the weave shows a thin layered gleam where gold paper strips meet silk. Traditional and contemporary at once, luxurious, patient, hand-crafted look, shown in soft side light that makes the metallic threads glow.
