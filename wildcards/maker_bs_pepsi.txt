In the style of Pepsi, a round globe emblem split into a red upper half and a blue lower half by a white wavy band, set against deep royal blue cans and bottles. Sans-serif lettering in bold lowercase or small caps, often placed beside or across the globe. Earlier versions used a bottle-cap shape with a serrated edge and a script wordmark in red on white. Cans have a bright blue field with the globe tilted or smiling, with silver tops. Palette of cobalt blue, bright red and white, with a clean, youthful, pop-culture look, glossy metallic cans, and sleek modern minimal variants in black for the zero-sugar line.
