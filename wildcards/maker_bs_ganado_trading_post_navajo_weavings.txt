In the style of Ganado Trading Post Navajo Weavings, handwoven wool rugs with a deep Ganado red ground, black and white, and grey borders. A bold central diamond, often with stepped serrated edges or a cross motif, is placed in the centre, with smaller diamonds or hooks echoing in corners. Colours are earthy and limited, namely brick red, charcoal black, ivory and soft grey, with strong contrast. The border is a single frame with notches or crosses, and edges are neat. Weave is tight and flat with a slight handspun irregularity. Geometry is crisp, rectilinear and strongly symmetrical, with a warm, desert look.
