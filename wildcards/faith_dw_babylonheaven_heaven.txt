above rise the three heavens of stone, the lowest of jasper set with the stars, the middle of deep blue stone where the younger gods sit, and the highest of pale red-flecked stone, the home of Anu
in the highest heaven Anu, a bearded man in his seventies, sits on a throne in a crown of several pairs of horns, the symbols of the gods shining around him, a crescent moon, an eight-pointed star and a winged sun disc
across the sky the gods ride, Shamash, a bearded man in his fifties with rays rising from his shoulders, stepping up between two mountains, and Ishtar, a woman in her thirties, with arrow quivers rising from her shoulders
