Styled in a design language of glossy, luminous, lavishly romantic glamour where everything is staged to look aspirational. Saturated jewel-bright colour with golden mustard yellows, rich reds and crisp snowy whites. Heartfelt, dreamy and optimistic, with a polished late 1990s feel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
