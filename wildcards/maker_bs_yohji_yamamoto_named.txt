Styled in a design language of voluminous draped black design cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette becomes a dark, shadowy cloud. Materials are matte wool gabardine, crinkled cotton, heavy linen, felted wool and fluid jersey, with raw edges, oversized folds, tilted asymmetric edges, trailing hems and long scarf-like panels. Tailoring is slouchy, androgynous, deliberately undone and poetic. Austere, moody, romantic and quietly rebellious.. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
