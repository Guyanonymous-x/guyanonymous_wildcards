in the valley stands the holy shrine of Lalish, stone buildings with tall white cone-shaped roofs fluted in sharp ridges, a black snake carved in relief on the stone beside the main door
barefoot Yazidi pilgrims in their twenties to their seventies in white clothes walk the stone courtyard of Lalish, women in white headscarves and men in white robes with red and white checked headcloths
at the Feast of the Assembly men in their thirties to their fifties lead a white bull to the shrine of Sheikh Shams at Lalish and cut its throat, the blood running across the stones of the hillside
