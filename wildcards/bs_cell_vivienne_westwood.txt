__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of theatrical, rebellious and romantic British design that mixes punk with historical grandeur. Tartan is the main pattern, in clashing red, black, green, yellow, pink and lilac checks, cut on the bias and often twisted. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
