in the old mourning rites recorded among the Arrernte, women kin in their thirties to sixties cut their own scalps with digging sticks until blood runs down their faces, and men in their twenties to fifties gash their thighs with stone knives
present-day kin in their twenties to seventies in T-shirts, skirts and jeans stand in the smoke of burning green eucalyptus leaves, a smoking ceremony to send the spirit on and cleanse the house of the dead
