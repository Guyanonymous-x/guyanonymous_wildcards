In the style of Kosta Boda, Swedish contemporary art glass, bold, colourful and hand-blown, with chunky abstract forms, bright stripes, spots and swirled colour layers, and cheerful faces, birds, fish and clowns. Thick transparent bodies in saturated magenta, cobalt, orange, emerald and cool teal, glossy smooth surfaces and organic asymmetrical silhouettes. Joyful, playful and sculptural, with light refracting through thick glass.
