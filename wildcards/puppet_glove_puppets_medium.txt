a set of glove puppets with carved wooden heads, each about as long as an adult's forearm. Each puppet is a soft cloth body that slips over an adult's hand, the forefinger going up into the head and the thumb and middle finger into the two little arms. The heads are carved wood painted in bright colors, a king with a gold crown and a curled gray beard, a queen with a white veil, a red devil with small horns and a brown dog with floppy ears. __puppet_glove_puppets_var__.
