a Skull and Bones initiation in the courtyard of the Tomb at night, as the secret 2001 tape and later accounts describe it. In a small stone courtyard enclosed by Gothic towers, new members in plain clothes kneel one at a time before a group of older members dressed in costume, and a burning torch stands in an iron holder against the wall. One member sits in a high chair dressed as a Pope in white robes and a tall white mitre, one foot in a white slipper embroidered with a gold monogram resting on a stone skull, and beside him stand a member dressed as the Devil in red with horns and a member dressed as Don Quixote in a dented tin helmet and a thin false mustache. A new member kneels and bends to kiss a skull held out to him in both hands, while behind him two older members act out a mock murder, one pretending to cut the other's throat with a prop knife as the other slumps against the stone wall.
