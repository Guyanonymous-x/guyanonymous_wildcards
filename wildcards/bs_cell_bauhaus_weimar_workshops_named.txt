__bs_subject_heritage__ styled in a design language of expressionist and early constructivist work in wood, metal, glass and textile. Forms are built from stacked cubes, slats and planes in primary red, yellow, blue, black and white, with bold geometric shapes such as circle, square and triangle. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
