Styled in a design language of classic, established, serious heritage luxury. Presentation is traditional and restrained, with deep bottle green, deep red and black, and warm cream or off-white framed by thin black linework, oval or shield-shaped frames. Palette is cream, burgundy, black, gold and green. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
