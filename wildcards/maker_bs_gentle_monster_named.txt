In the style of Gentle Monster, playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde. Oversized, sculptural forms in chunky glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, with thick geometric rims, rounded squares, pointed wings and big wraparound shields. Metal in silver or gold with fine joints, and tinted transparent areas in blue, green, orange or mirror gradient. Small engraved logo marks in tiny white capitals. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Gentle Monster in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
