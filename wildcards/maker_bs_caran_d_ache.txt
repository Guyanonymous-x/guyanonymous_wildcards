Styled in a design language of a vivid, saturated and carefully graded spectrum with neat aligned rows. Hexagonal forms are glossy in a deep colour matching their core, with a white or gold imprint and a smooth round end dipped in the same colour. Silver, red or royal blue metal surfaces carry a bright mountain-like or lettered emblem. Metal hexagonal forms have a glossy lacquer and crisp chiselled look. Colours are intense, strong reds, cyans and yellows, in a pristine, orderly, Swiss feel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
