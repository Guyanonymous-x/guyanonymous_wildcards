In the style of the Polish School of Posters, post-war hand-painted film and theatre posters. The image is an expressive, surreal and witty painted or collaged illustration, typically a single bold metaphor instead of a literal scene, such as a giant eye, a flower with a thorn, a distorted face, a bird with a human body, a single broken object, or a childlike doodle on an empty space. Colours are saturated and unusual, such as acid yellow, blood red, deep blue, hot pink, mustard, turquoise and black, with ragged brushwork, gouache texture, torn paper edges and uneven print texture. Lettering is hand-drawn, quirky, irregular and integrated into the composition, in large loose letters in a contrasting colour. The format is a tall portrait sheet with roughly textured paper. The mood is poetic, dark, humorous, expressive and arty.
