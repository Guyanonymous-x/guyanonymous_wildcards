Styled in a design language of a flat-grey bevelled surface treatment. Every panel and tile is light grey with a raised edge made from a white highlight line on the top and left and dark grey shadow lines on the bottom and right, while recessed areas are white with an inset dark edge. A flat teal green field fills the ground. Title strips are solid navy blue fading to light blue, carrying bold white sans-serif text and tiny square grey controls. Icons are chunky pixel pictograms in a sixteen colour palette, with yellow folders, dithered grey patterns and a four-colour waving flag emblem in red, green, blue and yellow. Lettering is crisp bitmap sans-serif. Blocky, beige-grey and nostalgic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
