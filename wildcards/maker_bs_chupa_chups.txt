Styled in a design language of playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop. Simple round shapes and clean sticks of form, with a glossy, candy-like shine and thin crinkled shimmer. Flavours are signalled by colour, pink, orange, green, cola brown and lemon yellow, some swirled with two colours. Hot-pink or red fields radiate outward in a sunburst. Cartoon children and sugary cheerfulness. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
