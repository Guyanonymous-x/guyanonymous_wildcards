Styled in a design language of a flawless gloss black polyester finish with smooth, crisp straight lines and uniform surfaces. Shapes have a simple curved edge, clean planes and square or rounded supports. Boxy shapes have neat panels and plain brass accents. Alternative finishes include satin ebony, polished mahogany, walnut and white. The mood is modern, tidy, dependable, schoolroom and conservatoire, with perfect reflections. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
