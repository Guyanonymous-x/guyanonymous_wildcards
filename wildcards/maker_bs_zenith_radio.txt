In the style of Zenith Radio, American radios and televisions of the 1930s to 1950s. Wood cabinets with streamlined curved corners and a large round dial with an illuminated glass face in cream with black numerals. Bakelite knobs in black or ivory, tall slim fabric grilles with gold lines, chrome trim bars and rounded speaker panels. Early televisions have wood cabinets with a small curved glass screen and rounded edges. A lightning bolt Z logo appears at the front. Mood is polished, elegant and mid-century domestic.
