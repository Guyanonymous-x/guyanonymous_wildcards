Styled in a design language of meticulous, hygienic, bustling and warm, with an exacting clean presentation. Small steamed parcels with paper-thin translucent skins, each gathered by hand into a tidy spiral of fine pleats with a little knot at the top, sitting in a round bamboo steamer with glints of broth showing. Open glass-walled kitchens show white-uniformed cooks in caps, masks and gloves under bright white light, working on stainless steel tables. Modern rooms have pale oak, blond wood screens, red brushed lettering signs, dark slate floors and slatted lanterns. Palette of cream, bamboo tan, steam white and one red accent.
