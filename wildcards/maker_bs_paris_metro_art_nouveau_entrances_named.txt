Styled in a design language of Art Nouveau cast iron and glass painted in bottle green, with whiplash curved stems that bend like plant shoots, sinuous railings shaped like flowering vines, and amber glass panels forming a shell or scalloped canopy. Tall slender supports curve like orchid stems holding glowing amber glass with organic ribbed forms. Dragonfly-wing glazing, botanical curves, delicate yet industrial, ornate organic ironwork, deep green with golden light, a belle epoque city mood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
