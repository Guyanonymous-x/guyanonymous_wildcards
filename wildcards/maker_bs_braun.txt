Styled in a design language of calm, rational, ornament-free functional design. Simple geometric volumes with softly rounded corners, tight even gaps and precise tolerances. Matte or satin surfaces in off-white, pale grey, black and graphite, with natural light wood or clear translucent material used sparingly. Small round or flush details sit neatly grouped on a strict grid, with one tiny accent of orange, yellow, green or red marking the one thing that matters. Lettering is a compact, even, lowercase-feeling grotesque sans-serif, often as a plain black wordmark on white, and graphics use bold primary colour bars and rigorous grids. Fine regular perforations add texture. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
