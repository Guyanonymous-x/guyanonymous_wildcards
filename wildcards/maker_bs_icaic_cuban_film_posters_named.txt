Styled in a design language of vibrant, witty, politically charged and strongly handmade silkscreen design. A few flat, saturated colours in layers with no gradients, bright red, yellow, cobalt blue, orange, green, pink and black, laid down one screen at a time on cheap rough paper with slightly uneven edges and visible misregistration. Pop art, psychedelia and constructivism blended with satire. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
