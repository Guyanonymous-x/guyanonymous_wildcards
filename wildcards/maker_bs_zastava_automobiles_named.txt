Styled in a design language of small, square-cut, boxy, cheap, honest, utilitarian and distinctively late socialist era. Flat near-vertical planes, thin black plastic trim, rectangular shapes, compact blocky proportions, a pronounced horizontal shoulder line and sharply cut corners. Nothing is decorated and every edge is straight. Colour is plain gloss red, sky blue, white, yellow, silver-grey, beige or orange, sometimes with a thin bright stripe. A small stylised emblem sits quietly in a corner. Materials are hard brown or black plastic and plaid or striped cloth. Modest, practical and worn with pride. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
