In the style of Philips, homely, rounded and optimistic. Wood or bakelite with rounded edges, big round glass faces with illuminated names, and pale ivory or chocolate-brown plastics. Big ribbed round knobs and piano-style keys. Fabric panels with vertical gold slats and a small star and wave mark. Later pieces use bright plastic in cream, mustard and pastel with gently curved forms. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
