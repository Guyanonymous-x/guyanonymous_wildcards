Styled in a design language of honest, rugged New England outdoor craft. Materials are heavy cotton duck canvas, brushed flannel in red, navy and green plaid checks, chunky wool knit, waxed fabric and tan or brown leather trim with bold doubled seam lines. Palette is forest green, deep navy, brick red, khaki, oatmeal cream and rust. Contrast piping and edging in a second colour add simple colour blocking. Emblem is a rectangular woven mark with cream background. Details are dull brass and plain utilitarian shapes that look sturdy, practical and softly weathered. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
