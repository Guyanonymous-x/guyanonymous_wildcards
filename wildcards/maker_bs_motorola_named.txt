Styled in a design language of bold tech-era and metallic. 2000s forms are razor-thin with brushed aluminium finish, etched flat metal and blue backlit glow. The emblem is a stylised M with two pointed bat-wing peaks, rendered in black on white, white on red, or red on black inside a circle or a square. Palette is red, white, black, metallic silver and electric blue. Confident, engineered and mid-century industrial. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
