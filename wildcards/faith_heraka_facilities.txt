kao kai, the kao kai, the temples of Heraka, the reformed faith of the Zeme, Liangmai and Rongmei Naga peoples of the green hills where Manipur, Nagaland and Assam meet in northeast India, first built in the late 1920s by the prophet Haipou Jadonang at his village of Kambiron as houses for the worship of Tingwang, the one supreme god of the sky, halls of timber, bamboo and thatch on the high ground of the village with rows of bamboo benches and a raised platform for the one who leads the prayers, and carried on today in the prayer houses of the Heraka villages, where the faithful gather at sunrise and on full moon days, __faith_fa_kaokai_scene__
