the men of the town in their twenties to sixties seated in ranks on the cane couches, with copper-brown skin, the hair shaved at the sides and standing in a stiff crest on top, dark tattooed lines on chests and arms, in breechcloths of red trade cloth, deerskin leggings and moccasins, silver crescents at the throat
two attendants in their thirties carrying large conch-shell cups of steaming black drink, a dark tea of roasted yaupon holly leaves, across the square to the chief, a man in his sixties wrapped in a white blanket mantle with a fan of crane feathers in his hand
women in their twenties to fifties with copper-brown skin and long black hair coiled and tied with ribbons, in knee-length wrap skirts of blue trade cloth and short loose calico jackets, carrying baskets of new green corn to the edge of the square
