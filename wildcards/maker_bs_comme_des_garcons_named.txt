In the style of Comme des Garcons, deconstructed avant-garde design with asymmetric, deliberately distorted silhouettes, bulbous padded humps, oversized stiff shapes, exposed raw seams, unfinished frayed edges, holes, inside-out construction and layered mismatched panels. Dominant colour is severe black, then stark white, grey and a single shock of red, with occasional gingham, polka dot, tartan or flat pop prints. Materials are felt, crumpled cotton, distressed knits, lace, patent and cheap-looking synthetics treated as luxury. Proportions ignore the body, parts are too long or placed in the wrong spot, and forms hang like sculptures. Austere, intellectual and rebellious, with stark empty space. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
