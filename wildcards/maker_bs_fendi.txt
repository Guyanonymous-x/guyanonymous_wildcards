Styled in a design language of roman luxury, witty, tactile and richly crafted. The core pattern is a repeating monogram of two mirrored capital letters, shown in brown on tan, black and white, or bright colours, and used on wallpaper-like grids, ribbons and bold single-motif graphics. Materials are plush fur, fine leather, woven strips, marquetry-like patchwork and smooth lacquer, with sleek clean shapes in camel, tan, chocolate, black, cream and sunny golden yellow. Playful touches include candy-coloured trims, leopard spots and cartoon-eyed charms. Lettering is a heavy, tightly stacked sans-serif in capitals. Packaging is mustard yellow boxes with black edging, and sleek black gift cartons. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
