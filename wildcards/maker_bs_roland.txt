In the style of Roland, 1980s Japanese electronic instruments. Dark grey or black angular metal and plastic cases with wide control surfaces, rows of tiny coloured plastic buttons in orange, red, blue and cream, slim sliders and small red LED displays. Drum machines have rows of small rounded square pads in red, orange, yellow and white. Printed labels use fine white lettering with grouped panel lines. Synthesizers have long keyboards, wedge-like profiles and a bold stripe with the model name. Mood is techy, club-ready and boxy.
