dark rain clouds rise over the hills of Jepira and move inland over the dry desert, gray curtains of rain falling on the cactus and goat pens, the dead coming home
rain falls on the Guajira, the red earth darkening and pools forming among the cactus, Ramón, a Wayuu man in his forties, and Yolanda, a woman in her thirties, standing in the open with their faces lifted
the shades of the dead rise from Jepira into the clouds as pale figures and fall back to the land as long silver streaks of rain over the desert
