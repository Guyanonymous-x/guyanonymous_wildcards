behind the village rise forested hills, and on the highest peak a cluster of tall bamboo poles hung with red cloth and peacock feathers marks the seat of the hill gods
in the forest clearing of Medaram the goddesses Sammakka and Saralamma, a mother and her grown daughter, take the form of tall bamboo poles smeared with red vermilion and yellow turmeric on raised platforms of earth, small boxes of vermilion tied to them and a red sari laid at their feet, under a spreading fig tree, Koya priests in their forties to sixties in white wraps tending them and heaps of yellow jaggery offered before them
on a rocky hilltop sits a cave mouth shaded by trees, offerings of rice, coconuts and red cloth laid on a flat stone before it
the hills roll away in ridges of teak and sal forest down to the wide brown Godavari river, small shrines of stones and bamboo poles set along the ridge tops
