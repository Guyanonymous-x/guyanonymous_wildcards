In the style of Studebaker, early 1950s American coupes with a pointed bullet nose like an aircraft spinner at the centre of the grille, a low wide stance and a thin wraparound rear window. Slim body with a long low hood, a low roofline, an almost European flat-sided silhouette, and a gently tapering tail. Chrome bumpers, small fins, and round tail lamps. Late 1950s versions with gold or turquoise hardtops and a long low wagon shape. Pale pastel two-tone paint in pink and white, sky blue and cream. Inside, a streamlined dash with a big round dial and brushed metal. Quirky, futuristic, a bit strange.
