Styled in a design language of literate, apothecary-inspired restraint with quiet luxury. Amber brown glass or matte brown containers with plain cream-white paper surfaces, set in neat black type in a thin classical roman, followed by long detailed text lists. Black or brass fittings, squat shapes and muted aluminium. Spaces use raw timber, concrete, brass, stacked brown vessels and dim warm lighting. Palette is amber brown, cream, black and grey-green. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
