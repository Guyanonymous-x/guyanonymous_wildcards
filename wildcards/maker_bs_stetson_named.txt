Styled in a design language of proud, rugged American frontier heritage with an old-world crest. A detailed engraved emblem of two animals flanking a shield, with a scroll banner and a founding year, is printed in brown, gold or cream. Materials are dense fur felt, burnished leather, braided cord, canvas, brass and washed denim with a sun-faded feel. Palette is silver belly grey, tan, saddle brown, black, deep burgundy and denim blue. Round, domed, generous shapes, soft rolled edges and warm, dignified craftsmanship. Packaging is circular or boxy in cream and maroon, with gold stamped type. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
