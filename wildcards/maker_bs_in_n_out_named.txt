Styled in a design language of clean, retro Californian, bright and uncluttered. Repeating borders of small red palm-tree motifs and slim red bands run along white paper and cardboard surfaces, with simple flat red and white colour blocking and glossy printed coatings. A limited palette of red, white and sunny yellow keeps everything crisp. Matte uncoated paper and shiny red lacquered details. Fresh, nostalgic, honest and sunny. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
