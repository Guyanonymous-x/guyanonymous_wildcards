Styled in a design language of understated, grown-up sporty design with a calm, confident, practical air. Palette is heathered grey as the core, joined by navy, burgundy, forest green, cream and white, with occasional bright teal, volt yellow or coral accents. Textures mix soft suede, fine mesh and matte synthetic in layered overlays with muted tonal blocking. Packaging uses sturdy boxes in red, teal or white with simple tidy type. Nothing is flashy, everything is balanced, refined and comfortable. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
