Styled in a design language of calm, precise, utilitarian simplicity. Forms are clean and regular, with no ornament, flat planes, tidy seams and a minimal, modular logic that repeats across everything. Materials are soft smooth cotton, fine knit, brushed fleece and light technical fabric in a quiet palette of white, grey, navy, black and beige. A single saturated signal red square carries bold white capital lettering in blocky katakana style and Latin type, and this red-and-white pairing runs through labels and packaging. Packaging is crisp white cardboard and clear film with a neat grid of small type, ordered, honest, generous with whitespace and made for everyday use. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
