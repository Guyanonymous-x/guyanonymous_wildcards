Styled in a design language of opulent, romantic Indian heirloom richness with a vintage, slightly distressed and layered look. Deep earthy jewel colours such as rich red, burnt orange, marigold, mustard, moss green, teal and faded rose cover the surface. Heavy hand embroidery in dense gold metallic thread, silk thread, sequins, mirror work and tassels combines with block-printed florals and tiger or paisley motifs on silk, velvet, organza and tulle. Sheer layers have scalloped borders. Ornament is chunky antique gold with uncut stones, big gems and pearl ropes, layered thickly. Regal, heritage mood, warm golden light and a faded-palace feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
