Styled in a design language of exaggerated, heroic, comic-epic fantasy and science fiction with chunky, stylised proportions. Oversized armour, huge shoulder shapes and enormous weapons, painterly saturated textures with hand-painted highlights, and glowing magical effects in cyan, orange and violet. Frames and borders are rich stone and metal trimmed in gold, with bevelled edges. Poses are dramatic and low-angle, towering over the viewer. Bold, polished, vibrant and larger than life, with strong silhouettes that read instantly and a confident sense of fun. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
