Rosicrucian Park in San Jose, California, the grounds of a modern Rosicrucian order built in an ancient Egyptian style. A city block of gardens is set with buildings in an ancient Egyptian style, their walls sloping inward, with tall square gateways, painted columns topped with carved papyrus flowers and a museum front modeled on the gates of the great temple of Karnak. Stone statues of Egyptian gods and animals stand in the gardens, among palm trees, lawns and pools, a hippopotamus goddess standing upright on her hind legs to one side of the museum doors. Inside the museum a dark corridor leads into a copy of a rock-cut Egyptian tomb, its walls painted with scenes of the afterlife, and glass cases hold mummies wrapped in brown linen, painted coffins, small stone gods and jewelry.
