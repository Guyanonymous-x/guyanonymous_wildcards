In the style of Nalli Silks, South Indian Kanchipuram and Kanjivaram silk saris, heavy hand-woven pure silk with rich lustre and a broad contrasting border, a separate pallu end piece and woven zari gold or silver thread motifs. Motifs include temple towers, checks, peacocks, parrots, elephants, mango paisley, lotus flowers and fine vertical stripes. Colours are vivid and deep, such as peacock blue, magenta, parrot green, mustard, maroon, saffron and black with gold, often with border colours contrasting sharply against the body. The fabric is thick, stiff, glossy and drapes in heavy pleats, shown folded in stacks on white sheets in a brightly lit shop.
