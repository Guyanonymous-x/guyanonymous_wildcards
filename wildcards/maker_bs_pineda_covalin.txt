Styled in a design language of intricate, richly coloured silk prints drawn from pre-Hispanic and folk culture, such as sun-disc calendars, jaguars, eagles, serpents, agave, skulls, floral crowns, hummingbirds, corn, cacti, tile patterns and embroidery. Prints are dense and symmetrical, framed by decorative borders, in bold turquoise, cochineal red, marigold orange, jade green, magenta, cobalt and gold on silk twill. Hand-rolled edges and bright accents. Festive, ornate, folk-art mood with warm, saturated colour. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
