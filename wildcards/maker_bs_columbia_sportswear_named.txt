Styled in a design language of practical, layered, weather-ready design in bold colour-block panels of red, bright blue, yellow, forest green and black, with contrasting yokes, sleeves and trims. Khaki and sage utility pieces with vented panels, many small zip pockets and tab details. Sturdy nylon ripstop, quilted waterproof outer fabrics, fleece, silver dotted reflective lining, drawcords and chunky zips. Corduroy and plaid trims add warmth. Finishes are matte and sturdy, with a chunky 1990s outdoor fashion attitude. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
