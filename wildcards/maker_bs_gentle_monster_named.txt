Styled in a design language of playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde. Oversized, sculptural forms in chunky glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, with thick geometric rims, rounded squares, pointed wings and big wraparound shields. Metal in silver or gold with fine joints, and tinted transparent areas in blue, green, orange or mirror gradient. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
