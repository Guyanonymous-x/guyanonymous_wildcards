In the style of Jacobo y Maria Angeles Workshop, joyful, hallucinatory, highly saturated carved and painted folk art. Light soft wood is hand-carved into lively mythical creatures, often with a human face or braided hair on an animal body, caught mid-action while flying, scratching or leaping. After a smooth base coat, the whole body is covered in intricate fine-line ornament in vivid colours of magenta, turquoise, saffron yellow, orange, leaf green and black, in dense repeating patterns of dots, scrolls, flowers, plants, stepped geometric motifs and ancient temple-wall designs. Eyes are large and glossy. Light satin finish, with every surface patterned and nothing left blank.
In the style of Wixarika Huichol Beadwork, ceremonial imagery rendered in dense blazing colour. Tiny glass beads are pressed one by one into a layer of wax and resin over carved forms, leaving a shining mosaic surface with a slightly bumpy glittering texture. Flat areas use thick coloured wool pressed into wax in tight spiralling lines that follow each shape like contour maps. Motifs are deer, maize, cactus, scorpions, five-petalled flowers, sun faces, rain clouds and diamond-backed snake bodies, with zigzag lines radiating outward. The palette is neon-bright and contrasting, fuchsia, turquoise, lime green, electric blue, orange, yellow, red and black with white outlines, filled completely so no background shows. Psychedelic, symmetrical and intricate.
In the style of Haitian Metal Drum Art, raw, intricate, spiritual and richly patterned flat sculpture cut from recycled steel. Panels are flattened, drawn with chalk, then chiselled and punched so the line work appears as cut-through openings and raised ridges. Subjects are a tree of life, mermaids, birds, fish, sun faces, spirits with elaborate headdresses and crosses, drawn with symmetrical swirling leaves, scales, dotted outlines, hearts and curling vines. The metal is left dark grey and rust-brown, sometimes polished to rough silver with burn marks, or hand-painted in bright enamel like red, yellow and turquoise. Shapes are oval, round or irregular with ragged cut edges. A rugged industrial material turned into delicate lace.
In the style of Zhostovo Trays, a glossy black lacquer ground, sometimes red, blue, green or white, carrying a lush bouquet of large garden and wild flowers loosely tossed with leaves, buds and fruit. Painting is quick and full-brushed with soft shaded modelling, in oil colours of deep crimson, pink, orange, yellow, blue and fresh green against the dark. A smaller scatter of blossoms and berries surrounds the main bunch. Edges are trimmed with gold or silver scrolls, dots and little leaf ornaments. The finish is polished to a mirror shine under many layers of lacquer. Lush, sumptuous, festive and warm.
In the style of Dymkovo Toy, hand-moulded clay coated in milky-white chalk for a smooth matte porcelain-like finish. Forms are plump, stylised, simple and rounded, with bell-shaped bodies, tiny heads and exaggerated puffy volumes. Painting is crisp, playful and poster-bright in red, orange, yellow, blue, green and black, using large circles, checks, diagonal stripes, wavy lines, dots and rhombuses, with rosy circular cheeks and small black dot eyes. Small details are picked out in glittering gold leaf. Cheerful, flat, geometric and slightly naive, like a patterned doll made of ceramic icing.
In the style of Fedoskino Lacquer Miniatures, glossy black lacquer built up layer upon layer, often with a bright red or vermilion hidden interior, carrying a small, finely detailed realistic painting framed by the dark. The painting uses many translucent glazes so figures and skies glow with soft inner light, over a shimmering gold, silver leaf or mother-of-pearl underlayer. Edges may be framed in gold ornament, braids and flowers. The finish is mirror-smooth, deep and jewel-like in black, crimson, gold and sky blue. Polished, narrative and luxurious.
In the style of Petrykivka Painting, lavish flat floral ornament painted freehand on white or coloured grounds. Flowers are stylised and fantastical, shown flat from above or side-on, with fern-like leaves, curling tendrils, berries, tiny grape clusters and the occasional bird. Brush strokes are visible and clearly shaped, a comb stroke, a seed, a teardrop, a tapering curl, with berries made by a fingertip dab giving a lighter centre ringed with a darker edge. Colours are strong and clear, scarlet, cobalt, magenta, orange, lemon yellow and leaf green on white, black, red, yellow or deep blue. Symmetrical, gracefully balanced, lively and celebratory.
In the style of Dala Horse, simple, friendly, folk-rustic and unmistakably Scandinavian. Small stout carved wooden forms in simple flat planes, with a thick arched curve, notched edges, sturdy legs and a bulky chest. Body painted glossy deep red or orange-red, sometimes blue, black, white or natural. Trim is decorated freehand with flower scroll patterns in white, green, yellow and blue, painted quickly in loops, petals and dots in a flower and swirl vocabulary. The finish is smooth, shiny and lightly worn.
