In the style of Off-White, industrial yellow and black diagonal hazard stripes, quotation marks placed around ordinary words in plain Helvetica-like sans serif capitals, as if labelling the object, arrows with crossed diagonal bars printed on sleeves and backs, and zip ties used as tags on bags and shoes. Garments in matte black, white and off white with exposed construction tape, raw seams, orange safety webbing and large printed text such as 'FOR WALKING'. Sneakers reworked with visible foam, extra laces and printed labels in capitals. Photocopy grey graphics, caution tape, road marking and construction site motifs, a deconstructed luxury streetwear mood with sharp minimal layouts and white gallery backdrops.
