Styled in a design language of white porcelain decorated in a crowded palette of underglaze cobalt blue, iron red and gold. Panels of chrysanthemums, peonies, pine, bamboo, cranes and phoenix are packed into fan, circle and brocade-pattern reserves that fill every part of the surface. Black outlines, lacy borders, flaring scalloped edges and lobed forms are typical. Red and blue dominate while green and black add accents, and gilding glows on petals and borders. Tall, rounded shapes give an opulent, ornate and festive effect. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
