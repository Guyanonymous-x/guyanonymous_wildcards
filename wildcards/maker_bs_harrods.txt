Styled in a design language of opulent, ceremonial British luxury in deep pine green and gold. Carrier-bag style flat panels, ribbon trim, gold foil stamping, tonal embossing and polished brass details give a rich, heavy feel. Palette is dark green, antique gold, cream and burgundy, with occasional cream and terracotta stone-like tones. Fine rope-like cord, tassels, silk lining and quilted textures add comfort. Dignified, heritage-rich, gilded and grand. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
