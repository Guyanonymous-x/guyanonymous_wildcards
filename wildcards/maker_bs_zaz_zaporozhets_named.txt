Styled in a design language of tiny, boxy and rounded, humble, endearing and utilitarian. Slim supports and big curved glass give an airy look. Colour is flat, cheap-looking gloss in pea green, cream, sky blue, light yellow, orange-red, brick red and grey, with one thin chrome line. Spartan detailing in thin metal with a single round instrument. Drably cheerful. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
