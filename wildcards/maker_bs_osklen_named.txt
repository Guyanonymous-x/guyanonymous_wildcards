Styled in a design language of relaxed coastal luxury sportswear, lightweight and technical, in natural and eco-friendly materials such as organic cotton, linen, wool, recycled fibres and exotic fish leather. Palette is sand, off-white, navy, olive, grey, terracotta, deep teal and ocean blue with pops of orange. Prints show waves, sea creatures, forest leaves and rainforest-inspired graphics, with sleek minimal detailing. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
