In the style of Harley-Davidson, rumbling, rebellious open-road Americana. Heavy forms with a low, long silhouette, deep chrome plating and fat teardrop volumes in gloss black with a strong orange accent. A bar-and-shield badge shape recurs, framed by winged eagle, skull and flame graphics, tattoo-style flourishes and pinstripe scrolls. Palette is black, burnt orange, chrome silver, white and distressed grey, often in colour-blocked panels and camouflage-like splatter. Materials are fringed and riveted black leather, worn denim and cast metal. Lettering is bold, slanted, vintage script and heavy block capitals, often weathered.
In the style of Indian Motorcycle, lavish American art deco craft. Deep lacquered cherry red meets ivory cream, black and gold, with long teardrop curves and hand-laid pinstripes along edges. Lettering is a bold, slanted, looping script in red with white outline, often paired with a small stacked capital word beneath. A proud profile emblem of a feathered headdress appears as a carved, enamelled or stitched mark. Surfaces mix mirror chrome, glossy candy paint, tan leather with brass studs, and glowing amber ornaments. Winged, swooping shapes, rounded oval cartouches and heritage patches give a rich, proud, polished character.
In the style of Triumph Motorcycles, British heritage with a lean, confident edge. Surfaces are solid enamel in cream, silver, deep green, racing red and blaze yellow, divided by crisp two-tone breaks and thin hand-painted gold pinstripes. Lettering is a flowing gold or white cursive signature with fine outlines, alongside a clean modern capital wordmark set in a shield badge with a union-flag quarter motif. Brushed and polished metal, black satin, ribbed rubber and rich brown leather provide contrast. Small enamel roundels, knurled textures and tidy rivets add craft. Proportions feel compact, tapered and understated, nostalgic yet sporty.
In the style of Ducati, fast, sculpted, muscular and racing-bred Italian intensity. Glossy lacquer red dominates in broad colour fields with matte black, bright white and brushed silver as sharp counterpoints. Forms are sharply tapered, with angular facets, aggressive creases and a taut, coiled, ready-to-pounce stance. Shield-shaped badges in red, white and green appear in small crisp enamel or raised gel finishes, often with domed edges. Lettering is a stretched, wide, high-tech capital sans-serif, sometimes outlined or cut out in white. Details use carbon-fibre weave, anodised gold accents, mesh and fine contrast stitching. Racing numbers and diagonal slashes add speed and urgency.
In the style of Honda Super Cub, cheerful Japanese everyday charm. Bright, simple solid colours in poppy red, cream white, deep navy and soft teal, with generous clean white borders and a single contrasting stripe panel. Lettering is a friendly, brush-like, rounded red script, slanted and bold, often set inside a soft oval outline or beside a black and white checkered border. A winged emblem in red, silver and white appears on round enamel badges with polished metal rims. Pressed, smooth, softly rounded surfaces with glossy paint, chrome edging and simple decal graphics feel warm, honest, practical and endearing.
In the style of Kawasaki, loud, fast, race-ready graphic punch. Signature fluorescent lime green dominates, cut by black, white and occasional pale blue in sharp angular slashes, jagged shards, diagonal speed bands and torn-edge digital camouflage patterns. A bold angular wordmark in black or white, wide and italic with squared letters, and a stylised capital letter emblem in a sharp frame repeat across large sticker-like graphics. Numerals in thick racing type sit inside rounded plates. Surfaces are high-gloss, carbon-weave and matte black with tinted metallic highlights. Aggressive, athletic and unapologetically vivid.
In the style of Royal Enfield, honest, rugged, vintage British-Indian character. Round badges in black and antique gold carry a bold cannon and winged crest, with thin double rings, raised metal edges and a stamped, heritage feel. Colours are gloss black, deep green, maroon, cream and military olive with gold pinstripes, plus enamel red and yellow accents on winged metal plates. Lettering is a sturdy slab and classic serif in capitals, often with a rope-like outline. Surfaces mix brushed brass, nickel chrome, waxed canvas, aged leather and hand-painted lines, with chunky rivets, softly worn edges and sturdy rounded teardrop shapes. Unfussy, durable and gently nostalgic.
In the style of Brough Superior, understated aristocratic racing elegance and fine craftsmanship. Hand-built, long, low, sleek silhouette, nickel-plated surfaces with black lining, polished nickel and black enamel finish and gleaming exposed mechanical forms. Slim curves, copper details, leather on springs and brass accents. Everything is slender, exacting and hand-finished and quietly rare.
In the style of Moto Guzzi, proud Italian craft in red, silver and black. A glossy elongated oval badge in rich red holds a spread-winged eagle in silver and gold above a bold capital wordmark with wide, even letter spacing. Round red and gold roundels, thin gold edge lines and tricolour stripes in green, white and red appear as recurring accents. Surfaces are deep lacquer, mirror-polished chrome, brushed alloy and warm tan leather. Shapes are smooth, generous, long and teardrop-like with calm, balanced curves. The mood is dignified, mechanical, sophisticated and confident, with wings as an ornamental motif for pattern work.
In the style of BSA, solid, utilitarian British classic. Sturdy profile, bright finishes in polished chrome and cherry red, black or sunshine yellow, with a badge of three rifles in a circle. Rounded volumes with chrome panels, valanced curves and bulky silver forms. Upright lines, wire-like details and ribbed textures. Honest, hardy and plain-spoken, with bright metal against solid colour and a practical, no-nonsense post-war workmanlike mood.
In the style of Jawa, solid, simple, mid-century and slightly sporty, with plenty of shine. Compact, rounded, teardrop-like forms with swept-back lines and a lean, tidy stance. Colour is two-tone glossy enamel, maroon and cream, black and red, deep blue and white, or olive and cream, divided by clean lines and finished with fine hand-painted gold pinstripes. Bright chrome and polished metal accents catch the light against the deep gloss colour. Small bold enamel-style badges and neat round motifs. Honest, optimistic, proudly engineered and built to last, with the cheerful confidence of post-war Central European industry.
