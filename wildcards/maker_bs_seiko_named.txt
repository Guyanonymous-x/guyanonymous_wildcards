Styled in a design language of clean, practical, quietly sophisticated and slightly retro. Stainless steel with sharp faceted edges, mixing polished and brushed surfaces under clear domed glass. Palette is deep navy blue, black, silver, champagne and sunburst green with fine radial brushing, plus bold red as a crisp accent. Applied baton markers with luminous infill and slim bright needles give refined detail. Japanese-inspired patterns such as overlapping scale waves in red and white, fine woven lines and subtle gradient fades recur. Restrained, precise and considered. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
