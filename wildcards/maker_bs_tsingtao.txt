Styled in a design language of traditional, slightly old-fashioned Chinese-German colonial heritage with neat bordered frames. Green grounds carry a cream oval edged in green, an illustrated landmark of a small pavilion on a rocky shore in dark green. Palette is dark green, cream, red and gold. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
