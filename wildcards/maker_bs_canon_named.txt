In the style of Canon, dependable, precise and quietly handsome. Compact rectangular forms with a flat black or satin silver plane, fine black leatherette or wood-grain texture and crisp machined edges. Tiny metal details with fine knurling and thin ridges give a tidy, engineered feel, and a narrow red band accent marks top-grade pieces. Palette is restrained black, silver and white with a single confident signal red used for the emblem and highlights. The mark is a bold red serif-like script with flowing joined letters, often set white on a solid red panel. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Canon in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
