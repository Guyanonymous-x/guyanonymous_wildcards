Styled in a design language of tall slender supports curve like orchid stems holding glowing amber glass with organic ribbed forms. Dragonfly-wing glazing, botanical curves, delicate yet industrial, ornate organic ironwork, deep green with golden light, a belle epoque city mood. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
