among the roots lies the lower world, where Erlik, the lord of the dead, a heavy man in his sixties with a black beard and a black robe, sits in a palace of dark iron beside a black river
below the roots the dead live in camps of tents and reindeer, their tents dark and upside down, their reindeer black, and the river of the dead flows past them toward the north
the lowest roots plunge into a dark sea, where the shaman's spirit helpers, a bear, a fish and a loon, guide the shaman down to fetch back a sick person's lost soul
