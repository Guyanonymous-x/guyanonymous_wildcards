In the style of Saab in the 1970s and 1980s, a quirky Swedish hatchback or saloon with a short stubby nose that slopes down, a deeply curved windscreen, a rounded cabin like a cockpit, and a wide hatch tail. Rectangular headlamps with small wipers, a small grille with a gryphon head on a red circle badge, black plastic bumpers, and a distinctive curved roof pillar. Wheels are alloys with a distinct pattern of holes. Paint in black, silver metallic, deep red, white, and dark blue. Interior has deep bucket seats in grey or blue cloth, an asymmetrical wraparound dash with centre vents and big round dials, and a floor-mounted ignition. Mood is aeronautical, practical and eccentric.
