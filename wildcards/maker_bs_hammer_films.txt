Styled in a design language of gothic horror with richly saturated colour in blood red, deep green and midnight blue. Candle-lit castles, fog-wrapped graveyards and crypts, ornate Victorian costumes and capes, fangs, dripping crimson blood, dramatic painted imagery with looming shadows. Lush, lurid storybook creepiness. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
