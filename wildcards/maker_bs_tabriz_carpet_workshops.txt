Styled in a design language of finely knotted wool and silk surfaces with a large central medallion on an open or dense field, framed by a wide main border and several narrow guard borders. Colours are rich and layered, ivory, deep madder red, indigo, navy, soft turquoise and gold. Motifs are curling vines, palmettes, trees, vases of flowers and cartouches, drawn in very precise, curved lines, with quarter medallions echoing in the corners. The texture is short and velvety with sharply defined detail, strict symmetry and a spacious, courtly composition. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
