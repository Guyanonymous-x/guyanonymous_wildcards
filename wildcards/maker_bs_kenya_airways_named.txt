Styled in a design language of warm, hospitable, proud, bright and savannah-like identity in red, white and black. Older schemes run four bands of red, green, black and white in the national flag colours. Red uniforms with black and white scarves and neat caps. Red against clear blue sky. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
