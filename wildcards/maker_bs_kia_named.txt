Styled in a design language of youthful, confident, sharp modern design. Shapes are crisp, sculpted and sporty, with taut sharp lines, tiger-like pointed motifs and star-map dot patterns. Finishes are gloss black, satin metal and soft-touch matte. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
