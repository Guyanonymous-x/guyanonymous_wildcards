Styled in a design language of honest, rugged, vintage British-Indian character. Round badges in black and antique gold carry a bold cannon and winged crest, with thin double rings, raised metal edges and a stamped, heritage feel. Colours are gloss black, deep green, maroon, cream and military olive with gold pinstripes, plus enamel red and yellow accents on winged metal plates. Surfaces mix brushed brass, nickel chrome, waxed canvas, aged leather and hand-painted lines, with chunky rivets, softly worn edges and sturdy rounded teardrop shapes. Unfussy, durable and gently nostalgic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
