Styled in a design language of lavish American art deco craft. Deep lacquered cherry red meets ivory cream, black and gold, with long teardrop curves and hand-laid pinstripes along edges. Surfaces mix mirror chrome, glossy candy paint, tan leather with brass studs, and glowing amber ornaments. Winged, swooping shapes, proud, polished character. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
