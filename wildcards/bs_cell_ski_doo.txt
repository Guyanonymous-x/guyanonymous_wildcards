__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of compact, low, cheerful, adventurous and retro 1960s and 1970s snow-country machinery. Bulbous moulded fibreglass volumes swoop down toward short curled forms, with a high bubble of clear glass, upswept ducktail curves, ribbed black rubber and exposed springs. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
