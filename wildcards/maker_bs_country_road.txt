Styled in a design language of relaxed smart-casual design with clean classic shapes, such as breton stripes and tailored easy lines. Palette is natural and understated, with white, navy, sand, oatmeal, olive, khaki, soft grey, terracotta and occasional sky blue. Materials are cotton, linen, wool and denim, and details are minimal with natural wooden accents and neat stitching. Fresh, easy, sun-bleached beach house mood, in bright airy space. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
