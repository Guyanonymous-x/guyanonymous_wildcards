on the summit of Mount Meru stands Amaravati, the city of heaven of Indra, palaces of gold and gardens with the wish-granting tree, and Indra rides Airavata, a white elephant with many tusks, a man in his thirties in a gold crown holding the thunderbolt
in the hall of Indra the apsaras, dancers of heaven, women in their twenties in silk and jewels, dance before his throne, and the gandharvas, men in their twenties, play the vina and drums beside them
Mount Meru rises at the center of the world as a tall golden mountain with straight steep sides of gold, silver, lapis and crystal, the sun and moon circling it and the heavens of the gods stacked on its summit
