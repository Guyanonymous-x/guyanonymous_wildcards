Styled in a design language of futuristic and streamlined postwar American design. A very low, smooth, rounded teardrop body, a narrow chrome-bar front with a wide mouth and a swept-back profile. Waltz blue, beige, maroon and dark green. Small whitewall roundness and a fully enclosed louvred finish at the back add futurism. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
