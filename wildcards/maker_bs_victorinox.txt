Styled in a design language of Swiss precision and functional compactness. Glossy translucent or matte signal red surfaces with a white cross on a shield emblem inlaid near the top, polished stainless steel nested neatly in thin layers with brass-coloured accents. Smooth rounded rectangular profiles with a gently pinched waist and fine laser-etched small text in clean sans-serif capitals. Black polymer, olive, blue, silver and translucent coloured variants appear on tougher or playful versions. Bright signal red against cool silver steel and white, tidy and orderly, with many functions folded into a small body. Packaging uses red and white cards, silver surfaces and thin clear windows with neat technical diagrams. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
