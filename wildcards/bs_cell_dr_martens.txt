__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of defiant British rebellion with chunky, hardwearing, scuffed-and-loved attitude. Surfaces are glossy or crinkled leather in black and cherry red, with visible contrast stitching in bright yellow, thick matte rubber textures and metal studs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
