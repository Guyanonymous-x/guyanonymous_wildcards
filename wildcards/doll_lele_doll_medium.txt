a Mexican Lele cloth doll from Queretaro, a stuffed folk doll about as tall as an adult's forearm. She is sewn from soft cotton, her face a flat round of cloth with two black stitched eyes, a small stitched smile and round pink cheeks. Her black yarn hair is braided into two long braids woven through with bright satin ribbons that hang down past her shoulders. __doll_lele_doll_var__.
