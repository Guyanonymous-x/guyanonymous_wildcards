In the style of Pan Am, mid-century jet-age optimism and worldly glamour. A deep blue circle encloses a pale globe drawn with fine latitude and longitude lines in clean white linework. Lettering is bold, lowercase-leaning, evenly weighted sans-serif with generous tracking, in white on blue. Palette is cerulean and navy blue, crisp white, sandy beige, with a thin contrasting stripe in red or sky blue running along edges. Materials are glossy vinyl with white piping, canvas, polished aluminium and bright enamel. Shapes are boxy, rounded at the corners and clean, with tidy trim and flat graphic composition. Cheerful, confident, optimistic and travel-minded, with timeless retro polish. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
