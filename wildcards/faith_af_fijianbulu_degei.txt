Degei, the great serpent, lies coiled in a dark cave in the Nakauvadra hills, his huge body of dark green and brown scales filling the cave, his eyes on the souls before him
the souls of the dead, men and women in their thirties to eighties, kneel before Degei in his cave as he judges them, some sent on to rest and others turned away
Bulu, the underworld below the sea, a land of thatched houses with high roofs among coral and blue water, the dead living there with their chiefs
