Styled in a design language of youthful swinging-sixties mod design. The emblem is a simple five-petal daisy with a round centre ring, drawn as a bold flat black shape on white or reversed in white, repeated in rows or scattered as a cheerful pattern in rainbow colours on black. Materials are shiny PVC, patent, crisp cotton, jersey and opaque coloured knit with white contrast edges. Shapes are short, tidy, simple and cut with sharp precision for a playful, modern energy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
