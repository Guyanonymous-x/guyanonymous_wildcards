In the style of Jim Thompson, hand-woven silk with a lustrous, slightly slubby shimmer. Surfaces show iridescent two-tone shot effects in jewel colours such as sapphire, emerald, ruby, saffron, fuchsia, plum and turquoise, plus rich plains. Prints show stylised animals, tropical leaves, orchids, lotus, bamboo, pagoda motifs and bold stripes, drawn in a flat painterly style with generous colour fields. Rainbow-like ranges of colour sit side by side. Warm, exotic, elegant mood with teak tones and soft tropical light.
In the style of Batik Keris, wax-resist dyed cotton and silk in dense repeating patterns of parang diagonal waves, overlapping circles, cloud swirls, floral sprays, birds and trailing vines. Typical colours are warm sogan brown, deep indigo, dark black, cream and golden yellow, plus brighter coastal tones in red, turquoise and green. Fine crackle lines show where the wax cracked, and slight irregularities reveal the hand-drawn pen or copper stamp. Formal, refined, ceremonial mood, with a draped, flowing quality.
In the style of Danar Hadi, royal-court batik, hand-drawn and stamped on cotton and silk in elaborate classical patterns, with diagonal blade-like bands, circle grids, star flowers and lotus motifs filling the cloth evenly in fine linework. Colours are dignified earth tones of sogan brown, midnight indigo, cream white and soft black, with occasional touches of gold paint. Surfaces are hand-finished with a slightly crackled wax texture. Dignified, traditional, courtly mood, in dim museum-like light.
In the style of Batik Danar Hadi, hand-drawn wax-resist patterning in an earthy palette of soga brown, indigo blue, cream and black, with occasional red and gold. Motifs are classical and rhythmic, such as diagonal bands of interlocking S shapes, circles in a quatrefoil grid, scattered tiny flowers and winged cartouches, repeated across the whole surface. Fine dotted lines and slightly wavering white outlines show the hand of the maker, and thin crackle veins and tonal depth appear where the wax cracked. Courtly, refined and heritage-rich.
In the style of Bat Trang Ceramic Kilns, soft crackled ivory and cream-white glaze with freely brushed cobalt blue painting, and sometimes brown, green and iron-brown tones. Decoration is flowing and relaxed, with lotus, chrysanthemum, bamboo, birds, dragons, wave scrolls and key-fret borders, either brushed in blue or incised into the material. Forms have thick walls, swelling shoulders and a slightly uneven hand-thrown quality. Gentle, rustic, subtle and unpretentious.
In the style of Royal Selangor, polished silvery grey pewter with a soft lustrous gleam and either smooth or hammered finish. Heavy-bottomed, rounded forms with simple curves, sometimes mixed with dark wood or leather accents. Engraved or embossed decoration of hibiscus flowers, bamboo, batik-like flowers and rope borders. Cool, bright, refined, with a tropical colonial elegance. Surfaces are clean and tactile, with ornament kept shallow and restrained against large plain areas of polished metal.
