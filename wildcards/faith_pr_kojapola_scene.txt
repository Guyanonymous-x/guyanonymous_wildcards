at Pola in the soft gray light after a monsoon shower, the village bullocks lined up under a gateway of mango leaves strung on a rope, each animal washed and rubbed with oil until its hide shines, its horns painted bright orange and its forehead marked with red powder, and a farmer in his fifties feeds his pair sweet flatbreads from his hand
