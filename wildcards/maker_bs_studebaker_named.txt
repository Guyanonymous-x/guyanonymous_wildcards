In the style of Studebaker, quirky, futuristic and a bit strange American design of the 1950s. Low, wide stance, a slim body, a low profile, an almost European flat-sided silhouette and a gently tapering end. Chrome edges, pointed bullet-nose forms like an aircraft spinner and a streamlined panel with a big round dial and brushed metal. Gold or turquoise tops. Pale pastel two-tone paint in pink and white, sky blue and cream.
