In the style of Korg, analogue synthesizers and rhythm machines of the 1970s and 1980s. Instruments are long, shallow wedge shaped keyboards with a slanted metal control panel, brushed or anodised charcoal and silver, covered in rows of small round knobs, chunky slider caps and tiny coloured lever switches in orange, red, blue and white. Labels are screen printed in clean white or orange sans serif capitals with thin line diagrams of waveforms, envelopes and signal routing, and a row of patch sockets sits at the upper left. Side cheeks are dark stained wood or black vinyl clad. Keys are cream white and matte black with a firm square profile. Small red LED digits and amber or green indicator lights glow on the panel. Drum machines are compact grey boxes with rows of tiny rectangular rubber pads and a small knob bank. Mood is functional, technical, compact and precise, with a slightly futuristic hi-fi flavour of the late 1970s and the cool grey of 1980s digital workstations.
