In the style of Google Material Design, a flat but layered surface treatment where every element looks like a sheet of coloured paper lying on another, with soft diffuse shadows beneath to show height. Surfaces are flat solid colour with no bevels or gloss, in bold indigo, teal, pink, orange and purple, alongside clean white and light grey sheets with gently rounded corners. Round floating discs carry a single white pictogram and cast a soft shadow. Icons are simple geometric glyphs with even line weight. Lettering is a clean friendly geometric sans-serif in white or dark grey. Generous spacing, tidy grids, circular ripple shapes and cheerful flat colour blocks.
In the style of Microsoft Fluent, translucent frosted glass panels with a soft blur and fine noise texture floating over a coloured backdrop, rounded corners, subtle shadows, a fine light highlight that glows around the point of attention, and soft depth layering. Thin line icons or layered glyphs with soft colour gradients, a clean humanist sans-serif, pastel gradient backgrounds in blue, violet and teal swirls, and rounded pill shapes. Calm, airy and polished, with bright accent colours on neutral white or charcoal, like a late 2010s and 2020s software look.
In the style of Windows 95 era interface, a flat-grey bevelled surface treatment. Every panel and tile is light grey with a raised edge made from a white highlight line on the top and left and dark grey shadow lines on the bottom and right, while recessed areas are white with an inset dark edge. A flat teal green field fills the ground. Title strips are solid navy blue fading to light blue, carrying bold white sans-serif text and tiny square grey controls. Icons are chunky pixel pictograms in a sixteen colour palette, with yellow folders, dithered grey patterns and a four-colour waving flag emblem in red, green, blue and yellow. Lettering is crisp bitmap sans-serif. Blocky, beige-grey and nostalgic.
In the style of Early Mac OS interface, a one-bit black and white pixel surface treatment with no gradients. Shapes have thin crisp black outlines, small rounded corners and a hard offset shadow along the right and bottom edges. Grey is made only from fine dithered checkerboard and dotted patterns, and title strips are filled with thin horizontal pinstripes with a tiny square at one end. Icons are chunky hand-drawn pixel pictograms with a cheerful smiling face motif, a pointing arrow and a wristwatch shape. Lettering is a rounded, friendly pixel typeface with wide curvy letters. Later versions add soft pastel greys, lilac and teal tints with subtle bevels. Charming, crisp, hand-crafted and playful.
