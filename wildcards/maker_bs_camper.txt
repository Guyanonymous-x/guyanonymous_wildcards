Styled in a design language of quirky, comfortable, rounded and chunky, playful, down-to-earth, relaxed and a bit nerdy. Mismatched pairs where each side differs in colour or detail. Plain cardboard in white and brown. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
