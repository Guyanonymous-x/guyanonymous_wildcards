and the worshipper pours milk over the five stones from a small spoon of bright copper, one stone after another, the milk running down into the brass plate where it gathers in a white pool
and the worshipper lays offerings on the stones, three-part green bilva leaves on Shiva, a sprig of green tulsi basil on Vishnu, a red hibiscus flower on Devi, a red flower on Surya and a few blades of green durva grass on Ganesha
and the worshipper waves a small brass lamp with a burning lump of white camphor in slow clockwise circles before the stones, the other hand holding up a small brass bell with a handle topped by a little figure
and the worshipper dabs a dot of yellow sandalwood paste and a dot of red kumkum on each stone with the ring finger, a little heap of rice grains and a whole coconut set beside the plate
