Styled in a design language of no-brand minimalism, quiet, modest and thoughtful. Plain, simple, no ornament and no unnecessary features. Natural, raw, unbleached materials such as pale wood, kraft paper, cotton, linen, white ceramic and matte plastic in off-white, beige, oatmeal, grey, charcoal and muted navy. Finishes are matte, soft and tactile, with neat even stitching and honest construction. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
