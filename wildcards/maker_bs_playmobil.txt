Styled in a design language of small hard plastic forms with oversized round shapes, simple dot marks, a curved smile line, C-shaped curves and standardised blocky, stiffly jointed construction. Bright primary red, yellow, blue and green plus brown, grey and skin tones in a matte to satin finish, with clean moulded seams and thin moulded details. Everything is simplified into cheerful, friendly, clip-together pieces with tiny printed faces and flat painted patterns. Packaging uses clear windows and bold colour panels in a cheerful, wholesome tone. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
