Styled in a design language of heavy sterling surfaces in a lavish late-Victorian and Art Nouveau manner. Metal is hand-hammered or chased, with bright polished highlights, darker oxidised recesses and a martelled texture. Decoration includes embossed flowers, lily pads, whiplash curves, dragonflies, Japanese-inspired motifs, inlays of copper and brass in contrasting colour and flowing organic lines. Forms are rounded, with applied leaves and sculpted finishing details. The feel is rich, sculptural and ornamental, with small stamped marks. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
