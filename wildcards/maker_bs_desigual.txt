Styled in a design language of an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas, with a big stylised swirling logo. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
