A fire and one lamp on a sideboard rather than a center light, because a center light makes a room visible from the street and a lamp does not, a curtain drawn with a gap at the top, a hallway dark, a stair lit by nothing, a street outside under blackout or curfew with no lighting at all, and an interior in which the faces round the table are lit from below by firelight, which is both the truth of the room and the reason every painting of it looks the same
