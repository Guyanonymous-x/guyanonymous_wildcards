Styled in a design language of loud, rugged, jobsite-ready and high-visibility. Bright yellow plastic with black rubberised grips and black accent bands, boxy chunky shapes with strong ribbing and protective rubber bumpers. Chunky yellow and black blocks, with small silver steel parts. Yellow and black pairing repeats throughout, rugged and stackable in feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
