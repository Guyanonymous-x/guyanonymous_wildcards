Styled in a design language of folk-luxe printed wool with a wide repeating border and long knotted fringe trim. The field is deep black, scarlet, emerald, navy, ivory or cream, covered by an exuberant spread of big full-blown cabbage roses, peonies, tulips and leaves, with smaller sprigs and buds. Roses are shaded in raspberry, pink, orange and white with green and turquoise leaves, outlined in fine lines, with a hand-printed, slightly soft-edged look. Borders use paisley curls, garlands and scrollwork. Some are plain solid colour with only a rose border. Rich, floral, romantic and decorative. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
