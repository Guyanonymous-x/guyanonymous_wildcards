Styled in a design language of bold, sumptuous, eclectic luxury. Vivid vintage floral prints with butterflies, blooms, bees, tigers and snakes appear in dense, colourful, storybook layouts. Maximalist phases add embroidery, velvet, jewel tones, clashing colour, quirky retro mixes and ornate gilt metal. Packaging is deep burgundy red or moss green with matte tonal pattern. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
