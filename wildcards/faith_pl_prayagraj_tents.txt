a vast tent city spread over the sand to the horizon, thousands of white canvas tents and bright tents of orange, red and yellow set in long straight rows, divided by wide sandy roads and tall light poles
the camps of the akharas, the old orders of Hindu holy men, large tented enclosures entered through tall decorated gateways of painted cloth and bamboo, with long saffron and red flags flying from high poles above them
the tents at night lit with long strings of small white and colored lights along their roofs and gateways, glowing warm across the dark sand, with rows of bright lamps along the roads between them
