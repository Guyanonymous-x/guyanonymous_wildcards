In the style of Tolix, tough, thin, urban industrial design pressed from single sheets of steel. Simple tapered lines, rounded pressed curves, perforations used as the only pattern, and reinforced edges. Repeatable, economical forms with a visibly utilitarian build. Finish is matte lacquer, powder-coat or galvanised raw zinc in red, dark green, yellow, black, grey and silver, with dents, scuffs and a worn patina. Factory canteen and cafe terrace character, honest and unfussy.
