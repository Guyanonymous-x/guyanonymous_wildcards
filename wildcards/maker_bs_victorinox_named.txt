Styled in a design language of Swiss precision and functional compactness. Glossy translucent or matte signal red surfaces with a white cross on a shield emblem inlaid near the top, polished stainless steel nested neatly in thin layers with brass-coloured accents. Black polymer, olive, blue, silver and translucent coloured variants appear on tougher or playful versions. Bright signal red against cool silver steel and white, tidy and orderly, with many functions folded into a small body. Packaging uses red and white cards, silver surfaces and thin clear windows with neat technical diagrams. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
