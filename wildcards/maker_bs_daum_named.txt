Styled in a design language. Colours are mottled and graded from smoky orange to deep green, milky mist, powder blue and amber, with enamelled and gilded highlights. Naturalistic plant forms swell around bulbous bodies. Soft translucence, dreamy, rich, with a subdued glow like looking through frosted weather. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
