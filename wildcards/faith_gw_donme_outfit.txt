for the men of the 1700s, a long entari, an ankle-length robe of striped silk crossed over at the chest, a broad sash of patterned cloth wound around the waist, a long fur-trimmed coat over it, and a white turban wound over a small red cap
for the women of the 1700s, a ferace, a loose ankle-length outdoor cloak of dark blue wool with a broad square collar hanging down the back, and a yashmak, two layers of fine white muslin veil covering the hair and the lower face up to the bridge of the nose
for the men of the late 1800s, a stambouline, a dark buttoned frock coat reaching the knees, narrow dark trousers, a white shirt with a high stiff collar, and a red felt fez with a black silk tassel
for the women of the late 1800s, a black çarşaf, a long full cloak and skirt of black silk drawn over the head and body, with a thin black veil lowered over the face
