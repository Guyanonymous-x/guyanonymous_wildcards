In the style of Margilan Yodgorlik Silk Factory, vivid, sumptuous, handmade silky resist-dyed textiles. Lustrous silk is patterned by tying and dyeing the threads before weaving, so every motif has softly blurred, feathered edges. Designs are bold and flowing, with large almond shapes, teardrop and flame forms, pomegranates, jagged blossoms, stripes and zigzag bands in saturated jewel colours of ruby red, magenta, saffron yellow, indigo, emerald green and black, outlined by thin white lines on a deep ground. Some cloth is shiny and light, some blends silk and cotton for a matte feel. Natural dyes make colours glow but sit a little uneven. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
