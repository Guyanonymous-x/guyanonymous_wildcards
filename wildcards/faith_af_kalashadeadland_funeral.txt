the body of a man in his seventies lies on a bier in the jestak han, the house of the clan goddess, dressed in his finest, a feathered cap on his head and money tucked in his clothes
women in their twenties to eighties in long black dresses with bright embroidered borders, their kupas headdresses of cowrie shells on their heads, dance in a slow circle around the bier, arms over each other's shoulders
men in their twenties to sixties kill goats and cattle for the funeral feast, cutting their throats in the yard, the meat, bread and cheese shared among hundreds of guests from the three valleys
