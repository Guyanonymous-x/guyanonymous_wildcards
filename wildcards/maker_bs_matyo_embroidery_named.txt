Styled in a design language of dense, thick, glossy thread worked on black, white or dark blue cloth. Motifs are huge, full-blown roses, bold tulips, carnations, leaf sprays, buds and tendrils in heavily stylised form, often with a central flower surrounded by smaller blossoms and swirling leaves in mirrored symmetry. Palette is bright, high-contrast and joyful, red, pink, magenta, orange, yellow, light blue and green on black, or navy, crimson and deep green on white. Tight satin stitch gives a slightly raised, padded texture with black or gold outlines. Richly decorated, colourful, joyous and festive, as if a flower garden covered every surface. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
