at the feast of the dead in a log house the table is set with pancakes, porridge, beer and a lit candle, the empty place at its head left for the dead, the kin in their twenties to eighties at the benches
a woman in her fifties heats the bathhouse for the dead before the feast and lays out a clean towel and a birch switch, the door left open for them
the pale dead, men and women in their forties to nineties in white linen, come in through the door of the log house at night and sit down at the table among their living kin
