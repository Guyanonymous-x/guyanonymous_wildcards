in the early 1970s, gray city light and wet streets, a low even overcast the color of slate above the rooftops, the asphalt and the sidewalks dark and shining after rain, long reflections of headlights, traffic lights and shop windows stretched across the puddles, the brick house fronts dull red-brown and the black capes of the members matte against the gray
at night, streetlights and the lit windows of the chapter house, pools of yellow light from tall lampposts lying on the empty sidewalk, the house front in deep shadow above them, thin lines of warm candle glow showing around the edges of the black curtains on each floor, the fanlight over the door lit amber and a dark blue city sky with a faint orange haze over the roofs
in winter, snow on the steps, a thick white layer rounding each stone tread of the stoop and capping the iron railings, a single track of boot prints climbing to the black door, snow banked along the curb and over the roofs of parked cars, bare black tree branches against a pale gray sky and soft bluish daylight on the brick fronts
