Styled in a design language of futuristic, serene and floating, like a spaceship landed in a quiet street. Streamlined forms tapering gently to one end, very low smooth surfaces, volumes that seem to hover on thin supports, and pale tops set against darker bodies. Softly curved panels with no sharp creases and gently skirted lower edges. Pale grey, ivory, sea green, pastel blue and sand. Plush cloth and soft leather in sculpted, flowing shapes. Calm, modern and quietly strange. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
