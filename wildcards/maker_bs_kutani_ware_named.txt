In the style of Kutani Ware, dense, opaque overglaze enamels in five dark, saturated colours, namely deep green, Prussian blue, yellow, purple and aubergine red. A second manner uses a bright red ground with gold, carrying tiny figures and ornate panels in minute detail. Green is often laid on thickly as a background with a heavy, jewel-like gloss. Rich, theatrical, almost tapestry-like richness and a thick bright glaze. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kutani Ware in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
