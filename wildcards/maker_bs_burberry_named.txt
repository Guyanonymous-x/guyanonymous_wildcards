Styled in a design language of classic British heritage with a sharp check pattern. A tan, camel and honey base crossed by black, white and red lines forms a recognisable plaid, used as lining, trim or bold all-over cover. Materials are gabardine, trench-coat twill, wool and leather in stone, khaki, navy and black, with horn or metal buttons, buckles and belts, and tailored, belted, structured silhouettes. Muted rainy-day palette, now often with a vivid modern accent. Understated, proper, weatherproof and polished. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
