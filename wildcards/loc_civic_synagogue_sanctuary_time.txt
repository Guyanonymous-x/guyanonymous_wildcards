Friday at dusk, the evening service, the lamps on, the hall beyond laid for a meal
Saturday morning, the principal service of the week and the room's designed condition, three hours, a reading from one of the four scrolls carried from the ark to the central platform and back, the congregation standing and sitting perhaps forty times, and between thirty and 180 people in a room built for 400
Saturday at noon, a blessing in the hall
Saturday evening, a short service
Weekday mornings, a quorum of ten in a side room or in the first two ranks of seats, which is the room's commonest occupied state and uses perhaps a twentieth of it
A weekday evening, the same
The days of awe in the autumn, when the room is full to the gallery and extra seating is set in the aisles, which is the one time a year the building's capacity is used and is what the capacity was built for
A wedding or a bar mitzvah, when the platform is the center of the event
A memorial anniversary, when one lamp on the board is switched on and a family of four sits in seats that may have been theirs for a century; and the night, locked, with one lamp burning before the ark and nothing else
