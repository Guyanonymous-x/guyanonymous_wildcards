Styled in a design language of a rigorous three-band layout with a central white panel framed by two coloured stripes in orange, green and blue. Later work uses a Swiss-style grid with bold illustration. Mid-century uncoated paper feel, matte printing and crisp rules. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
