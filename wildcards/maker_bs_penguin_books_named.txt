In the style of Penguin Books, a rigorous three-band layout with a central white panel framed by two coloured stripes in orange, green and blue. Title and author are set in clean sans-serif or classic serif inside the white band, with a small bird mark at the bottom. Later work uses a Swiss-style grid with bold illustration. Mid-century uncoated paper feel, matte printing and crisp rules. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Penguin Books in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
