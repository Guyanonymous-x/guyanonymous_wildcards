In the style of Taiwan Beer, cheerful, social, straightforward and hot-evening, with strong green, gold and white colour blocking. Brown glass with a white and green paper surface bearing a small crest-like emblem. Premium versions switch to gold and white, a medal version uses green glass with white, red and green. Log-cabin style halls with wooden beams, long benches, round tables and neon green signs.
In the style of Kweichow Moutai, formal, festive, prosperous and traditional ceremonial design. A squat cylindrical white porcelain form with a short neck, glossy milk-white glaze and a slight shoulder curve, topped with a red ribbon tied over a seal and often a tiny hanging tag. A bright vermilion band wraps the middle, edged in gold. Special editions use red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Lacquer red or wood with yellow silk lining, embossed in gold and closed with a white cord or golden bow. Auspicious red, gold and white against dark polished tables.
In the style of Rooh Afza, rich, nostalgic, floral and festive South Asian lithographic art. A vivid red field framed by a decorative border of small stylised flowers. Illustrations of rose bunches, fruits, herbs and leaves are drawn in a fine old printing manner with delicate hatching. Forms are tall, square shouldered and slightly ribbed, filled with deep ruby colour and capped in red or gold. Cooling, sweet, perfumed, celebratory and heritage-rich.
In the style of Kingfisher Beer, social, festive, glamorous hot-afternoon design with a bird emblem. Cream or white ground with a bold red band and a gold rim. Slender brown glass and glossy cream and red surfaces are accented with gold rings. Warm red, gold, cream and a flash of electric blue, poolside and lively.
In the style of Singha, sunny, tropical, festive heraldic design. A deep red and gold border frames a central mythical lion in a rearing pose with a curly mane, tufted tail, raised paw and fierce expression, drawn in old engraving style and flanked by ornamental curls, a crown emblem and a royal bird crest. Surfaces are glossy deep red with gold and white detail and gold foil accents that add a sense of ceremony. Amber brown, brown and cream complete the palette. Chilled condensation, spicy grilled food and beach light. Lively and bold.
In the style of Tiger Beer, cool, bold, energetic, nocturnal and urban design in navy, gold and hot orange. A deep navy field with a bold arched gold border, a leaping big cat in orange gold stripes on black rendered in dynamic ink lines. A small crown, malt ears and a ribbon give a heraldic effect. Glossy navy surfaces with gold highlights and metallic silver. Ice buckets and red plastic tables at a night food market.
In the style of Ararat Brandy, deep, aged, ceremonial and warm amber heritage design. Heavy, rounded-shouldered forms with a deep amber glow, dark cork or gilded accents. Cream or ivory paper tones with a gold embossed border, a tall mountain with a snowy twin peak. A red phoenix emblem appears on newer versions. Rows of little gold stars signal quality. Rich brown, gold, burgundy and cream.

In the style of Sapporo Brewery, crisp, masculine, social, evening-lit and no-nonsense design with a star emblem. The star is drawn in a crisp geometric outline, centred. Silver foil highlights. Golden liquid with a thick white foam head. Black, gold, red and silver.
