In the style of Tesla, minimalist electric cars with smooth unbroken body surfaces, a closed-off nose with no grille, a low sloping fastback roofline and flush door handles. Slim LED headlamps and a thin horizontal tail light bar, a large glass roof, frameless windows, and a plain T-shaped badge. Aerodynamic turbine-style wheels with a flat cover, body-coloured trim and almost no chrome. The interior is a stark white or black cabin with a single huge landscape touchscreen, a wooden or matte strip, no instrument cluster and no buttons. Colours of pearl white, solid black, deep blue metallic, midnight silver and red multicoat. Clean, futuristic, software-first.
