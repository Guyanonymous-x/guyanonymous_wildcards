Styled in a design language of plain, hard-wearing, no-frills Tasmanian workwear design. Smooth, waxed or oiled leather in rustic brown, chestnut, black, tan, deep oxblood and slate grey, with a scuffed, honest, well-used character. Forms are rounded, stout and stripped back, with elastic panels, bold pull tabs, visible stitching and chunky black rubber and contrasting pale edge stripes. Seasonal ranges add stout colour pops in olive, sage, rust and navy. Honest, timeless, practical and tough. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
