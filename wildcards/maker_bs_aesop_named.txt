In the style of Aesop, literate, apothecary-inspired restraint with quiet luxury. Rich amber-brown glass and matte brown surfaces with plain cream or off-white paper labels, set in neat black small type with long, tightly spaced lines of detail. The wordmark is a thin, classical roman serif in lowercase or small capitals, surrounded by white space. Palette is amber brown, cream, black, olive green, terracotta and muted stone, with matte black and brushed brass trim. Wrapping is plain cardboard in earthy tones with a single line of black type. Tactile, thoughtful, minimal, sensory and understated, favouring texture and text over decoration. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Aesop in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
