Styled in a design language of austere, geometric, Art Deco modernist luxury. Plain rectangular clear glass with chamfered corners and flat-sided facets, with pale golden tones. Flat white fields with a thin black border. Plain white surfaces with a black outline. Palette is black, white and clear glass with a touch of gold. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
