Styled in a design language of severe, minimalist, intellectual luxury with matte black industrial nylon trimmed in cross-hatch textured leather and a small enamelled triangular silver plaque. Pared-down sheath shapes, A-line volumes with flat fronts and boxy forms in muted mint green, olive, mustard, dusty pink, grey, brown and black, with deliberately off-beat colour pairings and retro 1950s patterns. Thick platform volumes, chunky forms, clean seams, utilitarian pockets and ironic fur trim. Deadpan geometry and ugly-chic wit. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
