In the style of Nikon, Japanese single-lens reflex cameras of the 1960s to 1980s. Rugged, boxy bodies with a pentaprism hump, black enamel or silver chrome finish, and a leatherette covering with fine pebble grain. Heavy metal knurled dials on top for shutter speed and film rewind, a chunky film advance lever, and a bold yellow and black stripe on the lens mount. Lenses are black barrels with deep rubber-ribbed focus rings and engraved white, orange and green numerals. Wordmark is bold white capital letters on the prism front. Mood is rugged, professional and mechanical.
