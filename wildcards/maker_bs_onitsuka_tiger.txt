In the style of Onitsuka Tiger, slim retro low-profile leather and suede sports shoes from the 1960s and 70s, with a narrow elongated toe box, flat thin rubber outsole, rolled heel, and a contrasting side stripe formed by crossing wavy parallel lines on the upper. Colours are classic cream or white bodies with accents in red, navy, yellow, forest green and black, often two-tone suede and nylon mesh panels with a tonal heel counter. Details include perforated toe vents, a gum-colored sole edge, thin laces, small gold stamped tongue label with a striding big cat, and a tiny embroidered tiger head on the heel tab. Clean, nostalgic, compact athletic silhouette, subtle vintage gym mood.
