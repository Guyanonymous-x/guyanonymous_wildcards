Styled in a design language of rural, handmade, colourful and lovingly textured craft design. Handwoven cotton, silk and fine muslin in vivid turmeric yellow, red, indigo, leaf green and white, with block-printed borders, tiny woven floral motifs and fine stripes. The signature surface is quilted running-stitch embroidery forming flowers, fish, birds, trees and boats in rows of red, blue and green thread on white or black cloth, with a gentle wavy hand-stitched texture. Natural materials such as terracotta, jute, cane and brass add earthy warmth. Spaces are tall and bright with hanging fabrics, rustic wood and earthy walls. Like a village fair in a tidy modern hall. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
