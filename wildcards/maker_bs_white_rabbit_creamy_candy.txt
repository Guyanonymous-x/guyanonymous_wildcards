Styled in a design language of sweet, nostalgic, modest, childlike old Shanghai charm. A naturalistic ink drawing of a white rabbit stands before an old-fashioned artist palette, drawn with fine line shading in the manner of 1950s commercial art. Bright red and white with a large rabbit face, wide forward-looking eyes, a cartoonish cheerful animal on a pale blue sky ground. Vintage Art Deco borders. Limited white, red, blue and black palette with a slightly matte print texture. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
