Styled in a design language of British confectionery in cheerful cardboard tins and packs, with chocolate boxes tied in ribbon, colourful fruit gum tubes, foil-wrapped bars in bright blue, red and gold, and quaint, colourful shop displays. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
