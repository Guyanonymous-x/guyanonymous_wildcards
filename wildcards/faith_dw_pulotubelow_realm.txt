deep beneath the sea a wide bright land spreads out, green islands and white beaches under a dome of blue water, great round houses of chiefs with tall domed thatched roofs on carved posts among palms
the realm of Pulotu lies in a vast underwater valley, coral gardens of red, orange and white around its villages, schools of bright fish swimming between the thatched houses
in the center of Pulotu the water of life, Vaiola, a clear shining spring pool, its waters glowing pale blue, palms and flowering trees around its edge
