Shot through a standard 720nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a soft ivory and cream body against steel-blue shadows, the skin like polished wax and the eyes dark and glassy. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
