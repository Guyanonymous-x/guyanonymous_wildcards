Styled in a design language of simple solid wooden forms with rounded, lobed outlines and deep carved edges, covered in bold shallow gouge carving. Flowers, leaves and waves are cut with rounded petal edges and flat bevelled facets, leaving visible chisel marks. The lacquer is slightly matte, a vermilion or cinnabar layer over black that wears through at the high points to reveal a warm brown underlayer, so edges look softly aged. Colours stay in red-brown, black and warm wood tones. Folk-craft, sculptural, handmade and quietly rustic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
