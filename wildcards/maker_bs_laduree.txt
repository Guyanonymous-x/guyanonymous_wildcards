In the style of Laduree, pastel macarons in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow and coffee brown, stacked in neat pyramids. Boxes in pale mint green or pink with gold embossed script, gilt edges and a satin ribbon, wrapped in tissue. Shops with powder-blue and pistachio paintwork, gilded mouldings, painted ceilings of cherubs, marble tables and glass cabinets. Cursive gold lettering, a delicate oval crest, and floral flourishes. Palette of pistachio, rose, cream and gold. The look is Second Empire Parisian tearoom, dainty, sugary, ornate and gilded.
