Styled in a design language of small, upright, rounded, modest, cute and period-correct socialist-era design. A smooth compact body with a bulbous front, softly bulging separate haunches, a high slim roofline and small round lights flush with the surface. Thin chrome trim and a few horizontal chrome bars, with one thin chrome line along the flank. Later versions turn squarer with larger lights and bigger glass. Colour is plain glossy pastel, cream, sky blue, pistachio, pale grey, light red and mustard. Details are simple and metallic, with generous thin circular forms. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
