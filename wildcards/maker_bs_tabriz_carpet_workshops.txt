In the style of Tabriz Carpet Workshops, finely knotted wool and silk rugs with a central medallion on an open or dense field, framed by a wide main border and several narrow guard borders. Colours are rich and layered, with ivory, deep madder red, indigo, navy, soft turquoise and gold. Motifs include curling vines, palmettes, hunting scenes, trees of life, vases of flowers and cartouches, drawn in very precise, curved lines. The pile is short and velvety, with sharply defined details, strict symmetry and a spacious, courtly composition. Corner spandrels echo the medallion shape, and fringes are neat and white.
