the yuxin, the spirits of the forest, are shown as tall bright figures of men and women in their thirties to sixties covered in kene patterns, standing among the trees with feather crowns on their heads
the spirits of the animals move through the forest in their own forms, a jaguar, a boa, a harpy eagle and a tapir, each painted with glowing kene lines along its body
the yuxin of the sacred vine drift through the air as bright spiraling vines and leaves, flowers opening along them and small shining faces of adults appearing among the leaves
