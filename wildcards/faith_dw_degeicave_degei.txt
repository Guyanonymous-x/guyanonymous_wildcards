inside the cave lies Degei as a vast serpent, his long dark body coiled many times over itself on the floor of the cave, his scales glinting dull bronze in the gloom, his great head raised
Degei appears as some of the old stories tell, a serpent whose upper body is that of a gray-bearded man in his seventies, his lower body a coiled serpent of stone set into the rock of the cave
Degei lies sleeping in his coils beside a heap of stones, and Turukawa, the black dove who wakes him each morning, a sleek glossy black dove, sits on a ledge above his head, while a crack runs through the rock where he has turned
