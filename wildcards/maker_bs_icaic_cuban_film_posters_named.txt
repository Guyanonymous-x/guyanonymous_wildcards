In the style of ICAIC Cuban Film Posters, vibrant, witty, politically charged and strongly handmade silkscreen design. A few flat, saturated colours in layers with no gradients, bright red, yellow, cobalt blue, orange, green, pink and black, laid down one screen at a time on cheap rough paper with slightly uneven edges and visible misregistration. Lettering is hand-drawn, chunky, irregular, sometimes stencil-like. Pop art, psychedelia and constructivism blended with satire. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words ICAIC Cuban Film Posters in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
