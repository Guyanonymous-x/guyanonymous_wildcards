five or seven doorcases in line
a frieze and a cornice
their openings aligned to within a few millimeters across sixty meters of building
ten or fourteen door leaves of six panels each
hung to fold back flat against the wall rather than into the room
a brass door furniture set of knob
escutcheon and finger plate repeated at every opening
a cast-brass door stop let into the floor at every leaf
a run of carpet or a bare polished board along the axis
a pier glass or a chimney glass at the far end of the run placed to continue it
a single tall window or a glazed garden door terminating the view
a pair of candle stands at each threshold
