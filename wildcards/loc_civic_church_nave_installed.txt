a chancel arch at the east end and a tower arch at the west, which together produce the type's defining division - the nave is the parish's and the chancel is the priest's
a division that was legally and financially real
since the parishioners were responsible for repairing the nave and the rector for the chancel, and that split survives in the fabric as two different qualities of masonry and two different dates of roof in a great many churches; a rood screen across the chancel arch in the medieval arrangement
