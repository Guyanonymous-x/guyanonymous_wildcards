and in the open storage yard men carry tall clay jars of oil and reed sacks of barley on their shoulders up a mud-brick ramp to the granaries, while others pour grain into heaps on the swept ground
and in a long weaving house dozens of women sit at ground looms pegged to the floor, weaving wide lengths of wool cloth, while others spin thread with spindles at the door
and at the ration gate women and men line up holding out cups and baskets while a worker in his forties fills each one with barley from rough clay bowls with slanting walls
and beyond the walls herders in their thirties drive flocks of sheep and goats along a canal bank while plowmen behind teams of oxen cut furrows in the flat brown fields
and women in their thirties to fifties kneel at low stone slabs grinding barley into flour with long hand stones, piles of flour and baskets of grain around them
