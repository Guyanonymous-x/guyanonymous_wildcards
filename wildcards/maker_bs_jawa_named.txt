Styled in a design language of solid, simple, mid-century and slightly sporty, with plenty of shine. Compact, rounded, teardrop-like forms with swept-back lines and a lean, tidy stance. Colour is two-tone glossy enamel, maroon and cream, black and red, deep blue and white, or olive and cream, divided by clean lines and finished with fine hand-painted gold pinstripes. Bright chrome and polished metal accents catch the light against the deep gloss colour. Small bold enamel-style badges and neat round motifs. Honest, optimistic, proudly engineered and built to last, with the cheerful confidence of post-war Central European industry. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
