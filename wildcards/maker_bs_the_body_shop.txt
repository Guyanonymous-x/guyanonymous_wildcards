Styled in a design language of earnest, earthy, ethical design in deep forest green and white. Clear and pale frosted plastic is paired with matte green and cream surfaces, simple thin white type and an apple-green accent. Natural textures such as jute, kraft paper, wicker and raw cotton keep things honest, with hand-written paper tags and rounded botanical line drawings of leaves, seeds and fruit in flat green, cream and warm orange. Wholesome, sincere, natural and quietly proud. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
