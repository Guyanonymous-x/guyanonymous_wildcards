Styled in a design language of small, toylike, rugged, home-made Brazilian utility spirit. Boxy upright shapes with flat faces, a high squared mass and big rounded arches. Round forms sit in plain recessed fields with simple slot-like openings, black edging and small steel shapes. Bare fibreglass-like surfaces, flat planes and solid, shiny gel-coated colour. Palette of bright orange, lime green, bright yellow and sky blue. Cheerful, rugged, inventive and proudly improvised, as if built in a workshop for fun. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
