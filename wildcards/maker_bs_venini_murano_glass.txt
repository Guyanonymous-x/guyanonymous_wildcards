Styled in a design language of hand-blown art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered under clear casing. Techniques create fused patches of colour like woven fabric, delicate lace threads, vertical ribbons, trapped bubbles and acid-etched satin surfaces. Forms feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean mid-century modern lines, polished edges and a tiny etched mark. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
