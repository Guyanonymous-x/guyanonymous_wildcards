Styled in a design language of streamlined, futuristic, odd and aerodynamic. A long smooth teardrop body, a central spine-like fin running along the back, a tapering end with rows of cooling louvres, smooth flush sides and curved skirted forms. Disc patterns with chrome caps and bright trim used sparingly. Dark green, black, metallic grey and burgundy. Wide soft cloth or leather and warm wooden panelling with simple round faces. Eccentric, engineered and slightly strange. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
