Styled in a design language of hushed, monumental and hand-built grandeur. Tall formal verticals, long calm unbroken surfaces and a fine bright line drawn along the length of a form, finished with a tiny sculpted silver figure leaning into the wind as a crowning ornament. Mirror-polished stainless metal, slim vertical fluting, deep lustrous paint in claret, midnight blue, silver and black, often two-toned. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
