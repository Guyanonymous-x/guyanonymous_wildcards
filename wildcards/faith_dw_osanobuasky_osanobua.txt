Osanobua sits on a throne in his sky palace as a great king in his sixties with deep brown skin, in a high crown and a tall collar of red coral beads, all in white cloth, two attendants holding up his arms
Osanobua is shown only as a white light in the sky palace, the light falling on a throne draped in white cloth and dusted with white chalk, the gods standing before it
Olokun, his son, sits below in a palace under the waters, a king in his forties in white cloth and coral beads with mudfish for legs, mirrors and white cloth around him
