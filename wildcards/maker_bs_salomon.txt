Styled in a design language of technical, aggressive, engineered mountain-sport design with sculpted, angular silhouettes and sharp protective overlays. Mixed synthetic mesh, welded films, moulded rigid shells and rubberised textures with exposed cord details, all arranged in layered, fast-looking panels. Palette of black, charcoal, warm grey and white with sudden hits of bright red, electric blue, lime, orange or earthy mineral tones in muted colourways. Matte and gloss textures sit side by side, with chevron and ribbed patterns, giving a fast, slightly futuristic, precise feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
