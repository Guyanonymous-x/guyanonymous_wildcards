Styled in a design language of calm, precise, utilitarian simplicity. Forms are clean and regular, with no ornament, flat planes, tidy seams and a minimal, modular logic that repeats across everything. Materials are soft smooth cotton, fine knit, brushed fleece and light technical fabric in a quiet palette of white, grey, navy, black and beige. Packaging is crisp white cardboard and clear film with a neat grid of small type, ordered, honest, generous with whitespace and made for everyday use. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
