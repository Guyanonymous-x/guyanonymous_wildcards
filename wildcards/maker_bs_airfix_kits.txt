Styled in a design language of 1970s plastic model kits of gray or colored polystyrene parts on sprues with decal sheets, half-built aircraft, tanks and ships with brushed enamel paint and weathering, sold in slim boxes with a dramatic painted battle scene. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
