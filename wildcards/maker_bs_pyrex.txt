Styled in a design language of American mid-century domestic design in glossy glass and creamy opal milk glass. Rounded, plump forms in tidy graduated sets, with smooth, seamless, shiny surfaces. Sunny, saturated colours of turquoise, aqua, primary red, buttercup yellow, avocado green, burnt orange and pink, in flat solid fields. Printed patterns repeat in white or contrasting colours, with snowflakes, daisies, gooseberries, butterflies, stripes, flowers and atomic starbursts, along with retro gingham checks. Cheerful, nostalgic, optimistic and thoroughly wholesome. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
