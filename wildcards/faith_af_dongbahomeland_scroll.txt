the Road to the Gods scroll unrolled along the floor of a courtyard from the dead man's feet, a long narrow strip of painted cloth as wide as a man's shoulders, stretching past the gate
at the bottom of the scroll the hells, demons with horns, fangs and tails torturing the dead with blades and fire, painted in red and black
in the middle of the scroll the world of people, farmers, herders and animals, and at the top the heavens of the gods, figures seated on lotus thrones under bright clouds
the dongba points to each picture on the scroll with a long stick as he reads, the kin in their twenties to seventies in Naxi dress kneeling along its edges
along the whole length of the scroll march gods, people and demons among dozens of fabulous beasts, winged lions, horned serpents, birds with human heads and tigers with wings, each painted small in red, blue, green and yellow with a black outline
