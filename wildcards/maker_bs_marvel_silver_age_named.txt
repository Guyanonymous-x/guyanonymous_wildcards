Styled in a design language of dynamic, muscular heroes with dramatic foreshortening, bold black ink with heavy shadows and halftone dots in four-colour print. Primary red, blue and yellow with purple and green accents. Fists bursting out of the frame, cosmic energy crackles. Exuberant, heroic and larger than life. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
