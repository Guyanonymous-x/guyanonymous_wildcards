Styled in a design language of flamboyant, dense, sumptuous decoration that leaves little blank white. A rich palette of deep underglaze cobalt blue, fiery iron red and lavish gold, with panels of chrysanthemums, peonies, phoenixes, fans, waves and brocade-like patterns packed side by side. Blue areas alternate with red and gilded floral scrolls. Outlines are lobed and scalloped with ornate edges. Glossy glaze, heavy ornament, opulent and festive like woven silk brocade. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
