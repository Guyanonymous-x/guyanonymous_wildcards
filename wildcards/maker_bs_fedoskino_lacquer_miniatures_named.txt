In the style of Fedoskino Lacquer Miniatures, glossy black lacquer built up layer upon layer, often with a bright red or vermilion hidden interior, carrying a small, finely detailed realistic painting framed by the dark. The painting uses many translucent glazes so figures and skies glow with soft inner light, over a shimmering gold, silver leaf or mother-of-pearl underlayer. Subjects are storytelling scenes, winter landscapes and flower bouquets. Edges may be framed in gold ornament, braids and flowers. The finish is mirror-smooth, deep and jewel-like in black, crimson, gold and sky blue. Polished, narrative and luxurious.
