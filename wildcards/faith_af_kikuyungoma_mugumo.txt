the sacred mugumo, a great wild fig with a wide crown and a thick pale trunk wrapped in roots, stands on the ridge, the elders in their sixties to nineties in skin cloaks gathered under it
under the mugumo an elder in his seventies holds a ram with a pure brown coat as men in their forties to sixties kill it with a cut at the throat, offering it to Ngai and the ancestors
the elders in their sixties to eighties pour muratina, the honey beer, and milk from gourds onto the roots of the mugumo, facing Mount Kenya
