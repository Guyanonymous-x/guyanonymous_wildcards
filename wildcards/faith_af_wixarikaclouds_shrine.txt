in a small stone and adobe xiriki, a family shrine with a thatched roof, a clear quartz crystal the size of a thumb, the ancestor, is wrapped in cotton and tied to a feathered prayer arrow
a marakame, a shaman in his sixties in white cotton clothes embroidered with red and blue deer and a palm hat hung with red wool pompoms and turkey feathers, prays over the ancestor crystals with feathered wands
on the shrine floor lie offerings for the ancestors, small woven yarn disks, gourd bowls lined with colored beads, candles, and pieces of tortilla and corn
