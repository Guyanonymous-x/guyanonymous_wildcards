Styled in a design language of British traditional comfort with deep padded cushioning, tall sloping forms with ears, shaped polished walnut or mahogany timber with turned fronts, and short curving legs. Upholstery in floral chintz, tapestry weave, brocade, mustard velvet or green moquette, with buttoned details and fringed or piped seams. Cosy, grandmotherly and comfortable, with mechanics hidden under a skirted base, like a traditional lounge by a gas fire. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
