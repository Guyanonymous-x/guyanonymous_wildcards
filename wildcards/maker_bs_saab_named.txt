In the style of Saab, aeronautical, quirky, practical and eccentric Swedish design. Short, stubby, rounded forms with deeply curved glass-like surfaces, rectangular accents and a cockpit-like compactness. A round deep-blue enamel disc ringed in chrome carries a crowned red griffin head with white capital lettering, and is the signature mark. Palette is midnight blue, black, silver metallic, deep red and white. Finishes are satin black plastic edges, brushed alloy with a distinct pattern of round holes, grey or blue textured cloth and moulded rubbery trims. Lettering is plain, wide, evenly spaced sans-serif capitals. Asymmetric wraparound forms with big round faces give a functional, jet-inspired character. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Saab in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
