Styled in a design language of a one-bit black and white pixel surface treatment with no gradients. Shapes have thin crisp black outlines, small rounded corners and a hard offset shadow along the right and bottom edges. Grey is made only from fine dithered checkerboard and dotted patterns. Icons are chunky hand-drawn pixel pictograms with a cheerful smiling face motif, a pointing arrow and a wristwatch shape. Later versions add soft pastel greys, lilac and teal tints with subtle bevels. Charming, crisp, hand-crafted and playful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
