__bs_subject_heritage__ styled in a design language of white porcelain decorated in a crowded palette of underglaze cobalt blue, iron red and gold. Panels of chrysanthemums, peonies, pine, bamboo, cranes and phoenix are packed into fan, circle and brocade-pattern reserves that fill every part of the surface. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
