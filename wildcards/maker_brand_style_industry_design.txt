Styled in a design language of expressionist and early constructivist work in wood, metal, glass and textile. Forms are built from stacked cubes, slats and planes in primary red, yellow, blue, black and white, with bold geometric shapes such as circle, square and triangle. Woven abstracts are compositions of rectangles and stripes in saturated, earthy reds, blues, ochres and black. Rounded cylindrical forms use nickel or plain glaze. The mood is utopian, experimental, geometric and artisan. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.

Styled in a design language of everything built from straight horizontal and vertical lines and rectangles. The palette is limited to pure red, yellow and blue, with white, black and grey, set in flat colour blocks with thick black dividing lines. Objects are assembled from slim rails and flat panels that overlap and project at their ends, painted in contrasting colours with exposed ends. Spaces show asymmetrical balance and open planes. Lettering is blocky and constructed from squares. The mood is strict, rational, abstract and playful in its colour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
Styled in a design language of hand-painted surfaces with bold, loose, post-impressionist decoration. Brush marks are visible, with abstract blobs, stripes, spirals and stylised flowers or figures in strong colours, mustard, magenta, turquoise, orange, lilac, green and black. Compositions are irregular and asymmetrical with an unfinished hand-drawn quality. Underlying forms are plain and painted all over in dense patterns. Glazes are thick and opaque, with spots and swirls. The mood is cheerful, avant-garde, amateurish by design and bohemian. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
