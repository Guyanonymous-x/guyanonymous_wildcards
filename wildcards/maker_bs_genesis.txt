In the style of Genesis, Korean luxury cars with a big crest-shaped grille filled with a diamond mesh pattern, a winged badge, and two thin horizontal lines in the headlamps and tail lamps, giving a split twin-line lamp signature. Long bonnet, athletic stance, flowing parabolic line across the body side from front to rear, a low roof and flush doors. Wheels with a thin multispoke or mesh design. Interior of quilted nappa leather, metal knurled switches, a floating curved display, and an open pore wood trim. Colours of matte grey, Uyuni white, Savile silver, deep green and copper. Rich, elegant and sleek.
