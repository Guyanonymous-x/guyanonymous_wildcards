Styled in a design language of creamy tin-glazed white ground with vivid cobalt blue painted decoration, from pale wash to near-black. The glaze is shiny, slightly bluish-white and pooled in places. Grids of simple blue motifs repeat with matching corner motifs. The mood is crisp, domestic, nostalgic and traditionally Dutch. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
