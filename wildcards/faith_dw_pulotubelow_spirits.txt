the spirits of the dead, men and women in their forties to their eighties, gray-haired and bent, bathe in the pool of Vaiola and rise from it upright and strong, their hair black again and their skin smooth
spirits of chiefs in their fifties, their skin deep brown and tattooed, sit in a circle in the great house wrapped in fine mats, a wooden kava bowl with many legs in the center
the newly arrived dead, men and women in their thirties to their seventies in bark-cloth wraps, walk slowly along the sea floor toward the bright villages, their hair drifting in the water
