Styled in a design language of solid, dignified, classical and carefully made. Deep, glossy black polished forms with smooth curved sides, plain tall planes, rounded cheeks and thick tapered legs finished in brass. Warm walnut, mahogany or white lacquer with matte or satin sheen are alternatives. Ivory-white with ebony black contrast, and brass accents. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
