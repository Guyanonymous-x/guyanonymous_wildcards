The hooded figures wear long loose robes to the ground, some red, some black, with tall pointed hoods that leave only their faces showing.
The High Priest wears a robe of red and silver with a wide stiff collar, and the members watching from the benches wear summer jackets and flannel shirts.
Some hooded figures wear white robes with red linings, and the torchbearers wear plain black robes belted with rope.
