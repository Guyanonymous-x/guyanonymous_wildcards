The Senior Warden sits in a carved chair at the near end of the room behind a small wooden pedestal with a short gilded column standing on it, and the Junior Warden sits halfway along the right-hand wall behind a matching pedestal.
Two painted tracing boards lean on wooden easels near the altar, each a framed panel covered in symbols, a checkered floor, two pillars, a ladder rising into clouds and a star.
The officers wear wide blue collars hanging in a V down their chests, each ending in a small silver jewel, a square for the Master, a level for the Senior Warden and an upright plumb line for the Junior Warden.
Two deacons stand by the door holding tall black wands topped with small gilded doves, and a tyler, the doorkeeper, sits outside the door with a plain sword across his knees.
