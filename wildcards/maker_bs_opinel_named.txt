Styled in a design language of rustic French craft simplicity. Slim, tapered, teardrop-like forms in pale natural beech wood, olivewood and dark stained wood with a clear lacquer glow, joined to plain carbon steel that develops a soft grey patina. Small touches of colour in navy, olive, red, yellow and white lacquer. Kraft card, tin and cardboard packing in blue and cream, with simple fine rules. Honest, modest, countryside and timeless. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
