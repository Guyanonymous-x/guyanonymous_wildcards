the women, wives and grown daughters from their twenties to their eighties, wear long solid-colored dresses of dark blue, purple, green or brown to below the calf, each with a cape across the shoulders and a black apron pinned with straight pins, black stockings and shoes, and a white organdy prayer covering, a stiff heart-shaped cap over hair parted in the middle and pulled into a bun
the women in their twenties to seventies wear long dresses of plain dark cloth, black shawls around their shoulders and black bonnets with deep brims over their white prayer coverings, their faces framed by the bonnets
the women in their thirties to eighties wear long dark dresses with matching capes and aprons and small white mesh coverings tied under the chin with white strings, some Mennonite women in dresses printed with tiny dark flowers
