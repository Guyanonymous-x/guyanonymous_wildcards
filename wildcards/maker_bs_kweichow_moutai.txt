Styled in a design language of formal, festive, prosperous and traditional ceremonial design. A squat cylindrical white porcelain form with a short neck, glossy milk-white glaze and a slight shoulder curve, topped with a red ribbon tied over a seal and often a tiny hanging tag. A bright vermilion band wraps the middle, edged in gold, with large black or gold brush-style characters and a flowing line of small text. Special editions use red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Lacquer red or wood with yellow silk lining, embossed in gold and closed with a white cord or golden bow. Auspicious red, gold and white against dark polished tables. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
