In the style of Vitra, contemporary design furniture with sculptural moulded polypropylene and plastic shells in matte pastel and primary colours, thin steel rod legs or wooden dowel bases, and playful oversized forms like gnome-shaped stools and spidery floor lamps. Curvy lounge chairs with wrapped upholstery in felt, wool and leather, black ash and oak veneer, white lacquer. Reissued mid-century plywood and fibreglass classics sit beside deconstructed, geometric, exhibition-like pieces. Crisp Swiss and German gallery aesthetic, witty, colourful, clean shadow lines, studio product photography on seamless white backdrops.
