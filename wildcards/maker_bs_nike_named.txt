In the style of Nike, athletic motion and defiant confidence. Forms are streamlined and aerodynamic, with sweeping check-like curves, sharp tapered cuts and layered panels that suggest speed. Palette is stark black, white and grey with a single electric accent such as volt yellow, hot orange or crimson, and bold colour-blocked contrasts. Technical mesh, foam, rubber and glossy synthetic skins show engineered detail. Lettering is heavy, tightly set, italic or condensed sans-serif, often in huge scale. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Nike in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
