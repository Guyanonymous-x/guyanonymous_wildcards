Styled in a design language of quiet, northern, graphic and dignified design with a simple, bold and direct line. Simple powerful animals and spirit figures with big expressive eyes and feathers drawn as repeating curved shapes, printed in flat ink on thin pale cream or white handmade paper. The ink is deep black, with blue-grey, ochre, red-orange, green and teal appearing as flat blocks, with a soft grain from the stone and a subtle ridge around each shape, often with generous empty space around them. Sculpture is smooth, rounded, polished dark green-grey or black stone in heavy, compact forms of animals, figures and hybrid creatures. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
