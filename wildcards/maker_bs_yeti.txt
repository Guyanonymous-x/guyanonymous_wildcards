Styled in a design language of overbuilt, bombproof, American-outdoors design with thick, softly rounded rectangular forms, smooth heavy walls, chunky rubber trims, rope accents and tie-down slots. Double-walled stainless steel and rotomoulded plastic with durable powder-coated matte colour in tan, white, navy, charcoal, seafoam, desert clay, with accents of coral, teal and cream. Clean minimal surfaces, thick moulded edges, rugged, heavy-duty finish give the sense of something built to last for decades. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
