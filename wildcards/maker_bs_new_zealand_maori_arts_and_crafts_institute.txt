Styled in a design language of dense ancestral carving where every surface is covered in deep curved relief. Spirals, interlocked fern-frond curves and notched ridges flow continuously over bold stylised figures with large heads, wide inlaid shell eyes and protruding tongues. Dark polished brown wood is accented with red ochre, white and black, and woven lattice in diagonal patterns fills the spaces between carved elements. Powerful, sculptural, rhythmic and full of flowing curves. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
