Styled in a design language of sturdy German family-product design. Secondary accents in bright orange, green, yellow and sky blue. Trustworthy, orderly, cheerful, high-quality and European, with a polished, slightly nostalgic finish and generous clean margins around every printed block of colour. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
