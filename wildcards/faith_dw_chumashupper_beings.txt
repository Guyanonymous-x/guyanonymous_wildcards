Moon, a woman in her thirties in a skirt of shining white shells, sits beside the Sun's house, and Morning Star, a woman in her twenties with a crown of bright points, shines at the eastern edge
Sky Coyote stands in the sky world as a great coyote with fur the color of dry grass, and around him fly white eagles and the thunder beings in swirls of black and red
Slo'w, the great golden eagle, spreads his wings wide across the bottom of the sky world, holding it up on their dark brown feathers, his gold head turned toward the Sun
