Styled in a design language of edgy fast arcade-bright energy. Glossy black plastic with sharp angular wedges, circular recessed details, moulded ridges and shallow grooves. Cobalt blue is the signature. A later softer identity uses off-white and white surfaces with a swirling orange spiral emblem and light. Artwork is loud and saturated with bold outlines, hard-edged speed lines, white and orange accents and glowing red or green accent lights. Futuristic, quick, playful and confident. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
