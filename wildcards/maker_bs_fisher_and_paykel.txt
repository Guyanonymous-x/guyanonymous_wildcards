In the style of Fisher and Paykel, New Zealand domestic appliances. Washing machines and fridges are clean, boxy, rounded-corner white enamel cabinets, from the 1950s through the 1970s with a thin chrome strip, a simple round dial and a stylised metal badge. Later designs are bright white or stainless steel with a slim control panel, a smooth top, large soft-touch rounded dials, a gentle blue or green backlit display and neat vertical or horizontal handles of brushed steel. The signature is the double-drawer dishwasher, two slim stainless steel drawers stacked in a cabinet, with a recessed handle and a narrow screen. Ranges are plain stainless steel with big oven doors in black glass, chunky round knobs in silver and a plain, modern seamless profile. The mood is practical, quiet, friendly and design-led, in white, silver and grey with minimal ornament.
