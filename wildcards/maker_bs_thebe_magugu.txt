Styled in a design language of contemporary design that combines sharp tailoring with storytelling prints, with structured lines, pleats, crisp shirting and strong shoulders. Materials include wool, silk, crisp cotton and leather, with printed illustrations of portraits, plants, historical imagery and map-like graphics. Palette moves between deep forest green, burgundy, cobalt blue, mustard, cream, black and pastel pink, with precise seams, contrasting piping and nipped waists. Playful and intellectual, polished, artful and modern, in clean gallery light. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
