in a palace room of finely fitted stone, the mummy sits as women in their twenties to forties in long dresses pinned with silver pins set food and a cup of corn beer before him
Chuqui, a woman in her thirties, fans the dead king with a fan of feathers, keeping the flies off his face, while another, Cusi, in her twenties, changes his tunic for a fresh one
an attendant, Huaman, in his forties, the voice of the dead king, kneels by the mummy and speaks his wishes to Quispe, a visiting lord in his fifties, who bows before him
the room is hung with fine woven cloths and holds the king's gold and silver vessels, his weapons and his clothes, kept as they were in his life
