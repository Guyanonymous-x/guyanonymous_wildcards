In the style of Hongqi, majestic, ceremonial and traditional Chinese state grandeur. A huge waterfall of tall chrome vertical bars, a flag-shaped red emblem, long stately massing and a formal upright top line with rounded-square details. Early forms add palace-lantern style details, thin supports, white bands and wide chrome edging. Modern forms add a flowing long front with a central ridge and a slim full-width light line. Red wood, cream leather and silk-like fabrics. Palette of black, deep red, gold and ivory. Solemn, imperial and proud. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Hongqi in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
