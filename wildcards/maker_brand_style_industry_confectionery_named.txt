In the style of Cadbury, rich deep royal purple dominates every surface in glossy, foil-like finishes. A flowing gold script in thick looping cursive is the signature mark, carried on swooping ribbon swirls in white or gold. Cream and white accents, gold borders, ribbon bows, rounded friendly curves and soft chocolate swirl graphics add generosity. Forms are regular and softly moulded with the cursive mark embossed.
In the style of Toblerone, forms built from repeating triangular peaks like a row of mountains, with sawtooth silhouettes and sloping sides. Warm honey-gold and cream-yellow fields carry deep blue bands, brown and dark blue serif and rounded sans lettering, and white snow-capped highlights. A mountain silhouette emblem hides a secret figure in its negative space. Silver sheen, light embossing and clean pyramid repetition give a tidy, geometric rhythm.
In the style of Kinder, cheerful, safe, child-friendly design in clean white and bright red with accents of blue and warm yellow. A smiling cartoon child face is the mascot, framed in a red halo. Lettering is bold, red and rounded. Stripes, milk-splash shapes in white and blue, glossy red-and-white sheen and soft egg-like rounded forms keep everything playful and tidy. Small surprises of bright colour are tucked inside plain surroundings.
In the style of Hershey, plain, sturdy, warm American everyday design in a monochrome deep chocolate-brown field with silver or white lettering. Bold condensed sans-serif capitals run horizontally. Silvery metallic accents, flat segmented rectangles with raised square grids, and teardrop forms with a flat base and a small flag-like plume. Occasional red or gold accents mark seasonal variations. Minimal decoration, solid and dependable, with a nostalgic, unfussy honesty.
In the style of Haribo, bright, cheerful primary-colour German retro fun. Glossy translucent jewel-coloured shapes in red, green, yellow, orange and white show a sugar-dusted or polished glaze. A bright yellow-gold field carries bold, rounded lettering in red with an outline, plus cartoon children holding hands in a ring or a golden mascot figure. Shapes are small, soft, chunky and animated.
In the style of Pocky (Glico), slim, long, elegant forms with one end left bare, narrow and rhythmic. Saturated single-colour fields in red, pink and other flavour hues, each with a glossy lamination. Bold rounded lettering mixes Japanese and Latin characters. Palette is cheerful pastel and primary, with small flavour icons.
In the style of Meiji, tidy, friendly, nostalgic Japanese convenience design in red, chocolate brown, cream and white. Bright red fields with a white script mark, silvery metallic accents, and clean cream panels carry a small simple illustration. Charming shapes pair a narrow stem with a rounded dark cap.
In the style of Laduree, dainty, sugary Second Empire Parisian tearoom elegance. Pastel colours in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow and coffee brown, arranged in neat stacked pyramids. Pale mint or pink surfaces with gold embossed script, gilt edges and satin ribbon. Powder-blue and pistachio paintwork, gilded mouldings, painted ceilings of cherubs and marble. Cursive gold lettering, a delicate oval crest and floral flourishes. Palette is pistachio, rose, cream and gold.
In the style of Sprungli, genteel, understated Swiss confectionery craft, delicate and refined. Soft pastel surfaces in turquoise-blue, silver and cream, with small items nestled in pleated paper. Wordmark in a clean, thin, spaced serif in silver or white on pale aqua, with fine linear borders. Palette is pale aqua, silver, cream and chocolate brown.
In the style of Parle-G, bright, nostalgic, wholesome mass-market Indian design on a bold sunshine yellow field. A cartoon smiling toddler girl with dark hair and a round face sits in an oval frame, edged in red and white. Lettering is bold red and white sans-serif, combining Indian and Latin scripts. Palette is sunshine yellow, red, white and black.
In the style of Havanna Alfajores, friendly, sweet, nostalgic seaside design in a trustworthy clean cream and blue palette. Deep turquoise-blue or royal blue and white schemes with warm cream, a large rounded white script mark like a flowing signature over navy, slim gold or silver details and a small orange rising-sun mark. Soft rounded rectangular shapes, shiny foil, tin and maritime references such as a seaside town and small gulls. Round, glossy dark chocolate or white icing over soft pale layers. Warm wood-and-brass spaces, with dark wood, tall windows, beige marble and lamps with amber light.
In the style of Arnotts Biscuits, homely, nostalgic, cheerful family warmth. A mark in old engraving style, a brightly coloured bird in red, yellow, green and blue inside a red oval or circle. Shapes are distinctive and patterned, with fine diagonal ridges, neat grids of small holes, simple rounded characters and frosted rounds.
In the style of Chupa Chups, playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop. Simple round shapes and clean sticks of form, with a glossy, candy-like shine and thin crinkled shimmer. The mark is a bright daisy-like flower with a yellow circle in the middle and red petals, with the name in friendly rounded white and yellow letters, on bright red, yellow or orange. Flavours are signalled by colour, pink, orange, green, cola brown and lemon yellow, some swirled with two colours. Hot-pink or red fields radiate outward in a sunburst. Cartoon children and sugary cheerfulness.
