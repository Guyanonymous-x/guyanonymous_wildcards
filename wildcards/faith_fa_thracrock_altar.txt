at its highest point a round altar cut from the rock itself, a low flat-topped drum as wide as a threshing floor with a shallow rim, its top blackened by fire
a square basin sunk into the rock beside the altar with a narrow channel leading from its lip over the cliff edge, the stone around it stained dark red-brown with wine
a flight of steep narrow steps hewn straight up the rock face to the altar, each step worn hollow at its middle, with hand holds cut beside the upper ones
small clay cups, lumps of bread, honeycomb and bunches of grapes set in the cut niches as gifts, a bronze cauldron on three legs standing on the rock beside them
