In the style of Da Gama Textiles, South African shweshwe printed cotton. The fabric is a stiff starched cotton with a papery finish, ninety centimetres wide, printed with all-over repeating patterns that look like an intricate engraving. The classic colour is indigo blue with white discharged motifs, alongside chocolate brown, deep red, plum, teal and mustard. Motifs are small geometric diamonds, squares, circles, stripes, flowers, paisleys and tiny dotted trellis designs, finely detailed from the pinned engraving technique and very regular. The reverse is a flat solid colour and bears small printed brand marks of three cats. Garments include full A-line skirts, wrap dresses, aprons, headwraps and wedding outfits with panels sewn side by side, edged in braid and ribbon. Mood is traditional, cheerful, communal, crisp and deeply colourful. The cloth holds sharp creases when ironed, shows a faint white chalky bloom along folds, and is often seen in village yards, hanging on lines in bright sun.
