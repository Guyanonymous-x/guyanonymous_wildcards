Styled in a design language of rugged, minimal Scandinavian northern design. Materials are dense waxed cotton-poly canvas, sturdy nylon and soft wool in earthy tones of khaki, olive, forest green, navy, charcoal, sand and mustard, with occasional red, orange and pale blue accents. Shapes are simple geometric blocks with straight seams, bold colour blocking and contrast seam lines. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
