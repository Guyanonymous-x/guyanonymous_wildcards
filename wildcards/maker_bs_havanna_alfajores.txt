Styled in a design language of friendly, sweet, nostalgic seaside design in a trustworthy clean cream and blue palette. Soft rounded rectangular shapes, shiny foil, tin and maritime references such as a seaside town and small gulls. Round, glossy dark chocolate or white icing over soft pale layers. Warm wood-and-brass spaces, with dark wood, tall windows, beige marble and lamps with amber light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
