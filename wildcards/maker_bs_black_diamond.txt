Styled in a design language of precise, high-contrast mountain-gear design with exact engineered shapes. Anodised aluminium parts in vivid red, blue, orange, lime, purple and silver are machined with rounded forms, sharp stamped marks and small lightening cutouts. Matte black fabrics and chunky black housings carry bright accents, with glossy neon shells, vents and textured grips. Woven geometric patterns in bright zigzag and diamond repeats appear on cord and webbing. Packaging is clean white with strong black type. Technical, industrial, bold and exact. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
