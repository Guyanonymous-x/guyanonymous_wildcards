Styled in a design language of tropical colonial grace, airy, cool and symmetrical. White masonry with long arcades, Renaissance-style facade, tall arched openings, black-and-white tiles, bright green lawns and tall palms. Wide verandas with louvred shutters, slow fans, rattan, dark teak and polished brass. Patterned heritage tile floors and white linen. Palette is white, deep green, teak brown and brass. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
