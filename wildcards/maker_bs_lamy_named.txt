Styled in a design language of rigorous German modernist industrial design. Simple geometric solids, smooth cylinders, flat planes and strict uncluttered proportions with a single slim flat bar as an accent. Matte, fine-grained black, charcoal, white and satin brushed stainless steel, with bright saturated tones such as red, yellow, blue, lime and orange in plain solid blocks. Faceted surfaces, small round windows and subtle recessed details are the only ornament. Packaging is plain white or black card with sparse type and generous empty space. Functional, honest, disciplined, school-like and modern. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
