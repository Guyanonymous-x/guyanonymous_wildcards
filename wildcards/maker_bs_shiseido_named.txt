In the style of Shiseido, luxurious, feminine, carefully composed Taisho and early Showa elegance where Art Nouveau curves meet Art Deco geometry. Slim, faceted glass-like forms with domed tops, gilt metal accents, and lacquer red or ivory surfaces tied with ribbon. Graphics use a stylised camellia-like flower mark in white or gold on red, thin hand-drawn lettering in an Art Nouveau block script, sinuous floral borders and flat-colour illustrations of slender women with swept-up hair, in muted rose, jade green, indigo and warm cream. Gold foil edging, delicate line work, soft monochrome theatrical photography, and generous empty margins around small precise motifs. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
