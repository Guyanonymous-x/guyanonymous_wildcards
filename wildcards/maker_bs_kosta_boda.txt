Styled in a design language of Swedish contemporary art glass, bold, colourful and hand-blown, with chunky abstract forms, bright stripes, spots and swirled colour layers, and cheerful faces, birds, fish and clowns. Thick transparent bodies in saturated magenta, cobalt, orange, emerald and cool teal, glossy smooth surfaces and organic asymmetrical silhouettes. Joyful, playful and sculptural, with light refracting through thick glass. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
