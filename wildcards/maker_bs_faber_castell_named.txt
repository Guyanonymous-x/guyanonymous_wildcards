Styled in a design language of scholarly European heritage in deep green and gold. Surfaces feature fine hot-foil stamping, subtle embossing, rows of graded colour in neat ordered fans and polished wood, knurled metal and dark leather for luxury lines. Classic, craftsmanlike, dignified and warm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
