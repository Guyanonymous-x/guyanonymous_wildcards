Styled in a design language of bright, cheerful Brazilian pop design built from thin, lightweight, slightly squishy rubber forms. A rice-grain pattern of tiny raised ridges textures flat surfaces, matte to softly glossy. Colour is juicy and saturated, with solid yellow, lime, hot pink, turquoise, red, navy, orange and white, often two-tone with a contrasting accent and flag-like stripes. Graphic prints are exuberant tropical collages of fruit, leaves, feathers and playful faces in clashing neon, orange, teal and magenta with bold outlines. Fun, summery and tropical. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
