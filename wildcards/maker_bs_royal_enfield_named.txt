Styled in a design language of honest, rugged, vintage British-Indian character. Colours are gloss black, deep green, maroon, cream and military olive with gold pinstripes, plus enamel red and yellow accents on winged metal plates. Surfaces mix brushed brass, nickel chrome, waxed canvas, aged leather and hand-painted lines, with chunky rivets, softly worn edges and sturdy rounded teardrop shapes. Unfussy, durable and gently nostalgic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
