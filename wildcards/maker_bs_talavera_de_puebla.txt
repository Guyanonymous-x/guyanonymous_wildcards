In the style of Talavera de Puebla, Mexican tin-glazed earthenware with a thick, creamy white glossy glaze painted by hand with cobalt blue, yellow, green, orange and black motifs of flowers, birds, vines, geometric borders and Moorish-inspired arabesques. Plates, tiles, vases, jugs and sinks with slightly uneven edges and drips, bold brushwork, rustic, festive, sunlit courtyard and hacienda kitchen, joyful colour.
