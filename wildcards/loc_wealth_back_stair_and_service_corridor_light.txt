The darkest circulation in the building and the one people were most often hurt in, one small window on one half-landing out of three, so two half-landings and most of four flights have no daylight at all
Three of them painted over, each passing a little second-hand light from a corridor that is itself borrowing
Four tin candle sconces, which means the stair was climbed by candlelight carried in the same hand as the rail or set down at a half-landing and collected on the way back
The gas and then electric fittings arriving late and sparsely, at one per flight; and the characteristic condition of this space, knowing the stair by the feel of the nosings, which is how everybody who used it described it
