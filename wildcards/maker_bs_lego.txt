Styled in a design language of glossy hard plastic in saturated primary red, blue, yellow and white plus black, grey and bright lime and orange. Everything is a rectangular modular volume with a regular grid of round studs on top, crisp moulded edges and visible seams, so any subject reads as assembled from simple bricks. Characters have cylindrical yellow heads with dot eyes and a simple smile, claw hands and blocky bodies. Packaging and graphics use flat bright colour fields, small clean line diagrams and dynamic action imagery. Cardboard and kraft textures appear in newer eco touches. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
