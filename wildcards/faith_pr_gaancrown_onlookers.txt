Apache women and men in their twenties to eighties sit and stand in a wide ring around the fire, the women in long camp dresses of bright satin with ribbon trim and shawls, the men in cowboy hats and denim jackets

older women in their sixties and seventies in long cotton camp dresses and dark shawls sit on blankets in the front row, one throwing a pinch of yellow cattail pollen toward the dancers
