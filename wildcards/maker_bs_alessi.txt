In the style of Alessi, Italian postmodern kitchenware in polished stainless steel with playful anthropomorphic forms, such as kettles with bird-shaped whistles, citrus squeezers with spidery tripod legs, and cheerful little figures that hold corkscrews. Bright resin and plastic accents in red, yellow, pink, blue and green against mirror-polished steel, rounded cones, spheres and cylinders, cute faces and exaggerated spouts. Witty, toy-like and sculptural, domestic objects as pop-art ornaments, cheerful kitchen shelf photography with soft shadows.
