Styled in a design language of refined, finely detailed, understated and intellectual with a gentle sporty edge. A narrow shield-shaped chrome front, a small flag mark bearing a lance, tidy proportions, delicate supports, generous glazing, thin chrome trim along the flanks and a slightly tapered end. Rally work has boxy flared arches, white or grey accents and wide blue and red stripes. Pale grey, midnight blue, cream, ivory and rich burgundy. Wool or velour and wood veneer. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
