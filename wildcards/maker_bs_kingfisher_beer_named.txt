Styled in a design language of social, festive, glamorous hot-afternoon design with a bird emblem. Cream or white ground with a bold red band and a gold rim. Slender brown glass and glossy cream and red surfaces are accented with gold rings. Warm red, gold, cream and a flash of electric blue, poolside and lively. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
