the cave of the dead at Chalcatongo, a great dark opening in a mountainside above the town, the bodies of Mixtec kings wrapped in rich cloth bundles laid in rows inside it
the priestess of the dead, a woman in her sixties, Lady 9 Grass, a skull painted over her face and a skirt of woven cloth, sits at the mouth of the cave as the lords come to ask her counsel
lords in their thirties to sixties in feathered headdresses and jade ear spools carry the wrapped bundle of a dead king in his fifties up the path to the cave
