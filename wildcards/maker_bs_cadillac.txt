Styled in a design language of American luxury that mixes jet-age glamour with a sharp modern edge. Forms are long, low and wide with crisp vertical blades, razor creases and polished chrome edging. The emblem is a shield crest with a laurel wreath, shown as a jewelled multi-colour badge in retro work and as a flat monochrome black or silver outline in current work. Palette is pearl white, midnight black, champagne gold, deep ruby red and powder pink, with mirror-bright metal throughout. Lettering is wide, airy, widely spaced capitals or a flowing cursive. Materials include tufted leather, glossy lacquer, cut glass and stitched suede. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
