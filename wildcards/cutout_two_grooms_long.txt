life-size cardboard cutouts of two grooms, standing side by side. The two figures are printed on one wide flat sheet of corrugated cardboard, cut out around their outlines together with a thin white border at the edge. One printed groom wears a gray suit with a white shirt and a silver tie, the other a navy blue suit with a white shirt and a pale blue tie, and each has a white rose pinned to his lapel. They hold hands between them and smile out at the viewer, and a printed banner at their feet reads "JUST MARRIED" in silver script. At the cut edge the brown wavy layers of the cardboard show, and two folded cardboard struts at the back prop it upright on the floor. __cutout_two_grooms_var__.
