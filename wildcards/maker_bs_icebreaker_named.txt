In the style of Icebreaker, close-fitting fine soft merino knit, smooth, matte and slightly fuzzy, with flat seams and mountain-inspired minimalism. Colours are muted forest green, charcoal, deep navy, heather grey, rust, moss, stone and pale blue with occasional bright orange. Graphic touches include small sheep-like or mountain-line prints, subtle stripes and tiny sewn marks. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
