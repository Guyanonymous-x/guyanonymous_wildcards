Styled in a design language of cheerful, bouncy, golden-age arcade charm of the early 1980s. Characters are simple round shapes with big expressive eyes, drawn in a chunky low-resolution pixel grid on pure black. A yellow circle with a wedge mouth chases glowing dots through a neon blue outlined maze of rounded corridors, pursued by pastel ghosts in red, pink, cyan and orange with wavy skirts and blinking white eyes. Flat bright pixel-art fruit. Symmetrical insect-like shapes in blue, purple and yellow move in formation against single twinkling stars. Cartoon side art, a slanted black glowing logo, scanlines, limited saturated colours and hard pixel edges.
