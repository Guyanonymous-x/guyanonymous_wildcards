In the style of Telefunken, German tube radios and televisions of the 1950s and 1960s. Warm polished walnut or beech cabinets with rounded fronts, large illuminated station scales in glass with green and orange glowing eyes, and slim ivory keys across the bottom edge. Gold fabric speaker grilles with ornamental slots, chrome-plated dial trim, and big cream knobs. A diamond-shaped logo sits at the front. Mood is warm, glowing, heavy and domestic.
