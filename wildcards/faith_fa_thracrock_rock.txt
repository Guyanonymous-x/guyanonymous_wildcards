the sanctuary of Perperikon, a whole rocky hilltop of pale gray stone cut into rooms, stairways, thresholds and a long open hall with a carved stone seat, low walls of fitted blocks standing on the natural rock
the sanctuary of Tatul, a lone outcrop of pale volcanic rock shaped by hand into a blunt pyramid, a rectangular trough the size of a bed cut into its flat top and a deep arched niche hollowed in its south side
the sanctuary of Belintash, a long narrow table of bare gray rock high above forested ridges, its level top pocked with dozens of round cut pits linked by thin grooves, two stairways hewn up its side
the sanctuary of Gluhite Kamani, a cluster of tall gray-white crags among oak woods, their sheer faces cut with rows of small flat-backed niches in the shape of a doorway narrowing toward its top, dozens on every rock
