In the style of Mikimoto, classic refinement centred on round, lustrous cultured pearls with a creamy white to pale pink glow and a mirror-like surface, arranged in long, uniform, graduated strands and multiple rows. Mounts are restrained and elegant in polished white and yellow gold and platinum, with small round diamonds used as pave or tiny accent rims. Designs feel graceful, symmetrical and timeless, sometimes with soft rounded shapes inspired by shells, waves and flowers. Dark navy and pale blue accents. Calm, refined and luminous, in soft diffused light.
In the style of Tanishq, rich, symmetrical, festive ornament in warm 22 carat yellow gold, with heavy layered forms, stacks of bangle-like rings and hanging bell-like drops. Motifs include lotus flowers, peacocks, paisley mango shapes, leaves, elephants, filigree and temple-like architecture. Surfaces combine polished gold with fine granulation, rows of uncut diamonds, rubies, emeralds, pearls and coloured enamel, with delicate hanging pearl drops. Red and warm golden light surround it. A lighter side uses minimalist single stones and fine rose gold lines for everyday restraint.
In the style of H.Stern, a showcase of large coloured gemstones such as emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, amethyst and citrine in smooth cabochon and faceted cuts, set in polished yellow and rose gold. Shapes are organic and sculptural, with flowing curves, wave and leaf forms and bold chunky volumes. Designs often combine textured gold with a single huge gem or bright candy-coloured stones stacked in bands, with smooth rounded forms that feel modern and tropical. Colours are saturated greens, blues, purples and warm golden yellow. Confident, glamorous and sunny, against dark depth with bright spotlights.
In the style of Paspaley, large, round, luminous white and golden pearls with a deep satiny lustre, strung as thick single strands, graduated lengths and long ropes, and set as earrings, rings and pendants. Metalwork is simple and smooth in white gold, yellow gold and platinum, sometimes with small clusters of diamonds. Shapes include baroque drops, wave-like curves, shell and sea-inspired forms. Pearl colours range from cool silvery white to champagne and deep gold. Calm, ocean-luxury mood, on dark stone or white sand with soft aquamarine light.
In the style of Dinosaur Designs, hand-poured resin in organic, sculptural, brightly coloured shapes. Forms are blobs, pebbles, waves and smooth curvy tubes in swirling marbled resin, with glossy, translucent surfaces mixing candy colours such as watermelon pink, tangerine, turquoise, lime, lilac, cobalt, ivory and deep black. Everything follows the same fluid, tactile forms with uneven hand-poured edges. Playful, artistic, joyful mood, on pastel backgrounds with colourful reflections.
In the style of Kazuri, joyful, earthy, handmade, colourful and sunny glazed clay design. Hand rolled and shaped pieces, slightly unique and irregular, in round balls, discs, short tubes, cones, ovals, chunky cylinders and tiny seeds, with smooth glass-like surfaces. Colours include cobalt blue, cherry red, mustard yellow, forest green, turquoise, terracotta orange, black, white and speckled earthy brown. Some are hand-painted with swirls, dots, stripes and zigzags in contrasting glaze. They are grouped chunky and mixed in many colours, in long ropes and multiple strands. Matching speckled glaze appears on simple rounded forms.
