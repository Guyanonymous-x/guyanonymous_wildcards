In the style of Air Astana, a Kazakh airline identity of dark blue and gold. Aircraft have a white fuselage with a deep navy belly and a rich blue tail fin carrying a golden stylised ornament, which resembles a pair of wings or a nomadic motif, and gold lettering on the forward body in a clean wide sans serif. Special liveries wear a snow leopard stretched over the fuselage in grey spots, with white and blue snowy rock. Cabins have navy seats with a pattern of ornamental motifs in gold thread, grey carpets, and warm wood trims. Crew uniforms are smart navy with gold buttons and a silk scarf with a traditional pattern. Mood is steppe-wide, orderly, formal and cool, using navy, gold and white with distant mountains. Printed menus and boarding passes echo the same navy field with a thin gold ornamental border, and lounge interiors repeat the pattern as carved screens and rugs.
