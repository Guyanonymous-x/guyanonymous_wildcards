Zhang Jue, the Great Teacher, stands on a hill under the yellow sky, a lean man in his fifties with a long gray beard, in a long yellow robe and a yellow headcloth, a bamboo staff with nine knots in his hand
Zhang Jue heals the sick, a man in his fifties in a yellow robe holding out a bowl of water in which a burned paper charm floats, Li Wei, a sick man in his forties, kneeling to drink from it
Zhang Jue stands between his two brothers, Zhang Bao and Zhang Liang, men in their forties in yellow robes and armor, the three raising their staffs to the rising yellow heaven
