Styled in a design language of upright, dignified and solid formal engineering from the late analogue era. Forms are tall, squared and flat-sided with sharp creases, generous glazing on thin supports, and chunky edges with black rubber strips. A bright three-pointed star in a ring is the single recurring mark, set against wide polished surrounds and thin vertical ribbing. Palette is champagne, ivory, signal red, midnight blue, silver grey and black, with bright chrome catching light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
