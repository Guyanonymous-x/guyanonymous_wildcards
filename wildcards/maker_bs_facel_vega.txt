In the style of Facel Vega in the 1950s and 1960s, a French grand tourer with a long bonnet, a wide egg-crate grille set low in the nose, stacked headlamps in vertical pairs, and a wraparound glass windscreen with thin pillars. Large chrome bumpers, tail fins, a flat boot, and chrome side strips. Wheels are wire spokes with knock-off hubs. Paint in black, silver, bright red, cream and deep blue. Interior has leather seats, a dash filled with white-faced dials and toggle switches in polished metal, a wooden wheel with a wide rim, and polished metal trim. Mood is rich, glamorous and jet-age.
