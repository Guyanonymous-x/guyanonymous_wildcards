In the style of Havaianas, bright, cheerful Brazilian pop design built from thin, lightweight, slightly squishy rubber forms. A rice-grain pattern of tiny raised ridges textures flat surfaces, matte to softly glossy. Colour is juicy and saturated, with solid yellow, lime, hot pink, turquoise, red, navy, orange and white, often two-tone with a contrasting accent and flag-like stripes. Lettering is a rounded lowercase wordmark in white on red or red on white, with a small Brazilian flag mark. Graphic prints are exuberant tropical collages of fruit, leaves, feathers and playful faces in clashing neon, orange, teal and magenta with bold outlines. Fun, summery and tropical. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Havaianas in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
