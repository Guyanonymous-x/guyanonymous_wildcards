In the style of Apple, minimalist, seamless, premium and calm. Thin slab forms with uniformly rounded corners and continuous tight radii, in milled or brushed aluminium finishes of silver, space grey, gold and soft pastel anodised colour, with edge-to-edge glass and thin black borders. Early work favours glossy white translucent plastic. Fixings are hidden, controls are few and precisely cut, and repeated circular elements are grouped neatly. A small polished silhouette mark sits centred. Type is clean sans-serif with generous white space, soft translucent blur layers and glossy rounded glyphs. Overall sparse, white and quietly confident.
