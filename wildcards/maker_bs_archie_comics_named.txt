In the style of Archie Comics, wholesome teenage newsstand comic look with clean, uniform thin black outlines, simple friendly characters with round heads, dot eyes, button noses, bright wide grins and neat hairstyles. Flat saturated four-colour printing with halftone dots on slightly yellowed pulp paper in red, yellow, blue and green, sock-hop and soda-shop settings, speech balloons in a hand-lettered rounded face and a cheerful clean-cut mid-century small-town mood.
