Styled in a design language of lightweight, clingy, floaty silk jersey and chiffon printed with swirling kaleidoscopic psychedelic patterns of curving lines, arcs, petals and mosaic-like tiles in vivid aquamarine, hot pink, lime, violet, orange and turquoise. Fluid, breezy and sun-drenched. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
