a skeleton marionette of white wooden bones, about as tall as an adult's arm. Every bone is carved separately from wood and painted bright white, a round skull with deep black eye sockets and a grinning jaw on a hinge, a ribcage of curved slats, a spine of linked beads, and long arms and legs of jointed bones held together only by short loops of string. Its strings run up to the control bar so the puppeteer can pull the joints apart, the skull floats off the neck, the arms drift away from the shoulders, and the bones hang apart in the air before snapping back together. Under ultraviolet light the white bones glow a bright blue-white against the black stage. __puppet_skeleton_marionette_var__.
