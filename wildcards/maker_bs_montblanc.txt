Styled in a design language of massive cigar-shaped forms in deeply polished black precious resin with gold-plated rings and large two-tone gold accents. A white six-pointed star shaped like a snow-capped peak crowns the form, rounded and domed. Surfaces are glossy and deep black, with thin gold rings and fine engraved numbers. Special versions use striped, marbled or burgundy resin. Black leather with a gold emblem follows the same dark and gold luxury. The mood is stately, confident, executive and understated. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
