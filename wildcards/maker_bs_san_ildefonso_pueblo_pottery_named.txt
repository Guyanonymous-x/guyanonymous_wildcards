Styled in a design language of sleek, dark, formal, sculptural and timeless. Perfectly symmetrical, hand-built forms with a full rounded volume and a crisp change of angle where the curve turns. Designs appear in matte black against the shining black field, visible only as light shifts across them. Motifs are bold and graphic, such as coiled serpents, rain clouds, stepped shapes, lightning zigzags, mountains and small tracks, arranged in one confident band. Perfect balance, calm and ceremonial. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
