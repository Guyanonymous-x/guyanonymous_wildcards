In the style of Chanel, restrained Parisian elegance built on black and white. Strict monochrome of black, ivory and cream with gold, pearls and chains as the only sparkle, and a quiet touch of beige or pale pink. Forms are simple, boxy, balanced and tailored, with quilted diamond surfaces, braided trim, tweed boucle texture and interlocked double-C style marks. Lettering is thin, spaced, upright sans-serif or clean classic capitals. Nothing fussy, minimal ornament chosen with precision, luxurious soft materials and crisp craft. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
