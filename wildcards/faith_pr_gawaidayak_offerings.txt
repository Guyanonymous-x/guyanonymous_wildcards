on woven mats sit the piring, the offerings for the gods, small brass plates of sticky rice cooked in bamboo, rice cakes, boiled eggs, betel nuts and tobacco rolled in leaves
the offerings stand on a raised stand of split bamboo decorated with shredded palm leaves, small woven rattan baskets of yellow rice, puffed rice and eggs, and cups of rice wine
tall brown glazed jars of tuak rice wine, molded with coiling dragons, stand in a row along the wall beside plates of rice cakes wrapped in green leaves and bowls of puffed rice
