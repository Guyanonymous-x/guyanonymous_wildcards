Styled in a design language of fine hard-paste porcelain, bright white, translucent and highly glossy, hand-painted and gilded in very fine detail. Decoration is a Chinese-inspired bouquet of flowering branches with fluttering butterflies in soft pastel tints, with lattice-work borders, pierced openwork basket-weave edges and a thin gold rim. Other schemes use green-and-gold scale, pink-and-red bamboo, rose garlands and deep cobalt panels. Modelled animals are covered in a dense net of tiny fishscale pattern in rust red, turquoise, lilac, green or gold. Handles twist like branches and knobs are modelled like fruits or flowers. Refined, elaborate, delicate and old-world aristocratic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
