In the style of Kahala, a Hawaiian aloha shirt house. The shirts are short-sleeved with an open camp collar, straight hem, coconut-shell buttons and a chest pocket in which the pattern continues without a break, made from rayon, silk-like cotton or soft poplin. The prints are large and detailed, in vintage-poster fashion, with hibiscus, plumeria, bird of paradise, palm trees, monstera, bamboo, tiki masks, outrigger canoes, volcano views, sunset beaches, hula dancers, tapa-like geometric borders and quilt patterns. Colours are rich and warm, including turquoise, coral, teal, deep orange, mustard, palm green, navy and cream, often with a shaded or watercolour quality and a dark background for tropical flowers. Some designs reproduce mid-century lei-and-surf scenes in two or three tones. The overall look is relaxed, sunny and nostalgic, Waikiki in the 1950s, bright but warm, with a hand-drawn illustration feel.
