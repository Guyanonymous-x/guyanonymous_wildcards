Styled in a design language of nostalgic, glamorous, carefully crafted Art Deco and Belle Epoque decoration. Lavishly patterned lithographic prints of peacock feathers, ferns, roses, swallows, geometric fans, arcs and rays. Colours are rich and layered, teal and gold, burgundy and cream, coral and navy, emerald and gold, with metallic foil stamping, fine coloured borders and a central oval or square frame with ornate lettering in elegant serif and decorative capitals, and a tiny crest or ribbon. Heavy, smooth, pale creamy forms embossed with a crest. Rounded vintage glass. Colourful rows against warm wood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
