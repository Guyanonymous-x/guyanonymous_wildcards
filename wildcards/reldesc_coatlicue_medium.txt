Coatlicue, the earth mother, a towering stone goddess with a head of two facing serpents and clawed hands and feet, wearing a skirt of woven writhing serpents and a necklace of carved shells, the two serpent heads facing each other where a head would be, their forked tongues touching, the skirt carved as a mass of woven snakes, the clawed hands and feet sharp, the necklace of hands and hearts carved in grey stone, the whole figure towering over the dim hall, torchlight glancing off its surfaces
