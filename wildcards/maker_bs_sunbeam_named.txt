Styled in a design language of British cars and motorcycles with elegant lines, such as tidy open two-seaters with wire wheels, chrome windscreen frames, small round lamps, a sharp radiator grille and glossy paint in blue, cream and racing green. Racing heritage with tidy leather interiors. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
