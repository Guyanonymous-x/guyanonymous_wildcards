Chinese shadow puppets, flat jointed figures cut from thin translucent donkey hide, each about as long as an adult's forearm. The hide is scraped so thin that light glows through it, and each figure is dyed in clear bright colors, red, green, yellow and black, so the shadows on the screen are colored. Each figure is cut in fine detail, a general with a long beard and a tall plumed helmet, a lady in flowing sleeves and a scholar in a long robe. __puppet_chinese_shadow_var__.
