The onesie is a flat-printed illusion, the outfit appears on a plain stretch jersey suit with no real collars, belts or pockets.
It is a trompe-l'oeil onesie, a plain one-piece suit pretending to be the outfit through printing alone.
The print runs continuously across the front, back, sleeves, legs and hood without a break at the seams.
Printed shadows and fold lines suggest the garments, but the suit itself is smooth, flat and uniform in material.
