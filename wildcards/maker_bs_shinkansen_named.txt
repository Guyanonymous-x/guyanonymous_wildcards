Styled in a design language of sleek, precise, futuristic Japanese engineering expressed through clean colour blocking. Glossy pearl white and pale grey surfaces are cut by a single long, tapering stripe in deep cobalt or navy, sometimes joined by a thin line of sky blue, green, red or gold. Shapes are streamlined, with long smooth curves, softly pointed tips and seamless panel joins that feel aerodynamic. Finishes are lacquer-like, satin and flawless, with pale aluminium accents. Small circular and wing-like emblems appear in crisp flat vector form, with a feeling of speed, order, safety and quiet confidence. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
