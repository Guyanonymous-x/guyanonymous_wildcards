Styled in a design language of functional, friendly, clean and instantly recognisable, with a single hot orange accent. Bright orange moulded plastic in smooth, open, rounded loops, paired with silver stainless steel with a satin finish, joined by flat black or orange pivots. Grips are ergonomic, with soft thumb curves and a slightly textured inner surface, black contrast parts, clean tapers and a sleek shape ending in razor-bright metal. Orange and black used together. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
