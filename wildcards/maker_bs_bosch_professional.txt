Styled in a design language of robust, ergonomic, cool and professional design for any product. Hard bodies in a deep, distinctive blue plastic with black rubberised soft areas and bold red accents. Compact, chunky shapes with curved ergonomic handles, textured diamond-pattern grips and bright silver metal details. The blue and black pairing repeats everywhere, with tough blue-grey moulded surfaces, rugged carry cases with silver latches and a calm, dependable, no-nonsense feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
