In the style of Bauhaus workshops, German functional modernism built from primary geometry, circles, squares and triangles, using tubular steel, chromed frames, black leather straps, flat plywood, bent wood and white-painted surfaces. Palette of primary red, yellow and blue with black, white and grey. Slim cantilevered lines, nickel, opal glass spheres, abstract woven grids and lowercase sans-serif lettering. Rational, austere and factory-ready elegance. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Bauhaus workshops in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
