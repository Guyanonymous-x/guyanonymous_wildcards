At the head of a small dark lake among giant redwoods stands the Owl Shrine, a huge hollow concrete owl towering over the robed men at its feet, its rough gray concrete body blotched with green moss and gray lichen, shaped in rough knobs and ledges of natural rock. A small wooden boat glides across the lake toward the owl, poled by a ferryman in a long dark robe, and on the boat lies the effigy of "Dull Care", a figure wrapped in pale cloth, while rows of hooded figures in long robes wait on the shore holding burning torches. At the owl's feet stands a stone altar, where the hooded figures lay the wrapped effigy and set it on fire, its flames rising toward the owl, and fireworks burst over the lake while hundreds of club members watch from rows of benches across the water.
