In the style of SpaceX, minimalist, gleaming, modern reusable spaceflight. Smooth white cylinders with clean bold finishes, or polished bare stainless steel, with slender black accents. Lower areas are sooty and streaked with carbon from fiery returns. Rounded tips, slim grid shapes and minimal bold sans-serif lettering. Few marks, no ornament, only function. Sleek, futuristic, confident, utilitarian and quietly daring. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
