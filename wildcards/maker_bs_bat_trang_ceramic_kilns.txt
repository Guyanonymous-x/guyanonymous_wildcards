In the style of Bat Trang Ceramic Kilns, Vietnamese stoneware and porcelain in cream-white glaze with blue underglaze painting, as well as brown, green and iron-brown glazes. Decoration shows lotus flowers, chrysanthemums, dragons, bamboo, birds, wave scrolls and geometric key-fret borders, brushed in free, flowing cobalt blue or incised in clay. Forms are tall vases with swelling shoulders, jars with lids, bowls, plates and teapots, with thick walls and a slightly uneven hand-thrown quality. The crackled ivory glaze is soft and subtle, and the mood is gentle, rustic, and unpretentious.
