In the style of Farm Rio, exuberant Brazilian tropical womenswear, with flowing midi and maxi dresses, ruffled blouses, wrap skirts, jumpsuits and kaftans in viscose, cotton and chiffon. Prints are lush and hand-painted, with giant flowers, banana leaves, toucans, macaws, parrots, jaguars, monkeys, butterflies, fruit, palm trees, hummingbirds and stripes, in saturated pink, red, orange, yellow, emerald, turquoise and royal blue. Details include puff sleeves, tiered skirts, smocked bodices, tie straps, embroidered trim and bright pom-pom accents. Carefree, joyful, vacation mood, shown against jungle foliage and bright blue sky.
