Styled in a design language of gentle hand-painted wonder and quiet humanity. Soft watercolour and gouache backgrounds with luminous towering white cumulus clouds, deep green meadows, glowing blue skies and richly detailed foliage, in a warm natural palette of grass green, sky blue, earthy brown and sunset gold. Characters have simple rounded faces, small dot eyes and natural, expressive movement with clean thin outlines and flat soft shading. Cosy, cluttered, lived-in rustic spaces mix with fantastical creatures. Light is airy and nostalgic, calm, curious, and respectful of nature. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
