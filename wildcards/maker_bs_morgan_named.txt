In the style of Morgan, hand-built, charming and deliberately pre-war. Long louvred volumes, separate sweeping curves that stand apart from the main mass, a narrow flat middle, a rounded end and a tall upright chrome face with a slim vertical bar. Aluminium over ash wood, folding canvas and leather straps. Racing green, claret, cream, navy and ivory with polished brass and chrome. Leather, polished wood and round white faces. Handcrafted, nostalgic and cheerful. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
