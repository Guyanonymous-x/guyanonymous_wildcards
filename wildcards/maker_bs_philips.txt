Styled in a design language of optimistic Dutch industrial design. The emblem is a rounded shield containing wavy parallel lines and several four-pointed stars, drawn in bold cobalt blue on white or in white on blue, with a wide, clean. Palette is strong electric blue, white, pale grey, with ivory, mustard, tangerine and pastel tints in older pieces and fresh cyan, navy and sunrise gold in newer ones. Forms are friendly, rounded, smooth and gently curved with glossy and soft matte plastics, bakelite, light wood and satin metal. Clear, calm, geometric graphics with generous spacing, cheerful and trustworthy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
