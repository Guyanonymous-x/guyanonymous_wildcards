In the style of Sevres, French porcelain with intensely coloured ground glazes in deep royal blue, turquoise, apple green and rose pink, framed by gold gilding and reserved panels painted with birds, flowers, pastoral lovers and cherubs. Rococo shapes with scrolled forms and ormolu mounts, and white unglazed biscuit figures. Opulent, ornate, gleaming gold, refined courtly taste, with smooth glassy finish and rich colour set off by crisp gilded edging. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Sevres in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
