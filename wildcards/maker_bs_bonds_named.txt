Styled in a design language of clean, soft, honest, dependable everyday basics with a friendly sense of humour. Plain ribbed cotton jersey in white, navy, grey, pale blue and cream, deep scooped curves, stretchy simple cuts and small woven details. Soft one-piece shapes carry cheerful simple prints, stripes, polka dots, clouds, stars and small animals, in pastel yellow, mint, sky blue and pink. Down-to-earth and reassuring. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
