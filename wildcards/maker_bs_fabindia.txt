In the style of Fabindia, handloom and hand-block-printed Indian cotton clothing and home goods, with relaxed straight kurtas, long tunics, loose pyjamas, stoles, cushion covers and quilts in soft textured cotton, khadi and silk-cotton blends. Colours are earthy natural dyes such as indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow, rust, ochre, olive, charcoal and undyed cream. Patterns are small repeating floral and geometric block prints, ikat, bandhani dots, stripes and checks, with slightly irregular hand-printed edges and visible slub in the weave. Simple stitching, wooden buttons, rural craftsman feel, unfussy, honest, sun-washed look, shown on rustic wooden shelves with hanging cloth.
