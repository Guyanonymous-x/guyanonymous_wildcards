the initiation of a new member into a Freemasons' lodge, as the published rituals and exposés describe it. A candidate stands just inside the lodge room door, blindfolded with a black cloth hood, a soft white rope looped loosely around the neck and trailing down the back, the left side of the shirt opened to bare the left side of the chest, the left trouser leg rolled up above the knee and a loose slipper on the right foot.
