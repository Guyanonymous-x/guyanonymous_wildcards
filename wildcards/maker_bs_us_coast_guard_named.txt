Styled in a design language of cutters and boats with white hulls and a bold diagonal racing stripe of orange-red with a thin blue stripe beside it near the bow, buff-coloured masts and funnels, orange rescue helicopters with white trim, crews in dark blue uniforms and orange survival suits, and a crisp, alert, maritime-service character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
