Styled in a design language of futuristic, serene and floating, like a spaceship landed in a quiet street. Streamlined forms tapering gently to one end, very low smooth surfaces, volumes that seem to hover on thin supports, and pale tops set against darker bodies. Softly curved panels with no sharp creases and gently skirted lower edges. Pale grey, ivory, sea green, pastel blue and sand. Plush cloth and soft leather in sculpted, flowing shapes. Calm, modern and quietly strange.
