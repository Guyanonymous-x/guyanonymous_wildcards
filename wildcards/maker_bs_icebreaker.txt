In the style of Icebreaker, New Zealand merino wool base layers and outdoor knitwear, with close-fitting long-sleeve tops, leggings, hoodies, socks, beanies and lightweight jumpers in fine soft knit. Surface is smooth, matte and slightly fuzzy, with flat seams and mountain-inspired minimalism. Colours are muted forest green, charcoal, deep navy, heather grey, rust, moss, stone and pale blue with occasional bright orange. Graphic touches include small sheep-like or mountain-line prints, subtle stripes and tiny sewn labels. Simple, natural, adventure-ready mood, shown in snowy alpine and misty hills landscapes.
