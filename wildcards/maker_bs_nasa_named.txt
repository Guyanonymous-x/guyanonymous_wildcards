In the style of NASA, clean, technical, optimistic American spaceflight design. Bright white fields carry bold, geometric graphics in deep blue, signal red and black. A round blue disc speckled with white stars carries a vivid red chevron swoosh and a white orbit line, an emblem repeated as patches and stickers. A second mark uses unbroken, rounded, straight-stroked capital letters with no crossbars, in red or dark grey. Colour stripes in red and blue run in parallel, with small golden wing-like details. Surfaces are matte technical fabric, glossy white enamel, golden foil and fine stitching, with neat black labels. Orderly, hopeful, precise and quietly heroic, with a cool minimalist space-age modernism. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
