In the style of Renault in the 1970s and 1980s, a compact boxy hatchback with a square nose, flat bonnet, huge flat rectangular headlamps, and a large diamond-shaped badge in the centre of a flat black grille. Tall upright windscreen, large squared glass area, flat side panels with plastic rub strips, fat black bumpers, and a tailgate sloping gently. Steel wheels with small plastic hubcaps or small alloy wheels. Paint in bright orange, yellow, vivid red, light blue, off-white and beige metallic. Interior has bold striped or check cloth seats, a flat dashboard in black or brown plastic, a round instrument binnacle and big stalks. Mood is cheerful, functional and French-practical.
