In the style of Rooh Afza, rich, nostalgic, floral and festive South Asian lithographic art. A vivid red field framed by a decorative border of small stylised flowers, with a central cartouche holding swashing Latin and Arabic script lettering in white and yellow. Illustrations of rose bunches, fruits, herbs and leaves are drawn in a fine old printing manner with delicate hatching. Forms are tall, square shouldered and slightly ribbed, filled with deep ruby colour and capped in red or gold. Cooling, sweet, perfumed, celebratory and heritage-rich. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Rooh Afza in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
