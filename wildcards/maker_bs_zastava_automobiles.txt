In the style of Zastava, the Yugoslav car maker of the 1980s. Cars are small, square-cut, boxy hatchbacks and saloons with a flat, near-vertical nose, a thin black plastic grille, rectangular headlamps, black plastic bumpers and trim, a short bonnet, a pronounced shoulder line, flat side windows, and a hatchback with a sharply cut tail. Door handles are plain black, wheels have small steel rims with simple plastic or steel caps. Paint is plain gloss colour, such as red, sky blue, white, yellow, silver-grey, beige or orange, sometimes with a thin bright stripe. A small badge with a stylised Z sits on the grille. Interiors are plain, with hard plastic dashboards in brown or black, cloth seats in plaid or stripes. The mood is cheap, honest, utilitarian and distinctively late socialist era.
