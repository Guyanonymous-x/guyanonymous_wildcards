In the style of Le Creuset, French heritage craft in glossy, saturated enamel. Shapes are rounded, heavy, generous and softly domed, with smooth shoulders, gentle curves and sturdy loop forms. Signature palette is flame orange, deep cerise, cobalt, Mediterranean teal, sunny yellow, sage and cream, with satin black and shimmering pearl variations, usually shown as graduated rows of one colour per item. Surfaces are plain and uncluttered, with occasional floral or stripe motifs. Lettering is a simple, tidy sans-serif, with an embossed three-ringed emblem, giving a warm, durable, family-heirloom character. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Le Creuset in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
