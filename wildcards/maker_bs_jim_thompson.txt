In the style of Jim Thompson, Thai hand-woven silk with a lustrous, slightly slubby shimmer, used for scarves, cushions, ties, bags, home furnishings and loose pavilion-style jackets. Fabrics show iridescent two-tone shot effects in jewel colours such as sapphire, emerald, ruby, saffron, fuchsia, plum and turquoise, plus rich plains. Prints include stylised elephants, tropical leaves, orchids, lotus, bamboo, pagoda motifs, tigers, hand-painted whimsical animals and bold stripes, drawn in a flat painterly style with generous colour fields. Products are displayed rolled in rainbow stacks and packed in tidy boxes. Warm, exotic, elegant mood with teak wood interiors and soft tropical light.
