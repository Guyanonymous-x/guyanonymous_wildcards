in deep winter, everything white, heavy snow lying on every roof, fence and woodpile and weighing down the branches of the pines, the low sun small and pale in a hard blue sky, columns of white smoke standing straight up from the chimneys, long blue shadows on the drifts and frost feathering the window glass
in summer, the taiga green, the forest a deep wall of fresh needles and bright birch leaves under a high blue sky with white clouds, the clearings filled with tall grass, pink fireweed and yellow buttercups, vegetable gardens thick with cabbages, potatoes and sunflowers and the long northern daylight warm on the log walls
at sunrise, the temple lit gold, the first low rays striking the onion dome and the upper logs of the bell tower so that they glow a warm bright gold, the forest below still in cool blue shadow, bands of white mist lying in the valleys and the sky shading from pale yellow at the horizon to clear blue overhead
