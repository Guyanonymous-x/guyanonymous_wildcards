In the style of Philippine Airlines, bright, hospitable, tropical, crisp and patriotic identity built from sail-like triangles. Clean white grounds with italic blue capitals, and a device of one blue triangle and one red triangle pointing like sails, with a small eight rayed yellow sun over the blue. A thin red and blue stripe adds rhythm. Deep blue with red piping and woven patterns, soft light and warm timber. Blue, red, white and sunny yellow. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
