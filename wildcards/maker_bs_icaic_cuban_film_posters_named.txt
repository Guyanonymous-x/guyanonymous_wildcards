In the style of ICAIC Cuban Film Posters, vibrant, witty, politically charged and strongly handmade silkscreen design. A few flat, saturated colours in layers with no gradients, bright red, yellow, cobalt blue, orange, green, pink and black, laid down one screen at a time on cheap rough paper with slightly uneven edges and visible misregistration. Imagery is bold, simplified and symbolic, such as a giant eye, a cut-out head, a hand, a skeleton, a face split into colour planes or a single object enlarged to fill the sheet, with playful surreal collage-like juxtapositions and sharp cut-out edges. Lettering is hand-drawn, chunky, irregular, sometimes stencil-like. Pop art, psychedelia and constructivism blended with satire.
