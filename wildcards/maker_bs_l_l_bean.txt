Styled in a design language of honest, rugged New England outdoor craft. Materials are heavy cotton duck canvas, brushed flannel in red, navy and green plaid checks, chunky wool knit, waxed fabric and tan or brown leather trim with bold doubled seam lines. Palette is forest green, deep navy, brick red, khaki, oatmeal cream and rust. Contrast piping and edging in a second colour add simple colour blocking. Emblem is a rectangular woven mark with cream background, a dark green border and a plain serif wordmark in capitals with a small founding date and a pine tree sketch. Details are dull brass and plain utilitarian shapes that look sturdy, practical and softly weathered. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
