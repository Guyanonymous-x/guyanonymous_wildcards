In the style of Jaguar, long, low, sensuous and feline. Very long sweeping lines, a tight compact core set far back and flowing curved surfaces that taper to a slim point. Smoothly faired forms, fine bright metal detail and a single leaping animal motif used sparingly as a mark. British racing green, signal red, cream, metallic silver-blue and opalescent grey. Red or tan leather and polished wood. Elegant speed, curvaceous and graceful.
