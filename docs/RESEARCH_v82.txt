RESEARCH NOTES v82 (2026-11-04)
Four research passes, each with web searches and page fetches (Wikipedia, with some museum, craft and technique pages): sculpture and carving, ceramics glass and decorative craft, textile paper and miniatures, 3D renders stop-motion and installations. Notes are in artforms_sculpture.txt, artforms_craft.txt, artforms_textile.txt and artforms_render_install.txt, each ending with PATTERNS and SOURCES lists. A second pass per family wrote the per-form parts (subjects, materials, techniques, finishes, colours, scales, wear and display) with 15 to 25 further fetches.
Rules applied: every form described by its look; artworks only (not drawings or photographs); adults and animals only, figures clothed or draped, no nudity; no brands, trademarks or real artists in the prompts; plaque and label text in double quotes, one line each; scales carry their own article.
Weak spots: Wikipedia seldom states surface, colour or wear, so many finish and wear items come from general knowledge; the render family mixes screen-based and physical forms, so its shared display and light pools only partly fit (per-form display lists reduce this); material, technique and subject are chosen independently, so a few pairs clash; real artists' names are not included anywhere.
