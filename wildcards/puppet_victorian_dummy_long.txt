a Victorian hand-carved wooden ventriloquist dummy, its head about the size of a grown man's fist and its body as long as an adult's forearm. The face is carved from pale limewood and painted a glossy pinkish cream, with round rosy cheeks, a small turned-up nose and two glass eyes of deep brown set in the sockets. A straight cut runs down from each corner of the mouth to the chin, so the lower jaw drops open on a hinge to show a dark red painted mouth. The varnish on the face has yellowed and crazed into a web of fine cracks. The hair is carved in stiff waves and painted dark brown. The body is a stuffed cloth tube, with jointed wooden hands and small wooden feet in black painted boots, and a slit in the back of the jacket lets the performer's hand reach the head stick and the jaw lever inside. __puppet_victorian_dummy_var__.
