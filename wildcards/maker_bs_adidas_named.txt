Styled in a design language of sporty heritage built on strict repetition. Three parallel stripes of equal width run as the signature graphic. Palette is crisp white, black, navy and royal blue with red or green accents, often in strong colour blocks with thin contrast piping. Materials are smooth leather, suede, soft tracksuit knit and mesh with tidy stitching. Retro terrace and street energy mixed with sharp modern graphic grids, flat studio lighting and confident, casual, youthful attitude. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
