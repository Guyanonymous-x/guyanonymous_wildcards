In the style of Roycroft Arts and Crafts Community, plain handmade rectilinear construction in oak and hammered copper. Wood has square, sturdy members, exposed joinery, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish with visible grain. Copper shows hammer marks, rivets, strap-like fittings and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Roycroft Arts and Crafts Community in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
