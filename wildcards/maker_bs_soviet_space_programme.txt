Styled in a design language of blunt, heavy, utilitarian 1960s industrial heroism. Round spherical forms with small round openings, bare pea green and metallic silver surfaces, tapering volumes clustered around a central core, and brushed orange-tinted foil. Visible cables, rivets and welded seams. Raw, practical, monumental, heavy-engineered and undecorated, with a stern, proud Cold War mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
