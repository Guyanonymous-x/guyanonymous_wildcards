In the style of Winsor and Newton, classic British artists heritage in monochrome. Bold black lettering in wide, flared Roman capitals, with an ampersand set small between two stacked words, sits beside a rearing winged griffin crest drawn in fine black line engraving. Palette is ink black, crisp white, cream paper tones and rich deep blue, with a single bold red band as an accent. Surfaces are matte card, silver foil, satin enamel and polished brass with fine rule lines, thin borders and a tagline in small italic serif. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Winsor and Newton in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
