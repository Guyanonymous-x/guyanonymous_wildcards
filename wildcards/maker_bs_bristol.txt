In the style of Bristol in the 1950s to 1970s, a discreet British grand tourer with an aircraft-inspired shape, a long bonnet with a small oval grille in the nose with twin kidney-shaped openings, round headlamps set in the wings, and a smooth aerodynamic body. Body with a gently curved roof, a hidden spare wheel behind a flap in the wing, and subtle sculpted tail. Wheels are steel with simple chrome caps. Paint in dark green, grey, cream, black and claret. Interior has deep leather seats, polished walnut, simple clear round dials, and a thin large wheel. Mood is reserved, hand-built and clubby.
