In the style of Sega, 1990s Japanese game consoles and arcade hardware. Glossy black plastic shells with sharp angular wedge shapes and circular recessed cartridge slots, moulded ridges and vents, curved controller grips with three or six oversized round buttons and a round directional disc. Cobalt blue lettering and swirl logos, white and orange accents, loud saturated cabinet artwork with bold outlines and hard-edged speed lines, and glowing red or green power LEDs. Dreamcast-era units are pale off-white and square with a soft spiral on the lid. Typography is slanted bold italic capitals with a futuristic, fast feel. Mood is edgy, energetic and arcade-bright.
