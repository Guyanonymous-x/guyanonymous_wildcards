Styled in a design language of dry-transfer lettering look with black or coloured letters on translucent sheets and a waxy, slightly cracked appearance when rubbed down. Letterforms are bold, expressive 1960s and 70s display faces, chunky rounded letters, psychedelic swirls, inline shadows, stencil cuts, art-nouveau revival and space-age geometry. Layouts show a neat grid of tiny alphabets and punctuation, with registration marks and a pale blue guide grid. Applied words are slightly uneven, with tiny gaps and imperfect baselines. The mood is cut-and-paste, punk poster and retro graphic design.
