two men in their twenties and thirties who vowed it, each tied to the center pole by a long rawhide cord ending in two wooden skewers as thin as a finger pushed through pinched-up skin on his chest, leaning back until the cords are tight and the skin stands out, thin lines of blood running down to the waist of his breechcloth
women in their thirties to sixties in long dresses of tanned hide and of dark trade cloth sewn with rows of elk teeth, wool blankets around their shoulders and their braids hanging in front, dancing in place on the women's side with sprigs of sage in their hands
