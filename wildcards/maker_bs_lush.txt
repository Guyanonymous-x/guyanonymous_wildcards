Styled in a design language of handmade, playful and joyfully imperfect. Vivid swirled marbled patterns in hot pink, leaf green, purple, orange and turquoise, with confetti-like blocky shapes, collage cut-outs and fresh leaf motifs. Colour is intense and fresh against plain black and brown. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
