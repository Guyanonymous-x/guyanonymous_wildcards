In the style of Reyn Spooner, reverse-print Hawaiian shirts. The shirt is a short-sleeved button-up with a pullover or full-front placket, a soft flat collar, left chest pocket and a straight hem, in a thick cotton-poly blend woven in a plain oxford-like texture. The print is applied to the inside of the cloth, so the outer face looks washed, faded and muted as if sun-bleached, with soft tonal colours, navy, forest green, dusty rust, burgundy, sand, teal and faded sky blue. Motifs are large-scale tropical leaves, palm fronds, monstera, hibiscus, ferns, bamboo, canoes, surfboards, hula scenes and island maps arranged in repeating patterns, often in a single colour family with a lighter shade for outlines. Buttons are coconut-shell or dark plastic. The mood is relaxed, understated, preppy and island-casual, a subtle quiet version of the aloha shirt rather than a loud one.
