In the style of the euro banknote series, rectangular paper notes in distinct pastel colours per denomination such as grey, red, blue, orange, green, yellow and pale purple, increasing in size. Fronts show a classical arch, window or gateway on the left with a large numeral in the lower right and twelve small stars in a circle, backs show a bridge with a map of the continent. Architectural drawings of generic windows and doors from different historical periods, rendered in fine intaglio line work, microprint text, a holographic foil stripe or patch with a shimmering glossy effect, watermark portrait window and an embossed ink feel. Soft pastel gradient backgrounds, clean sans serif numerals and Latin and Greek lettering.
