Thathe Vondo, the holy forest, a dense dark mountain forest of tall yellowwood trees with straight pale trunks, tree ferns twice the height of a person, hanging creepers and moss, the burial forest of the chiefs of the Thathe clan, held to be guarded by a white lion and by the lightning bird, a single narrow dirt road passing through it under the closed canopy
Lake Fundudzi seen from the forested hillside above it, a long dark green lake lying in a deep steep-sided valley, held to be guarded by a white python and to be the home of the ancestors under its water, thick bush down to its reedy edge and one small sandy beach
the Phiphidi falls, a white waterfall dropping in two steps over a ledge of dark red-brown rock into a round green pool in thick riverside forest, the pool and the flat wet rock beside the falls the home of the water spirits, where the Ramunangi clan leaves its offerings
the edge of the holy forest where it meets the tea fields, a wall of huge dark trees hung with pale beard moss rising straight out of low clipped rows of bright green tea bushes, mist caught in the treetops and a footpath ending at a gap between two great trunks
a sacred pool on a mountain stream, still brown water under leaning fig trees and wild date palms, ringed by smooth gray boulders, a flat rock at its edge worn pale where clay pots and bowls of offerings are set down
