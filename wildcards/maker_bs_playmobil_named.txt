Styled in a design language of small hard plastic forms with oversized round shapes, simple dot marks, a curved smile line, C-shaped curves and standardised blocky, stiffly jointed construction. Bright primary red, yellow, blue and green plus brown, grey and skin tones in a matte to satin finish, with clean moulded seams and thin moulded details. Everything is simplified into cheerful, friendly, clip-together pieces with tiny printed faces and flat painted patterns. Packaging uses clear windows and bold colour panels in a cheerful, wholesome tone. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
