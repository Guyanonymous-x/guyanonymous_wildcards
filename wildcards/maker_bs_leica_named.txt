In the style of Leica, understated, crafted, timeless precision. Compact rectangular forms with a flat top plane, in black enamel or polished silver chrome, wrapped in finely pebbled black leather-like texture. Fine knurled rings, slim barrel-like proportions, engraved white and red numerals and a tiny red dot accent. Brass shows through worn paint on edges, giving honest patina. Minimal, no clutter, with a small white script mark and every control exactly placed. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
