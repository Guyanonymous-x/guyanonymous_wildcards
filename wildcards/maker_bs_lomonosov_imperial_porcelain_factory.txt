Styled in a design language of bright white porcelain in two manners. The imperial manner shows gilded edges, cobalt blue nets of dark blue lattice with gold dots and rich floral reserves in naturalistic colour, on fine, thin, delicate forms. Surfaces are lively and glossy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
