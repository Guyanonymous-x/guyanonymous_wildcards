Styled in a design language of venetian art glass with thin hand-blown walls and elegant organic, asymmetrical forms, smooth, rounded and softly irregular, with wavy edges like folded cloth. Colours are luminous amber, aquamarine, turquoise, lilac, milky white and rich red, with patchwork mosaic panels, bands of fused colour, delicate lattice threading and acid-etched matte surfaces. Light-catching, subtly imperfect, jewel-like and sculptural, in a mid-century Italian spirit. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
