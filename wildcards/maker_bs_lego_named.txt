In the style of Lego, glossy hard plastic in saturated primary red, blue, yellow and white plus black, grey and bright lime and orange. Everything is a rectangular modular volume with a regular grid of round studs on top, crisp moulded edges and visible seams, so any subject reads as assembled from simple bricks. Characters have cylindrical yellow heads with dot eyes and a simple smile, claw hands and blocky bodies. Packaging and graphics use flat bright colour fields, a red square badge with white rounded lettering, small clean line diagrams and dynamic action imagery. Cardboard and kraft textures appear in newer eco touches. Photography is bright, playful and toy-scale, with tiny scenes in macro focus and cheerful humour.
