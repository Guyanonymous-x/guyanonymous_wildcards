Styled in a design language of compact, precise, high-tech engineering with a confident premium feel. Clean rectangular and softly bevelled forms with tight panel gaps, miniaturised proportions and rows of neat small controls. Finishes are matte black, titanium silver and brushed metal, with occasional bold primary or translucent colour accents, and a deep gloss on screens and lacquer. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
