In the style of Talavera Uriarte, thick glossy milk-white tin glaze over rounded, weighty, hand-made forms, painted freely by hand in cobalt blue with accents of yellow, green, orange, black and sometimes turquoise. Decoration is loose and generous, with flowers, vines, leaves, birds, rosettes and geometric borders in slightly raised shiny paint. Pinholes, pooled colour and small unevenness in the surface are kept as part of the charm. Repeated panels join into large blue and white patterns. Rustic, festive, colonial and clearly handmade. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
