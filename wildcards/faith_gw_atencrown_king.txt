the tall blue crown of the pharaohs, called the khepresh, a high rounded helmet of bright blue that swells up and back from the forehead to a blunt ridge, its surface covered all over with small raised golden rings
the tall white crown of Upper Egypt, a smooth pale cone that narrows as it rises and ends in a rounded knob, standing high above the forehead
the double crown of the two lands of Egypt, a tall white rounded cone set inside a flat-topped red crown with a high straight back and a thin curling wire springing forward from its front
a striped headcloth of blue and gold, called the nemes, folded square across the brow with two pleated flaps hanging down over the shoulders, topped by the tall white and red double crown
