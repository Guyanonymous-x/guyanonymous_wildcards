Styled in a design language of neat, sporty, mass-market German design. Compact, tidy, sharply creased forms with narrow chrome edges and neat rectangular badges. Palette of signal yellow, bright orange, lime green, sky blue, white and black, plus period greys and enamel red. Clean ring-and-bolt geometry, dynamic diagonals, crisp and youthful, with a pragmatic, friendly, quietly sporty character. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
