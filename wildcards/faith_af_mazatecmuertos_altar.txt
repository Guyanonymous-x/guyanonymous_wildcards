in a house of adobe and tin roof the altar stands under a tall arch of green palm and sugarcane hung with orange marigolds and dark red cockscomb, loaded with tamales, oranges, bananas, bread and cups of chocolate
on a table covered in a white embroidered cloth, framed photographs of the family dead stand among candles, copal smoke, glasses of water and small cups of aguardiente, marigold petals strewn on the floor to the door
at the cemetery on a steep hillside the family graves are heaped with marigolds and cockscomb, candles burning in rows and a woman in her sixties in an embroidered huipil kneeling to lay out food
