Styled in a design language of polished tennis-club elegance with a playful, confident wink. Textured cotton pique with a small knit weave, ribbed edges and a clean, relaxed fit. Palette is tennis white plus deep bottle green, navy, red, sky blue and yellow, in solid blocks and bold stripes. Terry cloth bands, white canvas, crisp laundered surfaces and white court lines add sport cues. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
