Styled in a design language of small perforated rectangles with a thin white border, each featuring a single illustrated subject rendered in fine intaglio engraving or in colourful painted illustration. Patriotic Americana, mid-century commemorative theme, rich fine detail, limited ink palette of blue, red, green and sepia. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
