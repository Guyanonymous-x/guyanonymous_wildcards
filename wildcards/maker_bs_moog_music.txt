In the style of Moog Music, analogue synthesizers. Solid wood cabinets in dark walnut or black with a slanted control panel in black or silver, packed with chunky round knobs with white pointers, patch cords and jacks, small rocker switches, and printed white labels in a clear sans-serif. A long keyboard with white and black keys and a pitch ribbon. Colour accents are red, orange and white lines grouping the sections. Mood is warm, vintage, scientific and handcrafted.
