Styled in a design language of orderly, efficient, geometric modernism and strictly typographic. A deep, rich navy and a bright canary yellow form the core palette, joined by clean white and soft grey. A circular emblem encloses a bird in flight drawn in just a few simple strokes, like a seal, in blue on white or yellow on blue. Compositions use flat, wide colour fields, a strict grid, thin rules and generous empty margins. Finishes are matte and calm with crisp edges and precise alignment. Everything feels trustworthy, disciplined, cool-headed and purposeful, with understated German clarity and no ornament. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
