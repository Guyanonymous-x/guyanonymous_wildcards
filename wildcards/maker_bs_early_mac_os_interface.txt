Styled in a design language of a one-bit black and white pixel surface treatment with no gradients. Shapes have thin crisp black outlines, small rounded corners and a hard offset shadow along the right and bottom edges. Grey is made only from fine dithered checkerboard and dotted patterns, and title strips are filled with thin horizontal pinstripes with a tiny square at one end. Icons are chunky hand-drawn pixel pictograms with a cheerful smiling face motif, a pointing arrow and a wristwatch shape. Lettering is a rounded, friendly pixel typeface with wide curvy letters. Later versions add soft pastel greys, lilac and teal tints with subtle bevels. Charming, crisp, hand-crafted and playful. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
