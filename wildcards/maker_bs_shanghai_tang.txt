In the style of Shanghai Tang, glossy modern take on Chinese imperial and 1930s Shanghai dress, form-fitting silk qipao dresses and mandarin-collar jackets with cloth knot frog fastenings, side slits and piped edges. Colours are intense and playful, such as imperial yellow, lacquer red, fuchsia, jade green, turquoise, orange and purple, in a candy-bright palette alongside black and gold. Fabrics are satin, brocade, jacquard and embroidered silk with dragons, peonies, double happiness symbols, bats, clouds and lotus flowers, plus tangzhuang tops and padded quilted jackets. Home goods use lacquered red boxes, tassels and cloisonne enamel in a cheerful nostalgic, theatrical Hong Kong department-store mood.
