Nine raked windows giving a brilliant flat light off the sea by day, every screen at full brightness and the dark surfaces kept out of the glass
By night every fitting is switched to dim red, the sea and the horizon clear through nine windows and the room itself almost invisible
Six screens dimmed to a night setting and still the brightest thing present
A chart table with a shielded red lamp; and the reason the owner's visits to this deck are discouraged at night, which is that somebody opening the door from a lit stair costs the watch twenty minutes
