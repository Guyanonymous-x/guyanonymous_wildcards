The deacon stops the candidate in front of each officer in turn, and each officer raps once on the pedestal with a small gavel.
The candidate kneels at the altar, both hands flat on the open book, while the Master stands over the candidate holding the gavel.
The hood is lifted from the candidate's head, and the candidate blinks at the room of members standing in their aprons, every one of them with a hand held out flat in greeting.
