In the style of Ferrari, passionate, theatrical Italian speed. Long, low, wedge-sharp or voluptuous sculpted forms with sensual curves, flowing fenders, sharp creases, wide stance and dramatic intakes. Deep, glossy rosso red dominates, joined by yellow, black, ivory and silver, with a prancing horse emblem on a yellow shield and thin racing stripes. Surfaces are lacquer-bright with chrome, carbon, polished metal and tan leather. Lettering is elegant, slanted, flowing script and bold racing capitals. Photography is glamorous, high-contrast and low-angled, with motion blur, sunlight and raw emotion.
