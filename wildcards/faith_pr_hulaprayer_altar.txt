at the edge of the dance ground stands the kuahu, the altar of Laka, a low platform of stacked stones heaped with green maile vines, ferns and ti leaves and a block of yellow wood for the goddess
before the dancers lie ho'okupu, offerings wrapped in green ti leaves and tied with strips of leaf, set on a flat black stone with sprigs of red lehua blossom
behind the dancers a carved wooden ki'i, the image of a god with a wide open mouth and crested head, stands on a stone platform draped with lei
