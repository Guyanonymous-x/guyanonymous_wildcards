Styled in a design language of democratic Scandinavian practicality, bright and friendly. Simple, light, flat-planar shapes, modular rectangles with soft corners, visible joints and clever space-saving logic. Pale birch, pine and white laminate are paired with cheerful saturated blue and yellow plus red, green and soft pastels, in bold, playful patterns and plain colour blocks. Materials feel affordable but honest, such as light wood, painted board, woven textiles and moulded plastic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
