Styled in a design language of ceremonial imagery rendered in dense blazing colour. Tiny glass beads are pressed one by one into a layer of wax and resin over carved forms, leaving a shining mosaic surface with a slightly bumpy glittering texture. Flat areas use thick coloured wool pressed into wax in tight spiralling lines that follow each shape like contour maps. Motifs are deer, maize, cactus, scorpions, five-petalled flowers, sun faces, rain clouds and diamond-backed snake bodies, with zigzag lines radiating outward. The palette is neon-bright and contrasting, fuchsia, turquoise, lime green, electric blue, orange, yellow, red and black with white outlines, filled completely so no background shows. Psychedelic, symmetrical and intricate. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
