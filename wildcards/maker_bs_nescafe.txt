Styled in a design language of bold red and white with a warm coffee-brown depth. Palette is red, white, cream, coffee brown and gold, with dark charcoal for premium lines and deep green and tan for earthy variants. Surfaces are glossy lacquer, matt paper, foil film and cork-like textures. Decorative patterns use curling steam lines, bean shapes and hand-drawn leaf motifs in repeating tiles. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
