the range of 1820 with three houses, timber bars and a flued wall
the range of 1870 at its peak with seven or nine houses, iron bars, two boilers, a pineapple pit and a staff of fourteen, which is the arrangement here and which produced grapes in March and pineapples at all
the range of 1900 with the pineapples given up because they could be imported, which is the first thing to go
the range of 1918 growing tomatoes for a hospital
the range of 1930 with one house kept going and six cold, which is the standard retreat
the range of 1950 with the boilers out, the glass going and the vines dead, and a vine of forty years' training is killed by one unheated February, so the loss is absolute and instantaneous
the range of 1970 with the ironwork in a heap and the back wall standing with its peach nails still in it, which is the condition of hundreds of these
the range today rebuilt with aluminum bars and run on gas or biomass in a restored garden, which is happening
the bothy as a holiday let or a shop
the range mothballed, which does not work, because glass and iron need the heat as much as the plants do
the range open to the weather, where everything above the dwarf wall is gone in fifteen years and what survives is a brick back wall with nail holes, a line of iron bolt stumps, a sunken stokehole full of water and a pineapple pit that looks like a grave
