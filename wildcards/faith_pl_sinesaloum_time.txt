in the dry season, the light hazy and gold, a pale sky whitened by fine desert dust, the sun a soft blurred disk, the grass burned to straw yellow, the baobabs bare of leaves, the salt flats cracked and glaring and every distant palm faded to a dim gray shape
at sunset, the creeks orange and the mangroves black, the low red sun sinking behind the delta and turning every channel into a strip of glowing copper, the mangrove walls and the palms reduced to flat dark outlines, long lines of white egrets flying to their roosts across a sky of orange, pink and deep violet
in the rainy season, the land green, tall dark rain clouds piled over the delta, the baobabs covered in fresh leaves, fields of millet and peanuts bright green around the villages, the sandy tracks dotted with brown puddles and the salt flats turned to wide shallow mirrors of water
