In the style of Korean Air, a Korean airline identity of the late twentieth century. Aircraft have a white lower fuselage and a sky blue upper stripe over a wide silver grey cheatline, with a big round taegeuk emblem in swirling red and blue on the tail fin, a red upper half and blue lower half twisting around each other. The title is a bold rounded serif wordmark in light blue, in a heavy slab-like cut with a quirky character. The later redesign shifts to a dark navy outlined taegeuk and a tidy sans serif, using only the single word. Cabins feature calm light blue and grey seats with white head cloths, warm wood-toned panels, and cream carpeting. Crew uniforms are light blue and white with a neat scarf, pale gloves and a small cap. Mood is orderly, polite, spacious, sky-high and corporate, with cool blue colours and the clean white fuselage on the runway under a cloudless sky.
