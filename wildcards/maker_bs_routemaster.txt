Styled in a design language of a graphic language of one confident, glossy red applied across large unbroken surfaces. The red is deep, warm and slightly orange, set against a single cream or pale grey band and thin black or gold pinstripe edging. Shapes are softly rounded at the corners with smooth continuous curves, polished chrome or brushed aluminium trim and neat riveted seams. A red ring with a blue bar serves as the emblem. Secondary tones include moquette-like woven patterns in muted blue, green and rust, wood-grain laminate and black rubber details, with a proud, civic, nostalgic character. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
