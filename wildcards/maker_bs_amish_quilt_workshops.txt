In the style of Amish Quilt Workshops, quilts made with large simple geometric blocks of solid-colour wool or cotton, in deep, saturated tones. Typical palette includes black, plum purple, royal blue, forest green, wine red, brown and dusty rose, with no printed patterns. Designs include a centre diamond, bars, sunshine and shadow, and nine-patch squares, framed by wide borders and bound in contrasting colour. Fine, dense quilting stitches create feathers, wreaths, cables and grids in the dark areas, giving a subtle relief. The mood is bold, austere, modern-looking and graphic.
