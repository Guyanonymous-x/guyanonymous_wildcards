In the style of Hasselblad, Swedish medium-format cameras. A cubic body with a square profile, finished in satin black paint with bright chrome trim or all-black crinkle texture, topped with a folding waist-level viewfinder hood or a pentaprism. A large detachable square film magazine fits the back and a big round lens with chrome or black barrel, coloured aperture and shutter rings, and engraved white numerals mounts at the front. Winding crank on the side, a single large shutter button, and a bold white capital wordmark. Special editions use bright colours or exposed carbon. Mood is serious, rugged, professional and sculptural.
