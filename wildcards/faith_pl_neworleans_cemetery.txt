Saint Louis Cemetery Number 1, an old walled cemetery of raised white tombs built above the ground in the form of little houses with peaked roofs, packed close together along narrow crooked paths of broken paving and shell
rows of whitewashed tombs with black iron crosses and stone urns standing on their tops, low rusted iron fences around some of them and weeds growing from the cracks in their roofs
a crumbling old tomb with its plaster skin peeling away in sheets to show the soft red brick underneath, ferns sprouting from the gaps and a marble slab leaning loose across its front
