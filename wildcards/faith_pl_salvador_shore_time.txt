on February 2nd, the feast of Iemanjá, crowds with flowers on the beach, thousands of adults dressed in white and pale blue packed on the sand and the seawall, holding white roses, tall flower spikes and woven baskets filled with blossoms, small mirrors and perfume bottles, while fishing boats hung with blue and white streamers carry the baskets out over the sparkling water
on a bright morning, the sea pale turquoise near the sand and deep blue beyond, white clouds over the bay and hard sunlight on the wet sand
at sunset, the sun dropping into the bay behind the far island, the water copper and rose, the boats dark shapes against the glow
