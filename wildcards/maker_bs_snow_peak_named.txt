Styled in a design language of calm Japanese minimalism with refined metal craft. Brushed titanium, satin stainless steel, dark cast iron and pale natural wood combined in slim, precise, softly rounded geometric forms that stack, nest and pack flat. Palette is silver, charcoal, black, sand, olive and off-white with very sparse colour. The emblem is a simple black asterisk-like star of radiating lines. Surfaces are matte and uncluttered, edges are thin and clean, and joints are neat and understated. Quiet, thoughtful, tidy and made to last. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
