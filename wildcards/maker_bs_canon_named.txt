Styled in a design language of dependable, precise and quietly handsome. Compact rectangular forms with a flat black or satin silver plane, fine black leatherette or wood-grain texture and crisp machined edges. Tiny metal details with fine knurling and thin ridges give a tidy, engineered feel, and a narrow red band accent marks top-grade pieces. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
