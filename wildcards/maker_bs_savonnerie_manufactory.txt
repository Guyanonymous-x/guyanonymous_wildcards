Styled in a design language of thick, plush velvet texture with a sculptural, monumental pattern. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Wide borders imitate carved mouldings, with fruit and flowers in the corners. The feeling is of a royal palace, sumptuous, ordered and heavy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
