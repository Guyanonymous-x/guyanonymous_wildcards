Styled after a rich deep royal purple dominates every surface in a glossy, silky, velvety finish. Cream, white and warm gold act as accents, along with thin gold borders, curved swoosh lines and ribbon-like flowing shapes. Surfaces include metallic gold foil, satin paper and smooth lacquer with a soft highlight. Shapes are rounded, plump and generous, with gently curved corners and sweeping curves rather than angles. Decorative flourishes, swirls and subtle shimmer add a sense of treat and indulgence. The mood is warm, traditional, comforting and generous. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
