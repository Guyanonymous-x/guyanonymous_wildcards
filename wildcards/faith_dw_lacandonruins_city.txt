the stone city of Yaxchilán rises on terraces above a bend of the wide brown Usumacinta river, temples of gray limestone with tall pierced roof crests standing among giant forest trees
Structure 33, the great temple of Yaxchilán, stands on its high hill at the top of a long stair, three doorways in its front and a tall roof crest of stone lattice above
the plaza of the old city is overgrown with ferns and tree roots, carved stone slabs leaning among the trunks, howler monkeys climbing on the temple roofs
