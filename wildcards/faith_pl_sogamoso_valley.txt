the town of Sogamoso, a spread of low houses and buildings of brick and white plaster with red tile and gray sheet-metal roofs filling the valley floor, the twin towers of a pale church rising over a tree-shaded main square, straight streets running out toward the fields and the tall chimneys of a few factories at the edge
mountains around the valley, steep rounded ridges of the Andes closing the valley on every side, their lower slopes a patchwork of small fields and woods, their upper slopes bare brown and olive grassland, white clouds resting on the highest crests and blue shadows moving slowly across the folds
