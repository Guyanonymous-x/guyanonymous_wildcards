Egungun, the masquerade of the ancestors, a towering costumed figure with the face hidden, covered head to toe, wearing layered panels of rich cloth in many colors, beads, mirrors and cowrie shells, spinning in a dance, cloth panels flaring out like a wheel, in a dusty village square full of drummers and onlookers, every part of the body hidden under the cloth, the panels of velvet, satin and patterned cloth in red, gold, blue and green layered like scales, beads and tiny mirrors sewn all over, cowrie shells hanging in rows, the panels flying out in a circle as the figure spins, drummers and a crowd of villagers watching
