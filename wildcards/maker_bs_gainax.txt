Styled in a design language of nervous close-ups on eyes, bold high-contrast colour fields in red, purple, white and teal, hot-blooded exaggerated poses with extreme foreshortening, and a mix of gritty realism and flamboyant over-the-top energy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
