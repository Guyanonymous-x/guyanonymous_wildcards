Styled in a design language of mid-century American promotional elegance. A sharp, framed by streamlined curved panels. Palette is cream, ivory, walnut brown, warm gold, tomato red, teal and ink black on aged-paper tones. Forms are streamlined with generous rounded corners, stepped silhouettes and sunburst radiating lines, in polished figured wood veneer, glossy bakelite, chrome strips and brass trim. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
