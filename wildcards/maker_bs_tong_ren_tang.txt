Styled in a design language of imperial, scholarly, aromatic, sombre and prosperous, like an old apothecary of lacquer, brass and paper. Imperial yellow, vermilion red and gold with embossed dragon or lotus borders, traditional calligraphic characters and red seal stamps. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
