Styled in a design language of crisp, confident, premium European branding in deep emerald green with bright signal red and white. A bold red five-pointed star is the central repeatable emblem, set inside a round or oval seal edged with fine white and silver outlines. Finishes are glossy lacquer, brushed silver foil and embossed metallic details with a lustrous sheen. The mood is fresh, polished and international. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
