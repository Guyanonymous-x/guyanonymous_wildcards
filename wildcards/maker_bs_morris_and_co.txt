In the style of Morris and Co, English Arts and Crafts wallpaper and textiles with dense, flat, intertwined foliage, acanthus leaves, pomegranates, willow boughs, honeysuckle, tulips and birds, repeating in rhythmic symmetrical trellises and curving vines. Muted natural dyes of indigo blue, madder red, moss green, russet, olive and ochre on cream. Woodblock-printed, tapestry and embroidery look, hand-drawn with a medieval flavour, restful and rich, cottage and manor room hangings.
