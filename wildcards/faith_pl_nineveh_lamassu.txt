lamassu, huge stone guardian figures carved from single blocks of pale gray stone as winged bulls with the heads of bearded men wearing tall horned crowns, their long beards in tight rows of curls and their feathered wings swept back, standing in a pair at each side of a gate
a lamassu carved with five legs, two front legs planted side by side and three more along its flank, so it looks standing still from the front and striding forward when seen from the side
the base of a broken lamassu at a gate, only the great carved hooves, the lower legs and part of the feathered wing left on the stone block, pale chips of stone around it
