In the style of Wedgwood, English jasperware with matte unglazed stoneware in pale powder blue, sage green, lilac, black and cream, decorated with crisp white relief figures in classical Greek and Roman scenes, garlands, urns, putti and acanthus borders. Vases, cameo plaques, teapots and trinket boxes with fine sharp sprigged moulding applied to a smooth chalky background, along with creamy glazed tableware. Neoclassical symmetry, elegant and delicate, drawing-room mantel decoration.
