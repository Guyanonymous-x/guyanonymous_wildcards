In the style of Versace, maximal, flamboyant, high-shine glamour inspired by Roman antiquity and pop. The signature emblem is a gold medusa head medallion, framed by a repeating Greek key meander border, with baroque scrolls, acanthus leaves, garlands and ornate filigree. Palette is rich gold, jet black, crisp white, deep burgundy, royal blue, hot pink, turquoise and sunshine yellow, often clashing in dense, dizzying prints. Materials are silk, satin, embossed leather, metallic mesh, gilded hardware and lacquer. Lettering is bold, wide, sharp-edged capitals in gold or white on black. Packaging is black and red boxes with gilded patterns. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
