the round church of a Knights Templar house, built in the Middle Ages in a round form to recall the church of the Holy Sepulchre. A round nave of pale stone is ringed by slender polished dark marble columns holding up pointed arches, and on the stone floor between them lie carved stone effigies of knights in chain mail. The effigies lie on their backs with legs crossed and swords at their sides, their shields on their arms, their stone faces worn smooth.
