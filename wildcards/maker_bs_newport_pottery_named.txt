In the style of Newport Pottery, English Art Deco design hand-painted with bold geometric zigzags, triangles, diamonds, stylised trees, houses and flowers in bright enamel orange, yellow, red, green, blue and purple, with black outlines on a creamy ground. Angular conical shapes, triangular details, stepped forms and cubist curves. Cheerful, vivid, jazzy, gloriously flat and modernist 1930s, with flat colour and strong outlines throughout.
