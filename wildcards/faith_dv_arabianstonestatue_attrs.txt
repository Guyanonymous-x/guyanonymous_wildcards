beside the stone stand the three acacia trees of al-Uzza at Nakhla, hung with strips of cloth, a woman in her thirties in a dark mantle tying a strip to a branch
offerings are laid at the base of the stone, small bronze figures, bowls of incense, clay lamps and sheaves of grain
