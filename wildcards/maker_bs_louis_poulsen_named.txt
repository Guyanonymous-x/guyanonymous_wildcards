Styled in a design language of calm, sculptural Scandinavian restraint where light is softened and glare is controlled. Layered concentric curves, overlapping discs or dozens of overlapping leaf-like plates form smooth symmetrical shapes. Materials are polished steel, chrome, brass, copper and white lacquer with soft warm reflections. Smooth, restrained, elegant and perfectly proportioned, giving a soft diffused glow and a quietly luxurious modernist calm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
