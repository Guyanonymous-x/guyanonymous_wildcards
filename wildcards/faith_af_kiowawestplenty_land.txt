the land of plenty in the west, rolling hills of rich green grass and clear streams, huge herds of buffalo grazing to the edge of the sky, the camps of the dead along the river
in the camp of the dead men and women in their thirties to nineties in fine beaded buckskin feast on buffalo meat around fires before their painted tipis, racks of meat drying in the sun
the dead dance in a great circle on the green grass of the west, men in their twenties to seventies in feathered bustles and women in their thirties to eighties in fringed shawls
