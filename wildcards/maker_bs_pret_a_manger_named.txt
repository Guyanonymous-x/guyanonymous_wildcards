Styled in a design language of brisk, tidy, natural urban freshness. Surfaces are matte, with warm paper textures, clear glass and brushed steel accents. A secondary palette of leafy green, mustard, cobalt blue and berry pink appears in seasonal flat patterns of dots, leaves, snowflakes and confetti on bright wrapping. Stripes of burgundy on cream and small star sprinkles repeat as trim. Clean, healthy, quick and modern. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
