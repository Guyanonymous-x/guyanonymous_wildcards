Styled in a design language of cosy, graphic wool heritage from the American Northwest. Thick, tightly woven wool in bold geometric motifs such as stepped diamonds, zigzags, chevrons, triangles, banded stripes and large plaids, arranged in symmetrical, repeating bands. Palette is saturated rust red, turquoise, mustard gold, forest green, navy, cream and black, balanced in warm earthy blocks. Edges are bound or finished with neat fringe, and surfaces have a soft brushed nap and a sturdy, woolly hand. Emblem is a rectangular woven or printed label in deep blue with gold border, framed in a double outline. Rustic, warm, durable and handcrafted. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
