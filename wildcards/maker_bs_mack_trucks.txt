Styled in a design language of blunt, tough, muscular American heavy-duty character. A bulldog-like, forward-thrust mass, a top line that curves down steeply and a bold chrome standing animal ornament. Bright enamel in red, yellow, blue or black, with stainless accents. Chunky, compact, solid and no-nonsense, proportioned like a determined fighter, with polished highlights on a hard-working frame. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
