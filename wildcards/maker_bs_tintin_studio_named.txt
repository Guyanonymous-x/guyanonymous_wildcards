Styled in a design language of the clear line school with uniform thin black outlines, flat cheerful fills and no hatching. Meticulously accurate backgrounds drawn with research and care, characters with round simple faces, dot eyes and an upswept tuft of hair, a limited palette of sea blue, sand yellow, warm red and green, stage-like compositions. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
