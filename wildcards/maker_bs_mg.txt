In the style of MG, a small British open sports car with a tall upright radiator grille of chrome vertical slats under an octagon badge, a long flat louvred bonnet, cutaway doors, cycle wings and a rounded tail with a spare wheel. Round headlamps stand either side of the radiator, and a low folding windscreen sits above the cockpit. Later 1960s cars have a smooth body, chrome bumpers and wire-spoke wheels. Paint in racing green, red, cream, ivory and pale blue. Interior has bucket seats in red or black leather, an engine-turned or wooden dashboard with a tachometer and speedometer in chrome rims, and a slim banjo-spoke wheel. Mood is jaunty, spirited and cheerful.
