In the style of Raymond, classic worsted wool suiting and tailored menswear, formal, sober and gentlemanly. Smooth fine-twill cloth in navy, charcoal, black, grey and tan carries subtle pinstripes, herringbone, glen checks and windowpane patterns. The silhouette is precisely cut with structured shoulders, notched edges, a nipped waist and a sharp crease. Neat folded stacks and swatch-like order suggest craft and discipline. Dignified, traditional and aspirational, recalling mid-twentieth century formal tailoring, with warm wood panelling and polished brass tones. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
