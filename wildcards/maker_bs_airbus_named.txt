Styled in a design language of smooth, calm, composed European design in a restrained palette of deep royal blue, bright cyan, pale sky blue and clean white. Soft gradients fade from dark blue to light blue across large curved panels, divided by sharp-edged light cyan rays or a simple abstract slanted letter A set in a circle or square. Surfaces are glossy white paint, satin enamel and pearly metallic blue, always seamless and free of decoration. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
