Styled in a design language of youthful swinging-sixties mod design. Materials are shiny PVC, patent, crisp cotton, jersey and opaque coloured knit with white contrast edges. Shapes are short, tidy, simple and cut with sharp precision for a playful, modern energy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
