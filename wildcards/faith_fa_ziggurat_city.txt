at Ur, the city of Nanna the moon god, the ziggurat rising over flat-roofed houses of mud brick, the Euphrates and its belt of date palms close by
at Uruk, the city of the goddess Inanna and the sky god Anu, ringed by a great city wall, canals and green fields of barley along the river
at Eridu, the oldest city, on a low mound at the edge of a reed marsh, the house of the god Enki of the sweet waters
at Nippur, the holy city of Enlil, chief of the gods, the ziggurat standing over a bend of a canal and a crowded town of brick houses
