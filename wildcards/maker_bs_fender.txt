Styled in a design language of bright, twangy, sunny California mid-century. Sleek slab forms with offset contours, sunburst finishes fading from amber yellow through red to black, or solid Surf Green, Daphne Blue, Fiesta Red and cream. White, mint or tortoiseshell panels, chrome fittings, pale maple wood with black dot markers and gold-tan tweed with dark diagonal weave. Brown and silver nameplates with raised letters, black vinyl, silver cloth and chrome corner trim add rich surface variety. Lettering is a bold curved flowing script with a long swooping tail, in gold, silver or black. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
