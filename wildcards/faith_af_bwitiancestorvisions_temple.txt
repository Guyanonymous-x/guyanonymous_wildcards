the ebanza, the Bwiti temple, a long open hall of palm thatch on carved posts in a forest clearing, a small fire burning on the earth floor at its center
inside the temple the tall carved center post, painted red and white, rises to the thatch, the ngombi, an eight-stringed harp carved with a woman's face, leaning against it
in a corner of the temple stand the byeri, bark boxes the size of a cooking pot holding the skulls and bones of the lineage's ancestors, each topped by a dark carved guardian figure
