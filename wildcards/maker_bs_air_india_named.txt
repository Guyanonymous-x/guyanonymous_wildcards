In the style of Air India, warm, ornate, richly coloured design with a regal Indian character. Strong red and white colour blocking is paired with deep maroon, saffron, aubergine and burnished gold. A small arched motif drawn from ornamental scalloped arches and a gold circular emblem repeats as a graphic mark, often with fine curved lines that suggest a sun or a gateway frame. Lettering is wide, clean, slightly rounded sans-serif in white on red or red on white, with italic touches. Surfaces are glossy lacquer and gold foil, with jewel-like borders, paisley, jali lattice and floral geometry as pattern. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Air India in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
