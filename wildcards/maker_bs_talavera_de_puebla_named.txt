In the style of Talavera de Puebla, thick creamy white glossy tin glaze painted by hand in cobalt blue, yellow, green, orange and black. Flowers, birds, vines, geometric borders and Moorish-inspired arabesques are laid down in bold brushwork, with slightly uneven edges, drips and pooling glaze. Colours stay bright and flat, edged with a thin dark outline, with white ground left between motifs. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Talavera de Puebla in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
