In the style of Roycroft Arts and Crafts Community, plain handmade rectilinear construction in oak and hammered copper. Wood has square, sturdy members, exposed joinery, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish with visible grain. Copper shows hammer marks, rivets, strap-like fittings and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. Graphics use thick handmade paper, red initials, woodcut-like borders, heavy black type and soft suede textures. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic.
