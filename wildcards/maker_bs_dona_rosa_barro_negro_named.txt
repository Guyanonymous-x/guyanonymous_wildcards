In the style of Dona Rosa Barro Negro, hand-built forms with a deep, mirror-like metallic black sheen. Shapes are formed by hand with no turning, so they are softly rounded and slightly asymmetrical, with tall proportions, round bellies, narrow necks and playful figure shapes. The surface is rubbed smooth with a stone before smoke firing, giving a silky gunmetal-to-jet shine with soft grey matte patches where the burnish is lighter. Decoration is restrained, with applied flowers, rosettes, twisted ropes, delicate pierced lacework and fine incised lines. Earthy, sculptural and quietly elegant, on plain pale backgrounds. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
