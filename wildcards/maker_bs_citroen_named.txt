Styled in a design language of inventive, quirky, avant-garde French design with a warm poster-art spirit. A double chevron emblem in two stacked inverted arrows, often in golden yellow on deep blue or black, repeats everywhere. Mid-century forms are streamlined, rounded and futuristic, with pods, smooth skins and low crouching curves. Palette of golden yellow, navy, vermilion, pale green, ivory, grey blue, sand and burnt orange. Materials include checked cloth, moulded plastic and pressed metal. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
