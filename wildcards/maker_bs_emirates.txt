Styled in a design language of lavish, polished desert-luxury. Gleaming white grounds meet vivid ruby red in confident fields. Accents use rich gold, champagne, deep burgundy and warm sand beige, with soft gradients and satin sheen. Surfaces feel premium, with polished lacquer, burnished metal, supple leather and fine woven fabric. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
