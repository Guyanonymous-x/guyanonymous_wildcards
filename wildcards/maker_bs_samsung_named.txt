In the style of Samsung, polished, bright, modern and effortlessly smooth. Thin slab forms with softly curved edges and nearly invisible borders, finished in glossy glass, mirror metal and satin aluminium. Palette is graphite black, cloud white, silver, champagne gold, lavender, mint and a signature deep corporate blue, with soft gradient sheens that shift like liquid light. Small round details stand in neat clusters as precise accents. Lettering is a wide, clean, evenly spaced sans-serif in capitals inside a flattened blue oval. Clean grid layouts, generous empty space, rounded corner containers and calm, reassuring geometry make everything feel refined, friendly and technically confident. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
