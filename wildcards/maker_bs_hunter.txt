Styled in a design language of polished British countryside practicality with a glossy, waterproof finish. Smooth, seamless, sleek rubber and coated surfaces have a high sheen, with a subtle matte bloom, in deep racing green, navy, black, olive and bright seasonal colours such as red, yellow and pink. Shapes are clean, rounded and streamlined with minimal seams and tidy moulded edges. The emblem is a bold red rectangular badge with thick white capital lettering, repeated in strict upright sans-serif type. Heritage touches include tweedy checks, waxed cotton, brass hardware, dark green boxes and thin white rules. Refined, tough, rain-ready, quietly classic and tidy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
