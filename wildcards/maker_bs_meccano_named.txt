In the style of Meccano, engineering construction kit look of perforated flat metal strips, girders and plates with evenly spaced round holes, bolted together with tiny hex nuts and bolts. Red and green enamel or zinc and brass finishes, with brass gears, flanged wheels and cranks. Graphics show an illustrated scene of a huge machine at work in muted red, green and cream, with ornate Edwardian serif lettering, banner panels, line drawings and numbered parts like an engineering manual.
