the Georgia Guidestones, a granite monument in rural Georgia that stood from 1980 until 2022 and drew many conspiracy legends. Four tall slabs of gray granite stand upright in a cross shape around a central slab on a hilltop in a pasture, topped by a flat capstone. The slabs are carved with lines of text in eight languages, the English face reading "MAINTAIN HUMANITY UNDER 500,000,000 IN PERPETUAL BALANCE WITH NATURE" on its first line and "GUIDE REPRODUCTION WISELY" on its second.
