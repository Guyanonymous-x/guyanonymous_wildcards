Styled in a design language of military-surplus design with a clean technical edge. Palette is olive green, sage, black, navy, sand and camouflage prints. Surfaces are glossy nylon, matte ripstop. Disciplined, functional, cold-war aviation heritage and understated. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
