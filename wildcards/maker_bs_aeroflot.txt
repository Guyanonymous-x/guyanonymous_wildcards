In the style of Aeroflot, a bare polished aluminium fuselage with a thick pale blue stripe and a white wing and tail emblem, a red flag on the tail with a gold hammer-and-sickle-style emblem set within two wings, in a heavy, bold form. Cyrillic lettering in condensed capitals, painted large on the side of the fuselage. Cabin in drab greens and greys, patterned curtains, and thick velour seats. Palette of silver, light blue, red and olive green. The look is Soviet utilitarian, solid, no-nonsense, with large Cyrillic lettering and a heavy winged emblem.
