Eight, the master or a monitor lighting the stove, which takes an hour to be worth anything
Nine, the bell, the lobby, the register, which is called by name and marked, and the first scripture lesson, which is timetabled first because an inspector may come for it
Ten thirty, the main teaching, arithmetic on slates with the ball frame at the front
Noon, dinner, which for most is bread carried from home and eaten in the lobby or outside, and for those who live near is at home
One thirty, the afternoon, needlework for the girls and often drill or object lessons
Three, the light going in winter
Four, the bell, the pegs, the yard
Any day, the attendance officer at the door, which is the event the register exists for
Harvest, half the benches empty and an entry in the log book about it
