Styled in a design language of sober, understated and travel-worn elegance. Surfaces are matte black, oilcloth-like and softly pebbled, with gently rounded corners, a single elastic closure band and a thin ribbon accent. Palette is black, cream and ivory, with bright flat accent colours such as vermilion, teal, sage green, mustard and navy used one at a time in solid fields. Sewn construction and visible stitching show honest craft. Neat, minimal, modern, with soft ivory tone. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
