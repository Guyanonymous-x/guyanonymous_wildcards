the last room on Earth, which is exactly how everybody who works in it describes it, a small enclosed chamber at the end of a swing arm high on a launch tower
sealed and filtered so the spacecraft hatch can be opened in clean air
pressed up against the vehicle with a flexible seal
a bench for the closeout crew
a rack of clean garments and bootees for anybody entering
a silhouetted tool board with a strict accounting
a hatch-closing kit with a calibrated wrench
a communications panel to the launch control team
a sign-in log
strap and cushion stowage for the crew's ingress
a vacuum cleaner
a mirror
a wall of accumulated stickers, and an arm that swings away in the last minutes before launch leaving the room empty and attached to nothing
