Styled in a design language of honest, approachable, everyday American mass-market design. Palette of deep royal blue and white with poppy red, turquoise, pale yellow, cream and dark green. Later years bring clean utilitarian graphics, plain horizontal bands. Materials include painted steel, enamel, chrome strip, vinyl, woodgrain and canvas. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
