Styled in a design language of French Art Nouveau layered glass, thick and soft, with acid-etched and wheel-carved decoration of thistles, poppies, irises, mountain landscapes and wintry scenes. Colours are mottled and graded from smoky orange to deep green, milky mist, powder blue and amber, with enamelled and gilded highlights. Naturalistic plant forms swell around bulbous bodies. Soft translucence, dreamy, rich, with a subdued glow like looking through frosted weather.
