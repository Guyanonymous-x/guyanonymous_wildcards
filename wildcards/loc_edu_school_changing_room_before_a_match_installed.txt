a room that is wet
muddy and crowded for forty minutes twice a week and empty for the rest of the time
built entirely for that forty minutes, so the floor is laid to fall toward a gully
there is no fabric anywhere except what is carried in, and every fitting is bolted rather than screwed; a boot scraper outside the door, which is the room's first line of defense and the detail that tells you the room is entered from grass; benches slatted rather than solid so water drains through and the floor can be hosed; coat hooks above every bench place at a spacing that defines the room's capacity; a shower bay behind a half wall with no doors and no curtains
open and communal, which is the standard arrangement and the one most changed in recent builds toward individual cubicles; a floor gully running the length of the shower bay; mechanical extraction that is always undersized; a door that opens outward and has no vision panel, which is one of the very few such doors in a school and is unavoidable here
