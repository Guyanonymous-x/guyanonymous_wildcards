the dress of the Abkhaz people of the Black Sea coast of the Caucasus, the men in their thirties to seventies in the long dark cherkeska, a fitted wool coat to the knees with rows of cartridge pockets sewn across the chest, a tall fleece hat, soft leather boots and a dagger at the belt
the old festival dress of Abkhazia, adult men in black or gray wool coats belted tight with narrow silver-studded belts and a bashlyk, a pointed hood of camel-colored wool with long ends wrapped around the neck, the women in long dark velvet dresses with fitted bodices and long white veils
the women in their twenties to sixties in ankle-length dresses of dark silk or velvet, close-fitting to the waist and closed down the front with silver clasps, a long tunic coat worn open over the dress, a large three-cornered shawl of white or cream over the head with its point hanging down the back
the dress of elders at a village sanctuary, long dark wool coats and tall black or white sheepskin hats for the men, felt cloaks over their shoulders, the older women in long black dresses and black headscarves knotted under the chin
