The most extreme lighting condition in this entire family, a building with no installed lighting whatsoever, lit entirely by what was carried in, so that strobes, lasers, a projector and a few work lights define the whole volume and the roof forty feet up is never seen at all, smoke holding every beam, a torch at the door, phone screens, and then the single most characteristic image of the record, which is daylight arriving through a roof light at five in the morning onto a floor still full of people, at which point the building becomes visible and the spell ends
