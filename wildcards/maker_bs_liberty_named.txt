Styled in a design language of English Aesthetic Movement and Arts and Crafts design with fine, silky, soft-handed cotton lawn and silk. Dense, small-scale floral prints of tiny ditsy blossoms, paisley, peacock feathers, curling tendrils and Japanese-inspired motifs are packed tightly, with hand-drawn irregularity. Palette is rich and slightly dusty, with peacock blue, plum, damson, sage, mustard, coral, rose pink and deep teal, often on dark or cream grounds. Colours are layered, blended and watercolour-soft. A refined, romantic, bohemian handcrafted feel with a delicate drape. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
