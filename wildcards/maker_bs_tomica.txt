In the style of Tomica, small diecast toy cars about the length of a finger sold in colourful window boxes. Each car is a metal body in one glossy solid enamel colour, such as white, red, yellow, light blue or metallic silver, with a plastic clear windscreen, silver painted bumpers and lamps, tiny printed badges, and a hinged door or boot that really opens. Wheels are chrome five spoke hubs on small black plastic tyres, sometimes wide racing style rims. Cars are mostly everyday Japanese saloons, hatchbacks, minivans, fire engines, ambulances, taxis and buses in their real livery with painted stripes and lettering. The box is a cardboard sleeve with a picture window, in the classic black background with yellow flaps, later red and white, with an illustrated action scene of the car on a road, white sans serif model name, and a number tab. Foreign cars use blue and white boxes with a small flag. Mood is tidy, bright, practical, collectable and a little retro.
