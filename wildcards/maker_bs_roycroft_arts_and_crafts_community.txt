Styled in a design language of plain handmade rectilinear construction in oak and hammered copper. Wood has square, sturdy members, exposed joinery, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish with visible grain. Copper shows hammer marks, rivets, strap-like fittings and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
