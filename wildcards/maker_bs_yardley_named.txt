In the style of Yardley, soft, delicate English drugstore elegance from the 1930s to 1960s. A pale lilac and white palette with violet and purple printing, gold touches and cream surfaces. Woodcut-style vignettes of flower sellers in flowing curved compositions, copperplate script lettering and ornate border lines. Pastel metal accents and thick printed paper textures. Floral motifs and fine line illustration keep everything gentle. Mood is fragrant, ladylike, nostalgic, dainty and refined, with subtle floral romance.
