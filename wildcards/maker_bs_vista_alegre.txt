Styled in a design language of elegant, formal, light and slightly romantic Portuguese porcelain. Thin, bright white hard-paste body with a smooth shiny glaze, decorated in aristocratic traditions with gold rims, fine landscape vignettes in sepia or blue, delicate floral sprays, rose garlands, medallions, heraldic crests, Greek-key borders and raised relief. Forms have scrolling handles, high feet and flower-shaped finials. Modern versions turn to bold graphic prints, hand-painted strokes in cobalt, black or coral and sculptural art editions. Traditional palette is white, gold, navy, rose pink and sage green. Tiny gilt crowns and symmetrical arrangements.
