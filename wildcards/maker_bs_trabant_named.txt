Styled in a design language of tiny, boxy, austere, humble and slightly smoky East German design. A flat top surface, pressed plastic panels, flat sides, square corners with slight rounding, round forms and small chrome edges. Pastel pale blue, beige, sage green, orange and white with a faded matt finish. Pressed plastic panels and a thin faded look give honest austerity. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
