The best light in the attic by a factor of four, and the only daylight on that floor anybody ever lay and looked at, usually a dormer and sometimes a gable window, with a blind that can be drawn against the light and a curtain that can be drawn against the dark, which between them give the occupant the one thing nobody else on that floor has, which is control over their own light
The fire, giving a low warm light in the evening and the only firelight in the attic
One candle on the bedside table, not rationed; and the characteristic condition of the room, which is a person in bed in the afternoon with a blind half down, a fire in, a kettle on a trivet
