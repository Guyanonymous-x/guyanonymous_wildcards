Styled in a design language of cinematic, emotionally grounded realism with photoreal faces that carry the performance. Ruined or overgrown settings with ivy, rust and humid greenery, warm sun-dappled light, shallow depth of field and floating dust in the air. Everything has a worn, lived-in feel with scars, grime, scuffs and practical clothing. Colour is graded in teal and orange. Compositions feel like blockbuster stills, with clean, minimal white lettering. Human, tense, adventurous and tender, with a filmic polish.
