Styled in a design language of glamorous Italian mid-century luxury expressed in finish and material. Deep red-brown mahogany veneer polished to a mirror gloss, with caramel and honey striped wood inlay laid in long parallel bands. Bright polished chrome trim, slim white or cream painted lines and padded leather in cream, white and fiery red, lifted by sudden aqua turquoise accents. Smooth, long, tapering, streamlined silhouettes with soft rounded ends and sleek curved edges. Small enamel flag-colour details in green, white and red, a refined upright emblem and flowing. Handmade, sensual, sleek, sunlit and richly refined. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
