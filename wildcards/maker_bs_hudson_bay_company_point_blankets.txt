Styled in a design language of thick, heavy wool in a creamy off-white field with a few bold, flat, evenly spaced stripes near the edges, in green, red, yellow and indigo blue, with green and red dominating. Small black tick marks add a rhythmic accent. The surface is dense, napped and felted, feeling rustic, warm and hearty, with plainly finished edges and no further decoration. The overall look evokes trading posts, cabins and snow.
