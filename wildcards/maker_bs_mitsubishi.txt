Styled in a design language of tough, bold, motorsport-ready Japanese design built on a symmetrical emblem of three red diamonds meeting at their points to form a sharp triangular mark. It is always flat, solid and graphic in scarlet red on white or reversed in white or chrome on black. Shapes are angular, faceted and rugged, with diamond and rhombus cut-outs, aggressive creases and slanted edges. Palette is hot red, white, black, silver and gunmetal grey, with occasional rally-style blue and yellow stripes. Finishes are matte black, brushed steel and glossy enamel. Technical sport decals, chevrons and diamond repeats make a strong, corporate, geometric pattern. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
