Large overlapping close-up faces with intense theatrical expressions, hand-painted or digitally composited layering, glowing gold and fiery red accents, outline and drop shadow. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
