Styled in a design language of safe, calm, rational Scandinavian design. A circle with an arrow pointing up and to the right, a diagonal band across it, in plain silver or flat white, serves as the signature mark, with wide, evenly spaced. Forms are boxy, upright, sturdy and honest, later softened into clean, minimal, flowing shapes with a quiet surface. Palette of cool pale blue, Nordic grey, pure white, fog, forest green, mustard, beige and dark navy. Materials include light wood, soft wool and plaid cloth, pale leather, matte metal and frosted glass. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
