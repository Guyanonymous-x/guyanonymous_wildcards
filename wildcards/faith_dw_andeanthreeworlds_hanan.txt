Hanan Pacha fills the top of the painting, the gold disc of Inti the sun with a face, the silver disc of Mama Killa the moon, the bright star of Venus and a great Andean condor with black wings and a white collar soaring among them
the upper world glows above the clouds, the Milky Way flowing across it as a river of stars with the dark shapes of the llama, the fox and the toad seen in its dust clouds
a rainbow arches over the upper world, and the lightning god Illapa stands within it, a man in his forties in a gold tunic and a sling raised over his head
