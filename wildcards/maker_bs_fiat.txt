In the style of Fiat in the 1950s to 1970s, a tiny, round and cheerful Italian city car, with a bulbous egg-shaped body, a short nose, tiny bug-like headlamps, and a flat or lightly curved roof that is sometimes rolled open as a canvas top. A small round or rectangular badge on the nose, thin chrome bumpers, doors hinged at the rear in the oldest models, and a simple tail with a small window. Small steel wheels with chrome hubcaps. Paint in pastel yellow, powder blue, light green, cream, cherry red and white. Interior has minimal bare metal dash painted body colour, a single round speedometer, thin-rimmed wheel, and simple vinyl or fabric seats. Mood is playful, tiny, post-war cheerful.
