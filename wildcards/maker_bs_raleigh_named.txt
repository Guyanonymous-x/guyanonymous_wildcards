Styled in a design language of English heritage craft with an ornamental metal-badge tradition. Deep gloss green, black, maroon and cream enamel is lined with hand-painted gold pinstripes. Polished brass, patinated copper, riveted tan leather and chrome details give a refined, tidy, old-world, countryside gentleman feel with fine hand-finished decorative attention. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
