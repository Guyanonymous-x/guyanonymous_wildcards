__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of witty, surreal, cheeky Italian pop design. Recurring motifs are a solid red heart, a pair of mirrored question marks inside a circle, a teddy-bear silhouette drawn in simple black and white line, and oversized playful icons. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
