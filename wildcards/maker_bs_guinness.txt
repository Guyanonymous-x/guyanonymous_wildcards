In the style of Guinness, near-black stout in a straight tulip pint glass crowned by a creamy tan-white head. Labels are cream or off-white ovals and rectangles with a gold harp emblem in profile at the centre, a gold border and thin black serif capitals. Dark glossy bottles with a gold neck foil and cream label, black cans with a gold harp and a widget rattle, black and gold palette. Vintage poster art uses flat bright gouache illustrations of toucans, zoo animals and workmen on yellow, blue and red backgrounds with chunky hand-drawn lettering. Tone is heritage, solid, Irish, with gold-on-black embossing and a restrained serif layout.
