Styled in a design language of auspicious, celebratory and traditional Chinese. A bright festival red surface covered in golden brush calligraphy, with a golden border of traditional ornamental patterns and gold-coloured accents. A large central bold brush script sits on a plain, slightly glossy red field. Palette is festival red, gold and white. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
