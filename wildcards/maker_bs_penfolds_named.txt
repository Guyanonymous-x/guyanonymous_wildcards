Styled in a design language of classic, established, serious heritage luxury. Presentation is traditional and restrained, with deep bottle green, deep red and black, and warm cream or off-white framed by thin black linework, oval or shield-shaped frames. Palette is cream, burgundy, black, gold and green. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
