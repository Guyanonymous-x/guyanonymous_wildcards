Styled in a design language of witty, surreal, cheeky pop design with oversized graphic elements, heart shapes, question marks, peace signs, cartoon bears and balloon prints. Playful tailored shapes with giant buttons, fake dollar signs, tape measure and safety-pin trims, forms mimicking everyday objects, and black and white stripes against bright red, yellow and pink. Bold lettering across surfaces, couture cut with ironic parody. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
