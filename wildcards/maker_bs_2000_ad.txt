Styled in a design language of British sci-fi grit with hyper-detailed pen and ink, dystopian megacities of towering tenements and neon, helmeted enforcers in spiked armour, robots and aliens. Painted work with metallic airbrush gloss in lurid red, green and electric blue, black-and-white linework with dense hatching, jagged sound effects, a bold angular logo style and a loud, punky, sardonic and violent futuristic attitude. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
