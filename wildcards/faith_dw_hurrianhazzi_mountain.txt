Mount Hazzi rises in one great bare cone of white limestone straight from the sea, its upper slopes streaked with storm cloud and its foot dark with oak and pine
the mountain stands as a god itself, a giant bearded man in his sixties carved as a peak, his skirt marked with the rows of scales that stand for mountain slopes, bending to carry Teshub on his shoulders
the peak of Hazzi is crowned with a stone altar and a cedar post, clouds boiling around the summit and lightning running down its ridges to the shore
