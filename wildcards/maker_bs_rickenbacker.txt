In the style of Rickenbacker, electric guitars and basses with a distinctive thin semi-acoustic body, with a glossy red, fireglo sunburst, jetglo black, mapleglo natural or white finish and white plastic binding. Features include a slotted headstock, a sharply pointed cutaway horn on one side, a stylish chrome bridge cover, triangular fretboard inlays, a chrome toaster-shaped pickup and a white pickguard with a tapered contour. Some models show a rounded cutaway with a curvy body edge and a rear control panel with tiny knobs. The mood is jangly, mod, 1960s British Invasion and psychedelic.
