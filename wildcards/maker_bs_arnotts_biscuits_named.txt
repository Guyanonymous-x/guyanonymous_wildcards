In the style of Arnotts Biscuits, homely, nostalgic, cheerful family warmth. A mark in old engraving style, a brightly coloured bird in red, yellow, green and blue inside a red oval or circle. Colour carries meaning, with red as the base and yellow, blue or green panels, bold white rounded sans-serif titles, a swirl or ribbon behind, and large appetising photography. Shapes are distinctive and patterned, with fine diagonal ridges, neat grids of small holes, simple rounded characters and frosted rounds. Warm, friendly, lunchbox-and-kitchen-table feeling.
