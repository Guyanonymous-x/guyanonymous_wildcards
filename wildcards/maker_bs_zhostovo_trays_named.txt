Styled in a design language of a glossy black lacquer ground, sometimes red, blue, green or white, carrying a lush bouquet of large garden and wild flowers loosely tossed with leaves, buds and fruit. Painting is quick and full-brushed with soft shaded modelling, in oil colours of deep crimson, pink, orange, yellow, blue and fresh green against the dark. A smaller scatter of blossoms and berries surrounds the main bunch. Edges are trimmed with gold or silver scrolls, dots and little leaf ornaments. The finish is polished to a mirror shine under many layers of lacquer. Lush, sumptuous, festive and warm. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
