the dress of the Mentawai people of the islands off western Sumatra in Indonesia, lean adult men in their twenties to sixties bare but for a kabit, a long strip of soft pale bark cloth wound between the legs and around the waist, strands of red, yellow and green glass beads at the neck and red hibiscus flowers tucked into the hair
the sikerei, the shaman healers, with fine blue-black tattoo lines covering the chest, back, arms and legs in long curves and dots, bead headbands over long black hair tied back, flowers and leaves in the hair, bare bodies above bark loincloths
adult women in short skirts of shredded banana leaf and bark cloth, bare above the waist in the old form, many strings of tiny glass beads on the neck and chest, tattoo lines down the arms and across the shoulders, a band of leaves on the head
present-day dress at a rite, the men still in bark loincloths with bead necklaces and fresh leaves at the brow, the women in sarongs of printed cloth and short blouses, their arms and hands marked with old tattoos
