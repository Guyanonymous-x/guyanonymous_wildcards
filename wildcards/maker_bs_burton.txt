Styled in a design language of bold alpine-rebel graphics built on stark black and white. Palette is black, white and grey, then clashing neon pink, acid green, orange, cyan and purple in colour-blocked panels. Surfaces are glossy and slick with graphic prints of skulls, flames, camouflage, geometric shards and tropical foliage, plus matte technical nylon and fleece. Forms are loose, oversized and chunky with sharp angular cuts. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
