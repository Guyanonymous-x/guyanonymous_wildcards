In the style of Moog Music, warm, handcrafted, scientific vintage modernism. Bold black and white identity built from a wide, rounded, lowercase wordmark whose big circular letters are linked by a single continuous looping stroke, with a small slanted cut inside each round. Solid dark walnut and oiled hardwood paired with matte black and brushed silver panels, white printed labels in a clear sans-serif, and thin white lines that group areas together. Accents of orange, red and cream appear sparingly. Forms are slanted, wedge-like and sturdy with softly eased corners, exposed joinery, and a laboratory-meets-furniture honesty. Monochrome stamped badges and tidy technical diagrams in black on white. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Moog Music in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
