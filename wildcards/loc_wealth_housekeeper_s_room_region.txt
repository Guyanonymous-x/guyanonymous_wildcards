the English housekeeper's room as the type, universal in houses of any size from about 1700
the Scottish version, identical and often with a larger still-room function
the Irish version, identical
the French equivalent, the lingerie and the office of the femme de charge, which splits the linen and the stores into two rooms
the German Wirtschafterinnenzimmer
the Russian estate equivalent, where the same post exists with the same keys and a far larger staff
the American Gilded Age housekeeper's room, which imports the English arrangement and the English housekeeper with it; and the Anglo-Indian household, where the function is split between a memsahib keeping her own keys and a khansama or a butler holding the stores, which is a materially different distribution of authority and is worth its own record
