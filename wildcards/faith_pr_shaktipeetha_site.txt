at the Kalighat Temple in Kolkata in India, a temple with a curved sloping roof painted white with red and blue bands, the goddess a tall black stone face with three large eyes and a long gold tongue, decked in red silk and hibiscus garlands, the crowd Bengali women and men in their twenties to eighties, the women in red-bordered white saris with red powder in the parting of the hair, the men in white kurtas
at the Jwalamukhi Temple in the hills of Himachal Pradesh in India, a gilded dome above a stone courtyard, the goddess small blue and yellow flames burning steadily from cracks in the bare rock of the shrine floor, the crowd pilgrims in their thirties to eighties, the women in bright saris and the men in shirts and trousers with red scarves tied around their heads
