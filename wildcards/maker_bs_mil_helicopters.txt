Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy, utilitarian and Cold War. Large, chunky forms with bulbous pod-like volumes, big round windows, a long tapering rear, massive repeating blades on a thick hub, and exposed machinery in boxy housings. Short stubby supports. Colour is plain, either military olive-drab and matte green with stencil markings, or white and sky blue with a red star or winged emblem and a broad blue or red stripe, with large black lettering and a code. Riveted seams, patches, rows of screws and hatches look hardworking and slightly weathered.
