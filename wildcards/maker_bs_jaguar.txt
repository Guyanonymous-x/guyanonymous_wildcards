Styled in a design language of sleek, feline and elegant British speed. Long, low, flowing forms with sensuous curves that taper gracefully and pull forward like a muscle about to spring. A leaping cat and a growling cat face serve as recurring emblems, often in polished metal or soft gold on a clean field. Palette of British racing green, cream, signal red and silver, with a modern pastel side of dusky pink, warm coral and bright gold against crisp white. Delicate chrome, polished wood and supple red or tan leather in vintage mode, while modern mode uses wide-spaced lowercase letters, thin monogram lines and bold flat colour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
