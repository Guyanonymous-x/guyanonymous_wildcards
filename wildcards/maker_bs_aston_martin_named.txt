In the style of Aston Martin, lean, refined and quietly aggressive British luxury. Taut, flowing surfaces with a long, low stance and a crisp rising shoulder line. A spread-winged emblem in brushed silver or enamel sits as the signature mark, set with wide capital lettering in fine thin strokes. Palette of racing green, silver birch, midnight blue, gunmetal and warm tan, with lime and electric teal as sporting accents. Materials include hand-stitched quilted leather in rich chestnut, polished glass, fine carbon weave, satin aluminium and soft suede. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
