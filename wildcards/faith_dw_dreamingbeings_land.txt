across the land the Rainbow Serpent winds through the country as a vast snake of many colored bands, its body forming the creek beds and the low red ridges where it traveled
across the red sand the tracks of the Kangaroo ancestor run as lines of paired footprints between camps, and at their end he rests in the land as a great boulder of red rock under a desert oak
the Seven Sisters ancestors, women in their twenties to their forties, run across the desert from the man who chases them, leaving the rockholes and hills where they camped and their footprints in the sand
