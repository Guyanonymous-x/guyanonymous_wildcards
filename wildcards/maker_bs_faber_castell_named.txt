In the style of Faber-Castell, a deep dark-green lacquer finish with gold hot-foil stamped lettering and a double-knight emblem. Forms are hexagonal or round, with a smoothly dipped end in colour and fine grading marks. Surfaces are dark green, dark brown or black with gold lettering, with rows of items aligned in gradients. Luxury versions use polished wood with silver or platinum-plated fittings and knurled details. The mood is classic, scholarly, European and craftsmanlike. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
