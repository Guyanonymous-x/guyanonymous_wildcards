English almshouses, of which around seventeen hundred charities still operate some thirty-five thousand dwellings, which is the largest and oldest such system anywhere and includes foundations continuously occupied since the twelfth century
Scottish and Welsh equivalents
the Dutch *hofje*, which is the direct and beautiful counterpart and survives in numbers in Haarlem, Leiden and Amsterdam
the Flemish *begijnhof*, which housed lay religious women and is a related courtyard form
the German *Fuggerei* in Augsburg, founded in 1521 and still letting at its original nominal rent, which is the most famous single example in Europe
French *hospices* and *maisons-Dieu*; and the modern sheltered housing scheme, which is the same idea under a different legal and financial structure
