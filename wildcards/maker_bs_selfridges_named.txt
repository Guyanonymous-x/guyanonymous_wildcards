In the style of Selfridges, theatrical, showy retail-as-entertainment. Bold flat yellow fields with a plain black sans-serif capital wordmark, and bright, plain signage. Classical stonework blends with modern white displays. Palette is yellow, black, white and stone. Mood is confident, playful, daring and instantly recognisable, pairing heavy classical grandeur with flat, graphic modern colour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
