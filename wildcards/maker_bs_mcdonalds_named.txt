In the style of McDonalds, bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds. Lettering is soft, chunky, rounded sans-serif in white or yellow. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words McDonalds in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
