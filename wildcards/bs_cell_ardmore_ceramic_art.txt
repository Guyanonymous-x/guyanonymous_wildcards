__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of witty, lush, whimsical, tropical and highly decorative hand sculpted ceramics. Exuberant, playful forms covered with sculpted wild animals such as big cats, striped grazers, monkeys, snakes and birds that climb, perch and curl around the shapes. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
