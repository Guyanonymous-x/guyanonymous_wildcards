In the style of Fauchon, shiny hot-pink and black packaging with gold, in rectangular boxes with a thick laminated finish. Stores of black and pink with a Parisian window display of luxury delicacies, cakes lined in rows, and tall cylindrical tins. Logotype in thin white or gold serif capitals within a rich black panel, ribbons in fuchsia pink and cream. Palette of fuchsia pink, black, gold and white. The look is glossy, high-fashion French delicatessen, striking contrasts, sleek geometry, a little theatrical.
