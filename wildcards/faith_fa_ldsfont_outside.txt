the Salt Lake Temple in Utah, gray granite with six pointed spires and round windows, the gilded angel on the tallest middle spire of the east end, and high on the east front a stone panel with gilded capitals reading "HOLINESS TO THE LORD THE HOUSE OF THE LORD"
the Washington DC Temple in Maryland, a tall windowless block of white marble with six slim golden spires rising straight out of a forest of oak and maple, the gilded angel on the highest spire, and beside its doors bronze capitals reading "HOLINESS TO THE LORD THE HOUSE OF THE LORD"
the Cardston Alberta Temple in western Canada, a block of pale gray granite stepped inward to a low pyramid roof in place of spires, standing on a grassy rise above a small prairie town with the Rocky Mountains on the skyline, and over its entrance cut capitals reading "HOLINESS TO THE LORD THE HOUSE OF THE LORD"
the Laie Hawaii Temple on the north shore of the island of Oahu, a gleaming white flat-topped temple with carved bands of figures under its roof line, at the head of a long stair of reflecting pools lined with royal palms, green cliffs behind it, and by its door gilded capitals reading "HOLINESS TO THE LORD THE HOUSE OF THE LORD"
the Manti Utah Temple, built of cream-colored stone on a hill above a farming valley, a long building with a tower at each end capped by a rounded gray roof, wide lawns falling away below it, and on its east tower a stone panel with dark capitals reading "HOLINESS TO THE LORD THE HOUSE OF THE LORD"
