Styled in a design language of classic, understated American Ivy League polish. Fabrics are navy flannel, grey worsted, camel wool, oxford cloth, blue and white seersucker, madras plaid, tartan and cable-knit wool in muted tones of navy, burgundy, forest green, camel, cream and pale blue. Signature surface patterns are diagonal repp stripes in bold alternating colour bands, regimental stripes, fine pinstripes, windowpane checks and bold awning stripes, plus small repeating crests. The emblem is a golden fleece hanging within an oval, embroidered or printed in gold and navy. Details are mother-of-pearl, brass and tidy contrast piping. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
