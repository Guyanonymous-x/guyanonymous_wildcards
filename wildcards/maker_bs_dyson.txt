Styled in a design language of engineered, futuristic, glossy and rather industrial. Clear transparent plastic reveals inner structure against bright coloured parts in fuchsia, purple, yellow, nickel grey and iron. Satin nickel and polished metal finishes meet deep navy and black matte surfaces. Shapes are tubular, smoothly curved and ribbed, with exposed engineering, visible fixings and large chunky rounded elements. Thin glossy loops and tall tapering forms replace conventional silhouettes. Colour is used as bold coded blocks, with a vivid pink or yellow against cool grey. Clinical, precise and boldly colourful. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
