__bs_subject_packaging__ styled in a design language of ornate, quirky Victorian gentleman-perfumer elegance. Cut-crystal and clear glass with faceted details are finished with silken ribbon, tassels and gilt trim. Surfaces are heavy cream, deep pine green, oxblood red and inky black with thin gold borders. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
