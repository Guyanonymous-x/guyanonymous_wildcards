Styled in a design language of forms built from repeating triangular peaks like a row of mountains, with sawtooth silhouettes and sloping sides. Warm honey-gold and cream-yellow fields carry deep blue bands, brown and dark blue serif and rounded sans lettering, and white snow-capped highlights. A mountain silhouette emblem hides a secret figure in its negative space. Silver sheen, light embossing and clean pyramid repetition give a tidy, geometric rhythm. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
