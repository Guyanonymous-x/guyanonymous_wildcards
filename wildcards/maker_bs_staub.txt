Styled in a design language of French cast iron with matte black enamel interior and darkly glossy, speckled exterior in grenadine red, deep blue, graphite and basil green. Heavy rounded forms with textured domed tops crowned by a small brass or nickel animal-shaped finial, such as a cockerel, cat or pumpkin, and chunky vegetable shapes. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
