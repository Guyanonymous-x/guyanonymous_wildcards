the homeland lies far back among high limestone mountains of southern China, villages of wooden houses with steep roofs of thatch or gray tile on the slopes, terraced fields of rice and corn stepping down to a river
the old homeland spreads as a high land of cold mist and pine forest, fields of hemp and buckwheat on the slopes and log houses with smoke rising from their roofs, the ancestors' village at the head of the valley
the land of the ancestors opens beyond the last mountain, a bright valley where every house has its spirit altar of white paper on the wall above a shelf of offerings, and the paths between the houses are lined with bamboo
