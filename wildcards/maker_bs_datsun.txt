Styled in a design language of cheerful, honest. Palette is primary red, royal blue, cream white, sunny yellow, mustard and orange with warm off-white paper tones, often as flat colour blocks with thick stripes. Shapes are rounded, compact and friendly with small shields. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
