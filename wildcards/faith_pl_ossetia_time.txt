in summer, the slopes green, steep meadows thick with tall grass and blue, yellow and white wild flowers, the trees in full dark leaf, white clouds building over the ridges and the snow shrunk back to the highest peaks
in fall, the trees red and gold, the leaves turned copper, rust and bright yellow, fallen leaves drifted against the shrine walls, the grass faded to straw and the first fresh snow dusting the ridges above
in winter, snow on the shrine, a thick white cap on its roof and on the heaped horns, the boughs outlined in white, a single trodden path leading through deep drifts to the door, and the whole valley pale under a low gray sky
