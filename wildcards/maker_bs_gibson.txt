In the style of Gibson, American electric and acoustic guitars. Carved arched-top bodies with a rich, glossy cherry red or golden sunburst finish, cream binding around the edges, and a flamed maple top. Bold mother-of-pearl block or trapezoid inlays on a dark rosewood fingerboard, gold-plated hardware, twin chrome-covered humbucking pickups, four gold barrel knobs, and a swept-back headstock with a pearl inlay logo and crown shape. Mood is rich, warm, heavy and luxurious.
