below lies the Shaker village with its long white dwelling house, a red barn, a round stone barn and a white meetinghouse with a gambrel roof, all set among square green fields and straight fences
the village street runs straight between plain wooden buildings painted white, yellow and red, each with two front doors side by side, one for the brothers and one for the sisters, and tidy herb gardens in front
inside the meetinghouse the plain wooden floor shines, the white walls are lined with rows of wooden pegs where coats, hats and chairs hang, and tall windows let in the light from the fields
around the village spread orchards of apple trees, a pond, a garden of medicinal herbs in neat beds and low stone walls running up the green hills toward the woods
