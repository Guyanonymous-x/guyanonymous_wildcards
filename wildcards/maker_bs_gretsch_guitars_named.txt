Gold-plated and chrome accents gleam beside cream and black edge stripes. Emblems include a circular badge with a stylised letter G and a stylised V shape, with oversized decorative cut-outs. Details are bold stripes, rows of bright studs, striped and sparkle edging, and generous proportions. Palette is orange, gold, cream, black and red. Loud, showy and rockabilly cool. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
