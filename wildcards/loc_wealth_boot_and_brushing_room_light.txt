Poor and wrong for the work, which is the room's standing grievance, one small high window, often facing a yard or a wall and sometimes borrowed from a corridor, giving a weak gray light that falls on the upper half of the room while all the work is done on dark brown objects at bench height and below
A candle, then a lamp, then a bulb, each mounted on a wall rather than over the bench
The consequence that a boot is polished by feel and judged by carrying it to the doorway and holding it up to the daylight, which is what everybody did and is why there is a worn patch on the floor at the door
The stove's open ash door giving a low red light; and the room's one good light being the open outside door on a summer morning, which is also when the room is least needed
