Styled in a design language of tough, thin, urban industrial design pressed from single sheets of steel. Simple tapered lines, rounded pressed curves, perforations used as the only pattern, and reinforced edges. Repeatable, economical forms with a visibly utilitarian build. Finish is matte lacquer, powder-coat or galvanised raw zinc in red, dark green, yellow, black, grey and silver, with dents, scuffs and a worn patina. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
