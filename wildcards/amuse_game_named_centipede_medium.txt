a 1981 arcade cabinet of a mushroom field bug shooter, with "CENTIPEDE" on its lit marquee at the top. It is an upright cabinet with a painted fantasy scene of a giant green and red centipede rearing over an elf in a forest, and a large trackball set in the panel. Its screen shows a segmented centipede winding down through a field of pastel mushrooms, a bouncing spider and a little wand shooter at the bottom.
