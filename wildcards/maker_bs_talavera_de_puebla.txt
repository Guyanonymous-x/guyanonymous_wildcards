Styled in a design language of thick creamy white glossy tin glaze painted by hand in cobalt blue, yellow, green, orange and black. Flowers, birds, vines, geometric borders and Moorish-inspired arabesques are laid down in bold brushwork, with slightly uneven edges, drips and pooling glaze. Colours stay bright and flat, edged with a thin dark outline, with white ground left between motifs. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
