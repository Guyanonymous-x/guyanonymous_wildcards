Styled in a design language of calm, earthy, refined and architectural natural-wool tapestry. Flat-woven very tightly and finely in a rectangular format with a dense, almost smooth surface. Colours come only from undyed wool carded and blended, so the palette is creamy white, light and dark grey, tan, camel, chocolate brown and jet black, with no bright dye. The design is a symmetrical central geometric field with diamonds, stepped zigzags, concentric rectangles and terraced crosses, framed by a plain border and a crisp inner outline, with small tapering corner details. Balanced, precise and optical, with crisp straight edges. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
