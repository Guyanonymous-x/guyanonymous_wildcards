Styled in a design language of beige home computers and radios, from walnut-cased radios with a round dial to 1990s tower computers in cream plastic with a stepped front panel, a pair of drive bays, an angled power switch and a monitor of matching tone. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
