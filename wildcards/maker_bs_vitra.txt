Styled in a design language of Swiss and German design with a witty, collectible sensibility. Shapes are playful and sculptural, with rounded plastic, smooth lacquer, polished and powder coated metal, warm oak and walnut, and glossy ceramic. Palette combines soft pastels such as blush pink, pale blue and mint with strong primaries, deep forest green, black and bright white. Surfaces include bold geometric prints, checkerboards, dots and folk-inspired abstract motifs in flat colour. Objects feel like small sculptures, with miniature scale. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
