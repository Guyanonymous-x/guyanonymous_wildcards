Styled in a design language of deconstructed avant-garde design with asymmetric, deliberately distorted silhouettes, bulbous padded humps, oversized stiff shapes, exposed raw seams, unfinished frayed edges, holes, inside-out construction and layered mismatched panels. Dominant colour is severe black, then stark white, grey and a single shock of red, with occasional gingham, polka dot, tartan or flat pop prints. Materials are felt, crumpled cotton, boiled and distressed knits, lace, patent and cheap-looking synthetics treated as luxury. A simple, rounded, cartoon-like red heart emblem with two small oval eyes appears in black, red, green or white, above plain, bold, spaced. Rebellious, intellectual and playfully strange, with beauty found in imperfection. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
