Styled in a design language of solid wood steam-bent into continuous flowing curves, with slender round-section members forming loops, circles and S-scrolls. Structures are light, airy and sculptural, with delicate splayed supports joined by a ring brace, and surfaces of woven cane or pressed perforated wood. Finish is dark mahogany brown, walnut or ebonised black in satin lacquer, with visible screw heads and small metal collars at joints. Later work uses bent chrome tube with cane panels and black leather. Mass-produced, elegant, coffeehouse mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
