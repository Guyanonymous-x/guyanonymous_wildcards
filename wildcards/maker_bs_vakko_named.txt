Styled in a design language of large square twill silk printed with detailed painterly scenes, tile patterns, tulips, carnations, flower patterns, arabesques, waterside views and maritime and horse motifs, finished with hand-rolled edges. Colours are saturated cobalt, turquoise, coral red, emerald, gold and ivory, in dense multi-colour compositions bordered by a decorative frame. Tailoring is polished, with furs, silk, boxy jackets and long coats. Elegant, cosmopolitan, orientalist-meets-modern mood, with rich satin sheen and gold chain edges. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
