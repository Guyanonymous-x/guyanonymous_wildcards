In the style of Indomie, Indonesian instant fried noodle packaging. The pack is a flat glossy pouch in a bright red, orange and yellow gradient with white and gold sparkle, a big appetising photograph of stir fried noodles with a fried egg, shredded chicken, cucumber slices, tomato and a scattering of fried shallots, plus a drizzle of dark sweet soy sauce, on a white or wooden plate. The name appears in thick, rounded, red and white lettering with a heavy black outline and a small flame or chilli. Variant colours cover blue for soup, green for chilli, black for barbecue, each with a dish photo. Inside are small sachets in silver foil. Cup versions have a tall plastic cup with the same artwork. Lifestyle images show steaming bowls on a street stall counter with wooden stools and a boiling pot. Mood is hot, spicy, glossy, hungry, everyday and exuberant.
