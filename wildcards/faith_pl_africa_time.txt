in the dry season, harmattan haze, the dusty pale haze from the Sahara, making the sun a white disc, the sky a flat milky tan from edge to edge, distant trees faded to gray shapes, a film of fine dust on every leaf and roof and the grass bleached pale yellow
in the rainy season, the forest green and dripping, every leaf glossy and beaded with water, the earth dark red and puddled, tall grass and new vines pushing up along the paths, heavy gray clouds piled low over the treetops and thin mist hanging between the trunks
at dusk, smoke from cooking fires drifting over the trees, thin blue-gray layers of smoke flattening above the roofs and the dark treetops, the sky fading from orange near the horizon to deep violet overhead, small points of firelight glowing in the yards
