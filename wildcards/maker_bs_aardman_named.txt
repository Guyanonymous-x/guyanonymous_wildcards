Styled in a design language of hand-sculpted modelling clay forms with soft lumpy volumes, visible thumbprints and tool marks, and a slightly matte waxy skin. Shapes are squat, rounded and exaggerated, with big expressive eyes, wide toothy grins and chunky limbs, all deliberately a little wonky. Palette is warm and earthy with oatmeal beige, terracotta orange, mustard, moss green, dusty blue and off-white, plus the occasional vivid candy colour. Surfaces carry gentle crumpled seams and joins that celebrate handmade imperfection. Patterns lean on homely checks, florals and stripes in muted tones. Overall a cosy, witty, tactile and charmingly dishevelled craft character. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
