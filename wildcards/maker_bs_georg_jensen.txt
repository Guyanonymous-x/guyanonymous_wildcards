In the style of Georg Jensen, Danish sterling silver in softly hammered, organic sculptural forms, with smooth rounded bulbous bodies, grape clusters, berries, blossoms and curling leaves, and later streamlined modernist plain silver with sensuous curves. Flatware with fluid, bulging handles, teapots with carved ebony or ivory-coloured knobs and handles, bowls with openwork stems, brooches and pendants with cabochon stones. Gleaming matte silver, restrained, elegant and handcrafted Scandinavian luxury.
