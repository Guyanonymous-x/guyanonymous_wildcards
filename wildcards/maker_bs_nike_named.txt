In the style of Nike, athletic motion and defiant confidence. Forms are streamlined and aerodynamic, with sweeping check-like curves, sharp tapered cuts and layered panels that suggest speed. Palette is stark black, white and grey with a single electric accent such as volt yellow, hot orange or crimson, and bold colour-blocked contrasts. Technical mesh, foam, rubber and glossy synthetic skins show engineered detail. Lettering is heavy, tightly set, italic or condensed sans-serif, often in huge scale. Graphics and photography are high-contrast, gritty and dramatic, with sweat, spotlights and cropped close action.
