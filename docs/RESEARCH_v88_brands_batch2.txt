Research v88 brands batch 2 - Bing image grids via built-in browser, 1-2 per brand (screenshots 800x445). Old text rewritten as product-neutral essence. Mini, Volkswagen and BMW re-edited afterwards to remove vehicle-bound wording.
Rolls-Royce: Bing grids showed silver winged-figure ornaments on tall upright grilles, and dim warm interiors with cream/tan/oxblood leather, glossy burr wood, starlight headliner. Added starlight, double-R monogram, dark reverent mood.
Mini: Grid dominated by flag-print roofs, bonnet stripes, contrast two-tone roofs in red/blue/black; round chrome details. Added customisation, flag roof graphic, bright urban mood.
Land Rover: Grids showed vintage print ads in sand/ochre, expedition shots in dust/mud, boxy modern interiors with exposed structure, green oval badge. Added modern utilitarian interior and badge.
Volkswagen: Grid showed flat illustrated vintage posters in blue/teal/purple/coral, split two-tone vans, round blue badge. Added poster-art and dry humour, expanded era to present.
BMW: Grid was almost all tricolour light blue/dark blue/red stripes on black twin-slot grilles; added tricolour stripes, roundel, dynamic low-angle photography; era widened to present.
Mercedes-Benz: grids showed ad layouts (dark, spotlit silver, star ring on black) and 1970s-80s interiors (tan/blue leather, tufted velour, walnut, big dials). Rewrote as era design language, dropped part names, kept star mark and palette.
Toyota: grids showed the new three-oval identity in red/white graphic layouts, bright outdoor ads, black hourglass faces. Kept angular trim language, added identity and photo mood, removed parts.
Tesla: grids showed T emblem in silver on matte black, dark showroom, wide techno lettering, red/black/white packaging and posters. Added identity, packaging and photo mood, removed vehicle parts.
Louis Vuitton: grids showed monogram on brown, damier checks in bright colourways, saffron orange packaging with navy ribbon, dark spotlit campaigns. Generalised from luggage to all goods, added packaging and colourways.
Gucci: grids showed burgundy and moss green packaging with tonal pattern, floral/animal prints, home decor with tigers, bees and bright clashes. Generalised from fashion to all goods, added packaging and print motifs.
Hermes: grid showed orange boxes with brown ribbon, orange pebbled leather with gold hardware, busy bordered multicolour silk prints. Added packaging, hand-rolled edges, airy photography; dropped product-specific bag and clasp wording.
Ralph Lauren: grid showed navy and green storefront signs with gold serif caps, polo rider mark, collegiate patches and numbers, autumn campaign shots with plaid and puffer layers. Added signage, collegiate crests, wood and autumn mood.
Lacoste: grid dominated by giant sculptural crocodile in layered green faceted paper in bright campaign, plus big white croc cutout on lawn. Added scaled-up reptile idea, bottle green, flat colour fields.
Tiffany and Co: grid showed turquoise boxes with white ribbon, glossy teal still-lifes (even a berry basket in blue), dark teal-navy ad backdrops, serif wide-spaced lettering. Reframed from silverware to jewellery and luxury, added whimsical blue still-life.
Lego: grid showed red square logo, yellow cardboard and kraft packaging, minifigure series bags, billboards in bright colour fields. Added packaging graphics, lime and orange accents, eco cardboard texture.
Vans: Bing grid showed red-and-white shoe boxes, hand-painted slanted logo with underline, checkerboard, skate/punk collage ads, vintage cartoon ads. Old text over-bound to one shoe; rewritten as transferable skate-canvas language.
Converse: Grid showed wooden crate and kraft boxes, star-chevron badge, collegiate wide lettering, halftone vintage poster ads, moody cinematic product shots in blue/red/mustard. Old text was shoe-part specific; now general heritage-athletic language.
Pepsi: Grid showed the recent rebrand with black-and-electric-blue dark backgrounds, bold lowercase wide wordmark, globe with wave, neon glow, condensation. Old text was pre-rebrand (blue fields, serif-ish script); updated to current look.
Guinness: Grid showed black and gold harp emblem, slim serif capitals with signature flourish, matte black cans with colourful patterned panels, warm pub photography. Added modern colourful patterned vein and dropped bound details.
Starbucks: Grid showed green/white roundel, simplified logo without ring text, seasonal flat geometric patterned packaging in orange/pink/lilac, kraft and green aprons, soft sunlit photography. Old text was cafe interior only; broadened to graphics and materials.
McDonalds: Grid showed colourful flat-illustrated red/yellow/white packaging with wavy lines, dots and cut-out food shapes, kraft bags with big lettering, overhead bright photography. Old text was dated corporate and interior-bound; now covers the playful current graphics.
Braun: Grid showed plain black compact wordmark on white, bold primary colour-bar posters, grid-based vintage ad layouts, with modern branding adding teal. Kept essence, added wordmark and poster grid language.
Nintendo: Grid showed saturated red packaging and white rounded logo plate, cheerful character illustration on blue/red, white clean ad posters. Old text was console-hardware bound; now packaging, colour and illustration language.
