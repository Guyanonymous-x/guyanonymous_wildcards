Styled in a design language of small, plain, boxy, honest and rugged Czech design. Flat surfaces, round and softly squared forms, chrome-rimmed edges, slim outlines and smooth unfussy bodies with no ornament beyond one proud mark. Palette is pale beige, olive green, sky blue, mustard yellow and brick red, with black and silver. Materials are plain vinyl, flat grey or brown plastic, painted steel and simple chrome. Modest, utilitarian and unpretentious. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
