Styled in a design language of stately, sturdy, dated 1950s British-inspired formality. Rounded, bulbous volumes with a domed crown and a tall, upright, slightly pompous frontal presence. Chrome used as thin edging and as a few bold vertical accents, with one small winged ornament as a crowning flourish. Swelling curves, thin slender supports and plain painted metal surfaces with fine line work. Palette of ivory white, black, dull grey, taxi yellow and olive green. Dignified, unhurried and built to last through heat and dust. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
