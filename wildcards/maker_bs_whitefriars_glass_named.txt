In the style of Whitefriars Glass, heavy, thick-walled studio glass with bold sculptural shapes and a strongly textured surface. Irregular bark-like ridges, chunky bubbles, dimples, moulded ribbing and cased layers of strong colour create a tactile feel. The palette is deep and earthy, tangerine orange, meadow green, kingfisher blue, aubergine purple, ruby red, amber and smoky grey. Forms are cylindrical, tapered, faceted or swirled with heavy bases and slight asymmetry. Surfaces are glossy with a deep glowing inner colour, in a mid-century manner.
