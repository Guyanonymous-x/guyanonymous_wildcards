In the style of Gillows, English Georgian and Regency furniture made from rich figured mahogany, rosewood and satinwood with crisp neoclassical lines, reeded tapering legs, brass inlay, ebony stringing, and ornate lion-paw feet. Davenports, pedestal sideboards, library tables with leather inlay tops, sabre-leg chairs, secretaire bookcases and brass castors. Deep polished red-brown wood glow, formal symmetry, refined country house and gentlemen club interior.
