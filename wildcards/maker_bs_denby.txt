Styled in a design language of English stoneware in rich, earthy, drippy glazes, with chunky rounded forms, wax-resist patterns and tactile dark edges. Colours are deep brown, mustard, blue-green, olive, smoky grey-blue and cream, speckled and reactive with natural variation, in brown-and-cream swirled and pebble-textured designs. Heavy, hand-thrown look, warm, practical and rustic, with a retro 1970s feel, thick walls, soft rounded edges and a handmade, slightly uneven finish. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
