The men wear dark suits or matching red vests and black trousers with their red fezzes, and the women of the auxiliary wear white blouses and red skirts.
The clown unit wear white face paint, red noses, oversize shoes and baggy satin costumes in stripes and polka dots, with small fezzes perched on their wigs.
The motorcycle unit wears black leather vests over red shirts and fezzes strapped under their chins.
