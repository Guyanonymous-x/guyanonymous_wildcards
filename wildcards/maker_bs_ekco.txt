Styled in a design language of Art Deco British bakelite radios in circular, curved and stepped forms, in ivory, walnut brown, and green with chrome rings around the speaker. Round dials with illuminated glass stations, tall rounded cases and a streamlined feel, and later ivory and red plastic televisions with a rounded bezel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
