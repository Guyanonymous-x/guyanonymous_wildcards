the installation at completion, photographed with three cars and nobody in it
the installation in year three in regular use by a household that drives two of the six cars and keeps four, which is the normal pattern
the installation with the lift out of service and six cars underground for eleven weeks waiting for a hydraulic part, which happens and is the type's signature failure
the installation after a water ingress through a failed cover seal, with the cars moved out, the floor dried for six weeks and a dehumidifier running
the room in a house unoccupied and held as an asset, with six cars on trickle charge, which is a real and widely practiced procedure and is the most precise modern parallel to the mothballed state bedroom at record 10
the room converted by a second owner to a gym, a store or a wine room because they have two cars and no interest, which is common
the room with an electric fleet, where the fire strategy is revisited, the charging load becomes the house's largest single electrical demand and the insurance is renegotiated, which is the live change in this room type now
the room open to the weather, which for a sealed concrete box two stories down means the pumps stop and it floods completely, taking the cars with it, and is the single most expensive total loss described anywhere in this document
