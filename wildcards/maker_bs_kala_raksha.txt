In the style of Kala Raksha, Kutch artisan-cooperative textiles featuring Suf, Pakko and Kharek embroidery, applique and patchwork on handwoven cotton. Suf embroidery is precisely counted triangular geometry stitched in satin stitch from the back, forming tiny diamonds, zig-zags and stars in mirror-like symmetry. Colours are bold contrasts of magenta, orange, yellow, green, royal blue and white on black or deep indigo cloth, with some quilted ralli patchwork in reds and blacks. Pieces include tote bags, wall pieces, scarves, shawls and skirts, with small mirrors set at the centre of motifs. Meticulous, handmade, vibrant, desert-village craft mood.
