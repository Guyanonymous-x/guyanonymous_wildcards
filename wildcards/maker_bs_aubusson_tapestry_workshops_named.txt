Styled in a design language of flat, pile-free surfaces with a slightly chalky, dusty, antique hand, in soft pastel-leaning colour. The palette is faded rose pink, powder blue, sage green, cream, pale gold and muted terracotta. Designs use leafy verdure with birds, large acanthus leaves, floral bouquets and romantic medallions framed by ribbons, garlands and scrolling cartouches. Compositions feel airy, graceful, curved and symmetrical in a Rococo and Neoclassical manner, with delicate cream borders. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
