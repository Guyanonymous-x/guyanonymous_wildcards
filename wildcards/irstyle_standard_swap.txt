Shot through a standard 720nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a pale steel-blue sky over cream and ivory foliage, with soft gray shadows. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
