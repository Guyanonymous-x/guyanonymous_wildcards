Styled in a design language of exceptionally fine surfaces with very dense, minute detail and a glossy silk sheen. Composition is courtly and symmetrical, built around a central medallion with pointed arch shapes, tulips, carnations, curling serrated leaves and rosettes, plus touches of gold and silver shimmer. Colours are ivory, turquoise, rose, deep sapphire, emerald and ruby. Borders are narrow, multiple and perfectly even, with soft elegant curves. Imperial, lustrous, exquisite and delicate. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
