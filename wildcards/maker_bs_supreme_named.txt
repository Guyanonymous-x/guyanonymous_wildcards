Palette of red, black, white and occasional neon. Crisp screen-print ink, slightly cracked vinyl texture, thick outlines and deadpan minimal copy. Clean white spaces and a hard-edged downtown street-poster attitude, cool, smug and deliberately ironic. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
