Styled in a design language of hand-painted surfaces with bold, loose, post-impressionist decoration. Compositions are irregular and asymmetrical with an unfinished hand-drawn quality. Underlying forms are plain and painted all over in dense patterns. Glazes are thick and opaque, with spots and swirls. The mood is cheerful, avant-garde, amateurish by design and bohemian. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
