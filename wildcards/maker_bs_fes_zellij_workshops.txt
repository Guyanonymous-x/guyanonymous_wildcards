Styled in a design language of a mosaic of hand-cut glazed clay pieces fitted into an interlocking geometric puzzle. Stars with eight, twelve or sixteen points radiate from the centre, surrounded by kite, hexagon and chevron pieces in vibrant glossy colour, cobalt blue, emerald green, saffron yellow, white, black, deep red-brown and turquoise. Edges are crisp with thin pale grout lines, and surfaces are glassy. Precise, endlessly repeating tessellations create a jewel-like pattern with no empty space. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
