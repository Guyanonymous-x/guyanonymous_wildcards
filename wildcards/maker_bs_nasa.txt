Styled in a design language of clean, technical, optimistic American spaceflight design. Bright white fields carry bold, geometric graphics in deep blue, signal red and black. A round blue disc speckled with white stars carries a vivid red chevron swoosh and a white orbit line, an emblem repeated as patches and stickers. Colour stripes in red and blue run in parallel, with small golden wing-like details. Surfaces are matte technical fabric, glossy white enamel, golden foil and fine stitching, with neat black labels. Orderly, hopeful, precise and quietly heroic, with a cool minimalist space-age modernism. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
