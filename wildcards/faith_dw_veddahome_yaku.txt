the nae yaku, the spirits of the dead kin, stand among the trees as pale shining figures of men and women in their fifties to seventies in loincloths and cloth wraps, bows in the men's hands
Kande Yaka, the great spirit of the hunt, stands on the rock hill above the forest, a tall strong hunter in his forties with dark brown skin, long black hair tied up, a bow and a bundle of arrows in his hands
the spirits of the dead are present as a soft white glow drifting through the forest from tree to cave to hilltop, gathering above the place where the offering is laid
