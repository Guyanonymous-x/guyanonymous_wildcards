Styled in a design language of carefully revived classical roman letterforms and crisp new classics. Lettering is a fine serif with high contrast, bracketed serifs, balanced proportions and even colour, with a few geometric sans-serif and slab designs. Compositions use centred headings, small capitals, letter-spaced capitals and discreet fleurons. The look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, like a well-printed British book or newspaper. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
