in a house of palm thatch on stilts the dead woman in her sixties lies in a hammock, her face painted with fine black kene lines, her kin in their twenties to seventies weeping around her
the kin in their twenties to sixties cut their hair short and paint their bodies with black genipap for mourning, standing by the grave in the village clearing
in the old days, the kin roasted the bones of the dead man over a fire, ground them to powder in a wooden mortar and ate them in banana porridge, so the dead stayed with them
