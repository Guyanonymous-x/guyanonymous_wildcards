Styled in a design language of loud, fast, race-ready graphic punch. Signature fluorescent lime green dominates, cut by black, white and occasional pale blue in sharp angular slashes, jagged shards, diagonal speed bands and torn-edge digital camouflage patterns. Surfaces are high-gloss, carbon-weave and matte black with tinted metallic highlights. Aggressive, athletic and unapologetically vivid. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
