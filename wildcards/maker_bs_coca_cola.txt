Styled in a design language of joyful, refreshing, nostalgic Americana. Dominant saturated red paired with clean white, used in broad flat fields with a flowing white ribbon wave. Shapes are curvy, contoured and hourglass-like, with glossy glass, polished chrome and condensation droplets. Round discs and badge shapes, vintage enamel sign charm, and a timeless feel-good, shareable cheer. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
