Styled in a design language of honest, approachable, everyday American mass-market design. Palette of deep royal blue and white with poppy red, turquoise, pale yellow, cream and dark green. Later years bring clean utilitarian graphics, plain horizontal bands. Materials include painted steel, enamel, chrome strip, vinyl, woodgrain and canvas. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
