Styled in a design language of American portable typewriters with a rounded case in turquoise, ivory, grey or pale mint, round glass-topped keys with chrome rims, a sturdy metal lever carriage return and a thin ribbon spool. Carry cases in matching colours with leatherette and brass clasps. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
