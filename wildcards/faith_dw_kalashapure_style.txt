painted as a present-day naturalist painting of the Hindu Kush, the snow peaks, the high meadows and the walnut trees of the valley drawn true to life in clear cold colors
carved and painted in the manner of Kalasha woodcarving, the scene cut into dark walnut wood in rows of zigzags, rosettes and stylized horse heads, the carved lines picked out in white and red
painted with a border of the woven patterns of the Kalasha women's belts and headbands, small bright bands of red, yellow, green and orange in repeating steps running around the scene
