at the heart of the land rises Yungdrung Gutseg, a mountain of nine stacked tiers, each tier marked with the yungdrung, the Bon cross whose four arms bend at right angles and turn counterclockwise, the whole mountain shining crystal white
around the central mountain lie the temples, palaces and parks of Olmo Lungring, golden roofs on white walls, gardens of flowering trees, and four rivers flowing out from the foot of the mountain toward the four directions
the hidden land is ringed by a circle of snow mountains and beyond them by a wide ocean crossed by a path of light, and inside the ring lakes of turquoise water mirror the shining peak at the center
