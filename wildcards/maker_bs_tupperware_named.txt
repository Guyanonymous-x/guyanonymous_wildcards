In the style of Tupperware, cheerful domestic order in translucent, slightly frosted material with a matte waxy finish. Soft rounded corners, plump friendly volumes and snug, tight-fitting joints. Forms are designed to nest and repeat in neat, tidy modular stacks. No ornament beyond moulded rings and simple ribs. Bright, wholesome, practical, suburban and joyful. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
