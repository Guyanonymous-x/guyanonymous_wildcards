Burmese yoke thé marionettes from Myanmar, finely carved wooden string puppets each about as tall as an adult's arm. Each marionette has a carved wooden face painted smooth and pale, with fine black brows, painted eyes and red lips, and hangs from as many as sixty strings gathered to a wooden control. The costumes are rich velvet and satin sewn with sequins, glass jewels and gold thread, with flaring panels that stand out at the hips. __puppet_yoke_the_var__.
