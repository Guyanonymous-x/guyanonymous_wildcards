In the style of Montblanc, massive cigar-shaped fountain pens in polished black precious resin with gold-plated rings, a clip and a large two-tone gold nib. The cap is topped with a white six-pointed star shaped like a snow-capped peak, rounded with a domed shape. Barrels are glossy and deep black, with thin gold rings and engraved numbers. Special editions use striped, marbled or burgundy resin. Cases are black leather with a gold emblem, and notebooks and cufflinks follow the same dark and gold luxury. The mood is stately, confident, executive and understated.
