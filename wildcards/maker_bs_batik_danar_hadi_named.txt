Styled in a design language of hand-drawn wax-resist patterning in an earthy palette of soga brown, indigo blue, cream and black, with occasional red and gold. Motifs are classical and rhythmic, such as diagonal bands of interlocking S shapes, circles in a quatrefoil grid, scattered tiny flowers and winged cartouches, repeated across the whole surface. Fine dotted lines and slightly wavering white outlines show the hand of the maker, and thin crackle veins and tonal depth appear where the wax cracked. Courtly, refined and heritage-rich. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
