Styled in a design language of Italian mod-era pop style built from clean geometry. Smooth enamel shapes in cream, white, red, sky blue and olive green, often split into two clean tones by a long horizontal break. Brushed aluminium, chrome trim and flat matte panels look tidy and precise. Youthful, witty, minimal and sharply graphic, with retro ska energy. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
