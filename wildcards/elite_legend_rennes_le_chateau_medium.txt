Rennes-le-Chateau, the hilltop village in southern France at the heart of the Priory of Sion legend, a hoax of the 1950s and 1960s about a secret society guarding a hidden royal line. A small stone village sits on a bare hilltop above a wide valley, and beside its little church stands a small round-cornered stone tower with battlements on a cliff edge, the Tour Magdala.
