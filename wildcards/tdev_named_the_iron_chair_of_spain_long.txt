the iron chair of Spain, an iron chair with a brazier beneath, a heavy upright chair made entirely of black iron with iron straps at the wrists and ankles, an iron fire basket full of coals set beneath the seat, empty, a straight-backed armchair built of flat iron bars like a grill, iron cuffs riveted to the arms and front legs, a strap across the back, the fire basket hung between the legs right under the seat, the iron black and scaled from heat, the seat a grid of iron bars, the straps riveted and rusted, ash and burnt-out coals heaped in the fire basket below, in a stone museum hall about the Inquisition, red glow from the coals. Close up, the iron shows hammer marks, flaking black scale and orange rust bleeding into every crack and rivet head.
