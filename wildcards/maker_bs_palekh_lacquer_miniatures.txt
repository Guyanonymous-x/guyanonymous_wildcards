Styled in a design language of deep lustrous black glossy ground painted in egg tempera with fairy tale scenes. The palette is bright red, gold, emerald green, white and orange against black, with fine gold highlights outlining draperies and ornamental borders of tiny patterns. Red lacquer provides contrast. Details are miniature and precise, as in an icon, and the mood is mythical, jewel-like and storybook. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
