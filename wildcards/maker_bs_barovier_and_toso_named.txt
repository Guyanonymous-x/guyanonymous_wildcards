In the style of Barovier and Toso, Venetian hand-blown glass in lavish, light-filled forms, with delicate curved arms spreading from a central axis and bearing glass flowers, leaves and curling ribbons in clear crystal, rose pink, aqua and amber. Ornate scrolling branches and floral cups sit beside mid-century sculptural versions with cascading rods, tubes and ball drops in white and gold. Glossy, translucent, festive, ornate and glittering like a palace ballroom. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Barovier and Toso in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
