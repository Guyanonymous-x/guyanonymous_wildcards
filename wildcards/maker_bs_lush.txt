Styled in a design language of handmade, playful and joyfully imperfect. Matte black surfaces and kraft brown paper stamped and stickered with bold hand-drawn black marker lettering, doodles, tiny scribbled icons and rough brush type. The wordmark is chunky, uneven hand-cut capitals in white on black. Vivid swirled marbled patterns in hot pink, leaf green, purple, orange and turquoise, with confetti-like blocky shapes, collage cut-outs and fresh leaf motifs. Warm cream sticky labels, string and torn-paper edges. Colour is intense and fresh against plain black and brown. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
