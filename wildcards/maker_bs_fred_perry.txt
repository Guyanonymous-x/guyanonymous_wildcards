Styled in a design language of neat, tidy British sporting heritage with a mod and subculture edge. Signature graphic is the twin-tipped stripe in contrasting colours, appearing as trim on edges and borders. Palette is navy, white, black, racing green, burgundy and cream, with sharp contrast pairings like yellow on black or red and white on navy. Lettering is a clean, wide-spaced, light sans-serif in capitals, sometimes within a round badge. Packaging is cream and navy cartons with simple wreath marks. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
