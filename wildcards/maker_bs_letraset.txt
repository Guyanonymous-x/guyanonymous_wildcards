Styled in a design language of letterforms are bold, expressive 1960s and 70s display faces, chunky rounded letters, psychedelic swirls, inline shadows, stencil cuts, art-nouveau revival and space-age geometry. The mood is cut-and-paste, punk poster and retro graphic design. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
