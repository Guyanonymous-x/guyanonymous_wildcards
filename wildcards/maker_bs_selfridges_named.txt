Styled in a design language of bold, confident, high-impact British modernism built on one saturated sunshine yellow. Black twisted cord and plain black trim sit against the yellow with crisp contrast. Paper-like matte and semi-gloss finishes keep surfaces simple and graphic. Alternate colourways use hot pink, electric blue, lime and monochrome black and white in the same blunt layout, with a small ampersand-style mark. Little ornament, just strong blocks, large type and sharp edges. Loud, witty, fashionable and unmistakable. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
