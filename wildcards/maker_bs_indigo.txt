In the style of IndiGo, a modern low cost airline identity in deep blue. Aircraft have a white fuselage with a large indigo blue lower belly sweeping up toward the tail, ending in a deep indigo tail fin with a lighter sky blue accent stripe, and the small airline name in a rounded lowercase wordmark in indigo on the front body and tail. The logo is made of twenty round dots arranged in the shape of a plane, rendered in white or sky blue over the indigo. Engines and winglets are dark blue with a small white website address. Cabins are bright and simple with slim slim-line seats in blue and white, matching overhead lockers, and cheerful bright staff in navy tunics with an indigo belt. Boarding passes, tickets and ads use a clear white background, a rich blue block and playful, crisp sans serif type. Mood is clean, efficient, youthful, accessible and cool.
