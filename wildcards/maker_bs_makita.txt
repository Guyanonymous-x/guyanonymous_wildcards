In the style of Makita, Japanese power tools. Strong teal-blue-green plastic housings with black rubberised handles and black battery packs, a red stripe on the label, and tough silver or steel metalwork. Shapes are compact and rounded with chunky triggers, ribbed overmoulded grips, and neat rows of small ventilation slots. Text is bold white capital lettering and a boxed brand mark. LED work lights and bright yellow warning labels. Mood is solid, workmanlike and cool teal.
