Styled in a design language of exuberant, crowded, festive, handmade design. Hand-beaten metal volumes in long boxy shapes, every surface covered in loud hand-painted patterns such as stripes, checks, flames, flowers and animals, outlined in chrome strips. Abundant shiny decoration, tassels, fringes and many mirrors. Velvet or vinyl in saturated colour, polished rails and heaps of chrome shining in the sun. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
