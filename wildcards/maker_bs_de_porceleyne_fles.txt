In the style of Delft Royal Blue, Dutch tin-glazed earthenware painted by hand in rich cobalt blue on glossy white, depicting windmills, canals, sailing ships, tulips, milkmaids, farmhouses and floral scrolls, with fine brushwork and blue shading. Tiles, plates, tulip vases, clogs, and tall pyramid-shaped flower vases with multiple spouts. Slightly crackled glaze, soft blue-white contrast, charming, folksy Dutch heritage with elegant Chinese-inspired scenes.
