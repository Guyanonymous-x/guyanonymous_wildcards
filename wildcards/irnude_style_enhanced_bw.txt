Shot through an enhanced 665nm infrared filter and converted to black and white, the image shows a black-and-white image with silvery skin, a mid-gray background and faint veins, softer than deep infrared. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
