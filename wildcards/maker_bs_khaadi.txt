In the style of Khaadi, Pakistani handloom and printed cotton clothing. Garments are loose kurtas and shalwar sets in handwoven khaddar and lawn, with crisp matte textures and visible weave, in a palette of mustard, indigo, rust, olive, ivory and deep red. Prints are bold block-print-inspired motifs, large paisleys, florals, small repeating geometry and Ajrak style interlocking patterns in two or three colours, arranged along the hem or sleeve. Embroidery uses neat threadwork on the neckline and cuffs, with small mirrors and tassels. Dupattas are long rectangular scarves with stripes and fringed ends. Stores are airy and open, with warm wood shelving, hanging rails with fabric rolls, and a soft beige and white palette, and the logo is a small hand-drawn motif of fingers that seem to hold threads. Mood is earthy, artisanal, warm, modern-heritage and cheerful, shot in sunlit courtyards.
