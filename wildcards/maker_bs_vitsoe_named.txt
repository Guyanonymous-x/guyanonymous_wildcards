Styled in a design language of calm, minimalist and timeless. Thin vertical aluminium lines with slim aluminium planes hung from them on small pegs. Surfaces are powder-coated in white, black or light grey, or smooth plywood in pale birch, with sleek clean edges. Crisp panels in matte pale colours with narrow slots and small detailing. Everything is clean, orderly and rectilinear with thin lines and no decoration. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
