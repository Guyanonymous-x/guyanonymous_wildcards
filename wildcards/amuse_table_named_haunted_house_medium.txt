a 1982 pinball table by Gottlieb, a three-level haunted pinball, with "HAUNTED HOUSE" across the top of its backglass. Its playfield shows three playfields one above another, a hidden lower basement level seen through a window and eight flippers in all, and above it is a backglass of a spooky purple mansion on a hill at night with bats, a full moon and one lit window.
