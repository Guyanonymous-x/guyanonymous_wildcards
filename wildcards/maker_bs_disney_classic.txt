In the style of Disney classic animation, hand-inked cel characters with rubbery round shapes, bold black outlines, big eyes with highlights, white gloves on mouse characters, expressive squash-and-stretch poses, and lush painted backgrounds in soft watercolour with storybook castles, deep forests and twinkling night skies. Palette of royal blue, rose pink, golden yellow and emerald, with pastel gradients, multiplane depth, dreamy glowing light, fairy-tale ornate curlicue title lettering and warm nostalgic 1940s picture-book charm.
