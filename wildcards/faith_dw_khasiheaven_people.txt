Khasi pilgrims, men and women in their twenties to their seventies, climb the peak, the women in the jainsem, a long dress of two silk pieces knotted over each shoulder, the men in white shirts and turbans
at the Shad Suk Mynsiem dance women in their twenties in silk jainsem of gold and cream, with silver crowns rising to a tall plume at the back and heavy necklaces of red coral and gold beads, step slowly in a ring, their eyes lowered
men in their thirties to their fifties in sleeveless jymphong tunics, silk dhotis and plumed turbans, holding swords and whisks of white yak hair, dance around the women in a wider ring
a Khasi priest, a lyngdoh, a man in his sixties in white with a white turban, kneels before a flat stone holding a red rooster and a gourd of rice beer, an egg on a small wooden board before him for divination
