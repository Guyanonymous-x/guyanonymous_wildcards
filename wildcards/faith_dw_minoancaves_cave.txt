the Psychro cave on Mount Dikte opens down into the mountain, its great hall lit by lamps, tall stalagmites and stalactites shining wet, bronze double axes pushed into the cracks of the rock
the cave of Eileithyia at Amnissos is a long low chamber, a rounded stalagmite at its center fenced by a low stone wall and worn smooth by hands
the Kamares cave high on Mount Ida yawns wide over the Messara plain, offerings of painted cups and jars in white, red and orange swirls set on the rock floor inside
