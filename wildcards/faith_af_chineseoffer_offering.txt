a paper mansion made for the dead burns in the street, a house of bamboo strips and colored paper reaching the shoulders of the man beside it, a paper car, a paper television, paper servants and stacks of paper clothes set around it
bundles of hell bank notes burn in a bowl on the altar, large paper notes printed in green and red with the face of the Jade Emperor in the middle, reading "HELL BANK NOTE" in English capitals across the top and "天地銀行" in Chinese characters below, the number "10000" in each corner
