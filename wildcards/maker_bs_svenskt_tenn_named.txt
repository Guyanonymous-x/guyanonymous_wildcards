Styled in a design language of cheerful, bright, cultured, deliberately casual Swedish interiors. Large-scale botanical and fantastical prints on white or cream grounds, with detailed lush flowers, leaves, vines, birds, butterflies, maps and exotic fruit in saturated, slightly unexpected colours such as teal, mustard, coral, raspberry, tangerine and deep green, drawn with hand-painted freedom. Forms are light, comfortable and curvy, with spindly legs, rounded corners and woven cane, in mahogany or pale birch, with brass details. Pewter, glass and brass objects are plain and sculptural. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
