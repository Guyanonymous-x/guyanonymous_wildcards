Styled in a design language of opulent, romantic Indian heirloom richness with a vintage, slightly distressed and layered look. Deep earthy jewel colours such as rich red, burnt orange, marigold, mustard, moss green, teal and faded rose cover the surface. Heavy hand embroidery in dense gold metallic thread, silk thread, sequins, mirror work and tassels combines with block-printed florals and tiger or paisley motifs on silk, velvet, organza and tulle. Sheer layers have scalloped borders. Ornament is chunky antique gold with uncut stones, big gems and pearl ropes, layered thickly. Regal, heritage mood, warm golden light and a faded-palace feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
