Styled in a design language of Italian postmodern design with mirror-polished stainless steel and bright resin accents in red, yellow, pink, blue and green. Forms are playful and anthropomorphic, built from rounded cones, spheres and cylinders on spidery legs, with cute faces, exaggerated protrusions and cheerful little figures. Witty, toy-like and sculptural, treating everyday objects as pop-art ornaments, with soft shadows and cheerful display. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
