Styled in a design language of exuberant, crowded, festive, handmade design. Hand-beaten metal volumes in long boxy shapes, every surface covered in loud hand-painted patterns such as stripes, checks, flames, flowers and animals, outlined in chrome strips. Velvet or vinyl in saturated colour, polished rails and heaps of chrome shining in the sun. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
