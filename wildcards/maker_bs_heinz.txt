Styled in a design language. Palette is tomato red, warm cream, antique gold and deep navy, with a vivid mustard yellow or leaf green accent for variants. Surfaces are glossy printed paper, tinted glass and enamel with crisp thin keylines. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
