In the style of Hong Kong Tramways, narrow double decker electric street trams that run along a dense city road. Each tram is tall and slim, with an upright boxy body, two storeys of tall rectangular windows in slim frames, a slightly curved roof, a short pole of trolley on top, and open balcony fronts on the upper deck. The lower body is painted deep forest green, with a cream or pale yellow stripe along the window line, and the upper front carries a square destination box with white characters on black. The most distinctive sight is the full body advertising wrap in saturated colours, glossy photographs and bold Chinese and English lettering that covers the entire flank, so each tram looks like a rolling poster. Interiors have wooden slatted seats, brass handrails, a plain bell cord and wooden floors, while the roof is open to the street with a brisk breeze. Mood is busy, nostalgic, neon-lit and crowded, with the tram sliding beside tower blocks.
In the style of Star Ferry, small wooden double deck harbour ferries on a crowded Asian harbour. Each vessel has a low, broad hull with a rounded overhanging bow, a white upper cabin and a narrow upper deck with a canvas or wooden awning, ringed by a thin white railing. The hull and trim are painted deep bottle green below a clean white superstructure, with a gold rubbing strake, black funnel top, brass fittings and cream life rings. Names in white Latin letters and Chinese characters appear on the bow, always with the word star as part of them. Decks have slatted wooden benches that can flip to face either way, with a small ticket booth and a rope barrier at the pier. Interiors show creamy paint, rows of varnished timber seats and round portholes. The piers are green steel sheds with clock towers. Mood is leisurely, nostalgic, salty and civic, with skyscrapers behind, hazy sky and water in dark teal.
