Styled in a design language of extreme-cold expedition design, puffy, boxy, wide-shouldered, protective and rugged. Heavy, down-filled, quilted forms with large padded volumes, a fur-trimmed protective edge, a tall collar, snap-closed storm flaps, flapped patch pockets and a cinched waist. Fabric is dense, matte, slightly rough woven cotton-nylon in black, navy, graphite grey, sand, red or arctic white, with visible baffles. Reflective piping, leather pulls, knit trims, premium detailing give a serious and wealthy polar feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
