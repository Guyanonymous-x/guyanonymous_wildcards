the basilica of Sant'Apollinare Nuovo, a long brick church with a round bell tower, a plain gabled front of old red-brown brick behind a low white marble porch of slim columns, and beside it a tall cylinder of brick pierced by rings of arched openings that grow wider toward the tiled cone roof
the inside of the basilica, a long nave with rows of marble columns, two lines of twelve pale gray veined columns with carved leafy capitals carrying round arches, above them long bands of gold mosaic running the whole length of both walls under a row of windows and a gilded coffered ceiling
the apse with a mosaic of Christ, the rounded end of the church beyond the last arches, where Christ sits on a jeweled throne in a deep purple robe with a gold halo, one hand raised in blessing, between four winged angels in white holding long staffs, all on a ground of shining gold
