Styled in a design language of ornate, quirky Victorian gentleman-perfumer elegance. Cut-crystal and clear glass with faceted details are finished with silken ribbon, tassels and gilt trim. Surfaces are heavy cream, deep pine green, oxblood red and inky black with thin gold borders, embossed crests. Labels sit within delicate frames of double rules and tiny ornamental flourishes. Wax-seal accents, tissue paper, fine marbled endpaper patterns, tartan ribbons and glossy lacquered boxes with satin linings add collector charm. Eccentric, English, refined and gently theatrical. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
