a room whose entire geometry is derived from one requirement
the row spacing
the projection and the capture, so one person controls the room from one position; two screens flanking a central whiteboard, which is the arrangement that replaced the single central screen once lecture capture made a visible writing surface necessary again; lecture capture cameras and ceiling microphones, which are now standard and which changed the room by making the back rows viable; a wheelchair space on a mid-row landing with a companion seat, because a rake cannot be traversed, and the position of that landing is the single most revealing accessibility decision in the building; exit doors at the top and bottom of the rake, which is a means-of-escape requirement and sets the aisle widths
