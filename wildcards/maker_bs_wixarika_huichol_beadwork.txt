Styled in a design language of ceremonial imagery rendered in dense blazing colour. Tiny glass beads are pressed one by one into a layer of wax and resin over carved forms, leaving a shining mosaic surface with a slightly bumpy glittering texture. Flat areas use thick coloured wool pressed into wax in tight spiralling lines that follow each shape like contour maps. Motifs are deer, maize, cactus, scorpions, five-petalled flowers, sun faces, rain clouds and diamond-backed snake bodies, with zigzag lines radiating outward. The palette is neon-bright and contrasting, fuchsia, turquoise, lime green, electric blue, orange, yellow, red and black with white outlines, filled completely so no background shows. Psychedelic, symmetrical and intricate. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
