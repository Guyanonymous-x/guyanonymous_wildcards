Styled in a design language of thick, bulky, hand-made wool texture in chunky hand-spun undyed yarn with a slightly oily, hairy, rustic surface and a heavy, sturdy, weatherproof feel. Shapes are roomy and boxy with rounded soft shoulders and plain, unfussy edges. Palette is natural cream, oat, grey, charcoal, brown and black, with a rare small hit of red. Cosy, outdoorsy, woodsy, cabin warmth. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
