In the style of Muji, no-brand minimalism, quiet, modest and thoughtful. Plain, simple, softly rounded geometric forms with no logos, no ornament and no unnecessary features. Natural, raw, unbleached materials such as pale wood, kraft paper, cotton, linen, white ceramic and matte plastic in off-white, beige, oatmeal, grey, charcoal and muted navy. Finishes are matte, soft and tactile, with neat even stitching and honest construction. Lettering is small, plain, lightweight sans-serif in black or grey on neutral labels. Calm, airy, generous empty space, with soft daylight photography on uncluttered backdrops.
