Styled in a design language of quirky, offbeat 1970s American pragmatism. Stubby, lopsided proportions with one long mass, one short mass and an abruptly chopped, flat cut-off end. Square shoulders, bulging haunches and plain black plastic trim. Loud colours of bright orange, vivid green, mustard and avocado. Practical, rebellious and cheerfully strange. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
