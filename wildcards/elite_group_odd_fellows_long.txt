a lodge room of the Odd Fellows, an old fraternal order known for its three-link chain. The lodge room has a red carpet, a raised chair for the presiding officer, the Noble Grand, at one end, and on the wall above it a painted symbol of three linked oval chain links, each link holding one gilded capital letter, "F", "L" and "T". Along the walls hang painted canvases of the order's symbols, an all-seeing eye inside a triangle, a hand holding a red heart, an hourglass, a bow and arrows, and a scythe.
