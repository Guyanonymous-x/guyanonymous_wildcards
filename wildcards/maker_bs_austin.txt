In the style of Austin in the 1930s to 1950s, a rounded, upright and sensible British family saloon with a tall chrome radiator grille, curved separate wings, running boards on early cars, and large round headlamps on the wings. Bonnet with side louvres or a long hinged lid, rounded boot, narrow bumpers with overriders, and a bulbous trunk. Thin steel wheels with chrome hubcaps and a winged badge on the grille. Paint in black, maroon, deep green, grey, cream and two-tone shades. Interior has beige or maroon leather, polished wood dashboard with a small central speedometer, spindly steering wheel with a large rim, and thick carpet. Mood is stately, modest and homely.
