In the style of Thonet, solid wood steam-bent into continuous flowing curves, with slender round-section members forming loops, circles and S-scrolls. Structures are light, airy and sculptural, with delicate splayed supports joined by a ring brace, and surfaces of woven cane or pressed perforated wood. Finish is dark mahogany brown, walnut or ebonised black in satin lacquer, with visible screw heads and small metal collars at joints. Later work uses bent chrome tube with cane panels and black leather. Mass-produced, elegant, coffeehouse mood. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Thonet in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
