Styled after a showcase of large coloured gemstones such as emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, amethyst and citrine in smooth cabochon and faceted cuts, set in polished yellow and rose gold. Shapes are organic and sculptural, with flowing curves, wave and leaf forms and bold chunky volumes. Designs often combine textured gold with a single huge gem or bright candy-coloured stones stacked in bands, with smooth rounded forms that feel modern and tropical. Colours are saturated greens, blues, purples and warm golden yellow. Confident, glamorous and sunny, against dark depth with bright spotlights. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
