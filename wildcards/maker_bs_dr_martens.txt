Styled in a design language of defiant British rebellion with chunky, hardwearing, scuffed-and-loved attitude. Surfaces are glossy or crinkled leather in black and cherry red, with visible contrast stitching in bright yellow, thick matte rubber textures and metal studs. Palette is sulphur yellow and black as the signature pairing, with cherry red, white and occasional floral, tartan or acid-bright prints. Lettering is hand-drawn and uneven, with a bouncing baseline and a rough marker or stencil feel, often with an arched wordmark. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
