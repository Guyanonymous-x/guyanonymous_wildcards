Styled in a design language of precise, enthusiastic, sunny hobbyist modelmaking of the 1970s and 1980s. Illustration shows one subject in rich realism on a plain white ground, with crisp edges, glossy highlights, believable dirt, rust and exhaust staining, and dramatic low angles, framed by a thin black border with clean black. Parts have sharp rivets, hexagonal bolt heads, fine panel lines and neat decals in white, red and yellow. Glossy shells in bright racing red, over chunky details, silver metal parts and exposed black structure. Tidy exploded line drawings. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
