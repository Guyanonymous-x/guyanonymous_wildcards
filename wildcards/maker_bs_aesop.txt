Styled in a design language of literate, apothecary-inspired restraint with quiet luxury. Rich amber-brown glass and matte brown surfaces with plain cream or off-white paper labels, set in neat black small type with long, tightly spaced lines of detail. Palette is amber brown, cream, black, olive green, terracotta and muted stone, with matte black and brushed brass trim. Wrapping is plain cardboard in earthy tones with a single line of black type. Tactile, thoughtful, minimal, sensory and understated. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
