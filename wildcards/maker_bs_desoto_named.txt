In the style of DeSoto, dramatic and theatrical American showmanship of the mid to late 1950s. A tall vertical oval like a pursed mouth, heavy chrome brows, sharply pointed upswept edges with arrow-shaped trim and one long sweeping chrome spear that splits two-tone paint. Hooded, wraparound forms, plush fabric and a bold sculpted panel. Pink and charcoal, turquoise and white, sunset orange. Flamboyant, confident and a little overblown.
