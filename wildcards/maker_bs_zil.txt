Styled in a design language of heavy, imposing, ceremonial and austere Soviet state grandeur. Huge length and width, tall flat sides, a formal upright top line and a stately slab body in black gloss. Heavy chrome of bold vertical bars, wide edging with big bullet shapes, thin trim and white bands. Palette of black, deep burgundy, dark grey and cream. Stern, formal, secretive and built to impress. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
