Styled in a design language of British countryside heritage built for rain, mud and long use. Waxed cotton in olive, sage, navy and dark brown with a slightly shiny, creased, weathered surface that ages with character. Details include corduroy collars, heavy metal zips, storm flaps, poacher-style pockets and quilted diamond stitching, in practical, roomy, tailored shapes. Tartan and muted tweed-like linings add quiet pattern. Lettering is traditional, tidy serif in a small emblem. Photography is misty, green, damp, moody and rural, with dogs, estates and hedgerows. Timeless, sturdy and understated.
