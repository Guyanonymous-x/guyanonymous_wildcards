Styled in a design language of classic British artists heritage in monochrome. Palette is ink black, crisp white, cream paper tones and rich deep blue, with a single bold red band as an accent. Surfaces are matte card, silver foil, satin enamel and polished brass with fine rule lines. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
