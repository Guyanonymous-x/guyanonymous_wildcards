Styled in a design language of convincingly real, handmade, battle-worn fantasy craft. Surfaces show distressed paint, rust-brown stains, dull pewter, tarnished bronze and bone-white enamel, with scratches, dents, mud streaks, worn leather and heavy ring links. Organic forms are massive and sculpted, with pores, veins, wrinkles, hairs, tusks and wet glossy detail, in layered painted skin of olive, brown, ochre and grey. A playful retro strain is fat. Gritty, textured, painterly, detailed and storybook-epic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
