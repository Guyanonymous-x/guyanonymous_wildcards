In the style of Peugeot in the 1980s and 1990s, a neat, compact, well-proportioned hatchback or saloon with a clean wedge profile, a lion standing on its hind legs badge on the nose, and a rectangular black grille between slim wraparound headlamps. Smooth uncluttered flanks, a single crisp body line along the doors, black plastic bumpers, tidy rectangular tail lamps spanning the tail, and a rising beltline. Wheels are neat alloy designs with five or six holes. Paint in white, bright red, ocean blue, silver metallic, dark green and black. Interior has deep-pile cloth seats, a simple dash with a round binnacle of clear dials, grey plastic and thin stalks. Mood is tidy, practical and sporty.
