a Victorian china-head doll, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her head and shoulders are one piece of glazed white porcelain, shiny and smooth, with pink cheeks, a small red mouth and painted blue eyes outlined in a thin red line. Her black hair is molded and painted gloss black right on the porcelain, parted in the middle and drawn down in smooth waves over the ears. The glazed shoulder plate is sewn to a soft body of stuffed muslin, and her lower arms and boots are glazed porcelain too, the boots painted black with a gold tassel. She wears a long dress of printed calico with a high neck and a pinafore. __doll_china_head_var__.
