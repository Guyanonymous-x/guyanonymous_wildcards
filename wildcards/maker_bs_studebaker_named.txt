Styled in a design language of quirky, futuristic and a bit strange American design of the 1950s. Low, wide stance, a slim body, a low profile, an almost European flat-sided silhouette and a gently tapering end. Gold or turquoise tops. Pale pastel two-tone paint in pink and white, sky blue and cream. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
