In the style of Hasselblad, serious, sculptural Scandinavian precision. Cubic square-profiled forms in satin black paint with bright chrome trim, or all-black crinkle texture, sometimes with brushed gold or exposed carbon-weave special editions. Modular blocks that clip together, with deep matte black, graphite grey and silver, accented by tiny engraved white numerals and a single tiny colour mark. The emblem is a small boxed H in white on black, with widely spaced thin capital lettering. Packaging is dark matte boxes with fine foil mark, soft black foam and leather-grain wrapping. Heavy, exact, minimal and engineered. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Hasselblad in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
