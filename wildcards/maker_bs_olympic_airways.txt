Styled in a design language of glamorous, sunny and jet-set 1960s and 1970s. Gleaming white and polished silver divided by a navy blue horizontal band with a thin sky blue stripe. Plain white or deep blue fields carry five interlocking rings in crisp contrasting colours, sometimes stacked vertically. Sleek mod tailoring, short hems in navy, white and sky blue with tall boots. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
