from the earth rises the Column of Glory, a vast pillar of white light, and up it climb countless tiny sparks of light freed from plants, animals and people
the Elect, men and women in their thirties to sixties in white robes and white hats, eat a meal of melons, cucumbers and bread, and sparks of light rise from their mouths up the column
the soul of Mihr, a dead Elect man in his fifties, in white, rises up the Column of Glory, met by a shining maiden in her twenties holding a crown, a garland and a white robe for him
