In the style of Missoni, a rhythmic textile language of zigzag chevrons, flame-stitch peaks, wavy ripples and thin stripes, knitted or woven as continuous repeating bands. Colour moves in gradual tonal steps, with rows of teal, turquoise, coral, orange, mustard, raspberry, violet, olive, cream and black blending into shimmering optical vibrations. Some versions use soft sparkling metallic threads among matte wool yarns, others are fine open crochet-like knit with tassel-like twisted ends. Patterns can run diagonally, mirror at the centre or fade from dark to light. Lettering is a small, clean, uppercase serif wordmark on a plain tab. The overall mood is warm, bohemian and artisanal, with a soft handmade hand. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Missoni in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
