In the style of Mary Quant, youthful swinging-sixties mod design. Shapes are short, tidy, simple and cut with sharp precision for a playful, modern energy. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Mary Quant in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
