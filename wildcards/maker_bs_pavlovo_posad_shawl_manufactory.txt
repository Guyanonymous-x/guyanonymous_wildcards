In the style of Pavlovo Posad printed wool shawls from Russia. Each shawl is a large soft square of fine wool or wool-silk, folded diagonally and worn on the head or shoulders, bordered by long knotted silk fringe and edged by a wide band of repeating pattern. The field is a deep black, scarlet red, emerald green, navy, ivory or cream background covered with an exuberant arrangement of big full-blown cabbage roses, peonies, tulips and leaves, with smaller sprigs and buds in delicate patterns. The roses are shaded in raspberry, pink, orange and white with green and turquoise leaves, outlined with fine lines and with a hand-printed, slightly soft-edged look. The borders show paisley and oriental-style curls, garlands and scrollwork. Some shawls are a plain solid colour with a rose border only. The mood is folk-luxe, rich, floral, romantic and decorative, redolent of old Russian peasant festivals.
