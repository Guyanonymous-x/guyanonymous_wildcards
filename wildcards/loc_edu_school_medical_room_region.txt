Scottish schools, similar
American schools, where a school nurse or health aide is common and the room is a health office with a cot, a scale and a fuller formulary
French schools with an *infirmerie* and in larger schools a resident nurse, which is a substantially more clinical room
Japanese schools with a *hokenshitsu* and a dedicated health teacher, where the room is also a recognized refuge and a pupil may be there all day, a genuinely different institution
