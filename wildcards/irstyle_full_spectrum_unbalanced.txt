Shot through a full-spectrum camera with the filter removed and left with the auto white balance unbalanced, the image shows the whole frame in a strong magenta-pink cast, faded and dreamy. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
