Styled in a design language of an industrial, deconstructed luxury mood built on quotation marks placed around ordinary plain words in neutral capital sans-serif lettering, as if labelling the object. Diagonal yellow and black hazard stripes, arrows with crossed diagonal bars, plastic zip ties used as tags, exposed construction tape, orange safety webbing, raw seams and large printed words. Matte black, white and off-white with photocopy-grey graphics and road-marking motifs. Pieces look half-finished, with visible inner layers and printed labels. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
