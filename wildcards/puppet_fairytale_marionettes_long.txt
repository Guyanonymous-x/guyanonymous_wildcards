a set of fairy-tale marionettes hanging from their control bars. Four carved and painted wooden marionettes hang side by side in a row, a witch with a green face, a long warty nose, a black pointed hat and a tattered black cloak; a woodcutter with a red beard, a checked shirt and a tiny ax; a prince in a blue velvet tunic and a gold crown; and a gray wolf on all fours with a long snout and painted teeth. Each hangs from its own wooden cross bar on a nail, the strings tangled a little, their feet just off the floor. Their paint is bright and glossy, and the wolf's tail is a tuft of real gray fur. __puppet_fairytale_marionettes_var__.
