Styled in a design language of laid-back California skate-park grit with a handmade, lived-in charm. Thick cream-white vulcanised rubber with waffle texture, tall white edge bands and rounded, low, flat silhouettes. Hard-wearing duck canvas and suede with chunky double stitching, scuffed and creased on purpose. Palette is navy, black, red, olive and off-white, with a small square black and white checkerboard as the signature repeat, plus wavy side stripes and skull-and-crossbones doodles. Lettering is a bold, slanted, hand-painted style with a long swooping underline, set in a red rectangular tab or stacked on boxy red cardboard. Photography is sun-bleached, grainy and street-level, with graffiti, concrete ramps and punk zine collage.
