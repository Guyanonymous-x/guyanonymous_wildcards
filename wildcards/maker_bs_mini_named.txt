Styled in a design language of tiny, cheeky and endlessly customisable. Compact boxy volumes with everything pushed to the corners, short overhangs, round chrome forms, thin bright frames and a circular winged mark. Saturated colour in tartan red, island blue, racing green, yellow and white, with a contrasting second colour on the top surface, bold twin stripes and a flag-print graphic in red, white and blue. Toggle switches, oversized circular dials and playful retro-modern detail. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
