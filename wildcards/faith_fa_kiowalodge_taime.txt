before the cedar screen, the Taime keeper, a man in his sixties painted yellow, with a cap of jackrabbit skin and a sun circle painted on his chest and back, sitting cross-legged beside the small feathered image with an eagle-bone whistle on a cord at his neck
at the cedar screen, the Taime shields, round hide shields painted yellow with blue suns and green crescents, hung on a row of slim cut cedar trees planted in the sand on either side of the small feathered image, with a fan of crow feathers below them
