Styled in a design language of thick, soft heather-grey textures with bold, relaxed proportions. Soft saddle-brown leather with stamped marks, collegiate mixes of wool with leather, and thick, generous forms. Colours are earthy and rustic, forest green, burgundy, navy, camel, cream, red and black plaid and maple-red accents. Cosy, outdoorsy, friendly and unpretentious. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
