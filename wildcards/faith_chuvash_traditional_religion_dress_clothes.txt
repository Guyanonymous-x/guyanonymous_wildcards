the old dress of the Chuvash people of the Volga region of Russia, adult women in their twenties to seventies in long white linen tunic-dresses embroidered in red, black and gold geometric patterns on the chest, the shoulders and the hem, with a colored apron, the men in white linen shirts with red embroidery at the collar over white trousers
white linen shirts and dresses with wide bands of red embroidery in rosettes, diamonds and eight-pointed stars, belted with woven sashes, worn by elders at a sacred grove with white leg wraps and shoes woven from strips of lime-tree bark
the men in long white embroidered shirts worn outside white trousers and tied with red sashes, felt hats on their heads, the women in white embroidered dresses with ruffled hems and red aprons
festival dress of white homespun linen stitched with red and black crosses and diamonds, worn by elders in their sixties and seventies, the women's heads wrapped in the surpan, a long narrow white head shawl embroidered in red at its ends, which hang down the back
