Styled in a design language of cheerful, toylike miniature design. Boxy rounded volumes with a tall top, a short front, round friendly details and a small smiling shape above a stylised D mark. Black plastic edging, plastic trim around openings, plain discs and big sliding panels. Two-tone pastel paint with a white top. Large clear graphics. Mint green, baby pink, sky blue, cream and sunny yellow. Compact, sweet and playful. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
