In the style of Coca-Cola, joyful, refreshing, nostalgic Americana. Dominant saturated red paired with clean white, used in broad flat fields with a flowing white ribbon wave. Lettering is a looping, handwritten-looking script with generous swashes, balanced by simple bold sans-serif support text. Shapes are curvy, contoured and hourglass-like, with glossy glass, polished chrome and condensation droplets. Round discs and badge shapes, vintage enamel sign charm, and a timeless feel-good, shareable cheer. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
