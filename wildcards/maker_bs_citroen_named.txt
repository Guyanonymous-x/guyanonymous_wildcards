Styled in a design language of inventive, quirky. Mid-century forms are streamlined, rounded and futuristic, with pods, smooth skins and low crouching curves. Palette of golden yellow, navy, vermilion, pale green, ivory, grey blue, sand and burnt orange. Materials include checked cloth, moulded plastic and pressed metal. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
