Styled in a design language of refined, understated, heirloom and timeless haute craft. Slim round or oval forms in polished rose gold, yellow gold or platinum with elegant fine edges, thin rims and hand-stitched alligator leather in brown or black. Proportions are clean with every control discreet. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
