Styled in a design language of calm, earthy, refined and architectural natural-wool tapestry. Flat-woven very tightly and finely in a rectangular format with a dense, almost smooth surface. The design is a symmetrical central geometric field with diamonds, stepped zigzags, concentric rectangles and terraced crosses, framed by a plain border and a crisp inner outline, with small tapering corner details. Balanced, precise and optical, with crisp straight edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
