Styled in a design language of brushwork is crisp, symmetrical and orderly, sometimes softened with pastel overglaze, and three small wavy lines recur as a quiet signature. Heirloom elegance, precise and serene, with an orderly rhythm of repeated motifs and a cool, clean blue and white palette throughout. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
