Styled in a design language of American luxury that mixes jet-age glamour with a sharp modern edge. Forms are long, low and wide with crisp vertical blades, razor creases and polished chrome edging. The emblem is a shield crest with a laurel wreath, shown as a jewelled multi-colour badge in retro work and as a flat monochrome black or silver outline in current work. Palette is pearl white, midnight black, champagne gold, deep ruby red and powder pink, with mirror-bright metal throughout. Materials include tufted leather, glossy lacquer, cut glass and stitched suede. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
