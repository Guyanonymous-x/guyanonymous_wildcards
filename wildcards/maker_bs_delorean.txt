Styled in a design language of raw, futuristic, unpainted retro-science-fiction design. Surfaces are bare brushed stainless metal with a fine linear grain, left unpainted and cool silver grey, set against matte black plastic trim and dark charcoal fills. Forms are flat, wedge-like and razor sharp, built from straight edges, hard creases and slanted planes with no soft curves. Slim horizontal louvre patterns and recessed slots add rhythm. The emblem is a chunky rounded-rectangle monogram of three bold block letters stacked over a small spaced wordmark, in raised or etched metal. Lettering is wide, geometric and blocky. Accents are minimal, tiny warm orange or red points on an otherwise monochrome steel and black palette. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
