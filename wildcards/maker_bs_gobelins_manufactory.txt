Styled in a design language of woven wool and silk that looks like painting, built from dense hatching of finely graded coloured threads. Compositions are grand and theatrical, with mythological and historical drama, hanging garlands and architectural frames inside heavy borders of acanthus, laurel, fruit and gilt-looking scrollwork. The palette is deep and slightly aged, crimson, royal blue, forest green, ochre, gold and warm brown with pale highlights. The surface is tight, flat and smooth. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
