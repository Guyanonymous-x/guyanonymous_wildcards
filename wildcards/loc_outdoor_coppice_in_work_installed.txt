a woodland worked in cants on a rotation
with one area cut this winter and others at two
five and ten years of regrowth all visible at once
hazel stools cut low with the new rods springing from them
standard oaks left through the coppice
a ride or extraction route churned to mud
a dead hedge of brash along the boundary
a deer fence or a brash barrier round the fresh cut
bluebells and primroses exploding on the newly opened ground
