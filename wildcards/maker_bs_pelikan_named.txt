In the style of Pelikan, plump cigar-shaped volumes in celluloid or resin with a translucent window. Classic looks pair black with green striped, tortoiseshell or black bodies and gold-coloured trim bands. Details are slender, with fine engraved scrollwork on gold accents. Later looks appear in bright blue, red and white stripes with thick, rounded bodies. Colour schemes use deep green and white, with a small emblem of a parent bird and chicks. The look is tidy, sturdy and luminous.
