Styled in a design language of sunny, relaxed beach-culture graphics. Palette is muted sage, teal, deep navy, sand, cream, burnt orange and warm brown, with brighter aqua, coral and lime for retro tones. Textures are soft garment-washed cotton, quick-dry nylon and slub knit with contrast stitching and small woven labels. Easygoing, sporty and sun-faded. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
