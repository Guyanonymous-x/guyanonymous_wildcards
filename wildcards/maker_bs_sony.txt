In the style of Sony, Japanese consumer electronics of the 1980s and 1990s. Compact black or silver boxes with beveled edges, brushed aluminium faceplates and dense rows of small rectangular rubber or plastic buttons. Portable cassette players in metallic blue and silver with orange accents, Trinitron televisions with deep black cabinets and flat-sided tubes, mini hi-fi stacks with tiny green and amber LED level meters and blue-green vacuum fluorescent displays. Fine stencil-like capital sans-serif labels, thin embossed model numbers, small rounded corners and engineered, high-tech miniaturised proportions. Later years bring minimalist matte black slabs with glowing coloured lighting strips. Mood is precise, technical and aspirational.
