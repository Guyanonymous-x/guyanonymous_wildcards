an ojo de dios, a God's eye of yarn woven on crossed sticks, a Mexican Huichol charm, hung on a wall
a diamond of brightly coloured yarn woven round crossed sticks, the diamond as big as a face, yarn wound tightly in bands of red, yellow, green and purple around two crossed sticks, tassels hanging from the bottom, hung on a wall. Around it the wall is plain painted plaster, a light switch and a coat hook beside the door, the floor below scuffed wood. Soft daylight comes in from a window to one side
