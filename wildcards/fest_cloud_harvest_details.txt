countless tiny beads of water clinging to fine black mesh
a thin stream of water running off the end of a wooden gutter into a jar
fog drifting in thick white streaks through the net
drops of water caught in the gray beard of an old man
wet black stones of the path shining in the fog
a spider web beaded with water between two net poles
white robes darkened and heavy with damp at the hem
a row of filled clay jars dark with wet on the outside
