Styled in a design language of black and white manga look with dynamic action lines, bold inked outlines and screentone shading. Spiky-haired muscular heroes, enormous eyes, exaggerated expressions, dramatic speed-line backgrounds, colour work in neon orange, tightly packed panels and a hot-blooded, high-energy weekly adventure look. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
