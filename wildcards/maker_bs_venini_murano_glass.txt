Styled in a design language of hand-blown art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered under clear casing. Forms feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean mid-century modern lines, polished edges and a tiny etched mark. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
