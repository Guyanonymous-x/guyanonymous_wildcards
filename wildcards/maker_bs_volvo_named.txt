In the style of Volvo, boxy, upright, sturdy, safe and rational Swedish design. A flat top surface, a large diagonal strip bearing a circle with arrow mark, square shapes, upright supports, a flat-sided body with a thin crease, black edges with rubber trim and a long flat roofline. Beige, forest green, mustard, silver, white and dark blue. Thick supportive plaid cloth or leather and flat panels with large clear faces. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
