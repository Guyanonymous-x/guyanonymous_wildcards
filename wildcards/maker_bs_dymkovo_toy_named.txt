Styled in a design language of hand-moulded clay coated in milky-white chalk for a smooth matte porcelain-like finish. Forms are plump, stylised, simple and rounded, with bell-shaped bodies, tiny heads and exaggerated puffy volumes. Painting is crisp, playful and poster-bright in red, orange, yellow, blue, green and black, using large circles, checks, diagonal stripes, wavy lines, dots and rhombuses, with rosy circular cheeks and small black dot eyes. Small details are picked out in glittering gold leaf. Cheerful, flat, geometric and slightly naive, like a patterned doll made of ceramic icing. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
