In the style of Elzevir Press, tiny pocket-sized proportions with neat, crisp roman and italic text set in narrow columns. Title areas have an engraved frame or a small emblem of a tree wound with a vine beside an old man. Typography is compact and clear, with small numerals, ornamental headpieces of printers flowers and small decorated initials. Paper is white to cream, and surfaces are plain vellum or brown calf with gilt tooling and small marbled patterns. The mood is compact, practical, tidy and scholarly. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
