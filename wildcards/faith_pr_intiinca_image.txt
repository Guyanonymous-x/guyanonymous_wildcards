the Punchao, the golden image of the sun, a gold figure of a seated man with sun rays around his head, is brought out from the Qorikancha temple on a litter and set on a stone seat in the plaza
the mummies of the past emperors, wrapped in fine cloth, their faces covered with gold masks, sit on carved stools in a row in the plaza beside the living ruler, each attended by servants in their thirties to sixties
a great disk of beaten gold showing the face of the sun hangs on the temple wall facing the plaza, catching the first light and flashing back a hard gold glare over the crowd
