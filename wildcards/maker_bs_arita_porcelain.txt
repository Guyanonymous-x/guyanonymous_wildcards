Styled in a design language of refined thin white porcelain with a milky body, clear sparkling glaze and a great deal of generous empty space. Enamelled touches of red, green, yellow and sky blue are used sparingly. Outlines are elegant and crisp, including faceted and lobed edges. Airy, graceful, calm and exact, like quiet tea house tableware. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
