Styled in a design language of soft, understated Australian rural craft. Surfaces are dense rabbit-fur felt, matte, slightly fuzzy and suede-like, in fawn, tan, oat, cream, stone grey, olive, charcoal and black, with a darker tonal ribbon band and neat bound edges. Shapes are generous, flat and gently sloping with a soft dent and pinched creases that look broken in with use. Leather trim, small metal pins and plain stitched details appear sparingly. The identity is a plain black rectangular label with a bold white block wordmark, plus a cardboard-brown natural packaging look with a stamped circular crest and a woodcut style emblem. Practical, sun-faded, rugged and honest. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
