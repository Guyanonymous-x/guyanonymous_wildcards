a German bisque character doll from the early 1900s, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her head is matte bisque porcelain molded with a lifelike face rather than an idealized one, a pouting lower lip, slightly puffed cheeks, a small snub nose and painted eyes looking off to one side under faintly frowning brows. The skin tone is a warm pinkish cream with soft blush on the cheeks and chin. Her hair is molded and painted in short brown waves on the bisque itself. Her body is jointed composition with chubby limbs painted pink. She wears a simple cotton dress with a white pinafore over it and white knitted stockings with black strap shoes. __doll_german_bisque_var__.
