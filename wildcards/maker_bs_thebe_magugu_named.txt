Styled in a design language of contemporary design that combines sharp tailoring with storytelling prints, with structured lines, pleats, crisp shirting and strong shoulders. Materials include wool, silk, crisp cotton and leather, with printed illustrations of portraits, plants, historical imagery and map-like graphics. Palette moves between deep forest green, burgundy, cobalt blue, mustard, cream, black and pastel pink, with precise seams, contrasting piping and nipped waists. Playful and intellectual, polished, artful and modern, in clean gallery light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
