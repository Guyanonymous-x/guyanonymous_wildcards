a row of antique ventriloquist dummies in a long museum display case. Behind tall glass panes, dozens of old dummies sit side by side on three stepped shelves, their carved and painted faces turned toward the glass. Some wear tiny suits and bow ties, some sailor clothes, some clown costumes, and their glass eyes stare out in every color. The paint on the older faces has crazed and faded, and some jaws hang open. Each dummy has a small white card at its feet with typed black words, the nearest reading "CIRCA 1890". The glass reflects the lights of the gallery. __puppet_museum_dummy_var__.
