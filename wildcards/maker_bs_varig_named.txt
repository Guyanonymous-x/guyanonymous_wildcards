In the style of Varig, clean, optimistic, glamorous jet-age design. Polished bare-metal surfaces beneath white, separated by a thin navy-blue line, with the name in large dark blue capitals in a clean wide sans-serif. A vivid royal or cobalt blue field carries a large circular compass rose emblem in white and gold with dark rays around a central disc. A later scheme becomes a gold-yellow rose on deep blue. Earlier versions show a winged figure in a black circle and a bold black stripe. A thin italic script word as a small accent. Lots of reflective silver metal.
