Styled in a design language of confident German heritage in royal blue and white. Palette is deep ultramarine, snow white and black, with classic green, cream and tortoiseshell amber stripes and gold-coloured trim for premium looks. Materials are glossy resin, striped celluloid-like patterns, polished gold finish, fine engraved scrollwork and clean enamel. Forms are plump, rounded and smoothly tapered with generous proportions. Later pieces use playful bright colour-blocked stripes. Reliable, traditional, handsome and well made, with crisp white and blue surfaces. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
