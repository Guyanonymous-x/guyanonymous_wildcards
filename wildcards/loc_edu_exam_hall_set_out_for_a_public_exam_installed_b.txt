the sightlines
single occupancy, all facing one way; a clock visible from every seat, which in a long hall means a second clock at the back and is the commonest layout failure; a clear sightline from each invigilator position to every candidate, which is what determines where the aisles run; the apparatus of the gymnasium folded and the markings of the sports hall under the desk feet, both still visible and both irrelevant for three weeks; a door that is kept closed with a notice regime outside it; blackout or blinds against sun on the desks; heating that cannot be adjusted in the exam window
