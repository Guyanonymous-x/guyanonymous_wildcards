an avant-garde experimental puppet, a large abstract figure built from sticks, cloth and found objects, about as tall as an adult. Its head is an upturned metal bucket with two holes punched for eyes and a strip of red cloth for a tongue. Its body is a frame of bare branches lashed together with twine, hung with torn strips of gray, white and black cloth that drift when it moves. Its arms are long poles ending in old work gloves stuffed with newspaper, and its legs are a pair of crutches. Bottle caps, keys and spoons hang from strings around its neck, and it is moved from below on long rods by puppeteers dressed all in black, their faces hidden in black hoods. __puppet_absurd_puppet_var__.
