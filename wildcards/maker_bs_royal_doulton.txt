In the style of Royal Doulton, English ceramics ranging from stoneware vases with Art Nouveau tube-lined flowers in earthy brown, blue and green, to mottled red-and-black flambe glaze with running streaks, and hand-painted bone china figurines of ladies in crinoline gowns and character jugs with grinning caricature faces. Glossy, richly coloured, finely detailed with pastel flesh tones, floral garlands, gilded rims, and sculpted ruffles. Sentimental Victorian and Edwardian parlour cabinet display.
