Styled in a design language of English stoneware in rich, earthy, drippy glazes, with chunky rounded forms, wax-resist patterns and tactile dark edges. Colours are deep brown, mustard, blue-green, olive, smoky grey-blue and cream, speckled and reactive with natural variation, in brown-and-cream swirled and pebble-textured designs. Heavy, hand-thrown look, warm, practical and rustic, with a retro 1970s feel, thick walls, soft rounded edges and a handmade, slightly uneven finish.
