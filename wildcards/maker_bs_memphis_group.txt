Styled in a design language of Italian postmodern design built from clashing geometric blocks, triangles, cylinders and zigzags. Cheeky laminate in bold squiggle, terrazzo and speckled confetti patterns in pink, teal, mustard yellow, sky blue, black and white. Asymmetrical, wobbly, totem-like stacking, tapered legs, dayglo plastic and glass with stripes and dots. Flat saturated colour planes with thick black outlines. Playful, irreverent, cartoonish, kitsch and joyful, with a loud 1980s mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
