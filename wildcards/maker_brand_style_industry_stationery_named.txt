In the style of Staedtler, functional, tidy German school and drawing sensibility in a distinctive scheme. Hexagonal forms are lacquered in bold bands of bright blue and black with a vivid yellow section, or a navy and red set, with gold or white lettering. Contrast bands are neat and the lacquer is flat and smooth, with a classroom-like feel.
In the style of Faber-Castell, a deep dark-green lacquer finish with gold hot-foil stamped lettering and a double-knight emblem. Forms are hexagonal or round, with a smoothly dipped end in colour and fine grading marks. Surfaces are dark green, dark brown or black with gold lettering, with rows of items aligned in gradients. Luxury versions use polished wood with silver or platinum-plated fittings and knurled details. The mood is classic, scholarly, European and craftsmanlike.
In the style of Uni Mitsubishi Pencil, a deep, mirror-like glossy paint finish in dark burgundy or black, with a single gold foil-stamped line of tiny type and a slim gold band. Hexagonal forms are smooth and perfectly even, ending in clean flat edges with thin metal trim. Packaging is slim and rectangular in dark maroon, black or silver. Clear transparent forms with coloured accents appear in the more casual range. The overall mood is quiet, luxurious, Japanese, simple and meticulously made.
In the style of Moleskine, sober, minimal and travel-worn elegance. Hard-wearing matte black surfaces like oilcloth with rounded corners, a single ribbon-like accent, an elastic band closure and neat hidden pockets. Cream-ivory tones, with visible sewn construction and honest stitching. A narrow black-and-white band wraps the outside. Thin sans-serif type, no embellishment, understated manufacturing detail. Palette is black on cream.
In the style of Koh-i-noor Hardtmuth, orderly, precise, industrial-vintage and nicely engineered. Hexagonal wooden forms lacquered in a strong yellow, with gold or silver stamped logos and grades in small neat capitals on a hot-stamped foil band, ends dipped in dark green or black. Slim flat cardboard or tin in yellow, with a diamond icon and crest, ornate serif lettering and fine linework. Items are arranged in neat rows of crisp lacquered pieces, each in a matching hue with a gold ring. Rubbery cream and pink blocks. Quiet confidence of fine craft.
In the style of Penkala, precise, slim, early Art Deco and quietly luxurious. Slim cylindrical forms in glossy black, mottled red-and-black, pale marbled tones, dark blue or olive, with gold-plated or nickel bands, tapered ends and long clean lines finished with a small ball tip. Metal parts have fine ribbed or knurled grips, silver or gold finish and geometric engraved stripes. Cases are lined with velvet in dark green or burgundy and gold-stamped. Everything has a hand-finished glow, smooth seams, tiny engraved lines and a small stamped maker mark, giving a jewel-like object feel in muted period colours.
