Chinese shadow puppets, flat jointed figures cut from thin translucent donkey hide, each about as long as an adult's forearm. The hide is scraped so thin that light glows through it, and each figure is dyed in clear bright colors, red, green, yellow and black, so the shadows on the screen are colored. Each figure is cut in fine detail, a general with a long beard and a tall plumed helmet, a lady in flowing sleeves and a scholar in a long robe. The figures are jointed at the neck, shoulders, elbows, waist and knees with small knots of thread, so they move freely. A thin stiff wire runs from the neck and from each hand to a slim bamboo handle, and the heads can be lifted off and changed. __puppet_chinese_shadow_var__.
