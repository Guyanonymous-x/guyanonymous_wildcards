Rennes-le-Chateau, the hilltop village in southern France at the heart of the Priory of Sion legend, a hoax of the 1950s and 1960s about a secret society guarding a hidden royal line. A small stone village sits on a bare hilltop above a wide valley, and beside its little church stands a small round-cornered stone tower with battlements on a cliff edge, the Tour Magdala. Over the church door the Latin words "TERRIBILIS EST LOCUS ISTE" are carved in the stone, and inside, just past the door, a crouching painted demon with a twisted face holds up the holy-water bowl on its shoulders. Above the demon four painted angels make the sign of the cross, the church walls are painted with bright scenes and the floor is a checkerboard of black and white tiles.
