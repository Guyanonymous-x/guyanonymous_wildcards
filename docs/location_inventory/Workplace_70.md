## Faith-specific prayer and reflection rooms

Expanding record 31. A generic multifaith room works by removing everything; a room set up for one tradition is organised around orientation, seating, ablution and what may be depicted. Where practice within a tradition changes the room's layout rather than its decoration, variants are given separately.
