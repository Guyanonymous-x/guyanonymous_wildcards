Styled in a design language of cheap, cheerful, geeky and tough. Lightweight resin in black, grey, silver and bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal and rectangular outlines and chunky rubberised edges. Gold-finished and brushed steel variants add retro flair. Candy pastel, neon pink, teal and sunny yellow colour-blocking appears on rounded jelly-like shells. Printing is dense, small and technical. Utilitarian, nostalgic and playful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
