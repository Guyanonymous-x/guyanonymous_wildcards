Styled in a design language of cheerful, practical and boldly geometric French design. A diamond emblem, rendered as a flat lozenge, outlined chrome rhombus or interlocking angular strips, repeats as the signature motif, with its form changing from line art to solid shape. Palette of sunny yellow as the hero colour against black and white, with bright orange, vivid red, sky blue and beige. Shapes are boxy, squared and friendly, with soft rounded corners, rub strips and honest flat panels. Lettering is a clean, sturdy, wide sans-serif in capitals, or a bold outline. Pop-art optimism, striped and checked cloth, matte plastics and tidy modular grids. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
