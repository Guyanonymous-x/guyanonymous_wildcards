In the style of Bang & Olufsen, refined, sculptural, serene Scandinavian luxury. Slender, tall, minimalist forms in brushed and polished aluminium, black glass and pale oiled wood, with thin plates that seem to float and tapered cone or pyramid volumes. Fabric surfaces in charcoal or warm grey. Controls are almost invisible, hairline touch strips or a single large circular element, with subtle white or amber light and hidden panels that slide away. Razor-sharp edges with tiny perfectly even gaps. Palette is silver, black, natural oak, dark walnut and occasional soft earthy tones. Lettering is tiny, thin and lowercase. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
