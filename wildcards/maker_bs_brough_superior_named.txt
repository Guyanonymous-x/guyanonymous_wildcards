Styled in a design language of understated aristocratic racing elegance and fine craftsmanship. Hand-built, long, low, sleek silhouette, nickel-plated surfaces with black lining, polished nickel and black enamel finish and gleaming exposed mechanical forms. Slim curves, copper details, leather on springs and brass accents. Everything is slender, exacting and hand-finished and quietly rare. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
