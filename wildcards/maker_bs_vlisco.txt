In the style of Vlisco, wax-print cotton cloth sold across West and Central Africa, with enormous vividly coloured designs printed identically on both sides with resin, leaving fine crackle veins where dye seeped through the wax. Motifs are oversized flowers, fruit, birds, feathers, fish, keys, eyes, circles, hands, clocks, fans, scrolling vines and bold geometric patterns, outlined and shaded like block-print drawings. Palette is saturated and clashing such as cobalt and orange, scarlet and green, yellow and violet, turquoise and pink, black and white, with the crackle texture. The cloth is cut into wrapped skirts, head ties, tailored dresses and matching sets. Exuberant, expressive, festive market-stall mood.
