in summer, the forest green, warm clear sunlight falling through the pines in bright shafts, the clearing full of tall grass and yellow and purple wildflowers, the birches in full leaf and only the highest peaks still white
in winter, snow on the roof, a thick smooth cap of white on the steep shingles and the stone base, every pine branch loaded and bowed, the horns on the wall rimmed with frost and the gorge blue with cold shadow
at dawn, mist in the gorge, bands of white vapor drifting between the dark pines and hiding the river, the log shrine a black shape in gray half-light while the snow peaks far above catch the first pink sun
