Sai Kulwant Hall, a vast open-sided hall with a high ornate roof of gold and pastel panels held up on rows of tall slender columns, hung with crystal chandeliers above a gleaming white marble floor where thousands of adult devotees sit in close straight rows
the Mandir, the temple at the heart of the ashram, a wide low building painted in soft pink, sky blue and pale yellow, with arched porches, carved railings and a row of small rounded domes along its roof
the front of the hall with a broad raised stage of polished marble hung with flower garlands, and at its center a carved throne chair with a tall back and red velvet cushions
