Styled in a design language of weighty cast-iron forms with rounded, bulging volumes and a quiet, austere, wabi-sabi presence. Surfaces carry rows of small raised bumps like hailstones in a tight regular grid, or finely cast relief motifs of pine, cranes, blossoms and waves, sometimes with fine silver inlay. Finish is matte black, deep brown or rust. Small knob-like accents, gracefully curved dark metal details and short elegant projections. Simple, heavy, contemplative and ceremonial. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
