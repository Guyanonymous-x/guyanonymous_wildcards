__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of restrained Parisian elegance built on black and white. Strict monochrome of black, ivory and cream with gold, pearls and chains as the only sparkle, and a quiet touch of beige or pale pink. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
