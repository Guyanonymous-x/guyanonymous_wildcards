In the style of Kosta Boda, Swedish contemporary art glass, bold, colourful and hand-blown, with chunky abstract forms, bright stripes, spots and swirled colour layers, and cheerful faces, birds, fish and clowns. Thick transparent bodies in saturated magenta, cobalt, orange, emerald and cool teal, glossy smooth surfaces and organic asymmetrical silhouettes. Joyful, playful and sculptural, with light refracting through thick glass. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kosta Boda in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
