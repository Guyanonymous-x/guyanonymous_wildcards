Styled in a design language of soft, delicate English drugstore elegance from the 1930s to 1960s. A pale lilac and white palette with violet and purple printing, gold touches and cream surfaces. Woodcut-style vignettes of flower sellers in flowing curved compositions. Pastel metal accents and thick printed paper textures. Floral motifs and fine line illustration keep everything gentle. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
