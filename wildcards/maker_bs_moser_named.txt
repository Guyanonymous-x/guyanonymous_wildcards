In the style of Moser, luxurious Bohemian crystal richness with heavy cut and engraved decoration. Deep facets, fluting and columns, gold-gilded edges and enamelled gold frames, with etched floral and animal motifs such as stags, swans and acanthus leaves. Intense transparent colour in alexandrite purple, emerald green, cobalt, ruby, amber and smoky topaz. Rich, jewelled, glowing in warm lamplight, blending Belle Epoque grandeur with Art Deco elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
