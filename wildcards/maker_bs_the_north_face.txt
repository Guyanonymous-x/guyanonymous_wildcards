Styled in a design language of bold, rugged expedition design with a technical, mountain-inspired look. The emblem is three curved, nested arcs forming a half dome, set beside heavy, tightly stacked uppercase sans-serif lettering in white on red, or black on white. Strong colour blocking in red, black, white, yellow, royal blue and teal, split by diagonal seams and panels. Materials are glossy ripstop nylon, quilted puffy baffles, fleece, matte coated fabrics and webbing, with reflective piping and taped edges. Shapes are chunky, padded and geometric, and the brand has a confident, adventurous, high-performance character with oversized graphics and clean flat colour fields. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
