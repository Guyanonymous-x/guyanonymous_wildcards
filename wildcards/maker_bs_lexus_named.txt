Styled in a design language of precise, sharp-featured Japanese luxury. The emblem is a tall angular letter L inside a smooth oval ring, rendered in polished silver chrome with soft gradients and beveled edges, or in flat black line form. Surfaces are impeccably finished, with razor-sharp creases, deep sculpted folds, fine woven mesh and knurled metal textures in repeating geometric patterns. Palette is liquid silver, deep obsidian black, pearl white, burgundy red, deep blue and muted bronze. Finishes are mirror gloss, satin aluminium, lacquer and stitched leather. The overall mood is dramatic, refined, tailored and futuristic, with attention to micro detail. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
