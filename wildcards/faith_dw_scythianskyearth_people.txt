Scythian riders in their twenties to their forties in pointed felt caps, belted coats and wide trousers, with bows in cases on their hips, gallop across the steppe on small sturdy horses
Scythian priests in their fifties pour wine onto the heads of kneeling adult captives in their twenties and thirties, cut their throats over a bronze bowl, pour the blood over an iron sword set on a mound of brushwood and cut off each dead man's right arm at the shoulder
Erik, a Scythian priest in his forties, strangles a horse with a twisted cord as an offering to the sky and the earth, Lena, a woman in her thirties in a tall headdress, tending the hearth fire of Tabiti beside him
