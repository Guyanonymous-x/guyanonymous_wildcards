a Ghanaian Akuaba figure, a carved wooden fertility figure of the Asante people, about as tall as an adult's forearm. It is carved from dark wood and blackened to a deep glossy finish. Its head is a large flat disc, wider than the shoulders, with a small face carved low on it, high arched brows, a long straight nose and a tiny mouth, the forehead high and smooth. The neck is a long column ringed with carved rings. The body is a simple cylinder with two short arms straight out to the sides and a small chest, ending at the base in a flat round foot. Small bead strings hang from the head and neck. __doll_akuaba_var__.
