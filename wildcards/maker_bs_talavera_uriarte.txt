Styled in a design language of thick glossy milk-white tin glaze over rounded, weighty, hand-made forms, painted freely by hand in cobalt blue with accents of yellow, green, orange, black and sometimes turquoise. Decoration is loose and generous, with flowers, vines, leaves, birds, rosettes and geometric borders in slightly raised shiny paint. Pinholes, pooled colour and small unevenness in the surface are kept as part of the charm. Repeated panels join into large blue and white patterns. Rustic, festive, colonial and clearly handmade. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
