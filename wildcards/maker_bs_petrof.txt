Styled in a design language of solid, dignified, classical and carefully made. Deep, glossy black polished forms with smooth curved sides, plain tall planes, rounded cheeks and thick tapered legs finished in brass. Warm walnut, mahogany or white lacquer with matte or satin sheen are alternatives. Interior metalwork is gold bronze with angled strings and felt. The name appears in gold letters or on a small brass plate. Older models have simple candle brackets. Ivory-white with ebony black contrast, and brass accents.
