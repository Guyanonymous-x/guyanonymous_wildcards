In the style of Moorcroft, English art pottery with tube-lined, hand-painted designs of pomegranates, orchids, wisteria, toadstools and Art Nouveau flowers, outlined in raised slip lines like cloisonne. Deep, jewel-toned glazes of royal blue, forest green, burgundy, plum and gold, with rich flambe runs and glossy, smooth finishes. Rounded vases, bowls and ginger jars, luminous and velvety, collectible Edwardian shelf piece.
