Styled in a design language of restrained Parisian elegance built on black and white. Strict monochrome of black, ivory and cream with gold, pearls and chains as the only sparkle, and a quiet touch of beige or pale pink. Forms are simple, boxy, balanced and tailored, with quilted diamond surfaces, braided trim, tweed boucle texture and interlocked double-C style marks. Nothing fussy, minimal ornament chosen with precision, luxurious soft materials and crisp craft. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
