In the style of R.M. Williams, refined Australian rural heritage and craftsmanship. Smooth, polished, single-piece leather wrapped with minimal seams and visible hand stitching in chestnut, tan, black and oxblood. Workwear fabrics include moleskin, oilskin, heavy cotton drill, gingham and check flannel, plus wide leather belts, in khaki, oat, olive, navy, rust and burgundy. The emblem is a longhorn bull head with wide horns and a bold red label, and the wordmark is a spaced, upright serif in cream or gold on deep brown or black with a founding year line. Branded leather patches, brass studs and embossed marks give a quietly premium, rugged and enduring finish. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words R.M. Williams in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
