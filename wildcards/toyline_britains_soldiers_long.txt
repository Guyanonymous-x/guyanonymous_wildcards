a set of early twentieth century hollow-cast metal toy soldiers, each about as tall as a thumb is long. The soldiers are hollow-cast metal painted by hand in glossy enamel, some with movable arms. They stand in neat ranks in ceremonial uniform, bright red coats with gold buttons, white belts across the chest and tall black bearskin hats. They are sold tied onto card with thin string in a red box with a picture of the regiment on the lid, and they stand in neat ranks on a table. __toyline_britains_soldiers_var__.
