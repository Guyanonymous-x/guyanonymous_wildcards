on the posts, feathered headbands and capes hung on the walls, long orange and black flicker-feather bands, net capes covered with rows of dark overlapping feathers, and plumed hairpins hung carefully from wooden pegs on the posts of the roundhouse, glowing softly in the firelight
on the bench in the foreground, baskets of fine weaving with feathers and beads, small round coiled baskets of pale sedge root and dark fern patterned in zigzags and diamonds, some covered all over with tiny red, green and yellow feathers and hung around the rim with white clamshell disc beads and bits of shining abalone shell
on the bench in the foreground, clapper sticks, split elderwood sticks, straight pale lengths of elder branch as long as a forearm, each split down most of its length into two halves that strike together when shaken, the uncut handle end wrapped with cord and painted with red and black bands
