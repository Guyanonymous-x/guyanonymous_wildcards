in the newer way, the lighters' way, an oil lamp lit before sunset burns on a shelf, its small steady flame the only light in the room, the stove beside it cold
on the Sabbath morning, the rooms lie in plain daylight from the windows, the curtains open, the stove cold, and the house tidy and still
