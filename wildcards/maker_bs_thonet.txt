In the style of Thonet, bentwood furniture made from steam-bent solid beech rods formed into continuous flowing curves. Slender round-section frames, looping circles and S-scrolls in the backrest, a round or oval seat of woven cane or pressed plywood with tiny perforations, splayed delicate legs joined by a ring brace. Finish is stained dark mahogany brown, walnut or ebonised black with a satin lacquer, with visible screw heads and metal collars at joints. Cafe chairs, rocking chairs and hat stands, light, airy and sculptural, mass-produced Viennese coffeehouse mood, later chrome tubular steel cantilever chairs with cane panels and black leather in the 1920s.
