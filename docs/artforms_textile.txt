=== TEXTILE ===
woven tapestry | wool, cotton warp, silk, occasionally metal thread | weft-faced weaving on upright or horizontal loom, each colour woven only in its own block and knotted off, full-size cartoon behind warps, shuttles/bobbins | narrative scenes with many figures, hunts, millefleur flower fields on green ground, heraldic panels, verdure landscapes | dense tight surface, warp fully hidden, colour boundaries as stepped or hatched blocks, flat rep-like ribbing, plain lining on back of European pieces, silk/metal areas have sheen | limited standard palette, wool dyes; greens drift to blue; back often brighter than sun-faded front | often very large, friezes and tall heraldic panels | hung vertically on walls, sometimes draped over tables or beds; soft raking light | faded greens, cut around doors/windows, burnt-out metal threads, reused as covers | looks woven not painted, picture made of blocks of coloured yarn with visible weave ribbing | maker_ref: Gobelins, Aubusson, Flemish workshops, Unicorn Tapestries
rug and kilim weaving | wool (cotton or wool warp), goat hair [mem] | flat tapestry weave on narrow horizontal loom, slit tapestry (vertical slits at colour joins) or interlocked wefts, knotted-pile rugs use symmetrical or asymmetrical knots, strips sewn together | geometric medallions, stepped diamonds, hooked and amulet motifs (elibelinde figure, wolf's mouth, scorpion, evil eye), borders [mem] | kilim is smooth, pile-less, graphic; warp shows only as fringe tied in bunches; pile rugs show cut tufts and a soft nap | abrash (dappled shade change within one colour) from hand-dyed yarn; harsh synthetic aniline reds and oranges that fade | runner to room-size; old ones in two or three strips about three feet wide | flat on floor or hung; daylight | worn warps and wefts, fraying ends, faded aniline dyes, few very old survive | sharp stepped slit edges, abrash, knotted fringe | maker_ref: Anatolian kilims, Persian and Turkmen weavers, Hotan (Khotan) finds

crewel embroidery | fine two-ply wool crewel yarn on linen twill | stem, chain, split stitch outlines, satin fill, couching trellis, seed stitch shading, French knots, worked taut in hoop or frame | Jacobean Tree of Life on a mound, curling vines, stylised flowers, long-tailed birds, pagodas, birds and plants | raised, woolly, dimensional; stitches visibly slightly fuzzy; ground partly left bare | blue-greens, brighter greens, browns, dull pinkish red, indigo blues and natural dye reds and yellows | bed hangings, cushion, panel | framed or draped as curtains/hangings, warm side light | faded wool, moth holes, ground browning | thick wool stitches on plain linen with leafy curling vines | maker_ref: Stuart-era English crewel, colonial New England crewel
cross-stitch | cotton floss (six separable strands) on aida or evenweave linen | counted X stitches in grid, partial quarter/half stitches, backstitch outline, hoop | samplers with alphabets, borders of flowers and animals, initials, houses, painterly pictures with many shades; borders on household linen | tiny uniform X's forming a pixel-like grid, tiled look; fabric holes visible on aida; count 11 to 40 per inch decides how fine | hand-dyed floss gives soft shifts; multicoloured shaded designs | from coaster to wall panel | framed behind glass, hung, or as cushion tops, cards | browning linen, fading, foxing, thread loose on back [mem] | visible grid of X's like a mosaic or pixel art | maker_ref: traditional samplers, Montenegrin and Italian reversible cross stitch
goldwork | gold/silver/copper-coloured metal threads, purls, spangles, silk, felt padding | surface couching with fine silk, bricking of passing thread, cut purl, pearl purl, check purl, broad plate, satin stitch over padding, or nue (shaded gold) | church vestments, altar hangings, royal regalia, saints' garments, bullfighter suits | glittering, light plays on facets; raised padded shapes; beadlike purl; visible small couching stitches in rows | gold, silver, copper, with silk colours and pearls or gems | small motif to full vestment | lit by candle or spot light for glint | tarnish (silver blackens), flattened purl, loose couching [mem] | metallic glitter in raised brick-wall rows and pearl-like coils | maker_ref: opus anglicanum [mem], Spanish traje de luces, Japan thread
sashiko | white cotton thread on indigo cloth, occasionally red thread | running stitch in dense lines (moyozashi) or single stitches on grid (hitomezashi), kogin diamonds in rows | waves, mountains, bamboo, arrow feathers, pampas grass, fish scales, interlocking shapes; mended work clothes and firefighter coats | quilted, puckered, layered thick cloth; evenly spaced short white stitches | white on deep indigo | garment, panel, cloth | worn or hung; daylight | patched, faded indigo, worn layers; boro mending [mem] | crisp white geometric stitch lines on blue | maker_ref: Japanese fishing and farm villages, boro textiles
blackwork | black silk (or red for scarletwork) on white linen | double running (Holbein) stitch and backstitch, counted, geometric filling, stem stitch outline, free-stitch shading | geometric repeats, small florals, curving stems with flower and fruit shapes filled with patterns | fine crisp thread lines, reversible when double running, tonal shading with seeds resembles etching | black on white, red accents, occasional metallic | cuffs, collars, small panels | framed or on costume; light | linen yellowing, black thread browning [mem] | looks like a pen and ink etching in thread | maker_ref: Tudor English blackwork, Holbein
needlepoint | wool, silk, cotton, metallic cord on stiff mesh canvas | tent stitch (continental, basketweave, half cross) on mono, interlock or rug canvas, petit point on fine canvas | pillows, ornaments, seat covers, purse, hangings, pictorial or photo-based images | dense, firm, slightly raised, completely covers canvas, diagonal slanted stitches; stiff, no drape | colours by yarn change like a pixel image; sheen on silk | small to carpet | framed or upholstered | worn nap, canvas showing, moth [mem] | tiny slanted diagonal stitch grid, stiff hard fabric | maker_ref: Victorian Berlin woolwork [mem]
applique | felt, cotton, leather, wool on base cloth | patches folded under edges and stitched with straight or satin stitch, basting or glue, reverse applique (top layer cut away) | quilt blocks (Dresden Plate, Sunbonnet Sue), banners, aprons, bags, mola panels, Hastings Embroidery | layered shapes sit raised from ground, visible edge stitching or satin outlines | contrast with ground | block to banner | hung or on bed | fraying edges, lifted patches | clear layered shapes with stitched outline | maker_ref: Panamanian mola, Baltimore album quilts, Hawaiian quilts
felted wool art (wet and needle) | wool, mohair, alpaca, other scaled animal fibres; silk for nuno [mem] | wet felting: hot water, agitation, bamboo mat or burlap, fulling; needle felting: barbed needles stab fibre into shape; pre-felt cut and laid like mosaic | wall hangings, rugs, tents, hats, slippers, soft sculpture, dolls, figurines, 2D wool paintings, Shyrdak carpet cut-outs | dense soft matted surface with no weave, fuzzy at edges, mosaic seams, dimples from needles | rich dyed colours, blended fibre tones, sharp contrast in Shyrdak | thumb-size figurines to large rugs | on shelves, walls, floors; soft light | pilling, moth, flattening | fibre blends without any thread or stitching | maker_ref: Shyrdak (Kyrgyz), Claudy Jongstra, Joseph Beuys, Polly Stirling

knitted garments and Fair Isle | wool yarn | stranded colourwork in the round, two colours per row, floats carried behind, steeks cut open for armholes | jumpers, yokes, hats, with OXO and peerie bands | smooth V stitches, bands of motifs, fuzz and halo in wool | limited palette of about five colours | garment | worn or laid flat | pilling, felted elbows | horizontal bands of small repeating motifs | maker_ref: Shetland Fair Isle, Aran cables
macrame | cotton, jute, hemp, linen, leather, wool cord | square knots, half hitches, double half hitch (Cavandoli), hung from dowel | wall hangings, plant hangers, fringes, belts | tactile knotted ridges, twisted ply cord, tassel fringe | natural cream, undyed, or dyed | wall hanging to bedspread | hung on dowel | fraying, dust | knot rows with long combed fringe | maker_ref: Cavandoli
