Styled in a design language of friendly, honest and gently witty. Smooth continuous curves with domed tops and rounded ends, or cheerful flat-faced boxes split in two tones by a white band. Pastel and sun-faded colour in sea blue, coral red, mint, ivory, sunshine yellow and cream, with slim chrome trim and a round blue and white badge showing interlocked letters in a circle. Plain painted metal, simple fabric, big clear dials and little ornament. Graphic advertising uses flat illustrated poster art, generous white space, simple sans-serif headlines and dry understated humour. Photography is airy, sunny and road-trip nostalgic.
