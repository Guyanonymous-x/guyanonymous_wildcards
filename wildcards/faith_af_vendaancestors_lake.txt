Lake Fundudzi, a dark lake among steep forested hills, its waters still, the home of the ancestors and of a great white python that guards it
the sacred forest of Thathe Vondo, tall dark trees hung with moss, where the chiefs of the Venda lie buried, a white lion walking among the graves
the makhadzi, the sister of the chief, a woman in her sixties in a bright striped cloth of red, yellow, green and blue, pours beer into the waters of Lake Fundudzi
