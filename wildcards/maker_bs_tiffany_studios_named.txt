Styled in a design language of American Art Nouveau stained glass built from hundreds of small leaded opalescent pieces in rich greens, ambers, deep blues, orange and red, shaped into dragonflies, wisteria, peonies, poppies, dogwood and geometric borders. Bronze forms cast as tree trunks, lily pads or twisting stems with dark green-brown patina, iridescent peacock blue and gold glass, glowing jewel-like light and lush naturalistic mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
