a medicine chest on a shelf with glass bottles and a drenching horn in it, which was the hut's real cargo during lambing
a crook hanging on two nails inside the door where it can be reached without entering
a dog's sack bed on the floor by the door, because the dog was not a pet and slept where it could get out
a stable door with the top half hooked open so the shepherd could hear the flock while sitting out of the rain, which is why the door is that shape
a kettle on the stove and little else in the way of cooking, because the food was bread and cheese brought from the farm
