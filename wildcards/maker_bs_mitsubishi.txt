Styled in a design language of tough, bold. It is always flat, solid and graphic in scarlet red on white or reversed in white or chrome on black. Shapes are angular, faceted and rugged, with diamond and rhombus cut-outs, aggressive creases and slanted edges. Palette is hot red, white, black, silver and gunmetal grey, with occasional rally-style blue and yellow stripes. Finishes are matte black, brushed steel and glossy enamel. Technical sport decals, chevrons and diamond repeats make a strong, corporate, geometric pattern. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
