In the style of Georg Jensen, Danish sterling silver in softly hammered, organic sculptural forms, with smooth rounded bulbous bodies and ornament of grape clusters, berries, blossoms and curling leaves, and later streamlined plain silver with sensuous curves. Fluid bulging shapes, dark ebony or ivory-coloured accents, openwork stems and cabochon stones. Gleaming matte silver, restrained, elegant and handcrafted Scandinavian luxury. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
