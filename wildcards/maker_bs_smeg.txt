In the style of Smeg, Italian retro kitchen appliances. Curvaceous, rounded rectangular refrigerators, kettles and toasters in glossy pastel enamel colours such as cream, pastel blue, pink, mint green and red, with polished chrome handles, trim and rounded corners. Large thick metal rounded door edges recall 1950s American cars, with a bold chrome oval logo on the front. Controls are chunky chrome knobs and rounded toggles. Surfaces are shiny and seamless with a soft sheen. Mood is cheerful, nostalgic and kitchen-boutique fashionable.
