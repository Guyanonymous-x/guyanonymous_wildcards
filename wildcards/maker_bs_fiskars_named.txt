Styled in a design language of functional, friendly, clean and instantly recognisable, with a single hot orange accent. Bright orange moulded plastic in smooth, open, rounded loops, paired with silver stainless steel with a satin finish, joined by flat black or orange pivots. Grips are ergonomic, with soft thumb curves and a slightly textured inner surface, black contrast parts, clean tapers and a sleek shape ending in razor-bright metal. Orange and black used together. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
