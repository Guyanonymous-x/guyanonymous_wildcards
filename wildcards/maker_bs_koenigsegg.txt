In the style of Koenigsegg, a hyper-sleek Swedish supercar with a low wide body, a smooth rounded nose with a thin split front splitter, narrow slit headlamps, and a bubble canopy cabin. Doors that swing up and out in a dihedral hinge, a rounded rear with carbon fibre rear wing, exposed bare carbon fibre weave, and wide rear haunches over large wheels. Wheels are hollow carbon fibre or forged alloy with a five-spoke look. Paint in clear lacquer on bare carbon weave, ice blue, gloss white, orange, and gunmetal. A ghost-like badge of a shield on the nose. Interior has a minimal cabin of carbon, leather and aluminium, with a small vertical display and a small wheel. Mood is exotic, clean and ultra-technical.
