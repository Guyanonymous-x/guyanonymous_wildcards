Styled in a design language of brash, theatrical and futuristic Italian drama. Extremely low, sharp-edged wedge and hexagon forms, razor creases, flat planes meeting at hard angles, and slender angular cut-outs. A golden bull on a black shield, outlined in gold on matte black, serves as the signature emblem, with wide, angular. Palette of matte black, deep charcoal and gold with vivid lime green, orange, yellow and purple, often as saturated flat blocks. Materials include carbon weave, anodised metal, hex-stitched black leather, suede and glossy lacquer. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
