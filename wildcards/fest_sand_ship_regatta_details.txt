long trails of pale dust streaming behind the racing carts
one wheel lifted off the ground as a cart leans in the wind
cracks in the dry clay forming small flat plates
a sail bellied out full and tight
heat shimmer blurring the far end of the course
tire tracks crossing each other in long curves on the lake bed
a dust devil spinning in the distance
goggles pushed up on a dusty forehead, two clean rings around the eyes
