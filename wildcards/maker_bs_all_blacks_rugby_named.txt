Styled in a design language of powerful, stark monochrome in solid black with white and silver detail. Heavy matte textured fabric with a subtle tonal pattern, close fit, reinforced seams and sleek white trim. Dark atmospheres with spotlights and fog, a rugged muddy earthiness, intimidating, minimal and disciplined. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
