Styled in a design language of a flat-grey bevelled surface treatment. Every panel and tile is light grey with a raised edge made from a white highlight line on the top and left and dark grey shadow lines on the bottom and right, while recessed areas are white with an inset dark edge. A flat teal green field fills the ground. Icons are chunky pixel pictograms in a sixteen colour palette, with yellow folders, dithered grey patterns and a four-colour waving flag emblem in red, green, blue and yellow. Blocky, beige-grey and nostalgic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
