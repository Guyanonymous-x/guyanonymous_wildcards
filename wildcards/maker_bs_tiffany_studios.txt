In the style of Tiffany Studios, American Art Nouveau stained-glass table lamps with domed shades built from hundreds of small leaded opalescent glass pieces in rich greens, ambers, deep blues, orange and red, shaped into dragonflies, wisteria, peonies, poppies, dogwood and geometric borders. Bronze bases cast as tree trunks, lily pads or twisting stems with dark green-brown patina, favrile iridescent glass vases in peacock blue and gold, glowing jewel-like light, lush naturalistic mood.
