crisp and mint, freshly printed
sun-faded with pale blues and pinks
folded with deep creases in a grid
torn at the corners and stuck to a brick wall
pasted over older posters on a hoarding, half peeling
framed behind glass in a cinema lobby
pinned up in a projection booth, curling at the edges
stained by rain and peeling
in a lit display case outside a theater
pinned to a bedroom wall with tape
