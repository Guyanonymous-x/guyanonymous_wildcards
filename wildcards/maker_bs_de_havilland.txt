Styled in a design language of light, nimble, gracefully proportioned British forms of the 1930s to 1950s. Slim bodies with smooth wooden curves, elliptical outlines and softly curved coverings that hug what is inside. Taut fabric surfaces in silver, with bright trainer yellow for contrast, and aluminium panels left bare and polished. Glazed bubble forms, fish-like pod shapes and simple round roundel marks in red, white and blue. Streamlined, clean and elegant, craftsman-built, with a hopeful pre-war and post-war amateur charm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
