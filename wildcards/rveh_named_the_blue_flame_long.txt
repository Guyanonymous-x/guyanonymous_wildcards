The Blue Flame, a long rocket body with a tail fin, on salt flats at dawn, a long thin rocket car as long as a bus, painted white with blue, red and black, a small cockpit near the nose, its wheels in narrow fairings and a tall fin at the tail. It sits on a blinding white salt flat that runs to the horizon, a support crew of people in overalls standing beside it for scale, tyre tracks stretching away behind it
