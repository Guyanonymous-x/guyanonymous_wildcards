Styled in a design language of blunt, cheeky two-tone design. Thick glossy near-black surfaces contrast with a bright sunshine yellow field, with a red oval or ribbon label and a tiny union-flag-like accent. Forms are squat, rounded, heavy and soft-cornered, with ridged shoulders and a plain broad base. Palette is near-black, yellow, signal red, green and white, with gold foil in luxury variants and playful colour swaps in limited editions. Finish is high gloss with sharp highlights. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
