v86 pinback button research (read 2026-11-04)
Sources: AIGA Eye on Design "The Pin-back Button Was a Place for Self Expression Before Social Media"; Printify blog on selling pin buttons; Etsy pinback button market page.
Content people make and sell on pinback buttons:
- Political and campaign: short slogans, candidate portraits, causes (suffrage, prohibition, anti-war, civil rights, ecology, animal rights).
- Music: band logos, performers, album covers, punk and rock buttons (goth, hip-hop, country, blues, indie).
- Fandom and pop culture: cartoon and comic characters, film stars, 1980s nostalgia (games, films, retro toys), sold in sets of 6-48.
- Humor and slogans: joke text, rebus wordplay, self-referential lines, edgy or witty phrases, one message per button.
- Advertising and commercial: product imagery, giveaways.
- Identity and self-expression: points of pride, personal style, accessorising jackets, bags and hats.
- Events and personal: party favours, team spirit, class years, new family member, custom photo and name buttons.
- Collectible mini pins.
Design traits: one short message, bold text on solid or checked borders, hand-drawn cartoons, photographic portraits, decorative art, glossy full-colour print on steel with a safety-pin back, round (usually 25 to 58 mm), sets and packs.
Rule for this library: invented bands, fandoms, characters, slogans and causes only. No real brands, bands, shows or people.
