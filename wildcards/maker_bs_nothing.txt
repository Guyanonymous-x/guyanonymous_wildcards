Styled in a design language of stripped-back, nerdy and quirky transparent design. Clear see-through glass and plastic shells reveal the neat inner structure of printed circuit patterns, screws and coils, with white and pale grey inner panels in tidy modular rectangles. The palette is white, black and clear, with a single tiny signal red dot used as the only accent. Soft rounded squares, flat edges and thin white line segments sit in symmetrical arrangements. Surfaces are frosted, glossy-clear or matte. Minimal, playful, technical and calm. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
