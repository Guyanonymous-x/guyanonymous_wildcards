Styled in a design language. Palette is washed black, charcoal, bone white, sky blue, sea green, red and sandy tan, with vivid tropical tones for retro pieces. Surfaces carry cracked screen prints, painted brush strokes, hibiscus and palm patterns, tribal flames and horizontal band stripes. Fabrics feel soft, sun-faded, garment-dyed, relaxed and loose. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
