Styled in a design language of a treasured, perfumed, royal heritage gift look. Warm vermilion red, glossy gold and cream with sandy yellows, framed by an ornate border and a central heraldic mythical beast with a big curling mane, raised paws and a crown, drawn in a formal crest manner. Metallic gold surfaces with red printing, softly rounded rectangular forms and pressed emblems feel like a gift tied with a thin ribbon. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
