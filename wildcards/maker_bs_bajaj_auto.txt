In the style of Bajaj Auto, Indian scooters and three wheeled auto rickshaws of the 1970s to 1990s. The scooter is a rounded pressed steel body with a bulbous front mudguard, a curved leg shield, a bench seat with a vinyl cover, and a small spare wheel on the left side panel. Paint is thick glossy enamel in simple colours such as sky blue, cream, off white, rust red and olive green, with chrome side badges, round headlamp with a chrome rim, and a stubby upright mirror. Auto rickshaws are tiny three wheelers with a front mudguard and one headlamp, a canvas or sheet metal cab with open sides, and a black fold-down hood. Typical paint is lemon yellow and black in cities, or green and yellow, with a black bench seat, a meter box, painted slogans, and plastic flower garlands. Mood is cheerful, unpretentious, resilient and familiar, packed with colour and rattle on a dusty street.
