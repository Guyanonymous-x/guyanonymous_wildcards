Styled in a design language of playful, loud, pop-art design in light, thin, flat moulded plastic with a jelly-like gloss. Translucent and skeleton-like shells reveal the workings inside. Surfaces carry bold graphic patterns, stripes, dots, squiggles and artist-style prints in neon and pastel colours. Markings are tiny and printed, while chunky accent shapes pop in bright red, yellow or blue. Colours clash on purpose. Cheerful, cheap-chic, collectable and irreverent, with an unmistakable 1980s postmodern pop attitude. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
