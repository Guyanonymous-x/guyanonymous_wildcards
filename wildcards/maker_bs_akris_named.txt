Styled in a design language of Swiss quiet-luxury built on architectural minimalism, with precisely cut, straight, clean lines in double-faced wool, gabardine, silk crepe and crisp cotton. Palette is restrained, with black, ivory, camel, charcoal, navy, sand and soft grey, with occasional accents of coral or deep green. Details are subtle, such as laser-cut edges, flat welt seams, bonded edges, hidden closures and geometric architectural prints inspired by buildings. Silhouettes are clean, sharp and balanced, with a refined, understated, polished mood and cool gallery-like light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
