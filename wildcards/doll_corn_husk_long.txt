a corn-husk doll, a folk doll folded and tied from dried corn husks, about as tall as an adult's hand and wrist. The whole figure is made from pale straw-colored husks, soft and papery, with fine lengthwise ribs. The head is a round knot of husk folded over a small ball, tied off at the neck with a thin strip, and the arms are one rolled husk pushed through the body and tied at the wrists. The skirt is a wide bell of layered husks tied at the waist and trimmed straight at the hem, and a small husk shawl is folded over the shoulders. The face is left blank, only the smooth curve of the husk. __doll_corn_husk_var__.
