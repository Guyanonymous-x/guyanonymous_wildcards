Styled in a design language of loud, bold, rock and roll and imposing. Matte black pebbled vinyl covers every surface, set against woven basket-weave cloth in cream, tan or black. Trim is gold or silver piping and rows of brass-toned metal studs and corner caps. Palette is black, cream, warm gold and chrome with occasional oxblood leather and brown. Shapes are blocky, rectangular and heavy with softly rounded edges and a stacked, monumental feel. Surfaces suggest tough road-worn craft, with bright metal glints against deep black. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
