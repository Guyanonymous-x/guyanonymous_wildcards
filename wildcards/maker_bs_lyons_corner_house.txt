Styled in a design language of grand British tea shops with white and gold exteriors, marble pillars, art deco chandeliers, small round tables with white cloths, waitresses in black dresses with white aprons, tiered cake stands, china cups with gold rims and cream and cherry-red decor. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
