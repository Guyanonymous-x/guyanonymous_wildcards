Styled in a design language of solid, imposing, polished, status-heavy Swiss luxury. Robust round forms in polished and brushed stainless steel or warm yellow gold, with fluted edges, satin and mirror finishes side by side and a tiny five-pointed crown motif as the recurring emblem. Deep forest green is the signature colour, paired with gold, white, black, blue and cream. Packaging and graphics use rich green leather-grain, embossed gold crests and heavy lacquered boxes. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
