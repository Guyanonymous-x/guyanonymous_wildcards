In the style of Billabong, sunny, sporty, laid-back, youthful beach culture. Lettering is a rounded flowing script with a wavy line beneath, or a stack of bold slightly bubbly capitals, often with a wave swoosh in teal, blue or black. Forms are quick-drying and practical with triple stitching, small patches and laced or snapped details, in bright tropical flower, stripe or tie-dye prints. Colours are ocean tones, teal, navy, aqua, coral, lime and yellow against white and sun-faded grey. Big screen-printed graphics use waves, palms, retro scenes, hand-drawn lettering, sunsets and cartoon creatures. Sleek, slim black technical looks carry contrast colour panels.
