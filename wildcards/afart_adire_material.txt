It is factory cotton dyed in pots of indigo, the pattern painted first with a paste of cassava starch that keeps the dye out
It is cotton tied in hundreds of small knots with raffia before dyeing, each knot leaving a small pale ring
It is cotton stitched in tight lines with raffia thread before dyeing, the stitches pulled out after to leave pale lines
It is cotton with the cassava paste pressed through a cut metal stencil
