Styled in a design language of English art pottery feel with hand-painted designs outlined in raised lines like cloisonne. Lush stylised plants, flowers, fruit and toadstools in Art Nouveau rhythm fill the surface with flowing curves. Deep jewel-toned glazes of royal blue, forest green, burgundy, plum and gold, with rich flambe runs and a smooth, glossy, velvety depth. Rounded, generous shapes, luminous colour and a precious, collectable Edwardian handmade feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
