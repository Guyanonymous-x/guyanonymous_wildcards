four rivers flow down through the gardens in straight stone channels, one of clear shining water, one of white milk, one of golden honey and one of deep red wine, meeting in a great square pool at the center
the four rivers spill from the highest garden in four waterfalls, clear water, white milk, amber honey and ruby wine, running down the terraces side by side between banks of pearl and gold
the rivers wind through green lawns and flower beds, their beds of white pebbles and pearls showing through the clear water, the milk river pale and still, the honey slow and golden, the wine dark red
