In the style of Parker Pens, streamlined, slightly tapered cylindrical forms, bullet-like and sleek. Surfaces come in glossy burgundy, blue, black or grey, with fine herringbone, vertical grooves or a smooth finish. Caps and trim are brushed stainless steel or gold-filled, with a ribbed texture and a distinctive arrow-shaped accent that stands proud. Trim is thin chrome or gold bands. The mood is mid-century post-war optimism with good engineering. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
