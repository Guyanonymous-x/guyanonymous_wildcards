an animal marionette carved from wood, about as long as an adult's forearm. Its body is carved in jointed sections so it walks on four legs when the control bar rocks, each leg jointed at the knee and the foot. Its head nods on a loose joint at the neck, and its painted eyes are big and round with long painted lashes. The paint is folk-bright, with flowers and dots painted along its sides. Strings run from its head, back, tail and each foot up to a single rocking bar, so it can nod, wag and trot. __puppet_animal_marionette_var__.
