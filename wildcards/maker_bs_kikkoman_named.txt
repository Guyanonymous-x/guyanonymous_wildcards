Styled in a design language of precise, balanced, restrained Japanese practicality. Forms are slender, softly faceted and gently tapered, with small glossy red cone accents. Materials are clear glass with dark amber depth, glossy black enamel. Palette is deep red, black, white, amber and soft cream, with small bright yellow or green accents for variants. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
