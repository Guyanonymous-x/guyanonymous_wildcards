In the style of Barefoot Colombo, Sri Lankan handloom cotton textiles, with striped and checked woven fabrics in bold bands of saturated colour such as coral, turquoise, lime green, mustard, indigo, magenta and white. Pieces include cushions, throws, tablecloths, bags, sarongs, loose shirts and dresses, in plain-weave and slubby handwoven cotton with visible warp threads, tasselled fringes and playful geometric stripes. Occasional hand-painted or batik motifs of flowers and leaves. Relaxed, tropical, cheerful mood, shown in sunlit verandas with palm leaves and rattan, with a handmade, unpolished texture.
