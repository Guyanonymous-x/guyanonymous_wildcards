in a turquoise lake below the mountain rise the lu, the water gods, men and women in their thirties to their fifties with human bodies above the waist and long green serpent tails below, jewels in their hands
a lu queen in her forties rises from the still blue lake, her upper body human with long black hair, a crown of small snakes over her head, her coiled serpent tail shining green under the water
a clear spring flows from the rocks at the foot of the mountain into a pool, small white stones and offerings of milk and grain set at its edge where the lu live
