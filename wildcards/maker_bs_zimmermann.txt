Styled in a design language of romantic holiday design with floaty, feminine shapes, broderie anglaise, delicate lace, ruffles, puffed volumes, tiered layers, smocking, scalloped edges and tie-front details in linen, cotton, silk and chiffon. Prints are painterly florals, watercolour blooms, paisley, ditsy flowers, polka dots, stripes and bohemian tile motifs in cream, ivory, blush pink, sage green, butter yellow, cornflower blue, rich red and black. Cut-outs and frills appear in matching prints. Dreamy, sunny, coastal, bohemian mood, among wildflowers and white-washed walls. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
