Styled in a design language of tough, proud Australian design built around a heraldic lion emblem. A rampant lion stands upright on a rolling stone, drawn in solid bold red or black silhouette, often inside an oval or shield with the capital name beneath. Palette is signal red, black, white, silver and sunburnt tones such as gold, burnt orange and dusty tan. Shapes are strong, muscular and flat with bold stripes and thick colour blocks. Finishes are enamelled tin, cast metal, chrome and flat, slightly weathered painted surfaces. Lettering is heavy, wide, slab-like capitals, in sturdy, no-nonsense proportions. Retro tin-sign treatments with scuffed edges, rivets and a lightly rusted patina give a rugged, confident heritage feel. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
