Cold, even and north, and deliberately so, one gauzed window on a north wall giving a flat shadowless daylight that never carries any direct sun at any hour of any day of the year, which is the specification
A light that falls across the slate shelves and makes them look wet even when dry
No direct beam anywhere ever, so the room has no sense of time of day at all beyond brighter and dimmer, which with the muniment room makes two spaces in this tab that are outside the daily cycle
A candle or a lantern carried in, and later a single bulb which in many larders was never fitted because the room was used in daylight and nobody worked in it after dark; and the one strong visual effect being a side of beef or a dozen hares hanging in flat gray light against white limewash, which is as close to a still life as this document gets
