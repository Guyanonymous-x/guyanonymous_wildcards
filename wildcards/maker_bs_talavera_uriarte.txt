Styled in a design language of decoration is loose and generous, with flowers, vines, leaves, birds, rosettes and geometric borders in slightly raised shiny paint. Pinholes, pooled colour and small unevenness in the surface are kept as part of the charm. Repeated panels join into large blue and white patterns. Rustic, festive, colonial and clearly handmade. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
