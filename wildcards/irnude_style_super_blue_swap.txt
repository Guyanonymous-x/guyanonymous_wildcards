Shot through a super blue infrared filter that passes blue and infrared light and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a rose-pink body against an aqua and turquoise background, the skin soft and glowing. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
