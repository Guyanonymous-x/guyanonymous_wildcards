far to the west rises Mount Kunlun, a towering peak of jade and snow wrapped in clouds, where the Queen Mother of the West, a woman in her forties in a phoenix crown and robes of many colors, sits by the Jasper Pool, her garden of peach trees behind her
in the garden of the Queen Mother the peaches of immortality hang ripe and pink on twisted old trees, and maids of heaven, women in their twenties in flowing robes, carry the peaches on trays to the Peach Banquet
Mount Kunlun rises in nine tiers from the clouds, each tier ringed with walls of jade, its gates guarded by the Kaiming beast, a tiger with nine human heads, and the three blue birds of the Queen Mother flying down from the summit
