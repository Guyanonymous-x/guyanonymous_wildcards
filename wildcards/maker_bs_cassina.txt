Styled in a design language of Italian luxury modernism with deep, plump, tufted leather in cognac, black and cream on thin chrome or lacquered steel frames. Sculptural wooden members with a ladder-like rhythm and pale rush, wide leather planes, tubular steel supports and low lacquered surfaces with reflective glass. Rich materials include walnut, rosewood, polished marble, burnished brass and saddle-stitched hide. Sharp proportions blend craftsmanship with industrial precision. Refined, architectural, masculine and expensive, with a penthouse mood. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
