In the style of Canon, dependable, practical, quietly handsome. Compact boxy forms with a black or silver top plane, wood-grain or fine black leather-like covering, and rows of tiny metal dials and small silver buttons. Clean rectangular layout with a large angular crown, round dials and thick levers. Black cylindrical forms with subtle knurling and a thin red ring accent for top-grade pieces. Lettering is a stylised red script mark. Tidy, well-judged and unflashy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
