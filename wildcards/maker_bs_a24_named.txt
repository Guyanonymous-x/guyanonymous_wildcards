Styled in a design language of symmetrical compositions, wide-angle lenses, naturalistic light, surreal touches and an arthouse, intimate, slightly uncanny indie look. Plenty of calm negative space, deadpan framing and subdued styling keep the mood quirky, tender and a little unsettling. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
