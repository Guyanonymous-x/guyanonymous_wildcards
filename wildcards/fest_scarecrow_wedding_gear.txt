two giant scarecrows built of bundled straw on wooden frames
a veil of an old white lace curtain and a dress sewn from bedsheets
a huge patched black suit and a top hat of painted cardboard
burlap sack faces with painted button eyes and stitched smiles
an arch of corn stalks tied with ribbon
a bouquet of sunflowers and wheat as tall as a person
pitchforks and hay hooks for building the figures
