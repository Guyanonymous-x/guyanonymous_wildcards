Styled in a design language of tactile, classic, clean and understated retro-modern design. Flat-topped compact forms in two-tone silver and black, matte black paint over metal, fine textured black pebbled leatherette and machined aluminium with crisp engraved scales and numerals. Thin red ruler-like labels and small red accents. The wordmark is bold, wide, black capitals with a red flash through one letter, set on white, kraft brown or vivid green fields. Packaging favours kraft cardboard with simple black print and bright green or red colour blocks. Calm, analogue, hands-on and neatly proportioned. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
