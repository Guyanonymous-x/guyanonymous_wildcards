Styled in a design language of practical, quiet, friendly, design-led domestic calm. Clean boxy forms with rounded corners in bright white enamel, or brushed stainless steel, with smooth tops, seamless profiles and slim, flush details. Early work adds a thin chrome strip and a small metal badge. Later work has large soft-touch rounded controls, gentle blue or green glow and neat brushed edges. Slim stacked divisions, recessed grips, black glass panels and chunky silver accents show a modular, tidy logic. Palette is white, silver and grey with minimal ornament.
