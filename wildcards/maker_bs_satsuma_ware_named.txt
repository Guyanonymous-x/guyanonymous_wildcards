Styled in a design language of jewel-like, glowing surfaces of cream-ivory glaze with a fine network of crackle. Every area is covered in miniature, gardens, mountains and blossoms in vivid enamels of red, green and blue, enriched with lavish raised gold and bands of brocade pattern at borders. Elaborate sculpted and pierced details. Highly detailed, warm, glowing, precious and ornate, like an export-era cabinet treasure. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
