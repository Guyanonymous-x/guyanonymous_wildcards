In the style of Vivienne Westwood, theatrical, rebellious and romantic British design that mixes punk with historical grandeur. A signature orb emblem with a ring around a small sphere and a cross sits in gleaming gold or silver metal, embossed, stitched or printed. Tartan is the main pattern, in clashing red, black, green, yellow, pink and lilac checks, cut on the bias and often twisted. Materials include crushed velvet, brocade, corset-like satin, leather, pearls, chains and safety-pin hardware. Lettering is a flowing, curly serif script in black on white. Shapes are asymmetric, draped, nipped and exaggerated, with ruffles and bows, both aristocratic and defiantly anarchic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Vivienne Westwood in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
