Styled in a design language of minimalist Scandinavian-geek graphic design. The emblem is a chunky black hexagonal nut-like shape fused with a bold round form, set above a tiny, lowercase, light sans-serif wordmark with tight spacing. Everything is black on white or white on black with exact grids, thin technical outlines, small monospaced captions and numbered diagrams. Materials are anodised aluminium, matte white plastic and soft silicone in signal orange, light grey, cream and occasional pastel or neon pops. Forms are thin, flat, rectangular and precisely rounded, like clean engineering drawings made physical, playful and deadpan. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
