In the style of Melissa, glossy jelly-like plastic moulded in one piece with sculptural bows, flowers, buckles and cutouts. The surface is shiny, translucent or glittery with a faint sweet-candy look. Colours are jelly pink, lilac, mint, clear transparent, baby blue, yellow, red, black and metallic glitter. Shapes are playful and rounded, with chunky bases, bubble-like ends, ribbed textures and puffy edges. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Melissa in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
