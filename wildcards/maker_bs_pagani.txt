In the style of Pagani in the 2000s to present, a handcrafted Italian hypercar with a rounded organic body, a short nose with oval headlamps that blend into the wings, and a bubble-shaped cabin. Rear with four exhaust pipes stacked in the centre, round tail lamps, and a tall carbon rear wing. Exposed carbon fibre weave in the body and fine polished aluminium details. Wheels are forged alloys with five bolts and a turbine look. Paint in deep blue, silver, black, and tricolour stripes. Interior has milled aluminium controls, exposed carbon, tan leather in fine stitching, a round gear gate, and many tiny details. Mood is jewel-like, rounded and artisan.
