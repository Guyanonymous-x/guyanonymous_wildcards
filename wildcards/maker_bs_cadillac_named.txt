In the style of Cadillac, American luxury that mixes jet-age glamour with a sharp modern edge. Forms are long, low and wide with crisp vertical blades, razor creases and polished chrome edging. The emblem is a shield crest with a laurel wreath, shown as a jewelled multi-colour badge in retro work and as a flat monochrome black or silver outline in current work. Palette is pearl white, midnight black, champagne gold, deep ruby red and powder pink, with mirror-bright metal throughout. Lettering is wide, airy, widely spaced capitals or a flowing cursive. Materials include tufted leather, glossy lacquer, cut glass and stitched suede. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Cadillac in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
