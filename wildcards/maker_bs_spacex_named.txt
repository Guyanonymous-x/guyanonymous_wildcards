Styled in a design language of minimalist, gleaming, modern reusable spaceflight. Smooth white cylinders with clean bold finishes, or polished bare stainless steel, with slender black accents. Lower areas are sooty and streaked with carbon from fiery returns. Few marks, no ornament, only function. Sleek, futuristic, confident, utilitarian and quietly daring. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
