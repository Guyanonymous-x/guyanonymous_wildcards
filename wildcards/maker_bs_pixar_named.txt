Styled in a design language of warm, emotional, polished three-dimensional storytelling. Chunky, rounded, toy-like forms with simplified, appealing proportions, big expressive eyes and exaggerated, readable poses. Surfaces have soft subsurface glow, subtle textures and tactile detail in plastic, fabric, metal and skin. Environments are richly detailed and lived-in. Humorous, heartfelt and family-friendly. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
