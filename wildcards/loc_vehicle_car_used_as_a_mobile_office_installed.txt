an ordinary car in which every surface has been reassigned, the passenger seat as a desk
the passenger footwell as a filing system
the boot as a stockroom with a box per product line
the 12-volt socket running an inverter that runs a laptop charger
the phone mount as the single most important fitting because the car is a call center
a dash top used as a shelf despite the sun
a sun visor with a pen and a parking card clipped to it
cup holders holding two mugs and a phone
a seat that has done 40,000 miles a year and shows it in one bolster
