In the style of Brazilian-built small hatchbacks and sedans of the 1970s and 80s, a rounded boxy body with rear-engine air-cooled shapes, round headlamps in chrome bezels, a small badge on the nose, thin chrome bumpers with rubber strips and rounded bubble pillars. Hot-weather features like pop-out quarter vents, tiny steel wheels with dog-dish hubcaps and thin-line tyres. Beach buggies with a wide open body, bright paint and a bare dash. Colours of Brasilia yellow, Sahara beige, bright orange, green and mustard. Simple, sunny and pragmatic.
