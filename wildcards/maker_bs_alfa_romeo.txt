In the style of Alfa Romeo in the 1960s and 1970s, a low slim Italian sports coupe or spider with a distinctive triangular shield nose at the front, a circular badge with a red cross and a crowned serpent, and flanking mesh grille. Rounded headlamps set under glass or in the wings, long bonnet, small cabin, pointed tapered tail, and a Kamm-cut rear. Chrome bumpers, thin pillars, and alloy wheels with five holes or steel wheels with pretty hubcaps. Paint in alfa red, white, light blue, silver and dark green. Interior has leather or vinyl bucket seats, a large-rim wooden wheel with drilled aluminium spokes, deep-set round dials, and a floor-mounted shifter. Mood is passionate, lively and graceful.
