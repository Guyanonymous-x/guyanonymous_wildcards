Styled in a design language of thin-walled white porcelain with a glassy, faintly bluish transparent glaze, painted under the glaze in rich cobalt blue. Motifs include coiling lotus scrolls, dragons chasing flaming pearls, fish and water weed, peony sprays and bands of cloud and wave forms, repeated in tight concentric bands. Cobalt shades range from pale silvery blue to deep inky blue with dark pooled spots. Forms are elegant and balanced, with rounded shoulders and crisp edges. Overglaze enamel variants add coral red, apple green, yellow and turquoise outlined in fine black. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
