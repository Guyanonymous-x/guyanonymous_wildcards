In the style of Kala Raksha, artisan-made embroidery, applique and patchwork on handwoven cotton. Precisely counted triangular geometry is stitched in satin stitch, forming tiny diamonds, zig-zags and stars in mirror-like symmetry. Colours are bold contrasts of magenta, orange, yellow, green, royal blue and white on black or deep indigo cloth, with quilted patchwork in reds and blacks. Small mirrors are set at the centre of motifs. Meticulous, handmade, vibrant, desert-village craft mood.
