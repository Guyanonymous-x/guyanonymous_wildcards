In the style of Li-Ning, Chinese sportswear with a bold national flavour. The logo is a red geometric stroke that looks like a flowing check mark or abstract gymnast, sitting over bold red Chinese characters, often on a white or black ground. Products appear in strong colour blocks of imperial red, black, white and metallic gold, with big rectangle patches and block lettering on the chest. Basketball shoes have high chunky midsoles with visible cushioning pods, bold geometric panels, and details such as stripes or ornate dragon scale texture. Apparel includes tracksuits with stand collars, broad contrast stripes down the arms and legs, glossy nylon jackets and retro 1990s team colourways in red, yellow and green. Runway collections add Chinese calligraphy, cloud and dragon motifs, brocade panels and oversize silhouettes. Store signage is bright red with white text. Mood is energetic, patriotic, confident, youthful and a mix of retro sports nostalgia with new streetwear flair.
