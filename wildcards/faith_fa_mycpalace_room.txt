the room being the temple in the cult quarter at Mycenae, a small dim room with a low plastered bench and stepped platforms along its back wall, crowded with gray clay figures knee-high to a person, hollow bodies as jars with stiff raised arms and staring faces, and coiled clay snakes lying among them
