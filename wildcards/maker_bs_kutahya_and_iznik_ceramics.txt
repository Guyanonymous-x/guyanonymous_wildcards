Styled in a design language of intense colour painted under crystal-clear glaze, with cobalt blue, turquoise, sage green, tomato red and white. Symmetrical, flowing patterns of tulips, carnations, hyacinths, rosebuds, long saw-edged leaves and curling arabesques repeat across surfaces, with radiating floral rosettes and tile-like repetition. Glossy, bright, crisp and elegant, evoking mosque walls and bazaars. Colour areas are crisp and flat, with fine dark outlines and bright white ground showing between motifs. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
