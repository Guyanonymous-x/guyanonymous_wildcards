In the style of Mahindra, Indian off-road SUVs and pickups with a tall upright slab-sided body, a vertical seven-slot or multi-bar grille with a bold chrome ring, large round or squared headlamps, flat bonnet, black plastic bumpers, and a stubby short rear with the spare wheel on the tailgate. Big square wheel arches, steel or chunky alloy wheels, running board, a snorkel, and a stiff rugged stance. Inside, big dials, a plain dash, simple fabric seats. Newer models have a sharp twin-peak badge and twin-line LED headlamps. Colours of napoli black, white, red rage and dune beige. Tough and honest.
