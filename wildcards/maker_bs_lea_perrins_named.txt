In the style of Lea & Perrins, fussy, crowded Victorian heritage design. Squat rectangular forms with short necks are wrapped in cream-white paper printed in black, with ornate Victorian serif and slab lettering mixed with fine engraved text, plus orange-ochre panels and a small coat-of-arms-like crest. Folded wrapping, paper seals and black accents add craft detail. Palette is orange, black and cream with an aged, tea-stained patina. Mood is old English, authentic, reassuring and quietly traditional, with dense, symmetrical text blocks.
