Styled in a design language of plain, flat, symmetrical and repetitive, like a uniform grid of identical cylinders. A bright red upper two-thirds meets a white lower third, divided by a thin gold band. A gold medallion seal with scalloped edges sits at the centre with small ornamental flanking details. The main mark is a thin red connected looping script, with white sans-serif capitals for secondary text on red. Palette is red, white and gold. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
