In the style of Fujifilm, tactile, classic, clean and understated retro-modern design. Compact lean forms with a flat top plane in silver and black, matte black paint over metal and fine textured grips. Many machined aluminium dials with engraved numerals, and chunky rings with engraved scales wrapping round cylinders. A small offset window in one corner, red ruler-like marks and neat black pebbled texture. Lettering is crisp white capitals. Feels hands-on, analogue and calm. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
