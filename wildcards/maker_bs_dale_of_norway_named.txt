Styled in a design language of thick, dense, slightly fuzzy, close-knit wool with a patterned band in stranded colourwork, circular bands of stars, snowflakes, diamonds, animals, zigzags and stepped borders around the upper body. Colours are strong, navy, red, white and grey as the classic combination, plus charcoal, forest green and cream. Plain ribbed edges and pewter or metal fastenings. Pattern is clear and graphic in neat repeating bands. Snug athletic fit and crisp zigzag edging. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
