In the style of Mazda, calm, elegant, restrained design rooted in Japanese minimalism and a feeling of motion even when still. Surfaces are smooth and sculpted, with soft flowing highlights and reflections instead of hard creases, long graceful proportions and fine, slender chrome lines. The emblem is a slim circle holding an open winged capital M, drawn in a thin metallic outline, and the wordmark is airy, widely spaced and light in weight. Palette is deep crystal red, machine grey, soft pearl white, charcoal and black. Materials include matte metal, burgundy and cream leather, natural wood and fine stitching. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
