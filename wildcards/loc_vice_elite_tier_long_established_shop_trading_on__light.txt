Bowed plate-glass windows admitting good daylight to the front third of the shop and almost none to the back, which is how a Victorian shop was lit and is inadequate now, original pendant fittings converted to electricity and under-lit, cabinet lighting added in the 1970s and never updated, a cash desk kiosk with its own lamp, a basement lit by two bulkheads, and an interior whose light is beautiful at the window and poor everywhere else, which is the single greatest practical complaint of everybody who works there
