Styled in a design language of English Arts and Crafts pattern with dense, flat, intertwined foliage, acanthus leaves, willow boughs, pomegranates, tulips and small birds. Designs are symmetrical, rhythmic and repeating, with curving stems that flow in all directions and leave no empty ground. Palette is natural, earthy and slightly muted, with indigo, moss and sage green, madder red, mustard, cream, russet and deep forest tones. Outlines are crisp and hand-drawn, as in woodblock printing, with a flat tapestry-like depth. Handcrafted, rich, nature-loving and timeless, rooted in medieval craft. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
