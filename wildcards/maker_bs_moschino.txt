Styled in a design language of witty, surreal, cheeky Italian pop design. Recurring motifs are a solid red heart, a pair of mirrored question marks inside a circle, a teddy-bear silhouette drawn in simple black and white line, and oversized playful icons. Palette is jet black, chalk white, signal red, hot pink, sunny yellow and glittering gold. Surfaces include glossy enamel, black sequins, gold foil, quilted metallic sheen and flat printed colour. Trompe-l'oeil humour turns ordinary forms into ironic, exaggerated parodies. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
