Styled after a smooth, rounded, off-white games console with a gently curved case, a spiral swirl in orange at the top, a glowing round disc lid, a small display window on controllers and a cool, futuristic, minimal feel. Pale grey plastic, translucent blue and clear controllers with a built-in memory slot. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
