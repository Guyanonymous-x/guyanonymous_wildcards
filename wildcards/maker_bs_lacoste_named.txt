In the style of Lacoste, polished tennis-club elegance with a playful, confident wink. Textured cotton pique with a small knit weave, ribbed edges and a clean, relaxed fit. A single small green crocodile sits quietly as the only mark, while campaigns scale it up into giant sculptural reptiles in layered green paper and fabric facets, set on bright backdrops. Palette is tennis white plus deep bottle green, navy, red, sky blue and yellow, in solid blocks and bold stripes. Terry cloth bands, white canvas, crisp laundered surfaces and white court lines add sport cues. Lettering is simple, slim, spaced sans-serif capitals. Photography is bright, sunlit and graphic, with energetic poses against flat colour fields and lawn green.
