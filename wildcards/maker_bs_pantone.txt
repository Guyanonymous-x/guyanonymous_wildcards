Styled in a design language of flat solid swatches of saturated colour printed on glossy coated card, and a thin white border. Long narrow strips bound at one end so the colours spread in a rainbow fan, tidy grids of colour squares in every hue arranged by tone. Clean neutral labelling, white background, consistent proportions, bold flat saturated colours such as vivid red, teal, violet and orange. A neat reference system with a catalogue feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
