In the style of Kashan Carpet Workshops, closely knotted wool and silk carpets in a luxurious, deep colour scheme dominated by claret and ruby red, midnight blue and ivory, with a velvet-soft short pile. A large lobed central medallion with pendants floats on a field of dense scrolling vines, palmettes and arabesque blossoms, framed by a wide border with repeating floral cartouches. Fine detail, perfect symmetry and a slightly glossy sheen make them formal and elegant. Corners repeat quarter medallions, and tiny outlines in ivory define the stems and leaves.
