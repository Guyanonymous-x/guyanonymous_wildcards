Styled in a design language of friendly, honest and gently witty. Smooth continuous curves with domed tops and rounded ends, or cheerful flat-faced boxes split in two tones by a white band. Pastel and sun-faded colour in sea blue, coral red, mint, ivory, sunshine yellow and cream, with slim chrome trim and a round blue and white badge showing interlocked letters in a circle. Plain painted metal, simple fabric, big clear dials and little ornament. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
