In the style of Meccano, an technical-manual look built from printed panels in strong red, deep green, cream and mustard yellow. Bold slab and block capitals with heavy outline and drop shadow, set inside framed banner panels, often with a big stylised letter M in a contrasting square. Fine ruled borders, numbered labels, line-engraved diagrams in black ink on aged cream paper and tidy rows of evenly spaced round perforations. Metal surfaces in red enamel, green enamel, bright zinc and polished brass, joined by small hex bolts. Edwardian, orderly, industrious, tactile and nostalgic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Meccano in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
