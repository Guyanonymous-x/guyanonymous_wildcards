Styled in a design language of cheap, cheerful, geeky and tough. Lightweight resin in black, grey, silver and bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal and rectangular outlines and chunky rubberised edges. Gold-finished and brushed steel variants add retro flair. Candy pastel, neon pink, teal and sunny yellow colour-blocking appears on rounded jelly-like shells. Printing is dense, small and technical. Utilitarian, nostalgic and playful. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
