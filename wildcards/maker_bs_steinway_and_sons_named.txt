In the style of Steinway and Sons, formal grandeur in black and gold. A mirror-bright black lacquer finish has deep, flawless reflections and sweeping, flowing curves. Gold appears as a small lyre emblem with a decorative harp-like outline, and in widely spaced, thin serif capitals reading the name. Alternate finishes include dark mahogany, burl walnut, ebony and ivory-white with warm satin sheen. Details use polished brass, gilded cast ornament and tapered, turned supports with subtle carving. Palette is black, gold, ivory and rich dark wood. Restrained, noble, symmetrical and timeless, with a refined, expensive hush.
