a great pair of golden scales hangs in the sky, the good deeds of a man in his fifties piled in one pan as white light and his bad deeds in the other as black stones, angels in white watching beside it
each person holds the book of their deeds, the righteous taking it in the right hand and the wicked in the left hand, a crowd of men and women in their twenties to eighties reading in fear and joy
on a manuscript page the Prophet Muhammad stands at the basin of Kawthar, his face veiled in white and his head in a flaming gold halo, giving water to a line of believers in their thirties to sixties
the bridge of the Sirat stretches over the pit of fire as a thin bright line, some souls running across it as fast as lightning, others crawling, and some falling from it into the flames below
