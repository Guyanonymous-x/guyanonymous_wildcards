Styled in a design language of clean, technical, optimistic American spaceflight design. Bright white fields carry bold, geometric graphics in deep blue, signal red and black. A round blue disc speckled with white stars carries a vivid red chevron swoosh and a white orbit line. A second mark uses unbroken, rounded, in red or dark grey. Colour stripes in red and blue run in parallel, with small golden wing-like details. Surfaces are matte technical fabric, glossy white enamel, golden foil and fine stitching. Orderly, hopeful, precise and quietly heroic, with a cool minimalist space-age modernism. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
