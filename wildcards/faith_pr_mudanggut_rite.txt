the mudang stands a whole slaughtered pig upright on the points of a large iron trident and it stays balanced, taken as proof that the gods have accepted the offering
the mudang, in trance as the spirit of a dead man, weeps and speaks to the dead man's widow, Kyung-hee, a woman in her seventies, who sits on the floor before the mudang clutching a handkerchief
