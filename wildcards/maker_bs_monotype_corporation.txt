In the style of Monotype Corporation, printed matter and specimen sheets showing carefully revived classical roman typefaces and crisp new classics. Text is set in fine serif with high contrast, bracketed serifs, balanced proportions and even colour, along with a few geometric sans-serif and slab designs. Specimen pages are neat, black ink on cream paper, with rows of alphabets in graduated sizes, small numbers and ornamental rules. Layouts use centred headings, small caps, letter-spaced capitals and discreet fleurons. The overall look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, in the manner of a well-printed British book or newspaper.
