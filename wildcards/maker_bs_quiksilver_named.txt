Styled in a design language of palette is washed black, charcoal, bone white, sky blue, sea green, red and sandy tan, with vivid tropical tones for retro pieces. Surfaces carry cracked screen prints, painted brush strokes, hibiscus and palm patterns, tribal flames and horizontal band stripes. Fabrics feel soft, sun-faded, garment-dyed, relaxed and loose. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
