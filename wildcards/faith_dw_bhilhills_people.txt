Bhil worshippers climb the hill carrying new clay horses on their heads, men in their thirties to their sixties in white dhotis and bright red or orange turbans, women in their twenties to their fifties in wide red and yellow skirts and dark headcloths, silver bangles on their arms
a Bhil badwa, a healer and priest in his fifties with a gray beard and a white turban, sits before the shrine with a fire, offering coconut and grain while men and women in their twenties to their seventies sit behind him
below the hill lies a Bhil village of scattered houses with mud walls and red clay tile roofs, each among its own fields, women in their thirties grinding grain and men in their forties bringing goats home
