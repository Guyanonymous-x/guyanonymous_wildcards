Intense transparent colour in alexandrite purple, emerald green, cobalt, ruby, amber and smoky topaz. Rich, jewelled, glowing in warm lamplight, blending Belle Epoque grandeur with Art Deco elegance. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
