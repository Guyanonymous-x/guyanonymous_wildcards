In the style of Pilot Namiki Maki-e Pens, slim forms in glossy black or red lacquer decorated with delicate gold, silver and coloured powder. Tiny cranes, dragonflies, bamboo, plum blossom, waves and mountains appear in raised, sparkling gold with soft gradations, often covering the whole surface. Metal parts are gold or rhodium with fine engraved scrolls. Forms are smooth and elegant with rounded ends, presented in pale paulownia wood. The mood is quiet luxury, patient craft and nature-inspired. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
