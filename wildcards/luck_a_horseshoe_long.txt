an old rusty iron horseshoe with nail holes, nailed above a door with its open ends pointing up, the horseshoe as big as a hand spread wide, thick and worn on one side, the eight square nail holes rusted, two big nails holding it to the door, its open ends pointing upward like a cup, over a stable door. Around it the wall is plain painted plaster, a light switch and a coat hook beside the door, the floor below scuffed wood. Soft daylight comes in from a window to one side
