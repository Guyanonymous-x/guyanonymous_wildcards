on the altar lie the bloodless offerings, small heaps of husked white rice, ginger roots, bananas and fresh flowers on fresh green banana leaves
each worshipper sets down a small bamboo cup of rice and a few wild flowers on the altar, one after another, and bows
an elder in his sixties offers one red rooster, the only animal of the rite, cutting its throat with a knife over a leaf on the ground outside, its blood dripping onto the leaf
