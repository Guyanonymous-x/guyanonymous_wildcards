at Naicobocobo the soul of a chief in his sixties, in a wrap of masi and a necklace of whale teeth, leaps from the high cliff of Nacadruta into the sea far below
in the old days the widows of a dead chief, women in their thirties to fifties, are strangled with a cord of bark fiber by their kin to go with him to Bulu, their bodies laid beside his
the souls of the dead stand in a row at the cliff edge, one after another falling into the deep blue water, white spray rising below
