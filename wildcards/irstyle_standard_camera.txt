Shot through a standard 720nm infrared filter and shown straight from the camera, the image shows an almost monochrome image with a faint cool gray-blue cast, pale foliage and a darker sky. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
