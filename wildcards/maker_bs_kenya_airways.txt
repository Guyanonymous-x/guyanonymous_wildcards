In the style of Kenya Airways, an African airline identity in red, white and black. Aircraft have a white fuselage with the airline name in black italic capitals along the forward body, and a bright red tail fin carrying a stylised K inside a circle, in white and black. Older aircraft had four coloured stripes of red, green, black and white running the length of the fuselage in the colours of the national flag. Engines are red or white. A small flag appears near the cockpit, along with the slogan about pride of Africa. Cabins use red and charcoal seats with white head covers, grey carpets, and patterned fabrics with woven motifs. Crew wear red uniforms with black and white scarves and neat caps. Mood is warm, hospitable, proud, bright and savannah-like, with red against clear blue sky.
