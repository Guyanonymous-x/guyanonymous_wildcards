Nuosu women in their thirties to sixties in long skirts of bands of black, red and yellow and wide black headdresses keen beside the body, their hands raised to their faces
Yi people of Sichuan, Yunnan and Guizhou, Nuosu, Nasu and Lolopo, in their twenties to seventies in their own black, red and blue dress, lead a horse and a sheep for the funeral
