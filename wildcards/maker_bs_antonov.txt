Styled in a design language of rugged, utilitarian, enormous, heavy, honest and workmanlike. Deep boxy volumes with a broad horizontal stance, big glazed bulges, upswept rear lines, and long rows of small repeated supports under bulged housings. Doubled vertical elements and wide, hanging machinery add weight. Colour is simple, matte military grey or white with a blue and yellow horizontal band. A lighter vintage strain is fabric-like and braced with struts and wires in silver, green or orange. Spectacular in scale. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
