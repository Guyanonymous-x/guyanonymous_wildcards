In the style of Akubra, Australian felt bush hats made from rabbit fur felt, with a wide flat or slightly drooping brim, a tall crown with a centre dent and two pinched side dents, and a leather or grosgrain band with a small metal-free stitched trim. Colours are fawn, tan, cream, grey, olive, brown and black, with a pale ribbon band and matching bound edge. Surface is soft, slightly fuzzy matte suede-like felt, with sweat marks and creases developing with wear. Practical, rugged, sun-baked countryside mood, shown against red earth, eucalyptus and wide skies.
