in the night sky Tagai, the great fisherman of the Torres Strait Islanders, stands in his canoe of stars, the Southern Cross forming his left hand holding a fish spear, his crew of stars around him
along the Milky Way stretches the Emu in the Sky, her long neck and body made of the dark dust lanes between the stars, her head at the dark Coalsack beside the Southern Cross
the Seven Sisters shine in the sky as the bright cluster of the Pleiades, seven women of starlight, and the man who chased them follows behind them as the stars of Orion
