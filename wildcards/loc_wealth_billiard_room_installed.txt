because a side-lit table has a shadowed cushion, and this is the room that introduces roof glazing to the domestic plan; a trough light on four chains and a pulley or a counterweight to raise it; walls hung with a dark paper or matchboarded to dado height, and no silk and no good pictures, because of the smoke; a raised spectator bench two steps up along one long wall, which is the room's social arrangement, players on the floor
watchers above them; a floor of bare board or linoleum round the table and a strip of carpet only under the armchairs; a chimneypiece on the short wall; ventilation at the frieze and often a gas or electric extract in the lantern; one bell pull; and a double door or a spring-closing door to keep the smoke in
