In the style of Korg, functional, technical, compact, precise late 1970s and 1980s electronic design with a futuristic hi-fi flavour. Long, shallow wedge shapes with a slanted metal control surface in brushed or anodised charcoal and silver, covered in rows of small round knobs, chunky slider caps and tiny lever switches in orange, red, blue and white. Labels are screen-printed in clean white or orange sans-serif capitals with thin line diagrams of waves and signal routing. Side cheeks are dark stained wood or black vinyl. Cream white and matte black, square-edged elements. Small red digits and amber or green indicators glow. Cool grey digital mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
