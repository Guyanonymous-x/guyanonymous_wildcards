Styled in a design language of refined mid-century modernism with architectural calm. Forms are precise and minimal, using slim polished stainless steel, brushed chrome, glossy lacquer, travertine, marble and leather with crisp stitching and button tufting. Contours mix straight cantilevered lines with soft sculptural curves. Palette is restrained and sophisticated, with ivory, black, taupe, cognac, oatmeal and slate grey, lifted by rich textile colours in terracotta, tomato red, ochre, teal and cobalt. Woven fabrics show tactile geometric weaves, stripes, grids and abstract patterns with visible thread. Lettering is a small, tidy, lowercase or capital sans-serif wordmark with generous spacing, confident, understated and quietly luxurious. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
