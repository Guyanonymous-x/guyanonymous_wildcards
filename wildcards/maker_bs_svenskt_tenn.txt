In the style of Svenskt Tenn, Swedish interior house. The fabrics are printed in large-scale botanical and fantastical patterns on white or cream linen and cotton, with detailed lush flowers, leaves, vines, birds, butterflies, maps and exotic fruit in saturated, slightly unexpected colours such as teal, mustard, coral, raspberry, tangerine and deep green on a pale background, drawn with hand-painted freedom. Furniture is light, comfortable and curvy, with spindly legs, rounded corners and woven cane seats, in mahogany or pale birch, with glass-fronted cabinets, brass details, painted chests and wall-hung shelves. Pewter, glass and brass objects are plain and sculptural. Rooms are eclectic and sunny, mixing patterns, plants, and warm wood with an intentionally unplanned look. The mood is cheerful, bright, cultured, and deliberately casual.
