In the style of Zimmermann, Australian romantic holiday womenswear, with floaty feminine mini and maxi dresses, broderie anglaise, delicate lace, ruffles, puffed sleeves, tiered skirts, smocking, scalloped hems and tie-front details in linen, cotton, silk and chiffon. Prints are painterly florals, watercolour blooms, paisley, ditsy flowers, polka dots, stripes and bohemian tile motifs in cream, ivory, blush pink, sage green, butter yellow, cornflower blue, rich red and black. Swimwear features cut-out one-pieces and frilled bikinis in matching prints. Dreamy, sunny, coastal, bohemian mood, shown among wildflowers and white-washed beach houses.
