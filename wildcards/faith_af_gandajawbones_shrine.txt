inside the shrine the roof is held up by many wooden poles wrapped in barkcloth, the floor spread with fine dry grass, and a great curtain of rust-brown barkcloth hangs across the back
behind the barkcloth curtain lies the inner room, called the forest, where the king's relics are kept, spears and shields laid against the wall and drums standing in a row
the walls of close-set reed work rise into the dark cone of the thatch, and a row of spears stands upright in the grass floor before the curtain, their iron blades pointing up
