Styled in a design language of passionate, theatrical Italian speed. Long, low, wedge-sharp or voluptuous sculpted forms with sensual curves, flowing fenders, sharp creases, wide stance and dramatic intakes. Deep, glossy rosso red dominates, joined by yellow, black, ivory and silver, with a prancing horse emblem on a yellow shield and thin racing stripes. Surfaces are lacquer-bright with chrome, carbon, polished metal and tan leather. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
