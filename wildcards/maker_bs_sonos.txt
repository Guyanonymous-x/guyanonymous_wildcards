Styled in a design language of calm, matte, tactile modern minimalism. Palette is soft black, pure white, warm sand, kraft brown, sage, cloud grey and dusty blue with occasional deep red or orange glow on black. Forms are smooth cuboids, cylinders and softly rounded slabs, wrapped in fine perforated metal or woven textile with no visible fasteners. Surfaces are matte and slightly grainy with crisp edges. Natural moulded-fibre and kraft paper textures and quiet monochrome layouts with ample empty space. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
