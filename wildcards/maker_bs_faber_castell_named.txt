Styled in a design language of scholarly European heritage in deep green and gold. Dark forest and emerald green lacquer with matte and satin depth, paired with ivory, cream, black, brown and warm metallic gold or silver foil. Surfaces feature fine hot-foil stamping, subtle embossing, rows of graded colour in neat ordered fans and polished wood, knurled metal and dark leather for luxury lines. Classic, craftsmanlike, dignified and warm. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
