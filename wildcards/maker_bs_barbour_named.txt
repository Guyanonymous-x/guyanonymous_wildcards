Styled in a design language of British countryside heritage built for rain, mud and long use. Waxed cotton in olive, sage, navy and dark brown with a slightly shiny, creased, weathered surface that ages with character. Details include corduroy collars, heavy metal zips, storm flaps, poacher-style pockets and quilted diamond stitching, in practical, roomy, tailored shapes. Tartan and muted tweed-like linings add quiet pattern. Timeless, sturdy and understated. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
