Styled in a design language of muscular, heavy and handcrafted British grandeur. Wide, powerful, broad-shouldered forms with deep sculpted creases and a planted, substantial presence. Polished chrome and brushed steel accents, knurled metal texture, diamond-quilted leather in contrasting two-tone hides with fine piping and neat stitching, and open-pore burr wood veneer in glowing honey, walnut and dark brown. Palette of deep racing green, midnight blue, burgundy, cream, silver and black. A winged B emblem in a simple black and silver roundel with spread feathers repeats as the signature mark, set in restrained spaced capital lettering. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
