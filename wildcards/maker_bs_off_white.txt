Styled in a design language of an industrial, deconstructed luxury mood built on quotation marks placed around ordinary words in plain, neutral capital sans-serif lettering, as if labelling the object. Diagonal yellow and black hazard stripes, arrows with crossed diagonal bars, plastic zip ties, exposed construction tape, orange safety webbing, raw seams and large printed words. Matte black, white and off-white with photocopy-grey graphics and road-marking motifs. Parts are shown half-finished, with visible internal layers and printed labels. Sharp minimal layouts on white, gallery-like backdrops, ironic, labelled and quietly rebellious.
