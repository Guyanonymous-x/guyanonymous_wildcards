the wide brown Niger River flows past sandbanks, long wooden pirogues of Bozo fishers gliding over it, the water glittering where Faro lives below
Faro rises from the river as a pale water spirit in the form of a woman in her thirties, her long straight hair streaming, her lower body a great silvery fish tail curling in the current
Faro shows himself in the river in his first bodily form, a huge dark gray catfish longer than a canoe, with long whiskers trailing from his wide flat mouth, turning slowly in the brown water among the pirogues
a still green pool lies among the reeds at the edge of the bush, water lilies on its surface and a white egret standing in the shallows
