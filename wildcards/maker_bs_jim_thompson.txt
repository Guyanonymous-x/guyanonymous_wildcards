Styled in a design language of hand-woven silk with a lustrous, slightly slubby shimmer. Surfaces show iridescent two-tone shot effects in jewel colours such as sapphire, emerald, ruby, saffron, fuchsia, plum and turquoise, plus rich plains. Prints show stylised animals, tropical leaves, orchids, lotus, bamboo, pagoda motifs and bold stripes, drawn in a flat painterly style with generous colour fields. Rainbow-like ranges of colour sit side by side. Warm, exotic, elegant mood with teak tones and soft tropical light.
