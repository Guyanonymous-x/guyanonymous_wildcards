half past five, the hall unlocked and cold, the altar lit, water bowls filled in order, flowers checked, a stick of incense
seven, a sitting that any lay person may join and three or four do
mid-morning, the hall empty and the kitchen busy
a quarter to twelve, the one fixed daily event, food brought by lay people and formally offered, the monks eating their single or last meal of the day, the laity eating after and in the hall or the corridor
the afternoon, a few people sitting, a visitor shown the altar
seven in the evening, the best-attended weekday session, the hall lights off, the altar alone lit, forty cushions out, an hour of sitting and a talk from the platform
nine, cushions stacked, blankets folded, the hall cold again
a lunar observance day, the hall full and the sitting longer
the new year, all 120 shoe places taken, the hall overflowing into the corridor and the garden, a marquee if the weather allows
