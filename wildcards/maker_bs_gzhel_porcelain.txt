Styled in a design language of bright glossy white ground painted only in cobalt blue brushwork, in graduated tones from pale sky blue to deep navy. Forms are rounded and plump, with soft outlines. The blue appears washed and flowing against the clean white. The mood is folk-like, cheerful, fresh and quintessentially Russian. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
