In the style of Patagonia, honest outdoor utility with an earthy, conscientious spirit. Functional, roomy, rounded shapes with practical seams, tough stitching and unpretentious proportions. Colour blocking uses bright primaries such as red, royal blue, yellow and teal against natural fawn, forest green, rust and navy, often with vintage faded tones. Textures are brushed fleece, ripstop, canvas, wool and recycled fabric with a soft worn look. Graphics are simple jagged mountain skyline silhouettes in a rounded badge, with plain bold sans lettering. Photography is vast, real, unretouched landscapes.
