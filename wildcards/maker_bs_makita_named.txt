Styled in a design language of solid, workmanlike and cool teal design for any product. Strong teal-blue-green plastic with black rubberised areas, a red stripe accent and tough silver or steel metalwork. Compact, rounded, chunky shapes with ribbed overmoulded grips, neat rows of small ventilation slots and sturdy cases. Practical, hard-wearing and made for long working days. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
