In the style of Tesla, minimalist, clean and futuristic, with a software-first calm. Surfaces are smooth and unbroken, closed-off and flush, with low sloping lines, slim light bands, large glass planes and almost no chrome or visible fasteners. A sleek T-shaped emblem in brushed silver sits alone on dark matte fields. Palette is pearl white, solid black, graphite, midnight silver and one strong red, in sharp, high-contrast layouts. Materials are matte black, brushed metal, white vegan-leather-like upholstery and pale wood strips with nearly no buttons. Lettering is wide, geometric, widely spaced capitals, often with a squared techno feel. Photography is dark, glossy, studio-lit and cinematic, with spotlit edges and empty negative space.
