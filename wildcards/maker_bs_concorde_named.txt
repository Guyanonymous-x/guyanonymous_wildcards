Styled in a design language of needle-sharp, futuristic, elegant design in gleaming white. Long narrow forms taper to a point, with a sleek elongated triangle silhouette used as a repeated graphic shape. Surfaces are bright reflective white gloss, polished silver and pale grey metal with a heat-resistant ceramic sheen, kept clear of clutter. A single narrow cobalt or navy cheatline runs the full length of a form, with fine red and blue accent flashes on small panels. Seamless and streamlined, the palette stays almost entirely white and silver. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
