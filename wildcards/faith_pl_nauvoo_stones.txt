sunstones, carved stone capitals at the tops of the pilasters, the flat squared columns along the walls, each carved with a round full-cheeked face of the sun rising over a bank of clouds with rays fanning above it and two hands holding trumpets
moonstones, pale stone blocks at the bases of the pilasters, each carved with a crescent moon lying on its back with its horns pointing downward, cut in raised relief on the smooth limestone
starstones, carved five-pointed stars with long downward-pointing rays set in a row along the top of the walls just under the roof edge, one above each pilaster
