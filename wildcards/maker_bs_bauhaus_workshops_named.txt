In the style of Bauhaus workshops, German functional modernism built from primary geometry, circles, squares and triangles, using tubular steel, chromed frames, black leather straps, flat plywood, bent wood and white-painted surfaces. Palette of primary red, yellow and blue with black, white and grey. Slim cantilevered lines, nickel, opal glass spheres, abstract woven grids and lowercase sans-serif lettering. Rational, austere and factory-ready elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
