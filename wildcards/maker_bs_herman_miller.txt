In the style of Herman Miller, American mid-century modern furniture with molded plywood and fibreglass shells in organic curves, perched on thin wire struts or splayed wooden dowel legs with metal cross braces. Colours are warm walnut, ash and rosewood veneer, black leather with plump cushions, and bright pops of orange, mustard yellow, turquoise and white. Lounge chairs with stacked cushioned panels on a cast aluminium swivel base, bubble-shaped sofas, slatted platform benches, egg-shaped and kidney-shaped forms, exposed joints and brass or chrome hardware. Playful, optimistic, sunlit California living room and office look, clean honest construction, later ergonomic mesh task chairs in graphite with visible skeletal frames.
