Styled in a design language of blunt, tough, muscular American heavy-duty character. A bulldog-like, forward-thrust mass, a top line that curves down steeply and a bold chrome standing animal ornament. Stout upright chrome faces with horizontal bars, round chrome lamps, short heavy curves and thick chrome bars. Bright enamel in red, yellow, blue or black, with stainless accents. Chunky, compact, solid and no-nonsense, proportioned like a determined fighter, with polished highlights on a hard-working frame. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
