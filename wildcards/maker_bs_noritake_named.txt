Styled in a design language of refined, ornate, glittering early twentieth century export porcelain. Glossy bone-white glaze with a faint bluish translucency, formal shapes with scalloped edges, closed loops, small curls and bud or fruit finials. Hand-painted and gilded decoration with wide borders of heavy gold, cobalt blue and deep rose red, central scenic panels in sunset orange and teal, and raised enamel dots and beads standing proud like pearls. Later Art Deco versions turn angular, with stepped triangles, black and orange bands, fans, sunbursts and pearly rainbow lustre. Slightly nostalgic, treasured and cabinet-worthy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
