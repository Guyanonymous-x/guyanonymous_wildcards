life-size cardboard cutouts of a bride and groom, standing side by side. The two figures are printed on one wide flat sheet of corrugated cardboard, cut out around their outlines together with a thin white border at the edge. The printed bride wears a white gown with a fitted lace bodice and a full skirt to the floor, a long veil over her hair and a bouquet of white roses. The printed groom stands at her side in a gray suit with a white shirt, a silver tie and a white rose pinned to his lapel. They hold hands between them and smile out at the viewer, and a printed banner at their feet reads "JUST MARRIED" in silver script. At the cut edge the brown wavy layers of the cardboard show, and two folded cardboard struts at the back prop it upright on the floor. __cutout_bride_and_groom_var__.
