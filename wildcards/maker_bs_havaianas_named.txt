In the style of Havaianas, thin, flat, lightweight rubber forms with a glossy matte finish and a slightly squishy feel. The surface is textured with a rice-grain pattern of tiny raised ridges, with flat wide bands as the only structure. Colours are juicy, summery solids such as white, yellow, lime green, hot pink, turquoise, red, navy and orange, with a contrasting colour for the bands against the base and flag-like stripes. Two-tone, simple and cheerful, evoking beach sand, blue sea and bright sun. Casual and tropical mood.
