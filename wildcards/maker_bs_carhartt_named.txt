In the style of Carhartt, hard-wearing American utility design. Surfaces look broken in, with soft creasing, pale fading at the folds and honest wear. Branding is a small square label in a contrasting block colour with plain, upright, slightly condensed capital lettering, repeated on hang tags in kraft brown and black with cream type. Everything feels durable, unfussy, practical and built to be used for decades. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Carhartt in that lettering style and no other words.
