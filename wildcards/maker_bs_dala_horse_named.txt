In the style of Dala Horse, simple, friendly, folk-rustic and unmistakably Scandinavian. Small stout carved wooden forms in simple flat planes, with a thick arched curve, notched edges, sturdy legs and a bulky chest. Body painted glossy deep red or orange-red, sometimes blue, black, white or natural. Trim is decorated freehand with flower scroll patterns in white, green, yellow and blue, painted quickly in loops, petals and dots in a flower and swirl vocabulary. The finish is smooth, shiny and lightly worn.
