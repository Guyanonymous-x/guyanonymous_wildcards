In the style of Ardmore Ceramic Art, witty, lush, whimsical, tropical and highly decorative hand sculpted ceramics. Exuberant, playful forms covered with sculpted wild animals such as big cats, striped grazers, monkeys, snakes and birds that climb, perch and curl around the shapes. Surfaces are a dense riot of dots, lines and leafy patterns in bright glossy colours, including cobalt, emerald, orange, purple, turquoise, red and gold, with iridescent scales and shimmering lustres. Flowers and vines twist across surfaces in fine relief. Edges are outlined in dark. Every piece looks like a miniature jungle, bursting with colour and detail.
