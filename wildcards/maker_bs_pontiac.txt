Styled in a design language of aggressive, swaggering street-racer American muscle of the 1960s and 70s. Split twin-nostril shapes divided by a body-coloured beak, stacked or hidden forms, a waistline swelling over tall haunches, one long raised bulge and bold parallel stripes. Brushed metal rally patterns with bright trim rings and a red arrowhead mark. Carousel red, deep green, ivory and bright orange. Black vinyl bucket shapes with engine-turned panels add attitude.
