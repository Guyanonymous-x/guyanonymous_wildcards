to the right lie the gardens of Paradise, green terraces with rivers of water, milk, honey and wine, fruit trees bending low, and the blessed in green silk robes in their thirties reclining on couches under awnings
to the left lies Hell, women in their thirties to sixties in loincloths hung by their long black hair from burning trees, flames coming from their noses, a man whose tongue is cut with iron shears by a blue demon, others chained in red fire
to the left lies Hell, one vast dragon with green scales and a gaping red mouth full of fire, men and women in their thirties to sixties in loincloths tumbling into its jaws while angels in white with long staffs drive them on
above all things the Throne of God is shown as a vast field of white and gold light with eight angels holding up its base, the light pouring down over the plain, the throne itself a broad seat of light
