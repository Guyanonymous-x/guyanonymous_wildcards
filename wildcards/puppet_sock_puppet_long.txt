a homemade sock puppet worn on an adult's hand and forearm. It is made from a long knitted sock, the toe end folded in to make a mouth that opens and shuts when the hand inside opens and closes. Two big buttons are sewn on above the mouth as eyes, mismatched, one black and one blue, and a long tongue of pink felt is glued inside the mouth. A tuft of orange yarn hair is stitched to the top of the head and sticks out in loops. Small triangles of white felt are glued along the edge of the mouth as teeth, and the sock is stretched tight over the knuckles. The stitching is large and uneven. __puppet_sock_puppet_var__.
