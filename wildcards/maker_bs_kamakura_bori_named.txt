In the style of Kamakura-bori, simple solid wooden forms with rounded, lobed outlines and deep carved edges, covered in bold shallow gouge carving. Flowers, leaves and waves are cut with rounded petal edges and flat bevelled facets, leaving visible chisel marks. The lacquer is slightly matte, a vermilion or cinnabar layer over black that wears through at the high points to reveal a warm brown underlayer, so edges look softly aged. Colours stay in red-brown, black and warm wood tones. Folk-craft, sculptural, handmade and quietly rustic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kamakura-bori in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
