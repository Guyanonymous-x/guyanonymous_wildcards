the dark sanctuary at the very back of the temple, a small windowless chamber of carved sandstone walls with a single block of stone hollowed into a shrine standing on the floor, lit by a shaft of light from the doorway
the second pylon standing behind the courtyard, a smaller sloping gateway of sandstone set at a slight angle, its faces carved with rows of tall standing gods and the pharaoh bringing offerings to them
