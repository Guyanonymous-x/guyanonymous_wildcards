In the style of Billabong, Australian surf culture clothing. The core logo is a rounded script with a flowing wavy line running beneath, or a stack of the name in a slightly bubbly bold type, printed on boardshorts, tees and caps, often with a stylised wave swoosh in teal, blue or black. Boardshorts are long knee-length shorts with laced fly, a velcro-and-snap waistband, triple stitching and a small leg patch, made in quick-drying nylon or poly in bright colours and tropical flower, stripe or tie-dye prints. Colours are ocean tones, teal, navy, aqua, coral, lime and yellow against white, with sun-faded greys. T-shirts carry large screen-printed graphics of waves, palm trees, retro surf scenes, hand-drawn lettering, sunsets and cartoon creatures. Wetsuits are slim black neoprene with contrasting colour panels and sleek paneling. The mood is sunny, sporty, laid-back, youthful and sandy.
