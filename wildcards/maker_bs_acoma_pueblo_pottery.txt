Styled in a design language of crisp, disciplined, optical and graceful extremely thin-walled hand-coiled ceramics with fine-line painting. Rounded forms with high shoulders and small openings, and low wide shapes, with a very fine white slip surface that is smooth, matte and chalk-like. The walls are paper thin. Decoration is painted with a fine brush in black and rusty orange or red-brown on the creamy white ground, in dense precise geometric bands. Motifs are fine-line lattices and hatching, interlocking triangles, birds, rainbows, stepped clouds, animals with a heart line and scrolls, with bands wrapping horizontally and tidy empty zones at the base. Unglazed, powdery. Like delicate op-art on earthenware. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
