the Morning Star stands as a warrior in his twenties with his whole body painted red, his long hair tied with a single downy eagle feather, in a breechcloth and leggings, a war club in his hand, a bright four-pointed star shining above his head, his moccasined feet on the eastern hills near the bottom of the frame
the Evening Star stands as a woman in her twenties with long braided black hair, in a fringed deerskin dress, holding a bundle of tall corn stalks, a white star above her head, her moccasined feet in her garden of corn in the west near the bottom of the frame
the stars are drawn as a great field of crosses and points of light of many sizes, the largest, the Morning and Evening Stars, set at the right and left sides and the North Star at the top, the lowest stars near the bottom of the frame
the stars of the four quarters stand as four men in their thirties holding up the corners of the sky, each painted in his own color, black, yellow, white and red, their feet planted on the earth near the bottom of the frame
