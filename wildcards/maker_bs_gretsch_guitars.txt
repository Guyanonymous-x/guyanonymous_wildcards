Styled in a design language of flashy mid-century American showmanship. Finishes are deep orange lacquer, cherry red, pearl white, two-tone green and sparkling metallic, all glossy and deep. Gold-plated and chrome accents gleam beside cream and black edge stripes. Emblems include a circular badge with a stylised letter G and a stylised V shape, with oversized decorative cut-outs. Details are bold stripes, rows of bright studs, striped and sparkle edging, and generous proportions. Palette is orange, gold, cream, black and red. Loud, showy and rockabilly cool. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
