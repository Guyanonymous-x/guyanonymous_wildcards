Styled in a design language of bright glossy moulded vinyl and plastic in strong primary red, blue and yellow with a hot pink accent. Rounded friendly forms, chunky heroic shapes with exaggerated proportions and painted details. Surfaces are shiny, smooth, saturated and punchy, with a header-card look in red, white and black. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
