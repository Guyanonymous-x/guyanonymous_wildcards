Styled in a design language of formal Gilded Age elegance in luxury travel of the early 1900s. Walnut with green or burgundy plush upholstery, brass fittings and gaslight-style lamps. Heavy, solid, stately forms. Ornate, rich, ceremonial, hand-gilded and imposing, like a rolling grand hotel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
