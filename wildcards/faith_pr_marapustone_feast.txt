at the village edge the water buffalo given by the allied clans stand tied to stakes, and a man in his forties kills one with a single deep cut of a long machete across the throat, bright red blood pouring onto the dry ground, meat for the pullers
a row of pigs tied to bamboo poles lies at the side of the track, and men in their thirties to fifties cut and share out the meat on fresh banana leaves for the crowd of pullers
the horns of buffalo killed at earlier feasts hang in a long row on the front post of the clan's tall house, its thatched roof rising steeply to a high peak, beside the half-built tomb waiting for its stone
