Candle-lit castles, fog-wrapped graveyards and crypts, ornate Victorian costumes and capes, fangs, dripping crimson blood, dramatic painted imagery with looming shadows, lurid yellow and red lettering with jagged dripping edges and studio-built sets with painted skies. Lush, lurid storybook creepiness. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
