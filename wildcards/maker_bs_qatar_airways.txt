In the style of Qatar Airways, a Gulf airline identity in burgundy and grey. Aircraft have a pale grey and white fuselage with the name in large burgundy capitals on the front, the Arabic title nearby in a smaller darker grey, and a tail fin in deep burgundy carrying an oryx antelope silhouette in a pale grey ring with long curved horns. The finish is smooth and polished, with burgundy stripe accents around the engines and a thin line at the belly. Cabins are stately, in grey and burgundy leather, with lie-flat beds in a business suite with a sliding door and soft lighting. Lounges use marble floors, a huge sculpture and leather sofas. Crew wear deep maroon uniforms with a neat hat and veil, and a gold pin. Mood is luxurious, formal, quiet, upmarket and elegant, with deep maroon, warm grey and a gleam of metal.
