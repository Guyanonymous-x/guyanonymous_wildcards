Styled in a design language of refined Australian rural heritage and craftsmanship. Smooth, polished, single-piece leather wrapped with minimal seams and visible hand stitching in chestnut, tan, black and oxblood. Workwear fabrics include moleskin, oilskin, heavy cotton drill, gingham and check flannel, plus wide leather belts, in khaki, oat, olive, navy, rust and burgundy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
