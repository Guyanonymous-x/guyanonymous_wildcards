the merchant bridge is effectively identical worldwide, because it is specified by the same instruments, SOLAS chapter V, the IMO performance standards and the flag state's rules, which makes this one of very few records in the whole dataset where the regional variation is genuinely minimal and that fact is itself the interesting thing
the Japanese and Korean newbuild bridge, which tends to the most thoroughly integrated console
the European-built bridge, similar
the Chinese-built bulk carrier, which tends to a plainer fit with the same statutory equipment
the Russian and former Soviet bridge, with a distinct instrument layout and a longer survival of paper practice
the naval bridge, which is a different discipline with a darkened ship, an operations room below it and the conn separated from the command
the cruise-ship bridge, which is the widest of all and has a dedicated maneuvering station at each wing
the tug, river and inland-waterway wheelhouse, often a single raised cabin on a hydraulic column that can be lowered to pass under bridges
the fishing vessel's wheelhouse, which combines bridge, galley and mess in one space
the ferry's bridge, worked as a shuttle with two conning positions facing opposite ways; and the private yacht's bridge, which is this room with better joinery, a settee and a visiting owner
