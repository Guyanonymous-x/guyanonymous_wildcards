Styled in a design language of two mirrored open rings joined at the centre like a stylised gancio, cast in polished gold, antique brass or silver metal, or stamped in tone-on-tone relief. Materials are smooth calfskin, patent leather, suede, silk twill and grosgrain weave, in navy, black, bordeaux, nude, ivory, pale blue and soft pastel tones, often with a thin contrasting edge. Silk surfaces carry intricate painted-looking repeat prints in rich reds, blues and golds. Craft details are neat hand finishing, sculpted curves and quiet, timeless elegance. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
