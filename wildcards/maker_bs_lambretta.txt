Styled in a design language of Italian mod-era pop style built from clean geometry. Smooth enamel shapes in cream, white, red, sky blue and olive green, often split into two clean tones by a long horizontal break. Italian tricolour shield badges, concentric target roundels in blue, white and red, and black and white checkerboard bands repeat as signature graphics. Brushed aluminium, chrome trim and flat matte panels look tidy and precise. Youthful, witty, minimal and sharply graphic, with retro ska energy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
