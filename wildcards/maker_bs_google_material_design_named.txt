In the style of Google Material Design, a flat but layered surface treatment where every element looks like a sheet of coloured paper lying on another, with soft diffuse shadows beneath to show height. Surfaces are flat solid colour with no bevels or gloss, in bold indigo, teal, pink, orange and purple, alongside clean white and light grey sheets with gently rounded corners. Round floating discs carry a single white pictogram and cast a soft shadow. Icons are simple geometric glyphs with even line weight. Lettering is a clean friendly geometric sans-serif in white or dark grey. Generous spacing, tidy grids, circular ripple shapes and cheerful flat colour blocks. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Google Material Design in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
