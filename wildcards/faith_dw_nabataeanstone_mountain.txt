the mountains of Petra rise in great domes and ridges of rose-red sandstone streaked with bands of orange, cream and violet, the carved tomb fronts and temples cut into their faces
on the flat top of Jebel Madbah lies the High Place, a broad court cut into the rock with a raised altar, a drain for the blood of the sacrifice and a shallow pool, two tall obelisks cut from the living rock standing on the ridge nearby
the narrow Siq winds between towering red cliffs, its walls rising sheer on each side, a channel for water cut along its foot, a thin line of sky above
