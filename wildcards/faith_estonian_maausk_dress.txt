Estonian folk dress, adult women in their twenties to seventies in long wool skirts striped lengthwise in red, black, green and yellow, white linen blouses with embroidered collars, a woven belt and a small embroidered cap or a white headscarf
the men in white linen shirts with a silver brooch at the throat, dark wool vests and knee breeches, white knee socks and felt hats or flat caps
women in striped skirts and white aprons, embroidered sleeveless bodices, a round silver brooch at the neck and wreaths of flowers or a linen head cloth
gray or dark blue wool coats and trousers for the men, long striped skirts and shawls for the women, worn by adults at a sacred grove on a summer evening
