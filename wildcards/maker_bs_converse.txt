Styled in a design language of classic American court and garage-band heritage in its simplest form, for any product. Soft cotton canvas stretched over rounded, minimal shapes, framed by glossy off-white trim with a thin coloured stripe, flat cords and small shiny metal rings. A small circular badge carrying a star and chevron mark recurs as the emblem. Palette is optic white, black, cherry red, navy, mustard and faded pastels, with sports-team stripes and star motifs. Lettering is bold, wide, collegiate capitals, sometimes slanted and outlined, often in navy and red. Graphics feel like vintage athletic posters with halftone dots and ink-stamp texture, while photography is moody, cinematic and urban, with worn, creased, signed-by-use character and kraft wooden crate packaging.
