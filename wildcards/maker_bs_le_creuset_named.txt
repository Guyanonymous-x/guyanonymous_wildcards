Styled in a design language of French heritage craft in glossy, saturated enamel. Shapes are rounded, heavy, generous and softly domed, with smooth shoulders, gentle curves and sturdy loop forms. Signature palette is flame orange, deep cerise, cobalt, Mediterranean teal, sunny yellow, sage and cream, with satin black and shimmering pearl variations, usually shown as graduated rows of one colour per item. Surfaces are plain and uncluttered, with occasional floral or stripe motifs. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
