Styled in a design language of orderly German technical and classroom precision in strong blue. Deep cobalt and navy blue paired with black, white and a few sharp accents of red, yellow and green, used in crisp flat bands and stripes on smooth lacquered surfaces. The emblem is a stylised white silhouette of a helmeted head in profile inside a solid blue square. Natural kraft and pale wood tones appear in limited editions. Neat, reliable, practical, methodical and cheerfully educational. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
