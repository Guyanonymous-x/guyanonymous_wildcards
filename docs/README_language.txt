Guyanonymous' Wildcards - Language guidelines (v17)
===================================================
Everything here is meant to be encouraging and to embrace global diversity. Names, references and descriptions describe who people ARE, not what they have or have not done medically.
- No "halfway", "stopped partway", "pre-op" or "post-op" wording. A transition is not a race with a finish line: each person's path is their own.
- Identities are described by identity: trans man, trans woman, non-binary, genderqueer, androgynous, intersex, and people with a gradual or individualized transition who keep both masculine and feminine characteristics (individualized_fem / individualized_masc; "bigender" and "salmacian" are other community terms that fit this area).
- Renamed in v16: halfway_mtf/ftm -> gradual_fem/gradual_masc; halted_mtf/ftm -> individualized_fem/individualized_masc; *_nonop -> *_own_path.
- Where a particular person is described, mirror the language they use for themselves.
