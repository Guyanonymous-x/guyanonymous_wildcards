on the long offering tables lie a whole ox, a whole pig and a whole goat, skinned and pale pink, set on their bellies on wooden racks with their heads toward the tablet
the altar is set with rows of bronze vessels holding cooked grain, dried meat, fruit and wine, rolls of white and yellow silk and tall red candles burning
after the rite men and women in their twenties to sixties crowd around the offered ox, pulling hairs from its hide
