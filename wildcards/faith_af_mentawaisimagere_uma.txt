the uma, the clan longhouse raised high on wooden posts above the forest floor, its roof of sago palm thatch steep and wide, rows of skulls of monkeys, pigs and deer hung under its eaves for the spirits
at the edge of the forest the body of an old man in his seventies, wrapped in a mat of woven bark, lies on a raised platform of poles among the trees, his kin in their twenties to sixties standing near
inside the uma the kin in their twenties to seventies sit along the floor of split palm before a wall of hunting trophies, skulls of game hung in rows from the beams as gifts for the souls
