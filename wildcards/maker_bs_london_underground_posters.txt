Styled in a design language of early twentieth century transport graphics built from flat, hand-cut shapes. Outlines are crisp and forms are reduced to geometric blocks with gentle lithographic grain and slightly chalky texture. A bold red ring crossed by a horizontal blue bar acts as the recurring emblem. Lettering is a clean, even-stroked humanist sans-serif in capitals with wide spacing, set in tidy blocks with generous margins. Compositions are balanced, decorative and optimistic, with cubist and art deco rhythm in repeated shapes. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
