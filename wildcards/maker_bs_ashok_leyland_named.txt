Styled in a design language of rugged, colourful, overloaded, hand-decorated Indian heavy-duty design. Rounded steel or fibreglass surfaces in faded red and cream, with simple metal bars as protection. Gritty, dusty, golden hour mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
