A grand staircase of white marble with a polished brass handrail rises to a landing, where a large painting shows robed builders at work on an ancient temple.
The ceiling of the great hall is painted deep blue with gold stars arranged in the patterns of the night sky, and a large crystal chandelier hangs at its center on a long chain.
A long gallery on the upper floor holds framed charters, portraits of past Grand Masters in aprons and collars, and a carved wooden chair kept roped off on a low platform.
