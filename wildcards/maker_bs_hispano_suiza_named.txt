Styled in a design language of aristocratic, grand and sculptural luxury of the 1920s and 1930s. A long louvred mass, a tall narrow chrome front and large freestanding round forms. Deep blue, burgundy, black and silver with polished brass or nickel brightwork. Deep buttoned leather. Wire spokes, flowing separate wings and coachbuilt bodies in polished wood or aluminium add craftsmanship, with polished burr walnut and brass-bezelled instruments inside. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
