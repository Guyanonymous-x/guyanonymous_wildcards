Midnight to four, one officer and one lookout, red light, fifteen black windows, a radar, a VHF muttering, and the watch alarm reset twenty times an hour
Four, handover - a verbal report of traffic, weather, course, speed, the engine's mode and anything outstanding, then a signature in the bell book
Eight, the master up, the noon position worked, the day's work ordered, eleven emails to the operator
The forenoon, the second officer correcting charts and the passage plan for the next leg, which is a legal duty and takes two hours
Midday, the noon report
The afternoon, drills, inspections, paperwork, a cadet being taught to use a sextant that is then put back in its box
Arrival, the bridge's high point, forty minutes to three hours of concentrated work by five people
In port, the bridge dark and empty with a gangway watch two hundred meters away and nobody up here at all; and departure, when it all starts again
