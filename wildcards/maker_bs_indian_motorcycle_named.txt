Styled in a design language of lavish American art deco craft. Deep lacquered cherry red meets ivory cream, black and gold, with long teardrop curves and hand-laid pinstripes along edges. A proud profile emblem of a feathered headdress appears as a carved, enamelled or stitched mark. Surfaces mix mirror chrome, glossy candy paint, tan leather with brass studs, and glowing amber ornaments. Winged, swooping shapes, rounded oval cartouches and heritage patches give a rich, proud, polished character. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
