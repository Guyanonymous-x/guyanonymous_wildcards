Styled in a design language of precision drafting sensibility from Germany. Palette is matte black, brushed steel, silver, graphite and one vivid scarlet accent. Surfaces are knurled, crosshatched metal with fine machined ridges, satin anodised finishes and tiny white printed scale markings. Forms are hexagonal, cylindrical and tapered, slim and tool-like, with crisp edges and no ornament. Engineered, exact, draughtsman-like, serious and timeless. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
