In the style of Coca-Cola, joyful, refreshing, nostalgic Americana. Dominant saturated red paired with clean white, used in broad flat fields with a flowing white ribbon wave. Lettering is a looping, handwritten-looking script with generous swashes, balanced by simple bold sans-serif support text. Shapes are curvy, contoured and hourglass-like, with glossy glass, polished chrome and condensation droplets. Illustration and photography are warm, sunny and optimistic, showing smiling people, sparkle, ice and bubbles. Round discs and badge shapes, vintage enamel sign charm, and a timeless feel-good, shareable cheer.
In the style of Pepsi, a round globe emblem split into a red upper half and a blue lower half by a white wavy band, set against deep royal blue. Sans-serif lettering in bold lowercase or small caps, often placed beside or across the globe. Earlier versions used a serrated bottle-cap shape and a script wordmark in red on white. Bright blue fields with the globe tilted or smiling, silver tones, a palette of cobalt blue, bright red and white, a clean, youthful, pop-culture look, glossy metallic surfaces and sleek modern minimal variants in black.
In the style of Guinness, dignified heritage solidity in near-black and gold. Deep glossy black grounds contrast with creamy tan-white and off-white fields, framed by thin gold borders. A single gold emblem in profile sits dead centre, with restrained thin serif capitals and gold-on-black embossing. Layouts are symmetrical, orderly and traditional. A secondary playful vein uses flat, bright gouache-like illustration in yellow, blue and red with chunky hand-drawn lettering and whimsical creatures. Mood is rich, warm, proud and unmistakably Irish.
In the style of Heineken, crisp, clean and friendly European confidence built on deep emerald green, bright red and white with silver accents. One bold red five-pointed star is the repeatable emblem, often set on a cream-white oval edged in red. Lettering is lowercase, rounded and slightly tilted, with a thin red outline on white. Thin gold or red filigree trim may edge panels. Surfaces are glossy and uncluttered, with green dominating and the red accent popping against it.
In the style of Frooti, juicy, cheerful, summery and kid-friendly. Bright mango-yellow and green fields carry a glossy printed picture of ripe tropical fruit with leaves. A bold red and white curved wordmark in a bubbly rounded cartoon font. Compact shapes have rounded edges and soft creases. Palette is mango yellow, orange, green and red. Mood is fruity, tropical, high-contrast and playful, with a bold fruit picture and bubbly type.
In the style of Limca, refreshing, tangy, sunny and citrus-fresh. Lime-green fields with bright white and yellow, a swirly lime-green and white wordmark in a rounded bold font, and a graphic of halved citrus fruit with droplets. A sunburst of lemon yellow sits behind the lettering with a thin curved line. Condensation and ice emphasise coolness. Palette is lime green, bright yellow, white and a touch of blue. Mood is zesty, lively, crisp and vibrant, with rounded type.
In the style of Inca Kola, sunny, festive, Andean and luminous. A glowing golden-yellow colour fills clear glass, with yellow and red fields and a bold red banner. A circular emblem holds a sun-face and stepped pre-Columbian geometric shapes in red, black and gold. Lettering is chunky sans-serif with decorative stepped edges. Palette is canary yellow, red, black and white. Mood is joyful, proud, rooted in ancient geometric motifs and bright, with stepped patterns and a glowing yellow.
In the style of Guarana Antarctica, tropical, Brazilian, sunny and energetic. A vibrant leafy green ground with bold yellow and red shows a ring of bright red berries with black seeds, with a round badge in the centre bearing a polar animal silhouette. Lettering is white and yellow rounded sans-serif with a thin black outline. Glossy graphics and rounded friendly layouts. Palette is leafy green, golden yellow, scarlet red and white. Mood is fruity, lively, warm and approachable.
In the style of Tsingtao, traditional, slightly old-fashioned Chinese-German colonial heritage with neat bordered frames. Green grounds carry a cream oval edged in green, an illustrated landmark of a small pavilion on a rocky shore in dark green, and a red and green wordmark in classical lettering. Green accents and a red seal. Palette is dark green, cream, red and gold. Mood is orderly, proud, heritage and refined, with an illustrated landmark and classical type.
In the style of Wang Lao Ji, auspicious, celebratory and traditional Chinese. A bright festival red surface covered in golden brush calligraphy, with a golden border of traditional ornamental patterns and gold-coloured accents. A large central bold brush script sits on a plain, slightly glossy red field. Palette is festival red, gold and white. Mood is cheerful, lucky, festive and bold, with strong colour blocks, calligraphic marks and gilded trim.
In the style of Nescafe, bold, familiar, warm and morning-friendly. Squat round forms with bright red accents and red fields with white lettering, a shield-like or rounded red badge, and a cream window revealing warm contents. Lettering is a rounded bold sans-serif in white on red with a slim outline. Steam swirls rise above warm tones. Palette is bright red, white, brown and cream. Mood is comforting, clear, friendly and reassuring, with a strong red and white contrast.
In the style of Milo, sporty, energetic, wholesome, vibrant and child-friendly. Rounded cylindrical forms with a bright apple-green ground, a white and dark-green band with bold lettering in a rounded font, and a picture of an athletic child in action in sports gear. Green appears across every surface, with dusty chocolate-brown tones. Palette is apple green, dark green, white and brown. Mood is active, healthy and optimistic, with action poses and a bold green block.
