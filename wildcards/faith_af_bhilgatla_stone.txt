in the ground of the dead at the edge of the village stand rows of gatha, memorial stones of gray stone each as tall as a seated man, each carved with a rider on a horse and smeared with orange vermilion
a new memorial stone stands at the head of a row of older ones, the rider carved on it holding a spear, the sun and the moon cut above his head, fresh vermilion and oil shining on it
under a great mahua tree a group of weathered memorial stones lean together, some carved with riders and some with a man and his wife side by side, small clay horses set before them
