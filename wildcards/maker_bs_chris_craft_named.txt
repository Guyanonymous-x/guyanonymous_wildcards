In the style of Chris-Craft, classic, varnished, lakeside summer glamour in the 1930s-50s American tradition. Rich red-brown mahogany planking polished to a deep shine, with fine white seams between boards. Chrome-framed glazing, chrome fittings, slim tapering lines and rounded ends with flowing script lettering. Tufted leather in red and cream and neatly curved enclosures. Handcrafted, warm, nostalgic, sporty and carefree, like long sunny weekends on calm water. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
