In the style of San Ildefonso Pueblo Pottery, sleek, dark, formal, sculptural and timeless. Perfectly symmetrical, hand-built forms with a full rounded volume and a crisp change of angle where the curve turns. The surface is burnished to a glass-smooth gloss and smoked to a deep jet black. Designs appear in matte black against the shining black field, visible only as light shifts across them. Motifs are bold and graphic, such as coiled serpents, rain clouds, stepped shapes, lightning zigzags, mountains and small tracks, arranged in one confident band. Perfect balance, calm and ceremonial. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
