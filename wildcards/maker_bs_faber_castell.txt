In the style of Faber-Castell, pencils and colouring sets with a deep dark-green lacquer finish and gold hot-foil stamped lettering and a double-knight emblem. The barrels are hexagonal or round, with a smoothly dipped end in colour and fine grading marks. Tins and boxes are dark green, dark brown or black with gold lettering, with rows of pencils and pastels aligned in gradients. Luxury writing tools come in polished wood with silver or platinum-plated fittings and knurled details. Mood is classic, scholarly, European and craftsmanlike.
