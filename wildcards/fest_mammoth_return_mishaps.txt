A hut of tusks and hides has blown over in the wind
A spark from the fire has caught the edge of a fur cloak
A young mammoth has wandered close and the camp scatters
A hunter has slipped on the muddy riverbank and slid into the cold water
A flint blade has cut a hand and a woman wraps it in moss
The fire has gone out in the rain and people crowd round the smoking ashes
