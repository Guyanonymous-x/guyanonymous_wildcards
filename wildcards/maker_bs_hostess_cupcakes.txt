Styled in a design language of glossy shop-bought sweet treats in brightly coloured wrappers with chocolate cakes with white icing swirls, cream-filled pastries, shiny foil in blue and red, and light cardboard trays. Sweet, nostalgic and lunchbox-friendly. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
