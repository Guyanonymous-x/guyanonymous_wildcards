Styled in a design language of colour-saturated, studio-made period drama where everything is clearly built and artificial. Vivid reds, yellows and jade greens, silk with rich embroidery, and bold dramatic poses. Sweeping, choreographed movement and sudden emphasis. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
