Styled in a design language of muscular, wide and heavy grandeur with a gentleman sporting edge. Powerful swelling curves, a long forward mass, a deep sculpted shoulder and a low planted stance. Early work is open, riveted and raw in dark racing green with leather straps and visible fixings, later work is polished chrome, diamond-quilted leather in contrasting hides, knurled metal details and open-pore burr wood. Deep green, dark blue, silver, black and burgundy. Powerful, hand-finished and substantial. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
