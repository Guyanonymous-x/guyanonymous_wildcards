Purrukuparli, the first man, a man in his forties painted in ochre, stands at a grave holding a carved and painted pole, the first pukumani mourning of the islands
the spirit beings of the islands are shown as tall figures painted in cross-hatched ochre bands, standing among the trees and along the shore
