Styled in a design language of playful Italian modernism with sculptural confidence. Smooth, bold, softly rounded or crisp geometric forms in glossy moulded plastic and enamel metal, treated like art objects with sensual curves and compact proportions. Cheerful colours such as vivid red, pastel blue, mint green, lemon yellow and ivory are used in single strong tones, with black detail and small bright accents. Graphic design uses bold abstract dots, lines and colour fields on a grid. Witty, optimistic, human, refined and artistic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
