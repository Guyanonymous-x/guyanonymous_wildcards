Hongu Utux, a great rainbow of red, orange, yellow, green and blue, arches over a deep gorge between two mountain ridges, its curve wide enough for the dead to walk along
the rainbow bridge rises from a grassy ridge at the edge of the mountains and sweeps over a gorge of white cloud toward a bright land of green hills on the far side
below the rainbow bridge lies a deep gorge of dark rock, a fast white river at its bottom, large crabs clinging to the wet stones
