In the style of Beauvais Tapestry Manufactory, a playful, pastoral Rococo character. Scenes of gallant romance, children at play and fanciful animals are rendered in soft, sweet colour, pink, cream, sky blue, pale green and gold with brown outlines. Frames of flowers, ribbons, scallops and C-shaped scrolls surround each scene. The finish is fine and smooth and the colours glow like painted pastel. Forms are plump with flowing curves, and the mood is light, romantic and decorative. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Beauvais Tapestry Manufactory in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
