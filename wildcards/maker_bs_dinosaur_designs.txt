Styled in a design language of hand-poured resin in organic, sculptural, brightly coloured shapes. Forms are blobs, pebbles, waves and smooth curvy tubes in swirling marbled resin, with glossy, translucent surfaces mixing candy colours such as watermelon pink, tangerine, turquoise, lime, lilac, cobalt, ivory and deep black. Everything follows the same fluid, tactile forms with uneven hand-poured edges. Playful, artistic, joyful mood, on pastel backgrounds with colourful reflections. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
