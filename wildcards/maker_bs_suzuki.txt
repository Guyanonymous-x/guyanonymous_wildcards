Styled in a design language of compact, practical Japanese design with a bold. Palette is red, cobalt blue, with dependable greys, black and cheerful pastel accents. Shapes are small, tall, sturdy and friendly, with simple squared-off forms, gently rounded corners and clear flat panels. Finishes are matte plastic, painted enamel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
