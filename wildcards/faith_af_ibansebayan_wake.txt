a woman wailer in her sixties in a dark sarong sits by the body and leads the soul on its road, tears on her cheeks, her hands raised and her eyes closed
at the Gawai Antu the kin in their twenties to seventies set out rice, wine and small carved models of a longhouse, jars and tools for the dead to take back to Sebayan
