Styled in a design language of chunky rounded forms with thick softened corners and no sharp edges anywhere. Materials are glossy moulded plastic and warm lacquered wood with slightly worn paint. Palette is primary red, sunny yellow, cobalt blue and grass green against clean white and natural pale timber. Flat lithographed decoration shows simple smiling faces made from two dots and a curve, little cartoon animals and dotted rim details in a naive hand-drawn manner. Round peg-like silhouettes, oval badges and big circles repeat as a motif. Lettering is rounded, friendly and lowercase-leaning, set in white on a bold red band or oval emblem. Everything feels sturdy, cheerful, safe and handmade-simple. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
