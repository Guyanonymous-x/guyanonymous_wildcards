In the style of Camper, Mallorcan footwear. The shoes are quirky, comfortable, rounded and chunky, such as stitched leather sneakers with rounded toes, thick soles and vulcanised edges, and a rubber sole with a graphic design of bumps, circles or a wavy tread. Pelotas have a sole with tiny nubbles like a tennis ball. Twins are mismatched pairs in different colours or details, each foot visibly different. Uppers are soft leather or canvas in muted tones of brown, beige, white, grey, navy and olive, with a splash of red, yellow or green, and visible contrast stitching. Details include a simple small tag with the name, white laces and an opening of rounded padding. Boxes are plain cardboard in white and brown. The mood is playful, down-to-earth, relaxed and a bit nerdy.
