Styled in a design language of lavish flat floral ornament painted freehand on white or coloured grounds. Flowers are stylised and fantastical, shown flat from above or side-on, with fern-like leaves, curling tendrils, berries, tiny grape clusters and the occasional bird. Brush strokes are visible and clearly shaped, a comb stroke, a seed, a teardrop, a tapering curl, with berries made by a fingertip dab giving a lighter centre ringed with a darker edge. Colours are strong and clear, scarlet, cobalt, magenta, orange, lemon yellow and leaf green on white, black, red, yellow or deep blue. Symmetrical, gracefully balanced, lively and celebratory. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
