Styled in a design language of sensuous, artistic, aristocratic art deco sculpture. Flowing swept curves, teardrop and horseshoe outlines, fine parallel louvre lines and elegant tapering forms with a polished metal spine. Palette is French racing blue, black over red, deep cream, ivory and two-tone schemes. Finishes are nickel-polished metal, engine-turned aluminium in tight repeating swirls, soft red or brown leather and deep lacquer. Geometric sunburst and stepped motifs appear on flat graphic surfaces. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
