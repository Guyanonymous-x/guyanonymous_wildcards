Styled in a design language of calm, minimalist and timeless. Thin vertical aluminium lines with slim aluminium planes hung from them on small pegs. Surfaces are powder-coated in white, black or light grey, or smooth plywood in pale birch, with sleek clean edges. Crisp panels in matte pale colours with narrow slots and small detailing. Everything is clean, orderly and rectilinear with thin lines and no decoration. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
