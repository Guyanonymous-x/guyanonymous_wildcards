In the style of Shaw Brothers studio films, colour-saturated Hong Kong martial arts and costume dramas shot on sound stages with painted backdrops, vivid reds, yellows and jade greens, silk robes with embroidered dragons, sword fights in bamboo groves and inn courtyards, fast zoom shots, theatrical studio fog, bold hand-painted posters with brushed calligraphy titles, dramatic action poses and a vivid 1970s Cantonese pulp feel.
