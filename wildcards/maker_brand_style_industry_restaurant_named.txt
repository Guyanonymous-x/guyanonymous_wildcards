In the style of In-N-Out, clean, retro Californian, bright and uncluttered. White fields carry red lettering in a chunky rounded italic outline, with a bright red and yellow boomerang-like arrow swoosh and a pair of crossed yellow palm-like shapes. Repeating borders of small red palm-tree motifs and slim red bands run along white paper and cardboard surfaces, with simple flat red and white colour blocking and glossy printed coatings. A limited palette of red, white and sunny yellow keeps everything crisp. A tiny thin red script phrase sits beneath the name. Matte uncoated paper and shiny red lacquered details. Fresh, nostalgic, honest and sunny.
In the style of McDonalds, bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds. Lettering is soft, chunky, rounded sans-serif in white or yellow.
In the style of Nandos, warm, bold, casual, spicy and lively. A stylised black and red rooster emblem with a heart on its chest sits beside a heavy hand-cut wordmark in glossy chilli red or black. Palette runs through chilli red, terracotta, burnt orange, sunny yellow, teal, mint and soft peach, set in flat blocks. Surfaces carry African-inspired geometric patterns, bold arcs, semicircles, sun discs, zigzags and hand-printed abstract portraits with a screen-print look. Kraft cardboard and matte paper in warm earth tones are cut into angular tabs with colourful striped trims and rough linocut edges. Playful, cheeky short-hand lettering in black on bright fields. Vibrant, sociable, handmade and fiery.
In the style of Yo Sushi, playful, futuristic, pop-culture Japan-inspired and brisk. Sleek spaces with a continuous flowing line carrying small colour-coded rounds, slick white surfaces, glossy finishes, moulded plastic and neon lighting. Bright hot pink, red and black graphics with bubbly lettering, and large manga-style cartoon characters. Colour indicates value. Palette is hot pink, red, black, white and neon blue.
