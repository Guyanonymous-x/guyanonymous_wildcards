In the style of Land Rover, a rugged slab-sided utility four-wheel-drive vehicle with flat aluminium panels, an upright bluff nose, and a plain rectangular grille between round headlamps set into the wings. Square bonnet with exposed hinges, boxy cab, squared wheel arches, a spare wheel on the bonnet or tailgate, and a short stubby stance on chunky treaded tyres on steel or plain alloy wheels. Roofs have a safari-style roof rack or canvas top, with a contrast white roof on later estates. Paint in matt olive green, bronze green, sand, poppy red and pale grey. Interior is spartan, with painted metal dash, bare rubber matting, simple dials and tough fabric seats. Mood is honest, utilitarian and farmyard-tough.
