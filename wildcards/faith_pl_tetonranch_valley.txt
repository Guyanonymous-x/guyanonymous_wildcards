Paradise Valley, a wide valley of grassland along the Yellowstone river, a long flat-floored trough of tan and green pasture many times wider than the river that winds down its middle, dotted with ranch buildings and hay fields, with high mountain walls rising steeply on both sides
the Yellowstone river with cottonwoods, a broad swift river of clear green water sliding over gravel bars and around low islands, its banks lined with tall cottonwood trees with gray furrowed trunks and shimmering leaves, and thickets of red-stemmed willow
hay fields with round bales, wide mown meadows of short pale stubble striped by the cutting machines, dotted with big golden cylinders of rolled hay lying at even distances, a wheeled sprinkler line stretched across the far end
