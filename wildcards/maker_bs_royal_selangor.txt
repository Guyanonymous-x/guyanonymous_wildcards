In the style of Royal Selangor, Malaysian pewter tableware, polished silvery grey, soft lustrous metal with hammered or smooth finish, in tankards, bowls, tea sets, trays and vases, adorned with engraved or embossed motifs of hibiscus flowers, bamboo, batik-like flowers and rope borders. Heavy-bottomed, rounded forms with simple handles, sometimes mixed with dark wood or leather accents. Soft, bright, cool gleam, refined, tropical colonial table.
