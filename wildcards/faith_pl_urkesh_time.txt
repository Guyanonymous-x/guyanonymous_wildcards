in spring, the plain green with wheat and red with poppies, young wheat standing knee high in bright even green across the flat land, loose drifts of scarlet poppies and yellow wild mustard along the field edges and over the slopes of the mound, soft clear light under a blue sky with tall white clouds, and snow still showing on the far mountains to the north
in summer, the wheat harvested and the plain golden, wide fields of short pale stubble and scattered straw bales reaching to a horizon blurred by heat haze, the grass on the mound burned to straw yellow, the sun high and white in a cloudless sky, short hard shadows under the stone steps and dust lifting behind a distant tractor
at sunset, the mound dark against an orange sky, the long low hump of the Tell Mozan mound a flat brown-black shape with the notch of the trenches and the line of the great stairway just visible on its side, the sun a red disk touching the level horizon, bands of orange, rose and violet above it and the last light lying copper on the fields
