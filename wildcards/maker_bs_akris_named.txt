Styled in a design language of Swiss quiet-luxury built on architectural minimalism, with precisely cut, straight, clean lines in double-faced wool, gabardine, silk crepe and crisp cotton. Palette is restrained, with black, ivory, camel, charcoal, navy, sand and soft grey, with occasional accents of coral or deep green. Details are subtle, such as laser-cut edges, flat welt seams, bonded edges, hidden closures and geometric architectural prints inspired by buildings. Silhouettes are clean, sharp and balanced, with a refined, understated, polished mood and cool gallery-like light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
