In the style of Schiaparelli, witty surrealist couture where ordinary things become clever visual jokes. Illusion tricks make knitted, printed or embroidered details pretend to be something else, and familiar objects are reimagined as sculptural forms. Shocking hot pink is the signature colour, set against black, gold and deep jewel tones. Strong, padded, sharply exaggerated structure is paired with bold exposed fastenings and ornaments modelled as body parts, creatures or glass flowers, plus dense gold embroidery. Daring, artistic, humorous and sculptural, with 1930s avant-garde glamour.
