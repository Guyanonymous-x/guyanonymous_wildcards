In the style of Onitsuka Tiger, slim, retro, low-profile sporting design from the 1960s and 70s. Elongated narrow shapes with thin flat bases and a contrasting side stripe formed by crossing wavy parallel lines. Bodies are cream or white with accents in red, navy, yellow, forest green and black, often two-tone suede and breathable mesh with tonal reinforced parts. Small details include tiny perforations, pale gum-coloured edging, thin cords, and a tiny embroidered striding big cat mark. Clean, nostalgic, compact and athletic, with a subtle vintage gym mood.
