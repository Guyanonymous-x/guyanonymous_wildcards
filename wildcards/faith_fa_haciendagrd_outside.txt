the gurdwara seen from its gravel forecourt, a long single-story building of smooth brown plaster with rounded corners and deep-set windows, a broad golden dome with a slender spire rising from the middle of its flat roof, tall cottonwoods behind and the blue line of the Jemez Mountains on the skyline
the entrance, a pair of carved wooden doors under a porch of round log posts, racks of shoes along the wall beside it, a stone trough with running water for washing the feet, and members in white stepping in barefoot with their heads covered, above the doors a long carved wooden board with gold-painted capitals reading "SIRI SINGHASAN E KHALSA" and on the gatepost at the lane a small blue board with white capitals reading "SAT NAM"
beside the building a tall flagpole wrapped from top to bottom in saffron-orange cloth, flying a triangular saffron flag with a dark blue design of an upright double-edged sword inside a ring flanked by two curved swords, the golden dome behind it
the grounds of the community around the gurdwara, low brown houses with flat roofs, a dirt lane lined with coyote fences of upright sticks, vegetable gardens, an open-sided hall with long rows of mats where the shared meal is served, and dry hills spotted with dark juniper
