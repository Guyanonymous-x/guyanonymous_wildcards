In the style of Louis Poulsen, calm, sculptural Scandinavian restraint where light is softened and glare is controlled. Layered concentric curves, overlapping discs or dozens of overlapping leaf-like plates form smooth symmetrical shapes. Materials are polished steel, chrome, brass, copper and white lacquer with soft warm reflections. Smooth, restrained, elegant and perfectly proportioned, giving a soft diffused glow and a quietly luxurious modernist calm. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Louis Poulsen in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
