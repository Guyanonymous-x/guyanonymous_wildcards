the rock sanctuary of Perperikon crowns a high rounded hill of pale gray-brown volcanic rock, its walls, steps, rooms and a great round altar cut straight into the living stone, forested ridges falling away below
the sacred mountain Kogaionon rises steep and wooded from the Dacian forests, a dark cave opening in its side where Zalmoxis went down, a stair cut into the rock of the slope leading up to it
the rock shrine of Tatul stands on a stony peak of the eastern Rhodopes, a tall cut block of rock with two hollowed graves on its flat top, oak forest covering the ridges on every side
