Styled in a design language of heavy, thick-walled studio glass with bold sculptural shapes and a strongly textured surface. Irregular bark-like ridges, chunky bubbles, dimples, moulded ribbing and cased layers of strong colour create a tactile feel. The palette is deep and earthy, tangerine orange, meadow green, kingfisher blue, aubergine purple, ruby red, amber and smoky grey. Forms are cylindrical, tapered, faceted or swirled with heavy bases and slight asymmetry. Surfaces are glossy with a deep glowing inner colour, in a mid-century manner. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
