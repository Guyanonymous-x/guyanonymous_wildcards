Styled in a design language of minimalist, gleaming, modern reusable spaceflight. Smooth white cylinders with clean bold finishes, or polished bare stainless steel, with slender black accents. Lower areas are sooty and streaked with carbon from fiery returns. Few marks, no ornament, only function. Sleek, futuristic, confident, utilitarian and quietly daring. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
