Styled in a design language of joyful, refreshing, nostalgic Americana. Dominant saturated red paired with clean white, used in broad flat fields with a flowing white ribbon wave. Shapes are curvy, contoured and hourglass-like, with glossy glass, polished chrome and condensation droplets. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
