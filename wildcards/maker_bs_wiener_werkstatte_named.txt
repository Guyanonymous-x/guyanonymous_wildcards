Styled in a design language of viennese Secession craft with geometric abstraction, strict grids of black and white squares, concentric circles, stylised roses, wavy lines and bold block-printed patterns in orange, coral, turquoise, black and gold. Pierced squares in metal, ebonised wood, enamel, elongated forms with grid-patterned surfaces, chequered bands and stylised flowers. Sophisticated, rhythmic, ornamental modernism with precise linear elegance. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
