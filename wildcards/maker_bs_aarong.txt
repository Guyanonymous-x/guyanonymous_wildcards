In the style of Aarong, a Bangladeshi handicraft and clothing shop. Garments are saris, tunics and kurtas in handwoven cotton, silk and jamdani muslin, in vivid colours such as turmeric yellow, red, indigo, leaf green and white, with block-printed borders, tiny woven floral motifs and fine stripes. The most iconic craft is nakshi kantha, a quilted embroidery of running stitch forming flowers, fish, birds, trees and boats in rows of red, blue and green thread on white or black cloth, with a wavy hand-stitched texture. Homeware includes terracotta pots, jute baskets and rugs, cane stools, brass bells and hand painted wooden toys. Stores are tall and bright with hanging fabrics, rustic wood shelves, earthy walls and a gentle orange accent, and tags are cream paper with simple black type. Mood is rural, handmade, colourful, warm and lovingly textured, like a village fair in a tidy city building.
