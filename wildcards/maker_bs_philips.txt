In the style of Philips, Dutch radios and appliances of the 1930s to 1960s. Wooden or bakelite cabinets with rounded edges, big round dials in glass with illuminated station names, and pale ivory or chocolate-brown plastics. Controls are big ribbed knobs and piano-style keys. Fabric grilles with vertical gold slats, and a small star and wave logo. Later products use bright plastic in cream, mustard and pastel with gently curved forms. Mood is homely, rounded and optimistic.
