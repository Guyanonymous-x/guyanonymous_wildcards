Styled in a design language of carefully revived classical roman letterforms and crisp new classics. Lettering is a fine serif with high contrast, bracketed serifs, balanced proportions and even colour, with a few geometric sans-serif and slab designs. Layouts are neat, black ink on cream paper, with rows of alphabets in graduated sizes, small numbers and ornamental rules. Compositions use centred headings, small capitals, letter-spaced capitals and discreet fleurons. The look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, like a well-printed British book or newspaper.
