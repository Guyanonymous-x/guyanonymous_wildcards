The owl sits upright, its great round eyes and short hooked beak shaped in rough gray concrete, its folded wings and breast blotched with moss, ferns growing from cracks around its base.
The effigy of Dull Care lies on a wooden bier in the boat, a human-sized figure tightly wrapped in pale cloth and bound with cords, its face blank.
The torches are long wooden poles with burning heads of rag, and the robed men carry them in two lines that curve along the lakeshore.
