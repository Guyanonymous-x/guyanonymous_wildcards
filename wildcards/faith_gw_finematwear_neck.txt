an ula nifo, the whale-tooth necklace worn only by a high chief or the chief's ceremonial daughter or son as the mark of that rank, long curved pointed pieces of whale tooth strung side by side so that they fan out over the collarbones in a row of pale ivory points
an ula fala, a thick heavy necklace of pandanus fruit segments painted glossy bright red, each the size of a thumb, hanging in a long loop to the middle of the chest
a long garland of fresh white frangipani flowers and dark green leaves hanging to the waist, with a short necklace of small white cowrie shells at the throat
