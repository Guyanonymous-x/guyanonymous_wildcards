In the style of Ralph Lauren, nostalgic old-money American heritage, part ivy campus, part country club, part western ranch. Palette is deep forest green, navy, burgundy, camel, cream and crisp white, with a bright red accent. Patterns include cable knit, madras and tartan plaids, herringbone tweed, Fair Isle bands, blanket stripes and stars and stripes motifs. Materials are oxford cloth, wool, corduroy, weathered saddle leather and brass buttons. A small embroidered mounted rider on a polo pony sits quietly as a mark, alongside bold collegiate numbers and crests. Lettering is spaced serif capitals in gold on dark navy or green storefront signs. Photography is warm, sunlit, aspirational and storied, with wood panelling, dogs, horses and autumn light.
