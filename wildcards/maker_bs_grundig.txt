Styled in a design language of dependable postwar German craftsmanship. Palette is walnut brown, cream, soft gold, hammered grey-green, black and bright orange. Materials are figured wood veneer, brushed gold and chrome metal trim, enamelled badge plates, cream and brown plastic and fine woven fabric. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
