Styled in a design language of functional, all-night, highway roadside, flat and strongly lit. Low box forms with yellow and black trim, big windows, bright harsh overhead light, long counters and a checkered floor. Palette is lemon yellow, black, white and chrome. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
