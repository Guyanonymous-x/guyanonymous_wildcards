In the style of Raffles Hotel, a white colonial building with a long arcade, symmetrical Renaissance-style facade, black-and-white tiles, a bright green lawn and tall palms. Wide verandas with louvred shutters, ceiling fans, rattan chairs, dark teak and polished brass. A uniformed doorman in a white turban-like cap and white tunic at the entrance. Green dining area with white linen, Peranakan tile floors and tall arched windows. Palette of white, deep green, teak brown and brass. The look is tropical colonial, airy, cool and graceful with grand arches.
