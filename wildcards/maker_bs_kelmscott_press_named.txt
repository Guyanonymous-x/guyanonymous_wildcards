In the style of Kelmscott Press, dense Gothic-revival compositions in heavy blackletter lettering on thick handmade cream paper, with red ink for accents. Each page is framed by luxuriant woodcut borders of winding acanthus, vines, flowers, leaves and fruit, white on black, interlaced thickly. Large foliage initials on a dark ground open each section. Text is tightly justified with minimal leading and wide margins, giving a dark and ornate texture. Soft vellum and silk ties add tactile richness. The mood is medieval, rich and romantic.
