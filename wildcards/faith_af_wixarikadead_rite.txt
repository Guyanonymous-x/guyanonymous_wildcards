on the fifth night the mara'akame, a man in his sixties in an embroidered white shirt and a hat hung with feathers, sits by the fire holding his feathered wand, following the soul
the kin of the dead, men and women in their twenties to seventies in white cotton embroidered with deer and flowers, sit around the fire waiting for the soul to come back
the mara'akame in his fifties brushes the kin with his feathered wand to cleanse them, the soul shown as a small pale light hovering above the fire
