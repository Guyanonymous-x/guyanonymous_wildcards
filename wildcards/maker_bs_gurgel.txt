In the style of Gurgel, small Brazilian utility cars with a plastic-fibreglass body mounted on a steel tube backbone, a boxy upright shape, a flat bonnet and a high squared cabin with a tall doorless or canvas top look. Round headlamps in a plain recessed face, a short front with simple slot grille, black bumpers, big rounded wheel arches, small steel wheels and small tail lamps. Interior with bare fibreglass, flat seats and a small dash. Colours of bright orange, lime green, bright yellow and sky blue in solid, shiny gelcoat. Small, toylike, rugged and home-made.
