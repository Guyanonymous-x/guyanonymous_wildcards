Styled in a design language of tough. A rampant lion stands upright on a rolling stone, drawn in solid bold red or black silhouette. Palette is signal red, black, white, silver and sunburnt tones such as gold, burnt orange and dusty tan. Shapes are strong, muscular and flat with bold stripes and thick colour blocks. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
