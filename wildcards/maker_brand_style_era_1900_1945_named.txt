In the style of Guna Mola Cooperatives, graphic, rhythmic, vibrant and strongly handmade design. Two to seven layers of thin cotton in contrasting solid colours, the top layer cut away in curving shapes so the layers beneath peek through, with edges turned under and hemmed with tiny nearly invisible stitches. The design fills the whole panel with energetic horror vacui of tightly packed shapes in parallel concentric outlines, like a thick-lined labyrinth, depicting birds, fish, sea turtles, flowers, masks and abstract geometry. Colours are bright, fully saturated and placed in bold contrast, red against orange, black against yellow, green against hot pink, blue against white. Flat and symmetrical like stained glass.
In the style of Haitian Metal Drum Art, raw, intricate, spiritual and richly patterned flat sculpture cut from recycled steel. Panels are flattened, drawn with chalk, then chiselled and punched so the line work appears as cut-through openings and raised ridges. Subjects are a tree of life, mermaids, birds, fish, sun faces, spirits with elaborate headdresses and crosses, drawn with symmetrical swirling leaves, scales, dotted outlines, hearts and curling vines. The metal is left dark grey and rust-brown, sometimes polished to rough silver with burn marks, or hand-painted in bright enamel like red, yellow and turquoise. Shapes are oval, round or irregular with ragged cut edges. A rugged industrial material turned into delicate lace.
In the style of Acoma Pueblo Pottery, crisp, disciplined, optical and graceful extremely thin-walled hand-coiled ceramics with fine-line painting. Rounded forms with high shoulders and small openings, and low wide shapes, with a very fine white slip surface that is smooth, matte and chalk-like. The walls are paper thin. Decoration is painted with a fine brush in black and rusty orange or red-brown on the creamy white ground, in dense precise geometric bands. Motifs are fine-line lattices and hatching, interlocking triangles, birds, rainbows, stepped clouds, animals with a heart line and scrolls, with bands wrapping horizontally and tidy empty zones at the base. Unglazed, powdery. Like delicate op-art on earthenware.
In the style of San Ildefonso Pueblo Pottery, sleek, dark, formal, sculptural and timeless. Motifs are bold and graphic, such as coiled serpents, rain clouds, stepped shapes, lightning zigzags, mountains and small tracks, arranged in one confident band. Perfect balance, calm and ceremonial.

Balanced, precise and optical, with crisp straight edges.
