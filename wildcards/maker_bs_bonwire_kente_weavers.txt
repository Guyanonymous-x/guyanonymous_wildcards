Styled in a design language of hand-loomed narrow strips of silk and cotton, about four inches wide, sewn edge to edge into large cloths. Brilliant geometric patterns of interlocking bands, squares, zigzags, stepped diamonds and blocks in gold, kente yellow, deep red, emerald green, royal blue, black and white. Weft-faced areas alternate with striped plain sections, and supplementary thread motifs create tiny symbols such as ladders and checks. Surfaces are rich, thick, slightly ridged and rhythmic, each strip a different arrangement. Regal, ceremonial, celebratory mood.
