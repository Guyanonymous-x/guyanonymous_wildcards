In the style of Cross Pens, slim cylindrical forms with a clean, classical shape and a smooth finish in chrome, brushed gold-filled or lacquer. Surfaces carry a tight diamond-patterned engraving or fine ridged stripes, with a straight narrow accent ending in a tiny ball. Rings are thin and the profile is a perfectly even tube with a rounded top. Colours are silver, gold, black and deep blue. Leather or velvet linings add softness. The mood is business-like, classic, restrained and subtly formal. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Cross Pens in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
