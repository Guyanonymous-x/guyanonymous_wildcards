in the dark bush beyond the graves the mopaditi, pale spirit figures of men and women in their thirties to eighties painted in white ochre, stand among the trees in their own camp
the spirit of the dead man, in his sixties and painted in red and yellow ochre, walks away from the grave poles into the bush where the mopaditi wait around a small fire
the mopaditi are seen at night as dim white forms between the paperbark and stringybark trees, their faces painted over in dots, watching the living camp from the edge of the bush
