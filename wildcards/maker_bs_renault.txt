Styled in a design language of cheerful, practical and boldly geometric French design. A diamond emblem, rendered as a flat lozenge, outlined chrome rhombus or interlocking angular strips, repeats as the signature motif, with its form changing from line art to solid shape. Palette of sunny yellow as the hero colour against black and white, with bright orange, vivid red, sky blue and beige. Shapes are boxy, squared and friendly, with soft rounded corners, rub strips and honest flat panels. Pop-art optimism, striped and checked cloth, matte plastics and tidy modular grids. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
