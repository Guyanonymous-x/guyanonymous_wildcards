In the style of Denby, English stoneware tableware in rich, earthy, drippy glazes, with chunky rounded casseroles, mugs, plates and jugs, wax-resist patterns and tactile dark rims. Colours are deep brown, mustard, blue-green, olive, smoky grey-blue and cream, speckled and reactive with natural variation, in brown-and-cream swirled and pebble-textured designs. Heavy, hand-thrown look, warm, practical, rustic oven-to-table and retro 1970s dining room.
