Styled in a design language of glossy black polished lacquer with a mirror-like curved edge and a sweeping asymmetric curved side. Supports are sturdy, round and tapered, ending in brass, with a lyre-shaped gold-toned metal assembly. A gilt metal frame shows within, lettering is in gold, and contrast comes from white and ebony. Decoration is elegant and restrained. Alternate finishes are dark mahogany or burl walnut. The mood is grand, opulent, formal and concert-hall dignified.
