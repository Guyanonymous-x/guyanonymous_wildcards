Styled in a design language of robust, urban and friendly modern Indian design. Clean tall boxy volumes with a prominent upright form carrying a flowing wedge of blue or chrome, and a split arrangement with slim strips above and larger forms below. A bold angular shoulder, black cladding around openings, a strong squared stance and big geometric diamond-cut motifs. A swirling oval mark. Brightly painted tall flat masses and contrast stitching. Urban grey, pure white, flame red and deep black. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
