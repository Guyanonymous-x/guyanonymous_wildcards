Styled in a design language of kawaii softness built from round, plump, pillow-like shapes with tiny black dot eyes set wide apart and almost no features. Outlines are thin, even and gentle with flat fills. Palette is pastel pink, baby blue, lavender, mint, butter yellow and clean white, with a single red or hot pink bow-like accent. Surfaces are glossy, satin or glittery with a candy-sweet sheen, in translucent plastic, plush fleece and shiny paper. Scattered hearts, stars, tiny fruit and flowers form repeating confetti patterns on pale grounds. Generous empty space keeps every layout light, sweet and tidy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
