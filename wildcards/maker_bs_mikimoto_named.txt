Styled in a design language of classic refinement centred on round, lustrous cultured pearls with a creamy white to pale pink glow and a mirror-like surface, arranged in long, uniform, graduated strands and multiple rows. Mounts are restrained and elegant in polished white and yellow gold and platinum, with small round diamonds used as pave or tiny accent rims. Designs feel graceful, symmetrical and timeless, sometimes with soft rounded shapes inspired by shells, waves and flowers. Dark navy and pale blue accents. Calm, refined and luminous, in soft diffused light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
