Styled in a design language of American mid-century domestic design in glossy glass and creamy opal milk glass. Rounded, plump forms in tidy graduated sets, with smooth, seamless, shiny surfaces. Sunny, saturated colours of turquoise, aqua, primary red, buttercup yellow, avocado green, burnt orange and pink, in flat solid fields. Printed patterns repeat in white or contrasting colours, with snowflakes, daisies, gooseberries, butterflies, stripes, flowers and atomic starbursts, along with retro gingham checks. Cheerful, nostalgic, optimistic and thoroughly wholesome. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
