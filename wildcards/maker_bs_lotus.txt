In the style of Lotus, an extremely low, light and tiny British sports car with a fibreglass shell, a sharp wedge or rounded teardrop nose, pop-up or small round headlamps, and a high tapering tail. Roofline barely above the knee, wide haunches over small wheels, bare minimal bumpers, and a round green and yellow badge. Paint in British racing green with a bright yellow stripe, bright yellow, white, orange and metallic silver. Alloy wheels are small and finely spoked or in cast star form. Interior is basic and cramped, with thin bucket seats in cloth or vinyl, exposed aluminium, small instruments under the dash, and a leather-wrapped wheel. Mood is stripped, minimal, purposeful and track-bred.
