Styled in a design language of dense, sturdy Renaissance compositions with well-spaced roman lettering, italic accents and elaborate woodcut or engraved frontispieces. Frontispieces are architectural, with columns, pediments, allegorical figures, garlands and cartouches, plus a small emblem of a hand holding a compass. Black ink sits on warm cream laid paper, with decorative headpieces, ornamented initials and neat marginal notes. Arrangements are symmetrical and orderly. Surfaces are tooled calf or vellum. The look is rich, rational, ornate and humanist. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
