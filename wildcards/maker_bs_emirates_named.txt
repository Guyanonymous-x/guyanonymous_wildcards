Styled in a design language of lavish, polished desert-luxury. Gleaming white grounds meet vivid ruby red in confident fields. Accents use rich gold, champagne, deep burgundy and warm sand beige, with soft gradients and satin sheen. Surfaces feel premium, with polished lacquer, burnished metal, supple leather and fine woven fabric. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
