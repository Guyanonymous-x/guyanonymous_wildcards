Styled in a design language of understated island-casual, preppy and relaxed. Pattern is printed so the outer face looks washed, faded and muted as if sun-bleached, on a thick, plain, honest woven cloth. Colours are soft and tonal, navy, forest green, dusty rust, burgundy, sand, teal and faded sky blue, usually in one colour family with a lighter shade for outlines. Large-scale tropical foliage, flowers, island and ocean motifs repeat in an even, orderly rhythm. Shapes are simple and tidy with soft edges and natural dark details. A quiet, subtle, never loud version of tropical print. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
