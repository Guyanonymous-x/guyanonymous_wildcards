Styled in a design language of solid, utilitarian British classic. Sturdy profile, bright finishes in polished chrome and cherry red, black or sunshine yellow. Rounded volumes with chrome panels, valanced curves and bulky silver forms. Upright lines, wire-like details and ribbed textures. Honest, hardy and plain-spoken, with bright metal against solid colour and a practical, no-nonsense post-war workmanlike mood. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
