the dress of the Guanches, the first people of the Canary Islands off northwest Africa before the Spanish conquest, adult men and women in their twenties to fifties in tamarcos, cloaks of tanned goatskin sewn from several pelts with the hair side in, reaching the knees
tunics of soft goat leather dyed red or yellow, sewn with thin thongs and belted at the waist, with sandals of goat hide and caps of goatskin
the women in long skirts of goat leather to the ankle and capes over the shoulders, the men in short leather tunics, both with long hair
goatskin cloaks held at the throat with bone pins over leather tunics, worn by elders in a land of volcanic rock
