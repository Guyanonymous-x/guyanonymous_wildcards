The Master raps the gavel once on the arm of his chair, and every member rises and stands still with one hand held flat across the chest.
The deacons walk slowly around the checkered floor in step, one behind the other, carrying their wands upright, and turn at each corner in a square.
A member stands at a tracing board giving a lesson, pointing at the painted symbols one by one with a thin wooden pointer while the others listen from the benches.
