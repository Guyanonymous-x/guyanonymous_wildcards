Styled in a design language of wholesome teenage newsstand comic look with clean, uniform thin black outlines, simple friendly characters with round heads, dot eyes, button noses, bright wide grins and neat hairstyles. Flat saturated four-colour printing with halftone dots on slightly yellowed pulp paper in red, yellow, blue and green, sock-hop and soda-shop settings, speech balloons in a hand-lettered rounded face and a cheerful clean-cut mid-century small-town mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
