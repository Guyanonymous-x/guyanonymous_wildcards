Styled in a design language of viennese grandeur in glossy black or rich warm wood such as walnut, cherry or maple. Proportions are extended and generous, with a slender curved edge and a refined, quiet profile. Supports are carved and tapering, and openwork details are lace-like with shallow ornate shapes. Edging is thin, and trim in gold or brass is tasteful. Some versions have elaborate inlay, gilt carving and painted panels in the Baroque and Secession manner. The mood is aristocratic, warm, ornate and old-world. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
