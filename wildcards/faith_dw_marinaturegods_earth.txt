Mlande Ava, the Earth Mother, a broad woman in her fifties in a long white embroidered dress and a tall pointed headdress of white cloth and coins, rises from a field of ripe rye, ears of grain in her hands
Tul Ava, the Fire Mother, a woman in her forties in a red embroidered dress, stands among the fires of the grove, the flames curling around her hem
Mardezh Ava, the Wind Mother, a slender woman in her thirties in a long pale dress, her hair and sleeves streaming in the wind, sweeps over the fields bending the rye
