In the style of Air India, a polished white fuselage with a red-orange and white stripe along the windows, ending at a tail with a stylised red swan or centaur-archer disc shape. A distinct window frame with an Indian arched shape painted in red around each window. Interior with carved pillars, Mughal-inspired arches, crimson and gold fabric, and a mascot of a bowing character in a turban with a moustache. Palette of crimson, white and saffron, with gold trim. The look is warm, ornate, regal, Indian palace, with arched windows and bright cabin textiles.
