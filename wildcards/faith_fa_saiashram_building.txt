Sai Kulwant Hall, the vast darshan hall, a wide roof held up on rows of slender pillars over a floor of polished white marble, its front edged with carved arches painted pale blue, pink and cream and topped with small gilded domes
the Mandir, the old temple at the head of the hall, a two-story building painted pale pink, sky blue and soft yellow, its front crowded with ornate moldings, little balconies and a row of small domes in gold and white
the Mahasamadhi, the tomb of Sathya Sai Baba at the front of the hall, a long raised block of white marble carved with lotus petals and heaped with garlands of red roses and orange marigolds, under a canopy on four slender gilded pillars
the Poornachandra Auditorium, a huge hall with a broad curved roof, its long front painted pale yellow and pink, a row of tall square pillars along it and wide doors standing open
