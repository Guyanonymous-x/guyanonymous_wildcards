In the style of Royal Copenhagen, thin translucent white porcelain with hand-painted cobalt blue patterns, small blue flowers, ribbed fluted edges and delicate lace-like pierced rims. Brushwork is crisp, symmetrical and orderly, sometimes softened with pastel overglaze, and three small wavy lines recur as a quiet signature. Heirloom elegance, precise and serene, with an orderly rhythm of repeated motifs and a cool, clean blue and white palette throughout. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
