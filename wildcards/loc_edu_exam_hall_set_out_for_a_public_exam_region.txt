English and Welsh public examinations, where the regulations are national and produce an identical room in every school, which is why this configuration is so recognizable
Scottish examinations under their own authority with equivalent rules
American standardized testing, usually conducted in ordinary classrooms rather than in a hall, which removes this room type entirely
French *baccalauréat* in a hall with a similar formality
Indian board examinations, held at enormous scale in halls and classrooms with their own long-established invigilation conventions
systems with continuous assessment and no terminal examination, where the room simply does not exist
