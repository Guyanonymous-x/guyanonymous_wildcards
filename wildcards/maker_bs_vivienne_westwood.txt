Styled in a design language of theatrical, rebellious and romantic punk meets historical costume. Tartan in red and black, buckled straps and zips, safety pins, corseted waists and bustier shapes, ruffled pirate volumes, hooped skirt structures, high platform volumes and draped asymmetrical jersey. An orb motif of a ringed globe with a cross in gold or silver, pearls, gold sequins and pink and green accents. Torn, slashed and layered. Irreverent, scholarly and sensual. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
