In the style of Halas lace from Hungary. This is a fine, hand-made needle lace stitched with a single needle and fine thread on a fixed pattern outline. Pieces are collars, doilies, table mats, bookmarks, cuffs, fans and framed panels, with a dense, even, light-as-air lace structure, tiny buttonhole stitches that create a solid motif surrounded by open meshes, and picots on the edges. Motifs are stylised Hungarian folk flowers such as tulips, roses and carnations, bell flowers, heart shapes, leaves, buds and vines, along with birds, symmetrical lozenges and scalloped borders. The colour is traditionally off-white or cream, but historical pieces appear in soft peach, pale orange, light green and butter yellow silk. The look is fine, airy, geometric-floral, delicate and graceful, with a handmade crispness, resembling frost patterns.
