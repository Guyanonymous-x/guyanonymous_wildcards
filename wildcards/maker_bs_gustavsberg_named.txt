Styled in a design language of warm, humane, playful Scandinavian modern studio ceramics. Soft, curved, slightly lopsided organic shapes such as pebble forms, leaf dishes, squashed spheres and wide-hipped jugs, with soft shoulders and visible finger marks. A signature dark green-brown stoneware is inlaid with delicate silver leaf patterns. Plain white with coloured berries and flowers. Intimate handmade surfaces and a calm earthy palette that suits teak and linen. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
