Styled in a design language of dainty, sugary Second Empire Parisian tearoom elegance. Pastel colours in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow and coffee brown, arranged in neat stacked pyramids. Pale mint or pink surfaces with gold embossed script, gilt edges and satin ribbon. Powder-blue and pistachio paintwork, gilded mouldings, painted ceilings of cherubs and marble. Cursive gold lettering, a delicate oval crest and floral flourishes. Palette is pistachio, rose, cream and gold. Mood is ornate, gilded, feminine and delicate.
