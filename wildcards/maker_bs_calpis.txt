Styled in a design language of clean, fresh, summery, childlike, retro design built on a polka dot pattern. An even field of round white dots on a rich blue ground, standing for a starry Milky Way, sometimes inverted to blue dots on white. Tall slim shapes with narrow necks, wrapped in thin paper bearing the dots, or the same pattern at a larger scale with a glossy finish. Cloudy white tones, a pale blue ribbon of colour behind. Flavour variants shift accents to orange, peach and green but keep the dots. Limited blue and white palette, gentle geometry. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
