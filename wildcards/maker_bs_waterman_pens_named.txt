In the style of Waterman Pens, elegant slim, rounded forms in black hard rubber, red ripple pattern or sterling silver filigree overlay. Ripple designs swirl in mottled red and black, and metal overlays show Art Deco geometric grooves, basketweave and lacy filigree. Details include thin gold-filled bands, gently arched lines and fine ornament on metal accents. Colours are warm, rich and aged, with an orange-red sheen. Velvet lining adds softness. The mood is refined, Edwardian and Art Deco. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Waterman Pens in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
