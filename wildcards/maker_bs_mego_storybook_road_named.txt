Styled in a design language of 1970s storybook fantasy action figures, each shaped to its character with soft storybook paint, gingham dresses, straw-stuffed cloth, silver vacuum-metalized plastic and fake lion fur, sold on blister cards with a yellow road winding to a green city. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
