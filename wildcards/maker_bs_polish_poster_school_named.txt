Styled in a design language of expressive, surreal and witty hand-painted or collaged illustration built on a single bold metaphor rather than a literal scene, such as a giant eye, a distorted face, a flower with a thorn or a childlike doodle on empty space. Colours are saturated and unusual, acid yellow, blood red, deep blue, hot pink, mustard, turquoise and black, with ragged brushwork, gouache texture, torn paper edges and uneven print texture. Rough paper grain. Poetic, dark, humorous and arty. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
