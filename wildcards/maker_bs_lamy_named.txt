Styled in a design language of rigorous German modernist industrial design. Simple geometric solids, smooth cylinders, flat planes and strict uncluttered proportions with a single slim flat bar as an accent. Matte, fine-grained black, charcoal, white and satin brushed stainless steel, with bright saturated tones such as red, yellow, blue, lime and orange in plain solid blocks. Faceted surfaces, small round windows and subtle recessed details are the only ornament. Packaging is plain white or black card with sparse type and generous empty space. Functional, honest, disciplined, school-like and modern. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
