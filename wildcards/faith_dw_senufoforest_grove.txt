the sinzanga, the sacred grove, stands at the edge of the village as a dense dark island of tall old trees and tangled vines in the open savanna, a narrow path leading in under the branches
deep in the grove a small clearing opens among huge silk-cotton trees with buttressed trunks, the ground swept clean and a ring of carved wooden stools set at its edges
the grove rises above the round thatched houses and granaries of the village, its great trees dark green against the dry yellow fields of millet and yams around it
