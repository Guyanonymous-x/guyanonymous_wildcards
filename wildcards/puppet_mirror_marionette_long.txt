A carved wooden marionette of a woman in a long black gown stands on a small dressing table in front of an oval mirror in a gilded frame. Her painted face is turned to the glass, and in the mirror her reflection looks back, showing the strings running up from her head and hands and disappearing at the top of the picture. The dressing table holds a tiny powder box, a comb and a hand-held mirror, all in her scale.
