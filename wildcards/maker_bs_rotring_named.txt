Styled in a design language of precision drafting sensibility from Germany. Palette is matte black, brushed steel, silver, graphite and one vivid scarlet accent. Surfaces are knurled, crosshatched metal with fine machined ridges, satin anodised finishes and tiny white printed scale markings. Forms are hexagonal, cylindrical and tapered, slim and tool-like, with crisp edges and no ornament. Engineered, exact, draughtsman-like, serious and timeless. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
