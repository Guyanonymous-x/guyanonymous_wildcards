old erong, wooden coffins carved in the shape of a long boat with upturned ends, rest on the ledges, the dark wood cut with curling spirals and buffalo-head patterns, some split open with age so the gray bones and skulls of the long dead lie among the planks
coffins shaped like a buffalo and like a pig, carved from single hardwood logs, lie on a deep ledge under an overhang, their red, black and yellow paint faded and peeling and their lids slipped to one side
rows of small square wooden doors close the chambers cut into the rock, each door carved with buffalo heads and spirals and painted red, white, yellow and black, the rock around them streaked with dark water stains
