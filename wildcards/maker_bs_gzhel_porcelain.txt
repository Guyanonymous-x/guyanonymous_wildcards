In the style of Gzhel Porcelain, white glazed porcelain and earthenware painted only in cobalt blue brushwork, in graduated tones from pale sky blue to deep navy. Decorations are floral sprigs, leaves, swirling petals, birds, scalloped rims and simple landscapes, painted freely with a wide brush in a single stroke style. Forms include teapots, vases, figurines of animals, tea sets and samovar-like shapes, with rounded and plump outlines. The white ground is bright and glossy, and the blue appears washed and flowing. The mood is folk-like, cheerful, fresh and quintessentially Russian.
