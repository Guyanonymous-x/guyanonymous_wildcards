Styled in a design language of refined mid-century modernism with architectural calm. Forms are precise and minimal, using slim polished stainless steel, brushed chrome, glossy lacquer, travertine, marble and leather with crisp stitching and button tufting. Contours mix straight cantilevered lines with soft sculptural curves. Palette is restrained and sophisticated, with ivory, black, taupe, cognac, oatmeal and slate grey, lifted by rich textile colours in terracotta, tomato red, ochre, teal and cobalt. Woven fabrics show tactile geometric weaves, stripes, grids and abstract patterns with visible thread. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
