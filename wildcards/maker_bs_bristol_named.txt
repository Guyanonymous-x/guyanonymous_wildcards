In the style of Bristol, discreet, reserved, hand-built and clubby grand touring with an aircraft-inspired shape. A long mass, a smooth aerodynamic body and a gently curved roof. Dark green, grey, cream, black and claret. Deep leather, polished walnut and clear round faces. Small oval nose with twin kidney-shaped openings, round forms set in the flanks and hidden details behind flaps add quiet eccentricity. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
