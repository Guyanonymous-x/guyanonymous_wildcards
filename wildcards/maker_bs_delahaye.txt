Styled in a design language of flamboyant, theatrical, curvaceous Art Deco coachwork. Very long sweeping lines, a teardrop body, pontoon forms flowing over the base, a long mass running into a sloping end, fine chrome bars and chrome spears running through the curves. Two-tone schemes such as ivory over dark blue, silver over burgundy and deep black. Cream or red leather and engine-turned metal with round faces. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
