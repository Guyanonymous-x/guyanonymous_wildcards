Styled in a design language of neat, compact, well-proportioned French design with a sporty tidy hand. Clean wedge-like outlines, smooth uncluttered surfaces with a single crisp crease, black plastic edging and slim wraparound shapes. Palette is white, bright red, ocean blue, silver, dark green and black, with fine grey detailing. Materials include glossy enamel paint, deep-pile cloth, grey textured plastic and brushed chrome. Practical, lively and carefully balanced. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
