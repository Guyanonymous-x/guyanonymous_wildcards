In the style of Dior, Parisian couture refinement with soft feminine grace. Silhouettes are structured and sculpted, with nipped waists, rounded curves, full flowing volumes and delicate ribbon bows. Palette is dove grey, pearl white, blush pink, navy, black and touches of gold. Lettering is a spaced, elegant serif in capitals or a fine cursive. Packaging uses grey boxes, ribbons and gold-sparkled prints. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Dior in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
