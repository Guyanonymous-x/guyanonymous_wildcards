Styled in a design language of raw, futuristic, unpainted retro-science-fiction design. Surfaces are bare brushed stainless metal with a fine linear grain, left unpainted and cool silver grey, set against matte black plastic trim and dark charcoal fills. Forms are flat, wedge-like and razor sharp, built from straight edges, hard creases and slanted planes with no soft curves. Slim horizontal louvre patterns and recessed slots add rhythm. Accents are minimal, tiny warm orange or red points on an otherwise monochrome steel and black palette. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
