Styled in a design language of thick, dense, glossy-glazed cream or white stoneware with a heavy hand-thrown feel, big strap handles and sturdy rounded forms. Decoration is stamped by hand with cut sponges dipped in cobalt blue underglaze, producing clusters of tiny dots, peacock-eye circles, small florals, feathery sprigs, scallops, windmill patterns and rings of leaves in rhythmic repeats. Main colour is deep cobalt on white, with secondary green, yellow, brown, rust and purple for flowers and borders. The glaze is clear, smooth and shiny, pooling slightly at the base, with bold blue stripes at the edges. Rustic, cheerful, tidy and cottage-like. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
