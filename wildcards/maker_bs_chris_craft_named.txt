Styled in a design language of classic varnished American craftsmanship. Warm honey to red-brown mahogany planking with fine pale seams, polished to a deep glassy shine. Chrome and brass trim, tufted leather in red and cream, and small enamel badge discs in orange, black and cream with bold stripes. Rounded, streamlined, softly flared forms with slim tapering lines. Vintage red, navy, cream and chrome palette, handcrafted, nostalgic, sporty and carefree. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
