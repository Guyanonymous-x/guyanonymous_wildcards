below the earth a steep stair leads down into Xibalba to a crossroads of four roads, one red, one black, one white and one yellow, beside a river of dark blood and a river of yellow pus
in Xibalba the death lords One Death and Seven Death sit on stone benches, skeletal figures drawn as old men with bony ribs, swollen bellies and collars of eyeballs on their bare chests, smoking cigars
in the underworld the Hero Twins Hunahpu and Xbalanque, men in their twenties in short loincloths and thick hip yokes of padded leather, play the rubber ball game against the death lords in a stone court with sloping walls
the dark lowest levels of Xibalba hold the houses of trial, one full of moving stone knife blades, one of prowling jaguars, one of swooping bats with blade-shaped snouts, one of hail and ice, one of flames
