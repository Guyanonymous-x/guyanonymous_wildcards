Styled in a design language of sturdy, warm American chocolate-brown design. Flat colour accents in red, gold, orange, purple or blue distinguish variants against the dark brown base. Surfaces include silver metallic foil, satin paper wrap, raised square grid embossing and rounded rectangle panels. A teardrop silhouette with a small flat base and rounded peak recurs as an emblem. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
