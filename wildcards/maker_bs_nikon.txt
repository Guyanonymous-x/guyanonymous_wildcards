Styled in a design language of rugged, professional and mechanical. Boxy forms with raised angular humps, in black enamel or silver chrome with finely pebbled leatherette and heavy knurled metal. Deep rubber-ribbed bands and engraved white. Yellow and black stripe accents, and dense, tough, serious finishes made to be used hard. Strong high-contrast pairing of saturated golden yellow and black throughout. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
