In the style of Casio, cheap, cheerful, geeky and tough. Lightweight resin in black, grey, silver and bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal and rectangular outlines and chunky rubberised edges. Grey-green segmented digits and tiny grid layouts of squares in grey, orange and blue. Gold-finished and brushed steel variants add retro flair. Candy pastel, neon pink, teal and sunny yellow colour-blocking appears on rounded jelly-like shells. Printing is dense, small and technical, with many tiny white capital labels and thin rules around edges. The logo is a bold, wide, geometric sans-serif in white or black on blue or red. Utilitarian, nostalgic and playful.
In the style of Seiko, clean, practical, quietly sophisticated and slightly retro. Stainless steel with sharp faceted edges, mixing polished and brushed surfaces under clear domed glass. Palette is deep navy blue, black, silver, champagne and sunburst green with fine radial brushing, plus bold red as a crisp accent. Applied baton markers with luminous infill and slim bright needles give refined detail. Japanese-inspired patterns such as overlapping scale waves in red and white, fine woven lines and subtle gradient fades recur. Lettering is a stylised, wide, bold black or silver sans-serif wordmark, with tiny silver capitals for supporting text. Restrained, precise and considered.
In the style of Rolex, solid, imposing, polished, status-heavy Swiss luxury. Robust round forms in polished and brushed stainless steel or warm yellow gold, with fluted edges, satin and mirror finishes side by side and a tiny five-pointed crown motif as the recurring emblem. Deep forest green is the signature colour, paired with gold, white, black, blue and cream. Lettering is crisp, upright serif capitals in gold or white, widely spaced. Packaging and graphics use rich green leather-grain, embossed gold crests and heavy lacquered boxes.
In the style of Omega, purposeful, elegant and exploratory with a vintage space-age touch. Round steel and gold forms with clean faceted edges, polished or brushed, under domed glass. Matte black, silver, deep ocean blue and sunburst surfaces with contrasting inset circles, baton markers and luminous infill. Black aluminium bands carry white scale numerals. Warm tan leather with white stitching, tapering steel and bi-colour gold and steel details add richness. The emblem is a small Greek omega arch, repeated as a refined mark. Lettering is crisp, slim, widely spaced white capitals on black or deep blue.
In the style of Patek Philippe, refined, understated, heirloom and timeless haute craft. Slim round or oval forms in polished rose gold, yellow gold or platinum with elegant fine edges, thin rims and hand-stitched alligator leather in brown or black. Cream, silvered or deep blue surfaces with finely guilloched texture, slender leaf-shaped hands, applied gold classical numerals or batons, and discreet small windows. Proportions are clean with every control discreet. Typography is fine serif capitals and delicate cursive.
In the style of Nomos Glashutte, spare, precise, calm and modern minimalism. Slim round stainless steel with thin straight edges, a very narrow rim and a flat domed glass over a pure white or silvery-white surface. Slender blued steel hands, thin black numerals in a clean geometric sans-serif, a tiny subsidiary circle and a thin ring track. Plain brown or cordovan leather. Transparent backs reveal a decorated mechanism with stripes and blued screws.
In the style of Junghans, pared-back, functional and disciplined modernist. Round or rectangular faces with extremely clean layouts, slim baton or simple stick hands, thin black line markers or short numerals in a geometric sans-serif, and gently domed glass. Matte steel or gold with minimal detail. Colours are white, black and grey with occasional warm orange or yellow accents. Small cylinders or squares of off-white enamel or wood with tiny perforation details. Quiet and rational.
In the style of Citizen Watch, reliable, modern, understated, engineered and quietly elegant Japanese precision. Round or slightly cushion shapes in brushed and polished stainless steel or titanium, with flat clear glass and slim rims. Clean legible faces in silver white, deep navy, black or champagne, with applied baton markers, sharp sword-shaped pointers with pale luminous fill and a fine printed track. Sporty variants add alternating smooth and serrated sections, bold luminous dots and rugged links. Dress versions use lacquered black, mother-of-pearl or paper-textured white with raised star or moon motifs. Brushed surfaces with polished edges. Tidy thin capital text, cool metallic shine, lit from above.
In the style of HMT Watches, modest, dependable, sentimental mid-century craftsmanship. Small round or softly cushioned forms with thin edges and plain reverse sides, finished in polished steel or gold plating. Surfaces are simple and uncluttered in silver white, cream or sunburst gold, with slim baton marks, thin tapering gilt or steel strokes and a fine ring of tiny ticks. Lettering is neat serif capitals, small and evenly spaced. Details are restrained, in thin black or brown leather and plain steel, with the occasional glimpse of fine gilded workings under clear glass. Quiet, middle-class, gift-like and warmly nostalgic.
