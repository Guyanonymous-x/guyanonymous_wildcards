In the style of the Belgian Tintin studio, the clear line school of comics with uniform thin black outlines, flat cheerful fills, no hatching, meticulously accurate backgrounds of ships, aeroplanes, ports and desert bazaars, characters with round simple faces and dot eyes, an upswept tuft of hair, and a limited palette of sea blue, sand yellow, warm red and green, stage-like panel compositions, tidy hand-lettered balloons and the glossy hardcover album format with a flat colour spine.
