In the style of Amul, Indian dairy packaging and the hand painted topical hoarding. Butter packs are small rectangular foil wrapped blocks in yellow with a red band, a bold red wordmark in rounded sans serif capitals with a thick outline, and a little girl mascot with a blue polka dot dress, a red bow in her blue-green hair, a button nose and a cheeky smile, drawn in clean cartoon ink lines. Billboards are a yellow or sky blue poster with a witty cartoon of the girl standing next to a bold hand-lettered headline in white or red on a stripe, a slab of toast with butter beneath, and a tiny red logo tag in the corner. Colours are flat poster paint, with strong saturated red, yellow, blue and white. Milk packets are glossy plastic pouches in blue and white. Typography is hand-painted, jaunty and slightly uneven. Mood is witty, homey, playful, everyday-Indian and nostalgic, painted in mural style on wall and street.
