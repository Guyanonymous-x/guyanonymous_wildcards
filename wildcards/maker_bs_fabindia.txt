Styled in a design language of handloom and hand-block-printed cotton design with a rural craftsman feel. Relaxed, straight, loose shapes in soft textured cotton and silk-cotton blends show visible slub in the weave. Colours are earthy natural dyes such as indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow, rust, ochre, olive, charcoal and undyed cream. Patterns are small repeating floral and geometric block prints, ikat, dotted tie-dye, stripes and checks, with slightly irregular hand-printed edges. Stitching is simple, with wooden fastenings. Unfussy, honest, sun-washed and warm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
