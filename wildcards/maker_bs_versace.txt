Styled in a design language of maximal, flamboyant, high-shine glamour inspired by Roman antiquity and pop. The signature emblem is a gold medusa head medallion, framed by a repeating Greek key meander border, with baroque scrolls, acanthus leaves, garlands and ornate filigree. Palette is rich gold, jet black, crisp white, deep burgundy, royal blue, hot pink, turquoise and sunshine yellow, often clashing in dense, dizzying prints. Materials are silk, satin, embossed leather, metallic mesh, gilded hardware and lacquer. Packaging is black and red boxes with gilded patterns. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
