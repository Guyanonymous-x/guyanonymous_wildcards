In the style of Spode, English transfer-printed pottery with dense blue-and-white printing of classical landscapes with ruins, bridges, towers and trees, framed by borders of flowers and shell-like motifs. Cream-white ground carries intricately engraved line shading in deep indigo blue, and the same approach appears in Chinese-style willow scenes, floral chintz and red or green variants. Charming, traditional, old-world and cottage-like. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Spode in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
