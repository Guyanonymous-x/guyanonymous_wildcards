In the style of Lamy, rigorous German modernist industrial design. Simple geometric solids, smooth cylinders, flat planes and strict uncluttered proportions with a single slim flat bar as an accent. Matte, fine-grained black, charcoal, white and satin brushed stainless steel, with bright saturated tones such as red, yellow, blue, lime and orange in plain solid blocks. Faceted surfaces, small round windows and subtle recessed details are the only ornament. The wordmark is an extra bold, wide, slanted black sans-serif in capitals, often cropped large against white. Packaging is plain white or black card with sparse type and generous empty space. Functional, honest, disciplined, school-like and modern. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Lamy in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
