Styled in a design language of warm, bold, casual, spicy and lively. Palette runs through chilli red, terracotta, burnt orange, sunny yellow, teal, mint and soft peach, set in flat blocks. Surfaces carry African-inspired geometric patterns, bold arcs, semicircles, sun discs, zigzags and hand-printed abstract portraits with a screen-print look. Kraft cardboard and matte paper in warm earth tones are cut into angular tabs with colourful striped trims and rough linocut edges. Vibrant, sociable, handmade and fiery. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
