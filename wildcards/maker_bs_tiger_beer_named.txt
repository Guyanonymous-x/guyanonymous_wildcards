Styled in a design language of cool, bold, energetic, nocturnal and urban design in navy, gold and hot orange. A deep navy field with a bold arched gold border, a leaping big cat in orange gold stripes on black rendered in dynamic ink lines. A small crown, malt ears and a ribbon give a heraldic effect. Glossy navy surfaces with gold highlights and metallic silver. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
