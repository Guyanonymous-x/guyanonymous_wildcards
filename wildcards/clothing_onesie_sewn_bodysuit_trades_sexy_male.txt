It is a complete costume of fishmonger in a sexy masculine costume-shop style in one piece, shown full length from collar to ankles, with a rubber apron over a waterproof smock, waders on the legs, a hat (as the hood), rubber boots (as the feet), a fish knife sewn or attached to the suit. The upper half carries the top and the leg tubes carry the lower garment, so the whole outfit reads at once. Novelty embellishments, puffy padded shoulder pieces, terry-loop textured stripes and glitter-flecked tulle layered at the hem. Everything is chosen to be fun to look at and tactile to feel, bumpy, fluffy, shiny and soft in turn.
