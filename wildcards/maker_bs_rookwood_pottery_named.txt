In the style of Rookwood Pottery, high-gloss glazed surfaces with softly blurred, naturalistic painted decoration. The signature look has dark backgrounds blending from brown to amber, orange and olive green, with flowers, leaves, fruit and animals painted as if seen through mist. Later looks use matte glazes in soft green, blue and pink, or Art Deco geometry with stylised flowers. Forms are rounded with simple lines, and glazes pool and drip. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
