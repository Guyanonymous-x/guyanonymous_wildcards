Styled in a design language of formal, traditional pride in navy, red, white and silver. A bold swooping ribbon shape in red and blue, tapering to a pointed tip, billows like a flag in motion and works as a swift, speed-implying stripe. White fields meet deep navy and rich red in strong, balanced colour blocks, with thin silver or pale grey borders. Surfaces are clean gloss and fine woven cloth with tonal sheen. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
