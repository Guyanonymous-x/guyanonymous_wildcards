Styled in a design language of suburban childhood adventure in the 1980s with kids on bicycles silhouetted against a huge glowing moon, warm backlit mist and lens flares, golden-hour streetlight beams, flashlights in dark woods and wonder-struck faces lit from below. Soft airbrushed glow in deep blue and orange, wide-eyed friendly creatures and a warm nostalgic magic-hour glow. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
