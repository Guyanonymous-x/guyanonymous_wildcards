In the style of Nambu ironworks, Japanese cast-iron tetsubin kettles and teapots, rounded bulging bodies with arare pattern, rows of small raised bumps like hailstones, topped with a small knob lid, a swing handle of iron or bronze, and a short curved spout. Matte black, deep brown or rust-coloured finish, sometimes with inlaid silver or relief motifs of pine, cranes, plum blossoms and waves. Heavy, weighty, tea ceremony quietness, simple, wabi-sabi and austere.
