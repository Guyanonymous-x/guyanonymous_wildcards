In the style of Laika, stop-motion craft with intricately carved faces, delicate printed expressions, stylised angular or spindly bodies and textured knitted and sewn costumes. Atmospheric miniature sets with moody lighting, moss, mist and dark woodland, a slightly eerie and gothic palette of teal, dusty purple, ochre and charcoal with punches of bright colour. Tactile handmade detail down to individual hair, wood grain and fabric weave, with a dreamy fairy-tale spookiness. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
