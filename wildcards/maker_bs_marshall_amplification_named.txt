In the style of Marshall Amplification, loud, bold, rock and roll and imposing. Matte black pebbled vinyl covers every surface, set against woven basket-weave cloth in cream, tan or black. Trim is gold or silver piping and rows of brass-toned metal studs and corner caps. A flowing white or gold cursive signature script sits on a black or gold plate, with tiny stamped capitals in small engraved type. Palette is black, cream, warm gold and chrome with occasional oxblood leather and brown. Shapes are blocky, rectangular and heavy with softly rounded edges and a stacked, monumental feel. Surfaces suggest tough road-worn craft, with bright metal glints against deep black. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
