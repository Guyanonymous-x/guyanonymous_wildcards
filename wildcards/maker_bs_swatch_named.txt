Styled in a design language of playful, pop, graphic design in lightweight moulded plastic with a glossy, jelly-like sheen. Forms are smooth, rounded, thin and flat, and often translucent or skeleton-like to reveal colourful inner parts. Palette is loud and saturated, with primary red, yellow, blue, green, hot pink and orange against black and white. Surfaces carry bold stripes, dots, zigzags, colour-block geometry, comic-like motifs and art-collaboration prints. Packaging is cheerful, with boxes covered in busy patchwork graphics, tilted lids and clear windows, colourful, collectible and fun. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
