Styled in a design language of bright white ground painted under a brilliant clear glaze with vivid fresh colour. The palette is cobalt blue, turquoise, emerald green and a thick, slightly raised tomato red, all drawn crisply with fine black outlines. Patterns are stylised garden flowers such as tulips, carnations and hyacinths, with long curling feathery leaves and cloud bands, arranged symmetrically in flowing arabesques that fill the surface and run along borders and arches. Surfaces are glossy, crisp and fresh, with rhythmic, balanced repetition that feels like a flowering garden. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
