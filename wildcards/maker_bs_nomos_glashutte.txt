Styled in a design language of spare, precise, calm and modern minimalism. Slim round stainless steel with thin straight edges, a very narrow rim and a flat domed glass over a pure white or silvery-white surface. Slender blued steel hands, thin black numerals in a clean geometric sans-serif, a tiny subsidiary circle and a thin ring track. Plain brown or cordovan leather. Transparent backs reveal a decorated mechanism with stripes and blued screws. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
