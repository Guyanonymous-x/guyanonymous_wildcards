Rajasthani kathputli string puppets from India, carved wooden figures each about as tall as an adult's forearm. Each puppet has a single carved block of mango wood for the head and body, painted with a bright face, big almond-shaped eyes outlined in thick black, arched brows, a long nose and a curled black mustache on the men. The women wear a long veil over the head, and the men wear tall turbans of red and yellow cloth. __puppet_kathputli_var__.
