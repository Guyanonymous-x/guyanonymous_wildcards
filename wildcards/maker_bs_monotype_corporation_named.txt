In the style of Monotype Corporation, carefully revived classical roman letterforms and crisp new classics. Lettering is a fine serif with high contrast, bracketed serifs, balanced proportions and even colour, with a few geometric sans-serif and slab designs. Compositions use centred headings, small capitals, letter-spaced capitals and discreet fleurons. The look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, like a well-printed British book or newspaper. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Monotype Corporation in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
