Styled in a design language of ornate, quirky Victorian gentleman-perfumer elegance. Cut-crystal or clear glass with faceted details, tied with ribbon and silken tassels, in heavy cream or deep green surfaces with gold embossed crests. Wax-sealed accents, tissue wrapping and rounded hat-like shapes. Panels are cream with gold borders and fine engraved Victorian serif, topped with a small animal emblem. Spaces use dark green panels, polished brass and glass-fronted wood. Palette is deep green, ivory, gold and burgundy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
