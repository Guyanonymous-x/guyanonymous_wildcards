In the style of Penfolds, an Australian wine label with a classic traditional look. The bottle is a dark green Bordeaux style with sloping shoulders, topped by a tall foil capsule in deep red or black and a long tight cork. The label is a cream or off-white rectangle, with an elaborate oval or shield-shaped heading in thin black lines, an engraved-looking vignette, a signature-like stylised script, and neat serif capital lettering in black and dark red, with a vintage year and a bin number in bold numerals. Premium bottles have a heavy dark brown or deep burgundy label with a gold foil rule border, and the name is set in embossed gold capitals. Black wax or red wax seals appear at the top. The cellar scenes feature rows of oak barrels, dark brick vaults, copper pipes and bottle racks covered in dust, in a restrained palette of cream, burgundy, black, gold and green. The mood is established, serious and heritage-rich.
