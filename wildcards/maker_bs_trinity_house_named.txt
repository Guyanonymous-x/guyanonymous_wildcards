Styled in a design language of English lighthouse and lightship design with tall tapering white-painted stone towers, black or red painted lantern galleries with brass and glass lantern rooms, red-painted lightships with a lantern on a central mast, navy blue and gold detailing, polished brass instruments, scrubbed timber floors and a sober, steadfast, seafaring character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
