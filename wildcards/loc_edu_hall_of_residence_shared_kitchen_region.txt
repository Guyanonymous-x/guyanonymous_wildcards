British halls of residence with self-catered flats of six to twelve, which is now the dominant model and is what produces this room
catered halls with a dining hall, where the kitchen is a small pantry and the social life happens elsewhere, which is the older Oxbridge and civic university pattern
American dormitories with a communal floor kitchen used far less because board plans are near-universal, so the room exists but is not the social center
continental European student residences, often with a kitchen per corridor and a stronger cooking culture
purpose-built private student studios, which abolish the room entirely
