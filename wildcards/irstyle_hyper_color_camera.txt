Shot through a hyper color 470nm infrared filter and shown straight from the camera, the image shows a pale mint and aqua sky over lavender-violet foliage, with deep plum trees and teal water. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
