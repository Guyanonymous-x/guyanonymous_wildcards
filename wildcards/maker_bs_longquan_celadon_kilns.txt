In the style of Longquan Celadon Kilns, thick-glazed stoneware in a soft, jade-like green, from pale plum and sea green to deep leaf green, with a lustrous, unctuous, slightly translucent finish that pools thicker in recesses. Forms are restrained and sculptural, such as lotus-petal bowls, plum vases with swelling shoulders, tripod incense burners and fish-handled vases. Decoration is minimal, with carved or moulded lotus petals, incised combed lines and sometimes a fine crackle network. Rims fade lighter where the glaze thins, and the unglazed foot ring burns to russet brown. The mood is quiet, scholarly and understated, like polished river stone.
