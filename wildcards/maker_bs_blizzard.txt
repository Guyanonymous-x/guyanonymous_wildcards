Styled in a design language of exaggerated, heroic, comic-epic fantasy and science fiction with chunky, stylised proportions. Oversized armour, huge shoulder shapes and enormous weapons, painterly saturated textures with hand-painted highlights, and glowing magical effects in cyan, orange and violet. Frames and borders are rich stone and metal trimmed in gold, with bevelled edges. Poses are dramatic and low-angle, towering over the viewer. Bold, polished, vibrant and larger than life, with strong silhouettes that read instantly and a confident sense of fun. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
