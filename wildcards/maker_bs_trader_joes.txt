Styled in a design language of friendly, quirky, nautical-tiki neighbourhood charm. Small tote-style forms in off-white canvas have contrasting navy, forest green, mint, yellow or sky-blue edge piping. Flat hand-drawn illustrations of vegetables, fruit, fish, sailing boats, flowers and cows in primary red, green, yellow and blue fill patchwork-like panels. Cheerful, thrifty, wacky and welcoming. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
