In the style of Khokhloma, glowing gold-like swirling patterns painted on black or deep red lacquer. Curling vines, rowan berries, strawberries, leaves and flowers in red, black, gold and green flow in rhythmic curves across glossy varnished wood. A shiny golden-yellow undertone shows through the hardened varnish. Rich, warm, curvilinear, festive and fairy-tale peasant treasure. Ornament is hand-brushed in confident repeating curls, evenly spread over the whole surface with little empty ground. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
