In the style of Alpine in the 1960s and 1970s, a tiny, rounded, low French sports coupe with a fibreglass body, a swooping curved roofline, a short nose with round headlamps set in the front, and a tapered rear with a short tail. Smooth rounded wings, a small oval front air intake, a very low windscreen, and a rear window sloping into the tail. Wheels are small, wide steel or alloy with chrome nuts. Paint in bright blue, orange, white, and yellow, often with stripes along the bonnet. Interior has bucket seats in cloth and vinyl, a simple dash with round dials, and a small leather wheel. Mood is nimble, light and rallying.
