Styled in a design language of Italian design with a humorous, anthropomorphic spirit. Materials are mirror-polished stainless steel, glossy resin and plastic in candy colours, porcelain and chrome. Palette is cheerful and saturated, with lemon yellow, cherry red, turquoise, lime green, hot pink, orange, black and white. Surfaces carry bold dots, stripes and playful abstract patterns, and finishes are bright and slick. The overall mood is Memphis-influenced, sculptural, ironic and fun. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
