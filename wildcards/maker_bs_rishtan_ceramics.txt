In the style of Rishtan Ceramics, Uzbek hand thrown glazed pottery. The clay is thrown into wide shallow plates, round bowls, tall jugs, pitchers and teapots with simple, generous rounded forms. The surface is coated with a milky white slip and a clear alkaline ash glaze, giving a soft gentle sheen and a faint pale turquoise glow, then painted freehand in turquoise, dark cobalt blue and chocolate brown. Patterns include flowers, branches, pomegranates and pitchers, with swirling leafy borders and a central rosette, along with tiny dotted lines and fine hatching. Some have a calm single motif of a bird or a sprig in the middle. The painted lines are slightly uneven, which gives life. Dinnerware is arranged on a round tablecloth, with tea, nuts and flatbread. Workshop shelves show stacks of plates. Mood is warm, rustic, sunlit, homely and cool, in turquoise and blue.
