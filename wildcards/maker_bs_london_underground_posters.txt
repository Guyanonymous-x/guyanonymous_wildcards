Styled in a design language of early twentieth century transport graphics built from flat, hand-cut shapes. Outlines are crisp and forms are reduced to geometric blocks with gentle lithographic grain and slightly chalky texture. A bold red ring crossed by a horizontal blue bar acts as the recurring emblem. Compositions are balanced, decorative and optimistic, with cubist and art deco rhythm in repeated shapes. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
