Styled in a design language of refined, ornate, glittering early twentieth century export porcelain. Glossy bone-white glaze with a faint bluish translucency, formal shapes with scalloped edges, closed loops, small curls and bud or fruit finials. Hand-painted and gilded decoration with wide borders of heavy gold, cobalt blue and deep rose red, central scenic panels in sunset orange and teal, and raised enamel dots and beads standing proud like pearls. Later Art Deco versions turn angular, with stepped triangles, black and orange bands, fans, sunbursts and pearly rainbow lustre. Slightly nostalgic, treasured and cabinet-worthy. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
