Styled in a design language of carefully revived classical roman letterforms and crisp new classics. The look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, like a well-printed British book or newspaper. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
