half past five, the first housemaid up, the boxes taken, the candle lit
six to eight, the morning round, eleven bedrooms, the slops emptied into a pail, the chamber pot carried out, the grate cleaned with the hessian cloth down and the cinders sifted, a new fire laid, the hearth washed, the carpet swept with damp tea leaves, the washstand filled from a can and the dressing table dusted
eight, hot-water cans filled and carried to eleven doors and set outside them with a towel over the top to keep them warm
nine, the closet's hour, slop pails emptied, chamber pots washed with vinegar and whiting, cans scoured, cloths rinsed and hung
the afternoon, the heavy cleaning on a rota, one room a day stripped and done properly
six, the cans refilled and carried up again for before dinner
ten, the beds turned down, the fires made up, the cans taken a third time
midnight, the closet dark, four cans upside down on a draining board
