In the style of Marimekko, fresh, cheerful Nordic modernism with bold large-scale graphic prints on cotton, giant poppy-like flowers in red, pink and orange on white, thick vertical and horizontal stripes in blue and white, round dot patterns, simple organic leaf shapes and flat colours with a hand-printed, hand-cut paper look and black outlined shapes. Loose boxy shapes, flat unshaded areas of colour, generous empty white space between motifs, and a bright, optimistic, unfussy mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
