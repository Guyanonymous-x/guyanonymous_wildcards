In the style of Yakult, tiny single serve probiotic drink bottles. The bottle is a squat pale plastic vessel about the size of a thumb joint, with a rounded shoulder, a ribbed and faceted waist like a small cut-glass tumbler, and a wide mouth sealed by a thin foil lid in white with red print. A glossy white wrap label carries the wordmark in bold red letters with a soft pink and blue accent band, a small drop or bubble motif and tiny fine print in black. The drink inside is pale milky peach. Bottles are sold in tight multipacks of five or ten bound in a printed paper sleeve, or arranged in rows on a shop fridge shelf, so the red and white pattern repeats. Delivery carts and carry boxes are white and pale pink with the same red lettering. Everything is clean, bright, hygienic and friendly, with rounded forms, plenty of white, one hot red accent and a mild medical-nutrition feeling.
