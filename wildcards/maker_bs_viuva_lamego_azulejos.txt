Styled in a design language of cool, bright, narrative and decorative with a serene seaside light. Square glazed ceramic with a glossy surface and slightly uneven edges, assembled into complete wall-scale panels, mostly cobalt blue on a creamy white ground, depicting baroque scenes with scrolling acanthus, cherubs, figures, gardens, ships, harbours and festivals, framed by swags and volutes. Other patterns show repeating geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau versions have curving plants, lilies, irises and stylised maidens with delicate gold-green outlines.
