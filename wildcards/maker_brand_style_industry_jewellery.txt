In the style of Mikimoto, classic pearl jewellery centred on round, lustrous cultured pearls with a creamy white to pale pink glow and mirror-like surface, strung into long uniform strands, graduated necklaces, opera-length ropes and multi-row chokers with a small clasp. Settings are restrained and elegant, using polished white and yellow gold and platinum, with small round diamonds as pave or tiny accent rims, pearl drop earrings, pearl studs, floral and wave-like brooches, and a modest cluster of pearls on rings. Designs feel graceful, symmetrical and timeless, sometimes with soft rounded shapes inspired by sea shells, waves and flowers, presented on dark navy velvet or in pale blue boxes. Calm, refined and luminous mood, soft diffused lighting.
In the style of Tanishq, Indian bridal and festive jewellery in warm 22 carat yellow gold, with heavy temple necklaces, chokers, layered chains, jhumka bell earrings, bangles in stacks, maang tikka headpieces and nose rings. Motifs include lotus flowers, peacocks, paisley mango shapes, leaves, elephants, filigree and floral temple architecture. Surfaces combine polished gold with fine granulation, rows of uncut diamonds, rubies, emeralds, pearls and coloured enamel, with delicate hanging pearl drops. Designs are rich, symmetrical and festive, displayed on red velvet and lit by warm golden light. Lighter diamond collections use minimalist solitaires and fine rose gold lines for everyday wear.
In the style of H.Stern, Brazilian fine jewellery that showcases large coloured gemstones such as emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, amethyst and citrine in smooth cabochon and faceted cuts, set in polished yellow and rose gold. Shapes are organic and sculptural, with flowing curves, wave and leaf forms, fluid cuffs, big cocktail rings, bold pendants and chunky chain necklaces. Designs often combine textured gold with a single huge gem, bright candy-coloured stones stacked in bands, and smooth rounded forms that feel modern and tropical. Colours are saturated greens, blues, purples and warm golden yellow. Confident, glamorous and sunny mood, shown against dark velvet with bright spotlights.
In the style of Paspaley, Australian South Sea pearl jewellery featuring large, round, luminous white and golden pearls with a deep satiny lustre, strung as thick single strands, graduated necklaces, long ropes, and set as earrings, rings and pendants. Metalwork is simple and smooth in white gold, yellow gold and platinum, sometimes with small clusters of diamonds. Shapes include baroque drops, wave-like clasps, shell and sea-inspired forms. Pearl colours range from cool silvery white to champagne and deep gold. Calm, ocean-luxury mood, shown on dark stone or white sand with soft aquamarine light.
In the style of Dinosaur Designs, Sydney hand-poured resin jewellery and homewares in organic, sculptural, brightly coloured shapes. Bangles, chunky rings, drop earrings and necklaces are formed as blobs, pebbles, waves and smooth curvy tubes in swirling marbled resin, with glossy, translucent surfaces mixing candy colours such as watermelon pink, tangerine, turquoise, lime, lilac, cobalt, ivory and deep black. Vases, bowls, platters and candlesticks follow the same fluid, tactile forms with uneven hand-poured edges. Playful, artistic, joyful mood, shown on pastel backgrounds with colourful reflections.
In the style of Kazuri, Kenyan hand made ceramic beads and pottery. Beads are rolled and shaped by hand from local clay, then glazed in glossy colours and fired, so each one is slightly unique and irregular. Shapes are round balls, discs, short tubes, cones, ovals, chunky cylinders and tiny seeds, with smooth glass-like surfaces and a visible hole. Colours include cobalt blue, cherry red, mustard yellow, forest green, turquoise, terracotta orange, black, white and speckled earthy brown. Some are hand-painted with swirls, dots, stripes and zigzags in contrasting glaze. They are strung into chunky necklaces, multi-strand bracelets, long beaded ropes and earrings on cotton thread or leather cord, often mixing many colours. Pottery includes mugs and bowls with matching speckled glaze. Mood is joyful, earthy, handmade, colourful and sunny.
