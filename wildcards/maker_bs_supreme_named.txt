Styled in a design language of palette of red, white and black with occasional neon or pastel variations and checkerboard or camouflage fills. Crisp screen-print ink, slightly raised and cracked vinyl texture, woven jacquard and chunky embroidery. Smooth heavy cotton, fleece, nylon and glossy enamel finishes keep everything blunt, cool, smug and deliberately ironic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
