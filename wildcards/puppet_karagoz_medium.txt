Turkish Karagoz shadow puppets, flat translucent figures of dyed leather, each about as tall as an adult's hand and forearm. The two main figures are Karagoz, with a round black beard, big round eyes and a huge round black hat wound in thick folds, and Hacivat, with a pointed beard and a tall hat, both shown in profile. They are cut from camel hide scraped thin and dyed red, green, blue and yellow, so the light glows through them. __puppet_karagoz_var__.
