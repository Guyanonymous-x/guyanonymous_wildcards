Styled in a design language of hard-wearing American utility design. Heavy, tightly woven cotton duck with a dry, slightly rough hand, finished in tobacco brown, faded tan, charcoal black, moss green and washed navy. Surfaces look broken in, with soft creasing, pale fading at the folds and honest wear. Construction is visible and reinforced, with bold triple stitching, metal rivets, boxy generous proportions and sturdy flat seams. Everything feels durable, unfussy, practical and built to be used for decades. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
