Styled in a design language of compact, clever British engineering with a playful modern twist. Slender round steel-like tubing, tidy brazed joints and hinged forms packed into neat compact blocks. Glossy solid colours in lime, orange, turquoise, sky blue, racing red, black and white, often with a contrast accent on a few details and silver or black metal fittings. Quirky, practical, urban, precise and optimistic, with tidy, minimal outlines and bold single colours. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
