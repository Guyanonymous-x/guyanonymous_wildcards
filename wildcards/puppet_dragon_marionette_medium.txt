a dragon marionette with a long jointed body, about as long as an adult is tall. Its body is a chain of a dozen carved wooden sections linked by leather hinges, so it curves and ripples as it moves, each section painted with overlapping green and gold scales. Its head is large and carved with a long snout, flaring nostrils, bulging red eyes and a hinged jaw full of white pointed teeth. __puppet_dragon_marionette_var__.
