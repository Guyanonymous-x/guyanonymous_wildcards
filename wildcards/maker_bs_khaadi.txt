Styled in a design language of earthy, artisanal, modern-heritage handloom textiles. Loose, easy silhouettes in crisp matte cotton with a visible hand woven grain. Palette is mustard, indigo, rust, olive, ivory and deep red. Patterns are bold block-print-inspired motifs, large paisleys, florals, small repeating geometry and interlocking designs in two or three colours, set along edges and borders. Neat threadwork trims every edge, with small mirrors, tassels and long fringed ends. Warm, cheerful and sunlit, as in an open courtyard. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
