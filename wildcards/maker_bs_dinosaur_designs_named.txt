Styled in a design language of hand-poured resin in organic, sculptural, brightly coloured shapes. Forms are blobs, pebbles, waves and smooth curvy tubes in swirling marbled resin, with glossy, translucent surfaces mixing candy colours such as watermelon pink, tangerine, turquoise, lime, lilac, cobalt, ivory and deep black. Everything follows the same fluid, tactile forms with uneven hand-poured edges. Playful, artistic, joyful mood, on pastel backgrounds with colourful reflections. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
