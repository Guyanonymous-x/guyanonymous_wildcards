In the style of White Rabbit Creamy Candy, sweet, nostalgic, modest, childlike old Shanghai charm. Plump cream-coloured rectangles wrapped in a thin edible rice paper film, then in waxed paper twisted and printed in white with red, blue and black hand lettering in both Chinese characters and Latin script. A naturalistic ink drawing of a white rabbit stands before an old-fashioned artist palette, drawn with fine line shading in the manner of 1950s commercial art. Bright red and white with a large rabbit face, wide forward-looking eyes, a cartoonish cheerful animal on a pale blue sky ground. Vintage Art Deco borders. Limited white, red, blue and black palette with a slightly matte print texture.
