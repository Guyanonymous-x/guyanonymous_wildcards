Styled in a design language of graceful, smooth, refined Italian grand touring elegance. Long flowing outlines, tight sloping curves, wide sculpted shoulders and boomerang-like accents with restrained, polished surfaces. A three-pronged trident emblem in chrome, silver or black is the recurring mark, often set on a deep red or royal blue field. Palette is deep blue, silver, dark burgundy, pearl white and black. Finishes are mirror chrome, pearl lacquer, polished wood, carbon weave and tan, red or cream leather with fine contrast stitching. Details are few, precise and quietly expensive. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
