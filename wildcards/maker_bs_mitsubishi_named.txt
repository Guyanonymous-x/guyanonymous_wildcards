In the style of Mitsubishi, tough, bold, motorsport-ready Japanese design built on a symmetrical emblem of three red diamonds meeting at their points to form a sharp triangular mark. It is always flat, solid and graphic in scarlet red on white or reversed in white or chrome on black. Shapes are angular, faceted and rugged, with diamond and rhombus cut-outs, aggressive creases and slanted edges. Palette is hot red, white, black, silver and gunmetal grey, with occasional rally-style blue and yellow stripes. Finishes are matte black, brushed steel and glossy enamel. Lettering is wide, heavy, evenly spaced capitals in a bold sans-serif. Technical sport decals, chevrons and diamond repeats make a strong, corporate, geometric pattern. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Mitsubishi in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
