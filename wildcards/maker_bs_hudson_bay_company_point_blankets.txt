In the style of Hudson Bay Company Point Blankets, thick, heavy wool blankets in a creamy off-white field with four horizontal stripes near each edge, in green, red, yellow and indigo blue. The stripes are bold, flat and evenly spaced, with the green and red dominating, and small black tick marks woven into one edge to show size. The cloth has a dense napped, felted surface that feels rustic, warm and hearty, with finished edges and no further decoration. Blankets are sold folded with a woven tag. Variants offer a sturdy single stripe, but the classic four-stripe look suggests trading posts, cabins and snow.
