In the style of Tiffany Studios, American Art Nouveau stained glass built from hundreds of small leaded opalescent pieces in rich greens, ambers, deep blues, orange and red, shaped into dragonflies, wisteria, peonies, poppies, dogwood and geometric borders. Bronze forms cast as tree trunks, lily pads or twisting stems with dark green-brown patina, iridescent peacock blue and gold glass, glowing jewel-like light and lush naturalistic mood.
