Styled in a design language of warm, rustic, sunlit, homely hand thrown pottery in turquoise and blue. Simple, generous rounded forms with wide shallow curves. The surface has a milky white slip under a clear glaze giving a soft sheen and a faint pale turquoise glow, then painted freehand in turquoise, dark cobalt blue and chocolate brown. Patterns include flowers, branches, pomegranates and pitchers with swirling leafy borders and a central rosette, tiny dotted lines and fine hatching. Some have one calm motif such as a bird or sprig in the middle. Slightly uneven painted lines give life.
