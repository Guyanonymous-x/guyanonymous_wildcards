a teaching space with no building
no services and no permanent structures, which is the deliberate inverse of every other record here and whose entire infrastructure is rigged each session and inspected each time, so the fixtures are knots
and the plan is the clearing's own shape; a fire circle, which is the camp's center in exactly the way a carpet is the center of a reception classroom
with log seats at a spacing that lets an adult pass behind them and a hearth that is scraped to mineral soil and ringed; a tarpaulin rigged on a ridge line between trees with a fall to shed water
which is the safety system and is social rather than physical; a hand-washing station because food is eaten here; tools kept rolled and counted in and out, which is the same discipline as the shadow board in the workshop arrived at independently; a first aid rucksack hung high; a route in from a gate or a parking lot, which is the only built element involved
