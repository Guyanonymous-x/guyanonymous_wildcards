the inside of the Tomb of the Skull and Bones society, as rumor, old break-in accounts and members' hints picture it. A high dark room of the Tomb has walls painted in deep maroon and lavender with bands of yellow, crowded with skulls, bones and old pictures of death, and a long wooden table with heavy carved chairs runs down its middle. At the far end of the room a door is painted with the number "322" in large gold numerals, the society's number, said to lead to its inner sanctum, and above it hangs a carved skull over crossed bones. In a tall glass case against one wall stands a full human skeleton, yellowed and wired at the joints, and on the shelves around it rows of human skulls sit in a line, one of them in its own small glass case, the one members are said to call "Geronimo".
