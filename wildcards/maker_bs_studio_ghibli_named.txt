In the style of Studio Ghibli, hand-painted animation feel with lush watercolour and gouache backgrounds, towering cumulus clouds in deep blue skies, rolling green meadows, wind in the grass, foggy forests, old stone villages and steam-age machinery with soft rounded edges. Characters have simple faces with small dot-like noses, big expressive eyes and natural proportions, gently flowing hair and clothes in earthy cream, olive, sky blue and warm red. Soft cel shading, warm glowing light, hand-drawn detail, a quiet pastoral and fantastical atmosphere.
