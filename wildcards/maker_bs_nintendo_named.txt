In the style of Nintendo, playful, approachable, toy-like yet neatly built. Compact rounded rectangles with chunky friendly proportions in glossy and matte plastic. Palette of white, light grey, red and black with bright primary or pastel accents in red, blue, yellow and green, arranged in tidy symmetrical layouts. Vintage looks use cream-grey with dark red trim and gold lettering, later looks use clean white or black slabs with colourful contrasting parts. Cross shapes and small round coloured discs recur as motifs. Rounded sans-serif lettering, often in red. Cheerful and tidy.
