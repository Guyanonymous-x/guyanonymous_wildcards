in the village below stand low houses of mud and wattle with steep roofs of grass thatch and swept yards, and in each house a clay ancestor pot sits in a dim corner, the spirits of the dead drawn as pale shapes near it
at the village edge stands the shrine of the village goddess, a few smooth stones under a small thatched shelter, smeared with turmeric and vermilion and hung with garlands of marigolds
the village spreads along a stream, its houses of red mud with thatched roofs, cattle in the lanes, and above the roofs the ancestors drawn as faint figures of light patrolling the sky
