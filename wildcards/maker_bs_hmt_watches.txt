Styled in a design language of modest, dependable, sentimental mid-century craftsmanship. Small round or softly cushioned forms with thin edges and plain reverse sides, finished in polished steel or gold plating. Surfaces are simple and uncluttered in silver white, cream or sunburst gold, with slim baton marks, thin tapering gilt or steel strokes and a fine ring of tiny ticks. Lettering is neat serif capitals, small and evenly spaced. Details are restrained, in thin black or brown leather and plain steel, with the occasional glimpse of fine gilded workings under clear glass. Quiet, middle-class, gift-like and warmly nostalgic. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
