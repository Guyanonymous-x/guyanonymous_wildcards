Styled in a design language of gentle, practical, trustworthy Japanese domestic design. Plump rounded forms with soft corners in cream white, pearl grey or brushed stainless steel, with a single dark glass or black panel carrying small pale green backlit marks and simple rounded controls. Slim cylinders in satin steel or soft pastel enamel and sturdy folding handles. Surfaces are matte or satin, never flashy, with fine seams and tidy joints. Tiny line pictograms. Clean, quietly cheerful and reassuringly well made. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
