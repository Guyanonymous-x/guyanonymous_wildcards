Styled in a design language of bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
