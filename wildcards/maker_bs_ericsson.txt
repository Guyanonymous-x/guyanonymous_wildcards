Styled in a design language of sleek, grey and corporate. Slim grey or black forms with a hinged cover, a small greyish-green display and neat grids of rounded rectangular keys. Cool silver-blue finish, rounded corners and a clean oval mark. Earlier 1950s pieces had a one-piece curved bakelite body in ivory, black or red. Cool, tidy and businesslike. Surfaces stay smooth and uncluttered, with restrained colour, even spacing and a calm, professional mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
