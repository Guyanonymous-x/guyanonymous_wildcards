at summer solstice sunrise, the orange disk of the sun lifting over the horizon to the northeast in line with the leaning Heel Stone, its first rays shooting through the gaps of the stone circle, the eastern faces of the pillars glowing gold, long shadows streaming over dewy grass and a pale clear sky above
at winter solstice sunset, the low red sun sinking in the southwest framed between two great upright stones, the sky banded crimson, orange and deep cold blue, the pillars black against the glow, frost whitening the grass and the thin breath of the watching adults hanging in the air
on a gray day, a low unbroken ceiling of pale cloud over the downs, soft flat light with faint shadows, the stones a darker damp gray streaked with rain, the grass a deep wet green and the far ridges blurred by drizzle
