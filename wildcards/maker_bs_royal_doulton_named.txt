In the style of Royal Doulton, English ceramics ranging from stoneware with Art Nouveau tube-lined flowers in earthy brown, blue and green, to mottled red-and-black flambe glaze with running streaks, and hand-painted figures in crinoline gowns with grinning caricature faces. Glossy, richly coloured and finely detailed with pastel flesh tones, floral garlands, gilded edges and sculpted ruffles. Sentimental Victorian and Edwardian parlour feel.
