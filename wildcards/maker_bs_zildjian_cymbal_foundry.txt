Styled in a design language of hand-hammered bronze with a bright warm gold sheen, circular and gently dished with a raised central swell. The surface shows concentric lathe grooves and dense dimpled hammer marks, with darker burnished tones in the recesses and polished highlights on the ridges. Older looks are dark, brown and dull gold, newer looks are brilliantly polished and mirror bright. Edges are thin and slightly wavy. Any marking is small, ornate and printed in black or gold ink. Warm, metallic, tactile and handmade. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
