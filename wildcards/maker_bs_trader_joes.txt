Styled in a design language of friendly, quirky, nautical-tiki neighbourhood charm. Small tote-style forms in off-white canvas have contrasting navy, forest green, mint, yellow or sky-blue edge piping. Flat hand-drawn illustrations of vegetables, fruit, fish, sailing boats, flowers and cows in primary red, green, yellow and blue fill patchwork-like panels. Cheerful, thrifty, wacky and welcoming. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
