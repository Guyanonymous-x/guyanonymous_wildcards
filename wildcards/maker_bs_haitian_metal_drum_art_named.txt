Styled in a design language of raw, intricate, spiritual and richly patterned flat sculpture cut from recycled steel. Panels are flattened, drawn with chalk, then chiselled and punched so the line work appears as cut-through openings and raised ridges. Subjects are a tree of life, mermaids, birds, fish, sun faces, spirits with elaborate headdresses and crosses, drawn with symmetrical swirling leaves, scales, dotted outlines, hearts and curling vines. The metal is left dark grey and rust-brown, sometimes polished to rough silver with burn marks, or hand-painted in bright enamel like red, yellow and turquoise. Shapes are oval, round or irregular with ragged cut edges. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
