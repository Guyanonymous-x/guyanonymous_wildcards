In the style of Nongshim, Korean instant noodle packaging. Shin Ramyun style bags are deep red and black, using a big bowl photograph of glossy noodles in a fiery red broth with floating slices of green onion, egg yolk and mushroom, set against a dark matte background. The title is in heavy white and yellow Korean characters with a strong brush-like edge and a small hanja-style character in a red square seal. A bright flame graphic and a hot chilli pepper accent point to spiciness. Cup versions have a black and red wrap with the same dish picture and a peel foil lid. The corporate mark is a small seed shaped logo with an arc in red and green beside the wordmark. Snack bags in orange and yellow show shrimp crackers or potato chips with cartoon accents. Mood is bold, hot, hearty and no-nonsense, with saturated red, black and golden yellow, steaming bowls and a late-night convenience store feel.
