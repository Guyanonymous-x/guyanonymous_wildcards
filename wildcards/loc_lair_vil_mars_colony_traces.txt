red dust drifted against every wall
cracked dome glass patched with sealant
rover tracks across the plaza
scorch marks round the landing pads
frost on the cold side of the domes
scuffs on the marble
rust-red stains on white panels
dents from falling rocks
loose cables in the tunnels
spilled water steaming on the floor
sand drifts in the airlocks
burn marks from a rebellion
