In the style of Lisa Folawiyo, bold wax print cotton combined with heavy hand-embellishment, using beads, sequins, crystals, pearls, metallic thread and shell-like details sewn in dense clusters across edges and focal areas. Silhouettes are modern and sculptural, with cropped shapes, flared waists, structured shoulders, ruffled trumpet flares, fitted lines and playful cut-outs. Prints are bold and graphic in electric blue, tangerine, fuchsia, yellow, emerald and black and white, with embroidery that highlights the pattern outlines. Glamorous, festive, Afro-modern mood, glossy with sparkle and contrasting textures. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Lisa Folawiyo in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
