the sheikhs and pirs, Yazidi men in their forties to eighties with tan weathered skin and full white or gray beards, in long white robes and wide white trousers, with black or white cloth wound around their heads and black sashes at the waist
the qewals, the Yazidi temple players, men in their thirties to seventies with dark mustaches, in white shirts and dark waistcoats, each holding a large round frame drum of stretched skin or a long thin reed flute
the Baba Sheikh, the spiritual head of the Yazidis, a man in his seventies with a long white beard, in a white robe and a white turban wound with a black cloth, walking slowly with a wooden staff, sheikhs in white behind him
the feqirs, Yazidi holy men in their fifties to seventies with gray beards, in long tunics of coarse black wool belted with a red cord, black woolen caps on their heads
