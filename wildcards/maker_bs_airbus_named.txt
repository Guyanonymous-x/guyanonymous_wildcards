Styled in a design language of smooth, calm, composed European design in a restrained palette of deep royal blue, bright cyan, pale sky blue and clean white. Soft gradients fade from dark blue to light blue across large curved panels, divided by sharp-edged light cyan rays or a simple abstract slanted letter A set in a circle or square. Surfaces are glossy white paint, satin enamel and pearly metallic blue, always seamless and free of decoration. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
