Styled in a design language of folk decoration painted entirely in cobalt blue on bright white glaze. Brushstrokes shade fluidly from deep ink to pale sky blue, forming flowing flowers, leaves, scrolls, dots and drops that wrap around the form. Glossy smooth surfaces, a clean blue and white contrast, rounded plump forms and a joyful, fresh, handmade charm. Decoration is hand-brushed with confident single strokes, leaving plenty of bright white ground around each motif. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
