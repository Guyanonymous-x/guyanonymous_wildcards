In the style of Havanna, an Argentine alfajor and coffee house brand. The alfajor is a round sandwich of two soft pale cookies with a thick dark layer of dulce de leche in the middle, glazed fully in glossy dark chocolate or white meringue-like icing, sold individually wrapped in a shiny foil. The wrapper and tin boxes are a deep turquoise-blue or royal blue and white scheme with a warm cream, a large rounded white script logo in the form of a flowing signature over a navy background and slim gold or silver details, and the circle of a small orange rising-sun mark. The packaging of boxes has soft rounded rectangular shapes, with maritime references such as a seaside town and small gulls. The cafes are warm wood-and-brass spaces with dark wood counters, tall windows, beige marble tables and lamps with amber light. The mood is friendly, sweet and nostalgic seaside Argentina with a trustworthy clean cream and blue palette.
