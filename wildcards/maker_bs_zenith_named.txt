Styled in a design language of American console radios and televisions with smooth wooden cabinets in walnut and mahogany, fabric speaker cloths in gold and brown, large rounded screens, chrome-edged dials, pushbuttons and a bold lightning-bolt-shaped pattern in gold. Cream plastic hand-held remote controls with tubular parts. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
