__bs_subject_fashion__ in a style of soft, understated Australian rural craft. Surfaces are dense rabbit-fur felt, matte, slightly fuzzy and suede-like, in fawn, tan, oat, cream, stone grey, olive, charcoal and black The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
