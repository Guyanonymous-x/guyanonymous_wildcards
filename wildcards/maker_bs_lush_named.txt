Styled in a design language of handmade, playful and joyfully imperfect. Vivid swirled marbled patterns in hot pink, leaf green, purple, orange and turquoise, with confetti-like blocky shapes, collage cut-outs and fresh leaf motifs. Warm cream sticky labels, string and torn-paper edges. Colour is intense and fresh against plain black and brown. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
