Styled in a design language of English stoneware in rich, earthy, drippy glazes, with chunky rounded forms, wax-resist patterns and tactile dark edges. Colours are deep brown, mustard, blue-green, olive, smoky grey-blue and cream, speckled and reactive with natural variation, in brown-and-cream swirled and pebble-textured designs. Heavy, hand-thrown look, warm, practical and rustic, with a retro 1970s feel, thick walls, soft rounded edges and a handmade, slightly uneven finish. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
