In the style of Maxhosa Africa, South African knitwear built on bold Xhosa beadwork patterns turned into jacquard knit, with sharp geometric bands of triangles, diamonds, chevrons, zigzags, stepped blocks and horizontal stripes. Colours are vibrant and contrasting, such as red, orange, mustard, cobalt blue, emerald, turquoise, pink and black against cream or deep neutral grounds, in rich saturated blocks. Items include chunky sweaters, cardigans, blankets, scarves, beanies, long coats, dresses and trousers in mohair, merino wool and soft cotton. Patterns repeat symmetrically across the chest and sleeves like beaded collars. Warm, proud, celebratory, heritage-meets-contemporary mood.
