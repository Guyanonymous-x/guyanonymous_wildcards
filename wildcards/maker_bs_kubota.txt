In the style of Kubota, compact tractors and farm and construction machines in a single unmistakable colour scheme. The body panels and bonnet are bright safety orange, sprayed in a glossy even enamel, over dark grey or black chassis, wheel centres and engine covers. Tractors have a short rounded nose with a slatted front grille, rectangular work lights, slim chrome exhaust stack, a flat step platform and a high-backed black seat under a canopy or glass cab with big wraparound windows. Tyres are deep chevron treaded agricultural rubber. The logo is a white capital wordmark on an orange rectangle with a thin grey band along the bonnet side, plus small white model numbers and warning decals in yellow and black. Mini excavators and skid steers follow the same orange body with black rubber tracks or tyres, boxy booms and bright steel buckets. Mood is sturdy, dependable, tidy and slightly cheerful, like well maintained equipment sitting in a green rice field.
