Styled in a design language of graceful Parisian elegance with a tender, romantic polish. Soft, fluid, gently draped forms are trimmed with satin ribbons, oversized bows, ruffles and delicate gathers. Fine silks, satin, taffeta and crisp black grosgrain are enriched with jewelled embroidery, crystal beading, gold-toned metal and sparkling ornamental trim. Palette is powder blue, rose, lilac, cream, black and soft metallic gold, often as tonal harmonies or dramatic black and white. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
