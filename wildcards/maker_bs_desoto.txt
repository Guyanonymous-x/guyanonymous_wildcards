In the style of DeSoto, mid to late 1950s American cars with a tall vertical oval grille shaped like a pursed mouth, a huge chrome bumper that wraps around the nose, heavy chrome brows over twin headlamps, and tall pointed tail fins with arrow-shaped lamp trim. Long sweeping side chrome spear splits the two-tone paint, with a hooded roof and a wraparound screen. A wide whitewall tyre and full-disc hubcaps. Interior with a bold dash, push-button shifter and plush fabric. Colours of pink and charcoal, turquoise and white, sunset orange. Dramatic and theatrical.
