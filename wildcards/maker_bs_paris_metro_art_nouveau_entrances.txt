In the style of the early Paris Metro station entrances, Art Nouveau cast iron and glass structures painted in bottle green, with whiplash curved stems that bend like plant shoots, sinuous railings and balustrades shaped like flowering vines, and amber glass panels forming a shell or scalloped canopy over the stair. Tall lamp standards that curve like orchid stems holding glowing amber glass bulbs with organic ribbed shapes, and an enamelled lava stone plaque showing the station name in flowing, curved lettering with swelling ends. Dragonfly wing glazed canopies, botanical curves, delicate yet industrial, ornate organic ironwork, deep green with golden light, belle epoque city mood.
