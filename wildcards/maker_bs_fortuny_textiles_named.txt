In the style of Fortuny Textiles, velvet and cotton surfaces densely stamped with metallic gold and silver pigment on deep, mottled backgrounds. Colours are rich and muted, aubergine, indigo, burnt sienna, olive, ochre, antique rose and dusty teal, with a hazy, layered, hand-printed look. Patterns are Renaissance pomegranates, arabesques, stylised animals, ogee lattices and medallions repeating in tight rows. A faint shimmer from the metal, a soft aged glow and a velvety hand give a rich, theatrical, exotic mood, lit by softly diffused light.
