Styled in a design language of everything built from straight horizontal and vertical lines and rectangles. The palette is limited to pure red, yellow and blue, with white, black and grey, set in flat colour blocks with thick black dividing lines. Objects are assembled from slim rails and flat panels that overlap and project at their ends, painted in contrasting colours with exposed ends. Spaces show asymmetrical balance and open planes. Lettering is blocky and constructed from squares. The mood is strict, rational, abstract and playful in its colour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
