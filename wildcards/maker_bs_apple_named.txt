Styled in a design language of pared-back, precise and quietly luxurious. Single continuous forms with perfectly rounded corners, razor-thin edges, seamless joins and no visible fasteners. Materials are brushed aluminium, polished glass and soft-touch matte finishes in silver, space grey, white, black and a few muted pastel tints, with colour mainly coming from the screen or content. Everything is reduced to the essential, with ample negative space. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
