Styled in a design language of tough, honest off-road design. Tall upright slab-sided volumes with a vertical seven-slot or multi-bar pattern, a bold chrome ring, large round or squared details, a flat upper surface, black plastic edging and a stubby short tail. Big square arches, chunky forms and a raised intake shape. Simple fabric. Newer forms add a sharp twin-peak mark and twin-line LED details. Deep black, white, bold red and dune beige. Rugged, stiff-stanced and blunt. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
