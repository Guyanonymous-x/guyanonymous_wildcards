Styled in a design language of glossy tree-bark dye. Rows of small, bold black symbols such as concentric circles, spirals, crosses, ladders and hearts repeat inside a grid divided by hand-drawn comb lines. Backgrounds are dyed deep red, brown, indigo or black, or left natural cream. Symbols are slightly irregular with inky edges, giving a handmade rhythm and strong graphic quality. Narrow woven strips are stitched together into a wide cloth. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
