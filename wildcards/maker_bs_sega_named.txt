In the style of Sega, edgy fast arcade-bright energy. Glossy black plastic with sharp angular wedges, circular recessed details, moulded ridges and shallow grooves. Cobalt blue is the signature, in wide italic capitals framed inside a bold red or blue horizontal band with a thin white outline. A later softer identity uses off-white and white surfaces with a swirling orange spiral emblem and light, rounded lowercase lettering. Artwork is loud and saturated with bold outlines, hard-edged speed lines, white and orange accents and glowing red or green accent lights. Futuristic, quick, playful and confident. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Sega in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
