In the style of Vista Alegre, elegant, formal, light and slightly romantic Portuguese porcelain. Forms have scrolling handles, high feet and flower-shaped finials. Modern versions turn to bold graphic prints, hand-painted strokes in cobalt, black or coral and sculptural art editions. Traditional palette is white, gold, navy, rose pink and sage green. Tiny gilt crowns and symmetrical arrangements. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Vista Alegre in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
