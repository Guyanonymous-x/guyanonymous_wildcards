Styled in a design language of witty surrealist couture where familiar things turn into clever visual jokes. Shocking hot pink is the signature colour, set against black, white, deep red, gold and rich jewel tones. Surfaces glitter with dense gold embroidery, faceted crystal clusters in pink, ruby and smoky grey, glossy lava glass and sculptural gilded metal in the shape of flowers, insects, lobsters, eyes, lips and anatomical forms. Silhouettes are sharp and structured, with strong shoulders and exaggerated, theatrical shapes. Optical illusion tricks make drawn or embroidered details mimic real ones. Ornaments are cast, enamelled and boldly oversized. Daring, artistic, glamorous, with a playful sense of the unexpected and a lavish gold flourish. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
