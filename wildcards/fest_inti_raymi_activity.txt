The emperor stands on the stone altar with his arms raised to the sun, a golden sun disc flashing on his chest
The emperor is carried on a golden throne high on the shoulders of bearers, the crowd parting before him
A priest lifts a golden cup of chicha, maize beer, toward the sun and pours it onto the stones
Two priests hold up a white llama figure of straw and cloth over the altar fire as an offering
Women in red scatter flower petals on the path before the litter
Rows of dancers stamp and turn together, long feathered staffs raised high
Warriors with wooden spears and round shields march in step across the field
Messengers run across the field to the emperor and kneel before him
Priests light a fire on the altar with a curved gold mirror that catches the sun
