Six, a housemaid in with coal, water and linen, the grate done, two jugs of hot water carried up the back stair from the still room
Eight to ten, the room's main function, dressing, shaving, hair, which with a valet or a lady's maid present is two hours of two people working in a 20 square meter room
Eleven, the slops carried out, the bath emptied if there was one, the room aired
The afternoon, the room used again for an hour to change
Seven, dressed for dinner, candles lit at the swing glass
Eleven at night, undressing, the valet waiting outside the door
Midnight, the close stool emptied for the last time; and a bath night, which is once or twice a week, forty minutes of carrying and twenty of bathing
