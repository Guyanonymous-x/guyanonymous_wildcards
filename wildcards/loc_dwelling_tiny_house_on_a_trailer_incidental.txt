a weight placard on the chassis and a spirit level in a locker, which together are the two most consequential objects in the whole record because a tiny house must be under a legal towing weight and must be leveled on arrival or none of the doors or drains work
a stair with drawers built into every riser, which is the most characteristic single piece of tiny-house joinery and exists because no space can serve one purpose
a sleeping loft with a meter of headroom in which the occupant sits up but cannot stand, which is the physical compromise everybody makes and the one most often regretted
a tiny wood stove the size of a microwave, which is a real product category and is almost too much heat for the volume so the door gets opened in January
a composting or incinerating toilet, which is the single most-discussed component in the entire tiny-house world
a water inlet and a graywater outlet on the outside wall, which is how the house connects to the world and is the limit of its independence
a propane locker vented to the outside
a window seat with drawers under it
a ladder that is also a towel rail
