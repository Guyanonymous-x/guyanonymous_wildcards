Styled in a design language of spare, precise, calm and modern minimalism. Slim round stainless steel with thin straight edges, a very narrow rim and a flat domed glass over a pure white or silvery-white surface. Plain brown or cordovan leather. Transparent backs reveal a decorated mechanism with stripes and blued screws. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
