Styled in a design language of rugged, active and outdoorsy design. Small matte black and dark grey rubberised cubes with chunky rounded corners, bold white icons, and transparent protective shells. Black plastic frames, folding tab-like joins, orange, yellow or blue rubber accents and chunky round fastener details. Edges are softly bevelled and surfaces carry a fine matte grain. Packaging uses kraft cardboard layers, die-cut sleeves, black window boxes and crisp blue and white graphic bands. Tough, compact, action-ready and cheerful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
