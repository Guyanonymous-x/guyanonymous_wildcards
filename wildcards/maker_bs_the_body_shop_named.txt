In the style of The Body Shop, earnest, earthy, ethical design in forest green and white. Painted forest-green surfaces carry white lettering, with glossy dark green and white graphics and plain clear plastic with simple matt finishes and thin white type. Rows of small refillable forms, jute and kraft textures, wicker and hand-written stickers keep things natural. Small simple illustrations depict natural ingredients. Palette is green, white and natural fibre tones. Mood is honest, practical, utilitarian, friendly and conscientious, with no gloss or luxury.
