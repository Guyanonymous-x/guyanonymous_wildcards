Ammit, the Devourer, crouches by the scales, her head that of a crocodile with long jaws, her front a lion with a spotted mane, her back the thick gray hindquarters of a hippopotamus
Ammit, crocodile-headed, lion-bodied and hippo-legged, leans forward from her stand by the scales, her long jaws open and her eyes on the heavy heart
Ammit seizes the red heart of the wicked in her crocodile jaws and swallows it, her lion forepaws braced on the floor of the hall
