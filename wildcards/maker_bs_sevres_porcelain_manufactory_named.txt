In the style of Sevres Porcelain Manufactory, smooth glossy porcelain with deep saturated ground colours, rich royal blue, turquoise, delicate rose pink and apple green, all heavily gilded. Reserved panels hold finely painted cherubs, pastoral figures, birds and flower bouquets in naturalistic colour. Gilt borders, scrolls, beading and lacy foliage trim every edge, and curves flow like branches or flower stems. Forms are swelling, ornate and Rococo. Unglazed matte white areas have a silky, snow-like texture. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Sevres Porcelain Manufactory in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
