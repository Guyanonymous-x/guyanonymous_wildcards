Styled in a design language of polished tennis-club elegance with a playful, confident wink. Textured cotton pique with a small knit weave, ribbed edges and a clean, relaxed fit. Palette is tennis white plus deep bottle green, navy, red, sky blue and yellow, in solid blocks and bold stripes. Terry cloth bands, white canvas, crisp laundered surfaces and white court lines add sport cues. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
