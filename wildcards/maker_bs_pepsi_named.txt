Styled in a design language of bold, electric, pop-culture energy built on a round globe divided into red, white and blue. A white wave runs through the circle, which tilts, smiles or splits into bold slices and large flat discs. Palette is deep cobalt and midnight blue with bright red, crisp white and pops of electric blue glow, with black used for modern, edgy variants. Surfaces are glossy, chilled and metallic, covered in condensation, ice and fizz bubbles, with dark backgrounds lit by neon rim light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
