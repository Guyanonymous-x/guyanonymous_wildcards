In the style of Tong Ren Tang, imperial, scholarly, aromatic, sombre and prosperous, like an old apothecary of lacquer, brass and paper. Long dark wooden lattice facades with carved eaves, lacquered red pillars, a horizontal black board with large gold brush calligraphy, tall hanging vertical signs and red paper lanterns. Imperial yellow, vermilion red and gold with embossed dragon or lotus borders, traditional calligraphic characters and red seal stamps. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
