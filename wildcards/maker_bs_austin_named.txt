Styled in a design language of rounded, sensible and homely British motoring heritage. Stately soft curves, tall upright proportions and narrow chrome edging. Paint in black, maroon, deep green, grey, cream, sky blue and pastel red, often in two-tone panels with a fine contrasting line. Printed literature uses flat gouache-style illustration, simple colour blocking. Polished wood veneer, thick leather in beige and maroon, and tidy chrome details. Modest, dependable, comfortable, wholesome and nostalgic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
