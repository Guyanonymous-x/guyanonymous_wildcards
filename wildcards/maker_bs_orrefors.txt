Styled in a design language of Swedish art glass, thick, crystal-clear and heavy-walled, with deep engraved or wheel-cut scenes, ethereal trapped air bubbles and abstract wavy colour patterns inside the material. Forms are streamlined, tapering and smooth, in a cool transparent palette with accents of slate blue, smoky grey and soft green. Understated, refined, sculptural Scandinavian modernism, pure and sparkling in cold light. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
