Styled in a design language of artisan-made embroidery, applique and patchwork on handwoven cotton. Precisely counted triangular geometry is stitched in satin stitch, forming tiny diamonds, zig-zags and stars in mirror-like symmetry. Colours are bold contrasts of magenta, orange, yellow, green, royal blue and white on black or deep indigo cloth, with quilted patchwork in reds and blacks. Small mirrors are set at the centre of motifs. Meticulous, handmade, vibrant, desert-village craft mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
