Styled in a design language of joyful, bright, folkloric design that mixes wide kimono-like sleeves, loose smock shapes, patchwork, quilting and layered ethnic prints in a single look. Large flowers, stripes, ginghams, checks and paisley are combined at clashing scale in saturated tomato red, yellow, turquoise, emerald, pink and purple. Soft cotton, jersey, knits and light gauzy weaves are cut in a relaxed, unstructured, unisex way, and big knitted surfaces carry jungle animal motifs. A cheerful, colourful, exotic jungle-and-garden fantasy, sunlit, slightly naive, with a fluffy hand-painted look. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
