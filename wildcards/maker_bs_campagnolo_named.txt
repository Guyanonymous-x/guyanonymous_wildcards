In the style of Campagnolo, classic Italian racing refinement in jewel-like metalwork. Mirror-polished and satin silver aluminium and chrome with fine engraving, scalloped cut-outs, drilled patterns and hand-finished machining marks. The emblem is a small winged roundel in a rounded shield, shown embossed in pewter-look metal or printed in blue, gold and yellow on oval decals with a flowing handwritten script wordmark. Colours are deep royal blue, lemon yellow, red and cream with silver, often in oval or lozenge shaped badges and green or red frames. Ornate yet precise, nostalgic, proud, mid-century craft with a collectable patina. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Campagnolo in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
