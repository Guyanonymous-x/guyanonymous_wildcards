In the style of Kartell, Italian injection-moulded plastic furniture, glossy and translucent polycarbonate in crystal clear, smoky, ruby red, fluorescent orange, lime and acid pink. Chairs with sculptural baroque silhouettes, transparent ghost-like forms, stackable stools, lighting in clear plastic shaped like pleated shades and bulbous gourds. Smooth seamless curves, bright jewel colours, high-gloss reflections and light passing through the material. Playful, fashionable, pop and kitsch with modern luxury, gallery-white showroom photography, reflective floors.
