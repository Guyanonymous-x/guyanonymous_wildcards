Styled in a design language of meticulous, hygienic, bustling and warm, with an exacting clean presentation. Small steamed parcels with paper-thin translucent skins, each gathered by hand into a tidy spiral of fine pleats with a little knot at the top, sitting in a round bamboo steamer with glints of broth showing. Palette of cream, bamboo tan, steam white and one red accent. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
