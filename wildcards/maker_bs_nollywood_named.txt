Styled in a design language of high-energy, melodramatic, richly saturated imagery built as dramatic collage compositions. Bold West African wax-print and woven cloth patterns, elaborate sculpted headwear, abundant jewellery and showy displays of wealth, with bright daylight and crowded frames. Emotion is turned up to the maximum, colours are loud and shiny, and the finish has a lively, direct, early digital home-video energy. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
