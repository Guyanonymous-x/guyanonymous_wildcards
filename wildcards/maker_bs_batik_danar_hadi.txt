In the style of Batik Danar Hadi, Javanese hand-drawn wax-resist cotton and silk cloths in earthy palette. Colours include soga brown, indigo blue, cream and black, with occasional red and gold. Patterns are classical, such as diagonal parang bands of interlocking S shapes, kawung circles in a quatrefoil grid, truntum small flowers, and sido mukti cartouches with birds and wings. Fine dotted lines and wavering white outlines from hand-applied wax form the delicate work. Cloth shows crackle veins where the wax cracked, and tonal depth. The mood is courtly, rhythmic, refined and heritage-rich.
