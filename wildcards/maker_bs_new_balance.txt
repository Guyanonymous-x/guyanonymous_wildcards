Styled in a design language of chunky, understated performance design in a restrained palette of heather grey suede and mesh with navy, burgundy or forest green accents. A large slanted capital N in contrasting colour is the main mark. Layered suede and breathable mesh panels, thick tapered foam in off-white, solid structured rear shapes, stitched ribs and a gum or white rubber texture. Tonal grey monochrome versions with only hints of colour at small details. Practical, wide-fit, slightly orthopaedic, honest in craft and unfashionable on purpose, with a dad quality and 1980s jogging heritage. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
