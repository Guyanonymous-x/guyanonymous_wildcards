Styled in a design language of tough, dense, precision-machined industrial design. Brushed and bead-blasted stainless steel, black oxide and matte black coatings are paired with a strong yellow or gold accent and occasional anodised colour in orange, blue or olive. Forms are compact, rectangular and softly bevelled, with rows of small perforated holes, crisp stamped edges, visible rivets and tight seams. The mark is a bold capital wordmark in wide, heavy, slightly squared sans-serif, often set beside a bold geometric letter L emblem inside a rounded square, in black and yellow. Black matte boxes and sleeves with thin yellow lettering. Utilitarian, rugged and confident. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
