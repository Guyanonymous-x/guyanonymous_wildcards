In the style of Plymouth, 1957 and 1960s American cars with sharp tall tail fins that blade backward, a flat, wide, bright grille with a sailing-ship badge, and long sculpted flanks with a sweeping two-tone chrome sweep. Dual headlamps, a very low hood, full-width chrome bumper and large thin roof with a huge rear window. Muscle versions have a bold hood scoop, a rear-wing spoiler tall as a hand, and loud decals with a cartoon bird. Chrome rimmed wheels with whitewalls. Colours of Plum Crazy purple, Vitamin C orange, lemon twist yellow and Sassy Grass green. Bold and cheeky.
