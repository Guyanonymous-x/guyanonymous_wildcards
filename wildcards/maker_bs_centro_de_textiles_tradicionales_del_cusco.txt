In the style of the Andean weaving cooperative of Cusco in Peru, hand-woven backstrap-loom textiles in natural dyed wool. Pieces are long narrow bands, square blankets, shoulder bags, belts, ponchos and table runners made of sheep, alpaca or llama yarn with a slightly fuzzy hand-spun texture. The warp-faced weave shows crisp horizontal stripes of plain colour alternating with bands of dense pattern, patterned panels of diamonds, zigzag lines, small stepped figures, llamas, birds, lakes drawn as flowers inside diamonds, Milky Way meanders and tiny geometric motifs which are reversible on both sides. Colours are earthy plant-dye shades, deep indigo and sky blues, cochineal reds, pinks and purples, yellow-greens, mustard, walnut brown, cream and charcoal, and each community has its own dominant palette such as maroon with white or red with green. Edges are finished with braided cords and tassels. The look is rich, rustic, rhythmic and symmetrical, a precise ancient textile language rendered in warm natural tones.
