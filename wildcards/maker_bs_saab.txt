Styled in a design language of aeronautical, quirky, practical and eccentric Swedish design. Short, stubby, rounded forms with deeply curved glass-like surfaces, rectangular accents and a cockpit-like compactness. Palette is midnight blue, black, silver metallic, deep red and white. Finishes are satin black plastic edges, brushed alloy with a distinct pattern of round holes, grey or blue textured cloth and moulded rubbery trims. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
