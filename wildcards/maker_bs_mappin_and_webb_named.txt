Styled in a design language of British silverware and jewellery in polished sterling, with finely engraved fluted forms, ornate candelabra, shining teapots on trays, cutlery with decorated handles and presentation cases lined with royal blue velvet. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
