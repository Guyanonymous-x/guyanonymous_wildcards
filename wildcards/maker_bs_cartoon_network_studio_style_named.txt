Styled in a design language of flat bold-colour 2D cartoon look with thick uniform outlines, geometric simplified characters, huge heads, dot or pie-cut eyes and noodle limbs. Exaggerated reaction faces, graphic backgrounds in solid saturated fields of magenta, teal, lime and orange. Clean vector shapes, quirky off-kilter perspective and a loud, irreverent, pop-art look. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
