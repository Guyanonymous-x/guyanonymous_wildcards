In the style of Matyo Embroidery, dense, thick, glossy thread worked on black, white or dark blue cloth. Motifs are huge, full-blown roses, bold tulips, carnations, leaf sprays, buds and tendrils in heavily stylised form, often with a central flower surrounded by smaller blossoms and swirling leaves in mirrored symmetry. Palette is bright, high-contrast and joyful, red, pink, magenta, orange, yellow, light blue and green on black, or navy, crimson and deep green on white. Tight satin stitch gives a slightly raised, padded texture with black or gold outlines. Richly decorated, colourful, joyous and festive, as if a flower garden covered every surface. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Matyo Embroidery in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
