In the style of Ntonso Adinkra Cloth Makers, handwoven cotton stamped with carved stamps in a dark, glossy tree-bark dye. Rows of small, bold black symbols such as concentric circles, spirals, crosses, ladders and hearts repeat inside a grid divided by hand-drawn comb lines. Backgrounds are dyed deep red, brown, indigo or black, or left natural cream. Symbols are slightly irregular with inky edges, giving a handmade rhythm and strong graphic quality. Narrow woven strips are stitched together into a wide cloth. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Ntonso Adinkra Cloth Makers in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
