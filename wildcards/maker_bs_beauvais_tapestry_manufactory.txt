In the style of Beauvais Tapestry Manufactory, woven tapestries and upholstery panels with a playful, pastoral Rococo character. Scenes show gallant shepherds, children at play, garden romances, exotic animals and fables, rendered in soft, sweet colours such as pink, cream, sky blue, pale green and gold with brown outlines. Frames of woven flowers, ribbons, scallops and C-shaped scrolls surround chair backs, screens and sofas. The weave is fine and smooth and the colours glow like a painted pastel. Mood is light, romantic and decorative, with plump forms and flowing curves.
