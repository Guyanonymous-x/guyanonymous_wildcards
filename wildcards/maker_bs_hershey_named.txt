Styled in a design language of sturdy, warm American chocolate-brown design. Flat colour accents in red, gold, orange, purple or blue distinguish variants against the dark brown base. Surfaces include silver metallic foil, satin paper wrap, raised square grid embossing and rounded rectangle panels. A teardrop silhouette with a small flat base and rounded peak recurs as an emblem. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
