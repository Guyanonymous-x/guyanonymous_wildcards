Styled in a design language of rectangular paper printed in black and green ink on cream cotton stock, with a central oval portrait of a statesman engraved in dense fine line work, ornate guilloche rosettes in each corner. Newer versions add pastel peach. Traditional engraving, banking authority and tradition. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
