Styled in a design language of plain, hard-wearing, no-frills Tasmanian workwear design. Smooth, waxed or oiled leather in rustic brown, chestnut, black, tan, deep oxblood and slate grey, with a scuffed, honest, well-used character. Forms are rounded, stout and stripped back, with elastic panels, bold pull tabs, visible stitching and chunky black rubber and contrasting pale edge stripes. Seasonal ranges add stout colour pops in olive, sage, rust and navy. Honest, timeless, practical and tough. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
