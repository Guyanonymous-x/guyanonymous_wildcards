In the style of Zildjian Cymbal Foundry, hand-hammered bronze cymbals with a bright warm gold sheen, circular and dished with a raised central bell. The surface shows concentric lathing grooves and dense dimpled hammer marks, with darker, burnished tones in the recesses and polished highlights on the ridges. Traditional versions look dark, brown and dull gold while newer versions are brilliantly polished and mirror bright. A small hole in the bell takes the strap, edges are thin and slightly wavy, and a printed stamp with an ornate logo, in black or gold ink, sits near the bell. Variations include flat rides, crash cymbals and hi-hats.
