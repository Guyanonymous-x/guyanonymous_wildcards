In the style of Shaw Brothers, colour-saturated, studio-made period drama where everything is clearly built and artificial. Painted backdrops, theatrical fog, artificial lighting and stylised settings of bamboo, courtyards and lanterns. Vivid reds, yellows and jade greens, silk with rich embroidery, and bold dramatic poses. Sweeping, choreographed movement and sudden emphasis. Graphics are hand-painted with brushed calligraphic lettering and exaggerated, pulpy emotion. Heightened, operatic, vividly coloured and gloriously artificial, with a 1970s pulp feel and a slightly grainy, warm film-stock finish.
