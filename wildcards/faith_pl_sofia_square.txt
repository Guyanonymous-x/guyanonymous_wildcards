Sofiyska Square, a wide open square paved with gray stone, the bronze equestrian statue of the Cossack leader Bohdan Khmelnytsky, a man on a rearing horse with a raised mace, on a granite base in its middle
the edge of the square where a line of militsiya, the police of the time, stand with shields before the cathedral gates
the square crowded, people spilling across the paving, a few old Soviet cars, boxy Ladas and Volgas, parked along the curb
