the shared common house at the center of a co-housing scheme of twenty to forty private households
common meals cooked by a rota two to four times a week, with a sign-up sheet and a small charge
a kitchen designed and equipped to a near-commercial standard because cooking for forty is not domestic cooking
facilities that are deliberately removed from the private houses and placed here so that they must be shared, the guest room
the laundry
the workshop
the big dining space, which is the central design idea of co-housing and is why the private houses can be small
a parking lot at the edge and a pedestrian green in the middle with every front door facing it
governance by residents through working groups and a general meeting
a legal structure of freehold or leasehold with a management company, and a development process that typically took the founding group five to ten years
