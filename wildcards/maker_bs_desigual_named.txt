Styled in a design language of an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
