Styled in a design language of glossy jelly-like plastic moulded in one piece with sculptural bows, flowers, buckles and cutouts. The surface is shiny, translucent or glittery with a faint sweet-candy look. Colours are jelly pink, lilac, mint, clear transparent, baby blue, yellow, red, black and metallic glitter. Shapes are playful and rounded, with chunky bases, bubble-like ends, ribbed textures and puffy edges. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
