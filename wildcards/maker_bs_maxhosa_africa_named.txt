In the style of Maxhosa Africa, bold beadwork patterns turned into jacquard knit, with sharp geometric bands of triangles, diamonds, chevrons, zigzags, stepped blocks and horizontal stripes. Colours are vibrant and contrasting, such as red, orange, mustard, cobalt blue, emerald, turquoise, pink and black against cream or deep neutral grounds, in rich saturated blocks. Materials are chunky mohair, merino wool and soft cotton. Patterns repeat symmetrically like beaded collars. Warm, proud, celebratory, heritage-meets-contemporary mood. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Maxhosa Africa in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
