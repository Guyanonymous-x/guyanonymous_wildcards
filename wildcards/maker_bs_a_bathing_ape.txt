Styled in a design language of loud, cartoonish, collectible streetwear graphics. A simple ape head in side profile serves as the emblem, often a tiny patch on a plain field. Camouflage is the core pattern, a large blobby shark-skin style camo in unexpected bright colourways such as lime green, pink, purple, yellow, blue and rainbow gradients, alongside classic olive and grey versions. Glossy star shapes, wide stacked stripes and big chunky stencil-like lettering sit on oversized, boxy, heavy cotton, fleece and nylon forms. Glow-in-the-dark prints, bold white text and a playful hip-hop Tokyo attitude run through everything, applicable to any object from bags to tableware. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
