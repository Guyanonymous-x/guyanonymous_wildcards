Styled in a design language of quirky, offbeat 1970s American pragmatism. Stubby, lopsided proportions with one long mass, one short mass and an abruptly chopped, flat cut-off end. Square shoulders, bulging haunches and plain black plastic trim. Loud colours of bright orange, vivid green, mustard and avocado. Practical, rebellious and cheerfully strange. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
