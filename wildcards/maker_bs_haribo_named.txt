In the style of Haribo, bright, cheerful, playful German retro fun. Palette is glossy primary red, sunshine yellow, orange, lime green and clear sky blue, with metallic gold foil and deep black outlines for contrast. Materials are translucent jelly-like gloss, crinkly shiny metallic film and clear windows showing a jumble of small chunky shapes, soft, smooth and slightly sugar-dusted. Lettering is bold, rounded, capitalised and set in red with a heavy black outline and white edge, often arched over a golden mascot-like cartoon figure with a friendly smile and a red neck bow. Forms are small, plump, rounded and simple. Colour does the shouting, and the whole language feels sunny, sweet, nostalgic and instantly readable. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Haribo in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
