Styled in a design language of sunny, relaxed beach-culture graphics. Palette is muted sage, teal, deep navy, sand, cream, burnt orange and warm brown, with brighter aqua, coral and lime for retro tones. Textures are soft garment-washed cotton, quick-dry nylon and slub knit with contrast stitching and small woven labels. Easygoing, sporty and sun-faded. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
