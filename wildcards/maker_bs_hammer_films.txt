In the style of Hammer Films gothic horror, richly saturated Technicolor in blood red, deep green and midnight blue, candle-lit castles, fog-wrapped graveyards and crypts, ornate Victorian costumes with heaving bodices and capes, fangs, dripping crimson blood, dramatic painted posters with screaming women and looming shadows, lurid yellow and red title lettering with jagged dripping edges, studio-built sets with painted skies and a lush lurid storybook creepiness.
