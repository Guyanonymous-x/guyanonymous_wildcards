Styled in a design language of warm, casual, woodsy cabin-in-the-woods outdoor lifestyle. Soft saddle-brown leather with stamped marks, collegiate mixes of wool with leather, and thick, generous forms. Colours are earthy and rustic, forest green, burgundy, navy, camel, cream, red and black plaid and maple-red accents. Cosy, outdoorsy, friendly and unpretentious. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
