Styled in a design language of bold alpine-rebel graphics built on stark black and white. The emblem is a jagged, snow-capped mountain silhouette with a pointed peak in white on black. An older emblem is a chunky interlocked letter B form in thick rounded black strokes. Palette is black, white and grey, then clashing neon pink, acid green, orange, cyan and purple in colour-blocked panels. Surfaces are glossy and slick with graphic prints of skulls, flames, camouflage, geometric shards and tropical foliage, plus matte technical nylon and fleece. Forms are loose, oversized and chunky with sharp angular cuts. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
