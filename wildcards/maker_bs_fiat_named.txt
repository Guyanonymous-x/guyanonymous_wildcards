Styled in a design language of tiny, round, cheerful and playful post-war Italian design. Bulbous egg-like and bubble forms, softly curved tops, thin chrome edges and simple rounded rectangles. Palette is pastel yellow, powder blue, mint green, cream, cherry red, orange and white, often in flat, single-colour fields. Surfaces are painted body-colour metal, simple vinyl, canvas and lightly polished chrome. Flat vintage illustration blocks with simple shapes and bright optimistic colour make everything feel light and sunny. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
