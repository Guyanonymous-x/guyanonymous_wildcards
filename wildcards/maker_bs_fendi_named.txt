In the style of Fendi, Roman luxury with plush, playful, craft-heavy richness. A repeating double-letter monogram pattern in brown on tan is a core surface idea, alongside patchwork and inlaid fur or leather worked like fine marquetry. Fluffy trims are dyed in bright candy colours, and whimsical little charms with cartoon eyes add humour. Overall shapes are sleek and clean in camel, black and white, compact and carried close to the body, secured by neat metal details. Warm, tactile, witty and indulgent. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
