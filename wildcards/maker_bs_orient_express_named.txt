In the style of Orient Express, glamorous 1920s and 1930s European luxury travel. Deep midnight blue and cream with gold lettering and fine gold lines, polished walnut and mahogany marquetry, etched glass, brass fittings and plush velvet in claret and sapphire. Art Deco geometry with stepped borders, sunbursts and elegant stylised lettering. Soft lamplight, crisp white linen and silver service, a hush of mystery, decadence and cosmopolitan elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
