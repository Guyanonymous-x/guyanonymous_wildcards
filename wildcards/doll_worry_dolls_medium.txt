a set of Guatemalan worry dolls, tiny handmade dolls in a small woven pouch, each about as long as an adult's fingertip. Each doll is a few sticks bound together and wrapped tightly in bright thread and scraps of woven cloth, with a tiny round head and a dot of a face. They are dressed in the colors of Guatemalan weaving, red, yellow, green, blue, purple and pink, the women in skirts and the men in trousers and hats. __doll_worry_dolls_var__.
