Styled in a design language of Soviet-era graphic confidence in deep cobalt navy, pale sky blue, white and a punchy red. A circular emblem with outspread, feathered bars on both sides sits centred over a small disc, drawn flat in navy or silver-grey. Colour blocking is bold and optimistic, with sweeping diagonals, a broad pale blue band across bare metallic silver or white, and red used as a decisive accent. Surfaces are polished aluminium, flat gouache-like colour and slightly worn enamel. Flat graphic geometry, rounded rectangles and radiating lines give an idealistic, mid-century modern, utilitarian feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
