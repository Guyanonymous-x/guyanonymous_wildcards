Styled in a design language of royal-court batik, hand-drawn and stamped on cotton and silk in elaborate classical patterns, with diagonal blade-like bands, circle grids, star flowers and lotus motifs filling the cloth evenly in fine linework. Colours are dignified earth tones of sogan brown, midnight indigo, cream white and soft black, with occasional touches of gold paint. Surfaces are hand-finished with a slightly crackled wax texture. Dignified, traditional, courtly mood, in dim museum-like light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
