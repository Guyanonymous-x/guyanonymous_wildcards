Styled in a design language of loud, cartoonish, collectible streetwear graphics. A simple ape head in side profile serves as the emblem, often a tiny patch on a plain field. Camouflage is the core pattern, a large blobby shark-skin style camo in unexpected bright colourways such as lime green, pink, purple, yellow, blue and rainbow gradients, alongside classic olive and grey versions. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
