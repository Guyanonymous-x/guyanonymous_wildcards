Styled in a design language of warm, ornate, richly coloured design with a regal Indian character. Strong red and white colour blocking is paired with deep maroon, saffron, aubergine and burnished gold. A small arched motif drawn from ornamental scalloped arches and a gold circular emblem repeats as a graphic mark, often with fine curved lines that suggest a sun or a gateway frame. Surfaces are glossy lacquer and gold foil, with jewel-like borders, paisley, jali lattice and floral geometry as pattern. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
