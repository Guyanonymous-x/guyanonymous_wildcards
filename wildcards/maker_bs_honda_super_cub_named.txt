Styled in a design language of cheerful Japanese everyday charm. Bright, simple solid colours in poppy red, cream white, deep navy and soft teal, with generous clean white borders and a single contrasting stripe panel. A winged emblem in red, silver and white appears on round enamel badges with polished metal rims. Pressed, smooth, softly rounded surfaces with glossy paint, chrome edging and simple decal graphics feel warm, honest, practical and endearing. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
