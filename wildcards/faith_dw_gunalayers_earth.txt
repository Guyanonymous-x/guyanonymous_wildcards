in the middle band lies the earth, the small low islands of Guna Yala crowded with houses of upright cane and steep palm thatch, coconut palms leaning over the white sand and dugout canoes drawn up on the shore
in the middle band lies Nabgwana, Mother Earth, painted as a woman in her sixties lying along the band, rivers, forest and corn fields growing from her body, the green mainland mountains of Darién rising behind the islands
in the middle band stands the onmaked nega, the gathering house, a long hall of cane walls under a huge steep roof of palm thatch reaching almost to the ground, hammocks slung inside it between the posts
