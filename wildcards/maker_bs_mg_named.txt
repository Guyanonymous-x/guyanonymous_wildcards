In the style of MG, small, jaunty, open sporting spirit in a British 1930s to 1960s mood. Compact, low, cheerful proportions with separate sweeping curves, rounded ends and bright polished chrome rims against enamel. Racing green, red, cream, ivory and pale blue. Red or black leather, engine-turned metal and warm wooden panels. A repeated octagon motif and fine vertical lines. Spirited, plucky and simple, with period charm. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
