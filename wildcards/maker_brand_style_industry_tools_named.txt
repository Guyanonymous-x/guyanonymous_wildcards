In the style of Victorinox, Swiss precision and functional compactness. Glossy translucent or matte signal red surfaces with a white cross on a shield emblem inlaid near the top, polished stainless steel nested neatly in thin layers with brass-coloured accents. Smooth rounded rectangular profiles with a gently pinched waist and fine laser-etched small text in clean sans-serif capitals. Black polymer, olive, blue, silver and translucent coloured variants appear on tougher or playful versions. Bright signal red against cool silver steel and white, tidy and orderly, with many functions folded into a small body. Packaging uses red and white cards, silver surfaces and thin clear windows with neat technical diagrams.
In the style of Opinel, rustic French craft simplicity. Slim, tapered, teardrop-like forms in pale natural beech wood, olivewood and dark stained wood with a clear lacquer glow, joined to plain carbon steel that develops a soft grey patina. Small touches of colour in navy, olive, red, yellow and white lacquer. The emblem is a small crowned open hand with a plain serif capital wordmark and the words Savoie France in a thin line below, usually in navy blue or black on a cream, white or deep blue field. Kraft card, tin and cardboard packing in blue and cream, with simple fine rules. Honest, modest, countryside and timeless.
In the style of Leatherman, tough, dense, precision-machined industrial design. Brushed and bead-blasted stainless steel, black oxide and matte black coatings are paired with a strong yellow or gold accent and occasional anodised colour in orange, blue or olive. Forms are compact, rectangular and softly bevelled, with rows of small perforated holes, crisp stamped edges, visible rivets and tight seams. The mark is a bold capital wordmark in wide, heavy, slightly squared sans-serif, often set beside a bold geometric letter L emblem inside a rounded square, in black and yellow. Black matte boxes and sleeves with thin yellow lettering. Utilitarian, rugged and confident.
In the style of Maglite, robust machined-aluminium design with a heavy, solid feel. Perfectly round cylindrical forms with fine diamond knurling, thick walls, crisp bevels and visible machining lines. Finishes are hard-anodised matte black as the core, with silver, titanium grey, deep red, blue and olive variants and small engraved capital lettering along the surface. The mark is a bold black wordmark in chunky, rounded, slightly squashed capitals with a hyphen-like gap, paired with a round shield-like emblem containing a swooping curved cut. Black and white packing with plain, strong graphics. Tough, dependable, no-frills and industrial.
In the style of Bosch Professional, robust, ergonomic, cool and professional design for any product. Hard bodies in a deep, distinctive blue plastic with black rubberised soft areas and bold red accents. Compact, chunky shapes with curved ergonomic handles, textured diamond-pattern grips and bright silver metal details. Lettering is crisp white capital sans-serif with small red warning marks. The blue and black pairing repeats everywhere, with tough blue-grey moulded surfaces, rugged carry cases with silver latches and a calm, dependable, no-nonsense feel.
In the style of Makita, solid, workmanlike and cool teal design for any product. Strong teal-blue-green plastic with black rubberised areas, a red stripe accent and tough silver or steel metalwork. Compact, rounded, chunky shapes with ribbed overmoulded grips, neat rows of small ventilation slots and sturdy cases. Lettering is bold white capitals inside a boxed mark, with bright yellow warning labels as small accents. Practical, hard-wearing and made for long working days.
In the style of DeWalt, loud, rugged, jobsite-ready and high-visibility. Bright yellow plastic with black rubberised grips and black accent bands, boxy chunky shapes with strong ribbing and protective rubber bumpers. Chunky yellow and black blocks, with small silver steel parts. Text is bold black block capitals on yellow with heavy outlined panels. Yellow and black pairing repeats throughout, rugged and stackable in feel.
In the style of Milwaukee Tool, aggressive, rugged, masculine and industrial. Signature bright red plastic with black rubberised grips and black blocks, bold stripes of red against black, tough angular shapes with big chunky proportions and reinforced rubber guards. Lettering is large white or black block capitals in a heavy italic sans-serif. Metal parts are raw silver steel. Red and black combine with sturdy, heavy-duty detailing.
In the style of Fiskars, functional, friendly, clean and instantly recognisable, with a single hot orange accent. Bright orange moulded plastic in smooth, open, rounded loops, paired with silver stainless steel with a satin finish, joined by flat black or orange pivots. Grips are ergonomic, with soft thumb curves and a slightly textured inner surface, black contrast parts, clean tapers and a sleek shape ending in razor-bright metal. Fine laser-etched lettering in a clean sans-serif. Orange and black used together.
