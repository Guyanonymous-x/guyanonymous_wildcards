In the style of Starbucks, welcoming neighbourhood warmth in deep siren green and creamy white. A round emblem of a crowned, twin-tailed maiden sits inside a ring of capital lettering, shown as a clean green and white roundel, often cropped large or reduced to a simple line mark. Palette is forest green, cream, espresso brown and black, with seasonal bursts of warm orange, red, pink and lilac in flat geometric patterns, leaves, beans and swirls. Materials are matte paper, kraft cardboard, ceramic, brushed metal and green fabric, finished in clean minimal layouts. Lettering is rounded, friendly sans-serif with small handwritten touches. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
