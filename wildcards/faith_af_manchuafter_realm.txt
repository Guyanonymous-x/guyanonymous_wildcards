above the house the clan ancestors ride on clouds, men and women in their fifties to eighties in Qing dynasty robes and fur hats, the animal spirits of the clan beside them, a tiger, a bear, an eagle and a wolf
below the earth the Nishan Shaman, a widow in her thirties in her shaman's skirt and antlered crown, crosses a dark river on her flat drum toward the city of Ilmun Han, lord of the dead, to bring back a stolen soul
the land of the clan dead lies along a river in the northern forest, rows of log houses under birch and pine, the ancestors in their sixties and seventies hunting, fishing and riding as they did in life
