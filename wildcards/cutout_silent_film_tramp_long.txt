a life-size cardboard cutout of a silent film comedian dressed as a tramp. The figure is printed on a flat sheet of corrugated cardboard, cut out around its outline with a thin white border at the edge. The printed man is small and slim, with a short square black mustache under his nose, dark arched eyebrows and a pale face. He wears a black bowler hat, a tight black jacket buttoned too small, and a thin bamboo cane hooked over one arm. His baggy dark trousers sag at the knees and pool over huge worn shoes turned out at the toes, a crooked bow tie sits at his collar, and he tips his hat with one hand, his feet set in a wide duck stance.
