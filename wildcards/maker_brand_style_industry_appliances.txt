In the style of Smeg, Italian retro kitchen appliances. Curvaceous, rounded rectangular refrigerators, kettles and toasters in glossy pastel enamel colours such as cream, pastel blue, pink, mint green and red, with polished chrome handles, trim and rounded corners. Large thick metal rounded door edges recall 1950s American cars, with a bold chrome oval logo on the front. Controls are chunky chrome knobs and rounded toggles. Surfaces are shiny and seamless with a soft sheen. Mood is cheerful, nostalgic and kitchen-boutique fashionable.
In the style of KitchenAid, American stand mixers. A heavy cast metal body with a smooth curved bulbous top, a tilting head that pivots on a rear hinge, and a tall flat-bottomed steel or glass bowl with a curved handle. Glossy enamel in empire red, onyx black, white, pistachio, pastel blue and cream, with a polished chrome speed lever on the side and a round attachment hub at the front. Beaters and dough hooks in brushed silver. Stainless script lettering on the front. Mood is sturdy, generous and homey with a vintage curve.
In the style of Dyson, British high-tech household machines. Transparent cyclone chambers in clear plastic reveal the inner mechanics, set against bright coloured plastic parts in purple, fuchsia, yellow, nickel grey and iron. Parts are tubular and ribbed, with exposed engineering, smooth curved plastic moulding, visible fixings and large chunky wheels. Bladeless fans are thin glossy rings on a tall tapering stand. Small colour-coded buttons and tiny LCD readouts. Lettering is small and bold. Mood is engineered, futuristic, glossy and rather industrial.
In the style of Zojirushi, Japanese kitchen appliances and vacuum flasks with a calm, trustworthy domestic look. Rice cookers are plump rounded boxes or domes with soft corners, in cream white, pearl grey or brushed stainless steel with a single dark glass or black panel carrying a small pale green backlit display and simple rounded buttons. Vacuum bottles are slim cylinders in satin stainless steel or soft pastel enamel with a flip top push button lid, a sturdy folding carry handle and a small shoulder strap. Hot water dispensers are tall tanks with a smooth glossy body and a neat spout lever. The company mark is a small side view elephant silhouette beside a red or black wordmark in friendly rounded letters. Surfaces are matte or satin, never flashy, with fine seams and tidy rubber gaskets. Instruction icons are tiny line pictograms. Mood is gentle, practical, clean and quietly cheerful, with the reassuring weight of well made kitchen equipment.
In the style of SodaStream, home sparkling water makers. The machine is a small tabletop appliance with a slim upright column in black, white or brushed steel, a plain rounded top head with a clear push lever or button, and a flat base that holds a screw-in reusable carbonating bottle. The bottle is a clear or tinted tall plastic or glass flask with a slim neck and a screw cap, labelled with a fill line, and often paired with a coloured accent such as red, blue, green or bright orange. Gas cylinders are slender gunmetal grey or lime green steel tanks with a small collar. Newer minimalist models in matte steel have a clean pared back form with a single dial. Flavour syrups come in bright tall bottles with flat labels in juicy colours. Photographs feature bubbles rising in glasses, fruit slices, ice and a bright white kitchen counter. Mood is clean, modern, eco-minded, fresh and friendly.
In the style of Fisher and Paykel, New Zealand domestic appliances. Washing machines and fridges are clean, boxy, rounded-corner white enamel cabinets, from the 1950s through the 1970s with a thin chrome strip, a simple round dial and a stylised metal badge. Later designs are bright white or stainless steel with a slim control panel, a smooth top, large soft-touch rounded dials, a gentle blue or green backlit display and neat vertical or horizontal handles of brushed steel. The signature is the double-drawer dishwasher, two slim stainless steel drawers stacked in a cabinet, with a recessed handle and a narrow screen. Ranges are plain stainless steel with big oven doors in black glass, chunky round knobs in silver and a plain, modern seamless profile. The mood is practical, quiet, friendly and design-led, in white, silver and grey with minimal ornament.
