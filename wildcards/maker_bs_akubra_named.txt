In the style of Akubra, soft rabbit fur felt shaped with a wide, flat or slightly drooping edge, a tall form with a centre dent and two pinched side dents, and a plain leather or ribbon band with a bound edge. Colours are fawn, tan, cream, grey, olive, brown and black, with a pale ribbon accent. The surface is soft, slightly fuzzy, matte and suede-like, with sweat marks and creases developing with wear. Practical, rugged, sun-baked countryside mood, against red earth, eucalyptus and wide skies. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
