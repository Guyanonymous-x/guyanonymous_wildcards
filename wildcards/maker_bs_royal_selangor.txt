Styled in a design language of polished silvery grey pewter with a soft lustrous gleam and either smooth or hammered finish. Heavy-bottomed, rounded forms with simple curves, sometimes mixed with dark wood or leather accents. Engraved or embossed decoration of hibiscus flowers, bamboo, batik-like flowers and rope borders. Cool, bright, refined, with a tropical colonial elegance. Surfaces are clean and tactile, with ornament kept shallow and restrained against large plain areas of polished metal. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
