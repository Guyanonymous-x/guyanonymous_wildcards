The fixed day, which is the week's anchor and is known across the camp to the hour
An hour before, a queue already forming at a barrier that is not yet up
Two in the afternoon, the van in and the table going up in front of people who are watching it being done
The first twenty minutes, when the pre-packed bags go and the transaction is quick and almost wordless
The middle hour, when the sorted crates are picked over and the conversations happen, which is the part the volunteers value and the funder does not count
The last twenty minutes, when what is left is the box of sizes nobody takes
The close, the tally signed, the barriers down and the parking lot empty within ten minutes; and the six days afterward, when the table is not there and the camp's edge is just an edge
