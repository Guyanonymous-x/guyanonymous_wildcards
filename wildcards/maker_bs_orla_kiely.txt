In the style of Orla Kiely, repeating retro 1960s inspired motifs such as the stem pattern of oval leaves on a thin stalk, multi petal flowers and acorns, in warm palette of orange, brown, mustard, olive, teal and cream, printed on laminated cloth bags with tan leather trim, and also on ceramics, wallpaper and dresses. Neat grid layout, geometric nature, flat colour, nostalgic mid century Scandinavian look.
