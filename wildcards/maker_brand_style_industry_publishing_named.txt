A tiny emblem of a dolphin coiled around an anchor appears in black ink on cream laid paper. Surfaces are soft vellum or brown leather with blind tooling and gilt edges. The look is elegant, humanist, scholarly and compact, with quiet, evenly inked colour.
In the style of Plantin Press, dense. Black ink sits on warm cream laid paper, with decorative headpieces. Arrangements are symmetrical and orderly. Surfaces are tooled calf or vellum. The look is rich, rational, ornate and humanist.
Each page is framed by luxuriant woodcut borders of winding acanthus, vines, flowers, leaves and fruit, white on black, interlaced thickly. Soft vellum and silk ties add tactile richness. The mood is medieval, rich and romantic.
In the style of Doves Press, austere and elegant compositions with no illustration and no border. Surfaces are limp vellum or morocco with a simple gilt line pattern. The mood is pure, calm, disciplined and typographic.
In the style of Elzevir Press, tiny pocket-sized proportions with neat. Paper is white to cream, and surfaces are plain vellum or brown calf with gilt tooling and small marbled patterns. The mood is compact, practical, tidy and scholarly.
In the style of Penguin Books, a rigorous three-band layout with a central white panel framed by two coloured stripes in orange, green and blue. Later work uses a Swiss-style grid with bold illustration. Mid-century uncoated paper feel, matte printing and crisp rules.
In the style of Taschen, bold, oversized and confidently sensual graphic design with a gallery-grade polish. Forms are large clean rectangles with crisp square edges and generous margins. Surfaces are thick matte or satin boards, linen, leatherette and glossy printed art, with foil stamping and embossing used sparingly. Palette is rich saturated colour, a single flat field of deep red, jet black, cream or electric yellow, set beside vivid full-bleed imagery of strong contrast.
