Styled in a design language of witty surrealist couture where familiar things turn into clever visual jokes. Shocking hot pink is the signature colour, set against black, white, deep red, gold and rich jewel tones. Surfaces glitter with dense gold embroidery, faceted crystal clusters in pink, ruby and smoky grey, glossy lava glass and sculptural gilded metal in the shape of flowers, insects, lobsters, eyes, lips and anatomical forms. Silhouettes are sharp and structured, with strong shoulders and exaggerated, theatrical shapes. Optical illusion tricks make drawn or embroidered details mimic real ones. Ornaments are cast, enamelled and boldly oversized. Daring, artistic, glamorous, with a playful sense of the unexpected and a lavish gold flourish. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
