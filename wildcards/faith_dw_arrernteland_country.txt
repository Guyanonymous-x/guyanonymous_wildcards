the ridges and hills of Mparntwe are the Yeperenye, the great caterpillar ancestors, their long humped bodies forming the red ranges and gaps around the town, painted with stripes of white and red on their backs
at Anthwerrke, the narrow gap in the ranges now called Emily Gap, the caterpillar ancestors are painted on the rock face in tall stripes of red ochre and white, and a pale sandy creek bed runs through between the red walls
the wild dog ancestor lies in the land at Mparntwe as a great red rock and a line of hills, his tracks and camps shown as circles and lines across the country around him
a waterhole in a red rock gorge of the MacDonnell Ranges is the resting place of an ancestral being, deep green water reflecting the sky, ghost gums and river red gums growing at its edge
