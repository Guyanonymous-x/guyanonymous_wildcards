In the style of Asics, Japanese performance running and court shoes of the 1970s and 1980s. The upper is a mix of smooth suede and light nylon mesh panels, sleek and low, with a slightly flared heel tab and a gently curved toe cap. Two crossed diagonal stripes in a contrasting colour run along the side of the shoe, usually white on navy, or red, yellow and blue on pale grey, with a thin scalloped overlay near the laces. Midsoles are thick wedges of cream or white foam, sometimes with a visible gel window or a ribbed heel, over a crepe or waffle patterned gum rubber outsole. Colourways are earthy and sporty, such as cream with burgundy, navy with sky blue, or grey with orange and lime. A small chunky italic wordmark in white sits on the tongue and heel, with an olympic-era retro feel. Mood is athletic, light, vintage, understated and slightly nerdy-cool.
