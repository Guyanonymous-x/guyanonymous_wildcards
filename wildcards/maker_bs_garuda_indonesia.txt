Styled in a design language of gracious, calm, tropical, island-like identity in deep blue and aqua. Clean white grounds with a swooping blue and turquoise stripe sweeping into solid royal blue, carrying a stylised bird emblem made from five bent lines like a spreading wing in aqua and white. An earlier scheme is red and white with a bright orange stripe and a triangular bird. Soft blue upholstery, batik patterns in brown and indigo, wood panels and gentle warm light. Fitted batik with a flower in the hair. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
