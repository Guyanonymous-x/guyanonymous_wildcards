In the style of Mulberry, British country-house heritage in rugged yet polished leather. Glossy or naturally grained hide in oak brown, chocolate, oxblood and navy is shaped into sturdy, practical, generous forms. Solid brass fittings and studs are used sparingly, with contrast stitching and a small tree emblem embossed in gold. Surfaces are meant to age and soften with use, gaining a warm patina. Traditional, durable, countryside, confident and quietly rich.
