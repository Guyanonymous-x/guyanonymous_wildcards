Styled in a design language of passionate, theatrical Italian speed. Long, low, wedge-sharp or voluptuous sculpted forms with sensual curves, flowing fenders, sharp creases, wide stance and dramatic intakes. Deep, glossy rosso red dominates, joined by yellow, black, ivory and silver. Surfaces are lacquer-bright with chrome, carbon, polished metal and tan leather. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
