Styled in a design language of modernist flat 2D look with minimal geometric characters made of simple shapes, thin line work, little or no shading and large blank backgrounds in single flat colours. Witty visual metaphors, abstract pop-art colour blocks in red, yellow, black and sky blue, jazzy off-balance compositions and a sophisticated deadpan, urbane 1960s graphic-design look. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
