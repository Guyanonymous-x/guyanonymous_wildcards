Styled in a design language of confident Japanese audio-era branding. Palette is brushed silver, matte black, champagne gold with accents of vermilion red, orange and amber, in strong blocky colour contrast on labels and printed graphics. Finishes are thick brushed aluminium, glossy black lacquer, smoked acrylic and engraved fine scale lines. Forms are wide, flat, low and balanced with calm horizontal emphasis, precise rectangles and slim rounded corners, serious, weighty and polished. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
