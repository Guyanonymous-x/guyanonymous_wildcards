In the style of FSO Syrena, small, upright, rounded, modest, cute and period-correct socialist-era design. A smooth compact body with a bulbous front, softly bulging separate haunches, a high slim roofline and small round lights flush with the surface. Thin chrome trim and a few horizontal chrome bars, with one thin chrome line along the flank. Later versions turn squarer with larger lights and bigger glass. Colour is plain glossy pastel, cream, sky blue, pistachio, pale grey, light red and mustard. Details are simple and metallic, with generous thin circular forms. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words FSO Syrena in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
