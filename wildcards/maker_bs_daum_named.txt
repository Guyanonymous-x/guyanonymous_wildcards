Styled in a design language of French Art Nouveau layered glass, thick and soft, with acid-etched and wheel-carved decoration of thistles, poppies, irises, mountain landscapes and wintry scenes. Colours are mottled and graded from smoky orange to deep green, milky mist, powder blue and amber, with enamelled and gilded highlights. Naturalistic plant forms swell around bulbous bodies. Soft translucence, dreamy, rich, with a subdued glow like looking through frosted weather. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
