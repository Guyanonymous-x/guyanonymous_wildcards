Styled in a design language of stately, hushed, old-money American luxury of the 1930s and 40s. Tall, narrow, pointed-shield shapes with fine vertical bars, very long masses and sweeping separate curved wing forms. Deep gloss black, maroon, dark green and cream with a thin coach line. Walnut veneer, mohair or leather, elegant engine-turned metal, whitewall roundness and a chrome flying ornament. Poised, quiet and dignified. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
