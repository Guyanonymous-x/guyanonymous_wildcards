In the style of Bechstein, concert and salon grand pianos in glossy ebony black, with a classic Germanic design. Curved cases have a sharply defined rim, bold Jugendstil carved legs or plain tapering legs, and a gold-leaf painted decoration on historic art cases showing Neo-classical or Art Nouveau scenes in muted colours. The lid is large and the music desk is carved. Gold-toned lettering marks the fallboard. Alternative woods include rosewood, walnut and burr maple with warm mellow tones. The mood is cultured, stately, ornate, and drawing-room elegant.
