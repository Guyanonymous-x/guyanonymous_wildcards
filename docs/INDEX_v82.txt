Family mixes: af_sculpt_prompt, af_craft_prompt, af_fibre_prompt, af_render_prompt (each also _group_prompt and _short). All-family mixes: af_prompt, af_group_prompt, af_short, af_forms.
Shared per family: af_<family>_display, af_<family>_light, af_<family>_shot; af_render_marks; af_marks (plaque and label text in quotes).
