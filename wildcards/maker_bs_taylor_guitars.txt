Styled in a design language of modern California craftsmanship, clean, bright and precise. Wood is light, finely grained and blond, with koa, sapele and rosewood in rich reddish-brown for contrast. Finishes are very thin, flawless and glossy, with smooth rounded bevelled edges and a sleek, slim, contemporary silhouette. Inlay is minimal, with slender abalone and mother-of-pearl in organic shapes of birds, feathers, vines and leaves, with fine black and cream edge lines. Lettering is a warm, rounded serif wordmark in gold or cream on a red or brown oval badge, with a curved arc of small capitals above it. Palette is honey, caramel, cream, chestnut and burgundy. Friendly, polished and refined. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
