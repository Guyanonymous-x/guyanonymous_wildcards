In the style of Pilot Namiki Maki-e Pens, slim lacquered fountain pens with a glossy black or red urushi ground decorated with delicate gold, silver and coloured powder. Designs show tiny cranes, dragonflies, bamboo, plum blossom, waves and mountains in raised, sparkling gold with soft gradations, often covering the whole barrel and cap. The metal nib is gold, with fine engraved scrolls, and fittings are gold or rhodium. The forms are smooth and elegant, with rounded ends. The pen comes in a box of paulownia wood. The mood is quiet luxury, patient craft and nature-inspired.
