Styled in a design language of a rigorous three-band layout with a central white panel framed by two coloured stripes in orange, green and blue. Title and author are set in clean sans-serif or classic serif inside the white band, with a small bird mark at the bottom. Later work uses a Swiss-style grid with bold illustration. Mid-century uncoated paper feel, matte printing and crisp rules. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
