Styled in a design language of bold German technical-graphics identity. Printed graphics and labels use vivid orange, ultramarine, black and cream with striking stylised geometric imagery. Materials are polished walnut and beech veneer, chrome-plated metal, cream enamel, bakelite and enamelled badge plates with raised edges. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
