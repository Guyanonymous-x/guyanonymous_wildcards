Styled in a design language of sensuous, artistic, aristocratic art deco sculpture. Flowing swept curves, teardrop and horseshoe outlines, fine parallel louvre lines and elegant tapering forms with a polished metal spine. A small red oval badge with white capital lettering and a ring of tiny dots serves as the mark. Palette is French racing blue, black over red, deep cream, ivory and two-tone schemes. Finishes are nickel-polished metal, engine-turned aluminium in tight repeating swirls, soft red or brown leather and deep lacquer. Lettering is tall, narrow, stylised deco capitals in vertical stacks. Geometric sunburst and stepped motifs appear on flat graphic surfaces. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
