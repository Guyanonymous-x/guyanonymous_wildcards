in Kay Pacha between the two worlds the mallqui, the dried bodies of the dead, sit bundled upright in fine cloth with feathered headdresses, carried in procession through the stone streets of Cusco
on the mountain slope a puma of gold stands for this world, Kay Pacha, and the stone terraces, the green fields of potatoes and the fitted stone walls of Cusco spread below it
a priest in his fifties in a red tunic pours chicha from a gold cup onto the earth for the dead, a llama of pure white standing beside him
