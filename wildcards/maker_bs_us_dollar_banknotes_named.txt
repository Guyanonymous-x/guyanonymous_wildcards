In the style of US dollar banknotes, rectangular paper printed in black and green ink on cream cotton stock, with a central oval portrait of a statesman engraved in dense fine line work, ornate guilloche rosettes in each corner, scrollwork borders and a heavy numeral in the corners. A circular green seal with a ring of text, a black seal, serial numbers in green and black, a blackletter heading, microprinting and fine engraving, and on the reverse a green building vignette framed by a lace-like decorative border. Newer versions add pastel peach, blue or yellow tints and a colour-shifting numeral. Traditional engraving, banking authority and tradition. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words US dollar banknotes in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
