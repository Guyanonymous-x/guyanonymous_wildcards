Styled in a design language of bold beadwork patterns turned into jacquard knit, with sharp geometric bands of triangles, diamonds, chevrons, zigzags, stepped blocks and horizontal stripes. Colours are vibrant and contrasting, such as red, orange, mustard, cobalt blue, emerald, turquoise, pink and black against cream or deep neutral grounds, in rich saturated blocks. Materials are chunky mohair, merino wool and soft cotton. Patterns repeat symmetrically like beaded collars. Warm, proud, celebratory, heritage-meets-contemporary mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
