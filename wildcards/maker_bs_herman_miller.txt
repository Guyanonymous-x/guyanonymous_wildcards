Styled in a design language of American mid-century modern design with molded plywood and fibreglass in organic curves, perched on thin wire struts or splayed wooden dowel legs with metal cross braces. Colours are warm walnut, ash and rosewood veneer, black leather with plump cushioning, and bright pops of orange, mustard yellow, turquoise and white. Egg, kidney and bubble shapes, slatted surfaces, exposed joints and brass or chrome hardware. Playful, optimistic, sunlit and honestly constructed, with later graphite skeletal frames and mesh. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
