Styled in a design language of glossy ebony black with a classic Germanic design. Curved forms have a sharply defined edge, bold Jugendstil carved supports or plain tapering ones, and gold-leaf painted decoration on historic art pieces showing Neo-classical or Art Nouveau scenes in muted colours. Proportions are large and details are carved. Alternative woods include rosewood, walnut and burr maple with warm mellow tones. The mood is cultured, stately, ornate and drawing-room elegant. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
