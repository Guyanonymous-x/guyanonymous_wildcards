above the last heaven opens the Pleroma, a boundless field of white and gold light, rows of shining figures of the aeons, robed men and women in their thirties, standing in pairs to welcome the soul home
far above, the hidden Father is shown only as a vast still light beyond every ring of the heavens, the four great lights and Barbelo, a tall robed woman of light in her thirties, standing before it
in the Pleroma Sophia, a tall woman in her thirties robed in pale blue light, kneels and is lifted by the Savior, a robed man of light in his thirties, the returning souls gathering around them
