Styled in a design language of orderly, polite, spacious, corporate and sky-high, with cool blue colours and a clean white body under a cloudless sky. White lower areas and a sky blue upper stripe over a wide silver-grey line, with a big round emblem of swirling red and blue halves twisting around each other. Light blue and white uniforms with neat scarves and pale gloves. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
