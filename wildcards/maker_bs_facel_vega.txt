Styled in a design language of long, low, horizontal masses built from stacked pairs of vertical forms, very thin slender supports and generous polished chrome edging with a crisp, flat, squared-off finish. Palette of black, silver, bright red, cream and deep blue. Supple leather, polished metal fittings, fine wire-like patterns and white-faced round graphics. Opulent yet athletic, an expensive and slightly dangerous elegance. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
