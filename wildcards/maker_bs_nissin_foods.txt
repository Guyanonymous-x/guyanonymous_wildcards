In the style of Nissin Foods, instant noodle packaging of the 1970s and 1980s. Noodles come in a tall wide-mouthed disposable cup of foamed white polystyrene with a printed paper wrap in bold red, yellow and white, big italic chunky lettering in red outlined in white or black, and a bright photo or illustration of steaming noodles with shrimp, egg and green spring onion. A peel back foil lid in silver or printed paper is held down by a thin tab. Bags use shiny metallic film with stripes of red and yellow, thick sans serif titles slanting upward and a round or oval window showing the golden crimped noodle block. A square block of curly fried noodles sits on a plain table beside a plastic fork. Colours are saturated, appetising and slightly garish, using hot red, mustard yellow, black and white with starburst flashes saying new or tasty. Mood is cheap, fast, fun and nostalgic, evoking late night snacks and convenience-store shelves.
