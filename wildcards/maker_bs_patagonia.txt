Styled in a design language of honest outdoor utility with an earthy, conscientious spirit. Functional, roomy, rounded shapes with practical seams, tough stitching and unpretentious proportions. Colour blocking uses bright primaries such as red, royal blue, yellow and teal against natural fawn, forest green, rust and navy, often with vintage faded tones. Textures are brushed fleece, ripstop, canvas, wool and recycled fabric with a soft worn look. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
