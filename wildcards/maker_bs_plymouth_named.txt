Styled in a design language of bold and cheeky American design from the late 1950s through the 1960s. Wide, flat, low proportions with long sculpted sides, a sweeping two-tone contrast line and bright chrome bars running the full width. Sharp, tall, blade-like projections slanting backward give a jet-age thrust, while loud graphic decals and a cartoon character add cheek. Saturated purple, vivid orange, lemon yellow and bright green against shiny metal. Confident, playful, exaggerated and optimistic, with a love of speed and showmanship. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
