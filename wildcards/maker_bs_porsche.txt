Styled in a design language of athletic, low and compact, design driven by function. Smooth, rounded, sculpted forms with a sloping, curved profile, soft arching haunches, round pairs of lamps and clean, taut surfaces with minimal trim. Finishes are glossy paint, satin black, brushed aluminium, titanium and carbon weave in silver, black, graphite, white, guards red and deep blue, with occasional bright accent colours. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
