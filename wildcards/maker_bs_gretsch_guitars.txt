In the style of Gretsch Guitars, large hollow-body electric guitars with a flamboyant orange or deep red-brown lacquer finish, a gold-plated pickguard and bold decoration. Details include twin cutaways, gold sparkle or white binding, a G-shaped brand cutout on the body, a large V-shaped tailpiece in gold, and a tall headstock with a wide shape. Hardware is flashy, with chrome, gold, thumbscrew plates and tall knobs. Pickups sit in gold-plated surrounds. Alternate finishes include white falcon pearl, two-tone green and sparkle. The mood is rock and roll, showy, rockabilly and retro.
