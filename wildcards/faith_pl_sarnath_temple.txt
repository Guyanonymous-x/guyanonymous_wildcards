the Mulagandhakuti Vihara, a modern temple with a tall tower, a Buddhist temple of pale pinkish-buff sandstone built in 1931, with a high tapering central tower ringed by small spires, a porch with carved pillars at its entrance, a large bronze bell hanging in front and a straight path through gardens leading to its door
inside the temple, painted murals of the Buddha's life, tall walls covered from end to end with soft-colored scenes painted by a Japanese artist, the Buddha in robes of orange and gold seated under a tree, crowds of listeners, demons, deer and palaces in muted greens, blues and earth reds, above a polished stone floor
a golden Buddha statue in the temple, a life-sized gilded figure seated cross-legged on a marble altar, both hands raised before the chest in the teaching gesture, a calm downcast face and a halo behind the head, with rows of flower offerings, candles and small brass bowls of water before it
