Styled in a design language of brutal, loud, cartoonish tough-guy American muscle of the early 1970s. Wide aggressive stance, a long flat mass with bulging twin humps, squared-off edges with a pronounced hip, a bold bumblebee band and an abrupt ducktail kick at the end. Slotted metal textures, black vinyl and chunky proportions. High-impact violet, lime green, purple, bright orange and pale green, with red stripes. Swaggering, noisy and showy.
