Styled in a design language of plain handmade rectilinear construction in oak and hammered copper. Wood has square, sturdy members, exposed joinery, flat slats and a warm medium-brown fumed finish with visible grain. Copper shows hammer marks, rivets, strap-like fittings and a deep brown or warm penny-red patina. The mood is simple, honest, rustic and monastic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
