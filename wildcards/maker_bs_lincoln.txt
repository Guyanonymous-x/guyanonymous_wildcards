Styled in a design language of understated, sober, expensive American flagship luxury of the 1960s. Slab-sided, perfectly flat surfaces, a thin knife-edge horizontal line, long level forms and a square, formal outline. A four-pointed star set within a plain rectangle or a round enamel plaque is the repeated mark, in chrome, navy and black. Minimal chrome comes in a thin shoulder strip, slim vertical forms and fine horizontal bars. Brushed metal and deep leather give grave dignity. Palette is black, midnight blue, silver, white and a touch of gold. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
