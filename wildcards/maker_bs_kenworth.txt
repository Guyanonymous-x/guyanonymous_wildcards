Styled in a design language of proud, polished, long-nosed American heavy-duty highway presence. Tall, upright, mirror-bright chrome faces, bold chrome edge strips and heavy chrome bars set against deep two-tone paint split by a confident stripe. Round lamps, tall stainless columns and polished aluminium accents. Proportions are long, flat and roomy with a commanding stance. Everything is gleaming, mirrored and meticulously cared for, bold and confident, king-of-the-road. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
