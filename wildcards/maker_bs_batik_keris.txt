In the style of Batik Keris, Indonesian batik cloth and clothing, with wax-resist dyed cotton and silk in dense repeating patterns of parang diagonal waves, kawung overlapping circles, mega mendung cloud swirls, floral sprays, birds and trailing vines. Typical colours are warm sogan brown, deep indigo, dark black, cream and golden yellow, plus brighter coastal tones in red, turquoise and green. Fine crackle lines show where the wax cracked, and slight irregularities reveal the hand-drawn canting pen or copper stamp. Used for long-sleeved batik shirts, sarongs, kebaya blouses, dresses and table linen. Formal, refined, ceremonial Javanese mood, shown draped on bamboo racks.
