In the style of Star Ferry, leisurely, nostalgic, salty and civic harbour charm. Low, broad forms with rounded overhanging fronts, a white upper level and thin white railing. Deep bottle green below clean white, with a gold rubbing line, black cap, brass fittings and cream rings. Names in white Latin letters and Chinese characters. Creamy paint, varnished timber and round porthole shapes. Skyscrapers behind, hazy sky and dark teal water.
