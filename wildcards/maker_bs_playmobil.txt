Styled in a design language of small hard plastic forms with oversized round shapes, simple dot marks, a curved smile line, C-shaped curves and standardised blocky, stiffly jointed construction. Bright primary red, yellow, blue and green plus brown, grey and skin tones in a matte to satin finish, with clean moulded seams and thin moulded details. Everything is simplified into cheerful, friendly, clip-together pieces with tiny printed faces and flat painted patterns. The logo is a rounded, lowercase, bold sans-serif wordmark in blue with a smiling curve above it, on a bright yellow or blue graphic field. Packaging uses clear windows and bold colour panels in a cheerful, wholesome tone. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
