British students' union bars, run by a separate charitable union with a trading arm, which is a distinctive constitutional arrangement and produces a distinctive room
Irish equivalents, similar
American campus pubs and student centers, where lower drinking ages do not apply and the room is correspondingly a cafe or a games room rather than a bar, which is the largest single international difference in this record
Australian university bars, closer to the British model
continental European student *Mensa* and *Studentenkneipe* arrangements, often run by student associations and markedly cheaper
institutions in countries where alcohol is not sold, where the equivalent social room is a cafe and is often better used
