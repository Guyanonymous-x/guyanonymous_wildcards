Styled in a design language of flat, symmetrical, uniform and instantly legible. A bold red upper field meets a clean white lower field with a thin gold line between them, and a round gold medallion seal with a fine embossed ring sits dead centre. Colour is applied in broad flat blocks with no gradients, in red, white and gold, with occasional pastel or deep green variations of the same layout. Finish is satin printed metal and smooth coated paper, with identical elements repeated in tidy grids. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
