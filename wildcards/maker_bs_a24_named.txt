Styled in a design language of minimalist, a stark centred or off-balance photograph, soft grainy film texture and pastel or sun-bleached colour or deep moody darkness. Symmetrical compositions, wide-angle lenses, naturalistic light, surreal touches and an arthouse, intimate, slightly uncanny indie look. Plenty of calm negative space, deadpan framing and subdued styling keep the mood quirky, tender and a little unsettling. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
