Styled in a design language of fiery red and deep green heritage built around a diamond-shaped emblem. Patterns repeat diamonds and fine lines edge to edge in dense tiles. Palette is hot red, forest green, cream and gold, with bright orange, yellow and teal used as colour-coded variant accents. Surfaces are glossy enamel, tinted glass and crisp printed paper with a vintage stamp feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
