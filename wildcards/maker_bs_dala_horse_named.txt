Styled in a design language of simple, friendly, folk-rustic and unmistakably Scandinavian. Small stout carved wooden forms in simple flat planes, with a thick arched curve, notched edges, sturdy legs and a bulky chest. Body painted glossy deep red or orange-red, sometimes blue, black, white or natural. Trim is decorated freehand with flower scroll patterns in white, green, yellow and blue, painted quickly in loops, petals and dots in a flower and swirl vocabulary. The finish is smooth, shiny and lightly worn. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
