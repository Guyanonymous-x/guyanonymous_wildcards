In the style of Canon, 1970s and 1980s Japanese film cameras. Compact boxy SLR bodies with a black or silver top plate, wood-grain or fine black leatherette covering, rows of tiny metal dials and small silver buttons, and a red-lettered diagonal bar logo. Lens barrels are black with subtle knurling and a red ring accent around the front of professional lenses. Bodies have a clean rectangular layout with a large angular pentaprism, a round film-speed dial and a thick advance lever. Lettering is a stylised red script mark. Mood is dependable, practical and quietly handsome.
