Styled in a design language of blunt, comic, strong two-tone design that shouts for attention. A squat, round-shouldered, stout silhouette like a small cauldron, with a broad base, wide mouth and ridges at the shoulder. Glossy bright yellow fields meet dark navy or black bands, with deep red and brown accents. Lettering is chunky white serif capitals with a scrolled feel. Form is simple, heavy and instantly memorable. Mood is cheeky, confident, unapologetic and wonderfully recognisable, polarising yet beloved.
