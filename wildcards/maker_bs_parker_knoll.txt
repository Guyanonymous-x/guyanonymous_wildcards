In the style of Parker Knoll, British reclining wing-back armchairs with deep padded seat cushions, tall sloping high backs with ears, and shaped, polished walnut or mahogany arms with turned fronts, resting on short curving legs. Upholstery in floral chintz, tapestry weave, brocade, mustard velvet or green moquette, buttoned details and fringed or piped seams. Cosy, grandmotherly, comfortable, rocker-recliner mechanism hidden under a skirted base, traditional lounge by a gas fire.
