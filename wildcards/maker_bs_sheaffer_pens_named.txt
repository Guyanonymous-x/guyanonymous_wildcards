Styled in a design language of bulbous, torpedo-shaped forms in glossy striped celluloid colours, pearl and jade green, marine black, grey, burgundy and blue. Thin chrome or gold trim rings circle the body, and a distinctive inlaid white dot punctuates the top. Gold accents are long and triangular or hooded. Lines are straight, flat and sleek, with a spear-like taper. Surfaces are smooth, rounded, streamlined and glossy, in an Art Deco and early jet-age manner. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
