Styled in a design language of dramatic and theatrical American showmanship of the mid to late 1950s. A tall vertical oval like a pursed mouth, heavy chrome brows, sharply pointed upswept edges with arrow-shaped trim and one long sweeping chrome spear that splits two-tone paint. Hooded, wraparound forms, plush fabric and a bold sculpted panel. Pink and charcoal, turquoise and white, sunset orange. Flamboyant, confident and a little overblown. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
