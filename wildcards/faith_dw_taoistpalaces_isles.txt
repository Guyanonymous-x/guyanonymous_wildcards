out in the Eastern Sea rise the islands of Penglai, Fangzhang and Yingzhou, steep green peaks above white surf, golden palaces and terraces on their summits and white cranes flying around them
the Eight Immortals, adults from their twenties to their seventies, cross the sea toward Penglai, each riding the waves on a magic object, Zhang Guolao on a white donkey, Lü Dongbin with a sword on his back, He Xiangu, a woman in her twenties, holding a lotus
on Penglai grow trees of jewels and the herb of immortality, deer and cranes walk on lawns among pavilions of white jade, and a palace stands at the summit in a haze of colored cloud
