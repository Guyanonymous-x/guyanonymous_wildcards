Styled in a design language of large simple geometric blocks of solid-colour wool or cotton in deep, saturated tones. The palette is black, plum purple, royal blue, forest green, wine red, brown and dusty rose, with no printed patterns. Designs use a centre diamond, bars, stepped squares and nine-patch grids, framed by wide borders and bound in contrasting colour. Fine, dense stitching creates feathers, wreaths, cables and grids in the dark areas, giving a subtle relief. The mood is bold, austere, modern-looking and graphic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
