Styled in a design language of slim, retro, low-slung sporting design from mid-century Japan. Elongated narrow silhouettes with clean flat surfaces and a signature motif of crossing wavy parallel stripes in contrasting colour. Palette is cream and white grounds with accents of crimson, cobalt blue, mustard yellow, forest green, navy and black, often in tri-colour combinations on glossy lacquered or soft suede surfaces paired with fine mesh and tonal overlays. Tiny perforations, pale gum-coloured edging and neat contrast stitching add detail. Clean, nostalgic, athletic and compact. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
