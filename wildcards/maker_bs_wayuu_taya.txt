Styled in a design language of handmade, tropical, energetic and slightly raw design. Soft round-bottomed cylinder forms crocheted in a tight single-strand spiral so the fabric is dense and slightly stiff, with a flat circular base and a flat wide strap. A band in one solid colour contrasts with a body covered in large bold geometric designs. Patterns are repeating diamonds, zigzags, stepped squares, crosses, labyrinth-like meanders and stripes in thick yarn. The palette is joyful and high-contrast, hot pink, turquoise, lemon yellow, orange, red, royal blue and lime green against black, white or cream, often with a gradient or striped panel. Braided cords with large tassels, twisted rope, pompoms and visibly wavy lines and woollen texture. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
