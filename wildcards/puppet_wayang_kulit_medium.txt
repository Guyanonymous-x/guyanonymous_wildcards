Indonesian wayang kulit shadow puppets, flat figures of punched and painted leather on long rods, each about as tall as an adult's arm. Each figure is cut from thin stiff buffalo hide in profile, with a long thin nose, a narrow slanting eye and a slender neck bending forward, and the whole surface is punched through with hundreds of tiny holes in lace-fine patterns of curls and dots, so light shines through it. The figures are painted in gold, red, black and blue, the faces of noble heroes gold or black. __puppet_wayang_kulit_var__.
