Styled in a design language of cheerful, unpretentious, resilient and familiar, packed with colour on a dusty street. Rounded pressed-steel bodies with bulbous curves, vinyl-covered seating and chrome badges. Thick glossy enamel in sky blue, cream, off-white, rust red and olive green, with round chrome-rimmed lamps and stubby upright details. Tiny compact forms with canvas or sheet metal enclosures, open sides, lemon yellow and black or green and yellow pairings, painted slogans and plastic flower garlands. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
