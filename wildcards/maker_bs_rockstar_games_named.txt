Styled in a design language of gritty, lived-in, cinematic realism with a satirical edge. Sun-bleached streets, smoggy sunsets, a grainy handheld feel and heavy atmospheric haze. Compositions are organised as a grid of stylised comic-like panels with thick black outlines and flat saturated colour. Colour moods swing between dusty western earth tones and retro pastel neon. Raw, cynical, sweaty and sharply observed, with a worn print finish that feels like a slightly lurid, rebellious portrait of society. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
