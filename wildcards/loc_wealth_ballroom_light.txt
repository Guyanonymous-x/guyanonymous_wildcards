The brightest interior in the document by a wide margin and the only one designed around a measured quantity of light, ninety-six candles in two chandeliers and 128 more in sixteen sconces, all of it multiplied by four full-height mirrors, giving a flat brilliant light with almost no shadow anywhere above waist height, so faces are lit from every direction at once and nobody in the room has a bad side
The floor is lit well enough to show the chalk
The two chandeliers each throw a faint double shadow down and the wax falls in the two circles
By daylight the room is a disappointment and everyone says so, a pale over-tall space with 140 empty chairs and a hard north light showing every mark on the floor; and in the electrified version the same fittings carry lamps that produce a third of the light and none of the flicker, and the room has never looked right since
