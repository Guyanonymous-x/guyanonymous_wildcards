the Arkansas river, a wide slow river of brown-green water flowing between low banks of sand and red clay, long pale sandbars showing in mid-channel, willows and cottonwood trees leaning over the edges, driftwood logs stranded on the bars and a great blue heron wading in the shallows
bottomland woods, dense lowland forest on the flat river plain, tall oaks, pecans, sycamores with patchy white bark and cottonwoods rising over a tangle of cane, vines and saplings, the ground dark and damp with fallen leaves and pools of standing water reflecting the trunks
fields near the river, flat farmland of dark rich soil stretching away from the mounds, long rows of soybeans and corn, hay meadows with big round bales, wire fences, a red barn and a metal grain bin in the distance and a line of trees marking the riverbank
