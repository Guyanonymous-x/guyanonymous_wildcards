In the style of Spode, English transferware pottery with dense blue-and-white underglaze printing of an Italian landscape with a classical ruin, a bridge, a tower and trees, framed by a border of flowers and shell-like motifs. Cream-white earthenware plates, platters, tureens, teapots and jugs with intricately engraved line shading in deep indigo blue, also Chinese-style willow landscapes, floral chintz and red or green transfer variants. Cottage dresser display, charming and traditional with an old-world look.
