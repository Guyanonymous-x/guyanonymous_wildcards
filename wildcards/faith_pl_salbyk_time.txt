on the summer solstice, people gathered at sunrise, a crowd of adults standing together inside the stone wall and facing east as the red rim of the sun rises over the hills, some with arms raised, a small fire burning on the ground and the first light turning the slabs pink and gold
in winter, snow and the stones dark, the whole steppe a flat white sheet under a pale gray sky, drifts piled against the base of the wall, dry grass stalks poking through, and the tall slabs standing nearly black and bare above the snow
at sunset, the stones casting long shadows, the low orange sun shining between the slabs so their rough faces glow amber and rust on one side while dark bars of shadow stretch far across the golden grass, the sky deep blue above and peach at the horizon
