In the style of Lomonosov Porcelain, Russian Imperial porcelain, including its revolutionary 1920s Suprematist and agitprop work, white thin cups, plates and teapots with geometric abstract red, black and white shapes, hammer and sickle motifs, stars, factories, bold angular lettering, and stylised propaganda figures. Also the classic cobalt net pattern with gilded cell edges and white relief bumps. Striking graphic energy, flat bold colours, historical museum display.
