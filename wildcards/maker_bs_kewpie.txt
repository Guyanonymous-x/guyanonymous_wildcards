In the style of Kewpie, Japanese mayonnaise packaging built around a chubby baby doll mascot. The soft squeeze bottle is clear or translucent white plastic with a flexible body, a bright red screw cap and a star shaped nozzle opening that squirts fine ridged lines. Its label is mostly white with the red wordmark and a small round doll figure with a cheerful face, wide set eyes, rosy cheeks, plump rounded belly, tiny wings and a single curled tuft of hair on top, drawn in simple flat outline with pink and cream fill. Older glass jars have a tin lid and a paper label with the same doll in blue and red. Mesh bags of the doll shape show an orange red net lattice cross hatched pattern. Advertising uses pale yellow backgrounds, egg yolk golds and fresh vegetables arranged in neat circles. Mood is cute, wholesome, babyish and nostalgic, with soft round forms, a limited red, yellow and white palette and a clean modern food-photograph finish.
