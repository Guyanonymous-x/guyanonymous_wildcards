Styled in a design language of clever, friendly and precise Japanese engineering expressed through clean, light forms. Shapes are compact and tidy, with thin crisp edges, gently rounded corners, and sporty swept wedges that end in a neat squared cut. Palette is championship white, signal red, deep blue, silver and black, with red used as a small energetic accent. Surfaces are glossy enamel, brushed aluminium and matte plastic. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
