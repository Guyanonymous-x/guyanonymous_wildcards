In the style of Garuda Indonesia, an airline identity in deep blue and aqua. Aircraft have a clean white fuselage with a swooping blue and turquoise stripe along the belly sweeping into the tail, which is a solid royal blue fin carrying a stylised bird emblem formed from five bent lines, like a spreading wing, in aqua and white. The name is set in clean blue capitals in a wide, evenly spaced sans serif. Earlier liveries have a red and white scheme with a bright orange stripe along the windows and a triangular bird on the tail. Cabins use soft blue seats with batik pattern head covers in brown and indigo, wooden panels and gentle warm light. Crew wear fitted kebaya blouses in blue or brown batik with sarongs, and a delicate flower in the hair. Mood is tropical, gracious, calm, island-like and warm, with turquoise seas and green hills.
