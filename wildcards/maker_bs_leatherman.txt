Styled in a design language of tough, dense, precision-machined industrial design. Brushed and bead-blasted stainless steel, black oxide and matte black coatings are paired with a strong yellow or gold accent and occasional anodised colour in orange, blue or olive. Forms are compact, rectangular and softly bevelled, with rows of small perforated holes, crisp stamped edges, visible rivets and tight seams. Utilitarian, rugged and confident. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
