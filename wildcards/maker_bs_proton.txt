Styled in a design language of proud, modest, practical, optimistic national design of the 1980s and 1990s. Compact, boxy, upright volumes with flat sides, a straight shoulder line, slim chrome edging, rectangular shapes and plain black panels carrying a small badge. Emblems are a dark blue shield with a yellow crescent and star, or a yellow tiger head in a green roundel. Paint is solid gloss in white, red, silver, dark blue and champagne gold, with black plastic trim. Plain grey cloth textures. Sunlit and unpretentious. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
