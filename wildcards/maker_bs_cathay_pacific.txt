In the style of Cathay Pacific, a Hong Kong airline identity in green and white. Aircraft have a clean white fuselage with a thin jade green cheatline running under the windows, a dark green tail fin and a calligraphic brushwing emblem, a single stroke like a swept bird wing curving upward in white or pale green against the dark ground. The earlier version used a lettuce green and white two-tone with a bare metal belly and a small flag on the tail. Titles appear in an upright sans serif wordmark in dark green, with Chinese characters in a small simple script beside it. Cabins are carefully quiet, with warm timber toned panels, bottle green economy seats with pale head covers, flat beds and soft cream leather in business, and thin gold accents. Crew uniforms are crisp, in dark green, with a neat scarf and a small gold wing pin. Mood is calm, urbane, polished, glassy and travelled, set against the harbour skyline and clouds at dusk.
