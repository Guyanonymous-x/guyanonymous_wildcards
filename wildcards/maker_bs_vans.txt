Styled in a design language of laid-back California skater grit with a handmade, lived-in charm for any product. Thick cream-white glossy rubber surfaces with a fine waffle-like grid pattern, flat low rounded silhouettes and tall white edge bands, applied as trim or panels. Hard-wearing duck canvas and suede with chunky double stitching, scuffed and creased on purpose. Palette is navy, black, red, olive and off-white, with a small square black and white checkerboard as the signature repeat, plus wavy stripes and skull-and-crossbones doodles. Lettering is a bold, slanted, hand-painted style with a long swooping underline, set in a red rectangular tab or stacked on boxy red cardboard. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
