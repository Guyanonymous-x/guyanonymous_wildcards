Styled in a design language of hand-blown art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered under clear casing. Techniques create fused patches of colour like woven fabric, delicate lace threads, vertical ribbons, trapped bubbles and acid-etched satin surfaces. Forms feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean mid-century modern lines, polished edges and a tiny etched mark. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
