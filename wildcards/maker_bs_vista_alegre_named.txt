Styled in a design language of elegant, formal, light and slightly romantic Portuguese porcelain. Forms have scrolling handles, high feet and flower-shaped finials. Modern versions turn to bold graphic prints, hand-painted strokes in cobalt, black or coral and sculptural art editions. Traditional palette is white, gold, navy, rose pink and sage green. Tiny gilt crowns and symmetrical arrangements. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
