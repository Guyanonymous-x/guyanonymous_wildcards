Styled in a design language of a rich deep royal purple dominates every surface in a glossy, silky, velvety finish. Cream, white and warm gold act as accents, along with thin gold borders, curved swoosh lines and ribbon-like flowing shapes. Surfaces include metallic gold foil, satin paper and smooth lacquer with a soft highlight. Shapes are rounded, plump and generous, with gently curved corners and sweeping curves rather than angles. Decorative flourishes, swirls and subtle shimmer add a sense of treat and indulgence. The mood is warm, traditional, comforting and generous. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
