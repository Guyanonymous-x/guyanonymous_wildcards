Styled in a design language of bleak, decaying dark-fantasy atmosphere where beauty survives only as ruin. Crumbling gothic architecture, fog-filled distances, battered, tarnished metal, tattered cloth and grotesque, towering monstrous forms. Desaturated palette of ash grey, rust brown and sickly green, broken by a single shaft or glow of warm golden light. Sparse, moody lighting with deep shadow and heavy atmosphere. Intricate weathered detail on every surface. Melancholic, ominous, lonely and solemn, with a scale that makes the viewer feel small. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
