Styled in a design language of hand-hammered bronze with a bright warm gold sheen, circular and gently dished with a raised central swell. The surface shows concentric lathe grooves and dense dimpled hammer marks, with darker burnished tones in the recesses and polished highlights on the ridges. Older looks are dark, brown and dull gold, newer looks are brilliantly polished and mirror bright. Edges are thin and slightly wavy. Any marking is small, ornate and printed in black or gold ink. Warm, metallic, tactile and handmade.
