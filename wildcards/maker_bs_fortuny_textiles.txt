Styled in a design language of velvet and cotton surfaces densely stamped with metallic gold and silver pigment on deep, mottled backgrounds. Colours are rich and muted, aubergine, indigo, burnt sienna, olive, ochre, antique rose and dusty teal, with a hazy, layered, hand-printed look. Patterns are Renaissance pomegranates, arabesques, stylised animals, ogee lattices and medallions repeating in tight rows. A faint shimmer from the metal, a soft aged glow and a velvety hand give a rich, theatrical, exotic mood, lit by softly diffused light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
