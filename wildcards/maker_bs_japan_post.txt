In the style of Japan Post, a red mark of a stylised katakana character shaped like a letter T with a horizontal bar above, in a rounded square or circle, used on red post boxes, bright red delivery vans and motorbikes, uniforms and parcel boxes. Cylindrical red pillar post boxes with a rounded cap and a narrow horizontal slot, a white sign plate with the mark. Commemorative stamps in small vertical or horizontal formats with delicate woodblock print and watercolour scenes of cherry blossom, mountains, seasonal festivals and cute animal characters, thin black outlined frames and white sans text. Clean, cheerful, polite Japanese public service look, red and white with gold and soft pastel accents.
