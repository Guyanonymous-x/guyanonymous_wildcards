above the forest the ancestors of the lodge look down, chiefs in their sixties to nineties in long white or patterned gowns, red caps and coral bead necklaces, leopard skins over their shoulders, each holding a staff
the ancestors appear among the forest trees as faint glowing figures in their seventies, their faces marked with white chalk, the leopard spirit crouched among them, its spotted coat black on tawny gold
the newly dead chief, a man in his seventies, lies in state in the lodge on a bed spread with ukara cloth, dressed in a white shirt, a wrapper of blue and white cloth and a red cap, his staff beside him, members passing with gifts
