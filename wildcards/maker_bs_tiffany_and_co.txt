Styled in a design language of polished New York prestige built around one pale robin-egg turquoise blue. Smooth matte blue boxes and bags with crisp square corners are wrapped in white satin ribbon tied in a simple bow, and the blue appears as flat backgrounds, tinted glass and pastel surfaces in every setting. Metals are bright sterling silver, platinum and soft yellow gold, with clean sparkling stones in simple claw settings, chunky links, hammered texture and engraved leaf borders. Lettering is fine, wide-spaced serif capitals in black or silver. Photography is luminous, romantic and airy, with glossy reflections, soft glow and witty still-life arrangements of ordinary things in the signature blue. Elegant, clean and quietly exclusive. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
