on a flat rock stands a namkha, a frame of crossed sticks wound with diamond patterns of red, white, blue, green and yellow wool thread, as tall as a man's forearm, beside small dough cakes painted red and white
a fire of green juniper branches smolders on a stone altar, thick white smoke rolling up, while handfuls of roasted barley flour are thrown into it in pale clouds
milk and grains of barley are poured into a spring from a brass jug, and small clay figures of animals and people are set on the stones beside the water
