Styled in a design language of close-fitting fine soft merino knit, smooth, matte and slightly fuzzy, with flat seams and mountain-inspired minimalism. Colours are muted forest green, charcoal, deep navy, heather grey, rust, moss, stone and pale blue with occasional bright orange. Graphic touches include small sheep-like or mountain-line prints, subtle stripes and tiny sewn marks. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
