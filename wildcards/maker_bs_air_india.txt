Styled in a design language of warm, ornate, richly coloured design with a regal Indian character. Strong red and white colour blocking is paired with deep maroon, saffron, aubergine and burnished gold. A small arched motif drawn from ornamental scalloped arches and a gold circular emblem repeats as a graphic mark, often with fine curved lines that suggest a sun or a gateway frame. Surfaces are glossy lacquer and gold foil, with jewel-like borders, paisley, jali lattice and floral geometry as pattern. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
