Styled in a design language of large square twill silk printed with detailed painterly scenes, tile patterns, tulips, carnations, flower patterns, arabesques, waterside views and maritime and horse motifs, finished with hand-rolled edges. Colours are saturated cobalt, turquoise, coral red, emerald, gold and ivory, in dense multi-colour compositions bordered by a decorative frame. Elegant, cosmopolitan, orientalist-meets-modern mood, with rich satin sheen and gold chain edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
