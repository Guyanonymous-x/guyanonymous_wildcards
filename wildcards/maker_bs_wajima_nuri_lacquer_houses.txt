In the style of Wajima-nuri Lacquer Houses, bowls, trays, boxes and chopsticks with thick, glassy, flawless coats of black or deep vermilion urushi lacquer polished to a mirror depth. Surfaces are smooth, with a warm glow, and feel substantial. The foundation shows only in sturdy, rounded edges and neat foot rings. Decoration is restrained, with fine gold maki-e flowers, cranes and grasses, or inlaid chin-kin lines carved into the lacquer and filled with gold leaf. Red interiors contrast with black exteriors. The overall mood is formal, luxurious and calm, with crisp, perfectly even shapes.
