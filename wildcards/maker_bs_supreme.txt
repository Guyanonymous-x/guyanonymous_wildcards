In the style of Supreme, a flat rectangular box logo in white Futura Heavy Oblique italic capitals on a solid bright red field, used as a label, sticker, hoodie chest print and skateboard deck graphic. Heavy cotton hoodies, tees and caps in saturated red, black, white and occasional neon, with the box mark repeated or tiled as an all over pattern. Skate deck art uses collage of found photographs, crude photocopy halftones, high contrast black and white portraits, cartoon characters and art-gallery reproductions, always with the red rectangle in a corner. Crisp screen print ink, slightly cracked vinyl texture, bold thick outlines, deadpan minimal copy, New York downtown skate shop attitude, clean white retail interiors with stacked product and a hard edged street poster look.
