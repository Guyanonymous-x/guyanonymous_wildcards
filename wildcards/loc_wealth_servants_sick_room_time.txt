Seven, a housemaid up with coal and hot water, the fire lit, which is the first fire lit in the attic in a hundred years of that room's existence
Eight, breakfast on a tray, which is arrowroot or bread and milk
Mid-morning, ten minutes, the only authority who comes
Midday, beef tea
The afternoon, the room's long hours, one person in bed, a blind half down, the fire kept in by whoever passes, and two or three visits of five minutes each by women who have fourteen-hour days of their own
Four, the doctor if he has been sent for, which is a decision made downstairs and costs money, with the screen brought round
Evening, the fire made up, the candle lit, the bell cord within reach
The night, the fire banked, and the one person on that floor who can ring a bell and be answered
A week later, either back to work or not
