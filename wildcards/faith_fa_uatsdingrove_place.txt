Khetag's Grove, the holiest grove of the Ossetians, a nearly round island of huge old ash, beech and hornbeam trees standing alone on a flat open plain of fields near Alagir, with the wall of the Caucasus behind it, held to have come down from the mountain to shelter the hero Khetag, and inside it shaded clearings with long plank tables and a small prayer house of timber and stone
the Rekom sanctuary in the Tsei gorge, a small windowless hall of thick squared pine logs weathered almost black, set on a low platform of rough gray stones, its high shingled roof reaching far out over the walls on carved brackets, in a clearing of tall straight pines under gray cliffs and a hanging glacier
the shrine of Mady Mairam, the mother of the gods, in the forest near Rekom, the women's holy place, a mossy boulder and a small log shelter with a steep plank roof under old pines, ribbons and scarves tied to the branches around it
a village dzuar in a mountain grove, a log hut scarcely taller than a person with a roof of split planks and a single low door, standing among old birches and pines above a village of stone towers, a pole fence around it and a long table of planks beside it
