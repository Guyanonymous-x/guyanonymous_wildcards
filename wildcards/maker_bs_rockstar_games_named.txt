Styled in a design language of gritty, lived-in, cinematic realism with a satirical edge. Compositions are organised as a grid of stylised comic-like panels with thick black outlines and flat saturated colour. Colour moods swing between dusty western earth tones and retro pastel neon. Raw, cynical, sweaty and sharply observed, with a worn print finish that feels like a slightly lurid, rebellious portrait of society. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
