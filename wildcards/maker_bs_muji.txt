In the style of Muji, plain unbranded household and clothing goods in a quiet, anonymous no-logo aesthetic. Products are made from natural, undyed materials such as unbleached cotton, linen, paper, recycled cardboard, raw wood, pale beech, rubber, brushed aluminium and clear frosted polypropylene. Colours stay in beige, oatmeal, off-white, light grey, charcoal, navy and muted black, with matte textures and uncoloured finishes. Forms are minimal and rounded-rectangular, with soft edges, a single function, no decoration, tiny simple sans-serif labels in black on kraft brown paper or a white card tag, and stationery, storage boxes, sofas, notebooks and sweaters presented in neat grids. Calm, empty, modest, serene mood with soft daylight and generous empty space.
