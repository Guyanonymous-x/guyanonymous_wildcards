Styled in a design language of cool. Coded barcode-like or abstract geometric patterns, industrial hazard stripes in black and yellow, silver or metallic ink on matt grey stock, textured cardboard, muted greys, steel blues and muted pastel colours, grainy halftone photographs and a clinical, enigmatic, modernist attitude. Compositions are quiet and carefully spaced, with cool restraint, deliberate mystery and an industrial, coolly intellectual mood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
