Firelight from a hole, and nothing else that matters, one small unglazed or louvred window giving a gray side light onto the trough, which is where it is needed
A room that is otherwise dim, low and brick-colored; and for two or three hours in the middle of the night or the early morning, lighting the brick floor, the underside of the trough, the faggot stack and the lower half of anybody standing in the room, with their shadow thrown up onto the roof timbers, which is the most dramatic lighting condition in this tab and is produced by a bread oven
After the oven is raked and the door shut, a dull red line round the door's edge for six hours
