Styled in a design language of extremely fine, cobweb-thin knitted lace so light it could be drawn through a ring. Composition is a calm, symmetrical central field framed by a border and a distinct edge. Patterns are airy open-work motifs, small raised bobbles, diamond lozenges, leaf shapes, fir cones, bell flowers, snowflakes and ripples, finished with a scalloped, saw-toothed edge of tiny points. Colour is natural white or ivory, sometimes pale grey, soft peach or faded rose. The texture is fuzzy, misty and ethereal, with a delicate halo of loose fibres, like frost on a window or cobwebs in morning dew. Delicate, geometric and romantic, with mathematical patience. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
