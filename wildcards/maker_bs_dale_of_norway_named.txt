Styled in a design language of crisp, alpine, snowy and cosy Scandinavian knitwear. Thick, dense, slightly fuzzy, close-knit wool with a patterned band in stranded colourwork, circular bands of stars, snowflakes, diamonds, animals, zigzags and stepped borders around the upper body. Colours are strong, navy, red, white and grey as the classic combination, plus charcoal, forest green and cream. Plain ribbed edges and pewter or metal fastenings. Pattern is clear and graphic in neat repeating bands. Snug athletic fit and crisp zigzag edging. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
