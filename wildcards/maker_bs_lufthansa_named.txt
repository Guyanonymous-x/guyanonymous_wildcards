Styled in a design language of orderly, efficient, geometric modernism and strictly typographic. A deep, rich navy and a bright canary yellow form the core palette, joined by clean white and soft grey. Compositions use flat, wide colour fields, a strict grid, thin rules and generous empty margins. Finishes are matte and calm with crisp edges and precise alignment. Everything feels trustworthy, disciplined, cool-headed and purposeful, with understated German clarity and no ornament. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
