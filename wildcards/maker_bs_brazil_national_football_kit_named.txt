Styled in a design language of joyful, sunny tropical colour built on canary yellow with deep green and royal blue accents and crisp white trim. Simple clean colour panels, wide open v-shaped necklines and retro cuts. Lightweight, subtly woven pattern textures. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
