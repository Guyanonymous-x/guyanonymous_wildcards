In the style of US dollar banknotes, rectangular paper printed in black and green ink on cream cotton stock, with a central oval portrait of a statesman engraved in dense fine line work, ornate guilloche rosettes in each corner, scrollwork borders and a heavy numeral in the corners. A circular green seal with a ring of text, a black seal, serial numbers in green and black, a blackletter heading, microprinting and fine engraving, and on the reverse a green building vignette framed by a lace-like decorative border. Newer versions add pastel peach, blue or yellow tints and a colour-shifting numeral. Traditional engraving, banking authority and tradition. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
