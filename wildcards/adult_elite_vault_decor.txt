Hundreds of candles stand in clusters on every ledge and barrel, their wax run down into thick pale heaps
Oak wine barrels lie stacked along the walls, a few of them tapped with brass spigots
A long trestle table is spread with a white cloth, silver candlesticks and dishes of figs and cheese
Old leather armchairs and velvet cushions fill the side alcoves
Thick brocade curtains hang across the openings of the side vaults
