Styled in a design language of cool, bright, narrative and decorative with a serene seaside light. Other patterns show repeating geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau versions have curving plants, lilies, irises and stylised maidens with delicate gold-green outlines. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
