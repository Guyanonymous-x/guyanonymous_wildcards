Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy-duty, utilitarian machinery. Big rounded, bulbous masses with deep glazing, stubby add-on volumes and a long tapering tail. Riveted aluminium skin with visible fasteners and panel seams. Colour is either matte olive drab and grey, or white and red for rescue, with big black and white stencilled numbers and warnings. No ornament, everything functional, strong, workmanlike and built to lift and endure.
