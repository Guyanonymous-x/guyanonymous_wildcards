In the style of Emma Bridgewater, British earthenware in cream-glazed, spongeware style with hand-sponged blue, red and green spots, dotty hearts, stars, polka dots, and cheerful stripes, along with bold black lettering mottoes and rustic farm animals like hens, pigs and sheep. Chunky mugs, jugs, teapots and plates with painterly, irregular edges and speckled glaze. Cheerful, cottagey, homely kitchen dresser and Sunday breakfast table.
