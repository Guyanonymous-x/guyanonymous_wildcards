In the style of Waterman Pens, elegant fountain pens with slim, rounded barrels and caps in black hard rubber, red ripple pattern, or sterling silver filigree overlay. Ripple designs swirl in mottled red and black, and metal overlays show Art Deco geometric grooves, basketweave and lacy filigree. Pens have thin gold-filled bands, a lever filler on the side, a gently arched clip and a flexible gold nib with fine ornament. Colours are warm, rich and aged, with an orange-red sheen. Boxes are lined with velvet. The mood is refined, Edwardian and Art Deco.
