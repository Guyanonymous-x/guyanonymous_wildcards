in winter, sea ice and snow, the bay frozen into a white plain of ridged and broken ice all the way to the horizon, the shore and the taiga buried in deep snow, every spruce branch loaded white and the air hazy with frost under a pale low sun
in summer, the taiga green and the sea calm, the larches in fresh soft needles and the valleys thick with tall grass and white flower heads, the water lying smooth and blue-gray under a mild sky with high thin cloud, salmon leaping at the river mouth
on a foggy morning, the coast gray, thick sea fog hanging low over the beach so the cliffs and the boats fade to dim shapes a short walk away, the pebbles and driftwood beaded with wet, the water and the sky merged into one soft pale wall
