Styled in a design language of sleek athletic design with a confident, fast, low-slung attitude. Long tapering stripe forms sweep diagonally across surfaces as a single flowing accent curve. Palette is crisp white, deep black and signal red, with royal blue, forest green, mustard and cream for retro colour-blocked pairings. Materials include soft suede with a velvety nap, smooth nylon, terry cloth, glossy synthetic panels and clean piping. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
