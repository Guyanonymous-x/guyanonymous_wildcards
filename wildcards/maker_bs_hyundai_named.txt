Styled in a design language of futuristic, digital, geometric design. A slanted letter H inside an oval is drawn as a slim, cleanly spaced outline, and a newer version builds a tall H from sharp, parametric strokes. Pixel motifs recur everywhere, in small square dot grids, blocky eight-bit letterforms and chunky mosaic patterns. Surfaces are taut and folded like sharp paper origami, with crisp creases and flush smooth planes. Palette is deep navy, cool silver, glossy white, graphite, with neon accents of magenta, electric cyan and sunset orange that glow. Finishes are satin metal, translucent smoked glass and matte ceramic. Overall, a clean, optimistic, high-tech and slightly playful look. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
