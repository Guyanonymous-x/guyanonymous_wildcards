Styled in a design language of a showcase of large coloured gemstones such as emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, amethyst and citrine in smooth cabochon and faceted cuts, set in polished yellow and rose gold. Shapes are organic and sculptural, with flowing curves, wave and leaf forms and bold chunky volumes. Designs often combine textured gold with a single huge gem or bright candy-coloured stones stacked in bands, with smooth rounded forms that feel modern and tropical. Colours are saturated greens, blues, purples and warm golden yellow. Confident, glamorous and sunny, against dark depth with bright spotlights. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
