a 1981 pinball table by Bally, a deluxe pool hall pinball, with "EIGHT BALL DELUXE" across the top of its backglass. Its playfield shows drop targets painted as pool balls in solid and striped colors, a ball-save gate and a deluxe green and gold playfield, and above it is a backglass of a smoky pool table with three players and a lady in a red dress.
