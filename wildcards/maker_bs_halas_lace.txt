Styled in a design language of fine hand-made needle lace stitched with a single needle and fine thread, dense, even and light as air. Tiny buttonhole stitches form solid motifs surrounded by open meshes, with picots and scalloped edges. Motifs are stylised folk flowers such as tulips, roses and carnations, bell flowers, hearts, leaves, buds, vines, birds and symmetrical lozenges. Colour is traditionally off-white or cream, with soft peach, pale orange, light green and butter yellow in historical examples. Fine, airy, geometric-floral, delicate and graceful, with a handmade crispness, like frost patterns.
