a 1981 arcade cabinet of a girder-climbing platform game, with "DONKEY KONG" on its lit marquee at the top. It is a red upright cabinet with blue trim and a huge cartoon ape beating its chest on the side, a single joystick and one jump button. Its screen shows sloping red steel girders joined by cyan ladders, barrels rolling down, a giant ape at the top beside a captive lady, a tiny jumping carpenter in red overalls at the bottom.
