Styled in a design language of nerdy cosy 1980s home-tech character. Wide beige and brown-beige moulded plastic with sloped wedge profiles, grey and light brown rectangular detailing, tiny red accent dots and ribbed grooves. The emblem is a bold angular chevron letter shape in white, grey or blue with a red wedge. Secondary palette is royal blue with light blue borders and blocky pixel type. Friendly, hobbyist and square. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
