Styled in a design language of riveted polished-aluminium travel trailers with a rounded, bullet-like loaf shape, curved ends that taper smoothly to the roof, rows of exposed rivets along panel seams, a mirror-bright silver skin that reflects the sky, small rounded wraparound front windows, a single curved door, and inside, curved birch-ply cabinetry, pale laminate, chrome fittings and a cosy streamlined mid-century look. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
