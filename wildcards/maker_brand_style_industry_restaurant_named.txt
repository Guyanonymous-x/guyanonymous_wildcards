In the style of Waffle House, functional, all-night, highway roadside, flat and strongly lit. Low box forms with yellow and black trim, big windows, bright harsh overhead light, long counters and a checkered floor. Palette is lemon yellow, black, white and chrome.
In the style of In-N-Out, clean, retro Californian, bright and uncluttered. Repeating borders of small red palm-tree motifs and slim red bands run along white paper and cardboard surfaces, with simple flat red and white colour blocking and glossy printed coatings. A limited palette of red, white and sunny yellow keeps everything crisp. Matte uncoated paper and shiny red lacquered details. Fresh, nostalgic, honest and sunny.
In the style of McDonalds, bright, fast, family-friendly and instantly legible. A rounded double-arch letter shape in yellow recurs as the central mark, often alone and oversized. Forms are friendly, rounded and modular, in glossy moulded plastic, kraft-brown paper and card, and simple printed wraps. Newer graphics use playful flat illustration, bold dots, wavy lines and cut-out food shapes on red or yellow backgrounds.
In the style of Nandos, warm, bold, casual, spicy and lively. Palette runs through chilli red, terracotta, burnt orange, sunny yellow, teal, mint and soft peach, set in flat blocks. Surfaces carry African-inspired geometric patterns, bold arcs, semicircles, sun discs, zigzags and hand-printed abstract portraits with a screen-print look. Kraft cardboard and matte paper in warm earth tones are cut into angular tabs with colourful striped trims and rough linocut edges. Vibrant, sociable, handmade and fiery.
In the style of Yo Sushi, playful, futuristic, pop-culture Japan-inspired and brisk. Sleek spaces with a continuous flowing line carrying small colour-coded rounds, slick white surfaces, glossy finishes, moulded plastic and neon lighting. Colour indicates value. Palette is hot pink, red, black, white and neon blue.
In the style of Wagamama, austere, canteen-like Japanese-influenced minimalism with a cheerful bustle. Long communal wooden benches in a bright minimal hall with white walls. Thin paper in black and red in a hand-written style. Large simple bowls and plain utensils. Palette is black, red, white and pale wood.
