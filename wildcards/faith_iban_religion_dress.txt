the warrior dress of the Iban, the Dayak people of the rivers of Sarawak in Borneo, adult men in their twenties to sixties in a long loincloth of patterned cloth wrapped between the legs with its ends hanging front and back, bare above the waist, a sleeveless vest of animal skin, a cap topped with long black-and-white hornbill tail feathers, bead necklaces and a sword at the hip
bodies covered in tattoos of rosettes and lines on the shoulders, the throat and the backs of the hands, a rattan cap with tall hornbill plumes, strings of beads crossing the chest and a long blade in a carved wooden scabbard
a war jacket of woven cotton or bark cloth and a headdress of hornbill feathers rising high above the head, silver bands on the arms, a long shield and a sword held at the side
present-day dance dress at the Gawai harvest festival, the men in loincloths, feather headdresses and beaded vests, the women in short woven skirts with silver belts and coin collars
