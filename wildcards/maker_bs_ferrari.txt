In the style of Ferrari in the 1960s to 1980s, a low, wide mid-engine or front-engine sports coupe, with a long curved bonnet or wedge nose, round or pop-up headlamps, an egg-crate mesh grille, and large air intakes. Curvy haunches, a fastback roof with flying buttresses or side strakes, a rounded tail with four round lamps, and a prancing black horse badge on a yellow shield. Wheels are wire spokes or five-star alloys with a knock-off nut. Paint in rosso corsa red, giallo yellow, silver, black and dark blue. Interior has tan or black leather bucket seats, an exposed metal gear gate, a deep-dish wheel with a yellow horse centre, and rows of round dials. Mood is sensual, dramatic and fast.
