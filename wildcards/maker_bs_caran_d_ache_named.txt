Styled in a design language of a precise Swiss spectrum of intense, saturated colour arranged in tidy graded rows. Hexagonal faceted forms carry glossy lacquer in deep tones, and each is dipped at the end in a matching colour. Palette is vivid scarlet, cobalt, emerald, sunflower yellow and violet, set against dark charcoal, matte black and bright white. Metal surfaces are gold, silver, anodised and satin, with sharp chiselled edges and fine engraved lines. Luxurious, exact, jewel-like and colourful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
