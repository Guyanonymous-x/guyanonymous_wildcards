In the style of Gzhel, Russian folk ceramics painted in cobalt blue on bright white glaze, with brushstrokes in varied blue tones, flowing flowers, leaves, scrolls, dots and drops, on teapots, tea sets, samovar-shaped vessels, plates and glossy figurines of animals, birds and women in traditional dress. Fluid, smooth strokes shading from deep ink to pale sky blue, joyful and fresh, with a wonderful clean blue-white contrast and handmade charm.
