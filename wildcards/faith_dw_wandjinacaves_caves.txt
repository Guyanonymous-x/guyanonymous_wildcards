in the rock shelter the Wandjina lie painted side by side along the ceiling, great white faces with large black oval eyes ringed with lashes and a long nose, their heads ringed by a broad halo of red and yellow bands and fine red lines
on the shelter wall a single Wandjina stands taller than a person, a white body with lines of red dots running down it, the round white head ringed by a horseshoe-shaped halo of red rays, the dark eyes staring outward
beside the Wandjina on the shelter wall are painted Ungud the snake in long white coils, small figures of kangaroos, fish and yams, and red hand stencils, all freshly bright from repainting
