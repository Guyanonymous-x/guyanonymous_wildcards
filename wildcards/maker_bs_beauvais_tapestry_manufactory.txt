Styled in a design language of a playful, pastoral Rococo character. Scenes of gallant romance, children at play and fanciful animals are rendered in soft, sweet colour, pink, cream, sky blue, pale green and gold with brown outlines. Frames of flowers, ribbons, scallops and C-shaped scrolls surround each scene. The finish is fine and smooth and the colours glow like painted pastel. Forms are plump with flowing curves, and the mood is light, romantic and decorative. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
