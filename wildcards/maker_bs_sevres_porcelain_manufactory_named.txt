Styled in a design language of smooth glossy porcelain with deep saturated ground colours, rich royal blue, turquoise, delicate rose pink and apple green, all heavily gilded. Gilt borders, scrolls, beading and lacy foliage trim every edge, and curves flow like branches or flower stems. Forms are swelling, ornate and Rococo. Unglazed matte white areas have a silky, snow-like texture. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
