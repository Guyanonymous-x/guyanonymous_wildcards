Styled in a design language of saturated hot pink dominating everything, accented with turquoise, white, lilac, and soft pastel pink. Glossy moulded plastic and shiny vinyl in sweet, rounded, slim and elegant forms with arched, stylish proportions. Patterns include polka dots, gingham, stripes and glitter. Surfaces mix candy gloss, satin, tulle, faux fur, metallic foil and sparkly glitter finishes. Sweet, glamorous and aspirational, with both 1960s retro charm and modern glam variations, all bathed in monochrome pink with white highlights. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
