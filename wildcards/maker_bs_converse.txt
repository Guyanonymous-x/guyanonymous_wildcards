Styled in a design language of classic American court and garage-band heritage in its simplest form, for any product. Soft cotton canvas stretched over rounded, minimal shapes, framed by glossy off-white trim with a thin coloured stripe, flat cords and small shiny metal rings. A small circular badge carrying a star and chevron mark recurs as the emblem. Palette is optic white, black, cherry red, navy, mustard and faded pastels, with sports-team stripes and star motifs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
