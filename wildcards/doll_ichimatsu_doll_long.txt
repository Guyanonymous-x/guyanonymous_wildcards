a Japanese ichimatsu doll, a traditional standing doll with a lifelike face, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her face and hands are finished in gofun, a smooth chalk-white coating made of crushed shell, polished to a soft sheen, with small dark glass eyes, a tiny painted red mouth and fine painted brows. Her black hair is real hair cut in a straight bob with blunt bangs. She wears a silk kimono of a bright pattern tied with a stiff brocade obi sash in a large bow at the back, and white split-toe socks with small lacquered sandals. Her body is jointed so she can be dressed in different kimono. __doll_ichimatsu_doll_var__.
