Styled in a design language of polished, high-budget blockbuster visuals with vast sweeping vistas seen from a high vantage point and a sense of freedom and scale. Graphic elements are clean and minimal, with simple blue and white icons and glowing markers. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
