Styled in a design language of crisp, clean and friendly European confidence built on deep emerald green, bright red and white with silver accents. One bold red five-pointed star is the repeatable emblem, often set on a cream-white oval edged in red. Lettering is lowercase, rounded and slightly tilted, with a thin red outline on white. Thin gold or red filigree trim may edge panels. Surfaces are glossy and uncluttered, with green dominating and the red accent popping against it.
