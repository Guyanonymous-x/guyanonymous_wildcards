a 1950s toy playset of soft single-color plastic figures and a lithographed tin building. The figures are each about as tall as a thumb is long, unjointed and unpainted, cast in one color of soft plastic in fixed action poses with crisp molded detail of faces, hats and weapons. The tin building is printed with bright lithographed windows, doors and brick. The set comes in a large flat box with a painted scene of the whole playset on the lid, and it is laid out on a living room rug. __toyline_marx_playsets_var__.
