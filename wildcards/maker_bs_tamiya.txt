In the style of Tamiya, plastic scale model kits and radio controlled car boxes of the 1970s and 1980s. Box tops show a single vehicle or tank painted in rich illustrated realism on a plain white background, with crisp edges, glossy highlights, believable dirt, rust and exhaust staining, and dramatic low angles. A thin black frame and a small logo of two stars, one red and one blue, sit in the corner with clean sans serif lettering in black, red and blue. Inside, parts hang on pale grey, olive or black plastic sprues with sharp moulded rivets, hexagonal bolt heads, fine panel lines and neat decal sheets in white, red and yellow. Buggy and truck bodies are translucent polycarbonate shells painted from the inside in bright racing red, yellow and white with big sponsor-style stripes and number roundels, over chunky knobby tyres, silver aluminium dampers and exposed black plastic chassis. Instruction booklets use tidy exploded line drawings. Mood is precise, enthusiastic, hobbyist and sunny.
