the lagoon lying flat and brown behind the shrine, edged with low mangrove and reeds, white egrets standing in the shallows and a sandbar dividing its still water from the breakers of the open sea
the mouth of the lagoon where it cuts through the beach to the sea, a channel of fast brown water between banks of pale sand, coconut palms leaning over it and fishers' huts of palm matting on the far bank
a wide mudflat at the lagoon's edge at low water, cracked gray mud crusted white with salt, small crabs at their holes, and a line of wooden stakes for nets running out into the shallow water
