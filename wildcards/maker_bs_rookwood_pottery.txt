In the style of Rookwood Pottery, glazed art pottery vases with a high-gloss, dripping or smooth glaze and painted decoration. The signature standard glaze has dark backgrounds blending from brown to amber, orange and olive green, with painted flowers, leaves, fruit and animals, such as nasturtiums or pansies, in a naturalistic, softly blurred manner. Later styles include matte glazes in soft green, blue and pink, as well as Art Deco forms with geometric and stylised flowers. Forms are rounded, with simple lines and a mix of tall vases and bowls. Marks on the base include a stamped flame-ringed emblem.
