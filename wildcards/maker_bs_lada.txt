Styled in a design language of frugal, sturdy, Soviet-era design. Shapes are plain, squared-off and utilitarian, made of flat panels with minimal ornament. Palette is faded orange, mustard yellow, olive green, brick red, cream, sky blue and warm brown, as flat uncoated colour blocks and bold stripes. Finishes are painted enamel, thin chrome trim, stamped metal and worn printed paper with a nostalgic, sun-faded patina. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
