Styled in a design language of thick, plush velvet texture with a sculptural, monumental pattern. Layouts are formal and symmetrical, with large acanthus scrolls, floral bouquets, shells, garlands and architectural cartouches on a dark ground of deep blue, black, brown or crimson. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Wide borders imitate carved mouldings, with fruit and flowers in the corners. The feeling is of a royal palace, sumptuous, ordered and heavy.
