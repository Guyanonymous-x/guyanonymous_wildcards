Styled in a design language of dependable, precise and quietly handsome. Compact rectangular forms with a flat black or satin silver plane, fine black leatherette or wood-grain texture and crisp machined edges. Tiny metal details with fine knurling and thin ridges give a tidy, engineered feel, and a narrow red band accent marks top-grade pieces. Palette is restrained black, silver and white with a single confident signal red used for the emblem and highlights. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
