In the style of Polaroid, instant film cameras of the 1970s and 1980s. Bulky, sleek folding or boxy bodies in beige, white or black plastic with a dark leatherette grip panel, a large single lens set above a bright rectangular flash bar and a big red rectangular shutter button. A chocolate-brown and tan palette is typical, with a rainbow stripe in red, orange, yellow, green and blue across the front. Film prints are square with a thick white border and a wide chin at the bottom, glossy surface, soft warm washed-out colours and slightly faded saturation. Lettering is bold rounded sans-serif in white. Mood is nostalgic, friendly and snapshot-casual.
