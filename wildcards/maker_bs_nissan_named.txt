Styled in a design language of forms are sharp yet flowing, with boomerang and V-shaped motifs, crisp folded creases, muscular swelling surfaces and floating, blacked-out contrasts. Palette is pearl white, deep graphite, silver, gloss black and a confident signal red, sometimes with a single copper or orange accent. Finishes mix mirror chrome, satin metal and glossy piano black. Light-edged glowing outlines and minimal digital graphics give a calm, high-tech feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
