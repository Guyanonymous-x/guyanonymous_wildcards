In the style of Anglepoise, technical, balanced and flexible engineering shown honestly. Slender jointed members, exposed coiled springs, small pivots and a conical form poised in mid-air like a drawing compass, grounded by a heavy stepped round base. Enamel or powder-coated metal in cream, black, red, grey, racing green and polished chrome. Everything looks adjustable and functional, with a drawing office and workshop atmosphere, lean, precise and a little vintage. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
