Shot through a super blue infrared filter that passes blue and infrared light and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a dusty rose sky over aqua, turquoise and mint foliage, with deep teal trees. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
