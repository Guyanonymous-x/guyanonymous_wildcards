Styled in a design language of tiny buttonhole stitches form solid motifs surrounded by open meshes, with picots and scalloped edges. Motifs are stylised folk flowers such as tulips, roses and carnations, bell flowers, hearts, leaves, buds, vines, birds and symmetrical lozenges. Colour is traditionally off-white or cream, with soft peach, pale orange, light green and butter yellow in historical examples. Fine, airy, geometric-floral, delicate and graceful, with a handmade crispness, like frost patterns. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
