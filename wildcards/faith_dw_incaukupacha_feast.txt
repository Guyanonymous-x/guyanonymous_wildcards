at the November feast of the dead, Aya Marcay Quilla, men in their thirties carry a mummy in a litter through the streets, women in their forties walking beside with jars of corn beer
servants in their twenties to forties offer the royal mummies plates of food and cups of corn beer, then burn the food in a fire before them
a llama is led before the mallquis, its throat cut by a priest in his fifties, its blood caught in a gold bowl and offered to the dead
