Styled in a design language of carefully revived classical roman letterforms and crisp new classics. Lettering is a fine serif with high contrast, bracketed serifs, balanced proportions and even colour, with a few geometric sans-serif and slab designs. Compositions use centred headings, small capitals, letter-spaced capitals and discreet fleurons. The look is orderly, refined, classical and typographic, like a well-printed British book or newspaper. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
