Styled in a design language of energetic, vivid green wholesome design. Saturated apple green covers broad surfaces, set against deep forest green, white and warm chocolate brown. A bold chunky rounded wordmark in white with a dark outline and a slim banner sits on a green block, with a small red or yellow burst accent. Surfaces are glossy ridged metal, matt printed card and soft-touch plastic, with a stylised ribbon curve wrapping around shapes. Patterns use dynamic diagonal slashes, dotted energy sparks, hexagon cells and simple sporty line illustrations. Forms are rounded, cylindrical and sturdy with bevelled ribs. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
