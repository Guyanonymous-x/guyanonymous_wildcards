Styled in a design language of overwrought, odd and unforgettable American design. A tall vertical horse-collar shape set in the middle of a wide bar, wingtip boomerang forms, side sculpting with a bright spear and a cut-away dip along the flank. Two-tone pink and black, turquoise and white, yellow and brown. Open pillarless transparency, chrome rims with whitewall roundness and a rolling dome of graphics add oddness. Ambitious, fussy and strange. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
