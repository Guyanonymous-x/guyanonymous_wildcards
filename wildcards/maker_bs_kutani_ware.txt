Styled in a design language of dense, opaque overglaze enamels in five dark, saturated colours, namely deep green, Prussian blue, yellow, purple and aubergine red. Designs cover nearly the whole surface with bold landscapes, birds, flowers, geometric diaper patterns and fine black outlines. A second manner uses a bright red ground with gold, carrying tiny figures and ornate panels in minute detail. Green is often laid on thickly as a background with a heavy, jewel-like gloss. Rich, theatrical, almost tapestry-like richness and a thick bright glaze.
