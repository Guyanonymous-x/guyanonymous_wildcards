In the style of Pasabahce, hospitable, bright, tidy, sparkling domestic glass with an Ottoman flavour. Small, slim, waisted forms with a flared lip and a gently pinched middle, clear and thin. Larger pieces have cut and pressed patterns such as diamond facets, rosettes, fine vertical flutes and rows of tiny dots, with domed tops and tall necks. Coloured glass in cobalt blue, emerald green, amber and ruby red carries gilded rims and enamelled floral motifs of tulips, carnations and cypress in gold and white. Heavy rounded bases anchor the forms. Glassy reflections, warm light, patterned cloth and brass in the background. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
