Styled in a design language of close-fitting fine soft merino knit, smooth, matte and slightly fuzzy, with flat seams and mountain-inspired minimalism. Colours are muted forest green, charcoal, deep navy, heather grey, rust, moss, stone and pale blue with occasional bright orange. Graphic touches include small sheep-like or mountain-line prints, subtle stripes and tiny sewn marks. Simple, natural, adventure-ready mood, with snowy alpine and misty hill landscapes.
