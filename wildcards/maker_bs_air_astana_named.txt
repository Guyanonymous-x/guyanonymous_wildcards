Styled in a design language of orderly, formal, cool steppe-wide identity of dark blue and gold. White grounds with a deep navy base, rich blue areas carrying a golden stylised ornament like a pair of wings or a nomadic motif. Special treatments stretch a grey-spotted wild cat across the surface with white and blue snowy rock. Navy seating patterned with ornamental motifs in gold thread, grey carpets and warm wood trim. Smart navy with gold buttons and silk in a traditional pattern. A navy field with a thin gold ornamental border, with carved screens and rugs echoing it. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
