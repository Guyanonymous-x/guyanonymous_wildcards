Styled in a design language of quiet luxury built on woven leather made of soft strips interlaced in a tight, even basketweave grid, applied as the surface of any object. Strips are thick and wide, supple and slightly slouchy, with minimal hardware and no visible logos. Palette is butter yellow, parakeet green, burnt orange, taupe, chocolate and black, rich but never loud. A single discreet gold knot may serve as an accent. Understated, tactile, soft to the touch, with quiet craftsmanship as the only signature. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
