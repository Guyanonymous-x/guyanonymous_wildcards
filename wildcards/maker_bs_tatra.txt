In the style of Tatra in the 1930s and 1940s, a streamlined aerodynamic Czech saloon with a long smooth teardrop body, a central stabilising fin running down the rear deck, a tapering tail with air-cooling louvres, and three headlamps with the middle one set in the centre of a bulbous nose. Smooth flush sides, windows set in curves, a split rear window, and skirted wings. Wheels are steel discs with chrome hubcaps. Paint in dark green, black, metallic grey, and burgundy. Interior has wide bench seats in cloth or leather, a wooden dash with simple dials, and a slim large wheel. Mood is futuristic, odd and streamlined.
