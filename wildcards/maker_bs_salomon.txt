Styled in a design language of technical, aggressive, engineered mountain-sport design with sculpted, angular silhouettes and sharp protective overlays. Mixed synthetic mesh, welded films, moulded rigid shells and rubberised textures with exposed cord details, all arranged in layered, fast-looking panels. Palette of black, charcoal, warm grey and white with sudden hits of bright red, electric blue, lime, orange or earthy mineral tones in muted colourways. Matte and gloss textures sit side by side, with chevron and ribbed patterns, giving a fast, slightly futuristic, precise feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
