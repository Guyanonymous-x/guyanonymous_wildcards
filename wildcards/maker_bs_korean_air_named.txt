In the style of Korean Air, orderly, polite, spacious, corporate and sky-high, with cool blue colours and a clean white body under a cloudless sky. White lower areas and a sky blue upper stripe over a wide silver-grey line, with a big round emblem of swirling red and blue halves twisting around each other. The title is a bold, rounded serif wordmark in light blue, in a heavy slab-like cut with quirky character. A later redesign shifts to a dark navy outlined emblem and a tidy sans-serif. Interiors in calm light blue and grey with white head cloths, warm wood-toned panels and cream carpeting. Light blue and white uniforms with neat scarves and pale gloves.
