Styled in a design language of thin white porcelain used as a canvas for striking graphic energy. Also a classic cobalt net pattern with gilded cell edges and small white relief bumps. Flat bold colour, dynamic diagonal compositions, a stern idealistic mood and a historical museum feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
