Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy, utilitarian and Cold War. Large, chunky forms with bulbous pod-like volumes, big round windows, a long tapering rear, massive repeating blades on a thick hub, and exposed machinery in boxy housings. Short stubby supports. Colour is plain, either military olive-drab and matte green with stencil markings. Riveted seams, rows of screws and hatches look hardworking and slightly weathered. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
