the dabuyaba, the long temple house of the ancestors with walls of poles and a steep thatched roof of palm, its earth floor crowded with men and women in their twenties to eighties in bright dresses and head wraps
dancers in their thirties to eighties shuffle in a slow tight circle on the earth floor of the temple, their arms linked and their shoulders swaying, as three drummers in their forties to sixties play large drums
a long table in the temple heaped with food for the ancestors, cassava bread, rice, fish, roasted pork and chicken, rum and coffee, the pigs and chickens killed for the feast lying beside it
