In the style of Gentle Monster, playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde. Oversized, sculptural forms in chunky glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, with thick geometric rims, rounded squares, pointed wings and big wraparound shields. Metal in silver or gold with fine joints, and tinted transparent areas in blue, green, orange or mirror gradient. Small engraved logo marks in tiny white capitals. Spaces are more art gallery than shop, with huge kinetic sculptures, robotic arms, animal-like figures, towering mirrored walls, glowing plant-like installations and surreal landscapes of foam, resin, concrete and silicone in stark white or black, with a single object placed like an artefact on a plinth. Moody, dramatically lit photography against odd backdrops.
