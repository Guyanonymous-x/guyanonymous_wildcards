An open quadrangle in full daylight with pale setts throwing light back into the box doorways, and an arch at one side framing a view out
The boxes themselves lit by their open top leaf and by one small barred or louvred window high on the outer wall, which is the lighting condition of almost every stable interior and is the reason stable paintings look as they do
The hay loft lit by a pitching door and two skylights, with dust in the beams whenever hay is moved, which is continuous; and at night nine lanterns on nine hooks, one carried, and a yard of wet setts reflecting them
