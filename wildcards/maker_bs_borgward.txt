Styled in a design language of handsome German saloons and coupes with smooth pontoon bodies, a gentle wrap-around windscreen, a wide oval grille with a chrome diamond centre, and slim tail fins. Soft two-tone paint in cream with maroon, grey with turquoise, and a clean, expensive look with polished chrome and neat interiors. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
