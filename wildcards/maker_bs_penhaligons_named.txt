In the style of Penhaligons, ornate, quirky Victorian gentleman-perfumer elegance. Cut-crystal and clear glass with faceted details are finished with silken ribbon, tassels and gilt trim. Surfaces are heavy cream, deep pine green, oxblood red and inky black with thin gold borders, embossed crests, a crowned heraldic emblem and engraved copperplate capitals. Labels sit within delicate frames of double rules and tiny ornamental flourishes. Wax-seal accents, tissue paper, fine marbled endpaper patterns, tartan ribbons and glossy lacquered boxes with satin linings add collector charm. Lettering is spaced classical serif in gold on black or dark ink on cream. Eccentric, English, refined and gently theatrical. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Penhaligons in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
