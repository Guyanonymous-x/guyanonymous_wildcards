the bunyip, the swamp monster of Aboriginal Australian tradition, a large, heavy creature of rivers and billabongs, like a flightless bird crossed with an alligator, dark, shaggy fur or slick reptile skin, muddy and wet, with a long neck, a horse-like or dog-like face, tusks, flippers and a long tail, rising from a dark billabong at night among the reeds, its body heavy and dark, part bird and part seal, a long neck and flippers, rising from a muddy billabong at dusk, gum trees reflected in the water
