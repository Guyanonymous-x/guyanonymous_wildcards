the senior men of the village, in their sixties and seventies, carry a straw boat as long as a man's arm span, with a little straw mast and rudder and loaded with tiny offerings, down to the sea and launch it into the waves
the haenyeo throw handfuls of millet seed out over the gray sea from the rocks, scattering it far over the water, while the head simbang reads the fall of the seeds for the year's catch
the simbang welcomes the Dragon King of the sea, bowing low toward the harbor, and the haenyeo pour rice wine from small bottles onto the black rocks at the water's edge
