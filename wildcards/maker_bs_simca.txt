Styled in a design language of jaunty, post-war and optimistic French pontoon design. A rounded body, a wide-mouth chrome opening, round forms, wraparound glazing, chrome trim strips along the flanks, a rounded end with chrome edges, a swallow mark and pastel two-tone paint. Cream over sea green, pink over grey, turquoise and sand. Bench forms in vinyl and cloth and chrome-ringed dials keep the mood sunny. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
