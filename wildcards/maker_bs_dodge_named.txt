Styled in a design language of brutal, loud, cartoonish tough-guy American muscle of the early 1970s. Wide, aggressive, squared-off forms with bulging humps, a pronounced hip curve, and a bold bumblebee band or thick vertical stripes wrapped around the body. Stripes are black over yellow, red, orange or grey, sometimes with a ducktail kick shape. A round badge and a ram motif act as marks. Surfaces are slotted metal textures, glossy high-impact paint, black vinyl and chunky proportions. Palette is violet, lime green, purple, bright orange, pale green, yellow and black. Swaggering, noisy and showy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
