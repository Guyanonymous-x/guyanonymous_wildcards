a long room with a high ceiling and tall sash windows down both sides
deliberately airy because bad air was understood as the danger
beds ranged along both walls with the heads to the wall
an open fireplace at one or both ends as the only heat
bare scrubbed boards with sand
a water cistern or a bucket stand
a privy or close stool in an adjoining closet
lime-washed walls
a board of rules fixed by the door and read aloud
no plumbing at the bedside and no artificial light beyond candles
a matron's room at the stair head
