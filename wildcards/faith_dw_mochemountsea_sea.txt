the cold Pacific rolls in long gray-green waves onto a pale beach, small rocky islands offshore crowded with brown sea lions and white with the droppings of thousands of seabirds
fishermen in their thirties ride small reed boats over the waves, each boat a tight bundle of pale golden totora reeds with an upturned prow, the rider kneeling and paddling with a split cane
under the waves a great sea monster with a fish body, crab claws and a fanged face rises from the deep, the water around it full of fish, rays and spiral shells drawn in fine lines
