Styled in a design language of precise, high-contrast mountain-gear design with exact engineered shapes. Anodised aluminium parts in vivid red, blue, orange, lime, purple and silver are machined with rounded forms, sharp stamped marks and small lightening cutouts. Matte black fabrics and chunky black housings carry bright accents, with glossy neon shells, vents and textured grips. Woven geometric patterns in bright zigzag and diamond repeats appear on cord and webbing. The emblem is a solid black diamond shape in a stacked pair of angled facets. Packaging is clean white with strong black type. Technical, industrial, bold and exact. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
