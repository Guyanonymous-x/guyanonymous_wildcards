a rented room in somebody's back yard, which houses an enormous number of people and is almost entirely undocumented, a single room of corrugated iron and timber built against the rear wall of a formal township house so that one wall is masonry and the rest are sheet
a door to the yard and usually one small window or none
no running water
no sanitation of its own
access to the yard's shared tap and lavatory on terms set by the landlord
an electricity connection from the main house charged by the landlord
a floor of earth
concrete or lino depending on what the tenant has laid
no security of tenure whatsoever since the arrangement is verbal in most cases
a rent paid weekly or monthly in cash
several such shacks in one yard so the yard is a shared space with its own social arrangements, and a landlord who lives ten meters away
