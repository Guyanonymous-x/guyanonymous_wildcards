__bs_subject_media__ styled after a rigorous three-band layout with a central white panel framed by two coloured stripes in orange, green and blue. Later work uses a Swiss-style grid with bold illustration. Mid-century uncoated paper feel, matte printing and crisp rules. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
