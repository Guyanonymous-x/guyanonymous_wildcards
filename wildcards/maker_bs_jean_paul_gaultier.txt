Styled in a design language of cheeky, theatrical Parisian pop-couture. The core pattern is navy and white horizontal Breton stripes, with variations in black, red, turquoise and pale blue. Surfaces include sheer tattoo-print mesh in sailor motifs, anchors, hearts, roses and swallows, plus pinstripe wool, corsetry satin, shiny tin metal and printed metal sheets in cream, pink and teal. Lettering is a slim hand-drawn, slightly crooked black and white wordmark in two lines, often stamped inside a stripe block or a metallic oval. Shapes mix masculine and feminine cues with cinched, sculpted curves, pointed cone silhouettes and sleek hourglass forms. Colour is witty, saucy and bold, with a playful, ironic, avant-garde attitude. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
