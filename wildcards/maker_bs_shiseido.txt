Styled in a design language of luxurious, feminine, carefully composed Taisho and early Showa elegance where Art Nouveau curves meet Art Deco geometry. Slim, faceted glass-like forms with domed tops, gilt metal accents, and lacquer red or ivory surfaces tied with ribbon. sinuous floral borders and flat-colour illustrations of slender women with swept-up hair, in muted rose, jade green, indigo and warm cream. Gold foil edging, delicate line work, soft monochrome theatrical photography, and generous empty margins around small precise motifs. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
