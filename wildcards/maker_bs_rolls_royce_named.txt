Styled in a design language of hushed, monumental and hand-built grandeur. Tall formal verticals, long calm unbroken surfaces and a fine bright line drawn along the length of a form, finished with a tiny sculpted silver figure leaning into the wind as a crowning ornament. Mirror-polished stainless metal, slim vertical fluting, deep lustrous paint in claret, midnight blue, silver and black, often two-toned. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
