In the style of Vlisco, wax-print cotton cloth with enormous vividly coloured designs printed identically on both sides with resin, leaving fine crackle veins where dye seeped through the wax. Motifs are oversized flowers, fruit, birds, feathers, fish, eyes, circles, hands, fans, scrolling vines and bold geometric patterns, outlined and shaded like block-print drawings. Palette is saturated and clashing such as cobalt and orange, scarlet and green, yellow and violet, turquoise and pink, black and white. Exuberant, expressive, festive market mood. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Vlisco in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
