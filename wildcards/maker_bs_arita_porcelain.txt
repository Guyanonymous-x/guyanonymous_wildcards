In the style of Arita porcelain, Japanese white porcelain with crisp underglaze cobalt blue paintings of landscapes, pines, bamboo, cranes and flowers, and enamelled colourful versions in red, green, yellow and sky blue on milky white body with generous empty space. Thin elegant dishes, octagonal bowls, bottle vases, lidded jars with minimalist asymmetrical compositions, delicate brushstrokes, clear sparkling glaze. Refined, airy, graceful, tea house tableware.
