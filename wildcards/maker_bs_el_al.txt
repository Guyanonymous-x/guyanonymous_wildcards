In the style of El Al, an Israeli airline identity in blue and white. Aircraft have a white fuselage with a sweeping blue cheatline outlined in silver, running along the bottom, curving up over the nose and fading toward the tail, and a plain white or blue tail with a flag. The name is set in a bold blue wordmark in both Latin and Hebrew script letters across the forward fuselage, and aircraft carry names of cities near the cockpit. The older livery used a turquoise tail with the star of David flag at the top and a turquoise and navy cheatline across the windows. Cabins have blue seats with white head covers and a slim wood-look trim, with crisp blue and white carpets. Crew wear smart navy uniforms with a pale blue blouse and scarf. Mood is crisp, clean, secure, patriotic and cool, in sunlit blue sky.
