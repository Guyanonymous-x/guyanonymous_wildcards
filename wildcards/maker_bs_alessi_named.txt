In the style of Alessi, Italian design with a humorous, anthropomorphic spirit. Shapes are rounded, soft and toy-like, with cartoonish faces, tiny feet, perky tips and swelling bellies, mixed with sharply polished geometric forms. Materials are mirror-polished stainless steel, glossy resin and plastic in candy colours, porcelain and chrome. Palette is cheerful and saturated, with lemon yellow, cherry red, turquoise, lime green, hot pink, orange, black and white. Surfaces carry bold dots, stripes and playful abstract patterns, and finishes are bright and slick. Lettering is a simple, rounded or lowercase sans-serif, in a compact wordmark. The overall mood is Memphis-influenced, sculptural, ironic and fun. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Alessi in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
