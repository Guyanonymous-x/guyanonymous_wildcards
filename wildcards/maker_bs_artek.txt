In the style of Artek, Finnish modernist furniture of pale laminated birch with a distinctive bent L-shaped leg that curls into a smooth scroll beneath the seat. Round three-legged stools, simple tables, cantilevered chairs with lightly curved plywood seats, and hanging pendant lamps. Natural birch, white lacquer, black linoleum tabletops and colourful lacquered legs in red, yellow, blue and green, woven webbing and canvas upholstery in stripes. Warm, humane, Scandinavian functionalism, soft rounded edges, light blond wood grain, nordic apartment and school hall mood.
