Styled in a design language of clean, optimistic, glamorous jet-age design. Polished bare-metal surfaces beneath white, separated by a thin navy-blue line. A vivid royal or cobalt blue field carries a large circular compass rose emblem in white and gold with dark rays around a central disc. A later scheme becomes a gold-yellow rose on deep blue. Earlier versions show a winged figure in a black circle and a bold black stripe. Lots of reflective silver metal. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
