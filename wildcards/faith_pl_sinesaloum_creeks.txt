winding mangrove creeks, bolongs, channels of brown water between walls of dark green mangrove roots, narrow tidal waterways looping through the delta, the mangrove trees standing on tangles of arched stilt roots crusted with gray oysters, glossy oval leaves meeting above, gray mud banks shining at low tide and white egrets perched along the branches
wide salt flats of white crust and pink pools near the delta, a bare level plain of cracked gray mud covered with a glittering skin of salt crystals, shallow square evaporation pits dug into it holding water in shades of rose, rust and pale violet, small cones of harvested white salt heaped beside them and a thin line of palms on the far horizon
a maze of islands and channels seen from above, hundreds of flat green mangrove islands of every shape separated by looping ribbons of brown, olive and silver water, pale sandbanks and tan salt pans between them, a few tiny boats drawing thin wakes and the blue Atlantic Ocean along one edge
