Styled in a design language of lean, refined and quietly aggressive British luxury. Taut, flowing surfaces with a long, low stance and a crisp rising shoulder line. A spread-winged emblem in brushed silver or enamel sits as the signature mark. Palette of racing green, silver birch, midnight blue, gunmetal and warm tan, with lime and electric teal as sporting accents. Materials include hand-stitched quilted leather in rich chestnut, polished glass, fine carbon weave, satin aluminium and soft suede. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
