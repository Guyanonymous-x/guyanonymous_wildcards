Styled in a design language of honest, tough, practical, unsinkable simplicity. Wide, stable, sturdy forms in white gelcoat with a thin navy or blue stripe low on the sides and a high flared front. Simple central arrangements with polished stainless details, grey non-slip surfaces and neat bolted-on fittings. No fuss, no ornament, durable, utilitarian, ready for work or play, with a straightforward coastal feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
