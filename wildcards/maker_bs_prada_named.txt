Styled in a design language of severe, intellectual minimalism with deadpan, slightly odd elegance. Shapes are pared-back, geometric and utilitarian, with flat planes, clean seams and a triangular enamel plaque as the signature emblem. Materials are matte black industrial nylon, saffiano cross-hatch leather, brushed metal, glossy plastic and sheer technical fabrics. Palette is black, white, grey, pale mint, powder blue, sage, mustard and dusty pink, with deliberately off-beat pairings and sudden acid accents. Packaging is tidy pale boxes and tags in soft pastels. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
