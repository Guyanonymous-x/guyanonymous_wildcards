In the style of Dymkovo Toy, hand-moulded clay coated in milky-white chalk for a smooth matte porcelain-like finish. Forms are plump, stylised, simple and rounded, with bell-shaped bodies, tiny heads and exaggerated puffy volumes. Painting is crisp, playful and poster-bright in red, orange, yellow, blue, green and black, using large circles, checks, diagonal stripes, wavy lines, dots and rhombuses, with rosy circular cheeks and small black dot eyes. Small details are picked out in glittering gold leaf. Cheerful, flat, geometric and slightly naive, like a patterned doll made of ceramic icing. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Dymkovo Toy in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
