the sacred grove is a tall stand of old linden, oak and birch trees, the ground beneath them swept clean, fresh spruce branches laid in rows with white cloths over them and flat loaves, pancakes and honey set out
before the holy tree of the grove stands a low table of fresh poles, rows of thin beeswax candles fixed along a long branch and burning, white cloth tied around the trunk
beside the trees great black iron cauldrons hang over wood fires, steam rising from the boiling meat and porridge, the smoke climbing straight up through the branches toward the sky
