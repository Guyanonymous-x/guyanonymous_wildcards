a gearbox in the older installations and a gearless machine in the newer
a sheave with the ropes passing over it
a controller cabinet that is either a relay rack from the 1970s or a microprocessor panel from last year and is quite often both
an overspeed governor with a tensioned rope running the height of the shaft
a hand-winding wheel and a brake release with a painted emergency procedure
a trapdoor or a hatch over the shaft that is the most frightening feature of the room
a lifting beam
a logbook
an access door that is locked and whose key is held by the building manager and the lift company
no ventilation to speak of, and a temperature that in summer is a known cause of lift failures
