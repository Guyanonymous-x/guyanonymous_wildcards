Styled in a design language of understated island-casual, preppy and relaxed. Pattern is printed so the outer face looks washed, faded and muted as if sun-bleached, on a thick, plain, honest woven cloth. Colours are soft and tonal, navy, forest green, dusty rust, burgundy, sand, teal and faded sky blue, usually in one colour family with a lighter shade for outlines. Large-scale tropical foliage, flowers, island and ocean motifs repeat in an even, orderly rhythm. Shapes are simple and tidy with soft edges and natural dark details. A quiet, subtle, never loud version of tropical print. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
