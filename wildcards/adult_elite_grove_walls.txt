Giant redwood trunks rise on every side, their red-brown bark deep-furrowed, climbing out of sight
Rustic cabins of rough redwood planks stand among the trees, strung with lines of paper lanterns
Ferns and fallen branches crowd the ground between the trunks, and fog drifts low among them
A half-timbered camp lodge with a steep shingled roof stands at the edge of the clearing, its porch strung with paper lanterns
