Styled in a design language of warm, handcrafted, scientific vintage modernism. Bold black and white identity built from a wide, rounded, with a small slanted cut inside each round. Solid dark walnut and oiled hardwood paired with matte black and brushed silver panels, and thin white lines that group areas together. Accents of orange, red and cream appear sparingly. Forms are slanted, wedge-like and sturdy with softly eased corners, exposed joinery, and a laboratory-meets-furniture honesty. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
