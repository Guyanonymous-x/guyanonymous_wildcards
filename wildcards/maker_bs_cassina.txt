In the style of Cassina, Italian luxury modernist furniture with deep, plump, tufted leather sofas in cognac, black and cream on thin chrome or lacquered steel frames. Sculptural wooden chairs with ladder backs and pale rush seats, tubular steel stool bases, wide-armed leather armchairs and low lacquered tables with reflective glass tops. Rich materials include walnut, rosewood, polished marble, burnished brass and saddle-stitched hide. Refined, architectural, masculine and expensive, with sharp proportions blending craftsmanship and industrial precision in a Milanese penthouse setting.
