Styled in a design language of stately, sturdy, dated 1950s British-inspired formality. Rounded, bulbous volumes with a domed crown and a tall, upright, slightly pompous frontal presence. Chrome used as thin edging and as a few bold vertical accents, with one small winged ornament as a crowning flourish. Swelling curves, thin slender supports and plain painted metal surfaces with fine line work. Interiors are wide, plain and practical. Palette of ivory white, black, dull grey, taxi yellow and olive green. Dignified, unhurried and built to last through heat and dust.
