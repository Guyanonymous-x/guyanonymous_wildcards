Styled in a design language of modern European night trains with long carriages in a deep night-sky blue livery with thin lighter accent lines, flush windows and clean modern surfaces, inside compact sleeping compartments and capsule-like mini cabins in pale wood-effect panels, grey upholstery, soft blue accent lighting and neat folding fittings, with a calm, efficient, contemporary feel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
