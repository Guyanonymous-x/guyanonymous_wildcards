This World, a great island floating on the waters, the Great Smoky Mountains rising blue in long ridges, forests of oak, hickory and chestnut covering them
the water beetle bringing up the soft mud from under the waters to make the land, the great buzzard flying low over it, his wings beating the mud into mountains and valleys
a Cherokee town by a river in the mountains, log houses and a large round council house on a mound, the sacred fire burning in its center
