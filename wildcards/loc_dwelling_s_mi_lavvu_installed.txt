the dwelling of a herding people in the Arctic, and a design built entirely round a central fire, a frame of straight poles
three of them forked and locked together to carry the rest
covered now in canvas and formerly in reindeer hide or wadmal
a smoke hole at the apex with an adjustable flap
a floor of birch twigs and hides laid directly on snow or ground
an interior with a recognized order in which the space behind the fire opposite the door is the storage and formerly the sacred place
sleeping places arranged round the wall with feet to the fire
a structure that stands in high wind because of its cone
enough warmth from a small fire to be comfortable at minus twenty-five, and a design that has been in use for a very long time and is still put up by herders during the migration and the round-up
