a graffito in pencil from a winder in 1897 next to one from 1974
an oil-can with the spout bent
a mug on a ledge with a ring in it
a rag
a glove with the fingers stiff with oil
a dead pigeon in the louvre
a bootlace
a ballpoint
a screwdriver with a split handle
a kettle and two cups kept up here
a sweet wrapper
a feather drift in the corner of every landing
