Styled in a design language of quirky, offbeat 1970s American pragmatism. Stubby, lopsided proportions with one long mass, one short mass and an abruptly chopped, flat cut-off end. Square shoulders, bulging haunches and plain black plastic trim. Sticker-style lettering, bold red, white and blue stripe sets, plaid in orange and brown, woodgrain panels and oversized round forms picked out in matching colour. Loud colours of bright orange, vivid green, mustard and avocado. Practical, rebellious and cheerfully strange. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
