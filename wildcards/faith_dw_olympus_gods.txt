Apollo stands at the center, a man in his twenties wholly nude, his penis and scrotum visible, as some vase painters drew him, a lyre in his hand and a laurel wreath on his long hair, Artemis in a long belted dress with her bow and quiver beside him
the twelve gods feast together, Athena in her thirties in a helmet and long robe, Hermes in his twenties in a winged hat, Aphrodite in her twenties in a thin robe, Poseidon in his fifties with a trident
