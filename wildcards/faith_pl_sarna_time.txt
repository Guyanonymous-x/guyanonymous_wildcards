at Sarhul in spring, the sal trees flowering, the spring festival of the sal blossom, the crowns pale with cream flowers and fresh coppery new leaves under a warm clear sky, the grove crowded with adult villagers in white and red and the ground strewn with petals
in the monsoon, the fields green, heavy gray rain clouds low over the hills, every rice plot brimming with water and bright with young shoots, the red paths turned to dark mud and the sal leaves washed glossy and dripping
at dusk, the grove dark, the sal trunks black columns against a fading orange and violet sky, the space under the leaves deep in shadow, a single clay lamp glowing before the stones and the first cooking fires showing in the village behind
