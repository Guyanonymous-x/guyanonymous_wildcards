after the last memorial rite the ancestor, a man in his eighties in an old court robe, rises from the home shrine into a sky of gold clouds to join the ancestral kami of the household
the ancestral kami, men and women in their fifties to nineties in old robes of white and pale colors, stand among the cedar trees of the village shrine, watching over the rice fields below
at the summer Mitama Matsuri thousands of paper lanterns glow in walls along the shrine paths, and the spirits of the dead, pale robed figures in their forties to nineties, walk among them
