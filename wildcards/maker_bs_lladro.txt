In the style of Lladro, Spanish porcelain figurines. The figures are tall, graceful and elongated, representing young women with parasols, shepherd girls, dancers, ballerinas, children with geese, nuns, clowns, horses and swans, with slim bodies, long limbs, small heads and flowing, swirling robes. Faces are serene, with softly closed or gentle eyes, a pale rosy complexion and tiny delicate hands. The porcelain is mostly matte, chalky and soft, in pastel colours such as pale blue, grey-green, lilac, rose, cream, white and muted gold, with occasional glossy accents and fine hand-applied porcelain flowers. Forms are fluid and somewhat stretched, with the folds of clothes made into rippling, wavy ribbons. A glazed gres line has deeper earthy tones. The look is sentimental, romantic, delicate and fragile. The bases are small and rounded, with tiny flowers and ribbons, the poses are quiet and tender, and displays use soft spotlighting in glass cabinets with mirrored backs.
