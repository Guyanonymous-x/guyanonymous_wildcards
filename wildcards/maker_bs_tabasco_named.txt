In the style of Tabasco, slim, tiny, hand-finished heritage with narrow proportions, a long neck, flat shoulder and small rounded base. Vivid fire-engine red-orange glows through clear glass, accented in green. Layers combine a deep green oval band and a red diamond centre with white serif capitals and a thin gold border. Accent colours vary to signal variants. Palette is fiery red, forest green, cream and gold. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
