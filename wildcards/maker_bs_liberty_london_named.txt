Styled in a design language of fine, silky, lightweight printed cotton with dense small-scale floral prints of tiny ditsy blooms, paisley, peacock feathers, vines and stylised Arts and Crafts leaves. Jewel tones of plum, teal, mustard, rose, sage and deep blue on cream or dark grounds. Fine line drawing, Art Nouveau curves and a hand-drawn quality, bias-bound edges, patchwork and lush velvets. A mix of English country garden and Oriental bazaar. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
