Styled in a design language of cheerful Japanese everyday charm. Bright, simple solid colours in poppy red, cream white, deep navy and soft teal, with generous clean white borders and a single contrasting stripe panel. Pressed, smooth, softly rounded surfaces with glossy paint, chrome edging and simple decal graphics feel warm, honest, practical and endearing. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
