wooden peg dolls, small simple dolls made from turned wooden pegs, each about as tall as an adult's finger. Each doll is a smooth round wooden head on a straight body that narrows at the neck and flares a little at the base, smooth all the way down. They are painted by hand, a few dots and lines for eyes and a smile, rosy cheeks, painted hair and clothes in bright colors. The painted clothes go around the whole peg, a dress with buttons, a sailor shirt, an apron, and some have tiny felt hats glued on. The wood grain shows through the thin paint. __doll_peg_dolls_var__.
