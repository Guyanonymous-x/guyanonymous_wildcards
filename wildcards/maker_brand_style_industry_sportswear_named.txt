In the style of Lacoste, polished tennis-club elegance with a playful, confident wink. Textured cotton pique with a small knit weave, ribbed edges and a clean, relaxed fit. Palette is tennis white plus deep bottle green, navy, red, sky blue and yellow, in solid blocks and bold stripes. Terry cloth bands, white canvas, crisp laundered surfaces and white court lines add sport cues. Lettering is simple, slim, spaced sans-serif capitals.
In the style of Fred Perry, neat, tidy British youth style with slim-fit knitted pique cotton, a two-button opening, twin-tipped stripes in contrast colours such as navy with white and red, or black with yellow, and an embroidered laurel wreath mark on the left chest. Knit cardigans, track shapes and white leather. Slim, clean lines. Mod and skinhead subculture heritage.
In the style of Adidas, sporty heritage built on strict repetition. Three parallel stripes of equal width run as the signature graphic, with trefoil and triangular marks and clean geometric panelling. Palette is crisp white, black, navy and royal blue with red or green accents, often in strong colour blocks with thin contrast piping. Materials are smooth leather, suede, soft tracksuit knit and mesh with tidy stitching. Lettering is lowercase, rounded or bold and geometric. Retro terrace and street energy mixed with sharp modern graphic grids, flat studio lighting and confident, casual, youthful attitude.
In the style of Puma, sleek, cool, sporty swagger of the 1970s and 80s. A curved swoosh-like band sweeping along the side, a leaping cat silhouette mark, soft suede in dark green, blue, red and black with a single white stripe. Velour with piped trim and ribbed edges, retro football shapes and colour blocking. Low, lean silhouettes, glossy and matte contrasts, and a confident athletic attitude.
