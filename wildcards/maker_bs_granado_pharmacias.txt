Styled in a design language of nostalgic, refined, slightly aristocratic nineteenth-century apothecary design. Simple old-fashioned materials such as glass, cork, frosted surfaces, tin and plain paper. Cream, white, pale pink or soft green grounds with a thin double-line border, centred oval cartouches, fine ornamental scrollwork and curling flourishes, bottle green, navy or gold. Dark polished wood, marble and tiled black and white or terracotta. Muted pastel and old-gold accents with restrained. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
