In the style of Philippine Airlines, an airline identity in sail triangles. Aircraft have a clean white fuselage with the airline name in italic blue capitals on the forward body, while the tail carries a stylised flag device of a blue triangle and a red triangle, pointing like sails, with a small eight rayed sun in yellow over the blue. The belly is white with a thin red and blue stripe near the windows, and the engines are plain white. A tiny national flag sits near the rear door. Special liveries mark anniversaries with large stylised numbers. Cabins use deep blue seats with red piping and woven patterns, soft light and warm timber. Crew wear modern dresses in blue or red with traditional sleeves. Mood is bright, hospitable, tropical, crisp and patriotic, with blue, red, white and sunny yellow.
