In the style of Savonnerie Manufactory, thick, plush velvet texture with a sculptural, monumental pattern. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Wide borders imitate carved mouldings, with fruit and flowers in the corners. The feeling is of a royal palace, sumptuous, ordered and heavy. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
