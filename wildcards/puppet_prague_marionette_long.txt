a Czech wooden marionette from Prague, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Its whole body is carved wood with jointed limbs that swing loose, the face carved with big round painted eyes, a long nose, rosy circle cheeks and a smiling red mouth. Its painted hair is dark brown. It wears a folk costume of bright embroidered cloth. Strings run from its head, hands and knees up to a simple wooden cross bar. __puppet_prague_marionette_var__.
