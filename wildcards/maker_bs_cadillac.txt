In the style of Cadillac, late 1950s luxury cars of vast length and width, towering pointed tail fins with twin bullet tail lamps, heavy chrome bumpers with large conical guards, and a jewelled egg-crate grille. Dual stacked headlamps, a long flat hood and a thin hardtop roof, with stainless steel trim and a wreath and crest badge. Pink, pearl white, powder blue and black paint with chrome everywhere. Interiors of tufted leather, thick carpets and sparkling dash trim. Later, sharp-edged, vertical-lamp, angular shield-grille cars with upright lines and a glassy tech look. Excessive, glamorous, jet-age opulence.
