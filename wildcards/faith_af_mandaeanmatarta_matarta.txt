along the road stand the matarta, a row of tall dark watch-houses with walled gates, each guarded by keepers in dark robes holding scourges, where the souls of the unrighteous are held in fire and smoke and lashed across the back until cleansed
in one watch-house the souls of liars and thieves stand chained in a dark pit, their bodies thin and bowed, flames rising around their legs, while the spirit Ruha, a woman in her forties with wild black hair and a dark robe, watches from the gate
the watch-houses rise one above another up the sky, each ringed by a black river, the righteous soul passing through each gate in white while soiled souls in gray rags stand behind iron bars, waiting for their sins to burn away
