Styled in a design language of passionate, graceful Italian sporting elegance. Slim, low, curved forms with long tapering lines and a triangular shield motif. Palette of rich red, cream paper, white, deep blue, silver, black and dark green. Materials include polished chrome, drilled aluminium, wood-rimmed details and rich leather. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
