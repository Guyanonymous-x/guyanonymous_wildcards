Styled in a design language of severe, intellectual minimalism with deadpan, slightly odd elegance. Shapes are pared-back, geometric and utilitarian, with flat planes, clean seams and a triangular enamel plaque as the signature emblem. Materials are matte black industrial nylon, saffiano cross-hatch leather, brushed metal, glossy plastic and sheer technical fabrics. Palette is black, white, grey, pale mint, powder blue, sage, mustard and dusty pink, with deliberately off-beat pairings and sudden acid accents. Lettering is a classic high-contrast serif in capitals with the word Milano beneath, often framed by a black rope or chain border. Packaging is tidy pale boxes and tags in soft pastels. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
