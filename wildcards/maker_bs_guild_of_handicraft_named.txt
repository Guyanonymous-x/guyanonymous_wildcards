In the style of Guild of Handicraft, Arts and Crafts silver, enamel and jewellery in a hand-wrought manner. Hammered metal shows visible tool marks, curved lines, wire twists and loose, flowing Art Nouveau lines. Peacocks, ships, tendrils, stylised flowers and hearts are set with cabochon moonstones, opals, turquoise or blister pearls and translucent coloured enamel in green, blue and rose. Objects look soft and slightly irregular, with a matte, pale silver surface and delicate suspended drops. The mood is romantic, medieval-influenced, simple and craftsmanlike. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Guild of Handicraft in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
