In the style of Air India, warm, ornate, regal Indian palace feeling in crimson, white, saffron and gold. Polished white surfaces carry a red-orange and white stripe, and a stylised red swan or archer disc emblem. Openings are framed in painted Indian arch shapes in red. Carved pillars, Mughal-inspired arches and crimson and gold fabrics. A bowing, turbaned, moustached mascot welcomes everyone with hospitality. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
