Styled in a design language of hyper-sleek, exotic, clean and ultra-technical Swedish engineering. A low wide body, narrow slit shapes, panels that swing up and out and exposed bare carbon-fibre weave under clear lacquer. Ice blue, gloss white, orange and gunmetal. Hollow carbon or forged five-spoke shapes keep it technical. __bs_motif__. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
