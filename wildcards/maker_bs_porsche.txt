Styled in a design language of athletic, low and compact, design driven by function. Smooth, rounded, sculpted forms with a sloping, curved profile, soft arching haunches, round pairs of lamps and clean, taut surfaces with minimal trim. Finishes are glossy paint, satin black, brushed aluminium, titanium and carbon weave in silver, black, graphite, white, guards red and deep blue, with occasional bright accent colours. Lettering is crisp, upright and engineered, with a crest-like badge. Interiors and objects are driver-focused, minimal and precise. Photography is moody, low-angled and sharply lit, with a disciplined, precise, understated performance feel.
