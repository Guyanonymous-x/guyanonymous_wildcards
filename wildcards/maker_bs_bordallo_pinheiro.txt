In the style of the Caldas da Rainha ceramic factory in Portugal, naturalistic majolica. Plates, bowls, tureens, jugs and platters are moulded in the form of oversized cabbage leaves, with veined, ruffled edges and glossy, vivid green glaze, with a pale ribbed back. Other pieces are modelled as realistic lobsters, crabs, snakes, frogs, lizards, fish, snails, beetles, flies and sunflowers, crawling across plates, ewers and jugs in lifelike detail. Glazes are glossy, juicy and saturated, with leaf green, mustard yellow, turquoise, coral, tomato red, aubergine purple and cream. The colours are exaggerated, the textures are lumpy and tactile, and the humour is cheeky. The look is whimsical, sculptural, rustic and slightly surreal, designed for a very Portuguese table. Colours glisten as though freshly wet, and pieces cluster on rustic tables among real vegetables, shellfish and wicker, with a humorous, almost trompe loeil effect.
