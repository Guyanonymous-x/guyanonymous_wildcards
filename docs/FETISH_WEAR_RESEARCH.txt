ROUND 2 DETAIL (Wikipedia pages Latex clothing, Corset, Catsuit, Stiletto heel, Dominatrix, Strait jacket)
LATEX CLOTHING: garment types: catsuits, bodysuits, leotards, stockings, gloves, socks, hoods (including bondage hoods), rubber cloaks, dresses, full suits, rubber mackintoshes. Sheet latex is cut flat and glued at the seams (seams glued, not sewn); moulded latex (dipped) makes hoods and gloves. Thickness about 0.18 to 0.5 mm. Finish: shiny, often polished to keep its sheen. Colour: classic black; naturally translucent so it can be dyed any colour, including metallic, white and bright colours. Fit: often skin-tight, loose styles exist. Looks like a "second skin", can seem glossy-coated.
CORSET TYPES: overbust (under the arms to the hips), underbust (below the bust to the hips), longline (past the hip bone), standard length, waist cincher (low ribs to above the hips), corset dress or hobble corset (covers the legs), demi-corset, stays, bodies, jumps, straight-front corset (S-curve), sport corset (elastic or mesh panels), waspie, Merry Widow, corselette, neck corset (posture collar with stays), corset brace, corset-style fashion garments (little or no boning). Boning: steel (spring or spiral), synthetic whalebone, plastic, historically whalebone, reed, cane; boning sits in channels. Busk: stiff flat front panel (wood or metal), split busk with hooks, spoon busk. Lacing: back lacing, top-down, bottom-up or both ("bunny ears"), metal eyelets. Fabrics: coutil, cotton, silk, satin, wool, leather, stretch jersey, mesh; lace trim and embroidery; contrast-colour boning; black common. Silhouettes: conical, hourglass or wasp-waisted, S-curve, longline.
CATSUIT: one-piece form-fitting garment covering torso and legs, often arms; fabrics lycra, spandex, chiffon, latex, velour, leather, PVC; front or back zipper; unitards are sport versions.
STILETTO HEEL: long thin heel, about 2.5 cm to 25 cm or more with platform; under 1 cm across at the ground; metal shaft inside; kitten heel (5 cm or less); semi-stiletto (thicker, cone-shaped); platform stilettos; pointed toes (1960s) or round toes (1980s).
DOMINATRIX COSTUME: one-piece black leather catsuit, latex and PVC clothing, tight leather corset, neck corset, long opera gloves or fingerless gloves, fishnet or seamed stockings with garter belt, peaked cap (uniform-inspired), thigh-high boots in leather or glossy PVC ("kinky boots"), very high stilettos, leather boots; black is the main colour; carried: flogger, whip, riding crop; "bizarre style" adds claws and tail whips; hoods and gas masks in medical-themed looks.
STRAIT JACKET: early canvas (sometimes leather), long sleeves tied around the back, closed at the back with buttons and strings, stockingette roll at the collar; variants cover the head, restrain the legs or secure to a bed.

PVC CLOTHING: woven polyester backing with a shiny PVC/polyurethane coating; two layers; pure PVC is stiff with a high gloss, polyurethane-rich is stretchy with a silkier shine; colours black, red, white, blue, orange, pink, silver, striped, transparent (1960s to early 1970s); garments: raincoats, boots, dresses, jackets, skirts, trousers, trouser suits, belts, bikinis, thigh-high boots; reads as bright, reflective, futuristic.
