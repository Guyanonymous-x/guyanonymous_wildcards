a doll shop window seen from the street. Behind a big pane of glass, dolls stand and sit on tiered shelves draped in velvet, porcelain dolls in lace dresses and bonnets, cloth dolls with yarn hair, slim fashion dolls in boxes with clear windows and a row of nesting dolls in painted red and gold. Across the top of the glass, gold painted letters outlined in black read "THE DOLL CORNER" in an arch. Small paper tags on strings hang from the dolls' wrists, the nearest reading "$12" in pencil, and a little doll carriage of wicker stands at the front holding a sleeping doll in a white bonnet. __doll_doll_shop_var__.
