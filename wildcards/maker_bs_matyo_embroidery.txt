Styled in a design language of dense, thick, glossy thread worked on black, white or dark blue cloth. Motifs are huge, full-blown roses, bold tulips, carnations, leaf sprays, buds and tendrils in heavily stylised form, often with a central flower surrounded by smaller blossoms and swirling leaves in mirrored symmetry. Palette is bright, high-contrast and joyful, red, pink, magenta, orange, yellow, light blue and green on black, or navy, crimson and deep green on white. Tight satin stitch gives a slightly raised, padded texture with black or gold outlines. Richly decorated, colourful, joyous and festive, as if a flower garden covered every surface. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
