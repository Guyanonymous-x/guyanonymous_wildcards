In the style of Kutahya and Iznik ceramics, Ottoman fritware tiles, plates and mosque lamps painted under a clear glaze with intense cobalt blue, turquoise, sage green, tomato red and white, in patterns of tulips, carnations, hyacinths, rosebuds, saz leaves, and curling arabesques. Radiating floral rosettes, repeated flowers on tile panels, symmetrical and flowing. Glossy, bright, crisp, elegant, a mosque wall and bazaar shelf look.
