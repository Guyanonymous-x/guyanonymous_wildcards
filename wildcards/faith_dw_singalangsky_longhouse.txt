a great longhouse of dark hardwood stands on tall stilts among white clouds, its roof of palm thatch running along its whole length and a wide open drying platform at its front
inside the sky longhouse a long covered gallery stretches into the distance, mats of rattan laid on its floor, gongs and jars standing along its wall, and old smoked skulls hung in rattan nets from the rafters
the longhouse of Singalang Burung rises above the clouds, a carved hornbill on a tall pole before it and a ladder carved from one log leading up to its door
