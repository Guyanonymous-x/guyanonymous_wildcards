Styled in a design language of volunteer first-aid design with uniforms in black and white or dark green, white eight-pointed cross symbols, ambulances in yellow-green and blue high-visibility chequered panels, green first-aid kits with white crosses, crisp white shirts, and a kindly, dependable, civic-volunteer character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
