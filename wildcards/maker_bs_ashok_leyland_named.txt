In the style of Ashok Leyland, rugged, colourful, overloaded, hand-decorated Indian heavy-duty design. Boxy upright forms with flat vertical faces, plain rectangular fronts with a single horizontal bar, wide round lamps and a high stance. Paint is solid blue, red, yellow or cream, worn and faded, with thick hand painted borders, flower and peacock scenes, bold slogans in painted lettering and chequered stripes. Strings of lucky charms and tassels dangle from edges. Rounded steel or fibreglass surfaces in faded red and cream, with simple metal bars as protection. Gritty, dusty, golden hour mood.
