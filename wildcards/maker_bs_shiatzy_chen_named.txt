Styled in a design language of elegant East meets West design that blends tailored modern cuts with Chinese tradition. Standing mandarin collars, frog knot closures and asymmetric side fastenings sit on slim, refined, graceful shapes. Fabrics are silk satin, brocade, jacquard, embroidered tulle and wool crepe, with embroidered or woven motifs of peonies, bamboo, cranes. Palette favours jade green, imperial red, ink black, ivory, deep blue and gold thread, with delicate piping, braided trim and tassel details. A poised, quiet-luxury, nostalgic old-city mood in soft studio light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
