Styled in a design language of Swedish art glass, thick, crystal-clear and heavy-walled, with deep engraved or wheel-cut scenes, ethereal trapped air bubbles and abstract wavy colour patterns inside the material. Forms are streamlined, tapering and smooth, in a cool transparent palette with accents of slate blue, smoky grey and soft green. Understated, refined, sculptural Scandinavian modernism, pure and sparkling in cold light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
