Styled in a design language of elegant, formal, light and slightly romantic Portuguese porcelain. Forms have scrolling handles, high feet and flower-shaped finials. Modern versions turn to bold graphic prints, hand-painted strokes in cobalt, black or coral and sculptural art editions. Traditional palette is white, gold, navy, rose pink and sage green. Tiny gilt crowns and symmetrical arrangements. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
