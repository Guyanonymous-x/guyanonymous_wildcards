Styled in a design language of parisian couture refinement with soft feminine grace. Silhouettes are structured and sculpted, with nipped waists, rounded curves, full flowing volumes and delicate ribbon bows. Palette is dove grey, pearl white, blush pink, navy, black and touches of gold. Packaging uses grey boxes, ribbons and gold-sparkled prints. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
