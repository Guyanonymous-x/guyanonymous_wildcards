Styled in a design language of flat limited-colour cartoon design with confident thick dark outlines and solid fills with almost no shading. Shapes are simple, blocky and rounded, with big ovals, wide stylised noses and bold geometric simplification of anything natural. Palette is mid-century saturated orange, turquoise, mustard, brick red, avocado green, sky blue and brown, often as loose painted blobs and speckled textures with a slightly gaudy retro charm. Repeating motifs include stylised rocks, leaves and zigzag stone patterns. The look is breezy, cheeky and cheerfully retro. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
