In the style of Lisa Folawiyo, Lagos womenswear mixing African wax print cotton with heavy hand-embellishment, using beads, sequins, crystals, pearls, metallic thread and cowrie-like details sewn in dense clusters across bodices, collars, sleeves and hems. Silhouettes are modern and sculptural, with cropped jackets, peplum waists, structured shoulders, ruffled trumpet skirts, fitted dresses, wide trousers and playful cut-outs. Prints are bold and graphic in electric blue, tangerine, fuchsia, yellow, emerald and black and white, with embroidery that highlights the pattern outlines. Glamorous, festive, Afro-modern mood, glossy with sparkle and contrast textures.
