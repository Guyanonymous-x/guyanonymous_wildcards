Styled in a design language of cheerful, friendly, confident Dutch design in a single bright sky blue against clean white. The signature is flat colour blocking with a large sweep of cornflower or azure, sometimes broken by thin horizontal pinstripes in two blues. Finishes are glossy enamel and clean matte paint with crisp edges. Palette stays disciplined with sky blue, white, navy and a rare touch of orange. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
