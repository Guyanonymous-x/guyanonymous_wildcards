Styled in a design language of minimalist, clean and futuristic, with a software-first calm. Surfaces are smooth and unbroken, closed-off and flush, with low sloping lines, slim light bands, large glass planes and almost no chrome or visible fasteners. Palette is pearl white, solid black, graphite, midnight silver and one strong red, in sharp, high-contrast layouts. Materials are matte black, brushed metal, white vegan-leather-like upholstery and pale wood strips with nearly no buttons. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
