In the style of Nothing, transparent tech gadgets. Clear see-through plastic and glass casings that reveal internal components, circuit boards, screws and coils. Palette is white, black and transparent with a signature bright red dot accent. Phone backs have glowing white LED light strips arranged in segments around the camera. Typography is dot-matrix lettering with round dots forming monochrome numerals, and icons are pixelated. Edges are flat and squared with soft corners. Mood is stripped-back, nerdy, futuristic and quirky.
