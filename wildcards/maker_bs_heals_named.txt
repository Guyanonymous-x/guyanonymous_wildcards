In the style of Heals, English Arts and Crafts and early modern design in plain, light limed oak and fumed oak, with simple rectilinear forms, square-section legs, chamfered edges, brass or iron hardware, cut-out hearts and slender tall proportions. Painted pastel finishes with delicate stencilled flowers, bold geometric printed linen and cotton in modern greens, oranges and inky blues. Tidy, sensible and cottage-modern. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
