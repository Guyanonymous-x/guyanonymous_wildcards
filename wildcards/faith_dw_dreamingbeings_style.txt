painted in acrylic on canvas in the manner of Central and Western Desert dot painting, the country seen from above as concentric circles for waterholes and camps, curving lines for travel and U-shapes for people sitting, built from countless dots of red, yellow ochre, white and black
painted on a flattened sheet of stringybark in the manner of Arnhem Land bark painting, in natural red and yellow ochre, white pipeclay and charcoal, the figures filled with rarrk, fine crosshatched lines drawn with a brush of a few long hairs
printed in the manner of a Torres Strait Islander linocut, bold black and white, the sea creatures, canoes and star figures cut with sharp edges and filled with dense patterns of waves and scales, with touches of ocean blue
painted in the x-ray manner of the rock art of western Arnhem Land, figures of animals and ancestors on a pale rock wall in red and white, their backbones and inner organs drawn inside their bodies
