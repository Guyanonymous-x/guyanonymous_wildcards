In the style of Guinness, dark Irish heritage with polished, premium restraint. Rich near-black grounds with creamy off-white and warm gold accents, with a single golden harp emblem placed centrally, drawn in fine, elegant outlines. Lettering is slim, tall, classic serif capitals with a looping handwritten flourish beneath, often gold-foiled or embossed. Layouts are symmetrical, orderly and spacious, with thin gold borders and a creamy foam-like white tone against deep black. Packaging uses matte black and bright, patterned colour panels with gold detailing, and sometimes festive illustrated motifs in blue, green, yellow and red. Photography is warm, low-lit, candlelit pub atmosphere with amber glow, wood, brick and friendly gatherings.
