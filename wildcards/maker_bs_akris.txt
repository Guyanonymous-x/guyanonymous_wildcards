In the style of Akris, Swiss quiet-luxury womenswear built on architectural minimalism, with precisely cut tailored jackets, straight trousers, sheath dresses, A-line skirts and clean coats in double-faced wool, gabardine, silk crepe and crisp cotton. Palette is restrained, with black, ivory, camel, charcoal, navy, sand and soft grey, with occasional restrained accents of coral or deep green. Details are subtle, such as laser-cut edges, flat welt seams, bonded hems, hidden closures and geometric architectural prints inspired by buildings. Silhouettes are clean, sharp and balanced, with a refined, understated, polished boardroom mood and cool gallery-like light.
