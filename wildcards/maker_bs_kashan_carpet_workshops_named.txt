Styled in a design language of closely knotted surfaces with a velvet-soft short texture and a slightly glossy sheen, in a deep luxurious palette of claret and ruby red, midnight blue and ivory. A large lobed central medallion with pendants floats on a dense field of scrolling vines, palmettes and arabesque blossoms, framed by a wide border of repeating floral cartouches. Corners repeat quarter medallions. Fine ivory outlines define stems and leaves. Detail is tiny and exact, symmetry is perfect, and the mood is formal, rich and elegant. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
