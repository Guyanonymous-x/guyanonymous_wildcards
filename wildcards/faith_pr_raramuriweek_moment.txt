on the last day the two sides meet in a wild mock fight in the churchyard, the Pharisees and the Soldiers grappling and wrestling in the dust, falling and rising, laughing, and the Soldiers win
the straw and cloth figure of Judas, dressed in an old hat and shirt and propped on a pole, is shot with old rifles and set on fire, flames and white smoke rising from it on Saturday
through the night the dancers circle the church again and again, step after step, past small fires burning in the churchyard, their painted bodies lit orange by the flames
women in their twenties to seventies carry the wooden statue of the Virgin out of the church in a slow procession, the Soldiers walking on either side with lances held upright
