In the style of Converse, classic American court and garage-band heritage in its simplest form, for any product. Lettering is bold, wide, collegiate capitals, sometimes slanted and outlined, often in navy and red. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Converse in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
