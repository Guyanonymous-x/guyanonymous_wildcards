San Rafael, a hilly town of old houses and a Spanish mission church, wooden Victorian houses with bay windows and porches climbing tree-lined streets, and on a rise above downtown a small white mission chapel with plain plastered walls, a red clay tile roof, a star-shaped window over its door and a row of bells hung on a wooden frame beside it
a main street of shops, a straight downtown street lined with two-story brick and stucco storefronts from the early 1900s, striped awnings over plate-glass windows, a movie theater with a tall vertical tower on its front, long 1970s sedans parked along both curbs and wooded hills closing the view at the far end
green hills above the town, steep rounded slopes covered in grass that is bright green in the rainy months, dark clumps of live oak and bay trees in the folds, houses half hidden among them on winding roads, and the long blue ridge of Mount Tamalpais rising behind
