Styled in a design language of energetic, patriotic, confident, youthful sportswear with bold national flavour mixing retro sports nostalgia and streetwear flair. A red geometric stroke like a flowing check mark or abstract athlete over bold red Chinese characters, often on white or black. Strong colour blocks of imperial red, black, white and metallic gold. Stand collars, broad contrast stripes down the arms and legs, glossy nylon and retro 1990s team colours in red, yellow and green. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
