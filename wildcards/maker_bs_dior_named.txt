In the style of Dior, the postwar silhouette of a tiny cinched waist, rounded sloping shoulders, emphasised bust and a voluminous calf-length sweep of fabric, pleated or cut as a full circle and held out by stiff underlayers. Structured forms in pale dove grey or black wool, with tulle and silk faille, sculpted curves, floral embroidery and rosettes. Soft greys, pearl white, black, powder pink and tiny lily-of-the-valley motifs. Quilted lattice with stitched diamonds, houndstooth, pearls and small dangling gold charms. Couture precision, ultra feminine, ladylike and opulent. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
