the Circular Mound Altar rises in three round tiers of white marble with carved white railings, open to the sky, a great round stone at its center
on the altar stands a spirit tablet of deep blue lacquer reading "皇天上帝" in gold, a canopy of blue silk above it, bronze vessels of food and wine set before it
at the altar the offerings are laid out in rows, bolts of blue silk, jade disks, bronze vessels of grain and a whole roasted ox on a long table
