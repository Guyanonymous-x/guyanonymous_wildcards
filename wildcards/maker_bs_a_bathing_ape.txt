Styled in a design language of loud, cartoonish, collectible streetwear graphics. A simple ape head in side profile serves as the emblem, often a tiny patch on a plain field. Camouflage is the core pattern, a large blobby shark-skin style camo in unexpected bright colourways such as lime green, pink, purple, yellow, blue and rainbow gradients, alongside classic olive and grey versions. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
