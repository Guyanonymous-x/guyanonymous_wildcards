Styled in a design language of nostalgic, glamorous, carefully crafted Art Deco and Belle Epoque decoration. Lavishly patterned lithographic prints of peacock feathers, ferns, roses, swallows, geometric fans, arcs and rays. Colours are rich and layered, teal and gold, burgundy and cream, coral and navy, emerald and gold, with metallic foil stamping. Rounded vintage glass. Colourful rows against warm wood. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
