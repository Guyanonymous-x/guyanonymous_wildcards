In the style of Delahaye, flamboyant, theatrical, curvaceous Art Deco coachwork. Very long sweeping lines, a teardrop body, pontoon forms flowing over the base, a long mass running into a sloping end, fine chrome bars and chrome spears running through the curves. Two-tone schemes such as ivory over dark blue, silver over burgundy and deep black. Cream or red leather and engine-turned metal with round faces. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Delahaye in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
