Styled in a design language of dark Irish heritage with polished, premium restraint. Rich near-black grounds with creamy off-white and warm gold accents, with a single golden harp emblem placed centrally, drawn in fine, elegant outlines. Packaging uses matte black and bright, patterned colour panels with gold detailing, and sometimes festive illustrated motifs in blue, green, yellow and red. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
