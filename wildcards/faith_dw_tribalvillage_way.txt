a Jarai grave house stands at the edge of the living village, a high thatched roof over the grave, carved wooden figures around it of a squatting man carved nude with his genitals carved plainly, a weeping woman and a pregnant woman
a buffalo is tied to a carved post before the grave house, its throat cut by a man in his thirties, its blood running onto the earth for the dead
a Sora shaman, a woman in her fifties, sits in a trance before the painted wall, speaking with the voice of the dead, a pot of rice beer beside her
