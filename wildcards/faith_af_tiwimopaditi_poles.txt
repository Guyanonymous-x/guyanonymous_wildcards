around the grave stand the tutini, the Pukumani poles, tall posts of carved ironwood rising well above the heads of the mourners, cut into windows, notches and pointed forms and painted all over in ochre dots and stripes
on top of the tallest poles sit tunga, bark baskets folded from a single sheet of stringybark and painted in red, yellow and white ochre, gifts left for the dead at the close of the ceremony
an older grave site in the bush, its tutini weathered pale gray and leaning, the ochre worn away by the rains, the poles left to fall back into the earth as the ceremony asks
