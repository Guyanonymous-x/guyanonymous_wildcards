In the style of Din Tai Fung, meticulous, hygienic, bustling and warm, with an exacting clean presentation. Small steamed parcels with paper-thin translucent skins, each gathered by hand into a tidy spiral of fine pleats with a little knot at the top, sitting in a round bamboo steamer with glints of broth showing. Palette of cream, bamboo tan, steam white and one red accent. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Din Tai Fung in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
