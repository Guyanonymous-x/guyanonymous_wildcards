Styled in a design language of sculptural, weightless, inventive design that treats cloth as architecture. Fine, permanent, heat-set pleats run in crisp vertical, horizontal or zigzag rows, springing back into shape and catching light in rippling waves. Geometric forms such as tubes, cubes, circles and folded origami-like volumes expand and collapse flexibly. Textiles are lightweight polyester, crinkled and bonded technical weaves, sheer layers and stretchy jersey, shown matte and slightly shimmering. Palette alternates between muted charcoal, ivory, black and indigo and sudden bursts of neon lime, tangerine, cobalt, magenta and bright turquoise, with playful colour-blocking and bold abstract graphics. Clever, light, joyful, modern and wonderfully free. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
