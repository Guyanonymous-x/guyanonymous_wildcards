in the old secret consort practice of the Hevajra Tantra, Tenpa, a yogi in his thirties, and his consort Dechen, a woman in her twenties, both naked, sit in sexual union in the yab-yum pose, Dechen on his lap facing him with her legs wrapped around his waist and her arms around his neck, both still, eyes half closed in meditation
the monks sit still with eyes half closed and hands resting in their laps, the right hand on the left, thumbs touching, in deep meditation
each monk holds a small brass vajra, a short scepter with prongs at each end, in the right hand and a small brass bell in the left, the arms crossed at the chest
a monk in his fifties at the front builds a mandala offering, heaping rice and colored stones on a round silver plate in rings, and lifts it in both hands toward the painting
on a low table before the painting monks lay out a mandala of colored sand, a square palace within circles, in white, blue, yellow, red and green, bending over it with thin metal funnels
