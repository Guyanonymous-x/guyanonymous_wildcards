Styled in a design language of proud Victorian-inspired engineering elegance. Gleaming deep green paint with orange and black lining, polished brass and copper accents, black dark-toned ends and red bases. Tall brass-edged nameplates sit on curved forms. Secondary colours are cream and chocolate brown. Metal is hand-polished, lines are fine and hand-drawn, and every part is crafted to be admired. Stately, dignified, traditional and richly finished. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
