Styled in a design language of brutal, loud, cartoonish tough-guy American muscle of the early 1970s. Wide, aggressive, squared-off forms with bulging humps, a pronounced hip curve, and a bold bumblebee band or thick vertical stripes wrapped around the body. Stripes are black over yellow, red, orange or grey, sometimes with a ducktail kick shape. A round badge and a ram motif act as marks. Surfaces are slotted metal textures, glossy high-impact paint, black vinyl and chunky proportions. Palette is violet, lime green, purple, bright orange, pale green, yellow and black. Lettering is heavy, blocky, slanted capitals with stencilled hard edges. Swaggering, noisy and showy. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
