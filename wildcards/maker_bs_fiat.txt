Styled in a design language of tiny, round, cheerful and playful post-war Italian design. Bulbous egg-like and bubble forms, softly curved tops, thin chrome edges and simple rounded rectangles. Square or round badges in red with white capital lettering and small crossed bar marks give a friendly, repeated emblem. Palette is pastel yellow, powder blue, mint green, cream, cherry red, orange and white, often in flat, single-colour fields. Surfaces are painted body-colour metal, simple vinyl, canvas and lightly polished chrome. Lettering is bold, rounded, slightly condensed sans-serif capitals. Flat vintage illustration blocks with simple shapes and bright optimistic colour make everything feel light and sunny. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
