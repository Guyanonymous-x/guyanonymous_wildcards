Styled in a design language of British heritage with a lean, confident edge. Surfaces are solid enamel in cream, silver, deep green, racing red and blaze yellow, divided by crisp two-tone breaks and thin hand-painted gold pinstripes. Brushed and polished metal, black satin, ribbed rubber and rich brown leather provide contrast. Small enamel roundels, knurled textures and tidy rivets add craft. Proportions feel compact, tapered and understated, nostalgic yet sporty. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
