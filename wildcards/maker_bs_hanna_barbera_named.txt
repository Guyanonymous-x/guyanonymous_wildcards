Styled in a design language of flat limited-colour cartoon design with confident thick dark outlines and solid fills with almost no shading. Shapes are simple, blocky and rounded, with big ovals, wide stylised noses and bold geometric simplification of anything natural. Palette is mid-century saturated orange, turquoise, mustard, brick red, avocado green, sky blue and brown, often as loose painted blobs and speckled textures with a slightly gaudy retro charm. Repeating motifs include stylised rocks, leaves and zigzag stone patterns. The look is breezy, cheeky and cheerfully retro. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
