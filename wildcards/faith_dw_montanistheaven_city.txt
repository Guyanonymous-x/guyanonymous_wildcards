the New Jerusalem comes down from the clouds as a great square city of shining walls of jasper, twelve gates of pearl, three on each side, and towers capped with gold, slowly settling toward the plain
the heavenly city hangs low in the sky over the plain, its walls of pale green stone glowing, its gates open, a broad golden street running through it, light pouring down from it onto the fields
the city descends in a ring of white clouds, its walls set with bands of colored stones, blue, green, red and violet, angels in long white robes standing on its walls looking down at the waiting faithful
