In the style of Aeroflot, Soviet utilitarian, solid, no-nonsense. Bare polished aluminium surfaces with a thick pale blue stripe, a white winged emblem, and a red field carrying a heavy bold gold device within two wings. Large condensed Cyrillic capitals painted boldly. Drab greens and greys, patterned curtains and thick velour. Palette is silver, light blue, red and olive green. Mood is sturdy, functional, imposing and unadorned, with big letters and a heavy winged emblem.
