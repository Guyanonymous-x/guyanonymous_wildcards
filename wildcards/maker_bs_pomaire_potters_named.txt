In the style of Pomaire Potters, earthy, handmade, humble and warm rustic terracotta. Heavy, thick-walled forms, unglazed or lightly burnished, in warm reddish-brown to dark grey-brown clay with a smooth matte surface that darkens with use. Shapes are chunky and round, with two ear handles, wide shallow curves and plump volumes. Plump little animal figures and small pinched and moulded folk figures add charm. Some are painted with simple flat colours or white slip dots and stripes, others left plain with finger marks and uneven rims. Kiln smoke gives mottled black clouds and variations. Rough wooden shelves in an adobe market. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Pomaire Potters in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
