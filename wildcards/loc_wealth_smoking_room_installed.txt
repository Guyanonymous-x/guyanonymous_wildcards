a smoking room set apart from the main rooms
a half-landing
a wing end or a basement; a position chosen so that the route from it to the bedrooms does not pass through a principal room, which in several houses means a back stair is provided purely so that men could go to bed without carrying smoke through the hall; walls matchboarded
papered in a dark heavy paper or hung with Moorish or Indian textiles and leather paper
every one of which was chosen to not show smoke; a ceiling that will be re-papered rather than re-painted; a floor of coir or rush matting with a rug over it, because matting can be taken up and beaten and a fitted carpet cannot; a chimneypiece with a good flue and often a hood
frieze ventilators and a heavy draft curtain
deliberately, so that no servant need come in
