In the style of Fes Zellij Workshops, mosaic panels of hand-cut glazed clay tiles inlaid into an interlocking geometric puzzle. Stars with eight, twelve or sixteen points radiate from the centre, with kite, hexagon and chevron pieces in vibrant glossy colours, namely cobalt blue, emerald green, saffron yellow, white, black, deep red-brown and turquoise. Fountains, walls and floors carry the repeating tessellations, and edges are crisp with thin white grout lines. Borders carry calligraphic bands or carved plaster, and surfaces sit below carved cedar and stucco. The result is a precise, endlessly repeating, jewel-like pattern.
