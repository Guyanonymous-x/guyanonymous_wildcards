Styled in a design language of joyful, earthy, handmade, colourful and sunny glazed clay design. Hand rolled and shaped pieces, slightly unique and irregular, in round balls, discs, short tubes, cones, ovals, chunky cylinders and tiny seeds, with smooth glass-like surfaces. Colours include cobalt blue, cherry red, mustard yellow, forest green, turquoise, terracotta orange, black, white and speckled earthy brown. Some are hand-painted with swirls, dots, stripes and zigzags in contrasting glaze. They are grouped chunky and mixed in many colours, in long ropes and multiple strands. Matching speckled glaze appears on simple rounded forms. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
