In the style of Savoy Hotel, a black and silver Art Deco courtyard and foyer with chromed trim, geometric stepped mouldings, curved glass, mirrored panels and sleek brass. The entrance has a tall gold statue on a column and a steel canopy above a one-way courtyard. Interiors with silver leaf, stylised sunburst motifs, lacquered woodwork, plush deep carpets and curving staircases. Palette of black, silver, gold and ivory. The look is streamlined, glamorous, 1930s Art Deco, with stepped geometry, lustrous materials and bold symmetry.
