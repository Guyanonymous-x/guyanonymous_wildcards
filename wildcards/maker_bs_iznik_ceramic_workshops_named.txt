Styled in a design language of bright white ground painted under a brilliant clear glaze with vivid fresh colour. The palette is cobalt blue, turquoise, emerald green and a thick, slightly raised tomato red, all drawn crisply with fine black outlines. Patterns are stylised garden flowers such as tulips, carnations and hyacinths, with long curling feathery leaves and cloud bands, arranged symmetrically in flowing arabesques that fill the surface and run along borders and arches. Surfaces are glossy, crisp and fresh, with rhythmic, balanced repetition that feels like a flowering garden. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
