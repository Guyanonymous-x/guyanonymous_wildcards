In the style of Faber-Castell, scholarly European heritage in deep green and gold. Dark forest and emerald green lacquer with matte and satin depth, paired with ivory, cream, black, brown and warm metallic gold or silver foil. The emblem shows two knights on horseback inside a small crest, with a refined serif wordmark in wide-spaced capitals and a tiny founding-year line beneath, in white on dark green. Surfaces feature fine hot-foil stamping, subtle embossing, rows of graded colour in neat ordered fans and polished wood, knurled metal and dark leather for luxury lines. Tins and boxes are dark green with gold lettering. Classic, craftsmanlike, dignified and warm. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Faber-Castell in that lettering style and no other words.
