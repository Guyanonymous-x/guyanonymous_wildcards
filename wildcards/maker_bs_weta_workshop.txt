In the style of Weta Workshop, a New Zealand creature and prop studio. Pieces look convincingly real and handmade, such as battle-worn medieval and fantasy armour, helmets with scratched edges, sculpted swords, shields, rune-etched blades, leather bracers, mail shirts with heavy ring links and weathered cloaks. Surfaces show distressed paint, rust-brown stains, dull pewter, tarnished bronze and bone-white enamel, with scratches, dents, mud streaks and worn leather straps. Creatures are massive and sculpted with pores, veins, wrinkles, hairs, tusks and wet glossy eyes, in silicone skin painted with subtle layers of olive, brown, ochre and grey. A sibling line of retro ray guns and steampunk blasters are fat chunky brass and copper rocket-like objects with dials, fins, vacuum tubes and lettering. The look is gritty, textured, painterly, detailed and storybook-epic.
