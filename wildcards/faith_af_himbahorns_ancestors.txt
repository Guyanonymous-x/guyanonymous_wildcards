at the okuruwo, the holy fire in the homestead, a low ring of embers between the round headman's hut and the thorn-branch kraal, the headman squats with a bowl of milk, and above the smoke rise the ancestors in their sixties to nineties, faint red-brown figures in leather and beads
the ancestors stand on the far hills as a line of tall faint figures in their sixties to nineties, men in headwraps and women with ochre plaits, looking down over the herds and the homestead
inside the kraal the sacred cattle of the ancestors stand in the dust, one ox with spreading horns tied near the holy fire, the headman in his sixties touching its back
