Styled in a design language of joyful, hallucinatory, highly saturated carved and painted folk art. Light soft wood is hand-carved into lively mythical creatures, often with a human face or braided hair on an animal body, caught mid-action while flying, scratching or leaping. After a smooth base coat, the whole body is covered in intricate fine-line ornament in vivid colours of magenta, turquoise, saffron yellow, orange, leaf green and black, in dense repeating patterns of dots, scrolls, flowers, plants, stepped geometric motifs and ancient temple-wall designs. Eyes are large and glossy. Light satin finish, with every surface patterned and nothing left blank. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
