painted in the manner of a New Kingdom tomb wall, flat clear colors of red ochre, yellow, blue and green on smooth white plaster, every figure with the head and legs in side view and the shoulders turned flat to the viewer
painted on a funerary papyrus scroll, fine black outlines filled with soft colors on pale cream reed paper, a ruled border along the top and columns of small painted picture signs beside each god
carved in the manner of a temple relief at Karnak or Edfu, the figures cut in sunk relief into golden sandstone and painted in bright colors, the edges sharp and the faded paint still bright in the deep cuts
worked as royal tomb jewelry, the figures in polished gold inlaid with dark blue lapis, red carnelian and turquoise glass, each feather and bead picked out in the stones
