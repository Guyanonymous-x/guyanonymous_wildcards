before the shrine the god's boat, a slim gilded model ship as long as two men lying down, a ram's head or falcon's head at prow and stern, a small veiled cabin amidships hiding the statue, resting on a stone block with its carrying poles laid alongside
the closed doors of the shrine, tied shut with a cord and sealed with a lump of clay, a single oil lamp on a tall stand lighting the gold and the painted walls where rows of small carved pictures of birds, eyes, reeds and seated figures run in columns from ceiling to floor
