Styled in a design language of a flat rectangular label in saturated signal red with heavy white italic capitals in a squeezed, bold sans-serif face, used like a stamp that can sit on any surface and be repeated or tiled as an all-over pattern. Palette of red, white and black with occasional neon or pastel variations and checkerboard or camouflage fills. Crisp screen-print ink, slightly raised and cracked vinyl texture, woven jacquard and chunky embroidery, thick outlines and deadpan minimal wording. Smooth heavy cotton, fleece, nylon and glossy enamel finishes keep everything blunt, cool, smug and deliberately ironic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
