In the style of Datsun, 1970s Japanese small sports cars and trucks with a long, low, rounded bonnet, a low oval mouth under thin chrome bumpers, round headlamps set in bright bezels and a short, tidy fastback body. Small tail lamps, thin pillars, a black rubber bumper insert, hubcaps or five slot steel wheels, side stripes, and a small bright badge on the front fender. Pickup trucks have a plain slab body with a chrome grille. Interior with simple black vinyl, round white-on-black dials, thin steering wheel with horn ring. Colours of orange, lime yellow, sky blue and white. Compact, cheerful, honest.
