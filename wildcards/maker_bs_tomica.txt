Styled in a design language of tidy, bright, practical, collectable and slightly retro miniature diecast design. Small compact forms in one glossy solid enamel colour such as white, red, yellow, light blue or metallic silver, with clear glazing, silver painted trim, tiny printed badges and parts that really open. Chrome five-spoke details on small black rubber. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
