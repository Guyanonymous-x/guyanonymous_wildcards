__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of intricate, richly coloured silk prints drawn from pre-Hispanic and folk culture, such as sun-disc calendars, jaguars, eagles, serpents, agave, skulls, floral crowns, hummingbirds, corn, cacti, tile patterns and embroidery. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
