__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of honest, rugged New England outdoor craft. Materials are heavy cotton duck canvas, brushed flannel in red, navy and green plaid checks, chunky wool knit, waxed fabric and tan or brown leather trim with bold doubled seam lines. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
