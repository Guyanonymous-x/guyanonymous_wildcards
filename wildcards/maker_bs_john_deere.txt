Styled in a design language of rural American utility with a bold two-colour identity. A vivid leaf green paired with strong sunflower yellow, in flat solid blocks and clean contrasting edges. A leaping deer silhouette in yellow sits inside a rounded square or framed green panel with a thin yellow outline. Stitched patches, woven twill, heavy canvas, enamelled metal plaques and matte painted surfaces carry the mark. Simple, symmetrical, no-nonsense layout with sturdy proportions, practical, dependable, honest and recognisable at a glance. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
