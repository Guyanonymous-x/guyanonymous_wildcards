The item is made from tanned human skin in a __sat_skin__ tone, cut from a matched set of hides so the colour is even apart from the slight natural variation of real skin. It looks like processed human skin, smooth and almost hairless, with fine pores, soft creases, faint fine lines and the occasional freckle or mole, a dry, slightly waxy tanned finish like old parchment or a well-worn saddle, and darker creases where it folds. Seams follow the irregular outlines of the original hides and are stitched with thick waxed thread. A few small human traces show at true human scale, in the same colour as the skin around them.
