Styled in a design language of compact, practical Japanese design with a bold, sharply angled letter S emblem made from two opposing pointed strokes. Palette is red, cobalt blue, white and bright yellow label tones, with dependable greys, black and cheerful pastel accents. Shapes are small, tall, sturdy and friendly, with simple squared-off forms, gently rounded corners and clear flat panels. Finishes are matte plastic, painted enamel, ribbed rubber and sticker-like flat graphics. Reflective chrome versions of the emblem and bold racing-style decals add a lively, energetic touch. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
