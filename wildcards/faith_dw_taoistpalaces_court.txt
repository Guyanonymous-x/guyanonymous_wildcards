the court of the Jade Emperor spreads among the clouds, red-pillared halls with golden roofs rising in tiers, ministers of heaven in their forties to their sixties in long robes holding tablets standing in rows, guards in armor at the gates
high above the court the Three Pure Ones, the highest gods of Taoism, three old men in their seventies with long white beards in robes of red, yellow and blue, sit on lotus thrones in a halo of light
