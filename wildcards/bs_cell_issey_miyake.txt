__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of sculptural, weightless, inventive design that treats cloth as architecture. Fine, permanent, heat-set pleats run in crisp vertical, horizontal or zigzag rows, springing back into shape and catching light in rippling waves. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
