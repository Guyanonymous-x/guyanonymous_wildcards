in winter, snow everywhere, deep white snow covering the fields, roofs and riverbanks, high plowed banks along the road, the river frozen and white, smoke rising from the chimneys and the silver steeple pale against a gray-white sky
in fall, the maples red, the trees along the street, the river and the field edges blazing scarlet, orange and gold, fallen leaves thick on the lawns and porches, the hay cut and the corn standing tan and dry under a clear blue sky
in summer, the fields green, lush hay and young corn in deep fresh greens under a bright blue sky with piled white clouds, flower boxes on the porches, long grass in the ditches and the river low and glinting in the sun
