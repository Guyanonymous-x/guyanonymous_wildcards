and the naked and robed sadhus charge into the river with arms raised and water splashing high, tridents held over their heads, dipping their whole bodies under the water three times and rising again with water streaming from their hair
and a woman in her sixties stands waist-deep in the river with palms pressed together, then dips under three times, and sets afloat a small cup of dried leaves holding marigold petals and a burning wick
and a man in his forties lifts water from the river in his cupped hands and pours it slowly back toward the rising sun, then fills a small brass pot with the holy water to carry home
and pilgrims in their twenties to sixties climb out onto the bank in dripping clothes, wringing out saris and dhotis, while a priest in his fifties under a large straw umbrella marks their foreheads with red and yellow paste
