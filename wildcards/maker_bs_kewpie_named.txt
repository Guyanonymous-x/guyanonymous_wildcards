In the style of Kewpie, cute, wholesome, babyish and nostalgic Japanese branding built around a chubby baby mascot. Soft squeezable forms in clear or translucent white plastic with bright red accents and star-shaped detailing that makes fine ridged lines. Surfaces are mostly white with a red wordmark and a small round doll figure with a cheerful face, wide-set eyes, rosy cheeks, plump belly, tiny wings and a single curled tuft of hair, drawn in simple flat outline with pink and cream fill. Net-like orange-red cross-hatched lattice patterns appear. Pale yellow backgrounds, egg-yolk golds, a limited red, yellow and white palette and a clean modern photographic finish.
