Styled in a design language of rugged, honest and expedition-ready utility for any product. Upright, flat-sided, squared-off forms with bluff faces, flat slab panels and exposed hinges, bolts and rivets. Matt and satin finishes in olive, bronze green, sand, pale grey, poppy red and white. Canvas, webbing, strapped-on carry frames, rubber mats and simple round dials carry a worked-hard spirit. Modern versions add clean utilitarian surfaces in black plastic, fabric and a bold exposed structural beam look. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
