each bather faces the rising sun, takes a deep breath with cheeks full, and ducks under, head and all, hair floating and spreading out in the water, then rises streaming
the bathers stand in a line waist-deep facing east, bending forward one after another to plunge their faces under, holding their breath, then turning to face west and dipping again
a woman in her forties rises from the water with her eyes closed and her long black hair plastered to her shoulders, and she scoops water in both palms and pours it over her head
after the seventh dip, the bathers wade back to the bank, where an elder in his sixties waits with folded blankets, and the healer brushes each bather with a cedar branch
