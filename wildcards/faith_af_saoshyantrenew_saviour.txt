Saoshyant, the last saviour, a man in his thirties with brown skin, long dark hair and a short beard, in a long white robe and a white kusti cord at the waist, stands on a mountaintop, a staff in his hand and rays of light around his head
beside the lake Kansaoya in the mountains of Sistan the maiden Vispa-taurvairi, a woman in her twenties in white, steps out of the clear blue water, and Saoshyant stands grown beside her, a man in his thirties in white
Saoshyant and his helpers, men in their thirties in white, kill the great ox Hadhayans with one cut to the throat and mix its fat with the white haoma into the drink of deathlessness for all people, the ox lying on its side by the fire altar
