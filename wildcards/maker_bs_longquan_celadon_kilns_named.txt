In the style of Longquan Celadon Kilns, thick-glazed stoneware in a soft, jade-like green, from pale plum and sea green to deep leaf green, with a lustrous, unctuous, slightly translucent finish that pools thicker in recesses. Forms are restrained and sculptural, with swelling shoulders and lotus-petal shapes. Decoration is minimal, with carved or moulded petals, incised combed lines and sometimes a fine crackle network. Edges fade lighter where the glaze thins, and bare unglazed areas burn to russet brown. Quiet, scholarly and understated, like polished river stone.
