In the style of Porsche, a compact rear-engined sports coupe with a sloping fastback roofline that falls from the windscreen to the tail, round headlamps rising from flared front wings, a low rounded nose, and wide muscular rear haunches. A smooth teardrop profile, a tail with a thin full-width light bar in modern versions, short overhangs, and a small retractable or fixed rear spoiler. Wheels are five-spoke alloys or forged star designs. Paint in guards red, silver grey, signal orange, racing yellow, gulf blue and black. A crest badge with a rearing horse sits on the nose. Interior has five round dials in a row under a deep hood, black leather and houndstooth cloth, aluminium trim, and a small leather wheel. Mood is purposeful, balanced and athletic.
