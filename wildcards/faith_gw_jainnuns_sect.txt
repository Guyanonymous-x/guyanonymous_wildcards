for a Svetambara nun, a white lower wrap tied at the waist and a white shawl over the shoulders and head, a soft whisk of white wool tufts on a short wooden handle held under one arm
for a Sthanakvasi or Terapanthi nun of the Svetambara sect, white wraps and a white shawl, and a small white cloth, the muhapatti, tied over the mouth by strings looped behind the ears
for a Digambara aryika, a single long white sari of plain cotton wound around the body and drawn up over the head, a whisk of glossy peacock feathers held in one hand and a small water pot of wood or brass in the other
for a Svetambara nun on her alms round, white wraps and shawl, a set of round wooden alms bowls wrapped in a white cloth bundle hanging from one hand and a tall thin wooden staff in the other
