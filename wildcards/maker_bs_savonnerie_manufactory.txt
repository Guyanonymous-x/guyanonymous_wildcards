Styled in a design language of thick, plush velvet texture with a sculptural, monumental pattern. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Wide borders imitate carved mouldings, with fruit and flowers in the corners. The feeling is of a royal palace, sumptuous, ordered and heavy. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
