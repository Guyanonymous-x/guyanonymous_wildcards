Styled in a design language of neat, tidy British sporting heritage with a mod and subculture edge. The emblem is a laurel wreath, stitched or printed in white, black or gold, often alone on a plain field. Surfaces are fine knitted pique, soft cotton, crisp woven twill and clean tailored lines. Signature graphic is the twin-tipped stripe in contrasting colours, appearing as trim on edges and borders. Palette is navy, white, black, racing green, burgundy and cream, with sharp contrast pairings like yellow on black or red and white on navy. Packaging is cream and navy cartons with simple wreath marks. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
