the dancers, women in their twenties to fifties with long black hair loose to the waist, wear pa'u skirts of brown and black patterned kapa, bark cloth, gathered at the hips, plain fitted tops of brown cloth, and thick lei of green maile vines over their shoulders, with bracelets of fern at their wrists and ankles
the dancers, women in their twenties to forties, bare above the waist in the old way, wear knee-length pa'u skirts of tapa bark cloth stamped with dark patterns, crowns of ferns on their heads and lei of yellow and red flowers around their necks
the dancers, men in their twenties to forties with strong brown bodies and short black hair, wear only malo, long loincloths of red kapa wrapped between the legs and around the waist, buttocks bare, with lei of green ti leaves and bracelets of dog teeth at the ankles
the dancers, women in their thirties to sixties of many builds, wear long full pa'u skirts of printed cotton in yellow and green, plain tank tops, and lei of white plumeria flowers, their hair tied back with fern
