In the style of Leica, understated, crafted, timeless precision. Compact rectangular forms with a flat top plane, in black enamel or polished silver chrome, wrapped in finely pebbled black leather-like texture. Fine knurled rings, slim barrel-like proportions, engraved white and red numerals and a tiny red dot accent. Brass shows through worn paint on edges, giving honest patina. Minimal, no clutter, with a small white script mark and every control exactly placed.
In the style of Hasselblad, serious, rugged, professional, sculptural. A cubic, square-profiled body in satin black paint with bright chrome trim, or an all-black crinkle texture. Modular blocks clip together with visible seams, large round forms in chrome or black, thin coloured rings and engraved white numerals. Angular crowning shapes and one large simple control. Bold white capital lettering. Special editions use bright colours or exposed carbon weave. Heavy, exact, engineered and block-like.
In the style of Polaroid, nostalgic, friendly, snapshot-casual 1970s consumer look. Bulky, sleek, boxy or folding forms in beige, white or black plastic with dark leather-like grip panels, one big circular feature and a bright rectangular accent. Chocolate-brown and tan palette with a rainbow stripe of red, orange, yellow, green and blue, and a big red rectangular control. Square images with a thick white border and a wide bottom margin, glossy, soft warm, slightly faded colour. Bold rounded white sans-serif lettering.
In the style of Nikon, rugged, professional, mechanical. Boxy forms with a raised angular hump on top, in black enamel or silver chrome with finely pebbled leather-like texture. Heavy metal knurled edges, chunky levers and a bold yellow and black stripe accent. Black cylindrical forms with deep rubber-ribbed rings and engraved white, orange and green numerals. Bold white capital lettering on the raised centre. Everything feels tough, dense and made to be used hard.
In the style of Canon, dependable, practical, quietly handsome. Compact boxy forms with a black or silver top plane, wood-grain or fine black leather-like covering, and rows of tiny metal dials and small silver buttons. Clean rectangular layout with a large angular crown, round dials and thick levers. Black cylindrical forms with subtle knurling and a thin red ring accent for top-grade pieces. Lettering is a stylised red script mark. Tidy, well-judged and unflashy.
In the style of Fujifilm, tactile, classic, clean and understated retro-modern design. Compact lean forms with a flat top plane in silver and black, matte black paint over metal and fine textured grips. Many machined aluminium dials with engraved numerals, and chunky rings with engraved scales wrapping round cylinders. A small offset window in one corner, red ruler-like marks and neat black pebbled texture. Lettering is crisp white capitals. Feels hands-on, analogue and calm.
In the style of Kodak, cheerful, familiar and snapshot-friendly. Early forms are leather-covered boxes with a round lens-like feature and a simple window. Mid-century pieces have art deco ribs and chrome stripes, then moulded plastic in the 1970s and 1980s. A bold yellow, red and black colour scheme with big sans-serif lettering and a red stylised K mark. Bright yellow surfaces with red panels.
In the style of GoPro, rugged, active and outdoorsy. Small matte black or dark grey rubberised cubes with a large round glass feature on the front, a tiny screen and a few chunky buttons with white icons. Black plastic frames with chunky tabs, folding prongs, orange-yellow or blue rubber accents and thumb-turned fixings. Transparent protective surfaces. Typography is bold white rounded sans-serif.
