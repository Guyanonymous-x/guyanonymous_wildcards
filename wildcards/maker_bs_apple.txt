Styled in a design language of pared-back, precise and quietly luxurious. Single continuous forms with perfectly rounded corners, razor-thin edges, seamless joins and no visible fasteners. Materials are brushed aluminium, polished glass and soft-touch matte finishes in silver, space grey, white, black and a few muted pastel tints, with colour mainly coming from the screen or content. Lettering is clean, light to medium sans-serif with generous spacing. Everything is reduced to the essential, with ample negative space. Photography is bright, soft-shadowed and shot on seamless white or gradient backdrops, calm, friendly and futuristic.
