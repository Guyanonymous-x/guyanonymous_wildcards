Styled in a design language of voluminous draped black design cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette becomes a dark, shadowy cloud. Materials are matte wool gabardine, crinkled cotton, heavy linen, felted wool and fluid jersey, with raw edges, oversized folds, tilted asymmetric edges, trailing hems and long scarf-like panels. Tailoring is slouchy, androgynous, deliberately undone and poetic. Austere, moody, romantic and quietly rebellious.. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
