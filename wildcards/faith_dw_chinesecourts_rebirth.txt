Meng Po, an old woman in her seventies in a plain gray robe, stands at a stall by the bridge ladling the soup of forgetting into bowls for a line of souls
the Naihe bridge arches over a dark river of blood, souls in their twenties to eighties crossing it in white, the good on a gold bridge above and the wicked falling into the water
the great wheel of rebirth turns at the edge of the last court, a huge wheel of red wood, souls stepping into its spokes to be born again as people, animals and birds
