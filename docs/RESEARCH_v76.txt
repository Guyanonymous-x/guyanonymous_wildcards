RESEARCH NOTES FOR v76 (2026-11-04)
Standing rule: research thin topics before building; sources cited.
Sources fetched:
- Arm blog, "History of the mobile form factor" (developer.arm.com): order of phone form factors: 1980s brick, mid/late 1990s small GSM bricks, late 1990s sliders and flips, early 2000s candy bars and clamshells (full-colour displays, cameras, FM radio, video calling), late 2000s physical-keyboard business phones, 2007 onward touchscreen slab, 2017 to 2020 revived bricks, sliders, foldables and flip returns.
- SlashGear, "The Evolution of the Amazon Kindle" (slashgear.com): e-reader generations from the 2007 wedge-shaped model with a keyboard and four-shade grey screen, through slimmer 2009 models, 2010 keyboard model, 2011 touch and buttoned basics, 2012 front-lit models, 2014 flat-glass front, 2016 stepped back, 2017 waterproof, 2022 pen note-taking 10.2-inch, 2024 colour e-ink.
- Galaxy Press, "Visual and Literary Description of Sci-Fi Subgenres" (galaxypress.com): little visual detail; used for the subgenre list only (space opera, space western, military, cli-fi, dystopian, post-apocalyptic, science fantasy, cyberpunk, steampunk and others).
- New Space Economy, "Human factors in crewed spacecraft" (newspaceeconomy.ca): phone-booth-sized crew quarters with sleeping bag and small window, foot loops and handholds, nets/straps/Velcro for storage, LED day-night lighting, neutral soft blue and grey colours, suction toilets, food rehydration heaters, grip spoons.
- Yanko Design (2026-07): foldable e-ink book concepts taking shape (used for the 'foldable e-ink' entry).
Other knowledge used (standard, well documented, not fetched): ISS and shuttle interiors (cable bundles, handrails, labels, taped notes), real e-reader and phone model histories for the maker_ files, instrument and clock collecting categories.
