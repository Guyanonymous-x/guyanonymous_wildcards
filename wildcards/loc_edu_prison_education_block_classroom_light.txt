Sealed diffusers at an even and slightly high level with no local control, so the room cannot be dimmed for a screen and cannot be brightened for close work
One non-opening window of laminated glass giving a flat daylight with a faint green cast and a view of a wall
No shadow anywhere and no warm light at any hour, which is the quality most often remarked on
In the evening, with the window black, the room reads exactly as it does at eleven in the morning, and the absence of any change through the day is itself the lighting condition
