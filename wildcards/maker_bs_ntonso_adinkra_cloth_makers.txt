Styled in a design language of handwoven cotton stamped with carved stamps in a dark, glossy tree-bark dye. Rows of small, bold black symbols such as concentric circles, spirals, crosses, ladders and hearts repeat inside a grid divided by hand-drawn comb lines. Backgrounds are dyed deep red, brown, indigo or black, or left natural cream. Symbols are slightly irregular with inky edges, giving a handmade rhythm and strong graphic quality. Narrow woven strips are stitched together into a wide cloth. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
