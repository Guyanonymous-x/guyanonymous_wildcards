Shot through a full-spectrum camera with the filter removed and converted to black and white, the image shows a soft, low-contrast black-and-white image, the skin wrapped in a luminous haze. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
