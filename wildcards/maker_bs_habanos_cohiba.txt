In the style of the Cuban cigar house Cohiba, a severe, luxurious tobacco-product look. The cigar band is a narrow strip with a black background and a bold yellow-gold geometric lettering that spells the name in angular, jagged capitals inspired by an indigenous Taino face mask, with a stylised yellow-and-black chequered pattern inside the main letters. The band wraps around the cigar in a diamond or tapered shape beside a smaller white and gold secondary band. The box is flat black or dark brown with rich tobacco-leaf lacquer, clean edges, brass-like gold foil embossing, a matte black label and a hologram sticker. The tobacco itself is smooth, shiny, dark oily brown wrapper with fine veins. A secondary dark green and white warning strip crosses the lid. Interiors show rows of cedar-wood cigars wrapped in thin cedar sheets, a deep red and brown glow, and a quiet, exclusive, masculine mood with a restrained black, yellow and cedar palette.
