Styled in a design language of each page is framed by luxuriant woodcut borders of winding acanthus, vines, flowers, leaves and fruit, white on black, interlaced thickly. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
