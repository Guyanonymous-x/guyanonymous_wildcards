In the style of Pakistan International Airlines, a South Asian flag carrier identity. Aircraft have a white upper fuselage and a pale beige or off white lower belly divided by a dark green stripe edged in gold, with a large waving national flag in green and white on the tail fin. The airline initials appear in big billboard capitals in dark green on the forward body, with a flowing calligraphic script mark in gold behind the cockpit and on the engine cowls. Special tail liveries feature regional folk art such as blue and white tile floral patterns, fine mosque tile decoration, bright needlework and bold red geometric kilim motifs. Early jets had a thin red and green cheatline. Cabins use deep green seats with gold accents, patterned carpets and scarves worn by crew in green and white. Mood is dignified, national, warm and proud, with green, white and gold as the key colours against bright blue sky.
