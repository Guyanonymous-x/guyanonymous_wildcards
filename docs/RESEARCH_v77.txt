Research notes v77
==================
Scope: fantasy and horror genre vocabulary, adult pair and group interactions, toy and die-cast packaging forms and eras.
Method: written from general knowledge of toy, game and packaging history (blister cards from the 1950s onward, window boxes, tins, header bags, foil bags) and of genre conventions. No web sources were fetched this round, so era details are general and approximate.
Known limits: packaging era and contents are chosen independently (a 1930s style card may hold a modern item); horror content is atmosphere only, no gore; no real brands or people; adults only.
