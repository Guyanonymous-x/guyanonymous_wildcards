In the style of Herman Miller, American modernism with playful, optimistic warmth. Forms are sculptural and organic, with moulded curves, tapered wooden forms, slender black or chrome metal lines and clean, uncluttered silhouettes. Palette mixes walnut and ash wood tones with warm white, charcoal and saturated accents such as tangerine, mustard, teal, lime and fuchsia, often applied in bold flat blocks. Surfaces are matte fibreglass, bent plywood, leather, felt and woven wool in graphic checks and dots. A bright red mark of a stylised letter M with a distinctive notched shape serves as the emblem. Lettering is lowercase or spaced capitals in a clear modern sans-serif, with thoughtful, humane and witty detailing. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Herman Miller in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
