In the style of Winsor and Newton, classic scholarly artists materials in small metal forms and wooden cases. Surfaces are silver or coloured foil with black or white lettering, cream panels and a bold red or black band, with a colour name and swatch. Mahogany or black japanned finishes hold rows of brilliant pigment in neat arrangements with brass fittings. Tapered wood in red, black or natural meets a gleaming crimped metal collar. Dense serif type and ornate Victorian borders give a studious, traditional feeling.
