In the style of Volvo in the 1960s to 1990s, a boxy, upright and sturdy Swedish estate or saloon with a flat bonnet, a large diagonal strip across the grille bearing a circle with arrow badge, square headlamps, and a boxy glasshouse with upright pillars. Flat-sided body with a thin crease, big square tail lamps, black bumpers with rubber trim, and a long flat roofline on the estate. Wheels are plain steel with chrome centre caps or simple alloys with five or six holes. Paint in beige, forest green, mustard, silver, white, and dark blue. Interior has thick supportive seats in plaid cloth or leather, a flat dash with large clear dials, and a big wheel. Mood is safe, rational and sturdy.
