Styled in a design language of cheerful, social, straightforward and hot-evening, with strong green, gold and white colour blocking. Brown glass with a white and green paper surface bearing a small crest-like emblem. Premium versions switch to gold and white, a medal version uses green glass with white, red and green. Log-cabin style halls with wooden beams, long benches, round tables and neon green signs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
