Styled in a design language of handwoven wool with a deep brick red ground, charcoal black, ivory and soft grey, in strong contrast. A bold central diamond with stepped, serrated edges, or a cross, sits in the centre, with smaller diamonds or hooks echoing in the corners. A single frame with notches or crosses surrounds it with neat edges. The surface is tight and flat with a slight handspun irregularity. Geometry is crisp, rectilinear and strongly symmetrical, with a warm desert look. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
