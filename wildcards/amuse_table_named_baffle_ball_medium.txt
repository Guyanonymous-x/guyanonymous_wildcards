a 1931 pinball table by Gottlieb, an early bagatelle pin game, with "BAFFLE BALL" printed across the top of the playfield card. Its playfield shows a small slanted wooden board studded with rows of nails, a few scoring cups and a spring plunger, the ball rolling down through the pins, and above it is a plain wooden case with a printed card under glass showing the scoring cups as colored rings.
