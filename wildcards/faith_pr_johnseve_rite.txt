in the rite of today, the head washing, Lorna, a priestess in her sixties in white, pours water from a white enamel basin over the bowed head of Avril, a kneeling woman in her thirties, then wraps the woman's wet hair in a white cloth
drummers in their twenties to sixties sit on low stools with tall wooden drums between their knees, their hands flat on the drumheads, and a ring of dancers in white turns around them in the firelight
a woman in her forties falls into trance among the dancers, her eyes rolled back and her body swaying, and two older women in their fifties and sixties in white hold her up by the arms
