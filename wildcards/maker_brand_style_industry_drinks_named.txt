In the style of Taiwan Beer, cheerful, social, straightforward and hot-evening, with strong green, gold and white colour blocking. Brown glass with a white and green paper surface bearing a small crest-like emblem, a bold sans-serif wordmark and a golden ring of wheat. Premium versions switch to gold and white, a medal version uses green glass with white, red and green. Metal finishes carry white with blue stripes and green lettering, or glossy gold, blue and white. Log-cabin style halls with wooden beams, long benches, round tables and neon green signs.
In the style of Kweichow Moutai, formal, festive, prosperous and traditional ceremonial design. A squat cylindrical white porcelain form with a short neck, glossy milk-white glaze and a slight shoulder curve, topped with a red ribbon tied over a seal and often a tiny hanging tag. A bright vermilion band wraps the middle, edged in gold, with large black or gold brush-style characters and a flowing line of small text. Special editions use red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Lacquer red or wood with yellow silk lining, embossed in gold and closed with a white cord or golden bow. Auspicious red, gold and white against dark polished tables.
In the style of Rooh Afza, rich, nostalgic, floral and festive South Asian lithographic art. A vivid red field framed by a decorative border of small stylised flowers, with a central cartouche holding swashing Latin and Arabic script lettering in white and yellow. Illustrations of rose bunches, fruits, herbs and leaves are drawn in a fine old printing manner with delicate hatching. Forms are tall, square shouldered and slightly ribbed, filled with deep ruby colour and capped in red or gold. Cooling, sweet, perfumed, celebratory and heritage-rich.
In the style of Kingfisher Beer, social, festive, glamorous hot-afternoon design with a bird emblem. Cream or white ground with a bold red band and a gold rim, rounded tops and strong upright serif capitals with a small crown icon. A bird in profile with a bright blue back, orange belly and long dark beak perches or dives above the name, drawn sleek and glossy. Slender brown glass and glossy cream and red surfaces are accented with gold rings. Warm red, gold, cream and a flash of electric blue, poolside and lively.
In the style of Singha, sunny, tropical, festive heraldic design. A deep red and gold border frames a central mythical lion in a rearing pose with a curly mane, tufted tail, raised paw and fierce expression, drawn in old engraving style and flanked by ornamental curls, a crown emblem and a royal bird crest. The name sits below in bold upright capitals in gold or white. Surfaces are glossy deep red with gold and white detail and gold foil accents that add a sense of ceremony. Amber brown, brown and cream complete the palette. Chilled condensation, spicy grilled food and beach light. Lively and bold.
In the style of Tiger Beer, cool, bold, energetic, nocturnal and urban design in navy, gold and hot orange. A deep navy field with a bold arched gold border, a leaping big cat in orange gold stripes on black rendered in dynamic ink lines, set above the name in big upright yellow capitals. A small crown, malt ears and a ribbon give a heraldic effect. Glossy navy surfaces with gold highlights and metallic silver. Ice buckets and red plastic tables at a night food market.
In the style of Ararat Brandy, deep, aged, ceremonial and warm amber heritage design. Heavy, rounded-shouldered forms with a deep amber glow, dark cork or gilded accents. Cream or ivory paper tones with a gold embossed border, a tall mountain with a snowy twin peak, strong serif capitals, an age numeral in a gold seal and a small vignette of a stone building. A red phoenix emblem appears on newer versions. Rows of little gold stars signal quality. Rich brown, gold, burgundy and cream.
In the style of Castle Lager, sociable, relaxed, sunny, outdoorsy heraldic design. Glossy green or silver surfaces carry the castle device and a strip of gold. Bright yellow plastic with black and red marks. Amber, green, gold and cream.
In the style of Tusker Lager, communal, sunny, savannah, relaxed and proud design in red, gold, black and cream. A cream and red field with an upright elephant head in profile with long curved tusks, drawn in bold black ink, above the name in strong capitals. Gold foil accents repeat with red, gold and black and the same emblem. Walls are decorated with big hand painted logos and slogans in bold yellow and red sign-painter lettering. Friends around wooden tables beneath thatch with grilled meat, on dusty roadsides. Bold black emblems on bright red.
In the style of Sapporo Brewery, crisp, masculine, social, evening-lit and no-nonsense design with a star emblem. Tall slim forms in matte black with a bold gold star and name, or silver with a red star in a circle, or cream and gold with thin ruled borders, and deep green with gold edging for special editions. The star is drawn in a crisp geometric outline, centred, with a wordmark in strong serif or slab capitals. Silver foil highlights. Golden liquid with a thick white foam head. Black, gold, red and silver.
