in the large chamber a procession of more than forty male gods carved in a long band at chest height along the left rock wall, walking in profile one behind the other toward the back, each in a tall pointed horned hat, a short belted kilt and shoes with upturned toes
along the right rock wall a procession of some twenty goddesses carved in profile, each in a tall round crown, a long pleated skirt that sweeps the ground and a cloak, one hand held out before her
on the back wall the meeting of the two lines, the storm god Teshub with a club on his shoulder standing on the bowed necks of two mountain gods, facing the sun goddess Hebat who stands on the back of a striding panther, small picture marks carved between their raised hands
behind the great goddess her son Sharruma carved standing on a panther with an axe on his shoulder, two smaller goddesses beyond him standing above a double-headed eagle with spread wings
on the wall facing the back scene the largest carving of all, King Tudhaliya the Fourth, taller than a living person, in a long robe and a close round cap, holding a staff curled at its lower end, standing on two rounded mountain peaks under a winged sun disc
