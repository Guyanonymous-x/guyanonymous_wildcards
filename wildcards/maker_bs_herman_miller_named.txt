Styled in a design language of American modernism with playful, optimistic warmth. Forms are sculptural and organic, with moulded curves, tapered wooden forms, slender black or chrome metal lines and clean, uncluttered silhouettes. Palette mixes walnut and ash wood tones with warm white, charcoal and saturated accents such as tangerine, mustard, teal, lime and fuchsia, often applied in bold flat blocks. Surfaces are matte fibreglass, bent plywood, leather, felt and woven wool in graphic checks and dots. A bright red mark of a stylised letter M with a distinctive notched shape serves as the emblem. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
