Styled in a design language of German functional modernism built from primary geometry, circles, squares and triangles, using tubular steel, chromed frames, black leather straps, flat plywood, bent wood and white-painted surfaces. Palette of primary red, yellow and blue with black, white and grey. Slim cantilevered lines, nickel, opal glass spheres. Rational, austere and factory-ready elegance. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
