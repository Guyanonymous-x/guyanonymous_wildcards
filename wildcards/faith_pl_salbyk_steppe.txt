the open steppe of feather grass, long silky grass waving silver, a treeless plain rolling away to the horizon, the fine plumed seed heads bending all one way in the wind so that pale shining waves cross the land, under an enormous sky with towers of white cloud
other kurgans with standing stones scattered over the plain, kurgans being ancient burial mounds, dozens of low rounded grassy humps dotting the flat valley near and far, each with a few gray upright slabs at its corners or along its edge, some leaning, in short broken rows
a dirt road crossing the steppe, two pale rutted tracks of dry earth running straight through the grass toward the stones, a strip of green between the wheel ruts, a haze of dust hanging over it and a line of wooden power poles marching beside it into the distance
