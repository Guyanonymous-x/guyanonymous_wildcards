In the style of Elie Saab, red-carpet couture gowns that are romantic and feminine, long sheer columns and full-skirted dresses with fitted bodices, plunging necklines, illusion lace, delicate floral applique and beading in gold, silver and crystal. Fabrics are tulle, chiffon, Chantilly lace, silk crepe and satin, in soft powder pink, blush, ivory, champagne, lilac, sky blue, emerald and rich red. Details include embroidered lace sleeves, sweeping trains, small flower petals scattered on skirts, cinched waists and sparkling bodices that fade into transparent skirts. Dreamy, luxurious, fairytale mood, shown in soft glowing light on elegant staircases.
