Styled in a design language of palatial underground halls with vaulted ceilings, rows of marble arches and columns in white, rose, grey and red stone, and polished floors in checkered dark and light granite. Huge crystal and brass chandeliers hang in the centre. Mosaics and stained glass showing heroic workers, soldiers, farmers and aviators in bright colours, bronze statues in alcoves, bas-relief carvings and gilded stars. Socialist realism and monumental classicism, theatrical lighting, symmetrical long perspective, warm golden light on polished marble, ornate coffers and sculpted ornament, like an underground cathedral or palace. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
