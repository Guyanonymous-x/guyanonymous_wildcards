Styled in a design language of bright glossy white ground painted only in cobalt blue brushwork, in graduated tones from pale sky blue to deep navy. Decoration is floral sprigs, leaves, swirling petals, birds, scalloped edges and simple landscapes, painted freely with a wide brush in single-stroke style. Forms are rounded and plump, with soft outlines. The blue appears washed and flowing against the clean white. The mood is folk-like, cheerful, fresh and quintessentially Russian.
