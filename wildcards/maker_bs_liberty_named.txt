In the style of Liberty, English Aesthetic Movement and Arts and Crafts design, with fine cotton lawn and silk printed in small-scale floral patterns, paisley, peacock feathers, stylised tulips and Celtic knotwork in soft jewel colours of plum, teal, rose, olive, mustard and indigo. Oak with simple pewter and copper fittings with beaten decoration, and a Tudor-revival timber-framed mood. Delicate, artistic, bohemian and romantic. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
