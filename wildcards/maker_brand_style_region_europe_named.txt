Colours are cool and soft, pale smoky grey, aqua green, ice-blue, amber, deep cobalt and deep purple, with swirled layers and trapped air bubbles. Some pieces are cut with simple vertical grooves or concentric rings, others engraved with fir branches or snowflakes. Mid-century forms have fluid free-form silhouettes, leaf-shaped and asymmetrical. Light through the glass casts soft pools of colour, evoking frozen lakes and fjords.
In the style of 66 North, austere, volcanic, windy and functional Icelandic weatherproof design. Bold, practical, enveloping volumes with overlapping layers, visible seams, crisp adjustable edges and generous scale, in matte waterproof surfaces in strong colours such as bright red, orange, deep blue, black, moss green and ochre. Surfaces use long parallel channels and stranded patterns in grey, cream and charcoal, and the mark is tiny and in plain capitals. Proportions are long, broad and roomy, like arctic working gear refined into minimalism. Subtle sheen under rain and chunky, oversized detailing.
In the style of Fiskars, functional, friendly, clean and instantly recognisable, with a single hot orange accent. Bright orange moulded plastic in smooth, open, rounded loops, paired with silver stainless steel with a satin finish, joined by flat black or orange pivots. Grips are ergonomic, with soft thumb curves and a slightly textured inner surface, black contrast parts, clean tapers and a sleek shape ending in razor-bright metal. Fine laser-etched lettering in a clean sans-serif. Orange and black used together.
In the style of Vista Alegre, elegant, formal, light and slightly romantic Portuguese porcelain. Forms have scrolling handles, high feet and flower-shaped finials. Modern versions turn to bold graphic prints, hand-painted strokes in cobalt, black or coral and sculptural art editions. Traditional palette is white, gold, navy, rose pink and sage green. Tiny gilt crowns and symmetrical arrangements.
In the style of Bordallo Pinheiro, naturalistic majolica, whimsical, sculptural, rustic and slightly surreal with cheeky humour. Forms are moulded as oversized cabbage leaves with veined, ruffled edges and vivid green glaze with a pale ribbed back. Realistic creatures such as lobsters, snakes, frogs, lizards, snails, beetles and flies crawl across surfaces in lifelike detail, along with sunflowers. Glazes are glossy, juicy and saturated, leaf green, mustard yellow, turquoise, coral, tomato red, aubergine purple and cream. Colours are exaggerated, textures lumpy and tactile, glistening as if freshly wet, with an almost trompe loeil effect.
In the style of Viuva Lamego Azulejos, cool, bright, narrative and decorative with a serene seaside light. Other patterns show repeating geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau versions have curving plants, lilies, irises and stylised maidens with delicate gold-green outlines.
In the style of Claus Porto, nostalgic, glamorous, carefully crafted Art Deco and Belle Epoque decoration. Lavishly patterned lithographic prints of peacock feathers, ferns, roses, swallows, geometric fans, arcs and rays. Colours are rich and layered, teal and gold, burgundy and cream, coral and navy, emerald and gold, with metallic foil stamping, fine coloured borders and a central oval or square frame with ornate lettering in elegant serif and decorative capitals, and a tiny crest or ribbon. Heavy, smooth, pale creamy forms embossed with a crest. Rounded vintage glass. Colourful rows against warm wood.
In the style of Lladro, sentimental, romantic, delicate and fragile porcelain. Tall, graceful, elongated bodies with slim limbs, small heads and flowing, swirling drapery whose folds ripple like wavy ribbons. Faces are serene, with softly closed eyes, a pale rosy complexion and tiny delicate hands. The surface is mostly matte, chalky and soft in pastel pale blue, grey-green, lilac, rose, cream, white and muted gold, with occasional glossy accents and fine hand-applied porcelain flowers. Bases are small and rounded with tiny flowers and ribbons, poses are quiet and tender, and light is soft. Deeper earthy tones appear in a rougher line.
In the style of Camper, quirky, comfortable, rounded and chunky, playful, down-to-earth, relaxed and a bit nerdy. Soft leather or canvas in muted brown, beige, white, grey, navy and olive, with a splash of red, yellow or green, visible contrast stitching, rounded ends, thick bases with vulcanised edges and graphic bumps, circles or wavy texture, sometimes tiny nubbles like a tennis ball. Mismatched pairs where each side differs in colour or detail. Simple small name tags, white accents and rounded padding. Plain cardboard in white and brown.
In the style of Desigual, an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas, with a big stylised swirling logo. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast.
In the style of Chupa Chups, playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop. Simple round shapes and clean sticks of form, with a glossy, candy-like shine and thin crinkled shimmer. The mark is a bright daisy-like flower with a yellow circle in the middle and red petals, with the name in friendly rounded white and yellow letters, on bright red, yellow or orange. Flavours are signalled by colour, pink, orange, green, cola brown and lemon yellow, some swirled with two colours. Hot-pink or red fields radiate outward in a sunburst. Cartoon children and sugary cheerfulness.
