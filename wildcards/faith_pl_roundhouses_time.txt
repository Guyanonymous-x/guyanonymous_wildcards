on a spring day, the hills green, fresh bright grass covering every slope, orange poppies and blue lupine blooming in drifts under the oaks, new pale leaves on the branches and soft white clouds in a clear blue sky
in summer, the hills gold, the grass dried to pale straw under a hard cloudless sky, the oaks dark green against it, heat wavering over the clearing and the earth roof of the roundhouse baked tan and cracked
at dusk in the fall, the last gold light on the oak crowns, the clearing in blue shade, firelight glowing at the low doorway and smoke rising from the roof
