Styled in a design language of joyful, colourful, folkloric design that mixes wide kimono-like volumes, loose smock shapes, patchwork, quilting and layered global prints within a single look. Large flowers, poppies, stripes, ginghams, checks and paisley are combined at clashing scale in saturated tomato red, yellow, turquoise, emerald, pink, purple and soft peach. Fabrics are cotton, soft jersey, knits and light gauzy weaves, cut in a relaxed, unstructured, unisex way. Cheerful, exotic, bold, playful and vividly graphic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
