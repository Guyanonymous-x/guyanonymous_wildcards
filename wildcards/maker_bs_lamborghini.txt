In the style of Lamborghini in the 1970s and 1980s, an extreme low flat wedge supercar with a pointed nose, razor sharp creases, pop-up headlamps, and a body that is little taller than a person at the waist. Hexagonal air intakes and louvres along the engine deck, scissor doors rising up, flat black rear louvred panels, and wide rear wheels with huge tyres. A golden bull badge on a black shield sits on the nose. Wheels are cast alloy with a star or telephone dial pattern. Paint in vivid lime green, orange, yellow, white, red and purple. Interior has angular black leather seats, a wedge dash with small switches and dials in pods, and a small steering wheel. Mood is brash, theatrical and futuristic.
