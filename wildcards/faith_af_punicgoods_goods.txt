around the body lie the grave goods, two red clay oil lamps with pinched spouts, a jug and a cup of red-glazed pottery, a painted ostrich eggshell bowl and a small bronze razor engraved with a figure of a god
at the throat of the dead woman hangs a gold amulet case as long as her finger, topped with a falcon's head, and a string of glass-paste beads with tiny bearded faces of blue, yellow and white glass
beside the head stands a terracotta mask as big as a face, a grinning wrinkled face with deep-cut eyes and a wide mouth, painted red and black, set to guard the dead against evil
a small carved stone stele stands by the tomb door, the sign of Tanit cut in it, a triangle with a bar across its top and a small round disk above, a row of small Punic marks cut along its base
