Shot through a super color 590nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a clear blue sky over golden-yellow and mustard foliage, with olive-gold trees, vivid and surreal. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
