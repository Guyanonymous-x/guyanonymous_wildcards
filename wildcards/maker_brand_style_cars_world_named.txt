In the style of Ford, honest, approachable, everyday American mass-market design. A blue oval badge with flowing white script lettering with a long looping tail serves as the signature mark, found as enamel signs, porcelain plates and chrome-edged emblems. Palette of deep royal blue and white with poppy red, turquoise, pale yellow, cream and dark green. Later years bring clean utilitarian graphics, plain horizontal bands, and simple blue and white signage. Materials include painted steel, enamel, chrome strip, vinyl, woodgrain and canvas.
In the style of Chevrolet, cheerful, optimistic diner-era American design. Bright chrome with bullet shapes, sharply pointed tapering forms, sweeping side flashes and two-tone lacquer. Palette is turquoise, coral, cream, sky blue, cherry red and ivory, with glossy metallic paint and polished steel highlights. Surfaces are smooth, curvy and streamlined, with jet-inspired forms and wide crosshatch mesh patterns. The emblem is a horizontal, slanted bowtie in gold, blue, red or chrome, often with a thin outline. Lettering is chrome script in flowing, looping letters, joined with bold capitals. Playful, confident, nostalgic and aspirational.

Materials include tufted leather, glossy lacquer, cut glass and stitched suede.
