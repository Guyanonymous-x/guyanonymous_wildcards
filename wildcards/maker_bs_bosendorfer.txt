Styled in a design language of viennese grandeur in glossy black or rich warm wood such as walnut, cherry or maple. Proportions are extended and generous, with a slender curved edge and a refined, quiet profile. Supports are carved and tapering, and openwork details are lace-like with shallow ornate shapes. Edging is thin, and trim in gold or brass is tasteful. Some versions have elaborate inlay, gilt carving and painted panels in the Baroque and Secession manner. The mood is aristocratic, warm, ornate and old-world. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
