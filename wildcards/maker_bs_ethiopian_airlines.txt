In the style of Ethiopian Airlines, an African flag carrier identity. Aircraft have a pale grey and white fuselage, with a lower belly painted pale grey and a flowing band of green, yellow and red lines sweeping up toward the tail. The tail fin is deep green with a yellow and red stylised bird or wing emblem. The airline name is shown in Amharic script and Latin capitals in dark green on the forward body. Cabins feature green seats with woven patterned cloth in red and gold, white head covers and wooden panels. Crew uniforms are green, yellow and red with traditional white woven cotton shawls trimmed with coloured borders. Mood is highland-bright, welcoming, proud and colourful, with green, gold and red against clear blue sky. Boarding gates and posters repeat the three colour bands as flowing ribbons, and cargo aircraft carry the same bands on a simpler white body.
