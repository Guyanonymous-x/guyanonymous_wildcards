In the style of Viuva Lamego Azulejos, cool, bright, narrative and decorative with a serene seaside light. Other patterns show repeating geometric lozenges, checkers, flowers and diamonds in blue, yellow, green and manganese purple. Art Nouveau versions have curving plants, lilies, irises and stylised maidens with delicate gold-green outlines. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
