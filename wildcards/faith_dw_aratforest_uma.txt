in a clearing stands the uma, the clan longhouse, raised on posts above the ground, its long roof of sago-palm thatch reaching low, a broad front porch of split wood hung with the skulls of pigs, monkeys and deer from past hunts and feasts
inside the uma along the rear wall hangs a wooden rack of offerings, bundles of leaves and flowers and small carved wooden birds, beside long rows of monkey and pig skulls above the smooth plank floor
beside the river stand two small houses of the clan on stilts, a dugout canoe drawn up on the bank below them and a garden of taro and bananas behind them at the edge of the forest
