in the old way the graveyard on the valley side is full of open wooden coffins with loose lids set on the ground, the bones and skulls of the dead in view among them
a gandau, a carved wooden figure of the dead man on a horse, as tall as a man, stands over his grave, his face cut stern, a turban on his head
in the present-day way the dead are buried in the earth of the graveyard under walnut trees, their graves marked with stones and carved wooden boards
