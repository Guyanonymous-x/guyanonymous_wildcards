the English smoking room as the type, with the Moorish or Indian costume at its most pronounced
the Scottish version, plainer, tartan-upholstered and placed off the billiard room
the Anglo-Indian version, which is where the decorative vocabulary is borrowed from and where the room is a verandah or a side room with real punkahs
the German Herrenzimmer, which is the same room with a different costume and survives longer as a type
the Ottoman selamlık, which is the actual institution the English room is dressed up as and which is a reception room for men with a wholly different social function
the American den, which merges the smoking room, the study and the gun room into one and is the version that survives into the twentieth century; and the gentlemen's club smoking room, which is where most of these objects and habits were standardized
