Styled in a design language of warm, humane, playful Scandinavian modern studio ceramics. Soft, curved, slightly lopsided organic shapes such as pebble forms, leaf dishes, squashed spheres and wide-hipped jugs, with soft shoulders and visible finger marks. A signature dark green-brown stoneware is inlaid with delicate silver leaf patterns. Plain white with coloured berries and flowers. Intimate handmade surfaces and a calm earthy palette that suits teak and linen. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
