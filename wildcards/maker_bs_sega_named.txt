In the style of Sega, edgy, fast, arcade-bright energy. Glossy black plastic with sharp angular wedge shapes, circular recessed details, moulded ridges and vents, and curved ergonomic forms with oversized round elements. Cobalt blue swirl marks with white and orange accents, loud saturated artwork with bold outlines and hard-edged speed lines, and glowing red or green points of light. A later softer variant is pale off-white and square with a gentle spiral motif. Lettering is slanted bold italic capitals that feel futuristic and quick. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
