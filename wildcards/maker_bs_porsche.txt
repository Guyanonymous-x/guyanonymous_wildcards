Styled in a design language of compact, balanced, purposeful and athletic. A smooth teardrop profile with one sloping line falling from front to back, round forms rising from flared surfaces, wide muscular curves and short overhangs. Thin full-width bands of light in modern work. Guards red, silver grey, signal orange, racing yellow, pale blue and black. A tiny crest used as a mark. Black leather, houndstooth cloth and aluminium trim.
