Styled in a design language of clean, modern, eco-minded, fresh and friendly home design for any product. Slim upright forms in black, white or brushed steel with plain rounded tops, a single clear push control and a flat base. Clear or tinted tall shapes with slender tapering tops carry a fine level line and a single colour accent such as red, blue, green or bright orange. Slender gunmetal grey or lime green steel adds a small collar. Newer pared-back forms in matte steel have a single dial. Flat labels in juicy colours, with rising bubbles, fruit slices, ice and a bright white counter in the imagery. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
