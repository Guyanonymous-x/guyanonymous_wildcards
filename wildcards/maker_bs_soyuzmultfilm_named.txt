Styled in a design language of hand-drawn and cut-out animation feel with soft gouache backgrounds in muted greens, earthy browns, dusty blue and warm ochre. Folk-tale forests, wooden huts and snowy villages, characters with round gentle faces, big noses and woolly fur textures, quirky puppet-like proportions, painterly grainy textures. Warm, melancholic, slightly surreal fairy-tale feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
