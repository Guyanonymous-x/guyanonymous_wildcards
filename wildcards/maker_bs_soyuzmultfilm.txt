In the style of Soyuzmultfilm, Soviet-era hand-drawn and cut-out animation with soft gouache backgrounds in muted greens, earthy browns, dusty blue and warm ochre, folk-tale forests, wooden huts and snowy villages, characters with round gentle faces, big noses and woolly fur textures, quirky puppet-like proportions, painterly grainy textures, simple slightly naive shapes, hand-lettered Cyrillic titles with a red star, and a warm, melancholic, slightly surreal fairy-tale feel.
