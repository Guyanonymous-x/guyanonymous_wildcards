Styled in a design language of orderly German technical and classroom precision in strong blue. Deep cobalt and navy blue paired with black, white and a few sharp accents of red, yellow and green, used in crisp flat bands and stripes on smooth lacquered surfaces. Packaging is clean, boxy and grid-based, thin ruled lines, small flags and tidy coloured tabs. Natural kraft and pale wood tones appear in limited editions. Neat, reliable, practical, methodical and cheerfully educational. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
