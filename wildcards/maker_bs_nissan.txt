Styled in a design language of sleek, drawn as a thin line outline and often shown in polished chrome, brushed silver or a flat two-dimensional black and white version. Forms are sharp yet flowing, with boomerang and V-shaped motifs, crisp folded creases, muscular swelling surfaces and floating, blacked-out contrasts. Palette is pearl white, deep graphite, silver, gloss black and a confident signal red, sometimes with a single copper or orange accent. Finishes mix mirror chrome, satin metal and glossy piano black. Light-edged glowing outlines and minimal digital graphics give a calm, high-tech feel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
