Styled in a design language of minimalist Scandinavian-geek graphic design. The emblem is a chunky black hexagonal nut-like shape fused with a bold round form, set above a tiny. Everything is black on white or white on black with exact grids, thin technical outlines. Materials are anodised aluminium, matte white plastic and soft silicone in signal orange, light grey, cream and occasional pastel or neon pops. Forms are thin, flat, rectangular and precisely rounded, like clean engineering drawings made physical, playful and deadpan. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
