In the style of Lomonosov Imperial Porcelain Factory, bright white porcelain in two manners. The imperial manner shows gilded rims, cobalt blue nets, a mesh pattern of dark blue lattice with gold dots and rich floral reserves in naturalistic colours. The revolutionary manner of the 1920s shows suprematist and propaganda designs with sharp red, black and white geometry, hammers, stars, workers and slogans in stencilled Cyrillic lettering. Figurines are lively and glossy. Forms are fine, thin and delicate in the imperial style, and flat plates in the later style are filled by boldly angular designs.
