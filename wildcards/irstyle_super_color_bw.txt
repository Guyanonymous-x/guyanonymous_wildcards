Shot through a super color 590nm infrared filter and converted to black and white, the image shows a black-and-white image with bright gray foliage and natural-looking contrast. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
