In the style of Sheaffer Pens, bulbous, torpedo-shaped forms in glossy striped celluloid colours, pearl and jade green, marine black, grey, burgundy and blue. Thin chrome or gold trim rings circle the body, and a distinctive inlaid white dot punctuates the top. Gold accents are long and triangular or hooded. Lines are straight, flat and sleek, with a spear-like taper. Surfaces are smooth, rounded, streamlined and glossy, in an Art Deco and early jet-age manner. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
