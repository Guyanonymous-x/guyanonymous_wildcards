Styled in a design language of vibrant, witty, politically charged and strongly handmade silkscreen design. A few flat, saturated colours in layers with no gradients, bright red, yellow, cobalt blue, orange, green, pink and black, laid down one screen at a time on cheap rough paper with slightly uneven edges and visible misregistration. Pop art, psychedelia and constructivism blended with satire. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
