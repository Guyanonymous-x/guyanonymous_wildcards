Styled in a design language of honest, tough, practical, unsinkable simplicity. Wide, stable, sturdy forms in white gelcoat with a thin navy or blue stripe low on the sides and a high flared front. Simple central arrangements with polished stainless details, grey non-slip surfaces and neat bolted-on fittings. No fuss, no ornament, durable, utilitarian, ready for work or play, with a straightforward coastal feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
