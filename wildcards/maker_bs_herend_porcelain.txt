In the style of Herend porcelain from Hungary. The body is fine hard-paste porcelain, bright white and translucent, with a high gloss. Tableware, vases, tureens and animal figures are hand-painted and gilded in very fine detail. The best-known decoration is a Chinese-inspired bouquet of flowering branches with fluttering butterflies in soft pastel tints, with a lattice-work border, a pierced openwork basket-weave on edges, and a thin gold rim. Other patterns include a green-and-gold scale, a pink-and-red bamboo, a rose-and-forget-me-not garland, and deep cobalt blue panels. Animal figures such as foxes, rabbits, cats, birds and fish are covered in a dense net of tiny crackle-like fishscale or fish-net pattern in rust red, turquoise, lilac, green, or gold. Handles are twisted into branches and knobs are modelled like fruits or flowers. The look is refined, elaborate, delicate and old-world, like a rich aristocratic dinner service.
