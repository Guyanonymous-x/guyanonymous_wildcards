In the style of Louis Poulsen, Danish pendant lamps with layered concentric shades that conceal the bulb, curved overlapping discs in white, copper, brass and chrome, forming a glare-free soft glow, and artichoke-shaped lamps made of dozens of overlapping leaves in polished steel or white. Smooth, sculptural, symmetrical, with warm reflections on a dining table. Elegant, calm, restrained Scandinavian hanging light.
