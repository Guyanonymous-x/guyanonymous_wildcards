In the style of Pan Am, mid-century jet-age optimism and worldly glamour. A deep blue circle encloses a pale globe drawn with fine latitude and longitude lines in clean white linework. Palette is cerulean and navy blue, crisp white, sandy beige, with a thin contrasting stripe in red or sky blue running along edges. Materials are glossy vinyl with white piping, canvas, polished aluminium and bright enamel. Shapes are boxy, rounded at the corners and clean, with tidy trim and flat graphic composition. Cheerful, confident, optimistic and travel-minded, with timeless retro polish.
In the style of British Airways, formal, traditional pride in navy, red, white and silver. A bold swooping ribbon shape in red and blue, tapering to a pointed tip, billows like a flag in motion and works as a swift, speed-implying stripe. White fields meet deep navy and rich red in strong, balanced colour blocks, with thin silver or pale grey borders. Surfaces are clean gloss and fine woven cloth with tonal sheen.
In the style of Air India, warm, ornate, richly coloured design with a regal Indian character. Strong red and white colour blocking is paired with deep maroon, saffron, aubergine and burnished gold. A small arched motif drawn from ornamental scalloped arches and a gold circular emblem repeats as a graphic mark, often with fine curved lines that suggest a sun or a gateway frame. Surfaces are glossy lacquer and gold foil, with jewel-like borders, paisley, jali lattice and floral geometry as pattern.
In the style of JAL, minimal, refined, precise Japanese design in one vivid vermilion red with white and charcoal. A solid red circle, like the rising sun, holds a white stylised crane drawn with a single sweeping curved neck and few feather strokes, a flat emblem of confident simplicity. Colour blocking is a clean white ground with one strong red shape, sometimes a dark grey band beneath it. Finishes are glossy lacquer and flat matte print with very crisp edges.
In the style of Swissair, rigorous Swiss International Style design built on pure red and white. A bold red field carries a white square-armed cross, and a slanted parallelogram of red cuts diagonally across white surfaces as a crisp shape. Finishes are glossy enamel and flat printed colour with sharp edges, set against bare white with generous empty space. A secondary palette of dark grey.
In the style of Lufthansa, orderly, efficient, geometric modernism and strictly typographic. A deep, rich navy and a bright canary yellow form the core palette, joined by clean white and soft grey. A circular emblem encloses a bird in flight drawn in just a few simple strokes, like a seal, in blue on white or yellow on blue. Compositions use flat, wide colour fields, a strict grid, thin rules and generous empty margins. Finishes are matte and calm with crisp edges and precise alignment. Everything feels trustworthy, disciplined, cool-headed and purposeful, with understated German clarity and no ornament.
In the style of SAS, crisp, Nordic, functional design in deep ultramarine blue with clean white. Colour blocking is broad and quiet, with a dark blue field, pale grey or white areas and one thin stripe set. A bold stripe motif in red, yellow and blue, each band equal in width, runs diagonally or horizontally as a lively accent against the reserved base. Finishes are satin paint, brushed light metal and smooth laminate, with a restrained, democratic, design-led Scandinavian rigour and no ornament.
In the style of KLM, cheerful, friendly, confident Dutch design in a single bright sky blue against clean white. The signature is flat colour blocking with a large sweep of cornflower or azure, sometimes broken by thin horizontal pinstripes in two blues. Finishes are glossy enamel and clean matte paint with crisp edges. Palette stays disciplined with sky blue, white, navy and a rare touch of orange.
In the style of Emirates, lavish, polished desert-luxury. Gleaming white grounds meet vivid ruby red in confident fields. Accents use rich gold, champagne, deep burgundy and warm sand beige, with soft gradients and satin sheen. Surfaces feel premium, with polished lacquer, burnished metal, supple leather and fine woven fabric.
In the style of Singapore Airlines, serene, polished, premium design in deep midnight navy and warm saffron gold. A stylised golden bird emblem of layered, upswept feather strokes sits on solid navy panels or crisp white fields. Surfaces are glossy lacquer, pearly white and soft metallic gold foil, with fine batik-like flowing pattern in muted gold on navy as a quiet secondary motif. Colour blocking is calm and symmetrical, with a broad navy band against white and a thin gold accent line.
In the style of Qantas, sunny, friendly, sporty and bold. A sweeping vivid red triangular wedge with a curved edge carries a white stylised leaping animal silhouette with a streamlined, stretched body. Colour blocking uses bright white, strong poppy red, soft grey and a thin dark charcoal accent, with a smooth high-gloss painted finish and crisp edges. Warm outback tones such as terracotta, sand and ochre appear as a secondary accent, plus a pale sky blue. Simple, geometric and graphic, with confident curves. Optimistic, athletic, proud and clean.
In the style of Aeroflot, Soviet-era graphic confidence in deep cobalt navy, pale sky blue, white and a punchy red. A circular emblem with outspread, feathered bars on both sides sits centred over a small disc, drawn flat in navy or silver-grey. Colour blocking is bold and optimistic, with sweeping diagonals, a broad pale blue band across bare metallic silver or white, and red used as a decisive accent. Surfaces are polished aluminium, flat gouache-like colour and slightly worn enamel. Flat graphic geometry, rounded rectangles and radiating lines give an idealistic, mid-century modern, utilitarian feel.
