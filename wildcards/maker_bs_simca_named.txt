Styled in a design language of jaunty, post-war and optimistic French pontoon design. A rounded body, a wide-mouth chrome opening, round forms, wraparound glazing, chrome trim strips along the flanks, a rounded end with chrome edges, a swallow mark and pastel two-tone paint. Cream over sea green, pink over grey, turquoise and sand. Bench forms in vinyl and cloth and chrome-ringed dials keep the mood sunny. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
