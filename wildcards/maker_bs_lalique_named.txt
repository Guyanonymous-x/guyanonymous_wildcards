In the style of Lalique, French Art Deco moulded glass that is frosted, satin-matte and crystal clear in the same piece. Deeply relief-moulded motifs of birds, fish, dragonflies, thistles, grapes and stylised female figures repeat in rhythmic bands with sharp, stepped, geometric edges. Milky opalescent glass glows pale blue against warm amber, and tints of sapphire, emerald, black and smoky grey add depth. Polished highlights contrast with velvety acid-etched textures. Lettering is slim, elegant, upright capitals, often etched small at the base as a signature. Luxurious, sculptural, light-catching and refined. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
