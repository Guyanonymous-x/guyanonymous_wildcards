In the style of Studio 189, Accra-made ethical fashion centred on hand-dyed indigo and hand-batiked cloth, adire-style resist patterns, hand-woven fabrics and block-printed African wax prints. Clothing is relaxed and modern, with wide-leg trousers, boxy tops, flowing wrap dresses, jumpsuits, kimono-style jackets and long skirts in cotton and linen. Palette includes deep indigo blue, white, earthy ochre, rust, black and bright accent colours such as orange and green, in tie-dye swirls, hand-drawn stripes, dots, geometric grids and flowing batik lines. Visible hand-dyed unevenness and crisp artisanal texture. Breezy, warm, socially conscious, sunlit West African mood.
