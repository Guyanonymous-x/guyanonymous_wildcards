the Hall of Mirrors, a tall round hall walled with tall panels of mirror between columns, so the room seems to go on forever, a great glass dome above it, a floor of polished stone
the Hall of Water, a round hall painted in deep blues, its walls covered with flowing painted waves and spirals in silver and white, a long pool-blue mosaic floor and a dome of glass above
the Hall of Earth, a round hall ringed by eight heavy columns, each carved and painted with figures of men and women reaching upward, the walls glowing in deep reds and golds, a domed ceiling over the center
the Labyrinth, a long winding passage lined with tall windows and doors of colored glass, each showing a god of an ancient faith, its walls painted with scenes from the history of humankind
the Hall of Spheres, a tall narrow hall cut deep into the rock, round glass spheres filled with colored liquid set in its walls, bright murals rising to its high ceiling
