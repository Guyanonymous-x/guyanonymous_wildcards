In the style of Brooks Brothers, classic, understated American Ivy League polish. Fabrics are navy flannel, grey worsted, camel wool, oxford cloth, blue and white seersucker, madras plaid, tartan and cable-knit wool in muted tones of navy, burgundy, forest green, camel, cream and pale blue. Signature surface patterns are diagonal repp stripes in bold alternating colour bands, regimental stripes, fine pinstripes, windowpane checks and bold awning stripes, plus small repeating crests. The emblem is a golden fleece hanging within an oval, embroidered or printed in gold and navy. Lettering is traditional serif capitals, often woven into small cloth marks in a vintage cream and navy look. Details are mother-of-pearl, brass and tidy contrast piping. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Brooks Brothers in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
