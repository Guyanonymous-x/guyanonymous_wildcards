Styled in a design language of tiny, round, cheerful and playful post-war Italian design. Bulbous egg-like and bubble forms, softly curved tops, thin chrome edges and simple rounded rectangles. Palette is pastel yellow, powder blue, mint green, cream, cherry red, orange and white, often in flat, single-colour fields. Surfaces are painted body-colour metal, simple vinyl, canvas and lightly polished chrome. Flat vintage illustration blocks with simple shapes and bright optimistic colour make everything feel light and sunny. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
