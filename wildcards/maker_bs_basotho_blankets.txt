In the style of Basotho Blankets, thick woolen blankets worn as wraps in Lesotho. The cloth is a heavy, tightly woven wool or wool blend with a soft nap, rectangular, with fringed ends and a plain selvage. Patterns are bold and geometric, such as wide stripes, large diamonds, squares, stylised leaf shapes, flowers and crowned shields. Classic designs include a cream and mustard ground with dark stripes, burgundy and navy blocks, a bold gold and black chevron, and a Victorian crown pattern in blue, red and cream. Colours are strong and earthy, with ochre, maroon, navy blue, forest green, black and cream. The blanket is pinned at the shoulder with a large safety pin and draped over riders on horseback wearing rubber boots and a conical straw hat. Mood is proud, blanket-wrapped, mountain-chilled and heritage-rich, set against rocky highlands in cool light.
