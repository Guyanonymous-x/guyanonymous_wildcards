Styled in a design language of bright glossy white ground painted only in cobalt blue brushwork, in graduated tones from pale sky blue to deep navy. Forms are rounded and plump, with soft outlines. The blue appears washed and flowing against the clean white. The mood is folk-like, cheerful, fresh and quintessentially Russian. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
