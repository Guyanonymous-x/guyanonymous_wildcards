Styled in a design language of friendly, simple, practical design from the 1960s and 1970s. Boxy but approachable white forms with softened corners, generous wraparound windows and plain, honest shapes that are easy to read. Painted sweeping side stripes in red, blue, gold or green add a note of optimism. Sturdy, unfussy, utility-first and casual, with a weekend-adventure cheerfulness and clean enamel finish. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
