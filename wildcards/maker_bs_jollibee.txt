In the style of Jollibee, a Philippine fast food chain with a cartoon bee mascot. The mascot is a round-bodied cartoon bee with a large rounded head, big white eyes, a cheerful wide smile, small antennae, tiny wings and a red blazer with a white chef hat, its fat body black and yellow striped, drawn in smooth glossy outline. The logo is a red wordmark in chunky rounded italic letters with a white outline and thick drop shadow, set beside the mascot on a bright yellow field. Restaurants have a bright red and yellow exterior with big glass windows, a smiling mascot statue at the entrance, a cheerful interior with shiny tables, plastic chairs and a play area, and staff in red shirts and caps. Packaging includes red paper boxes of fried chicken with gravy tubs, spaghetti in white tubs topped with red sauce, and pies in yellow sleeves. Mood is joyful, family-friendly, loud, sweet and very warm.
