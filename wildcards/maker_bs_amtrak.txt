Styled in a design language of American long-distance passenger trains with fluted and smooth stainless-steel carriage bodies, tall two-level double-deck cars with rows of large windows on both decks, a silver body with a long red, white and blue stripe along the side, blunt diesel locomotives in silver and dark blue, roomy reclining coach seats in blue fabric, and a plain, practical, wide-open-country character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
