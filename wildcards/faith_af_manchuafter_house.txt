inside the clan house the ancestor shelf hangs high on the west wall above the heated brick sleeping platform, a long red-painted board holding the wooden box of the clan spirits, cups of wine and steamed millet cakes set before it
in the courtyard stands the somo, the spirit pole, a tall bare pine pole with a small wooden bowl at its top filled with meat and grain for the crows, the sacred birds of the clan, a few crows perched on it
the clan record cloth hangs unrolled on the west wall, a long sheet of white cloth painted with columns of vertical black Manchu marks for the names of the ancestors, each set in a small red-edged box, generation below generation
