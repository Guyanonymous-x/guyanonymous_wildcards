the homestead stands in a ring on a dry plain, round huts of poles plastered with mud and dung, a cattle kraal of tall thorny branches at the center, mopane trees and pale grass beyond
long-horned cattle of red, black and white crowd the kraal behind the fire, their wide curving horns catching the light, a calf pen of branches against the fence
dry red hills rise behind the homestead under a vast sky, scattered thorn trees and pale rocks across the land, a dry riverbed winding toward the Kunene river
