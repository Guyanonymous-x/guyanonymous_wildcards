In the style of Vakko, large square twill silk printed with detailed painterly scenes, tile patterns, tulips, carnations, flower patterns, arabesques, waterside views and maritime and horse motifs, finished with hand-rolled edges. Colours are saturated cobalt, turquoise, coral red, emerald, gold and ivory, in dense multi-colour compositions bordered by a decorative frame. Tailoring is polished, with furs, silk, boxy jackets and long coats. Elegant, cosmopolitan, orientalist-meets-modern mood, with rich satin sheen and gold chain edges. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
