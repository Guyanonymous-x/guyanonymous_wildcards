the commonest self-built dwelling in the Western world and a genuine mass movement, a standard commercial panel van bought at three to eight years old
stripped
insulated with closed-cell foam or wool
battened and lined, and fitted out over six months of weekends into a dwelling of about six square meters
a bed running across or along the van
electrical systems of one to four leisure batteries charged from the alternator
solar panels and a mains hook-up lead
water in tanks of forty to a hundred liters with a pump
heating by diesel or gas air heater since a woodburner in a van is possible but rare
cooking by gas with a mandatory fixed vent
sanitation by portable or composting toilet and very often by public facilities
insulation and ventilation that determine whether the van is habitable in winter or a condensation trap
a registration that in the UK can be reclassified to motor caravan and in many countries cannot
insurance that must be declared, and an occupancy that ranges from weekends to permanent
