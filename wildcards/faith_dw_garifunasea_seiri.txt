far across the sea on the horizon lies Seiri, an island of white sand and tall coconut palms bathed in soft light, where the ancestors live in thatched houses of palm leaf and walk on the shore in white clothes
beyond the sea rises Yurumein, the green mountain island of Saint Vincent, its volcano peak wrapped in cloud, the old homeland of the ancestors, shown as a shining green land where the dead live together
beyond the far edge of the water a bright land of cloud and light opens over the sea, where ancestors in their sixties to their nineties, in white dresses and white shirts, sit together at long tables under almond trees
