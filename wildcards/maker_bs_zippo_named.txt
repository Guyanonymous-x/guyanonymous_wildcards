Styled in a design language of compact, rugged American metalwork with a vintage, collectable charm. Sturdy rectangular forms with softly rounded corners, polished chrome, brushed steel and brass finishes with a worn, handled shine. Surfaces carry crisp engraved or enamel-printed graphics such as pinups, flames, eagles, tattoo-style icons and retro geometric patterns, in saturated red, black, cream, teal and orange. Mid-century Americana, tough, reliable, personal and witty. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
