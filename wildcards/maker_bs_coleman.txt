Styled in a design language of friendly vintage Americana outdoor utility. Rounded steel edges, sturdy rolled seams, brass and chrome details and khaki canvas in heavy weave. Secondary graphics are simple green and cream box colour-blocking with small red accents. Honest, dependable, nostalgic, family-friendly and built to be used hard. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
