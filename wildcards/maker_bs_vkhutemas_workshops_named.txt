Styled in a design language of constructivist stark geometry in red, black, white and cream, with occasional yellow and grey. Compositions place diagonals, circles, wedges and sharp bars at dynamic angles. Structures use thin plywood, tubing and simple planes with exposed joints, or stacked boxes, cantilevers, cylinders and glass walls. Prints are flat and high-contrast with rough printed textures. The mood is revolutionary, industrial, energetic and abstract. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
