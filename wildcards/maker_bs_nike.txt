Styled in a design language of athletic motion and defiant confidence. Forms are streamlined and aerodynamic, with sweeping check-like curves, sharp tapered cuts and layered panels that suggest speed. Palette is stark black, white and grey with a single electric accent such as volt yellow, hot orange or crimson, and bold colour-blocked contrasts. Technical mesh, foam, rubber and glossy synthetic skins show engineered detail. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
