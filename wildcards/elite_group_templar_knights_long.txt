Knights Templar of the twelfth century, the warrior monks of the Crusades. Armored knights ride in a column across dusty hills, each in a white mantle over chain mail with a red cross on the left breast, their horses draped in white cloth. At the head of the column a knight carries a tall war flag split straight across, black over white, and behind the knights ride sergeants in black or brown mantles with the same red cross. The knights wear flat-topped steel helmets with narrow eye slits, or chain mail hoods, and carry long lances, kite-shaped shields painted white with the red cross and long straight swords at their hips.
