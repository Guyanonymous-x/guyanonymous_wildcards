In the style of White Rabbit Creamy Candy, small milky chews in a double wrapper. The candy is a plump cream coloured rectangle wrapped first in a thin edible rice paper film, then in a waxed paper twist printed in white with red, blue and black hand lettering in both Chinese characters and Latin script. A naturalistic ink drawing of a white rabbit stands or jumps in front of an old fashioned artist palette, drawn with fine line shading in the manner of 1950s commercial art. The outer pack is a flat sealed pouch or a bright cardboard box in red and white with a large rabbit face with wide forward-looking eyes, a cartoonish cheerful animal on a pale blue sky background. Display tins show vintage Art Deco borders. Colours are limited to white, red, blue and black, printed with a slightly matte texture. Mood is sweet, nostalgic, modest, childlike and old Shanghai, evoking sweet shops, school bags and festive gift trays.
