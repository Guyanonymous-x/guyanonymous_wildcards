In the style of Swanndri, New Zealand heavy wool bush shirts, long-sleeved hooded pullover jackets made of thick, felted woollen cloth in bold large checks and plaids, such as red and black, blue and black, green and black, mustard and navy. Cut is boxy and long, with a pullover half-button front or a front zip, an attached hood, big patch pockets, drawstring waist and reinforced elbows. Surface is dense, slightly fuzzy and water-resistant, with sturdy stitching and metal eyelets. Rugged farm and hunting mood, shown in misty green hills, sheep country and muddy tracks.
