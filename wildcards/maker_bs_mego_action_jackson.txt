Styled in a design language of early 1970s American action figures with tanned soft plastic heads and sculpted short hair, rubbery fully jointed bodies, simple painted faces and snap-fastened cloth outfits, sold in white boxes with a large photograph of the figure in action and a bold red stripe. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
