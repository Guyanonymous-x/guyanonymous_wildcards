the everyday dress of the Lugbara people of the West Nile region of Uganda and northeast Congo, adult men in their twenties to seventies in shirts and trousers or a kanzu, a long white robe to the ankles, the women in bright printed cloths wrapped at the waist and over the shoulders with head wraps
the elders in long white kanzu robes with a jacket over them, the women in long print dresses and headscarves
present-day village clothes, T-shirts, shorts and plastic sandals for the men, printed wrap skirts and blouses for the women
