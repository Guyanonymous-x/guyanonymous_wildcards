In the style of Mini in the 1960s, a tiny boxy two-box hatchback saloon with a wheel pushed to each corner, short overhangs, upright flat nose and tail, and a roof like a small lid. Round chrome headlamps on the front panel, a thin oval or chrome-frame grille, external welded seams along the body edges, exposed door hinges, sliding windows, and slim chrome bumpers. Tiny ten-inch wheels with steel discs and bright chrome trim rings. Paint in tartan red, island blue, Old English white, British racing green, with a contrasting white roof and stripes over the bonnet. Interior is minimal, with a central round speedometer pod, rubber floor mats, thin-spoked wheel, ribbed vinyl seats and a toggle switch row. Mood is cheeky, compact and playful.
