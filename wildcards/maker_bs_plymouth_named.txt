In the style of Plymouth, bold and cheeky American design from the late 1950s through the 1960s. Wide, flat, low proportions with long sculpted sides, a sweeping two-tone contrast line and bright chrome bars running the full width. Sharp, tall, blade-like projections slanting backward give a jet-age thrust, while loud graphic decals and a cartoon character add cheek. Saturated purple, vivid orange, lemon yellow and bright green against shiny metal. Confident, playful, exaggerated and optimistic, with a love of speed and showmanship. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
