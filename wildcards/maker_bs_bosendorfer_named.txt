Styled in a design language of viennese grandeur in glossy black or rich warm wood such as walnut, cherry or maple. Proportions are extended and generous, with a slender curved edge and a refined, quiet profile. Edging is thin, and trim in gold or brass is tasteful. Some versions have elaborate inlay, gilt carving and painted panels in the Baroque and Secession manner. The mood is aristocratic, warm, ornate and old-world. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
