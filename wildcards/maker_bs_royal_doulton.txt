Styled in a design language of English ceramics ranging from stoneware with Art Nouveau tube-lined flowers in earthy brown, blue and green, to mottled red-and-black flambe glaze with running streaks. Glossy, richly coloured and finely detailed with pastel flesh tones, floral garlands, gilded edges and sculpted ruffles. Sentimental Victorian and Edwardian parlour feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
