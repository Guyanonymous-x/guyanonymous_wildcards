Styled in a design language of warm, rhythmic, nostalgic and tropical old-city street-art flavour. Tall squared-shoulder clear glass with a sturdy cork, filled with amber or golden liquid. Cream or ivory paper with an aged, slightly weathered look, a big ornate nineteenth-century cartouche with a gold and red border, a small oval vignette of a fortress and a colonial tower. Contemporary versions add colourful screen-printed illustration in saturated flat inks of red, blue, yellow and green, with musicians, dancers, palm trees, vintage cars and skyline. Metallic foil and ribbed stamping in silver and gold. Faded colonial palette of mint green, terracotta, sun yellow, turquoise and sepia. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
