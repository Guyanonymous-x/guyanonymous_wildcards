Camp gates along the road carry carved and painted names, "HILL BILLIES" with a carved owl on the post, "CAVE MAN" over a gate of rough logs, "OWLS NEST" in gilded letters and "STOWAWAY" on a board shaped as a ship's sternboard.
A wooden sign on a tree trunk reads "WEAVING SPIDERS COME NOT HERE" in old carved capital letters, the club's motto.
A camp's bar is a counter of split redwood logs, crowded with bottles of whiskey and gin, a silver ice bucket and a row of heavy cut-glass tumblers.
