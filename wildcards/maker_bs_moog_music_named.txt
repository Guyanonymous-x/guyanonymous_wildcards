Styled in a design language of warm, handcrafted, scientific vintage modernism. Accents of orange, red and cream appear sparingly. Forms are slanted, wedge-like and sturdy with softly eased corners, exposed joinery, and a laboratory-meets-furniture honesty. Monochrome stamped badges and tidy technical diagrams in black on white. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
