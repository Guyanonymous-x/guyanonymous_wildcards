Styled in a design language of Swiss and German design with a witty, collectible sensibility. Shapes are playful and sculptural, with rounded plastic, smooth lacquer, polished and powder coated metal, warm oak and walnut, and glossy ceramic. Palette combines soft pastels such as blush pink, pale blue and mint with strong primaries, deep forest green, black and bright white. Surfaces include bold geometric prints, checkerboards, dots and folk-inspired abstract motifs in flat colour. Objects feel like small sculptures, with miniature scale, cheerful faces and abstract figures. Lettering is a clean, wide-set grotesque sans-serif in black or white, simple and gallery-like, with an artful, curious, humorous and slightly irreverent character. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
