In the style of ZIL, Soviet state limousines of the 1950s to 70s, huge long and wide with a heavy chrome grille of bold vertical bars, a wide bumper with big bullet guards, small round headlamps, tall flat sides, thin chrome trim and a formal roofline. Stately slab body, black gloss paint, white sidewall tyres, tall chromed hubcaps, and a bright star or hammer emblem. Interior with wool broadcloth, lace curtains on the rear window, jump seats, and massive wooden panels. Colours of black, deep burgundy, dark grey and cream. Heavy, imposing, ceremonial and austere.
