Styled in a design language of American utilitarian craft in pine, maple and cherry with plain, tapered, turned legs, ladder-like rhythm with woven tape, delicate swallowtail joints and plain painted finishes in barn red, mustard yellow, blue-green and milk-paint grey. Clean lines, no ornament, honest dovetails, spare, serene and tidy, with sunlight and handmade perfection, slender, carefully balanced proportions and a quiet, humble dignity in every joint. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
