Styled in a design language of safe, calm, rational Scandinavian design. A circle with an arrow pointing up and to the right, a diagonal band across it, in plain silver or flat white, serves as the signature mark, with wide, evenly spaced. Forms are boxy, upright, sturdy and honest, later softened into clean, minimal, flowing shapes with a quiet surface. Palette of cool pale blue, Nordic grey, pure white, fog, forest green, mustard, beige and dark navy. Materials include light wood, soft wool and plaid cloth, pale leather, matte metal and frosted glass. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
