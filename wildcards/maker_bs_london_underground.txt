Styled in a design language of deep tube railway design with ox-blood red glazed terracotta station fronts, curved platform tunnels lined in cream and coloured glazed tiles, round-profile red-white-and-blue trains with curved roofs and sliding doors, seats covered in dense multicoloured geometric moquette, Art Deco stations in brick, concrete and glass with tall drum-shaped ticket halls, bronze escalators with uplighter lamps, and an orderly, civic, modernist character. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
