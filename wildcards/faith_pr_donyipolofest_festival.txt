at Solung, in an Adi village, men in their twenties to sixties in cane helmets topped with the curved yellow beak of a hornbill, sleeveless woven coats and swords in bamboo sheaths gather at the split bamboo altar, a black mithun, a great humped forest ox, tied to a post beside it, in hazy September afternoon light
