Styled in a design language of clean, confident, engineered corporate design in rich cobalt blue, deep navy and crisp white. Broad colour blocking uses a dark blue base rising into a pale blue field, divided by a smooth flowing curve with a soft gradient and a single sharp swoosh of light blue. Finishes are glossy enamel and satin metal with perfectly smooth, seamless surfaces, plus fine technical line graphics. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
