Styled in a design language of compact, precise, high-tech engineering with a confident premium feel. Clean rectangular and softly bevelled forms with tight panel gaps, miniaturised proportions and rows of neat small controls. Finishes are matte black, titanium silver and brushed metal, with occasional bold primary or translucent colour accents, and a deep gloss on screens and lacquer. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
