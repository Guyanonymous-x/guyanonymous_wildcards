In the style of Bentley, a muscular British grand tourer with a broad mesh grille in a chrome surround topped by a winged B badge, powerful haunches, long bonnet and a short rear deck. Round paired headlamps flank the grille, a deep sculpted shoulder line runs the full body, and the stance is low, wide and heavy on large alloy wheels. Early open tourers wear dark British racing green paint with leather bonnet straps, wire-spoke wheels, cutaway doors and a flat riveted scuttle. Modern coupes show hand-polished chrome, diamond-quilted leather seats in contrasting hide colours, knurled metal switchgear, organ-stop vent pulls, polished burr walnut or open-pore wood veneer, and a rotating dashboard panel. Colours are deep green, dark blue, silver, black and burgundy.
