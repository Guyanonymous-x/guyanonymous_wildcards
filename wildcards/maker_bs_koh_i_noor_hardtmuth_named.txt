Styled in a design language of orderly, precise, industrial-vintage and nicely engineered. Hexagonal wooden forms lacquered in a strong yellow, ends dipped in dark green or black. Slim flat cardboard or tin in yellow. Items are arranged in neat rows of crisp lacquered pieces, each in a matching hue with a gold ring. Rubbery cream and pink blocks. Quiet confidence of fine craft. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
