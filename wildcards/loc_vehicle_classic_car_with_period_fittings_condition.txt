a sympathetically used car with original patina
a barn-find with the seats mouse-eaten, the leather hard and split open along every stitched seam, a nest of chewed foam and horsehair hollowed out of one squab, the chrome pitted to a gray bloom and the glass fogged on the inside with a film that wipes off brown, the headlining fallen in one sheet onto the rear seat, the dashboard veneer lifted and delaminated into waves, bird mess down the offside wing from a rafter directly above, a tire flat and perished square where it has stood for nineteen years
the same model as a new car in 1958
a car at a show with the bonnet up and a cloth on the wing
