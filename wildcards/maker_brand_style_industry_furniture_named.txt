In the style of Shaker Mount Lebanon, plain and sturdy construction in pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Slender turned posts, arching slats, woven tape or rush texture and tapered legs give light, upright forms. Flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, small wooden knobs and a smooth surface define the details. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and natural honey wood. Thin bent wood bands with swallowtail joints and copper tacks hold shapes together. The mood is serene, orderly, utilitarian and pure.
In the style of Thonet, solid wood steam-bent into continuous flowing curves, with slender round-section members forming loops, circles and S-scrolls. Structures are light, airy and sculptural, with delicate splayed supports joined by a ring brace, and surfaces of woven cane or pressed perforated wood. Finish is dark mahogany brown, walnut or ebonised black in satin lacquer, with visible screw heads and small metal collars at joints. Later work uses bent chrome tube with cane panels and black leather. Mass-produced, elegant, coffeehouse mood.
In the style of Herman Miller, American modernism with playful, optimistic warmth. Forms are sculptural and organic, with moulded curves, tapered wooden forms, slender black or chrome metal lines and clean, uncluttered silhouettes. Palette mixes walnut and ash wood tones with warm white, charcoal and saturated accents such as tangerine, mustard, teal, lime and fuchsia, often applied in bold flat blocks. Surfaces are matte fibreglass, bent plywood, leather, felt and woven wool in graphic checks and dots. A bright red mark of a stylised letter M with a distinctive notched shape serves as the emblem. Lettering is lowercase or spaced capitals in a clear modern sans-serif, with thoughtful, humane and witty detailing.
In the style of Knoll, refined mid-century modernism with architectural calm. Forms are precise and minimal, using slim polished stainless steel, brushed chrome, glossy lacquer, travertine, marble and leather with crisp stitching and button tufting. Contours mix straight cantilevered lines with soft sculptural curves. Palette is restrained and sophisticated, with ivory, black, taupe, cognac, oatmeal and slate grey, lifted by rich textile colours in terracotta, tomato red, ochre, teal and cobalt. Woven fabrics show tactile geometric weaves, stripes, grids and abstract patterns with visible thread. Lettering is a small, tidy, lowercase or capital sans-serif wordmark with generous spacing, confident, understated and quietly luxurious.
In the style of Vitra, Swiss and German design with a witty, collectible sensibility. Shapes are playful and sculptural, with rounded plastic, smooth lacquer, polished and powder coated metal, warm oak and walnut, and glossy ceramic. Palette combines soft pastels such as blush pink, pale blue and mint with strong primaries, deep forest green, black and bright white. Surfaces include bold geometric prints, checkerboards, dots and folk-inspired abstract motifs in flat colour. Objects feel like small sculptures, with miniature scale, cheerful faces and abstract figures. Lettering is a clean, wide-set grotesque sans-serif in black or white, simple and gallery-like, with an artful, curious, humorous and slightly irreverent character.
In the style of Artek, Finnish modernist design in pale laminated birch, with a single bent L-shaped support that curls into a smooth scroll beneath the surface. Forms are simple, round and light, with lightly curved plywood, soft rounded edges and visible blond grain. Natural birch and white lacquer are lifted by colourful lacquered parts in red, yellow, blue and green, black linoleum and striped woven webbing or canvas. Warm, humane Scandinavian functionalism with a nordic apartment and school hall mood.
In the style of Fritz Hansen, Danish modern design with sculptural moulded laminated wood in curvy ant, butterfly or egg silhouettes, with a pinched waist and no visible join between parts. Slim chromed steel tube supports on three or four points or a spiderlike cast aluminium base, tight leather or tweed wool upholstery, and coloured lacquered veneer in black, white, lime green, deep red and warm walnut. Surfaces are glossy and smooth with generous softness. Elegant, airy, organic in flow and never fussy.
In the style of Carl Hansen, Danish cabinetmaker craft in solid oak, teak and walnut with soaked, oiled or soaped finishes that show fine grain. Shapes include curved wishbone-like members with steam-bent Y-shaped rails, hand-woven natural paper cord, tapered rounded legs, floating sculptural arms and round-edged surfaces. Pegged joints and visible joinery are celebrated. Palette of honey, pale blond, soft brown and cream leather. Calm, tactile, handcrafted, minimalist Scandinavian warmth.
In the style of IKEA, democratic Scandinavian practicality, bright and friendly. Simple, light, flat-planar shapes, modular rectangles with soft corners, visible joints and clever space-saving logic. Pale birch, pine and white laminate are paired with cheerful saturated blue and yellow plus red, green and soft pastels, in bold, playful patterns and plain colour blocks. Materials feel affordable but honest, such as light wood, painted board, woven textiles and moulded plastic. Lettering is bold, rounded, plain sans-serif in capitals.
In the style of Cassina, Italian luxury modernism with deep, plump, tufted leather in cognac, black and cream on thin chrome or lacquered steel frames. Sculptural wooden members with a ladder-like rhythm and pale rush, wide leather planes, tubular steel supports and low lacquered surfaces with reflective glass. Rich materials include walnut, rosewood, polished marble, burnished brass and saddle-stitched hide. Sharp proportions blend craftsmanship with industrial precision. Refined, architectural, masculine and expensive, with a penthouse mood.
In the style of Kartell, Italian plastic design that turns industrial material into luxury. Forms are sculptural and sinuous, with smooth continuous curves, baroque silhouettes reinterpreted in modern moulded polycarbonate, and crisp square geometries. Surfaces are glossy, crystalline, transparent and tinted, in clear, smoke grey, amber, ruby, blush pink, sky blue and opaque black or white. Light refracts through thick translucent walls with soft edges and subtle bubbles, and some finishes are metallic, mirror or matte soft touch. Pattern is minimal, with faceted ridges and embossed textures. Lettering is a bold, clean, lowercase sans-serif wordmark in red or black, with a sleek, playful and glamorous Milanese attitude.
In the style of Memphis Group, Italian postmodern design built from clashing geometric blocks, triangles, cylinders and zigzags. Cheeky laminate in bold squiggle, terrazzo and speckled confetti patterns in pink, teal, mustard yellow, sky blue, black and white. Asymmetrical, wobbly, totem-like stacking, tapered legs, dayglo plastic and glass with stripes and dots. Flat saturated colour planes with thick black outlines. Playful, irreverent, cartoonish, kitsch and joyful, with a loud 1980s mood.
In the style of Stickley, American Arts and Crafts Mission design in solid quarter-sawn oak with visible straight grain and ray flecks, rectilinear boxy forms, vertical slats, flat panels, exposed mortise-and-tenon joinery with pegs and keys, and wide plank members. Dark fumed brown finish, hammered copper or iron hardware, thick leather or canvas, leaded glass. Honest, sturdy, handmade and simple, with a lodge-like craftsman mood.
In the style of Gillows, English Georgian and Regency craft in rich figured mahogany, rosewood and satinwood with crisp neoclassical lines, reeded tapering supports, brass inlay, ebony stringing and ornate lion-paw feet. Leather-inlaid surfaces, sabre curves, brass fittings and deep polished red-brown glow. Formal symmetry, refined country house and gentlemen club interior, with restrained, confident, well-proportioned detail and a warm, deep gloss over every surface.
In the style of Bauhaus workshops, German functional modernism built from primary geometry, circles, squares and triangles, using tubular steel, chromed frames, black leather straps, flat plywood, bent wood and white-painted surfaces. Palette of primary red, yellow and blue with black, white and grey. Slim cantilevered lines, nickel, opal glass spheres, abstract woven grids and lowercase sans-serif lettering. Rational, austere and factory-ready elegance.
In the style of Shaker workshops, American utilitarian craft in pine, maple and cherry with plain, tapered, turned legs, ladder-like rhythm with woven tape, delicate swallowtail joints and plain painted finishes in barn red, mustard yellow, blue-green and milk-paint grey. Clean lines, no ornament, honest dovetails, spare, serene and tidy, with sunlight and handmade perfection, slender, carefully balanced proportions and a quiet, humble dignity in every joint.
In the style of Heals, English Arts and Crafts and early modern design in plain, light limed oak and fumed oak, with simple rectilinear forms, square-section legs, chamfered edges, brass or iron hardware, cut-out hearts and slender tall proportions. Painted pastel finishes with delicate stencilled flowers, bold geometric printed linen and cotton in modern greens, oranges and inky blues. Tidy, sensible and cottage-modern.
In the style of Ercol, English post-war design in pale elm and beech. Slender spindles, sweeping hoops, curved arms, splayed tapering legs and simple bentwood construction. Light blond finishes and darker stained elm with visible swirling grain, round-cornered boxes with tapered feet and a gentle radiating rhythm. Warm rust, olive and mustard tweed tones. Warm, domestic and gentle, with a suburban 1950s feel.
In the style of G-Plan, British mid-century teak and afrormosia, low and sleek with tapered round legs, angular brass details and two-tone panels in white and black laminate. Sculptural shapes have slim wooden frames and chunky tweed cushioning in orange, mustard, olive and chocolate, with round-ended forms. Rich orange-brown oiled grain, with clean horizontal emphasis and slim tapering supports. Optimistic, comfortable and suburban 1960s.
In the style of Parker Knoll, British traditional comfort with deep padded cushioning, tall sloping forms with ears, shaped polished walnut or mahogany timber with turned fronts, and short curving legs. Upholstery in floral chintz, tapestry weave, brocade, mustard velvet or green moquette, with buttoned details and fringed or piped seams. Cosy, grandmotherly and comfortable, with mechanics hidden under a skirted base, like a traditional lounge by a gas fire.
In the style of Hille, British modernist moulded polypropylene, with one-piece seamless forms on four splayed legs in primary red, orange, white, black and lime, rounded rectangular profiles and gently flared edges. Plastic cubes, plain plywood and bent-ply surfaces, shiny high-impact polymer with a matte texture, simple geometry and mass-produced elegance. Pop, optimistic and space-age 1960s, with a friendly, bold, uncluttered look that suits mass production.
In the style of Tolix, tough, thin, urban industrial design pressed from single sheets of steel. Simple tapered lines, rounded pressed curves, perforations used as the only pattern, and reinforced edges. Repeatable, economical forms with a visibly utilitarian build. Finish is matte lacquer, powder-coat or galvanised raw zinc in red, dark green, yellow, black, grey and silver, with dents, scuffs and a worn patina.
In the style of Vitsoe, calm, minimalist and timeless. Thin vertical aluminium lines with slim aluminium planes hung from them on small pegs. Surfaces are powder-coated in white, black or light grey, or smooth plywood in pale birch, with sleek clean edges. Crisp panels in matte pale colours with narrow slots and small detailing. Everything is clean, orderly and rectilinear with thin lines and no decoration.
In the style of Godrej, solid, heavy, trustworthy mid-century Indian steel design. Tall rectangular boxy volumes of welded sheet steel with rounded corners, squat secure proportions and a single bold vertical chrome pull. Surfaces are thick baked enamel with a faint orange peel texture in grey green, olive, cream, rust brown, pale blue, dark green or black, with thin gold pinstripes and brass fittings. Round, oversized control details feel reassuringly chunky. Marks are looping cursive signature lettering in white, deep red or purple.
