the taotaomo'na stand among the latte as pale tall figures of ancient Chamorro men and women in their thirties to their sixties, the men in woven loincloths and the women in skirts of woven pandanus, their hair tied up
the taotaomo'na appear as the old Chamorro of long ago, Gadao, a man in his forties wholly nude, his penis and scrotum visible, his head shaved but for a topknot, and Ida, a woman in her thirties bare above the waist with a small apron of pounded bark, both pale and shining
in an old Chamorro house on latte stones, the polished skulls of the ancestors are kept in small baskets hung from the rafters, a man in his fifties in a woven loincloth sitting beneath them
Nina, a Chamorro woman in her forties in present-day clothes, walks along the jungle path, her head bowed as she asks the taotaomo'na for leave to pass, a pale figure of a tall man, Hugo, in his fifties watching from the banyan
