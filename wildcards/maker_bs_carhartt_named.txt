In the style of Carhartt, rugged American labourer character built on heavy duck canvas in tobacco brown, black and faded tan. Triple-stitched seams, riveted stress points, quilted flannel and corduroy contrast, with generous, boxy, hard-working proportions and folded, doubled-up reinforcement. A small square mark in red and white or brown. Stiff, dense cotton that softens, fades and scuffs with wear. Honest, durable, sturdy and unfussy, built to last a lifetime of hard work.
