Styled in a design language of slim forms in glossy black or red lacquer decorated with delicate gold, silver and coloured powder. Tiny cranes, dragonflies, bamboo, plum blossom, waves and mountains appear in raised, sparkling gold with soft gradations, often covering the whole surface. Metal parts are gold or rhodium with fine engraved scrolls. Forms are smooth and elegant with rounded ends, presented in pale paulownia wood. The mood is quiet luxury, patient craft and nature-inspired. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
