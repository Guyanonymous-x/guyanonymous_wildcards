Styled in a design language of wax-resist dyed cotton and silk in dense repeating patterns of parang diagonal waves, overlapping circles, cloud swirls, floral sprays, birds and trailing vines. Typical colours are warm sogan brown, deep indigo, dark black, cream and golden yellow, plus brighter coastal tones in red, turquoise and green. Fine crackle lines show where the wax cracked, and slight irregularities reveal the hand-drawn pen or copper stamp. Formal, refined, ceremonial mood, with a draped, flowing quality. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
