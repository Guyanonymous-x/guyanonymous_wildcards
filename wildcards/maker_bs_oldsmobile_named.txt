Styled in a design language of jet-age, confident and glossy American rocket-themed optimism. A bright wide front with a central round jet-like form, long sweeping chrome lines down each flank, wraparound curves and a canopy on thin supports. Chrome with spinning centres and gleaming bright trim throughout. Rocket red, white, mint green and sky blue, laid out as two-tone vinyl and shining surfaces. Streamlined, speedy motifs repeated everywhere. Cheerful and polished. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
