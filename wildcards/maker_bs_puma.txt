Styled in a design language of sleek athletic design with a confident, fast, low-slung attitude. Long tapering stripe forms sweep diagonally across surfaces as a single flowing accent curve. Palette is crisp white, deep black and signal red, with royal blue, forest green, mustard and cream for retro colour-blocked pairings. Materials include soft suede with a velvety nap, smooth nylon, terry cloth, glossy synthetic panels and clean piping. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
