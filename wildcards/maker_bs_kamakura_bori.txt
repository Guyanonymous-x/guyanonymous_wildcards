In the style of Kamakura-bori, carved wooden trays, bowls, boxes and mirrors cut from katsura or ginkgo and finished in red-brown or black lacquer. Carving is bold and shallow, using gouge cuts to form camellia flowers, peonies, cherry blossoms, leaves and waves, with rounded petal edges and flat bevelled leaf surfaces. The lacquer is slightly matte, built from a vermilion or cinnabar layer over black that wears at the high points to expose a warm brown underlayer. Forms are simple discs, rounded rectangles and lobed trays with deep carved rims. The look is folk-craft, sculptural and handmade, with visible tool marks.
