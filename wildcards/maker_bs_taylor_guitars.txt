In the style of Taylor Guitars, acoustic guitars with a modern, clean, sleek look. Bodies are slim with a cutaway, bright natural-coloured spruce tops, and sides and back in sapele, rosewood or koa with fine grain. Finishes are thin, glossy and flawless, and the edges have rounded bevels, especially at the lower arm. Inlays are minimal, such as slim abalone or ebony markers in an angular, elegant shape, with thin bindings and a rosette ring of dark inlay. Headstocks have a tapered, smooth curve with an engraved logo. The mood is precise, bright, contemporary, and carefully finished.
