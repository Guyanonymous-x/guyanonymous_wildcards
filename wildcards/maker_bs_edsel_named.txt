Styled in a design language of overwrought, odd and unforgettable American design. A tall vertical horse-collar shape set in the middle of a wide bar, wingtip boomerang forms, side sculpting with a bright spear and a cut-away dip along the flank. Two-tone pink and black, turquoise and white, yellow and brown. Open pillarless transparency, chrome rims with whitewall roundness and a rolling dome of graphics add oddness. Ambitious, fussy and strange. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
