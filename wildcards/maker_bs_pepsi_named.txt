In the style of Pepsi, bold, electric, pop-culture energy built on a round globe divided into red, white and blue. A white wave runs through the circle, which tilts, smiles or splits into bold slices and large flat discs. Palette is deep cobalt and midnight blue with bright red, crisp white and pops of electric blue glow, with black used for modern, edgy variants. Lettering is lowercase, wide, clean and geometric, often set in huge scale or tucked beside the globe. Surfaces are glossy, chilled and metallic, covered in condensation, ice and fizz bubbles, with dark backgrounds lit by neon rim light. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
