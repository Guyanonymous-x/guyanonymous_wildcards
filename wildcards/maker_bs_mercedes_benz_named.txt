In the style of Mercedes-Benz, upright, dignified and solid formal engineering from the late analogue era. Forms are tall, squared and flat-sided with sharp creases, generous glazing on thin supports, and chunky edges with black rubber strips. A bright three-pointed star in a ring is the single recurring mark, set against wide polished surrounds and thin vertical ribbing. Palette is champagne, ivory, signal red, midnight blue, silver grey and black, with bright chrome catching light. Interiors use tan or blue leather and tufted velour, warm walnut veneer, big round dials and large rotary knobs. Lettering is plain, even, upright capitals. Advertising is calm, dark and elegant, with spotlit silver surfaces and understated confidence.
