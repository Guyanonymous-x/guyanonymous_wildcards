below the earth lies the village of the dead, a camp of round tents of reindeer hide on a gray tundra, the dead in their thirties to eighties in fur coats tending their herds
the soul of the dead man rides down into the world below on a sled pulled by the reindeer killed at his pyre, the road dark and the snow pale blue
in the underworld the dead sit in a hide tent around a fire, men and women in their forties to eighties in fur, living on as they did above
