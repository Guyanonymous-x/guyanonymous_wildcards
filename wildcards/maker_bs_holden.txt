Styled in a design language of tough, sunburnt, no-nonsense Australian muscle. Long proportions, a wide split frontal face, round or square paired details and a flared body with a pronounced waist. Sporty versions carry a black stripe along the upper surface, bold side stripes, a small spoiler shape and a big number emblem. Steel rally-style motifs, vinyl bucket seating and three-spoke shapes. Palette of hot red, bright yellow, sunset orange and dark green. Loud, sun-baked and unfussy, with plenty of swagger. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
