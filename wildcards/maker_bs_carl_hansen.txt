In the style of Carl Hansen, Danish cabinetmaker furniture in solid oak, teak and walnut with soaked, oiled or soaped finishes showing fine grain. Curved wishbone-shaped backrests with a Y-shaped steam-bent top rail, hand-woven natural paper cord seats, tapered rounded legs, finely crafted pegged joints and visible wood joinery. Low lounge chairs with leather straps, sculptural oak armrests that float, round-edged dining tables. Palette of honey, pale blond, soft brown and cream leather. Calm, tactile, handcrafted, minimalist Scandinavian dining room and living room.
