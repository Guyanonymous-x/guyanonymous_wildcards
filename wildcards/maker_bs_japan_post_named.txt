Styled in a design language of a simple red mark built from a single stylised character with a horizontal bar above it, set in a rounded square or circle, used consistently in red and white. Rounded cylindrical forms with narrow horizontal slots and white plates. Clean, cheerful, polite public-service look with red, white, gold and soft pastel accents. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
