Styled in a design language of dignified, proud, warm national identity. Bright white fields above a pale beige base, divided by a dark green band edged in gold, with a large waving green and white flag motif. Folk art references include blue and white floral tile decoration, fine tilework, bright needlework and bold red geometric woven motifs. Green, white and gold glow against bright blue sky, with a thin red and green line in earlier versions. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
