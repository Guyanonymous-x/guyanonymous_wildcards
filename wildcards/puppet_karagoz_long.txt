Turkish Karagoz shadow puppets, flat translucent figures of dyed leather, each about as tall as an adult's hand and forearm. The two main figures are Karagoz, with a round black beard, big round eyes and a huge round black hat wound in thick folds, and Hacivat, with a pointed beard and a tall hat, both shown in profile. They are cut from camel hide scraped thin and dyed red, green, blue and yellow, so the light glows through them. Each figure has a single hinged arm and a hinged waist, held by a hole in the chest where a long stick fits at a right angle. Their clothes are cut with patterns of small holes and colored panels, a red jacket on Karagoz and a green robe on Hacivat. __puppet_karagoz_var__.
