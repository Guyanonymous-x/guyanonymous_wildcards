In the style of Chulucanas Ceramics, minimalist, tactile, graphic and folk-modern ceramics with smoky resist decoration. Smooth rounded thin-walled forms with softly bulging bodies, narrow necks and plump shapes, polished to a glossy sheen. Decoration comes from painting liquid clay slip over areas that stay light, then smoke blackens the bare areas, giving crisp contrasts of cream, orange-tan, chocolate brown and glossy black. Patterns are polka-dot spots, tight rows of dots, checkerboards, stripes, spirals, wavy lines, stepped angles and little fish or leaf motifs, often in two colours only, black on tan or tan on black. Like contemporary sculpture from an ancient workshop.
