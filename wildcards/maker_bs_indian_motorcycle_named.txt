In the style of Indian Motorcycle, rich, gleaming art deco Americana. Enormous deep valanced skirted curves, flowing teardrop volumes and long sweeping lines. Glossy deep red lacquer with cream or gold pinstripes and hand-lettered script, bright chrome, tan leather and a small glowing ornament. Lacquer colours such as Indian red, ivory and dark blue. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
