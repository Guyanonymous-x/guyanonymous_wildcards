__bs_subject_packaging__ styled in a design language of dainty, sugary Second Empire Parisian elegance. Pastel colours in pistachio green, rose pink, lilac, vanilla yellow, powder blue and coffee brown are laid out in soft ombre blocks. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
