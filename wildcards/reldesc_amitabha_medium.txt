Amitabha Buddha, the Buddha of infinite light, a serene red-skinned Buddha with half-closed eyes, long earlobes and a topknot of tight blue curls, wearing a flowing red monastic robe over one shoulder, his skin a warm coral red, his face perfectly calm, the robe falling in smooth folds over his crossed legs, the lotus pink with broad open petals, the halo glowing red and gold behind him, a pure land of jeweled trees and calm ponds in the distance, everything bathed in soft rosy light
