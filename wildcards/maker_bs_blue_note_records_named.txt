In the style of Blue Note Records, modernist mid-century graphic design with a cool, smoky, rhythmic feel. Compositions are asymmetrical and built on strict grids, with tightly cropped duotone imagery in deep blue, black and white, interrupted by flat blocks of orange, red, yellow, cyan or green. Lettering is bold, condensed or heavy sans-serif, set large, tilted, stacked and sometimes overlapping, with letters bleeding off an edge and lines of text rotated ninety degrees. Thin rules, circles, dots and angular bars add rhythm. Surfaces are matte ink on slightly textured paper with grainy halftone texture and visible overprint. The emblem is a small blue circle with a rounded lowercase wordmark. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
