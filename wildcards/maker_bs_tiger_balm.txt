Styled in a design language of vivid, nostalgic, bold medicinal design. The hero is a striding side-view big cat with a curved tail, drawn in an orange and black woodcut manner. Cartoon animals, yellow and black palette. Old pharmacy feel, pungent and strong. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
