In the style of Gustavsberg, warm, humane, playful Scandinavian modern studio ceramics. Soft, curved, slightly lopsided organic shapes such as pebble forms, leaf dishes, squashed spheres and wide-hipped jugs, with soft shoulders and visible finger marks. Glazes are matte and richly mottled, in sage green, rust, mustard, deep turquoise, charcoal grey and cream, pooling at the edges, with incised stripes and dots, circles, linear figures, animal motifs and silhouetted leaves. A signature dark green-brown stoneware is inlaid with delicate silver leaf patterns. Plain white with coloured berries and flowers. Intimate handmade surfaces and a calm earthy palette that suits teak and linen. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
