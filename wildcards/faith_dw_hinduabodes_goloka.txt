in Goloka, the world of cows, Krishna stands under a kadamba tree holding a bamboo flute to his lips, a man in his twenties with blue skin, in yellow silk and a crown with a peacock feather, Radha beside him, a woman in her twenties in a red and gold sari
the gopis, cowherd women in their twenties, dance the rasa in a ring with Krishna, a Krishna between each pair of women, on the bank of the Yamuna among flowering trees
Goloka spreads as green pastures and groves along a river, herds of white cows wearing small brass bells, and cowherds, men and women in their twenties to their fifties, bring pots of milk and butter to Krishna
