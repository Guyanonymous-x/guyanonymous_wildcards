Styled in a design language of low, mean and smooth custom-friendly American design of the late 1940s and 1950s. A smooth rounded slab body, a low chopped profile, fat round shapes, teardrop lines flowing into the sides, a sloping line, small forms in a rounded end and a chrome strip along the flank. Skirted lower edges. Candy maroon, primer grey, gloss black and cream. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
