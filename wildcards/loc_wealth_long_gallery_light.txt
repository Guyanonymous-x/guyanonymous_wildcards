The longest and most peculiar light in the building, eighteen windows in a continuous range down one side, so the room is lit from one flank along its whole length and the inner wall is washed evenly while the floor carries eighteen bright patches separated by eighteen bands of mullion shadow, which a person walking passes through at about one a second and which is the physical sensation the room was built to give
The light falls across the pictures rather than onto them, so the portraits are best seen from one direction and almost unreadable from the other
At the ends the light runs out and the clock at the far end is a silhouette; which leaves the middle third dark; and candles are carried rather than placed, so the room at night is a moving pool in a very long dark
