Styled in a design language of sculptural form in permanently heat-pressed micro-pleated synthetic cloth that springs back into shape, cut as oversized geometric volumes such as tubes, rectangles, circles and folded origami-like planes. Silhouettes are loose, weightless and body-skimming rather than fitted, with exaggerated cocoon shapes. Corrugated vertical or zigzag pleats catch light in ripples, alongside crinkled, bonded and sheer technical textures. Colour is either muted charcoal, black, ivory and indigo or sudden saturated blocks of orange, fuchsia, cobalt, lime and yellow, sometimes in rainbow stripes. Minimal seams, no visible fastenings, quiet architectural calm and bright airy light.
