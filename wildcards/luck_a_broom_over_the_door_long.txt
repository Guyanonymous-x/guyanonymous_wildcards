a broom over the door, a small broom hung over a doorway, a folk charm, over a door
a small besom broom of bundled twigs bound to a wooden handle, hung crosswise above a doorway, the twigs pale brown and bundled tight at the top with twine, splaying out at the bottom, the handle a straight peeled branch, two iron hooks holding it across the lintel, over a painted cottage door with dried herbs tied to the handle. Around it the wall is plain painted plaster, a light switch and a coat hook beside the door, the floor below scuffed wood. Soft daylight comes in from a window to one side
