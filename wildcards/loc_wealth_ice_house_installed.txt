always
which is the one feature everybody who writes about these buildings gets right because it is so obviously important; a grille floor over a void with a trapped drain to a distant soakaway, which is the feature everybody gets wrong and which is what separates an ice house that keeps ice for two years from a pit that loses it by June; a loading hatch in the dome, because ice is tipped in from above and lifted out from the passage; a charge of 60-120 tonnes of ice cut from the lake or the canal in January in blocks
