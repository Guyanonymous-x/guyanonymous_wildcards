Styled in a design language of practical, sturdy and adventurous design with a rally-bred streak. Shapes are boxy and honest, with squared-off volumes, flared curves, short overhangs and bold raised bulges. The emblem is a blue oval holding a cluster of six silver stars, seen in glossy chrome, flat white or embossed relief. Palette is saturated rally blue with gold accents, plus white, silver, black, forest green and warm orange. Materials are hardwearing woven fabric with bright contrast stitching, rubberised textures, matte plastic and scuffed metal. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
