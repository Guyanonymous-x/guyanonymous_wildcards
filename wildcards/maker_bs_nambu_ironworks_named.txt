In the style of Nambu ironworks, weighty cast-iron forms with rounded, bulging volumes and a quiet, austere, wabi-sabi presence. Surfaces carry rows of small raised bumps like hailstones in a tight regular grid, or finely cast relief motifs of pine, cranes, blossoms and waves, sometimes with fine silver inlay. Finish is matte black, deep brown or rust. Small knob-like accents, gracefully curved dark metal details and short elegant projections. Simple, heavy, contemplative and ceremonial. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Nambu ironworks in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
