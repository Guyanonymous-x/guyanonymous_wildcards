In the style of Tucker, a late 1940s rear-engined American sedan with a very low, smooth, rounded fastback body like a teardrop, three headlamps with the centre lamp in the nose that turns with the steering wheel, a narrow chrome-bar grille with a wide mouth, and a swept-back roof. Fully enclosed rear with a louvered engine cover, broad rear fenders and slim tail lamps. Chrome bumpers and a thin trim line, with small whitewall tyres and plain hubcaps. Interior with padded dash, central driving position gauge cluster and bench seats. Colours of waltz blue, beige, maroon and dark green. Futuristic and streamlined.
