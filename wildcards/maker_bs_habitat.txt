Styled in a design language of British swinging-sixties high street design, simple and affordable, with cheerful bright striped and checked cotton, white and primary-coloured enamel, chunky pine, stackable wood, earthenware, white porcelain, wire, paper lanterns, rush matting and bright rugs. Colours are red, yellow, white, orange and natural pine. Casual, youthful, continental bistro spirit with plants, bare floors and plain white walls, sociable and unfussy. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
