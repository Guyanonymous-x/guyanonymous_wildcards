Styled in a design language of energetic, vivid green wholesome design. Saturated apple green covers broad surfaces, set against deep forest green, white and warm chocolate brown. Surfaces are glossy ridged metal, matt printed card and soft-touch plastic, with a stylised ribbon curve wrapping around shapes. Patterns use dynamic diagonal slashes, dotted energy sparks, hexagon cells and simple sporty line illustrations. Forms are rounded, cylindrical and sturdy with bevelled ribs. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
