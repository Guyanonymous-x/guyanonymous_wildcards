Styled in a design language of calm, elegant, restrained design rooted in Japanese minimalism and a feeling of motion even when still. Surfaces are smooth and sculpted, with soft flowing highlights and reflections instead of hard creases, long graceful proportions and fine, slender chrome lines. Palette is deep crystal red, machine grey, soft pearl white, charcoal and black. Materials include matte metal, burgundy and cream leather, natural wood and fine stitching. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
