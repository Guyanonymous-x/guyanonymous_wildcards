In the style of Royal Delft, tin-glazed earthenware with a creamy white ground and a vivid cobalt blue painted decoration. Scenes include windmills, canals, sailing ships, tulips, villagers, birds and landscapes drawn with fine brushwork and tonal shading. Plates, tiles, tall tulip vases and vases with pyramids of spouts feature curling scrollwork, floral borders and small figures. The glaze is shiny, slightly bluish-white and pooled in places, and the blue ranges from pale to near-black. Tile pictures in grids repeat simple blue motifs in corner spandrels. The mood is crisp, domestic, nostalgic and traditionally Dutch.
