Styled in a design language of clean, bright, hygienic, friendly miniature design. A squat pale form about the size of a thumb joint, with a rounded shoulder and a ribbed faceted waist like a small cut-glass tumbler. Items are bundled in tight multiples or lined in rows so the red and white pattern repeats. Plenty of white, pale pink, one hot red accent and a mild medical-nutrition feeling. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
