Styled in a design language of late 1950s and early 60s forward-look American design, space-age, optimistic and airy. Long slender profiles, tall swept-back blade shapes, V-shaped winged ornaments, and flowing chrome side strips that divide contrasting colour panels. A chrome five-pointed star within a pentagon, and wing-like badges in polished gold or silver, are the recurring marks. Surfaces are mirror chrome, sculpted pressed panels, glossy lacquer and luxurious woven fabric. Palette is turquoise, flamingo pink, cream, charcoal, red and gold. Push-button crispness and confident, uplifting streamlining. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
