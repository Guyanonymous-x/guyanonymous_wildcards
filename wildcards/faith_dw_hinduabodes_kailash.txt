on the snowy peak of Kailash Shiva sits on a tiger skin in meditation, a man in his thirties with pale skin smeared with white ash and a blue throat, his matted hair coiled high with a crescent moon and the river Ganga springing from it, a trident beside him
Shiva and Parvati sit together on Kailash, Parvati a woman in her twenties in red silk on his left knee, Ganesha with the head of an elephant before them, and Nandi the white bull lying at the foot of the rock
Kailash rises as a great white peak, its four faces of crystal, ruby, gold and lapis, Lake Manasarovar shining blue at its foot with swans on the water, and the ganas, the stout attendants of Shiva with the faces of adults in their forties, dancing on its slopes
