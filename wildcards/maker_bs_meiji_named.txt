In the style of Meiji, tidy, friendly, nostalgic Japanese convenience design in red, chocolate brown, cream and white. Bright red fields with a white script mark, silvery metallic accents, and clean cream panels carry a small simple illustration. Flat cartoon drawings of woodland scenes in pale yellow and brown, rounded fonts, small bilingual text and orderly dividers sit on a simple grid. Charming shapes pair a narrow stem with a rounded dark cap. Mood is clean, cheerful, gentle and reassuring.
