at Beltaine, on the first night of summer, the herds of cattle are driven between two great bonfires on a bare hilltop to keep them from sickness, the flames rising high against a pale late-evening sky streaked with pink
at Samonios, the start of winter, the people gather at dusk at a great earth-banked enclosure on a hill, the last of the herds slaughtered for the winter feast, in cold gray light with the first fires lit
at Imbolc, at the end of winter, the women bring bowls of fresh ewes' milk and bundles of rushes to a sacred spring under bare oak and ash trees, in thin cold morning sunlight over frosty grass
at Lugnasad, the harvest gathering at the start of August, the tribes meet on a broad hilltop for horse races, trading and the settling of disputes, sheaves of wheat stacked around the field, in hot golden afternoon sun
