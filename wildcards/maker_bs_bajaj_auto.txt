Styled in a design language of cheerful, unpretentious, resilient and familiar, packed with colour on a dusty street. Rounded pressed-steel bodies with bulbous curves, vinyl-covered seating and chrome badges. Thick glossy enamel in sky blue, cream, off-white, rust red and olive green, with round chrome-rimmed lamps and stubby upright details. Tiny compact forms with canvas or sheet metal enclosures, open sides, lemon yellow and black or green and yellow pairings. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
