Styled in a design language of comfortable middle-class luxury of the 1950s, rounded and heavily chromed, dignified rather than flashy. A toothy chrome grin of many small bars, large round chrome bullet shapes, a row of three or four round portholes and a sweeping chrome spear along the side. Slightly bulbous, plump forms and a tri-shield motif. Cream, sea green, coral and maroon. Warm, prosperous and reassuring. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
