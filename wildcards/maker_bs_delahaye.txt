Styled in a design language of flamboyant, theatrical, curvaceous Art Deco coachwork. Very long sweeping lines, a teardrop body, pontoon forms flowing over the base, a long mass running into a sloping end, fine chrome bars and chrome spears running through the curves. Two-tone schemes such as ivory over dark blue, silver over burgundy and deep black. Cream or red leather and engine-turned metal with round faces. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
