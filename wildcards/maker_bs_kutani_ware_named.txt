Styled in a design language of dense, opaque overglaze enamels in five dark, saturated colours, namely deep green, Prussian blue, yellow, purple and aubergine red. A second manner uses a bright red ground with gold. Green is often laid on thickly as a background with a heavy, jewel-like gloss. Rich, theatrical, almost tapestry-like richness and a thick bright glaze. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
