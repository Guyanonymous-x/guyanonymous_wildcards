In the style of Crown Lynn, plump, rounded, thick, durable, friendly mid-century forms with simple lines, chunky handholds and glossy glaze. Palette is white, matte pastel, pink, turquoise and black, plus speckled turquoise, mustard, tangerine and sage. Glazes include a dripping trickle effect where one colour runs over another, and dark manganese brown with scratched-line patterns. Decoration is modern abstract florals, bamboo, diamonds, starbursts, ferns and spiral koru shapes in mid-century colours. Elegant curved necks and hollow forms appear in sculptural pieces. Retro, warm, optimistic, distinctly 1950s and 1960s. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
