In the style of Lanvin, soft, graceful, romantic femininity with a jeweled couture touch. Full, bell-like volumes sit over a fitted, lowered waistline, softened by bows, ribbons and gently gathered trims. Fine silk surfaces are richly worked with intricate beading and embroidery, with small flower appliques scattered lightly. Palette is powder blue, rose pink, lilac, silver and cream in delicate tonal harmony. Any emblem is a simple stylised line drawing inside a round frame in cream and blue. Tender, polished, sparkling and gently nostalgic, with early twentieth century elegance. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
