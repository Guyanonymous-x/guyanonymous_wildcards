Guna women in their twenties to eighties on the benches, in molas, blouses with front and back panels of layered cloth cut to show bright red, orange and black designs of birds and mazes, red and yellow headscarves and long blue wrap skirts, their forearms and calves wound tightly with strings of tiny orange and yellow beads
older women in their fifties to eighties with short cropped black hair, a thin black line painted down the nose and a small gold ring through the middle of the nose, sitting together and stitching molas in their laps
men in their twenties to seventies in shirts and trousers along the side benches, straw hats on their knees, and village wardens in their thirties to fifties walking between the benches with long thin sticks, and one taps the shoulder of a dozing man
