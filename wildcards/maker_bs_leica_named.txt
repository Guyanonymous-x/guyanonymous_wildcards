Styled in a design language of understated, precise, timeless German craft. Surfaces are matt black enamel, polished and brushed silver chrome, finely pebbled black leather-like texture and brass showing through at worn edges. Compact rectangular forms have flat planes and crisp edges, with fine knurled bands. Palette is black, silver, signal red and white, with occasional olive, tan or red leather accents. Minimal, free of clutter, with perfectly even gaps and honest patina. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
