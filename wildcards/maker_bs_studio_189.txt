Styled in a design language of ethical, handmade design centred on hand-dyed indigo, hand-batiked cloth, resist patterns, hand-woven fabrics and block-printed wax prints. Shapes are relaxed and modern, with wide legs, boxy tops, flowing wraps and kimono-like layers in cotton and linen. Palette includes deep indigo blue, white, earthy ochre, rust, black and bright accent colours such as orange and green, in tie-dye swirls, hand-drawn stripes, dots, geometric grids and flowing batik lines. Visible hand-dyed unevenness and crisp artisanal texture. Breezy, warm, socially conscious, sunlit mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
