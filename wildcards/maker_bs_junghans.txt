Styled in a design language of pared-back, functional and disciplined modernist. Matte steel or gold with minimal detail. Colours are white, black and grey with occasional warm orange or yellow accents. Small cylinders or squares of off-white enamel or wood with tiny perforation details. Quiet and rational. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
