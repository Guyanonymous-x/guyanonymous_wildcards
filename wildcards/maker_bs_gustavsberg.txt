In the style of Gustavsberg, Swedish mid-century studio ceramics. Pieces are hand-thrown or moulded vases, bowls, dishes, ashtrays, tiles and stoneware figurines in soft, curved and slightly lopsided organic shapes, such as pebble forms, leaf dishes, squashed spheres and wide-hipped jugs. Glazes are matte and richly mottled, in sage green, rust, mustard, deep turquoise, charcoal grey and cream, often with sgraffito, incised stripes and dots, circles, linear figures, animal motifs and silhouetted leaves. A signature type is a dark green-brown stoneware inlaid with silver, formed in rounded jars with a delicate silver leaf pattern. Tableware is plain white with coloured berries and flowers. The mood is warm, humane, playful and Scandinavian modern, with intimate handmade surfaces. Surfaces show glaze pooling at the edges, visible finger marks, soft shoulders on every vase and a calm, slightly earthy palette that suits teak shelves and linen curtains.
