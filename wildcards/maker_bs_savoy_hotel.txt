Styled in a design language of streamlined, glamorous 1930s Art Deco in black, silver, gold and ivory. Chromed trim, stepped geometric mouldings, curved glass, mirrored panels and sleek brass. A tall gold figure on a column and a steel canopy mark the entrance. Silver leaf, stylised sunburst motifs, lacquered woodwork, plush deep carpets and curving staircases. Bold symmetry and lustrous materials create drama. Mood is polished, elegant, confident and theatrical, with crisp stepped geometry throughout.
