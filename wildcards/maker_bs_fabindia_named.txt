In the style of Fabindia, handloom and hand-block-printed cotton design with a rural craftsman feel. Relaxed, straight, loose shapes in soft textured cotton and silk-cotton blends show visible slub in the weave. Colours are earthy natural dyes such as indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow, rust, ochre, olive, charcoal and undyed cream. Patterns are small repeating floral and geometric block prints, ikat, dotted tie-dye, stripes and checks, with slightly irregular hand-printed edges. Stitching is simple, with wooden fastenings. Unfussy, honest, sun-washed and warm. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Fabindia in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
