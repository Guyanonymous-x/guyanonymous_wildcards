Styled in a design language of thick, glassy, flawless coats of black and deep vermilion lacquer polished to a mirror depth with a warm inner glow. Forms are crisp, perfectly even and substantial, with sturdy rounded edges because the hard foundation shows at the contours. Red and black are set in strong contrast, one inside and one outside. Decoration is restrained and rare, fine gold powder painting of tiny flowers, birds and grasses in sparse arrangements, or hair-thin lines filled with gold leaf. Large areas stay plain and unbroken. The mood is formal, luxurious, calm and exact. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
