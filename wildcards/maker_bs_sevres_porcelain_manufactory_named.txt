In the style of Sevres Porcelain Manufactory, smooth glossy porcelain with deep saturated ground colours, rich royal blue, turquoise, delicate rose pink and apple green, all heavily gilded. Reserved panels hold finely painted cherubs, pastoral figures, birds and flower bouquets in naturalistic colour. Gilt borders, scrolls, beading and lacy foliage trim every edge, and curves flow like branches or flower stems. Forms are swelling, ornate and Rococo. Unglazed matte white areas have a silky, snow-like texture.
