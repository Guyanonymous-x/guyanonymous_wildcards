Styled in a design language of Dutch tin-glazed earthenware painted by hand in rich cobalt blue on glossy white, depicting windmills, canals, sailing ships, tulips, milkmaids, farmhouses and floral scrolls, with fine brushwork and blue shading. Tiered, pyramid-like forms appear in some work. Slightly crackled glaze, soft blue-white contrast, charming, folksy Dutch heritage with elegant Chinese-inspired scenes, crisp and tidy in finish, nostalgic and gentle in mood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
