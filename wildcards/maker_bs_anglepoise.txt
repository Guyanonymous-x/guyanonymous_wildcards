Styled in a design language of technical, balanced and flexible engineering shown honestly. Slender jointed members, exposed coiled springs and small pivots make a lean, angular, skeletal geometry, finished in crisp, tidy, engineered details. Enamelled and powder-coated metal in jet black, cream, pale grey, signal red, soft teal and mustard sits beside polished brass, chrome and brushed steel. Surfaces are smooth, satin and slightly industrial, with tidy rivets and neatly turned details. Lettering is slim, simple and technical, like a drawing label. Inspired by technical drawing, functional British modernism, precise, witty and understated. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
