Styled in a design language of weighty cast-iron forms with rounded, bulging volumes and a quiet, austere, wabi-sabi presence. Surfaces carry rows of small raised bumps like hailstones in a tight regular grid, or finely cast relief motifs of pine, cranes, blossoms and waves, sometimes with fine silver inlay. Finish is matte black, deep brown or rust. Small knob-like accents, gracefully curved dark metal details and short elegant projections. Simple, heavy, contemplative and ceremonial. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
