Styled in a design language of deep lustrous black glossy ground painted in egg tempera with fairy tale scenes. Elongated, towers, trees and onion domes. The palette is bright red, gold, emerald green, white and orange against black, with fine gold highlights outlining draperies and ornamental borders of tiny patterns. Red lacquer provides contrast. Details are miniature and precise, as in an icon, and the mood is mythical, jewel-like and storybook. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
