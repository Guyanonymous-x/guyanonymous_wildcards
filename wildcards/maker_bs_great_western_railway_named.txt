In the style of Great Western Railway, proud Victorian-inspired engineering elegance. Gleaming deep green paint with orange and black lining, polished brass and copper accents, black dark-toned ends and red bases. Tall brass-edged nameplates sit on curved forms. Secondary colours are cream and chocolate brown. Metal is hand-polished, lines are fine and hand-drawn, and every part is crafted to be admired. Stately, dignified, traditional and richly finished. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
