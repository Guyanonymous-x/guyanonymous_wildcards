In the style of Hornby, tinplate and later die-cast model railways in British livery, locomotives in glossy dark green, maroon, black and lined gold with polished brass domes and chimney caps, coaches in cream and crimson or chocolate and cream, tiny rivet and handrail details, tin lineside stations and signals with printed brickwork. Boxes are blue or red with a ruled border, a painted illustration of a speeding steam train with white smoke over green countryside, old-fashioned serif and block capitals, a nostalgic mid-century catalogue feel.
