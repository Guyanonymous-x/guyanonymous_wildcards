a priest digs a round pit in the earth on a riverbank with a dagger, and pours into it oil, honey, wine and milk from small clay jugs, then lays a flat loaf of bread at its edge
a priest in his fifties cuts the throat of a black piglet over the pit, its blood running down into the earth, and beside him lie a dead dove and a dead sparrow ready to be thrown in
a small ladder of silver is let down into the pit on a cord for the former gods to climb up, and above the pit the faint forms of the old gods rise out of the earth into the lamp light
