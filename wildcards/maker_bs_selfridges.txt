Styled in a design language of bold, confident, high-impact British modernism built on one saturated sunshine yellow. Black twisted cord and plain black trim sit against the yellow with crisp contrast. Paper-like matte and semi-gloss finishes keep surfaces simple and graphic. Alternate colourways use hot pink, electric blue, lime and monochrome black and white in the same blunt layout, with a small ampersand-style mark. Little ornament, just strong blocks, large type and sharp edges. Loud, witty, fashionable and unmistakable. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
