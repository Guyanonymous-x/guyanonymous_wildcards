In the style of Savonnerie Manufactory, thick, plush hand-knotted wool pile carpets with a velvet surface and a sculptural, monumental pattern. Layouts are formal and symmetrical, with large acanthus scrolls, floral bouquets, shells, cornucopia, garlands and architectural cartouches on a dark ground in deep blue, black, brown or crimson. Motifs are shaded to look three-dimensional, with ivory, gold, rose, sage green and yellow highlights. Borders are wide, with trompe-l oeil mouldings and corners holding fruit and flowers. The overall feeling is that of a royal palace floor, sumptuous, ordered and heavy.
