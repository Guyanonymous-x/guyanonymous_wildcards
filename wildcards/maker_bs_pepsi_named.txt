Styled in a design language of bold, electric, pop-culture energy built on a round globe divided into red, white and blue. A white wave runs through the circle, which tilts, smiles or splits into bold slices and large flat discs. Palette is deep cobalt and midnight blue with bright red, crisp white and pops of electric blue glow, with black used for modern, edgy variants. Surfaces are glossy, chilled and metallic, covered in condensation, ice and fizz bubbles, with dark backgrounds lit by neon rim light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
