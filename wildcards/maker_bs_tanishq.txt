Styled in a design language of rich, symmetrical, festive ornament in warm 22 carat yellow gold, with heavy layered forms, stacks of bangle-like rings and hanging bell-like drops. Motifs include lotus flowers, peacocks, paisley mango shapes, leaves, elephants, filigree and temple-like architecture. Surfaces combine polished gold with fine granulation, rows of uncut diamonds, rubies, emeralds, pearls and coloured enamel, with delicate hanging pearl drops. Red and warm golden light surround it. A lighter side uses minimalist single stones and fine rose gold lines for everyday restraint. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
