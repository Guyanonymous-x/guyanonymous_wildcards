Styled in a design language of Finnish modernist design in pale laminated birch, with a single bent L-shaped support that curls into a smooth scroll beneath the surface. Forms are simple, round and light, with lightly curved plywood, soft rounded edges and visible blond grain. Natural birch and white lacquer are lifted by colourful lacquered parts in red, yellow, blue and green, black linoleum and striped woven webbing or canvas. Warm, humane Scandinavian functionalism with a nordic apartment and school hall mood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
