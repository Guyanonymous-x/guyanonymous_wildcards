Styled in a design language of welcoming, proud, highland-bright and colourful identity. Pale grey and white grounds with a flowing band of green, yellow and red lines sweeping upward. A deep green area carries a yellow and red stylised bird or wing emblem. Green, yellow and red with white woven cotton trimmed in coloured borders. The three colour bands repeat as flowing ribbons. Green, gold and red against clear blue sky. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
