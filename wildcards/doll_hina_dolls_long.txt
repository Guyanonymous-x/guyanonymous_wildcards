a Japanese hina doll display, a tiered stand of dolls set out for the doll festival in early spring. A stepped stand of seven tiers covered in bright red felt rises taller than an adult. On the top tier sit the emperor and empress dolls in front of a gold folding screen, the emperor in a stiff dark robe with a tall black hat, the empress in many layered silk robes of red, green and gold, her black hair falling long. On the tiers below sit three court ladies in white and red holding sake vessels, five musicians with tiny drums and flutes, two ministers, and three servants, each doll with a small white face and fine painted features. The lowest tiers hold miniature lacquered furniture, a palanquin and an ox cart in black and gold. __doll_hina_dolls_var__.
