the court dress of Akhenaten's Egypt, the pharaoh, his queen Nefertiti and their courtiers in their twenties to forties in robes of fine white linen so thin the body shows through, finely pleated in narrow folds and tied under the chest with a long red sash
sheer pleated linen gowns falling to the ankles, the pleats pressed in close lines from shoulder to hem, broad collars of gold and colored faience beads over the shoulders, sandals of gilded leather
the king in a pleated kilt of white linen tied low on the belly with a sash whose ends hang in front, a sheer pleated tunic over it, a wide beaded collar and the blue crown, a tall rounded helmet of blue studded with small gold discs, the queen in a thin smooth gown leaving one shoulder bare with a pleated white shawl over it knotted beneath her right breast
courtiers of Amarna in pleated white linen wrapped and knotted at the waist, heavy dark wigs, kohl lines drawn around the eyes, gold armbands and bead collars of blue and red
