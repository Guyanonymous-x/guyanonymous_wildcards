In the style of Gobelins Manufactory, woven wool and silk that looks like painting, built from dense hatching of finely graded coloured threads. Compositions are grand and theatrical, with mythological and historical drama, hanging garlands and architectural frames inside heavy borders of acanthus, laurel, fruit and gilt-looking scrollwork. The palette is deep and slightly aged, crimson, royal blue, forest green, ochre, gold and warm brown with pale highlights. Figures are idealised and baroque in pose, with billowing drapery and dramatic clouds. The surface is tight, flat and smooth. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
