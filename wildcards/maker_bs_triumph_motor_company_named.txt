Styled in a design language of British sports cars and saloons with elegant sleek bodies, wire-spoked wheels, small chrome grilles, rounded fenders, a folding hood, and lively paint in British racing green, signal red and ivory. Cabins have leather bucket seats and a walnut dash with round gauges. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
