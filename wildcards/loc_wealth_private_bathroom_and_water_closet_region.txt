the American equivalent, which from 1890 is fitted new, one per bedroom, and which is the single largest divergence in domestic standards between the two countries in this whole subject
the French version, which keeps the cabinet de toilette and the bidet alongside the bath rather than replacing them
the Scottish version, with a bigger geyser
the Russian and Finnish version, which is a bathhouse and does not accept this room at all
