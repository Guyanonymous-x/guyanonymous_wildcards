Styled in a design language of warm, earthy, ancient, handmade red clay. Rich terracotta, brick-red and dark brown bodies with wide swelling bellies, narrow tops and sturdy, chunky shapes, sometimes playfully modelled like a face or bird. Decoration is applied with slip, incised lines and glaze, in spirals, hatched bands, sun discs, tulip and carnation flowers, stylised birds, geometric borders, zigzags and dots in cream, brown, turquoise, green and cobalt blue. Surfaces may be unglazed matte, or covered with glossy green or amber glaze, showing cracks and pooled colour. Rustic, folk and timeless. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
