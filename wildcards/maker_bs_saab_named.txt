Styled in a design language of aeronautical, quirky, practical and eccentric Swedish design. Short, stubby, rounded forms with deeply curved glass-like surfaces, rectangular accents and a cockpit-like compactness. Palette is midnight blue, black, silver metallic, deep red and white. Finishes are satin black plastic edges, brushed alloy with a distinct pattern of round holes, grey or blue textured cloth and moulded rubbery trims. Asymmetric wraparound forms with big round faces give a functional, jet-inspired character. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
