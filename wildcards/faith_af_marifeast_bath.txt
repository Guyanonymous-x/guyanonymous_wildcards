inside the steaming bathhouse the souls of the family, faint pale figures in their fifties to nineties, sit on the wooden bench while a man in his forties lays a fresh birch whisk beside each
after the bath the family stands in the yard holding cups of tea for the dead and inviting them in, the bathhouse door left open behind them
