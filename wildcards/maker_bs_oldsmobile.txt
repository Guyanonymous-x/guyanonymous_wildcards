Styled in a design language of chrome with spinning centres and gleaming bright trim throughout. Rocket red, white, mint green and sky blue, laid out as two-tone vinyl and shining surfaces. Streamlined, speedy motifs repeated everywhere. Cheerful and polished. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
