the souls of the faithful rise as figures of men and women in their twenties to eighties in white robes, their faces lifted, drawn upward in a long line into the light
the souls of the deniers stand low on dark ground far from the light, men and women in their thirties to seventies in dull brown, their heads bowed and their shadows long behind them
each soul stands before the light with the deeds of all its lives shown in a row of faint pictures behind it, scenes of work, prayer, giving and wrongdoing from many ages
the whole of the Druze people, all their souls, men and women in their twenties to nineties, stand gathered on the mountainside in their thousands, waiting in the dawn
