__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of Art Nouveau iridescent ceramics. The signature is a shimmering metallic lustre glaze that shifts between gold, green, magenta, purple, cobalt and copper-red as the light changes, like a beetle wing or an oil slick. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
