Styled in a design language of matte unglazed stoneware in pale powder blue, sage green, lilac, black and cream, decorated with crisp white relief figures from classical Greek and Roman scenes, garlands, putti and acanthus borders. Fine sharp applied moulding sits on a smooth chalky background, alongside creamy glazed surfaces. Neoclassical symmetry, elegant, delicate and drawing-room refined, with a pale, powdery finish and fine, even detail across every surface.
