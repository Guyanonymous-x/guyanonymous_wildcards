The whole estate stops, twenty to forty men cut, cart, break and ram 90 tonnes of ice in three to six days of the hardest and coldest labor of the year, which is paid at a premium and is remembered
February, the hatch covered, strawed and the mound made good
March to September, a basket of ice fetched out two or three times a week for the still room at record 24, the dairy at record 30 and the dining room at record 3, which is one man, a lamp, three doors, eleven rungs and a block and tackle, and takes twenty minutes
A hot week in July, when it is every day and the tally board is watched
September, the charge nearly gone and the bottom of the chamber a slush on a grille over a running drain
October, the drain's trap checked, the doors looked at, the building shut
November and December, nothing, and the household waits to see whether the lake will freeze
