Styled in a design language of cheerful, bouncy, golden-age arcade charm of the early 1980s. Characters are simple round shapes with big expressive eyes, drawn in a chunky low-resolution pixel grid on pure black. Flat bright pixel-art fruit. Symmetrical insect-like shapes in blue, purple and yellow move in formation against single twinkling stars. Cartoon side art, scanlines, limited saturated colours and hard pixel edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
