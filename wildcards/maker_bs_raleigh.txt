Styled in a design language of English heritage craft with an ornamental metal-badge tradition. Deep gloss green, black, maroon and cream enamel is lined with hand-painted gold pinstripes. Polished brass, patinated copper, riveted tan leather and chrome details give a refined, tidy, old-world, countryside gentleman feel with fine hand-finished decorative attention. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
