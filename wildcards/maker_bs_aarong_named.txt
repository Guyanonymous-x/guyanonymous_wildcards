Styled in a design language of rural, handmade, colourful and lovingly textured craft design. Handwoven cotton, silk and fine muslin in vivid turmeric yellow, red, indigo, leaf green and white, with block-printed borders, tiny woven floral motifs and fine stripes. Natural materials such as terracotta, jute, cane and brass add earthy warmth. Spaces are tall and bright with hanging fabrics, rustic wood and earthy walls. Like a village fair in a tidy modern hall. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
