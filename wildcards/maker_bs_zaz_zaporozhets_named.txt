In the style of ZAZ Zaporozhets, tiny, boxy and rounded, humble, endearing and utilitarian. Slim supports and big curved glass give an airy look. Colour is flat, cheap-looking gloss in pea green, cream, sky blue, light yellow, orange-red, brick red and grey, with one thin chrome line. Small wheels, plain caps and narrow proportions. Spartan detailing in thin metal with a single round instrument. Drably cheerful. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
