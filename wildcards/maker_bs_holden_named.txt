Styled in a design language of tough, proud Australian design built around a heraldic lion emblem. A rampant lion stands upright on a rolling stone, drawn in solid bold red or black silhouette. Palette is signal red, black, white, silver and sunburnt tones such as gold, burnt orange and dusty tan. Shapes are strong, muscular and flat with bold stripes and thick colour blocks. Finishes are enamelled tin, cast metal, chrome and flat, slightly weathered painted surfaces. Retro tin-sign treatments with scuffed edges, rivets and a lightly rusted patina give a rugged, confident heritage feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
