Styled in a design language of sunny, tropical, festive heraldic design. A deep red and gold border frames a central mythical lion in a rearing pose with a curly mane, tufted tail, raised paw and fierce expression, drawn in old engraving style and flanked by ornamental curls, a crown emblem and a royal bird crest. The name sits below in bold upright capitals in gold or white. Surfaces are glossy deep red with gold and white detail and gold foil accents that add a sense of ceremony. Amber brown, brown and cream complete the palette. Chilled condensation, spicy grilled food and beach light. Lively and bold. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
