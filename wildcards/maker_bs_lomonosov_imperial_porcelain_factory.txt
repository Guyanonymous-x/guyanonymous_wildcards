Styled in a design language of bright white porcelain in two manners. The imperial manner shows gilded edges, cobalt blue nets of dark blue lattice with gold dots and rich floral reserves in naturalistic colour, on fine, thin, delicate forms. The revolutionary manner of the 1920s shows suprematist and propaganda designs with sharp red, black and white geometry, stars, workers and slogans in stencilled lettering, filling flat surfaces with boldly angular designs. Surfaces are lively and glossy.
