Styled in a design language of bold red and white with a warm coffee-brown depth. Palette is red, white, cream, coffee brown and gold, with dark charcoal for premium lines and deep green and tan for earthy variants. Surfaces are glossy lacquer, matt paper, foil film and cork-like textures. Decorative patterns use curling steam lines, bean shapes and hand-drawn leaf motifs in repeating tiles. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
