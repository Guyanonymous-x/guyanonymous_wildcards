Shot through a hyper color 470nm infrared filter and converted to black and white, the image shows a black-and-white image close to ordinary panchromatic film, with slightly brightened leaves. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
