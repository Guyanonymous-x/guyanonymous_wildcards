Styled in a design language of serious, utilitarian corporate design. Flat slab forms with squared corners in deep matte black soft-touch finish, a fine grainy texture and razor-straight seams. A single vivid signal red appears as tiny dots, thin rules and small tabs against black and graphite grey, with minimal satin silver trim. Plain recycled-paper wrapping, black and red colour blocks and tidy right-angle layouts. Rugged, disciplined, no-nonsense and quietly confident. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
