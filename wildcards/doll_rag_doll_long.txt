a hand-sewn rag doll, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her body is soft stuffed cotton in a pale fabric, sewn with big plain stitches you can see along the sides of the arms and legs. Her face is flat cloth with two black button eyes, a small triangle nose stitched in pink thread and a curved smile embroidered in red. Her hair is thick loops of yarn stitched across the top of her head and tied in two bunches with ribbons. She wears a dress sewn from scraps of flowered cotton with a white apron, and striped stockings that are part of her legs. __doll_rag_doll_var__.
