built in the best ashlar on the estate by the best mason available and detailed to a standard above anything in sections B or C and frequently above the house itself, because this is the family's one permanent statement; a coffered dome with an oculus, or a vault; eight small high windows in the drum
set high so that the light falls on the tablets and nobody can see in; thirty-four loculi in brick arcading
built for four to six generations, each one bricked up and plaqued as it is filled; stone shelves for lead coffins, because lead creeps and a stacked coffin fails; a ventilation shaft, which is essential and is almost always blocked; a drain channel round the catacomb floor; two locks on the doors with two keys held separately; a podium and three steps, so the building stands above its grove; no heating
