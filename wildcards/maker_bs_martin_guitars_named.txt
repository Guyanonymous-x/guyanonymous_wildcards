Styled in a design language of old-world American craftsmanship in warm wood. Honey-gold spruce tone ages to amber, paired with deep brown rosewood, mahogany and dark ebony. Decoration is delicate and hand-made, with herringbone inlay bands, fine concentric rings, cream edge trim, tortoiseshell accents and mother-of-pearl in flowing vine, snowflake and floral patterns. Finishes are thin satin to soft gloss lacquer, naturally crackled with age. Palette is amber, cream, brown and black. Traditional, tender, heirloom and honest. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
