at the mouth of the cave Turukawa, a great brown hawk, sits on a nest of leaves and sticks over two white eggs, from which, in the old story, the first man and woman came
on the slope below the cave the first man and woman, adults in their twenties with dark brown skin and long tightly curled hair, step out among broken white eggshells into the forest, and Degei watches them from the cave
in a clearing below his cave Degei teaches the first people, a man and a woman in their twenties in skirts of leaves, to plant taro and yams, the broad green leaves rising from neat mounds of earth
