__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of rich, symmetrical, festive ornament in warm 22 carat yellow gold, with heavy layered forms, stacks of bangle-like rings and hanging bell-like drops. Motifs include lotus flowers, peacocks, paisley mango shapes, leaves, elephants, filigree and temple-like architecture. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
