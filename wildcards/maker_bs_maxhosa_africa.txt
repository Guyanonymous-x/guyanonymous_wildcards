Styled in a design language of bold beadwork patterns turned into jacquard knit, with sharp geometric bands of triangles, diamonds, chevrons, zigzags, stepped blocks and horizontal stripes. Colours are vibrant and contrasting, such as red, orange, mustard, cobalt blue, emerald, turquoise, pink and black against cream or deep neutral grounds, in rich saturated blocks. Materials are chunky mohair, merino wool and soft cotton. Patterns repeat symmetrically like beaded collars. Warm, proud, celebratory, heritage-meets-contemporary mood.
