Styled in a design language of hand-built, charming and deliberately pre-war British craft. Racing green, claret, cream, navy, ivory and polished silver with brass and chrome trim. Materials are aluminium panels over ash wood, quilted leather with contrast stitching, canvas, wire and polished timber. Forms are slim, long, curved and light, with flowing separate swept shapes and rounded ends. Handcrafted, nostalgic, cheerful and quietly proud. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
