In the style of Penhaligons, a cut-crystal or clear glass perfume bottle with a faceted stopper, wrapped in a ribbon and tucked into a heavy cream or deep green box with a gold embossed crest. Packaging includes a hat-box shape, tissue paper, a silken tassel, and a wax-sealed collar. Labels are cream with gold borders in fine engraved Victorian serif, and a small stag head or animal bearing sits above. Stores have dark green panels, polished brass fittings, and wood cabinets with glass. Palette of deep green, ivory, gold and burgundy. The look is Victorian gentleman perfumer, ornate and quirky.
