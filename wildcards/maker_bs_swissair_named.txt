Styled in a design language of rigorous Swiss International Style design built on pure red and white. A bold red field carries a white square-armed cross, and a slanted parallelogram of red cuts diagonally across white surfaces as a crisp shape. Finishes are glossy enamel and flat printed colour with sharp edges, set against bare white with generous empty space. A secondary palette of dark grey. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
