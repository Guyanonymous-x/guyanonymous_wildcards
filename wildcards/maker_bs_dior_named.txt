Styled in a design language of parisian couture refinement with soft feminine grace. Silhouettes are structured and sculpted, with nipped waists, rounded curves, full flowing volumes and delicate ribbon bows. Palette is dove grey, pearl white, blush pink, navy, black and touches of gold. Packaging uses grey boxes, ribbons and gold-sparkled prints. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
