Styled in a design language of witty, stylised and slightly gritty retro-futurist design with a limited palette of muted orange and cyan accents. Clean industrial spaces of white tile and panels with orange hazard markings. Orange-yellow warning graphics and clear, functional signs. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
