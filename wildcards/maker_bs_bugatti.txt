In the style of Bugatti in the 1930s, a sensuous coachbuilt sports car dominated by a tall horseshoe-shaped radiator grille in polished nickel, with a small red oval badge with white lettering. Long bonnet with fine louvres, flowing swept wings over the wheels, a boat-tail or teardrop rear, and a steeply raked windscreen. Aluminium or cast alloy wheels with eight spokes, large round headlamps, and a pointed or rounded tail. Paint in French racing blue, black over red, deep cream, and two-tone shades with a polished metal spine down the middle of the roof. Interior has soft leather seats in red or brown, engine-turned aluminium dash with small round dials, and a thin wood steering wheel. Mood is artistic, elegant and aristocratic.
