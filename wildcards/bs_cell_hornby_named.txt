__bs_subject_media__ styled in a design language of flat printed metal and card panels in tomato red, teal green, cream and deep blue with fine gold lining, tiny printed rivets and painted tile and masonry patterns. Glossy lacquer, polished brass details and slight enamel crazing give honest age. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
