Styled in a design language of restrained Parisian elegance built on black and white. Strict monochrome of black, ivory and cream with gold, pearls and chains as the only sparkle, and a quiet touch of beige or pale pink. Forms are simple, boxy, balanced and tailored, with quilted diamond surfaces, braided trim, tweed boucle texture and interlocked double-C style marks. Nothing fussy, minimal ornament chosen with precision, luxurious soft materials and crisp craft. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
