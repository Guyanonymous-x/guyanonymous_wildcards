__bs_subject_fashion__ styled in a design language of fine, silky, lightweight printed cotton with dense small-scale floral prints of tiny ditsy blooms, paisley, peacock feathers, vines and stylised Arts and Crafts leaves. Jewel tones of plum, teal, mustard, rose, sage and deep blue on cream or dark grounds. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
