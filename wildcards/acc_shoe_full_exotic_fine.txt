They stand on a 1.5-inch flatform of lacquered wood above a cushioned phylon sole. It closes with an antique-brass zipper with a leather pull on the outer side. Whimsical touches include dangling acorn-shaped toggles and a heel counter studded with tiny brass rivets spelling a secret word. They are finished with a tiny engraved metal plaque on the heel. It is fully lined in supple calfskin with a hand-stamped insole. Boutique window display, the shoes on a mirrored shelf, warm lighting.
