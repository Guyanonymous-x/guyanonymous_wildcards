Styled in a design language of Soviet utilitarian, solid, no-nonsense. Large condensed Cyrillic capitals painted boldly. Drab greens and greys, patterned curtains and thick velour. Palette is silver, light blue, red and olive green. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
