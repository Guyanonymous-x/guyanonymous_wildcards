the bus at the peak of its design type
with a crew of two and a layout that only worked because of them, an engine in a half-cab ahead of the front axle so the driver sits in a sealed cubicle alone with no contact with the passengers at all
an open rear platform with no door so passengers boarded and left at will, including while moving, which is the arrangement everybody who remembers it remembers fondly and which was abolished on safety grounds
a central pole on the platform
a staircase curving to a top deck
a conductor who collected fares
rang the bell
helped with prams and ran the vehicle socially as well as commercially
a bell cord along the ceiling
moquette double seats
chrome grab rails
ashtrays upstairs only because the lower deck was non-smoking
a cigarette machine
a destination blind wound by hand from the platform, and no heating worth mentioning on either deck
