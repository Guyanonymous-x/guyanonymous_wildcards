Mochi, which are small soft round rice cakes dusted in white starch
Flan, which is a golden custard with a glossy caramel top running down the sides
Churros, which are long ridged golden fried pastries dusted with sugar
Ice cream scoops, which are rounded scoops in a bowl with a slightly melting surface
