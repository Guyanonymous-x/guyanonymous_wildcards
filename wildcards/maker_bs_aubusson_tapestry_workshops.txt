In the style of Aubusson Tapestry Workshops, flat-woven wool and silk tapestries and rugs with soft, pastel-leaning colour. The palette uses faded rose pink, powder blue, sage green, cream, pale gold and muted terracotta. Designs include verdure scenes of leafy forests with birds, large acanthus leaves, floral bouquets, pastoral figures and romantic medallions framed by woven ribbons, garlands and scrolling cartouches. The weave is flat with no pile and a slightly chalky, dusty, antique hand. Compositions feel airy, graceful, curved and symmetrical, in a Rococo and Neoclassical manner, with delicate cream borders.
