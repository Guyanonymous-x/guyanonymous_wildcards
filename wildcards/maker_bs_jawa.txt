In the style of Jawa, Czechoslovak motorcycles. The bike has a compact two-stroke engine with finned cylinder and a rounded engine casing in silver, a teardrop fuel tank with a chrome knee pad side panel and a bold enamel badge, a flat bench seat, a swept-back handlebar, a round chrome headlamp, and thin tubular steel frame. Paint is two-tone glossy enamel, such as maroon and cream, black and red, deep blue and white, or olive and cream with fine hand-painted gold pinstripes on the tank. Twin chrome exhaust pipes flare slightly at the end like tapered trumpets. Mudguards are deep and curved, wheels are wire-spoked with chrome rims and a small chromed hub brake. Electrics are in a round chrome-ringed headlight with a bulb and a small instrument pod. The look is solid, simple, mid-century and slightly sporty, with plenty of chrome and shine.
