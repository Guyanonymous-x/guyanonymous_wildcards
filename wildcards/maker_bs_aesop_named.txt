Styled in a design language of literate, apothecary-inspired restraint with quiet luxury. Rich amber-brown glass and matte brown surfaces with plain cream or off-white paper labels, set in neat black small type with long, tightly spaced lines of detail. Palette is amber brown, cream, black, olive green, terracotta and muted stone, with matte black and brushed brass trim. Wrapping is plain cardboard in earthy tones with a single line of black type. Tactile, thoughtful, minimal, sensory and understated. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
