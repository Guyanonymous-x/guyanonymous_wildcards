In the style of Antonov, Ukrainian aircraft design. Aircraft are rugged, utilitarian and enormous, with a high wing set on a deep boxy fuselage, a glazed navigator nose with a downward-looking bulge, an upswept rear cargo ramp, and multiple small wheel bogies in a long row under bulged fairings on the belly. The large transports have double or twin tail fins, four wide engines under the wings, and a giant slab body painted in simple colours, matte military grey or white with a blue and yellow cheatline, a thin red or blue stripe and plain block lettering in Cyrillic and Latin script. A small classic biplane has fabric-covered wings with struts and wires, and a big radial engine in a rounded nose cowl, painted silver, green or orange. The look is heavy, honest, workmanlike and spectacular in scale.
