Styled in a design language of video rental stores in cobalt blue and bright yellow, with big blue ticket-shaped awnings, shelves of boxes with colourful cases, popcorn-yellow trim, floor-to-ceiling rows of films and a bright, orderly layout lit by strip lighting. Blue and yellow plastic baskets and a long counter in speckled laminate. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
