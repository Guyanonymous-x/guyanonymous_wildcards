In the style of Lancia in the 1950s to 1980s, a refined, finely detailed Italian saloon or coupe with a narrow shield-shaped chrome grille on the nose, a flag badge with a lance, slim headlamps, and tidy proportions. Delicate slim pillars, a large glasshouse, thin chrome trim along the flanks, a slightly tapered tail, and small bumpers. Wheels are pretty alloy or steel with chrome trim rings. Rally models have bold boxy flared arches, a white or grey roof, and wide stripes in blue and red. Paint in pale grey, midnight blue, cream, ivory, and rich burgundy. Interior has rich cloth in wool or velour, wood veneer, a neat dash with round dials, and a wide thin wheel. Mood is understated, intellectual and gently sporty.
