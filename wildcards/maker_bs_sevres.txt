In the style of Sevres, French soft-paste and hard-paste porcelain with intensely coloured ground glazes in deep royal blue, turquoise, apple green and rose pink, framed by gold gilding and reserved panels painted with birds, flowers, pastoral lovers and cherubs. Rococo vase and tureen shapes with scrolled handles and ormolu mounts, white biscuit porcelain figures. Opulent, ornate, gleaming gold, refined courtly Versailles taste.
