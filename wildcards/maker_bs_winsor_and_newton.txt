In the style of Winsor and Newton, artists colours in small metal tubes and wooden boxes with a classic, scholarly look. Tubes are silver or coloured foil with black or white lettering, a cream label and a bold red or black band, with the colour name and swatch. Mahogany or black japanned tin watercolour boxes hold rows of half pans in brilliant pigment, with a lift-out tray and a brass catch. Brushes have long, tapered wooden handles in red, black or natural, with a gleaming crimped ferrule. Designs use dense serif type and ornate Victorian borders, giving a studious and traditional feeling.
