on a gray English day, a low even sheet of pale cloud covering the whole sky, soft flat light with hardly a shadow, the grass of the mounds dull green and beaded with fine drizzle, the bracken dark rust, the pines at the field edge almost black and the river beyond a strip of dull pewter
at sunset, the mounds dark, the low red sun sinking behind the trees across the river and turning the sky orange, pink and pale green, the rounded mounds standing as smooth black humps along the skyline, their long shadows reaching over the grass and a last copper gleam on the distant water
in summer, the heather purple, wide drifts of heather in full bloom spreading rose and violet between the green mounds, the bracken tall and fresh, gorse bushes dotted with yellow flowers, small white clouds in a warm blue sky, butterflies over the grass and the turf on the mound tops dried to pale gold
