In the style of Centro de Textiles Tradicionales del Cusco, rich, rustic, rhythmic and symmetrical hand-woven textiles in natural dyed wool. Long narrow bands and square cloths in slightly fuzzy hand-spun yarn. The tight weave shows crisp horizontal stripes of plain colour alternating with dense patterned bands of diamonds, zigzag lines, small stepped figures, birds, animals, flowers inside diamonds, meandering lines and tiny geometric motifs, reversible on both sides. Colours are earthy plant-dye shades, deep indigo and sky blues, cochineal reds, pinks and purples, yellow-greens, mustard, walnut brown, cream and charcoal. Edges are finished with braided cords and tassels. A precise ancient textile language in warm natural tones. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Centro de Textiles Tradicionales del Cusco in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
