Styled in a design language of friendly, sweet, nostalgic seaside design in a trustworthy clean cream and blue palette. Soft rounded rectangular shapes, shiny foil, tin and maritime references such as a seaside town and small gulls. Round, glossy dark chocolate or white icing over soft pale layers. Warm wood-and-brass spaces, with dark wood, tall windows, beige marble and lamps with amber light. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
