Styled in a design language of nostalgic, refined, slightly aristocratic nineteenth-century apothecary design. Simple old-fashioned materials such as glass, cork, frosted surfaces, tin and plain paper. Tiny repeating floral and botanical prints and sometimes a small crowned crest. Dark polished wood, marble and tiled black and white or terracotta. Muted pastel and old-gold accents with restrained. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
