Styled in a design language of Danish cabinetmaker craft in solid oak, teak and walnut with soaked, oiled or soaped finishes that show fine grain. Shapes include curved wishbone-like members with steam-bent Y-shaped rails, hand-woven natural paper cord, tapered rounded legs, floating sculptural arms and round-edged surfaces. Pegged joints and visible joinery are celebrated. Palette of honey, pale blond, soft brown and cream leather. Calm, tactile, handcrafted, minimalist Scandinavian warmth. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
