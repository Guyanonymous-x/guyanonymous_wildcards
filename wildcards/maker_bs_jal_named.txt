In the style of JAL, minimal, refined, precise Japanese design in one vivid vermilion red with white and charcoal. A solid red circle, like the rising sun, holds a white stylised crane drawn with a single sweeping curved neck and few feather strokes, a flat emblem of confident simplicity. Lettering is bold, rounded, upright sans-serif capitals in red, grey or black, with wide stretched proportions and perfect spacing. Colour blocking is a clean white ground with one strong red shape, sometimes a dark grey band beneath it. Finishes are glossy lacquer and flat matte print with very crisp edges. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words JAL in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
