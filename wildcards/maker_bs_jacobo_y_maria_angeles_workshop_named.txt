Styled in a design language of joyful, hallucinatory, highly saturated carved and painted folk art. Light soft wood is hand-carved into lively mythical creatures, often with a human face or braided hair on an animal body, caught mid-action while flying, scratching or leaping. Eyes are large and glossy. Light satin finish, with every surface patterned and nothing left blank. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
