stone, grass and lead, in three materials and almost nothing else, ashlar and moulded stone in whatever the house quarried, which in practice means the cloister and the church are the same color and everything else on the site is rubble
forty-four bays of tracery reading as a stone screen rather than as windows
gray-buff flags underfoot with forty-eight darker ledger slabs let into them
which is the largest single area of one color anywhere in this document and is what the whole composition is arranged around
gray lead in four rainwater heads, four downpipes and a trough lining
dull brass on eleven spouts
dark oak in eleven carrels and a cupboard's lost doors
and no paint, no plaster, no textile and no color of any kind beyond the grass and the sky over it
