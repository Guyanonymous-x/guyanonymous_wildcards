Styled in a design language of bright, cheerful, playful German retro fun. Palette is glossy primary red, sunshine yellow, orange, lime green and clear sky blue, with metallic gold foil and deep black outlines for contrast. Materials are translucent jelly-like gloss, crinkly shiny metallic film and clear windows showing a jumble of small chunky shapes, soft, smooth and slightly sugar-dusted. Forms are small, plump, rounded and simple. Colour does the shouting, and the whole language feels sunny, sweet, nostalgic and instantly readable. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
