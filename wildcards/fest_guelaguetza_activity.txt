Women of the Flor de Piña dance step and turn in rows, each holding a whole pineapple on her shoulder
Men of the Danza de la Pluma whirl and leap, huge round feathered crowns on their heads swaying
Dancers throw oranges, bread rolls, small baskets and straw hats into the reaching crowd
A brass band marches with trumpets and tubas at the head of the street parade
Women in long skirts swirl the hems in both hands as they turn in pairs with their partners
Couples dance a slow jarabe, the man stamping his boots, the woman swinging her skirt, around a hat on the floor
A woman carries a basket of flowers on her head through the parade, one hand steadying it
Dancers in devil masks of carved wood with horns lunge at the crowd during a street dance
A woman pours mezcal from a clay jug into small cups for the dancers
