Styled in a design language of bold flat mid-century Scandinavian-inspired pattern design. Motifs are a stylised stem of oval leaves, rounded multi-petal flowers, acorns, circular blooms and overlapping ovals, repeated in neat grids, diagonal lattices and tessellated layers. Colour is warm and retro, with mustard yellow, burnt orange, olive green, chocolate brown, teal, navy, powder blue, pink and cream, often with a pop of lime or red. Shapes are simple, geometric and softly rounded, with crisp edges and no shading. Surfaces include laminated coated fabric, glossy ceramic glaze, printed cotton, embossed leather and wipe-clean prints, with tan leather trim. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
