In the style of Lincoln, understated, sober, expensive American flagship luxury of the 1960s. Slab-sided, perfectly flat surfaces, a thin knife-edge horizontal line, restrained fine horizontal bars, long level tops and a square formal outline. Minimal chrome in a thin shoulder strip, slim vertical forms and a star mark in a plain rectangle. Flat panels with brushed metal and deep leather create grave dignity. Black, midnight blue, silver and white. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
