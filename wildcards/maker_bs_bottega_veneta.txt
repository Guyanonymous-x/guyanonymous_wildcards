In the style of Bottega Veneta, intrecciato woven leather made of strips of soft nappa interlaced in a tight basketweave grid, in tones of butter yellow, parakeet green, burnt orange, taupe, chocolate and black. Pouch clutches with a gold knot closure, bags with no external logos, supple slouchy shape, thick wide strips, minimal hardware. Understated, tactile, quiet craftsmanship.
