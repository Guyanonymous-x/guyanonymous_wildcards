Styled in a design language of heritage travel luxury built on repeating pattern. Edges are bound in natural leather that ages to honey, with polished brass studs, clasps, locks and rolled handles, and mustard stitching. Packaging is warm saffron orange boxes with brown trim and navy ribbon. Playful bright reinterpretations sit beside classic ones. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
