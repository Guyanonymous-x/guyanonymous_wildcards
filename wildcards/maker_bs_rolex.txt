Styled in a design language of solid, imposing, polished, status-heavy Swiss luxury. Robust round forms in polished and brushed stainless steel or warm yellow gold, with fluted edges, satin and mirror finishes side by side and a tiny five-pointed crown motif as the recurring emblem. Deep forest green is the signature colour, paired with gold, white, black, blue and cream. Lettering is crisp, upright serif capitals in gold or white, widely spaced. Packaging and graphics use rich green leather-grain, embossed gold crests and heavy lacquered boxes. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
