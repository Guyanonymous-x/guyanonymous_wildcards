at the ch'alla, the sprinkling, each person pours the first few drops of beer or cane spirit from the glass onto the earth for Pachamama before drinking, the drops darkening the dust
at the Pachamama ceremony of the Kolla people of northern Argentina, a wide round hole dug in the earth, ringed with stones and lined with green leaves, is fed in turn by everyone present, who kneel and drop in handfuls of food, coca and drink
at an apacheta, a cairn of stones on a high mountain pass, travelers add a stone and a quid of chewed coca leaves to the heap, giving thanks for a safe crossing
at the ch'alla of a new house on Carnival Tuesday in Bolivia, the owners sprinkle beer and confetti on the walls and the earth and hang colored paper streamers over the doorway
