Buni, the land of the dead, a village of log houses on a riverbank where the dead fish, hunt and keep dogs as they did on earth, men and women in their twenties to nineties
the dead of Buni welcome the new soul with a feast of fish and millet, their kin in their forties to eighties in fish-skin robes coming out of the log houses to greet her
in Buni the light is dim and the seasons are turned around, the dead going about their fishing on a gray river under a pale sun
