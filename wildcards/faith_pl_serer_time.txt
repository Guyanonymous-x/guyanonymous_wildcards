in the dry season, the savanna gold, the grass burned dry to straw yellow, the fields harvested to pale stubble on sandy ground, the baobabs bare and gray, and a hard white sun in a dusty pale blue sky
in the rainy season, the fields green, young millet and peanut plants in fresh bright rows, new grass covering the sand, pools of brown rainwater shining along the tracks and towers of gray and white cloud building in the sky
at sunset, the baobabs dark against an orange sky, their fat trunks and crooked limbs turned to black shapes, the sun a red ball sinking into the haze on the flat horizon, and a few birds crossing the glow
