In the style of Edsel, overwrought, odd and unforgettable American design. A tall vertical horse-collar shape set in the middle of a wide bar, wingtip boomerang forms, side sculpting with a bright spear and a cut-away dip along the flank. Two-tone pink and black, turquoise and white, yellow and brown. Open pillarless transparency, chrome rims with whitewall roundness and a rolling dome of graphics add oddness. Ambitious, fussy and strange. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Edsel in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
