In the style of Rotring, precision technical drawing pens and mechanical pencils with a slim, hexagonal or round metal barrel in matte black or silver, with a knurled grip and a red ring around the top. The shape is industrial and tool-like, with a long conical metal nose, a retractable sleeve, a clip and tiny printed line-width numbers in white on the barrel. Colours are black, brushed chrome and a vivid red accent. Technical pens use colour-coded end caps and ink cartridges. Finishes are crisp and engineered, and the mood is draughtsman, architect studio and exacting accuracy.
In the style of Pelikan, piston-filling fountain pens with a plump cigar-shaped barrel and cap in celluloid or resin. Classic models have a black cap, a translucent ink window and a green striped, tortoiseshell or black body with gold-coloured trim bands. The cap clip is a slender arrow or a bird-shaped tail, and a gold nib bears fine engraved scrollwork. Later versions appear in bright blue, red and white stripes, along with a rounded, thick body. Inks and boxes use deep green and white, with a pelican and chicks emblem. The look is tidy, German, sturdy and luminous.
In the style of Montblanc, massive cigar-shaped fountain pens in polished black precious resin with gold-plated rings, a clip and a large two-tone gold nib. The cap is topped with a white six-pointed star shaped like a snow-capped peak, rounded with a domed shape. Barrels are glossy and deep black, with thin gold rings and engraved numbers. Special editions use striped, marbled or burgundy resin. Cases are black leather with a gold emblem, and notebooks and cufflinks follow the same dark and gold luxury. The mood is stately, confident, executive and understated.
In the style of Parker Pens, streamlined fountain pens and ballpoints with a cylindrical, slightly tapered body. Barrels come in glossy burgundy, blue, black or grey with a fine herringbone, vertical grooves or smooth finish. Caps are brushed stainless steel or gold-filled, with a ribbed texture, and a distinctive arrow-shaped clip that stands proud from the cap. A hooded nib is covered by a smooth tapered shield. Trim is thin chrome or gold bands. The mood is mid-century post-war optimism, with sleek, bullet-like forms and good engineering.
In the style of Waterman Pens, elegant fountain pens with slim, rounded barrels and caps in black hard rubber, red ripple pattern, or sterling silver filigree overlay. Ripple designs swirl in mottled red and black, and metal overlays show Art Deco geometric grooves, basketweave and lacy filigree. Pens have thin gold-filled bands, a lever filler on the side, a gently arched clip and a flexible gold nib with fine ornament. Colours are warm, rich and aged, with an orange-red sheen. Boxes are lined with velvet. The mood is refined, Edwardian and Art Deco.
In the style of Sheaffer Pens, bulbous, torpedo-shaped fountain pens in glossy striped celluloid colours, such as pearl and jade green, marine black, grey, burgundy and blue. The cap has a thin white-dot emblem on top and a slim chrome or gold trim ring. A distinctive inlaid white dot sits at the top of the cap, and a long nib is gold with a triangular or hooded shape. Clips are straight, flat and sleek, with a spear or tapering form. Barrels are smooth, rounded, streamlined and glossy, in an Art Deco and early jet-age manner.
In the style of Pilot Namiki Maki-e Pens, slim lacquered fountain pens with a glossy black or red urushi ground decorated with delicate gold, silver and coloured powder. Designs show tiny cranes, dragonflies, bamboo, plum blossom, waves and mountains in raised, sparkling gold with soft gradations, often covering the whole barrel and cap. The metal nib is gold, with fine engraved scrolls, and fittings are gold or rhodium. The forms are smooth and elegant, with rounded ends. The pen comes in a box of paulownia wood. The mood is quiet luxury, patient craft and nature-inspired.
In the style of Lamy, modernist pens with a simple, functional design in matte black plastic and brushed stainless steel. Barrels are straight cylinders with a faceted triangular grip section, and a thin sprung wire clip extends along the cap as a single flat bar. Surfaces are matte and fine-grained, with only small dots or ink windows. Colours are black, charcoal grey, white, and later bright red, yellow and blue. The tip of the nib is silvery steel, clean and plain. Boxes are minimal. The mood is German industrial design, school-like and elegantly austere.
In the style of Cross Pens, slim cylindrical pens and pencils with a clean, classical shape and a smooth finish in chrome, brushed gold-filled or lacquer. The barrel carries a tight diamond-patterned engraving or fine ridged stripes and a straight narrow clip with a tiny ball tip. Rings are thin and the profile is a perfectly even tube with a rounded cap that twists at the top. Colours are silver, gold, black and deep blue. The cases are leather or velvet lined. The mood is business-like, classic, restrained, and subtly formal.
