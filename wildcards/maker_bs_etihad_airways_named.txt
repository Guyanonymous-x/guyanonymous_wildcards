Styled in a design language of opulent, desert-warm, polished and glittering identity in sand and gold. A warm pale beige ground, almost desert sand, with a triangular tessellation in gold and silver facets like a faceted gem, fading toward one side. Fitted dark sand-coloured tailoring with a flowing scarf. Pale marble, golden light and sweeping curved roofs. A limited palette of gold, sand, taupe and white. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
