the Coyolxauhqui stone, a great round flat stone carved with the broken body of the moon goddess, her arms and legs cut apart, bells on her cheeks
a chacmool, a carved stone figure of a man lying on his back, his knees raised and his head turned to the side, holding a bowl on his belly, still painted red, blue and yellow, before the Tlaloc shrine
the Tlaltecuhtli monolith, a huge carved stone slab of the earth goddess crouching, painted in red and ochre
the wall of skulls, a low platform covered on its sides with hundreds of carved stone skulls in rows
