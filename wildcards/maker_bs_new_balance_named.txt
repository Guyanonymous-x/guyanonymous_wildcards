In the style of New Balance, understated, grown-up sporty design with a calm, confident, practical air. Palette is heathered grey as the core, joined by navy, burgundy, forest green, cream and white, with occasional bright teal, volt yellow or coral accents. Textures mix soft suede, fine mesh and matte synthetic in layered overlays with muted tonal blocking. A large capital N emblem in an angled, slashing form is the recurring mark, often in a contrasting colour. Lettering is plain, bold, upright sans-serif capitals. Packaging uses sturdy boxes in red, teal or white with simple tidy type. Nothing is flashy, everything is balanced, refined and comfortable. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words New Balance in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
