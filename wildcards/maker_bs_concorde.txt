Styled in a design language of needle-sharp, futuristic, elegant design in gleaming white. Long narrow forms taper to a point, with a sleek elongated triangle silhouette used as a repeated graphic shape. Surfaces are bright reflective white gloss, polished silver and pale grey metal with a heat-resistant ceramic sheen, kept clear of clutter. A single narrow cobalt or navy cheatline runs the full length of a form, with fine red and blue accent flashes on small panels. Lettering is small, spaced, lowercase or capital sans-serif in dark blue or black. Seamless and streamlined, the palette stays almost entirely white and silver. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
