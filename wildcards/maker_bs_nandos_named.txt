Styled in a design language of warm, bold, casual, spicy and lively. Palette runs through chilli red, terracotta, burnt orange, sunny yellow, teal, mint and soft peach, set in flat blocks. Surfaces carry African-inspired geometric patterns, bold arcs, semicircles, sun discs, zigzags and hand-printed abstract portraits with a screen-print look. Kraft cardboard and matte paper in warm earth tones are cut into angular tabs with colourful striped trims and rough linocut edges. Vibrant, sociable, handmade and fiery. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
