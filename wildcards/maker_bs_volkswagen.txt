In the style of Volkswagen in the 1950s and 1960s, a rounded beetle-shaped car with a domed roof, curved smooth bonnet, round bug-eye headlamps standing up on the wings, and sweeping separate fenders ending in running boards. The body is one smooth continuous curve with a tiny oval rear window, no grille at the front, and slim chrome bumpers. Small steel wheels with chrome hubcaps and a round badge on the nose. Paint in pastel beige, sea blue, coral red, mint green, ivory and cream, plus grey. Interior has a bare painted metal dash with a single round dial, thin two-spoke wheel, cloth or vinyl seats, and exposed painted door panels. Mood is cute, honest, friendly and economical.
