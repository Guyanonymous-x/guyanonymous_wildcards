Vietnamese water puppets, lacquered wooden figures that glide across the surface of a pool. The puppets are carved from light fig wood, lacquered and painted in glossy red, gold, green and black, each about as tall as an adult's forearm, a golden dragon with a long curling body breathing a spray of water, a farmer in a cone-shaped straw hat driving a water buffalo, fishermen with rods and a flock of ducks. __puppet_water_puppets_var__.
