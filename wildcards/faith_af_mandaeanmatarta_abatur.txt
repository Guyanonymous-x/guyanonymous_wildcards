at the top sits Abatur, the old judge, a man in his eighties with a long white beard and a crown of light, before a great pair of scales, the soul seated in one pan and Shitil, the purest of souls, a slim man in his twenties in white, seated in the other
Abatur sits enthroned at the gate between the worlds, his tall scales hanging from a beam before him, and beyond him the bright river of living water where the weighed soul is baptized once more and clothed in a robe of radiance
beyond the scales the World of Light opens, a land of shining white skies, rivers of clear living water and tall date palms made of light, where the souls of the righteous in white robes stand in rows with their arms raised to greet the newcomer
