Styled in a design language of folk, cheerful, playful and slightly naive fantastical animal and plant scenes stitched on cloth. A plain white or cream base, sometimes black, red or blue, filled edge to edge with flat. Motifs are large stylised birds, deer, rabbits, horses, lizards, butterflies and fish, mixed with lush curling flowers, leaves, trees and tiny dots, arranged symmetrically or in mirrored pairs around a central tree. Colours are saturated and not realistic, with sunny yellow, grass green, cobalt blue, hot pink, orange, red and purple. Dense, tight stitching with thick coloured thread gives a slightly raised, filled-in look with bold dark outlines. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
