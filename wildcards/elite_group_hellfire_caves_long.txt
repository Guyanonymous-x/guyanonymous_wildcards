the Hellfire Club of the 1750s, a club of English aristocrats who met in hand-dug chalk caves and an old abbey ruin, as the old accounts and rumors describe it. Long hand-cut tunnels of white chalk lead deep under a hill, opening into a round chamber where men in monks' robes sit at a long table with wine and candles. An underground stream runs through the tunnels, crossed by a little bridge, called by the club the River Styx, and beyond it lies the Inner Temple, a round chamber under a domed chalk roof. Niches in the chalk walls hold small statues of classical gods and goddesses, and the walls are smoke-stained above candle sconces.
