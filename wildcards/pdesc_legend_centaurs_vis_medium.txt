the centaurs, the horse people of Greek myth, beings with a muscular human upper body joined at the waist to the body and four legs of a horse, bearded faces and wild curly hair, wearing a pelt of a panther thrown over one shoulder, otherwise bare-chested, their human chests broad and bearded faces wild, their horse bodies glossy brown or dappled grey, bows and quivers on their backs, a forest edge of tall pines behind them
