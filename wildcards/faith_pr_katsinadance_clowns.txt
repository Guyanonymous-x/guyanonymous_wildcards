between the lines dance the Koyemsi, the mudhead clowns, men in their twenties to fifties painted pink-brown with mud, in round mud-colored cloth masks with knobs on the top and round open eyes and mouths
striped clowns, men in their twenties to forties painted with black and white bands from the face to the feet, two horns of cornhusk on their heads, tumble and play in the plaza, teasing the crowd
a line of women's katsinas, danced by men in their thirties to fifties in white blanket shawls and masks with whorls of hair, kneel and scrape notched sticks laid on gourds
