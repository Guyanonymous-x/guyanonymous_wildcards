Styled in a design language of small, portable, clean and balanced forms with crisp roman lettering and open round letters, and a slanted, narrow italic with joined strokes. A tiny emblem of a dolphin coiled around an anchor appears in black ink on cream laid paper. Surfaces are soft vellum or brown leather with blind tooling and gilt edges. The look is elegant, humanist, scholarly and compact, with quiet, evenly inked colour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
