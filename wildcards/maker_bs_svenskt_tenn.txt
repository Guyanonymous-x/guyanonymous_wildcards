Styled in a design language of cheerful, bright, cultured, deliberately casual Swedish interiors. Large-scale botanical and fantastical prints on white or cream grounds, with detailed lush flowers, leaves, vines, birds, butterflies, maps and exotic fruit in saturated, slightly unexpected colours such as teal, mustard, coral, raspberry, tangerine and deep green, drawn with hand-painted freedom. Forms are light, comfortable and curvy, with spindly legs, rounded corners and woven cane, in mahogany or pale birch, with brass details. Pewter, glass and brass objects are plain and sculptural. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
