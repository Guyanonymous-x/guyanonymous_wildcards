Styled in a design language of cheerful, nostalgic, boutique-fashionable retro. Curvaceous rounded rectangles in glossy pastel enamel such as cream, pastel blue, pink, mint green and red, with polished chrome trim, soft sheen and generously rounded corners. Thick softly rounded metal edges recall 1950s American automobile styling. Surfaces are shiny and seamless, with small chrome accents and matching colour from edge to edge. Black glass panels and chrome edging provide crisp contrast. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
