Irkalla lies deep under the earth as a city of seven walls of dark mud brick, one inside another, dust lying thick on every roof, door and bolt
the house of darkness stands in a cavern, a great hall of dark brick with a throne at its far end, the floor covered in gray dust and the walls bare dull brown brick
the city of the dead spreads under the mountains, rows of flat-roofed brick houses in gloom, the narrow streets filled with dust and the dead sitting in the doorways
