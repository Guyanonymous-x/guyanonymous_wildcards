In the style of Cowichan Coast Salish hand-knitted sweaters from Vancouver Island in Canada. The garment is a thick, bulky, hip-length cardigan or pullover in chunky hand-spun undyed sheep wool, with a shawl collar, full front opening with large buttons or a zipper, and rounded set-in sleeves. The wool is slightly oily, with a rustic hairy texture and a sturdy feel. The colour palette is natural cream, charcoal, grey, brown and black, sometimes with a small amount of red or a bright accent. The central design is a broad yoke or back panel in intarsia-type knitting depicting a large bird such as an eagle or thunderbird, a deer, a whale, a wolf, a bear head or a geometric band of triangles and diamonds, repeated in horizontal stripes across the chest and around the cuffs. Edges have a ribbed band in a plain colour. The look is cosy, outdoorsy, woodsy and cabin-like, with the heavy weight of waterproof wool.
