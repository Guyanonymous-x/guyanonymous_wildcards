Styled in a design language of witty, stylised and slightly gritty retro-futurist design with a limited palette of muted orange and cyan accents. Clean industrial spaces of white tile and panels with orange hazard markings. Bold, caricatured character, and simple circular or symbol-based marks in orange. Orange-yellow warning graphics and clear. Shapes are chunky and confident, surfaces slightly dirty, and the mood is dry-humoured, clever and sinister, with a mid-century science-fiction sensibility. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
