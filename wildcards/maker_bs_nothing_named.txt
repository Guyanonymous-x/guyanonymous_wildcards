In the style of Nothing, stripped-back, nerdy, futuristic and quirky transparent tech. Clear see-through plastic and glass reveal internal components, circuit boards, screws and coils. Palette is white, black and transparent with a signature bright red dot accent. Glowing white light strips arranged in segments around key features. Typography is dot-matrix lettering with round dots forming monochrome numerals, and icons are pixelated. Edges are flat and squared with soft corners. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
