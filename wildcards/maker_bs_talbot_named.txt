Styled in a design language of British and French cars of the 1930s and 1980s, from elegant touring cars with long bonnets and louvred hoods to boxy hatchbacks with plastic bumpers and blue, red and yellow stripes. Simple dashboards with big square switches. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
