Shot through a standard 720nm infrared filter and shown straight from the camera, the image shows an almost monochrome image with a faint cool gray-blue cast, the skin milky and smooth, the veins faint, and the hair a shade lighter than in life. Infrared light passes a few millimetres into the skin, so skin shows bright, smooth and milky, freckles and blemishes fade, surface veins show as fine dark lines because blood absorbs infrared, the lips and nipples render pale like the skin, the irises darken and the whites of the eyes turn grayish, and tattoo ink stands out dark.
