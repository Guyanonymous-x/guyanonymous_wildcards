Styled in a design language of earthy, artisanal, modern-heritage handloom textiles. Loose, easy silhouettes in crisp matte cotton with a visible hand woven grain. Palette is mustard, indigo, rust, olive, ivory and deep red. Patterns are bold block-print-inspired motifs, large paisleys, florals, small repeating geometry and interlocking designs in two or three colours, set along edges and borders. Neat threadwork trims every edge, with small mirrors, tassels and long fringed ends. Warm, cheerful and sunlit, as in an open courtyard. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
