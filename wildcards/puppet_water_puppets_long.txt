Vietnamese water puppets, lacquered wooden figures that glide across the surface of a pool. The puppets are carved from light fig wood, lacquered and painted in glossy red, gold, green and black, each about as tall as an adult's forearm, a golden dragon with a long curling body breathing a spray of water, a farmer in a cone-shaped straw hat driving a water buffalo, fishermen with rods and a flock of ducks. The puppeteers stand waist deep in the water hidden behind a bamboo screen, working the puppets on long poles and strings that run under the surface, so the figures seem to move across the water by themselves. The water is murky green and the figures leave small ripples behind them. __puppet_water_puppets_var__.
