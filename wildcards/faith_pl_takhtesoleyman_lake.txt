the spring lake, a perfectly round pool of clear water so deep it shows a dark sapphire blue at its center and turquoise at its edges, its surface still as glass and holding the reflection of the sky and the ruined walls
the edge of the lake, a low rim of pale stone crusted white and cream with mineral deposits left by the spring water, small channels cut in the stone carrying water away down the hill
the far shore of the lake, the clear blue water reaching to a low bank of pale stone, the brick ruins standing just beyond it
