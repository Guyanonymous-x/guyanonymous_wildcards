In the style of Jaguar in the 1960s, a long low sensuous sports car with a very long sweeping bonnet, tight cabin set far back, oval open-mouth grille, and fared-in headlamps under smooth glass or sloping wings. Flowing curved fenders, tapering fastback tail, delicate chrome bumpers, slim pillars, and wire-spoke wheels with knock-off spinners. A leaping cat mascot or growling cat face badge sits on the nose. Paint in British racing green, signal red, cream, metallic silver blue and opalescent grey. Interior has bucket seats in red or tan leather, polished wooden dashboard, a row of toggle switches and large chrome-ringed white-faced dials, and a slim wood-rimmed steering wheel. Mood is elegant speed, curvaceous and feline.
