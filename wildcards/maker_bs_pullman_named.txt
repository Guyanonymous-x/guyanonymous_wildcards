In the style of Pullman, formal Gilded Age elegance in luxury travel of the early 1900s. Deep dark green paint with elaborate gold-leaf lettering and lines, arched forms, small high openings and open railings. Walnut with green or burgundy plush upholstery, brass fittings and gaslight-style lamps. Heavy, solid, stately forms. Ornate, rich, ceremonial, hand-gilded and imposing, like a rolling grand hotel.
