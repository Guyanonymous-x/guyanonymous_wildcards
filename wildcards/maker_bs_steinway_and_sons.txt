Styled in a design language of formal grandeur in black and gold. A mirror-bright black lacquer finish has deep, flawless reflections and sweeping, flowing curves. Alternate finishes include dark mahogany, burl walnut, ebony and ivory-white with warm satin sheen. Details use polished brass, gilded cast ornament and tapered, turned supports with subtle carving. Palette is black, gold, ivory and rich dark wood. Restrained, noble, symmetrical and timeless, with a refined, expensive hush. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
