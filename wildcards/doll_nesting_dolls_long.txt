a set of Russian nesting dolls, matryoshka, wooden dolls that fit one inside another. The largest doll is a smooth round-bodied figure of turned wood about as tall as an adult's hand, painted as a woman in a headscarf, and each one splits at the waist to hold the next smaller one, down to a tiny solid doll the size of a fingertip. They stand in a row from largest to smallest. Each is painted with a round face with rosy cheeks, blue eyes and a small red mouth, framed by a scarf painted with flowers, and a painted apron covers the front of the body with bold folk flowers, roses and leaves in red, gold and green, all varnished to a high shine. __doll_nesting_dolls_var__.
