Styled in a design language of flat, symmetrical, uniform and instantly legible. A bold red upper field meets a clean white lower field with a thin gold line between them. Colour is applied in broad flat blocks with no gradients, in red, white and gold, with occasional pastel or deep green variations of the same layout. Finish is satin printed metal and smooth coated paper, with identical elements repeated in tidy grids. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
