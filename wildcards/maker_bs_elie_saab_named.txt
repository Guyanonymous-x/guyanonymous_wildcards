Styled in a design language of romantic, feminine red-carpet couture, with long sheer columns and full skirts over fitted upper lines, plunging necklines, illusion lace, delicate floral applique and beading in gold, silver and crystal. Fabrics are tulle, chiffon, lace, silk crepe and satin, in soft powder pink, blush, ivory, champagne, lilac, sky blue, emerald and rich red. Details include embroidered lace sleeves, sweeping trains, scattered flower petals, cinched waists and sparkling areas that fade into transparent layers. Dreamy, luxurious, fairytale mood, in soft glowing light. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
