Styled in a design language of slim cylindrical forms with a clean, classical shape and a smooth finish in chrome, brushed gold-filled or lacquer. Surfaces carry a tight diamond-patterned engraving or fine ridged stripes, with a straight narrow accent ending in a tiny ball. Rings are thin and the profile is a perfectly even tube with a rounded top. Colours are silver, gold, black and deep blue. Leather or velvet linings add softness. The mood is business-like, classic, restrained and subtly formal.
