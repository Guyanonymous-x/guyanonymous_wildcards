In the style of Fendi, Roman luxury, witty, tactile and richly crafted. The core pattern is a repeating monogram of two mirrored capital letters, shown in brown on tan, black and white, or bright colours, and used on wallpaper-like grids, ribbons and bold single-motif graphics. Materials are plush fur, fine leather, woven strips, marquetry-like patchwork and smooth lacquer, with sleek clean shapes in camel, tan, chocolate, black, cream and sunny golden yellow. Playful touches include candy-coloured trims, leopard spots and cartoon-eyed charms. Lettering is a heavy, tightly stacked sans-serif in capitals. Packaging is mustard yellow boxes with black edging, and sleek black gift cartons. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Fendi in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
