In the style of Lea & Perrins, a squat rectangular glass bottle with a short neck, wrapped in a cream-white paper wrapper printed in black with ornate Victorian serif lettering, an orange-ochre label, and a small coat-of-arms-like crest. The wrapper is folded over the bottle with a paper seal at the top and a black cap. Dark brown sauce, flecked and thin. Typography mixes decorative Victorian slab letters and fine engraved text in an orange, black and cream palette. The look is old English pantry heritage, with a fussy, crowded label and a reassuringly aged feel.
