In the style of Petrof, solid, dignified, classical and carefully made. Deep, glossy black polished forms with smooth curved sides, plain tall planes, rounded cheeks and thick tapered legs finished in brass. Warm walnut, mahogany or white lacquer with matte or satin sheen are alternatives. The name appears in gold letters or on a small brass plate. Ivory-white with ebony black contrast, and brass accents. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
