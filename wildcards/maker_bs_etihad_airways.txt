In the style of Etihad Airways, a Gulf airline identity in sand and gold. Aircraft have a warm pale beige fuselage, almost the colour of desert sand, and on the tail and rear body a triangular tessellation in gold and silver facets, like a faceted gem, fading to the front. The name is set in a golden calligraphic logo mark above the windows, with a small national emblem and a flag near the door. Cabins are sumptuous, with beige and brown leather, gold trim, wood veneer and sliding doors, and a three room residence with a living room, bedroom and shower. Crew wear fitted dark sand-coloured suits and a hat with a flowing scarf. Terminals use pale marble, golden light and sweeping curved roofs. Mood is opulent, desert-warm, polished and glittering, with a limited palette of gold, sand, taupe and white.
