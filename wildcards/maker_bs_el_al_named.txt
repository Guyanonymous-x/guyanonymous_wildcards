Styled in a design language of crisp, clean, secure, patriotic and cool identity in blue and white. White grounds with a sweeping blue line outlined in silver, curving and fading along the form, with plain white or blue areas and a flag. An older scheme uses turquoise topped with a six pointed star and a turquoise and navy line. Sunlit blue sky. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
