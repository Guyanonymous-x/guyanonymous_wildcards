Shot through a hyper color 470nm infrared filter and processed with the red and blue channels swapped, the image shows a pale pistachio-cream sky over magenta-purple foliage, with skin tones looking almost natural. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
