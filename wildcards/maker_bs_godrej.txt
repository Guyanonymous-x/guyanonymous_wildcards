In the style of Godrej, Indian steel almirahs, safes and refrigerators of the mid twentieth century. Cupboards are tall, rectangular, boxy cabinets made of welded sheet steel with heavy double doors, a vertical chrome handle with a key lock, and a full length mirror panel on one door. The paint is a thick baked enamel in grey green, olive, cream, rust brown or pale blue, with a slightly orange peel texture and rounded corners. Interiors have sturdy shelves, a small locker and a hanging rail. Safes are squat, painted dark green or black, with a big round combination dial, brass hinges and gold painted pinstripes. Refrigerators are rounded white boxes with a chrome handle and a small badge in cursive script. The logo is a looping signature wordmark, shown in white, deep red or later purple. Mood is solid, heavy, secure, domestic and trustworthy, like a bedroom wall of a post-independence home.
