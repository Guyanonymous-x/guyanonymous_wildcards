In the style of Trader Joes, a small neighbourhood shop with a Hawaiian shirt aesthetic, wood-panelled walls, hand-lettered chalkboard signs and hand-drawn cartoon fish and ships. Red hand-lettered logo in a cursive-brush type on a white field, and own-label products with brightly coloured packaging and playful, witty copy. Paper bags in kraft brown with a red mark, pastel flowers on shelf, and a tropical bell. Palette of bright red, cream, kraft brown and navy. The look is friendly, quirky, nautical-tiki, with hand-drawn illustration and casual, personal tone.
In the style of Harrods, a grand Edwardian terracotta-fronted department store lined in cream stone with hundreds of electric lights outlining its facade at night, green awnings and gilded signage. Green and gold shopping bags in a thick paper with ribbon handles, a green and gold plaque, and a plain serif wordmark in gold. Interiors of marble, polished brass, deep green carpets and ornate plaster ceilings. The Egyptian-themed escalator hall has gold sphinxes and hieroglyph panels. Palette of racing green, gold, cream and terracotta. The look is opulent, ceremonial, theatrical British luxury.
In the style of Selfridges, a monumental Edwardian stone facade with giant Ionic columns, a central clock and a bronze sculpture over the main entrance, and large display windows with theatrical window scenes. Shopping bags in bold flat yellow with a plain black sans-serif capital wordmark, and plain bright signage. Interiors blend classical stonework with modern white displays. Yellow, black, white and stone palette. The look is theatrical, showy, retail-as-entertainment, with a bold flat yellow paper bag that is instantly recognisable.
In the style of Galeries Lafayette, a central Art Nouveau glass and steel dome in stained-glass colours of gold, green and amber, rising above balconies with curved wrought-iron balustrades in gilded patterns. Tall store facade of cream stone with ornate carving, a gold-lettered sign, and a pale pink or cream paper bag with thin black capital letters. Mosaic floors, sweeping staircases and glass canopies. Palette of gold, cream, emerald and amber. The look is Belle Epoque, domed, ornate, light-filled and grand, with decorative ironwork and coloured glass.
In the style of Tong Ren Tang, a centuries old Beijing traditional medicine house. Shopfronts have a long dark wooden lattice facade with carved eaves, lacquered red pillars and a horizontal black board with large gold brush calligraphy characters, flanked by tall hanging vertical signs and red paper lanterns. Interiors show floor to ceiling rows of small dark wood drawers with brass pulls, each labelled in black ink on white tags, a high counter with a brass scale and abacus, and glass jars of dried roots, fungus and flowers. Products are sold in rectangular cartons or round tins in imperial yellow, vermilion red and gold, with embossed dragon or lotus borders, and individual pills are sealed in round wax shells in brown, red or yellow, stamped with a small seal mark. Labels use traditional characters in calligraphic strokes and red seal stamps. Mood is imperial, scholarly, aromatic, sombre and prosperous, like an old apothecary of lacquer, brass and paper.
In the style of Aarong, a Bangladeshi handicraft and clothing shop. Garments are saris, tunics and kurtas in handwoven cotton, silk and jamdani muslin, in vivid colours such as turmeric yellow, red, indigo, leaf green and white, with block-printed borders, tiny woven floral motifs and fine stripes. The most iconic craft is nakshi kantha, a quilted embroidery of running stitch forming flowers, fish, birds, trees and boats in rows of red, blue and green thread on white or black cloth, with a wavy hand-stitched texture. Homeware includes terracotta pots, jute baskets and rugs, cane stools, brass bells and hand painted wooden toys. Stores are tall and bright with hanging fabrics, rustic wood shelves, earthy walls and a gentle orange accent, and tags are cream paper with simple black type. Mood is rural, handmade, colourful, warm and lovingly textured, like a village fair in a tidy city building.
