In the style of Hindustan Motors, an Indian four-door saloon of rounded 1950s British-inspired shape with a bulbous domed roof, a tall rounded bonnet with a chrome upright grille of vertical bars and a winged hood ornament, separate round chrome headlamps on the front wings, and rounded fenders. Chrome bumpers and thin pillars, a big boot with a curved lid and small round tail lamps. Painted steel wheels with chrome hubcaps. Interior with a wide bench seat, a plain metal dash with a big round speedometer and a thin steering wheel. Colours of ivory white, black, dull grey, taxi yellow and olive green. Stately, dated and sturdy.
