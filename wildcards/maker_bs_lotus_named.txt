In the style of Lotus, lightweight, stripped-back and purposeful British racing minimalism. Thin moulded shells in low wedge or rounded teardrop outlines, with bare edges, wide swelling curves and nothing added that is not needed. Racing green is paired with a single bright yellow band, alongside black with gold pinstriping, white, orange and metallic silver. A small round badge in green and yellow with intertwined initials in a circle acts as the recurring mark. Lettering is clean, wide-spaced capitals, often in gold on black. Surfaces are glossy fibreglass-like sheen, exposed aluminium and thin carbon weave. Colour blocking is simple, sharp and light in spirit. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Lotus in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
