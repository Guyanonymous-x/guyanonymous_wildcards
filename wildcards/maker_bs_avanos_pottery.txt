In the style of Avanos red clay pottery in Cappadocia in Turkey. The clay is dug from the red silt of the river, giving a rich terracotta, brick-red and dark brown body. Pieces are thrown on a foot-powered kick wheel, often by hand, such as wide-bellied amphora, narrow-necked water jugs, pitchers, plates, lidded casseroles, wine jugs and large ornamental platters. Decoration is applied with slip, incised lines and glaze, with spirals, hatched bands, Hittite sun discs, tulip and carnation flowers, stylised birds, geometric borders, zigzags and dots in cream, brown, turquoise, green and cobalt blue. The surface can be unglazed matte, or covered with a glossy lead-free glaze in green or amber, showing cracks and pooled colour. Some jugs are shaped like a face or a bird. The look is warm, earthy, ancient and handmade.
