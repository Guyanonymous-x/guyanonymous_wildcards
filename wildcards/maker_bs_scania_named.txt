Styled in a design language of clean, crisp, modern Nordic engineering with a tall, flat-fronted, upright face. Broad forms crossed by one bold horizontal bar, narrow angular lights and wide, tall glazing. Rounded upper forms, long smooth skirts and tidy lines that flow back along the body. Colour is tidy and solid, in white, red, blue or yellow, with no clutter. Rational, safe, practical and quietly premium. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
