Styled in a design language of busy, nostalgic, neon-lit, crowded city design. Tall, slim, upright boxy bodies with two storeys of tall rectangular windows in slim frames, a slightly curved roof and open balcony fronts upstairs. Lower body in deep forest green with a cream or pale yellow stripe along the window line, and a square box with white characters on black. The most distinctive sight is the full-body advertising wrap in saturated colours, glossy photographs and bold Chinese and English lettering that covers the entire flank, so each object becomes a rolling poster. Interiors have wooden slatted seats, brass handrails and wooden floors.
