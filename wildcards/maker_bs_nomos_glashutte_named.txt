Styled in a design language of spare, precise, calm and modern minimalism. Slim round stainless steel with thin straight edges, a very narrow rim and a flat domed glass over a pure white or silvery-white surface. Plain brown or cordovan leather. Transparent backs reveal a decorated mechanism with stripes and blued screws. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
