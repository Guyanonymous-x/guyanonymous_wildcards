Styled in a design language of simple, sunny, pragmatic small-scale design. Rounded boxy body with rear-heavy proportions, round forms in chrome bezels, thin chrome edging with rubber strips and rounded bubble supports. Hot-weather details include pop-out vents and plain dish discs. Palette of bright yellow, sandy beige, bright orange, green and mustard. Friendly, sturdy, tropical and unpretentious. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
