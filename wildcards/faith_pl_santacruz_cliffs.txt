sea cliffs of dark volcanic rock, sheer walls of black, brown and rust-red lava rising straight out of the water, layered and cracked, streaked white by seabirds, with green succulent plants clinging to the ledges and swells surging and foaming white against their base
a sea cave, Painted Cave, one of the largest sea caves on earth, its mouth huge, a towering arched opening in the dark cliff, many times the height of the small boat entering it, its wet walls streaked with bands of green, yellow, orange and purple from lichens and minerals, the clear water inside glowing deep turquoise before fading to black
rock arches in the cliffs, natural bridges of dark rough lava standing out from the shore, the sea washing through their openings in green and white, brown pelicans and cormorants, black diving seabirds, lined along their tops and blue sky showing through the gaps
