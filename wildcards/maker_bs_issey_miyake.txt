Styled in a design language of sculptural, weightless, inventive design that treats cloth as architecture. Fine, permanent, heat-set pleats run in crisp vertical, horizontal or zigzag rows, springing back into shape and catching light in rippling waves. Geometric forms such as tubes, cubes, circles and folded origami-like volumes expand and collapse flexibly. Textiles are lightweight polyester, crinkled and bonded technical weaves, sheer layers and stretchy jersey, shown matte and slightly shimmering. Palette alternates between muted charcoal, ivory, black and indigo and sudden bursts of neon lime, tangerine, cobalt, magenta and bright turquoise, with playful colour-blocking and bold abstract graphics. Clever, light, joyful, modern and wonderfully free. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
