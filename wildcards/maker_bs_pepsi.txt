Styled in a design language of bold, electric, pop-culture energy built on a round globe divided into red, white and blue. A white wave runs through the circle, which tilts, smiles or splits into bold slices and large flat discs. Surfaces are glossy, chilled and metallic, covered in condensation, ice and fizz bubbles, with dark backgrounds lit by neon rim light.
