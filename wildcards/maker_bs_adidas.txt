Styled in a design language of sporty heritage built on strict repetition. Three parallel stripes of equal width run as the signature graphic, with trefoil and triangular marks and clean geometric panelling. Palette is crisp white, black, navy and royal blue with red or green accents, often in strong colour blocks with thin contrast piping. Materials are smooth leather, suede, soft tracksuit knit and mesh with tidy stitching. Retro terrace and street energy mixed with sharp modern graphic grids, flat studio lighting and confident, casual, youthful attitude. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
