Styled in a design language of rich, warm, heavy and luxurious. Carved arched forms with a glossy cherry red or golden amber burst finish, cream binding around the edges and a flamed maple figure. Bold mother-of-pearl block and trapezoid inlays on dark rosewood, gold-plated fittings and deep black gloss. Surfaces glow with warm translucent lacquer and fine cracking like a patina of age. A swept-back pointed silhouette and diamond shapes recur as emblems. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
