Guyanonymous' Wildcards v74 - index of new families
===================================================
Prefixes: book_ mag_ comic_ paper_ zine_ pamphlet_ reading_ bookmark_ (reading); sign_ signage_ text_ letter_ ad_ container_ (signage and labels); vint2_ (vehicle interiors; the older vint_ detail photographs are unchanged); bld_ (architecture; the older arch_ is unchanged); cb_ pw_ (creatures and powers); stamp_ card_ eph_ map_ atlas_ postcard_ rb_ graded_ collect_ (paper and print collections).

Rules for text. Any text meant to appear in an image is inside double quotation marks in these files. Every family with text has a _fill version whose text is a placeholder in quotation marks (for example "TITLE GOES HERE") to be edited by the user.
All names (authors, shops, brands, countries, teams, people) are invented. Human parts in creature hybrids are adult.
