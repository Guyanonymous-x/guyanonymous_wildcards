the still room of 1700 doing what its name says, distilling cordials, waters and medicines
the room of 1800, baking, tea and coffee room with the distilling gone
the room of 1880 at its fullest, a second maid under her, breads, preserves and all the hot drinks for the principal floor
the room of 1910 with the charcoal stove bricked up and a gas ring on the marble
the room of 1930 absorbed into the kitchen as a scullery or a store
the room as a tea-room kitchen in a house open to the public
the room in a divided house as a bathroom
the room mothballed, 140 jars boxed and the dresser empty
the room open to the weather
