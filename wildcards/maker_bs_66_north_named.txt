Styled in a design language of austere, volcanic, windy and functional Icelandic weatherproof design. Bold, practical, enveloping volumes with overlapping layers, visible seams, crisp adjustable edges and generous scale, in matte waterproof surfaces in strong colours such as bright red, orange, deep blue, black, moss green and ochre. Surfaces use long parallel channels and stranded patterns in grey, cream and charcoal. Proportions are long, broad and roomy, like arctic working gear refined into minimalism. Subtle sheen under rain and chunky, oversized detailing. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
