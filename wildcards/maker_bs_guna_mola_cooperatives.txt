Styled in a design language of graphic, rhythmic, vibrant and strongly handmade design. Two to seven layers of thin cotton in contrasting solid colours, the top layer cut away in curving shapes so the layers beneath peek through, with edges turned under and hemmed with tiny nearly invisible stitches. The design fills the whole panel with energetic horror vacui of tightly packed shapes in parallel concentric outlines, like a thick-lined labyrinth, depicting birds, fish, sea turtles, flowers, masks and abstract geometry. Colours are bright, fully saturated and placed in bold contrast, red against orange, black against yellow, green against hot pink, blue against white. Flat and symmetrical like stained glass. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
