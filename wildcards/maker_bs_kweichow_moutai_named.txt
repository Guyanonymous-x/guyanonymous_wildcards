Styled in a design language of formal, festive, prosperous and traditional ceremonial design. A squat cylindrical white porcelain form with a short neck, glossy milk-white glaze and a slight shoulder curve. A bright vermilion band wraps the middle, edged in gold. Special editions use red glaze, blue and white, or glossy black. Lacquer red or wood with yellow silk lining, embossed in gold and closed with a white cord or golden bow. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
