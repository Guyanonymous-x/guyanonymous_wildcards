In the style of Havaianas, Brazilian rubber flip-flops with a thin flat sole and Y-shaped straps, in glossy matte rubber. The footbed is textured with a rice-grain pattern of raised tiny ridges, and the strap is a flat, wide band that attaches under the toes. Colours are juicy, summery solids such as white, yellow, lime green, hot pink, turquoise, red, navy and orange, with a contrasting colour for the strap against the sole and stripes on the flag-like version. Two-tone, flat, lightweight and slightly squishy, shown against beach sand, blue sea and bright sun. Cheerful, casual and tropical mood.
