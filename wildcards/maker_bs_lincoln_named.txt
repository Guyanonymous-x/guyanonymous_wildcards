Styled in a design language of understated, sober, expensive American flagship luxury of the 1960s. Slab-sided, perfectly flat surfaces, a thin knife-edge horizontal line, long level forms and a square, formal outline. Minimal chrome comes in a thin shoulder strip, slim vertical forms and fine horizontal bars. Brushed metal and deep leather give grave dignity. Palette is black, midnight blue, silver, white and a touch of gold. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
