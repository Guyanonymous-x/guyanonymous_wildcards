on a hot summer day, the fields green and the air hazy, a pale blue-white sky with heavy cumulus clouds building, thick humid light softening the far tree line, the soybean rows and gardens lush and dark, the gravel lanes dusty and deep shade lying under the oaks
in fall, the woods gold and red, the hickories turned bright yellow, the oaks russet and deep red and the maples orange across every hill, fallen leaves drifting over the gravel lanes and tin roofs, pumpkins piled by the porches and clear slanting light under a deep blue sky
in spring, dogwood trees in white bloom at the edge of the woods, small trees with layered horizontal branches covered in flat four-petaled white flowers floating in pale drifts against the still-bare gray trunks behind, redbud trees in pink beside them, fresh green grass in the meadows and newly turned red-brown soil in the gardens
