Styled in a design language of minimal, refined, precise Japanese design in one vivid vermilion red with white and charcoal. A solid red circle, like the rising sun, holds a white stylised crane drawn with a single sweeping curved neck and few feather strokes, a flat emblem of confident simplicity. Colour blocking is a clean white ground with one strong red shape, sometimes a dark grey band beneath it. Finishes are glossy lacquer and flat matte print with very crisp edges. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
