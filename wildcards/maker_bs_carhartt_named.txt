In the style of Carhartt, hard-wearing American utility design. Heavy, tightly woven cotton duck with a dry, slightly rough hand, finished in tobacco brown, faded tan, charcoal black, moss green and washed navy. Surfaces look broken in, with soft creasing, pale fading at the folds and honest wear. Construction is visible and reinforced, with bold triple stitching, metal rivets, boxy generous proportions and sturdy flat seams. Branding is a small square label in a contrasting block colour with plain, upright, slightly condensed capital lettering, repeated on hang tags in kraft brown and black with cream type. Everything feels durable, unfussy, practical and built to be used for decades. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
