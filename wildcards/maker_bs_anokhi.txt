In the style of Anokhi, Jaipur hand-block-printed cotton textiles and clothing, with fine repeating floral sprigs, vines, small buta motifs, jaali lattices, paisleys and stripes printed in natural-looking dyes on soft white or cream cotton voile and mulmul. Colours are indigo, madder red, saffron, sage green, mustard, rose pink and charcoal, in two or three tones per print with slightly offset, hand-stamped edges. Garments are loose tunics, kurtas, quilted jackets, scarves, bedspreads and cushion covers, with simple bound seams and small tassels. Fresh, breezy, Rajasthani-craft mood, shown on sunlit whitewashed walls with piles of folded block-printed cloth.
