Styled in a design language of venetian hand-blown glass in lavish, light-filled forms, with delicate curved arms spreading from a central axis and bearing glass flowers, leaves and curling ribbons in clear crystal, rose pink, aqua and amber. Ornate scrolling branches and floral cups sit beside mid-century sculptural versions with cascading rods, tubes and ball drops in white and gold. Glossy, translucent, festive, ornate and glittering like a palace ballroom. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
