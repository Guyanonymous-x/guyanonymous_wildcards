In the style of Volga, Soviet saloons of the 1950s and 60s with a rounded pontoon body, a wide chrome mouth grille with a leaping deer hood ornament, round chrome headlamps, bold chrome side trim with a stripe, a long boot and fine rounded fins in later cars. Large chrome bumpers, whitewall-style rim hubcaps, thin pillars and a wide wraparound rear window. Interior with a three-spoke steering wheel, wide bench, large dials and mottled plastic. Colours of ivory over pale green, dark grey and black for officials, light blue and cream for taxis with a chequer stripe. Dignified, plump and official.
