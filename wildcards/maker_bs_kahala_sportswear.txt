Styled in a design language of relaxed, sunny, nostalgic tropical leisure with a hand-drawn vintage-poster illustration feel. Colours are rich and warm, turquoise, coral, teal, deep orange, mustard, palm green, navy and cream, sometimes limited to two or three tones for a mid-century look. Pattern runs continuously across joins and edges without a break. Soft, drapey, silky surfaces and open easy shapes. Bright but warm, mid-century holiday glamour. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
