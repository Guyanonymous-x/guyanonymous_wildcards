Rows of framed photographs of past Masters line the walls, each a man in a dark suit and an apron, with a small brass plate under each one engraved "PAST MASTER" and a year.
A blue silk lodge banner with a gold fringe hangs on a pole behind the Master's chair, embroidered with a square and compasses and the words "HARMONY LODGE No. 217" in gold thread.
A small organ stands in one corner under a dust cover, a wooden ballot box with a hole in its front sits on a side table, and a heavy leather-bound minute book lies open at the secretary's desk.
The secretary's and treasurer's desks sit on either side of the Master's platform, each with a small brass lamp, a stack of typed papers headed "MINUTES" and a crossed pair of gilded quill pens set on the front of the desk.
