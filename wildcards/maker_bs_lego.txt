In the style of Lego, glossy hard ABS plastic bricks in saturated primary red, blue, yellow and white plus black, grey and green, with a rectangular modular body and a row of round hollow-underside studs on top. Crisp moulded edges, tiny logo stamped on every stud, sharp mould lines. Minifigures with cylindrical yellow heads, dot eyes and simple smile, C-shaped claw hands, blocky torsos with printed tabards and short pegged legs. Sets sit in bold boxes with dynamic action photos on a bright colour field, exploded-view instruction booklets with clean line diagrams and numbered steps, chunky rounded sans-serif lettering, playful clean toy-photography lighting.
