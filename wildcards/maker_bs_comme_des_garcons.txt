In the style of Comme des Garcons, deconstructed avant-garde clothing with asymmetric, deliberately distorted silhouettes, bulbous padded humps, oversized stiff shapes, exposed raw seams, unfinished frayed hems, holes, inside-out construction and layered mismatched panels. Dominant colour is severe black, then stark white, grey and a single shock of red, with occasional gingham, polka dot, tartan or flat pop prints. Fabrics are wool felt, crumpled cotton, boiled and distressed knits, lace, patent and cheap-looking synthetics treated as luxury. Shapes ignore the body, sleeves too long or sewn in the wrong place, collars twisted, garments hung like sculptures. Casual line features a small heart with two eyes on plain cotton tees and cardigans. Stark white boutique spaces, austere, intellectual, rebellious mood.
