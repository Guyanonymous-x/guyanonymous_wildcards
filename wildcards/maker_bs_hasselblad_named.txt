In the style of Hasselblad, serious, rugged, professional, sculptural. A cubic, square-profiled body in satin black paint with bright chrome trim, or an all-black crinkle texture. Modular blocks clip together with visible seams, large round forms in chrome or black, thin coloured rings and engraved white numerals. Angular crowning shapes and one large simple control. Bold white capital lettering. Special editions use bright colours or exposed carbon weave. Heavy, exact, engineered and block-like.
