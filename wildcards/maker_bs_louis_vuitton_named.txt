In the style of Louis Vuitton, heritage travel luxury built on repeating pattern. Coated brown surfaces carry a monogram of interlocking L and V letters alternating with four-petal flowers and diamonds in tan gold, or a checkerboard of dark and pale squares in brown, grey and bright colourways. Edges are bound in natural leather that ages to honey, with polished brass studs, clasps, locks and rolled handles, and mustard stitching. Packaging is warm saffron orange boxes with brown trim and navy ribbon. Lettering is wide-spaced, thin, upright capitals in white or gold on black or cream. Playful bright reinterpretations sit beside classic ones. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Louis Vuitton in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
