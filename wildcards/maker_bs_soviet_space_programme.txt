Styled in a design language of blunt, heavy, utilitarian 1960s industrial heroism. Round spherical forms with small round openings, bare pea green and metallic silver surfaces, tapering volumes clustered around a central core, and brushed orange-tinted foil. White sections carry stencilled numbers, with bold red capital lettering and a red star mark. Visible cables, rivets and welded seams. Raw, practical, monumental, heavy-engineered and undecorated, with a stern, proud Cold War mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
