Half past five, the stove lit by the first man in the yard, which is the fire this room's whole function depends on
Seven, the day's harness taken down in order
Eight, the harness carried out and put to in the yard
Mid-morning, the room's real work, yesterday's harness taken apart on the cleaning hook, washed, soaped, oiled, the buckles done, the bits scoured, and hung back in order, which for one set of carriage harness is two hours by one man
One, the men's dinner in here, four mugs and a kettle on the stove, which is this room's second function and is why there is a chair
The afternoon, saddlery, rugs on the rack, a stirrup leather restitched
Four, the returning harness, wet, which is the day's hardest job because wet leather dried too fast is dead leather, so it comes in and goes near but not against the stove and is turned every twenty minutes for two hours
Seven, the stove damped, forty pieces of harness hanging in a warm room
Nine
A hunting morning, the room at five with four men in it and every bracket empty by half past
