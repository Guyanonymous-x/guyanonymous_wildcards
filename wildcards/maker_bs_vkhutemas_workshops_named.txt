In the style of Vkhutemas Workshops, constructivist stark geometry in red, black, white and cream, with occasional yellow and grey. Compositions place diagonals, circles, wedges and sharp bars at dynamic angles, with heavy angular sans-serif lettering and photomontage. Structures use thin plywood, tubing and simple planes with exposed joints, or stacked boxes, cantilevers, cylinders and glass walls. Prints are flat and high-contrast with rough printed textures. The mood is revolutionary, industrial, energetic and abstract. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Vkhutemas Workshops in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
