In the style of Opel in the 1960s and 1970s, a neat German coupe or saloon with a wide low nose, a rectangular black-mesh grille with a lightning bolt badge, and a coke-bottle waist with a rising line toward the tail. Round or rectangular headlamps, small chrome bumpers, thin pillars, and a gently curved fastback. Pop-out flared wheel arches in sports models, tail lamps in neat rectangular clusters, and steel wheels with chrome hubcaps or small alloy wheels. Paint in signal yellow, bright orange, lime green, white, and sky blue. Interior has bucket seats in checked cloth or vinyl, a simple dashboard with round dials, and a thin wheel. Mood is compact, sporty and mass-market stylish.
