Styled after an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
