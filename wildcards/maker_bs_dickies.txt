Styled in a design language of hard-wearing American workwear identity. Palette is khaki, black, navy, charcoal, olive and stone, with the red, yellow and royal blue badge as the single bright accent. Fabrics are smooth, tightly woven twill with a slight sheen, crisp pressed finish, bar-tack stitching and plain flat seams. Clean, neat, sturdy, blue-collar and street-ready. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
