Styled in a design language of classic British artists heritage in monochrome. Palette is ink black, crisp white, cream paper tones and rich deep blue, with a single bold red band as an accent. Surfaces are matte card, silver foil, satin enamel and polished brass with fine rule lines. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
