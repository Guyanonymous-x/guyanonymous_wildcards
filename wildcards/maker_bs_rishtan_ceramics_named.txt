Styled in a design language of warm, rustic, sunlit, homely hand thrown pottery in turquoise and blue. Simple, generous rounded forms with wide shallow curves. The surface has a milky white slip under a clear glaze giving a soft sheen and a faint pale turquoise glow, then painted freehand in turquoise, dark cobalt blue and chocolate brown. Patterns include flowers, branches, pomegranates and pitchers with swirling leafy borders and a central rosette, tiny dotted lines and fine hatching. Some have one calm motif such as a bird or sprig in the middle. Slightly uneven painted lines give life. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
