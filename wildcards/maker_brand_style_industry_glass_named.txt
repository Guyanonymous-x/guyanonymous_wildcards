In the style of Venini Murano Glass, hand-blown art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered under clear casing. Techniques create fused patches of colour like woven fabric, delicate lace threads, vertical ribbons, trapped bubbles and acid-etched satin surfaces. Forms feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean mid-century modern lines, polished edges and a tiny etched mark.
In the style of Whitefriars Glass, heavy, thick-walled studio glass with bold sculptural shapes and a strongly textured surface. Irregular bark-like ridges, chunky bubbles, dimples, moulded ribbing and cased layers of strong colour create a tactile feel. The palette is deep and earthy, tangerine orange, meadow green, kingfisher blue, aubergine purple, ruby red, amber and smoky grey. Forms are cylindrical, tapered, faceted or swirled with heavy bases and slight asymmetry. Surfaces are glossy with a deep glowing inner colour, in a mid-century manner.
In the style of Steuben Glass, crystal clear, flawless, heavy optical glass with pure transparency and thick, polished walls. Shapes are clean, simple and modernist, with crisp curves, tapered columns, deep facets and thick bases. Decoration is minimal, thin engraved lines of animals and plants, or sharp geometric cuts that refract light. Colour appears rarely, in subtle tints such as pale gold or aqua. The mood is cool, luminous, precise and quiet, like a block of ice shaped by hand.
In the style of Venini, Venetian art glass with thin hand-blown walls and elegant organic, asymmetrical forms, smooth, rounded and softly irregular, with wavy edges like folded cloth. Colours are luminous amber, aquamarine, turquoise, lilac, milky white and rich red, with patchwork mosaic panels, bands of fused colour, delicate lattice threading and acid-etched matte surfaces. Light-catching, subtly imperfect, jewel-like and sculptural, in a mid-century Italian spirit.
In the style of Lalique, French Art Deco moulded glass that is frosted, satin-matte and crystal clear in the same piece. Milky opalescent glass glows pale blue against warm amber, and tints of sapphire, emerald, black and smoky grey add depth. Polished highlights contrast with velvety acid-etched textures. Luxurious, sculptural, light-catching and refined.
In the style of Daum, French Art Nouveau layered glass, thick and soft, with acid-etched and wheel-carved decoration of thistles, poppies, irises, mountain landscapes and wintry scenes. Colours are mottled and graded from smoky orange to deep green, milky mist, powder blue and amber, with enamelled and gilded highlights. Naturalistic plant forms swell around bulbous bodies. Soft translucence, dreamy, rich, with a subdued glow like looking through frosted weather.
In the style of Baccarat, French lead crystal, clear and brilliant, with deep geometric diamond cuts, fluted columns, faceted forms and heavy bases. Cut hobnail diamonds and prismatic edges sparkle, tiered pendant drops hang from gilded bronze arms, and occasional bright red cut glass adds richness. Dazzling refraction and rainbow highlights under candlelight, formal, opulent and palatial in a Second Empire manner.
In the style of Orrefors, Swedish art glass, thick, crystal-clear and heavy-walled, with deep engraved or wheel-cut scenes, ethereal trapped air bubbles and abstract wavy colour patterns inside the material. Forms are streamlined, tapering and smooth, in a cool transparent palette with accents of slate blue, smoky grey and soft green. Understated, refined, sculptural Scandinavian modernism, pure and sparkling in cold light.
In the style of Kosta Boda, Swedish contemporary art glass, bold, colourful and hand-blown, with chunky abstract forms, bright stripes, spots and swirled colour layers, and cheerful faces, birds, fish and clowns. Thick transparent bodies in saturated magenta, cobalt, orange, emerald and cool teal, glossy smooth surfaces and organic asymmetrical silhouettes. Joyful, playful and sculptural, with light refracting through thick glass.
In the style of Iittala, Finnish Scandinavian design with calm, honest, timeless simplicity. Forms are organic and fluid, with undulating leaf-like curves, free-form wavy outlines inspired by lake shores, and pure cylindrical or softly rounded geometry. Materials are clear, tinted and frosted glass, smooth glazed ceramic and a few matte finishes. Palette is cool and clear with ice blue, aqua, smoky grey, moss and sea green, soft white, with sparing warm accents in orange, red and cobalt. Colour is translucent and stackable in tidy ranges. Quiet, functional and nature-minded.
In the style of Moser, luxurious Bohemian crystal richness with heavy cut and engraved decoration. Deep facets, fluting and columns, gold-gilded edges and enamelled gold frames, with etched floral and animal motifs such as stags, swans and acanthus leaves. Intense transparent colour in alexandrite purple, emerald green, cobalt, ruby, amber and smoky topaz. Rich, jewelled, glowing in warm lamplight, blending Belle Epoque grandeur with Art Deco elegance.
