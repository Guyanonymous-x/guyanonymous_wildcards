the men, husbands and grown sons from their twenties to their eighties, wear black broadfall trousers with a flap front, black suspenders, plain shirts of white or solid blue, black vests fastened with hooks and eyes, and black felt hats with wide flat brims, the married men with full beards and shaved upper lips
the men in their twenties to seventies wear the plain coat, a black suit coat with a standing collar running straight into its plain front edges, fastened with hooks, over white shirts buttoned to the neck, dark trousers, black shoes and round black felt hats with broad brims
the men in their thirties to eighties wear dark gray and black suits of plain cut, white shirts and black hats, the married Amish men with long beards and the Mennonite men clean-shaven with short hair, their hats hung on pegs once indoors
