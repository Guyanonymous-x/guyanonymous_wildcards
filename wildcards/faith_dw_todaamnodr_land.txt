Amnodr spreads out as rolling green grassland with shola forest in the valleys, as green as the Nilgiri hills and brighter still, with half-barrel huts of thatch and cane with tiny low doors at the front
in Amnodr a dairy temple stands apart, a low hut with a high thatched roof and a stone wall around it, where the dead dairymen tend the sacred milk
Amnodr lies in a wide green valley with clear streams, herds of great dark buffaloes with long curved horns grazing in it, and a cluster of half-barrel huts on a gentle slope
