In the style of Nothing, stripped-back, nerdy and quirky transparent design. Clear see-through glass and plastic shells reveal the neat inner structure of printed circuit patterns, screws and coils, with white and pale grey inner panels in tidy modular rectangles. The palette is white, black and clear, with a single tiny signal red dot used as the only accent. Soft rounded squares, flat edges and thin white line segments sit in symmetrical arrangements. Lettering is a dot-matrix typeface made of round dots, in white on black or black on white, with pixelated monochrome icons. Surfaces are frosted, glossy-clear or matte. Minimal, playful, technical and calm. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Nothing in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
