Styled in a design language of majestic, ceremonial and traditional Chinese state grandeur. A huge waterfall of tall chrome vertical bars, a flag-shaped red emblem, long stately massing and a formal upright top line with rounded-square details. Early forms add palace-lantern style details, thin supports, white bands and wide chrome edging. Modern forms add a flowing long front with a central ridge and a slim full-width light line. Red wood, cream leather and silk-like fabrics. Palette of black, deep red, gold and ivory. Solemn, imperial and proud. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
