Styled in a design language of expressionist and early constructivist work in wood, metal, glass and textile. Forms are built from stacked cubes, slats and planes in primary red, yellow, blue, black and white, with bold geometric shapes such as circle, square and triangle. Woven abstracts are compositions of rectangles and stripes in saturated, earthy reds, blues, ochres and black. Rounded cylindrical forms use nickel or plain glaze. The mood is utopian, experimental, geometric and artisan. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
