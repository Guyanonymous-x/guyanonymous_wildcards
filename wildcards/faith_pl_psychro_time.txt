in summer, bright sun outside the cave mouth, the entrance a blinding white arch when seen from within, hard light on the dry gray rocks and dusty green bushes of the hillside and a deep blue sky above
in winter, snow on Mount Ida, the whole upper mountain white under a heavy gray sky, drifts lying against the rocks around the cave and long icicles hanging from the lip of the entrance
in spring, the plateau green and flowering, fresh grass and young wheat across the plain, almond and apple trees in white and pink blossom, red poppies along the field edges under a soft clear light
