Styled in a design language of soft crackled ivory and cream-white glaze with freely brushed cobalt blue painting, and sometimes brown, green and iron-brown tones. Decoration is flowing and relaxed, with lotus, chrysanthemum, bamboo, birds, dragons, wave scrolls and key-fret borders, either brushed in blue or incised into the material. Forms have thick walls, swelling shoulders and a slightly uneven hand-thrown quality. Gentle, rustic, subtle and unpretentious. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
