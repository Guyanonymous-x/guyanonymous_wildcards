Styled in a design language of rugged, utilitarian, go-anywhere adventure. Upright, boxy, squared-off forms with flat panels, short overhangs, exposed bolts and hinges and a confident, tough stance. Matte and satin finishes in olive drab, khaki, sand, rust orange, forest green and black, with bright yellow or red used as a single bold accent. Seven vertical slot motifs and round forms repeat as a recurring mark. Stencilled, blocky, condensed lettering, canvas, webbing, cast metal and rubberised textures, and scuffed, weathered honesty. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
