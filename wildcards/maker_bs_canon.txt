Styled in a design language of dependable, precise and quietly handsome. Compact rectangular forms with a flat black or satin silver plane, fine black leatherette or wood-grain texture and crisp machined edges. Tiny metal details with fine knurling and thin ridges give a tidy, engineered feel, and a narrow red band accent marks top-grade pieces. Palette is restrained black, silver and white with a single confident signal red used for the emblem and highlights. The mark is a bold red serif-like script with flowing joined letters, often set white on a solid red panel. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
