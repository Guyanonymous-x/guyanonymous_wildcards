the submarine before it had rooms, a single pressure hull of small diameter with machinery
weapons
control positions
accommodation and galley all in one continuous space with no bulkheads worth the name
floor plates over a battery deck
a periscope in the center with a control position round it
a compass binnacle
hand-worked pumps and valves everywhere because almost nothing was powered
a diesel-electric arrangement with no snorkel so the boat had to be fully surfaced to charge
a conning tower hatch that was the only ventilation
tinned food stacked in every space
rationed fresh water in small tanks
damp, unheated air
hammock hooks on the pipe runs, and a habitability that was understood at the time to be a limit on how long a patrol could last
