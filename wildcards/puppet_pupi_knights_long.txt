Sicilian opera dei pupi knight puppets, heavy carved wooden figures in shining metal armor, each about as tall as an adult's waist. Each knight has a carved and painted wooden face with dark eyes, a curled black mustache and rosy cheeks, framed by a polished metal helmet with a tall plume of red or white feathers. The armor is hammered brass and tin, embossed with crosses, stars and curling patterns, and it shines under the lights. A thick iron rod runs from the top of the head down into the body to hold the whole weight, and a second rod moves the sword arm. Under the armor each knight wears a short velvet tunic of red, blue or green with gold fringe, and holds a sword and a round or kite-shaped shield painted with a lion or an eagle. __puppet_pupi_knights_var__.
