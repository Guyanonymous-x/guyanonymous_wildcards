Styled in a design language of small, square-cut, boxy, cheap, honest, utilitarian and distinctively late socialist era. Flat near-vertical planes, thin black plastic trim, rectangular shapes, compact blocky proportions, a pronounced horizontal shoulder line and sharply cut corners. Nothing is decorated and every edge is straight. Colour is plain gloss red, sky blue, white, yellow, silver-grey, beige or orange, sometimes with a thin bright stripe. A small stylised emblem sits quietly in a corner. Materials are hard brown or black plastic and plaid or striped cloth. Modest, practical and worn with pride. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
