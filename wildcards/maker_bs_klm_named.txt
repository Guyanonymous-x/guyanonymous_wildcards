In the style of KLM, cheerful, friendly, confident Dutch design in a single bright sky blue against clean white. A small crown emblem topped with a cross sits above bold, wide, upright sans-serif capitals, shown in blue on white or reversed in white on blue. The signature is flat colour blocking with a large sweep of cornflower or azure, sometimes broken by thin horizontal pinstripes in two blues, and a round badge version in navy with white lettering. Finishes are glossy enamel and clean matte paint with crisp edges. Palette stays disciplined with sky blue, white, navy and a rare touch of orange. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words KLM in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
