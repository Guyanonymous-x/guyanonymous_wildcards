Styled in a design language. Palette of red, white and black with occasional neon or pastel variations and checkerboard or camouflage fills. Crisp screen-print ink, slightly raised and cracked vinyl texture, woven jacquard and chunky embroidery. Smooth heavy cotton, fleece, nylon and glossy enamel finishes keep everything blunt, cool, smug and deliberately ironic. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
