In the style of Packard, stately, hushed, old-money American luxury of the 1930s and 40s. Tall, narrow, pointed-shield shapes with fine vertical bars, very long masses and sweeping separate curved wing forms. Deep gloss black, maroon, dark green and cream with a thin coach line. Walnut veneer, mohair or leather, elegant engine-turned metal, whitewall roundness and a chrome flying ornament. Poised, quiet and dignified. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
