at the foot of the right stairway, the Coyolxauhqui stone, a great round flat stone carved with the broken body of the moon goddess, her arms and legs cut apart, bells on her cheeks
at the top before the Tlaloc shrine, a chacmool, a carved stone figure of a man lying on his back, his knees raised and his head turned to the side, holding a bowl on his belly, still painted red, blue and yellow
at the foot of the pyramid, the Tlaltecuhtli monolith, a huge carved stone slab of the earth goddess crouching, painted in red and ochre
on the plaza on the left, the wall of skulls, a low platform covered on its sides with hundreds of carved stone skulls in rows
