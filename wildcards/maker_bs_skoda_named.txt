Styled in a design language of small, plain, boxy, honest and rugged Czech design. Flat surfaces, round and softly squared forms, chrome-rimmed edges, slim outlines and smooth unfussy bodies with no ornament beyond one proud mark. A round emblem holds a winged arrow in a ring, in black, silver and green, or in older blue and white versions, often as a small enamel pin or raised metal badge. Palette is pale beige, olive green, sky blue, mustard yellow and brick red, with black and silver. Materials are plain vinyl, flat grey or brown plastic, painted steel and simple chrome. Modest, utilitarian and unpretentious. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
