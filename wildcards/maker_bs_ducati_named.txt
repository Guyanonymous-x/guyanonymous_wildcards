In the style of Ducati, fast, sculpted, muscular and racing-bred Italian intensity. Glossy lacquer red dominates in broad colour fields with matte black, bright white and brushed silver as sharp counterpoints. Forms are sharply tapered, with angular facets, aggressive creases and a taut, coiled, ready-to-pounce stance. Shield-shaped badges in red, white and green appear in small crisp enamel or raised gel finishes, often with domed edges. Lettering is a stretched, wide, high-tech capital sans-serif, sometimes outlined or cut out in white. Details use carbon-fibre weave, anodised gold accents, mesh and fine contrast stitching. Racing numbers and diagonal slashes add speed and urgency. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
