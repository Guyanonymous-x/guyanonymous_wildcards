Styled in a design language of minimalist, clean and futuristic, with a software-first calm. Surfaces are smooth and unbroken, closed-off and flush, with low sloping lines, slim light bands, large glass planes and almost no chrome or visible fasteners. A sleek T-shaped emblem in brushed silver sits alone on dark matte fields. Palette is pearl white, solid black, graphite, midnight silver and one strong red, in sharp, high-contrast layouts. Materials are matte black, brushed metal, white vegan-leather-like upholstery and pale wood strips with nearly no buttons. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
