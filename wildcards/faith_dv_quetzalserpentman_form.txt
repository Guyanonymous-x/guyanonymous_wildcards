Quetzalcoatl appears as Ehecatl the wind god, a man in his forties with his body painted black, a red beak-like mask over his mouth, a tall cone-shaped hat of spotted jaguar skin and a cut spiral seashell on his chest, striding forward with his sandaled feet on the ground line near the bottom of the frame
Quetzalcoatl appears as the bearded priest-king, a tall man in his fifties with a long dark beard and a pale face, in a long robe painted with crosses and a feather headdress, a curved staff in his hand, his sandaled feet on the ground near the bottom of the frame
