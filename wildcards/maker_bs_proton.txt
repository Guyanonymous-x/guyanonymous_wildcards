Styled in a design language of proud, modest, practical, optimistic national design of the 1980s and 1990s. Compact, boxy, upright volumes with flat sides, a straight shoulder line, slim chrome edging. Paint is solid gloss in white, red, silver, dark blue and champagne gold, with black plastic trim. Plain grey cloth textures. Sunlit and unpretentious. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
