In the style of Olivetti, Italian office machines of the 1950s to 1970s treated as sculpture. Typewriters in bright signal red, lemon yellow, teal and orange glossy moulded plastic or pressed steel with soft flowing organic curves, rounded keyboards with white round-topped keys, and compact carry-case shells in matching colours. Calculators and computer cabinets have smooth enamelled panels, recessed rows of tiny coloured keys and small round dials. Lettering is lower-case or rounded sans-serif in white or black, often a small stripe of colour on the face. Mood is playful, elegant, optimistic and artisanal, with simple shapes polished to a gleaming industrial finish.
