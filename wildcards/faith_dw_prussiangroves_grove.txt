the holy grove spreads as a dark wood of huge oaks among which a clearing opens, its trees untouched by any axe, offerings of grain and amber laid at their roots
the grove runs down to the lagoon shore, where fishermen's huts and boats lie by the water and stones for offerings sit among the oaks
at the edge of the grove the land opens into fields of rye and barley and low wooden houses of the Prussian villages under thatched roofs
