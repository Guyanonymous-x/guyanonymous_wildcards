the room every intentional community actually lives in
regardless of what its founding document says it is about, a kitchen scaled for twenty to forty people cooking in rotation
a mix of domestic and catering equipment
a long table that is also the meeting room
the dining hall
the children's homework space and the place every decision gets argued about
a food system that is bought in bulk and cooked by rota
a cleaning system that is also a rota and is the thing that most often fails
a governance system of meetings
minutes and usually consensus decision-making whose outputs are pinned on the wall of this room
a division between communal and personal food that is negotiated and marked on the fridge
a building that is often a large old house
a farm or a former institution, and a social reality in which this room is where the community is strongest and where it breaks
