In the style of Wayuu mochila bags woven by cooperatives of women in La Guajira in Colombia. The bag is a soft round-bottomed shoulder bag shaped like a cylinder or bucket, crocheted in a tight single-strand spiral so the fabric is dense and slightly stiff, with a flat circular base and a flat wide strap in woven or braided cotton. A top band and strap are in one solid colour, while the body is covered in large bold geometric designs. Patterns are repeating diamonds, zigzags, stepped squares, crosses, labyrinth-like meanders, stripes and symmetrical motifs named for animals and desert elements, stitched in thick yarn. The palette is joyful and high-contrast, hot pink, turquoise, lemon yellow, orange, red, royal blue and lime green against black, white or cream, often with a bold gradient or striped panel. Finishing details are a braided drawstring with large tassels, or a twisted rope handle with pompoms. The look is handmade, tropical, energetic and slightly raw, with visibly wavy lines and woollen texture.
