In the style of Haviland Limoges, French white hard-paste porcelain with delicate hand-painted pastel roses, violets and wildflower sprays, pale pink, mint and lavender scalloped edges with gold rims, and moulded basketweave and lace patterns. Thin, translucent bright white body, soft floral garlands, rounded forms with curled details. Light, romantic, feminine, cottage garden and Belle Epoque in charm, with a smooth, glassy finish and a gentle, fresh colour palette throughout.
