In the style of Arita porcelain, refined thin white porcelain with a milky body, clear sparkling glaze and a great deal of generous empty space. Crisp cobalt blue painted decoration of landscapes, pines, bamboo, cranes and flowers sits in asymmetrical, minimalist compositions with delicate fluid brushstrokes. Enamelled touches of red, green, yellow and sky blue are used sparingly. Outlines are elegant and crisp, including faceted and lobed edges. Airy, graceful, calm and exact, like quiet tea house tableware.
