Styled in a design language of nostalgic, friendly and playful 1970s consumer design. Forms are boxy and softly rounded in cream, white or black plastic, with dark leatherette panels and glossy surfaces. The signature motif is a band of five parallel rainbow stripes in red, orange, yellow, green and blue, stepping across a clean white or deep black field. Palette is warm chocolate brown, tan and off-white with one bright red rectangular accent. Square shapes with thick white borders and a wider bottom margin recur everywhere, like frames and labels. Lettering is bold, rounded and friendly, set in white or black with generous spacing. Finish is soft, warm and slightly faded. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
