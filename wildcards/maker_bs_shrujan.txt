Colours are vivid magenta, scarlet, turquoise, canary yellow, green and white against black, indigo or deep red. Highly textured, glittering, tactile surfaces of shimmering mirrors and tight thread, with folk-craft joyfulness. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
