In the style of Zeppelin, rigid airships of the 1920s-30s with an enormous silver cigar-shaped hull stretching on a lightweight frame covered in taut aluminium-doped cloth, a small streamlined gondola beneath the belly with rows of windows, four cruciform tail fins, propeller engine pods on struts, black bold Gothic lettering along the hull and a red flag on the tail, with a stately and monumental majestic presence.
