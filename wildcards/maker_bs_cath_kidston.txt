Styled in a design language of sweet, cosy, nostalgic British vintage print design. Patterns are large, full-bloom cabbage roses in red, pink and blue on cream, pale duck egg green or powder blue grounds, plus tiny ditsy flowers, spots, cherries, strawberries, ticking stripes, gingham checks and quaint small repeat motifs. Prints are layered with a soft hand-drawn look and fresh, slightly faded 1950s colours of cherry red, rose pink, sky blue, mint, buttery yellow and white. Surfaces are matte cotton, glossy oilcloth, painted enamel and printed paper with red or pink trim and scalloped edges. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
