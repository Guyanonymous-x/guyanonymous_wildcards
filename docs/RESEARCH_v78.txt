Research notes v78
==================
Scope: cyberpunk, steampunk and western genre vocabulary; steampunk phone designs; home and decorative collectable types.
Method: written from general knowledge of the genres and of antique and vintage household goods. No web sources were fetched this round, so era details are general and approximate.
Known limits: character poses are shared per genre and can mismatch a character (a singer holding a rifle pose); collection type, era, colour and material are chosen independently (a 1900s lamp in an unlikely colour); no real brands or people; adults only.
