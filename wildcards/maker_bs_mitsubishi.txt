In the style of Mitsubishi, Japanese rally-bred sports sedans and SUVs with an aggressive nose, three red diamonds badge, a wide mouth lower intake, big round fog lamps and narrow slanted headlamps. A large bonnet vent, a big rear wing on a boot, pumped wheel arches, a short tail with tidy lamps, and multispoke white or gunmetal alloy wheels. Four-wheel-drive off-roaders have a chunky body, plastic cladding and a roof rail. Interior with bucket seats, round dials and a grey plastic dash. Colours of white, red, silver and black with decals. Tough, boxy and motorsport-ready.
