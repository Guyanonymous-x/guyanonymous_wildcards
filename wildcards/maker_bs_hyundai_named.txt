Styled in a design language of futuristic, digital, geometric design. A slanted letter H inside an oval is drawn as a slim, cleanly spaced outline, and a newer version builds a tall H from sharp, parametric strokes. Pixel motifs recur everywhere, in small square dot grids, blocky eight-bit letterforms and chunky mosaic patterns. Surfaces are taut and folded like sharp paper origami, with crisp creases and flush smooth planes. Palette is deep navy, cool silver, glossy white, graphite, with neon accents of magenta, electric cyan and sunset orange that glow. Finishes are satin metal, translucent smoked glass and matte ceramic. Overall, a clean, optimistic, high-tech and slightly playful look. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
