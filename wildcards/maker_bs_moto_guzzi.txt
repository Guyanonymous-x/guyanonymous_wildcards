Styled in a design language of proud Italian craft in red, silver and black. Round red and gold roundels, thin gold edge lines and tricolour stripes in green, white and red appear as recurring accents. Surfaces are deep lacquer, mirror-polished chrome, brushed alloy and warm tan leather. Shapes are smooth, generous, long and teardrop-like with calm, balanced curves. The mood is dignified, mechanical, sophisticated and confident, with wings as an ornamental motif for pattern work. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
