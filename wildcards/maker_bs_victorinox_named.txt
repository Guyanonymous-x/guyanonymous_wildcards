Styled in a design language of Swiss precision and functional compactness. Black polymer, olive, blue, silver and translucent coloured variants appear on tougher or playful versions. Bright signal red against cool silver steel and white, tidy and orderly, with many functions folded into a small body. Packaging uses red and white cards, silver surfaces and thin clear windows with neat technical diagrams. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
