In the style of Delahaye in the 1930s, a flamboyant French coachbuilt coupe or cabriolet with very long, sweeping lines, a teardrop-shaped body, pontoon wings that flow over the wheels, and a long bonnet running into a sloping tail. A narrow upright radiator grille with fine chrome bars, tapering headlamps often faired into the wings, side-mounted chrome vents, and chrome spears running through the wing. Wheels are pierced discs or wire spokes with chrome hub caps. Paint in two-tone combinations such as ivory over dark blue, silver over burgundy, and deep black. Interior has cream or red leather, an engine-turned dash with round dials, and a delicate wheel. Mood is theatrical, curvaceous and Art Deco.
