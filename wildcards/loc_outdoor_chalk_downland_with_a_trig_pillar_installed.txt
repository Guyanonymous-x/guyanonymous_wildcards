short springy turf grazed tight by sheep and rabbits
bare chalk showing on the path and in rabbit scrapes
a trig pillar in concrete with a flush bracket
a dew pond lined with clay
lynchets and a prehistoric boundary bank crossing the slope
a hillfort rampart on the crest
scrub invading where grazing has stopped
a barbed wire fence with a stile
a hollow way worn meters deep by centuries of traffic
