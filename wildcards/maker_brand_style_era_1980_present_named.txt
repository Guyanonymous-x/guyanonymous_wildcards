The surface has a milky white slip under a clear glaze giving a soft sheen and a faint pale turquoise glow, then painted freehand in turquoise, dark cobalt blue and chocolate brown. Patterns include flowers, branches, pomegranates and pitchers with swirling leafy borders and a central rosette, tiny dotted lines and fine hatching. Some have one calm motif such as a bird or sprig in the middle. Slightly uneven painted lines give life.
In the style of Kenya Airways, warm, hospitable, proud, bright and savannah-like identity in red, white and black. White grounds with the name in black italic capitals and bright red areas carrying a stylised letter inside a circle, in white and black. Older schemes run four bands of red, green, black and white in the national flag colours. Red uniforms with black and white scarves and neat caps. Red against clear blue sky.
In the style of Ardmore Ceramic Art, witty, lush, whimsical, tropical and highly decorative hand sculpted ceramics. Exuberant, playful forms covered with sculpted wild animals such as big cats, striped grazers, monkeys, snakes and birds that climb, perch and curl around the shapes. Surfaces are a dense riot of dots, lines and leafy patterns in bright glossy colours, including cobalt, emerald, orange, purple, turquoise, red and gold, with iridescent scales and shimmering lustres. Flowers and vines twist across surfaces in fine relief. Edges are outlined in dark. Every piece looks like a miniature jungle, bursting with colour and detail.
In the style of Ethiopian Airlines, welcoming, proud, highland-bright and colourful identity. Pale grey and white grounds with a flowing band of green, yellow and red lines sweeping upward. A deep green area carries a yellow and red stylised bird or wing emblem. The name is shown in a native script and Latin capitals in dark green. Green, yellow and red with white woven cotton trimmed in coloured borders. The three colour bands repeat as flowing ribbons. Green, gold and red against clear blue sky.
In the style of Royal Air Maroc, warm, hospitable, ornate and sunny identity of red and green geometry. White grounds with a vivid red area carrying a green five pointed star inside a round white emblem, and a slim green and red stripe. The name appears in Latin and Arabic script in bold dark red. Smart dark red with a fine scarf and decorative pin. Blue skies over terracotta walls, tilework in blue, green and white and palm trees. Carved latticework repeats an eight pointed star.
In the style of Jacobo y Maria Angeles Workshop, joyful, hallucinatory, highly saturated carved and painted folk art. Light soft wood is hand-carved into lively mythical creatures, often with a human face or braided hair on an animal body, caught mid-action while flying, scratching or leaping. After a smooth base coat, the whole body is covered in intricate fine-line ornament in vivid colours of magenta, turquoise, saffron yellow, orange, leaf green and black, in dense repeating patterns of dots, scrolls, flowers, plants, stepped geometric motifs and ancient temple-wall designs. Eyes are large and glossy. Light satin finish, with every surface patterned and nothing left blank.
In the style of Wayuu Taya, handmade, tropical, energetic and slightly raw design. Soft round-bottomed cylinder forms crocheted in a tight single-strand spiral so the fabric is dense and slightly stiff, with a flat circular base and a flat wide strap. A band in one solid colour contrasts with a body covered in large bold geometric designs. Patterns are repeating diamonds, zigzags, stepped squares, crosses, labyrinth-like meanders and stripes in thick yarn. The palette is joyful and high-contrast, hot pink, turquoise, lemon yellow, orange, red, royal blue and lime green against black, white or cream, often with a gradient or striped panel. Braided cords with large tassels, twisted rope, pompoms and visibly wavy lines and woollen texture.
In the style of Centro de Textiles Tradicionales del Cusco, rich, rustic, rhythmic and symmetrical hand-woven textiles in natural dyed wool. Long narrow bands and square cloths in slightly fuzzy hand-spun yarn. The tight weave shows crisp horizontal stripes of plain colour alternating with dense patterned bands of diamonds, zigzag lines, small stepped figures, birds, animals, flowers inside diamonds, meandering lines and tiny geometric motifs, reversible on both sides. Colours are earthy plant-dye shades, deep indigo and sky blues, cochineal reds, pinks and purples, yellow-greens, mustard, walnut brown, cream and charcoal. Edges are finished with braided cords and tassels. A precise ancient textile language in warm natural tones.
In the style of Chulucanas Ceramics, minimalist, tactile, graphic and folk-modern ceramics with smoky resist decoration. Smooth rounded thin-walled forms with softly bulging bodies, narrow necks and plump shapes, polished to a glossy sheen. Decoration comes from painting liquid clay slip over areas that stay light, then smoke blackens the bare areas, giving crisp contrasts of cream, orange-tan, chocolate brown and glossy black. Patterns are polka-dot spots, tight rows of dots, checkerboards, stripes, spirals, wavy lines, stepped angles and little fish or leaf motifs, often in two colours only, black on tan or tan on black. Like contemporary sculpture from an ancient workshop.
In the style of Weta Workshop, convincingly real, handmade, battle-worn fantasy craft. Surfaces show distressed paint, rust-brown stains, dull pewter, tarnished bronze and bone-white enamel, with scratches, dents, mud streaks, worn leather and heavy ring links. Organic forms are massive and sculpted, with pores, veins, wrinkles, hairs, tusks and wet glossy detail, in layered painted skin of olive, brown, ochre and grey. A playful retro strain is fat, chunky brass and copper with rivets and bold lettering. Gritty, textured, painterly, detailed and storybook-epic.
In the style of Zastava Automobiles, small, square-cut, boxy, cheap, honest, utilitarian and distinctively late socialist era. Flat near-vertical planes, thin black plastic trim, rectangular shapes, compact blocky proportions, a pronounced horizontal shoulder line and sharply cut corners. Nothing is decorated and every edge is straight. Colour is plain gloss red, sky blue, white, yellow, silver-grey, beige or orange, sometimes with a thin bright stripe. A small stylised emblem sits quietly in a corner. Materials are hard brown or black plastic and plaid or striped cloth. Modest, practical and worn with pride.
In the style of Roots Canada, warm, casual, woodsy cabin-in-the-woods outdoor lifestyle. A simple standing animal emblem on small branches, set above a name in a slightly rounded vintage serif, often rendered in a bright colour. Thick, soft heather-grey textures with bold, chunky lettering and roomy, relaxed proportions. Soft saddle-brown leather with stamped marks, collegiate mixes of wool with leather, and thick, generous forms. Colours are earthy and rustic, forest green, burgundy, navy, camel, cream, red and black plaid and maple-red accents. Cosy, outdoorsy, friendly and unpretentious.
In the style of Canada Goose, extreme-cold expedition protection, puffy, boxy, wide-shouldered and rugged. Heavy, long, insulated forms with generous padded volumes trimmed in fur, tall double edges, big overlapping flaps closed with snaps, patch details and an adjustable cinched waist. Surface is dense, matte, slightly rough woven cloth in black, navy, graphite grey, sand, red or arctic white, with quilted baffles sometimes visible. A round badge with a polar map and red text, reflective piping, leather pulls and a bold small tag.
In the style of 66 North, austere, volcanic, windy and functional Icelandic weatherproof design. Bold, practical, enveloping volumes with overlapping layers, visible seams, crisp adjustable edges and generous scale, in matte waterproof surfaces in strong colours such as bright red, orange, deep blue, black, moss green and ochre. Surfaces use long parallel channels and stranded patterns in grey, cream and charcoal, and the mark is tiny and in plain capitals. Proportions are long, broad and roomy, like arctic working gear refined into minimalism. Subtle sheen under rain and chunky, oversized detailing.
In the style of Camper, quirky, comfortable, rounded and chunky, playful, down-to-earth, relaxed and a bit nerdy. Soft leather or canvas in muted brown, beige, white, grey, navy and olive, with a splash of red, yellow or green, visible contrast stitching, rounded ends, thick bases with vulcanised edges and graphic bumps, circles or wavy texture, sometimes tiny nubbles like a tennis ball. Mismatched pairs where each side differs in colour or detail. Simple small name tags, white accents and rounded padding. Plain cardboard in white and brown.
In the style of Desigual, an exuberant, loud, joyful collage, optimistic, artistic, free and bohemian. Colours riot in turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Worn-in painted surfaces and sectioned stitched areas, with a big stylised swirling logo. Shapes stay simple so the busy print mix carries the look, with mismatched details and playful contrast.
