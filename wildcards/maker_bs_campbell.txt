In the style of Campbell, a straight cylindrical tin can with a bright red upper two-thirds and a white lower third, divided by a thin gold band. A gold medallion seal with embossed scalloped edges sits at the centre, flanked by ornamental elements. Lettering is in a thin red script of connected loops for the main mark, with white sans-serif capitals for the flavour name on the red field. Plain, flat, symmetrical, with a pale cream label and a ribbed can lid. The red-and-white palette gives a grocery shelf an instantly legible tin-can pattern, repeating as a grid.
