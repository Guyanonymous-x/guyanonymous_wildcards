In the style of Sony, precise, miniaturised, engineered high-tech design. Compact forms in black or silver with crisp bevelled edges, small softened corners and brushed aluminium faces. Dense, orderly rows of tiny rectangular elements with flat clean sides and deep black bodies. Palette of black, metallic blue and silver with sparing orange accents and small glowing green, amber and blue-green points of light. Lettering is fine, stencil-like capital sans-serif with thin embossed numbers. Later looks drift toward minimal matte black slabs with thin glowing coloured light lines. Mood is technical, exact and aspirational.
