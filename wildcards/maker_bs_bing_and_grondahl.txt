In the style of Bing and Grondahl, Danish porcelain in soft blue-grey and white, famous for underglaze tableware with elegant seagull and heron motifs, stylised flowers and tall reeds in shaded cobalt, plus delicate relief-moulded Christmas plates with winter village scenes. Thin glazed white porcelain, naturalistic Art Nouveau brushwork in muted pastel greys, creams, sea blues and lilac, smooth curved teapots, vases and figurines with crackled and sculpted surfaces, delicate and serene.
