Styled in a design language of rugged, colourful, overloaded, hand-decorated Indian heavy-duty design. Boxy upright forms with flat vertical faces, plain rectangular fronts with a single horizontal bar, wide round lamps and a high stance. Strings of lucky charms and tassels dangle from edges. Rounded steel or fibreglass surfaces in faded red and cream, with simple metal bars as protection. Gritty, dusty, golden hour mood. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
