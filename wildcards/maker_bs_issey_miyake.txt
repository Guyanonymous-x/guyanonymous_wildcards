In the style of Issey Miyake, sculptural garments in permanently heat-pressed micro-pleated polyester that springs back into shape, cut as oversized geometric volumes such as tubes, rectangles, circles and folded origami-like forms. Silhouettes are loose, weightless and body-skimming rather than fitted, with exaggerated sleeves and cocoon shapes. Fabrics have corrugated vertical or zigzag pleats that catch light in ripples, plus crinkled, bonded and sheer technical textiles, and a flat-packed accordion folding into wall-like discs. Colour is either muted charcoal, black, ivory and indigo or sudden saturated blocks of orange, fuchsia, cobalt, lime and yellow, sometimes in rainbow stripes or bold painted graphics. Minimal seams, no visible fastenings, sporty comfort, quiet architectural calm, bright airy gallery lighting.
