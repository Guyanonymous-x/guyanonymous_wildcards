In the style of the London Underground poster tradition, flat gouache and lithographic posters promoting trips to the countryside, museums and shops, with clean simplified landscapes of rolling green hills, cottages and villages, tall stylised trees and strong silhouettes in bold flat colour. Letterforms in a clear geometric sans serif with round O shapes, drawn in capitals with spacing carefully balanced. Red circle with a blue horizontal bar roundel mark on a white background used as the station sign. Limited palette of poster red, deep blue, mustard yellow, warm brown, sage green and cream. Cubist and Art Deco inspired simplified forms, serene travel optimism, cut-out shapes and flat layered planes, with lots of white margin and printed text under the image.
