Styled in a design language of glossy ebony black with a classic Germanic design. The mood is cultured, stately, ornate and drawing-room elegant. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere.
