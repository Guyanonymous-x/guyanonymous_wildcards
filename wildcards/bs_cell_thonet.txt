__bs_subject_home__ styled in a design language of solid wood steam-bent into continuous flowing curves, with slender round-section members forming loops, circles and S-scrolls. Structures are light, airy and sculptural, with delicate splayed supports joined by a ring brace, and surfaces of woven cane or pressed perforated wood. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
