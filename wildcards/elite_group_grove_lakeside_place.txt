The location is the Grove Stage, a big outdoor theater set into a slope among redwoods, its stage framed by living tree trunks and backed by a painted forest scene, with rows of benches for about two thousand men.
The location is the Dining Circle, a huge ring of long tables under the redwoods set with white plates, where hundreds of men eat together.
