__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of playful, futuristic, uncanny, theatrical and avant-garde. Oversized, sculptural forms in chunky glossy black, tortoise brown, milky translucent grey or pale pink, with thick geometric rims, rounded squares, pointed wings and big wraparound shields. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
