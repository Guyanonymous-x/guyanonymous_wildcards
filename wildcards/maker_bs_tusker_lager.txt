Styled in a design language of communal, sunny, savannah, relaxed and proud design in red, gold, black and cream. A cream and red field with an upright elephant head in profile with long curved tusks, drawn in bold black ink. Gold foil accents repeat with red. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
