Guyanonymous' Wildcards - Period dress, around the world and through time (v17)
=============================================================================
This layer celebrates global diversity: every era mixes Africa, the
Middle East, South and Central Asia, East and Southeast Asia, Oceania and the Pacific, the
Americas, the Arctic and Europe. No single region is the default.

37 eras: Palaeolithic (hide wraps, fig-leaf girdles, loincloths), Neolithic, Bronze Age, Iron Age,
500 BCE-1 CE, every century 1st-18th CE, early and late 19th century, then every decade
1900s-2010s (flappers, zoot suits, disco, jelly shoes, propeller beanies and other fads).
The 16th-century codpiece appears as a documented period garment piece.

Per era (key = pal, neo, bronze, iron, c5bce, c01..c18, c19a, c19b, d1900..d2010):
  period_<key>_garment / _head / _foot / _jewel / _bag / _accessory / _material / _ornament
  period_<key>_outfit       region-coherent outfit (all pieces from one region of that era)
  period_<key>_prompt       "<outfit>, in the fashion of <era>"
  period_<key>_mix          eclectic outfit mixing regions within the era
  period_<key>_mix_prompt   prompt version of the mixed outfits
Global pickers: clothing_period_garment, _head, _foot, _jewel, _bag, _accessory, _material, _ornament,
clothing_period_outfit, clothing_period_prompt, clothing_period_mix, clothing_period_mix_prompt, clothing_period_era,
clothing_period_era_prompt (picks an era's coherent prompt), clothing_period_era_mix_prompt (picks a mixed one).
Items are historical or cultural dress; use them respectfully.
