a Salzburg-style wooden string marionette from Austria, about as tall as an adult's arm. Its face is carved from limewood and painted a delicate porcelain white, with rosy cheeks, fine arched brows, painted blue eyes and a small red mouth. It wears a powdered white wig of curled hair tied at the back with a black ribbon. Its costume is rococo silk, a long coat of pale blue silk embroidered with silver flowers, a lace jabot at the throat, lace cuffs falling over the carved hands, white stockings and black buckled shoes on carved feet. Nine thin black strings run up from the head, shoulders, hands, knees and back to a wooden control bar held above. __puppet_salzburg_marionette_var__.
