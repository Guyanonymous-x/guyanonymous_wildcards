In the style of Vitsoe, calm, minimalist and timeless. Thin vertical aluminium lines with slim aluminium planes hung from them on small pegs. Surfaces are powder-coated in white, black or light grey, or smooth plywood in pale birch, with sleek clean edges. Crisp panels in matte pale colours with narrow slots and small detailing. Everything is clean, orderly and rectilinear with thin lines and no decoration. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Vitsoe in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
