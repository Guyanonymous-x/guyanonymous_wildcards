the New Temple, a vast flat-topped platform of dressed gray stone in alternating rows of large and small blocks, its walls rising sheer from the plaza with stairs climbing its face
the black and white portal of the temple, a doorway flanked by two carved stone columns, the column of black limestone on one side and of white granite on the other, both carved with winged bird figures with fangs
the Old Temple, a lower stepped stone building in the shape of a U opening toward the east, its wings enclosing a sunken courtyard, the stone facing partly fallen to show the rough core inside
a temple wall studded with tenon heads, carved stone heads of men turning into jaguars with bared fangs and bulging eyes, set into the stonework in a row high up on the wall
