Styled in a design language of mid-century Mexican modernist metalwork. Heavy hand-hammered forms with sculptural animal shapes like long-necked birds, seahorses, pelicans and fish whose bodies swell into smooth rounded volumes. Surfaces are bright polished metal with a warm slightly rippled hammered texture, set off by chunky cabochon inlays of deep green banded stone, blue lapis and azurite pressed flush into the metal. Silver married with copper and brass forms interlocking geometry, stepped-fret and feathered-serpent motifs abstracted into bold simple shapes with strong negative space. Festive, organic and luxurious, with a slightly imperfect handmade finish and visible tool marks. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
