Styled in a design language of witty, lush, whimsical, tropical and highly decorative hand sculpted ceramics. Exuberant, playful forms covered with sculpted wild animals such as big cats, striped grazers, monkeys, snakes and birds that climb, perch and curl around the shapes. Surfaces are a dense riot of dots, lines and leafy patterns in bright glossy colours, including cobalt, emerald, orange, purple, turquoise, red and gold, with iridescent scales and shimmering lustres. Flowers and vines twist across surfaces in fine relief. Edges are outlined in dark. Every piece looks like a miniature jungle, bursting with colour and detail. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
