A cornerstone at the base of the front wall is carved with the square and compasses and the words "LAID A.L. 5923" in deep carved capitals.
Brass plaques beside the doors list the lodges that meet inside, one reading "LODGE OF HARMONY No. 217" and another "ST. ANDREW'S LODGE No. 3".
Glass display cases along the hall hold old aprons painted with symbols, silver officers' jewels on faded ribbons and a worn leather-bound book of records open to a handwritten page headed "RECEIVED".
