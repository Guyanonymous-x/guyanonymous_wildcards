In the style of Shaker Mount Lebanon, plain and sturdy furniture and objects made from pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Tables, cupboards and drawers show clean flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, wooden knobs and a smooth surface. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, namely barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and the natural honey wood. Oval pantry boxes use swallowtail joints and copper tacks.
