Styled in a design language of English art pottery feel with hand-painted designs outlined in raised lines like cloisonne. Lush stylised plants, flowers, fruit and toadstools in Art Nouveau rhythm fill the surface with flowing curves. Deep jewel-toned glazes of royal blue, forest green, burgundy, plum and gold, with rich flambe runs and a smooth, glossy, velvety depth. Rounded, generous shapes, luminous colour and a precious, collectable Edwardian handmade feel. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
