Styled in a design language of quiet luxury built on woven leather, thick flat ribbons interlaced in a tight, even, diagonal checkerboard weave that covers the whole surface of any object. The weave is supple, plump and slightly puffy, sometimes oversized and chunky, sometimes in fine knotted braids. Colours are rich and unshowy, such as pale powder blue, butter cream, parakeet green, lilac, burnt orange, taupe, chocolate, ruby red and black, often one saturated tone per piece. Metal details are sparse, polished gold or brass, in simple triangular, knot-like or bar forms. Understated, tactile, soft, sculptural and wonderfully crafted, with a sleek, modern, sophisticated calm. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
