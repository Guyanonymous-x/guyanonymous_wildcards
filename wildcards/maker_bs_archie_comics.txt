In the style of Archie Comics, wholesome teenage newsstand comics with clean, uniform thin black outlines, simple friendly characters with round heads, dot eyes, button noses, bright wide grins and neat hairstyles, flat saturated four-colour printing with halftone dots on slightly yellowed pulp paper, red, yellow, blue and green panels, sock-hop soda-shop settings, speech balloons in a hand-lettered rounded face, and a cheerful clean-cut mid-century small-town mood.
