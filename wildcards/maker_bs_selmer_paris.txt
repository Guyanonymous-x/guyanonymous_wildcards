In the style of Selmer Paris, saxophones and clarinets with an elegant curved brass body finished in lacquer, with a warm honey-gold hue, or silver plating. Keys are neatly arranged, pearly rounded buttons with fine engraving on the bell showing swirling floral scrollwork in the Art Deco manner. The neck is long and gently bent, and bells are wide and flared. Tone-hole rims are finely drawn and key guards are tidy. Cases are black and plush-lined. The mood is jazz-age, Parisian, tidy and rich in warm gold.
