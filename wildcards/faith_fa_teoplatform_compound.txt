the compound of Atetelco, its White Patio a sunken square court with a temple platform on each of three sides, each with a short stair and a porch of two square pillars, the walls of the porches painted in shades of deep red and pink with rows of coyotes outlined in red and jaguars whose bodies are covered with a net of crossing lines, walking one behind another toward the central doorway, and with warriors in great feathered headdresses, and a second court beside it with an altar built as a tiny model temple
the compound of Tetitla, a maze of rooms and small courts, the main court with a red platform and a porch whose walls are painted with a goddess in a wide green feathered headdress, green earrings and a mask, her open hands pouring streams of water full of shells and green stones, and on another porch orange jaguars in feather costumes kneeling on a path before a temple
the compound of Tepantitla near the Pyramid of the Sun, a court with a low red platform and a porch painted from floor to ceiling, above, a great goddess facing forward under a tree full of birds and spiders, and below, hundreds of tiny painted people swimming, dancing and chasing butterflies around a mountain that pours out blue water
the compound of Zacuala, a wide main court floored in white lime plaster with a tall temple platform on its east side, the upright panels painted with red and green figures of the storm god with ringed eyes and fangs carrying ears of corn in a basket on his back, a deep porch on thick square pillars and a stone drain running out under the stair
a plainer compound of plasterers at Oztoyahualco on the northwest edge of the city, small courts with floors of packed earth and thin lime wash, a low platform painted with red bands on white, a stone altar with a small carved rabbit on it, and stacks of lime and polishing stones in a corner
