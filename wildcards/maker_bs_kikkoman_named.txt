In the style of Kikkoman, precise, balanced, restrained Japanese practicality. A bold red and black hexagonal crest with a stylised white mark sits on plain white or black fields with generous empty space. Forms are slender, softly faceted and gently tapered, with small glossy red cone accents. Materials are clear glass with dark amber depth, glossy black enamel, matt white ceramic and clean paper labels with fine hairline rules. Palette is deep red, black, white, amber and soft cream, with small bright yellow or green accents for variants. Lettering is clean, wide sans-serif capitals with small Japanese brush characters beside it. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Kikkoman in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
