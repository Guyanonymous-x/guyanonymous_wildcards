In the style of Datsun, cheerful, honest, retro Japanese design built around a bold roundel emblem. A red circle sits behind a solid blue horizontal band that carries the white capital name, framed by a thin white or chrome outline. Palette is primary red, royal blue, cream white, sunny yellow, mustard and orange with warm off-white paper tones, often as flat colour blocks with thick stripes. Shapes are rounded, compact and friendly with small shields, pennants and crest forms. Lettering is chunky, slightly rounded, upright sans-serif capitals, also set in condensed Americana type. Materials include enamelled tin, painted metal, chrome-edged plates, felt patches and glossy printed paper with a gently worn, nostalgic patina. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Datsun in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
