two doormats

a dehumidifier tank emptied every morning as a matter of routine with the amount of water in it a standing topic of conversation
a laminated notice about the door catch that swells in wet weather so the door has to be lifted on its handle to shut, which is written in a tone of long patience
a thermostat turned to maximum and left there because turning it down means the room is cold within twenty minutes
an extension lead running under a table edge with a strip of duct tape over it and the tape lifted at one corner
a laptop trolley with a wheel that has gone flat-spotted from the crossing
a cup of tea carried across the yard with a hand over it
a plant on the sill that has done well because the room is warm and humid
a patch of mold at the junction of the ceiling and the external wall in the corner furthest from the heater
a pencil rolling to the same corner of the floor because the chassis has settled
