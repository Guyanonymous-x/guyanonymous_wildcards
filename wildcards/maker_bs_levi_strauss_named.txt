Styled in a design language of durable American workwear heritage. Indigo denim in deep to faded blue with raised contrast stitching in orange-tan thread, rivets, bar-tack reinforcements and a small red tab accent. Surfaces show honest wear, whiskering, fading and visible twill diagonals. Construction is straight, sturdy and flat-felled with exposed seams. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
