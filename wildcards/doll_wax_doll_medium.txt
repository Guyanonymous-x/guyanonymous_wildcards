a nineteenth-century wax doll, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Her head and shoulders are poured wax, a soft glowing translucent cream with a faint blush, and her eyes are glass, deep blue. Strands of real hair are set one by one into the wax of her scalp, so her pale blond hair grows out of it in a natural line at the forehead. __doll_wax_doll_var__.
