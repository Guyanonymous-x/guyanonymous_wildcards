__bs_subject_home__ styled in a design language of Dutch tin-glazed earthenware painted by hand in rich cobalt blue on glossy white, depicting windmills, canals, sailing ships, tulips, milkmaids, farmhouses and floral scrolls, with fine brushwork and blue shading. Tiered, pyramid-like forms appear in some work. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
