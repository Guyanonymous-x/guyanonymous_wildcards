Styled in a design language of aeronautical, quirky, practical and eccentric Swedish design. A short stubby nose sloping down, deeply curved glazing, a rounded cockpit-like core and rectangular forms. A small gryphon head on a red circle as a mark, black plastic edges and one distinctive curved support. Alloy discs with a distinct hole pattern. Black, silver metallic, deep red, white and dark blue. Deep moulded forms in grey or blue cloth and an asymmetrical wraparound panel with big round faces.
