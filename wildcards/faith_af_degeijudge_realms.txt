far out over the sea lies Burotu, an island of white sand, coconut palms and red flowers, the favored dead in their thirties to sixties seated feasting in a thatched house there
the rest of the souls crowd into a long canoe on the lake, which overturns under them, men and women in their twenties to eighties sinking down through the dark water to Murimuria below
in Murimuria, a dim land under the water, some souls are punished, Inoke, a man in his forties, beaten with clubs and Litia, a woman in her thirties, pushed down into a pit of mud
