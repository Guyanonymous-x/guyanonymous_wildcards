Styled in a design language of plain, hard-wearing, no-frills Tasmanian workwear design. Smooth, waxed or oiled leather in rustic brown, chestnut, black, tan, deep oxblood and slate grey, with a scuffed, honest, well-used character. Forms are rounded, stout and stripped back, with elastic panels, bold pull tabs, visible stitching and chunky black rubber and contrasting pale edge stripes. The mark is a bright blue and green label with an italic wordmark and a small island outline, framed with cream and gold. Seasonal ranges add stout colour pops in olive, sage, rust and navy. Honest, timeless, practical and tough. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
