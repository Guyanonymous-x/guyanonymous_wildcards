life-size cardboard cutouts of two brides, standing side by side. The two figures are printed on one wide flat sheet of corrugated cardboard, cut out around their outlines together with a thin white border at the edge. One printed bride wears a white gown with a lace bodice and a full skirt to the floor, the other a fitted white satin gown with thin straps, and each holds a small bouquet of white and pink roses. A long veil falls from the first bride's hair and a crown of small white flowers sits on the second, they hold hands between them and smile out at the viewer, and a printed banner at their feet reads "JUST MARRIED" in silver script. At the cut edge the brown wavy layers of the cardboard show, and two folded cardboard struts at the back prop it upright on the floor. __cutout_two_brides_var__.
