Styled in a design language of rumbling, rebellious open-road Americana. Heavy forms with a low, long silhouette, deep chrome plating and fat teardrop volumes in gloss black with a strong orange accent. A bar-and-shield badge shape recurs, framed by winged eagle, skull and flame graphics, tattoo-style flourishes and pinstripe scrolls. Palette is black, burnt orange, chrome silver, white and distressed grey, often in colour-blocked panels and camouflage-like splatter. Materials are fringed and riveted black leather, worn denim and cast metal. Lettering is bold, slanted, vintage script and heavy block capitals, often weathered. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
