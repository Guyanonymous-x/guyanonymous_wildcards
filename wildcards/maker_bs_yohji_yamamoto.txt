In the style of Yohji Yamamoto, voluminous draped black clothing, coats, wide trousers, wrap skirts and long dresses cut in oversized, asymmetric layers that hang away from the body so the silhouette is a dark shadowy cloud. Fabrics are matte wool gabardine, crinkled cotton, heavy linen, felted wool, washed cotton and fluid jersey with raw edges, big collars, tilted lapels, trailing hems and long scarf-like panels. Palette is overwhelmingly black, then charcoal, deep navy, off-white and rare blood red. Tailoring is slouchy, androgynous and undone, with wide-brimmed black hats, flat shoes, and occasional large calligraphic or graphic prints in white on black. Poetic, melancholic, monastic mood, dim moody lighting, wabi-sabi imperfection.
