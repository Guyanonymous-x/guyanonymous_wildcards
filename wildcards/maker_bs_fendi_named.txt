Styled in a design language of roman luxury, witty, tactile and richly crafted. Materials are plush fur, fine leather, woven strips, marquetry-like patchwork and smooth lacquer, with sleek clean shapes in camel, tan, chocolate, black, cream and sunny golden yellow. Playful touches include candy-coloured trims, leopard spots and cartoon-eyed charms. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
