great white and gray rain clouds tower over the mountains, each cloud holding the faint face of an ancestor in their sixties to nineties, long rain serpents of blue and green curling out of them toward the earth
the rain mothers, women in their forties to sixties in long embroidered dresses, stand on the clouds and let loose rain serpents of turquoise and yellow that wind down over the ridges
the dead rise from the mountain shrines as thin streams of white mist that gather and swell into dark storm clouds, lightning flickering between them over the canyon
