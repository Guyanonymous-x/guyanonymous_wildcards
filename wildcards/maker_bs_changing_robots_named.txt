Styled in a design language of mid 1980s changing robot toys of plastic and die-cast metal parts that fold and turn from robot to vehicle, with hinges, swivels and sliding panels, painted in bold red, blue and yellow with chrome parts, sold in boxes with a painted battle and a grid pattern. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by color, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
