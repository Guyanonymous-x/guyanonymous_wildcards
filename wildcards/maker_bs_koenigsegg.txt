Styled in a design language of hyper-sleek, exotic, clean and ultra-technical Swedish engineering. A low wide body, narrow slit shapes, panels that swing up and out and exposed bare carbon-fibre weave under clear lacquer. Ice blue, gloss white, orange and gunmetal. A ghost-like shield mark. Hollow carbon or forged five-spoke shapes keep it technical. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
