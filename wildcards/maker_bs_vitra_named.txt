In the style of Vitra, contemporary gallery-quality design with sculptural moulded plastic in matte pastel and primary colours, thin steel rod supports or wooden dowel bases, and witty oversized forms. Curvy shapes are wrapped in felt, wool or leather, with black ash, oak veneer and white lacquer as calm counterpoints. Mid-century shapes are mixed with deconstructed, geometric, exhibition-like forms. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
