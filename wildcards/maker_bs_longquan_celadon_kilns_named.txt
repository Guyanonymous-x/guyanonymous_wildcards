Styled in a design language of thick-glazed stoneware in a soft, jade-like green, from pale plum and sea green to deep leaf green, with a lustrous, unctuous, slightly translucent finish that pools thicker in recesses. Forms are restrained and sculptural, with swelling shoulders and lotus-petal shapes. Decoration is minimal, with carved or moulded petals, incised combed lines and sometimes a fine crackle network. Edges fade lighter where the glaze thins, and bare unglazed areas burn to russet brown. Quiet, scholarly and understated, like polished river stone. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
