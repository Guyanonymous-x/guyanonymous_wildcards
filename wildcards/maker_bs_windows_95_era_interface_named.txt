Styled in a design language of a flat-grey bevelled surface treatment. Every panel and tile is light grey with a raised edge made from a white highlight line on the top and left and dark grey shadow lines on the bottom and right, while recessed areas are white with an inset dark edge. A flat teal green field fills the ground. Icons are chunky pixel pictograms in a sixteen colour palette, with yellow folders, dithered grey patterns and a four-colour waving flag emblem in red, green, blue and yellow. Blocky, beige-grey and nostalgic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
