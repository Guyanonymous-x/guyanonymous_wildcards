In the style of Knoll, high modernist furniture with polished chrome-plated flat steel bars bent in precise cantilever arcs, supporting thin black or tan leather sling seats and tufted cushions held by visible buttons and straps. Tulip-shaped pedestals with a single white moulded base and a round white marble or laminate tabletop, bent wire diamond-shaped chairs, glass-topped coffee tables on chrome X-frames, sculpted rosewood sideboards. Palette of white, black, chrome, cream and saturated primary upholstery in wool tweed. Minimal, architectural, boardroom and corporate lobby elegance, pared down to structure with sharp crisp edges and mirror shine.
