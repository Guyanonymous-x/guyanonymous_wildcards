Styled in a design language of a mosaic of hand-cut glazed clay pieces fitted into an interlocking geometric puzzle. Stars with eight, twelve or sixteen points radiate from the centre, surrounded by kite, hexagon and chevron pieces in vibrant glossy colour, cobalt blue, emerald green, saffron yellow, white, black, deep red-brown and turquoise. Edges are crisp with thin pale grout lines, and surfaces are glassy. Precise, endlessly repeating tessellations create a jewel-like pattern with no empty space. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
