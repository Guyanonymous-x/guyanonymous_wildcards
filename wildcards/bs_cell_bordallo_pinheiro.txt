__bs_subject_any__ styled in a design language of naturalistic majolica, whimsical, sculptural, rustic and slightly surreal with cheeky humour. Forms are moulded as oversized cabbage leaves with veined, ruffled edges and vivid green glaze with a pale ribbed back. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
