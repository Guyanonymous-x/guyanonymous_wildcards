In the style of Longquan Celadon Kilns, thick-glazed stoneware in a soft, jade-like green, from pale plum and sea green to deep leaf green, with a lustrous, unctuous, slightly translucent finish that pools thicker in recesses. Forms are restrained and sculptural, with swelling shoulders and lotus-petal shapes. Decoration is minimal, with carved or moulded petals, incised combed lines and sometimes a fine crackle network. Edges fade lighter where the glaze thins, and bare unglazed areas burn to russet brown. Quiet, scholarly and understated, like polished river stone. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Longquan Celadon Kilns in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
