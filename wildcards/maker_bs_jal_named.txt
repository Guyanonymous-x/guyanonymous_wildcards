In the style of JAL, minimal, refined, precise and symbolic Japanese restraint. White fields with a dark grey stripe, a red rising-sun circle, and a stylised red crane in flight formed from a circle and three sweeping lines. A thin red stripe pairs with a grey band. Lettering is clean, slim grey sans-serif with a red circle. Palette is red, white and slate grey. Mood is disciplined, crisp, elegant and quietly proud, based on a single circle-and-arc emblem and generous calm space.
