Faces are serene, with softly closed or gentle eyes, a pale rosy complexion and tiny delicate hands. The porcelain is mostly matte, chalky and soft, in pastel colours such as pale blue, grey-green, lilac, rose, cream, white and muted gold, with occasional glossy accents and fine hand-applied porcelain flowers. Forms are fluid and somewhat stretched, with the folds of clothes made into rippling, wavy ribbons. A glazed gres line has deeper earthy tones. The look is sentimental, romantic, delicate and fragile. The bases are small and rounded, with tiny flowers and ribbons, the poses are quiet and tender, and displays use soft spotlighting in glass cabinets with mirrored backs.
In the style of Camper, Mallorcan footwear. The shoes are quirky, comfortable, rounded and chunky, such as stitched leather sneakers with rounded toes, thick soles and vulcanised edges, and a rubber sole with a graphic design of bumps, circles or a wavy tread. Pelotas have a sole with tiny nubbles like a tennis ball. Twins are mismatched pairs in different colours or details, each foot visibly different. Uppers are soft leather or canvas in muted tones of brown, beige, white, grey, navy and olive, with a splash of red, yellow or green, and visible contrast stitching. Details include a simple small tag with the name, white laces and an opening of rounded padding. Boxes are plain cardboard in white and brown. The mood is playful, down-to-earth, relaxed and a bit nerdy.
In the style of Desigual, Barcelona fashion brand. The clothes are an exuberant, loud, joyful collage of prints, such as patchwork panels stitched from different floral, geometric and animal fabrics, bright embroidery, appliques, studs, sequins, lace inserts and ragged edges. Dresses and jackets mix several patterns on the same garment, such as colourful hearts, butterflies, flowers, zebra stripes, ethnic motifs, mosaic tiles, polka dots, graffiti and cartoon-like faces, in a riot of colours, turquoise, fuchsia, orange, lime, red, royal blue and yellow against black or white. Denim jackets have a worn-in painted look, bags are covered in stitched sections, and tags carry a big stylised swirling logo. The mood is optimistic, artistic, free, bohemian and colourful. Garment shapes stay simple, such as shirt dresses, bomber jackets and wrap tops, so the busy print mix carries the whole look, with mismatched buttons and playful contrast linings.
In the style of Chupa Chups, Spanish lollipops. The sweet is a round ball candy on a white paper stick, wrapped in thin shiny plastic twisted at the base, with a label in the centre on top. The logo is a bright flower, a daisy-like shape with a yellow circle in the middle and red petals, with the name in friendly rounded white and yellow letters across it, on a bright red, yellow or orange background. Flavours are marked by colours, such as pink strawberry, orange, green apple, cola brown and lemon yellow, and some sweets are swirled with two colours. Display stands are hot-pink or red and shaped like a round tree, with dozens of lollipops pointing outwards in a sunburst. Posters show cartoon children licking lollipops. The mood is playful, sugary, cheeky and very pop.
