the sky of Allah fills most of the painting, a vast clear dome of blue deepening to indigo at the top, the light within it brightest at the very summit
the night sky of Allah rises over the desert full of stars, the Milky Way arching across it and the Pleiades, the rain stars of the Arabs, shining high in the center
towering white clouds rise into the sky of Allah, rain falling from them in gray streaks over the far mountains and a broad band of bright light at their tops
