Styled in a design language of a flawless gloss black polyester finish with smooth, crisp straight lines and uniform surfaces. Shapes have a simple curved edge, clean planes and square or rounded supports, with a small gold or silver emblem of three crossed tuning forks. Boxy shapes have neat panels and plain brass accents. Alternative finishes include satin ebony, polished mahogany, walnut and white. The mood is modern, tidy, dependable, schoolroom and conservatoire, with perfect reflections. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
