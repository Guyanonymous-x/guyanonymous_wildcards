Thick ropes run from her head, wrists and knees up to the arms of two tall cranes on wheels, and her joints are huge wooden hinges. Teams of adult puppeteers in matching red uniforms haul on the ropes from platforms and from the ground, dozens of them, leaning back with the effort.
