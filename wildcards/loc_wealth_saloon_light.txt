Designed light, and the best-lit room in the building by a wide margin, four or five full-height windows on one side only, giving a strong directional daylight that the pier glasses are positioned to throw back across the room, so that the wall opposite the windows is lit almost as well as the wall beside them
A cove that catches the light and lifts the ceiling
Candlelight at night from twenty-four lights overhead and twenty-four more on the walls, all of it reflected and doubled by the glasses, which is the effect the room exists to produce and which no electric fitting has ever reproduced
Firelight on the inner wall; and in the modern house two table lamps and the chandelier dark because it has not been rewired
