Styled in a design language of serious, sculptural Scandinavian precision. Cubic square-profiled forms in satin black paint with bright chrome trim, or all-black crinkle texture, sometimes with brushed gold or exposed carbon-weave special editions. Modular blocks that clip together, with deep matte black, graphite grey and silver. Packaging is dark matte boxes with fine foil mark, soft black foam and leather-grain wrapping. Heavy, exact, minimal and engineered. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
