Styled in a design language. Forms are crisp, perfectly even and substantial, with sturdy rounded edges because the hard foundation shows at the contours. Red and black are set in strong contrast, one inside and one outside. Decoration is restrained and rare, fine gold powder painting of tiny flowers, birds and grasses in sparse arrangements, or hair-thin lines filled with gold leaf. Large areas stay plain and unbroken. The mood is formal, luxurious, calm and exact. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
