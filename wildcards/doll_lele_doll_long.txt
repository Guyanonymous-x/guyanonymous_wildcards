a Mexican Lele cloth doll from Queretaro, a stuffed folk doll about as tall as an adult's forearm. She is sewn from soft cotton, her face a flat round of cloth with two black stitched eyes, a small stitched smile and round pink cheeks. Her black yarn hair is braided into two long braids woven through with bright satin ribbons that hang down past her shoulders. She wears a white blouse embroidered at the neck with tiny flowers, a full skirt in a bright solid color trimmed with rows of lace and colored ribbon at the hem, and a ribbon crown of many colors on her head. Her arms are open wide at her sides. __doll_lele_doll_var__.
