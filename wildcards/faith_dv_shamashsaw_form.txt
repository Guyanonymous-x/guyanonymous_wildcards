Shamash sits on a square throne whose side is carved with the tall recessed doorway and stepped edges of a shrine front, a rod and a ring held out in his right hand, the saw-edged blade resting in his left, his feet in sandals on a footstool of carved bull-men near the bottom of the frame
Shamash rises between two mountains at dawn with one foot set on the near peak, rays streaming from his shoulders and the saw-edged blade raised in his hand, the mountain under his foot reaching down to the bottom of the frame
Shamash stands upright in judgment facing a king in his forties who raises one hand in greeting, the saw-edged blade held at his side, his sandaled feet on the ground line near the bottom of the frame
