In the style of Fiskars, Finnish scissors and garden tools. The signature object is a pair of scissors with bright orange moulded plastic handles in a smooth, open, rounded loop shape and silver stainless steel blades with a satin finish, joined by a flat black or orange pivot screw. Handles are ergonomic, with a soft thumb curve and a slightly textured or ridged inner surface, and the blades carry fine laser-etched lettering. Garden tools such as axes, loppers, saws and pruners have orange fibreglass or plastic handles with black grips, a clean taper and a sleek shape, with a razor-bright metal head. Packaging is orange and black with a clean sans-serif logotype. The mood is functional, friendly, clean and instantly recognisable, with a single hot orange accent.
