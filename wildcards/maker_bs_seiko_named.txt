In the style of Seiko, clean, practical, quietly sophisticated and slightly retro. Stainless steel with sharp faceted edges, mixing polished and brushed surfaces under clear domed glass. Palette is deep navy blue, black, silver, champagne and sunburst green with fine radial brushing, plus bold red as a crisp accent. Applied baton markers with luminous infill and slim bright needles give refined detail. Japanese-inspired patterns such as overlapping scale waves in red and white, fine woven lines and subtle gradient fades recur. Lettering is a stylised, wide, bold black or silver sans-serif wordmark, with tiny silver capitals for supporting text. Restrained, precise and considered. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Seiko in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
