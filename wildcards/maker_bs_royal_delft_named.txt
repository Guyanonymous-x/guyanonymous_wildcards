In the style of Royal Delft, creamy tin-glazed white ground with vivid cobalt blue painted decoration, from pale wash to near-black. The glaze is shiny, slightly bluish-white and pooled in places. Grids of simple blue motifs repeat with matching corner motifs. The mood is crisp, domestic, nostalgic and traditionally Dutch. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Royal Delft in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
