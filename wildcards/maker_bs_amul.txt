Styled in a design language of witty, homey, playful, everyday-Indian and nostalgic, painted in mural style on wall and street. Small rectangular foil-wrapped forms in yellow with a red band, and a little girl mascot with a blue polka dot dress, a red bow in blue-green hair, a button nose and a cheeky smile, drawn in clean cartoon ink lines. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
