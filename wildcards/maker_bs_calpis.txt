In the style of Calpis, Japanese milky soft drink concentrate packaging built on a polka dot pattern. The bottle or carton is covered in an even field of round white dots on a rich blue ground, standing for a starry Milky Way, with the later version inverting to blue dots on white. A bold simple wordmark sits in a rounded white or blue rectangle, usually in a soft rounded sans serif. Glass bottles are tall and slim with a narrow neck and a screw cap, wrapped in a thin paper label bearing the dots. Plastic bottles and small paper cartons use the same pattern on a larger scale, with a glossy finish. The drink is cloudy white and is served in a tall glass with ice, a straw, and perhaps a pale blue ribbon of colour behind it. Fruit flavour variants shift the accent colours to orange, peach and green while keeping the dots. Mood is clean, fresh, summery, childlike and retro, with a limited blue and white palette and gentle geometry.
