Styled in a design language of crisp, technical Japanese electronics identity. A wide, shown in white on black, orange on white or solid blue. Palette is graphite black, charcoal grey, white and a signature orange with cobalt blue and red as secondary accents. Forms are angular, flat, wedge-profiled and slim with sharp edges, matte textured plastic and satin metal, and tidy grids of small coloured markings and fine white printed lines. Packaging uses simple flat colour fields, minimal and confident. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
