Styled in a design language of lavish flat floral ornament painted freehand on white or coloured grounds. Flowers are stylised and fantastical, shown flat from above or side-on, with fern-like leaves, curling tendrils, berries, tiny grape clusters and the occasional bird. Brush strokes are visible and clearly shaped, a comb stroke, a seed, a teardrop, a tapering curl, with berries made by a fingertip dab giving a lighter centre ringed with a darker edge. Colours are strong and clear, scarlet, cobalt, magenta, orange, lemon yellow and leaf green on white, black, red, yellow or deep blue. Symmetrical, gracefully balanced, lively and celebratory. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
