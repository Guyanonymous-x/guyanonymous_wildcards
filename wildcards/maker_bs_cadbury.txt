In the style of Cadbury, deep royal purple wrappers in a glossy foil and paper finish, with a flowing gold script wordmark in thick looping lettering, often placed in a swooping white or gold ribbon swirl. Chocolate bars are moulded into rectangular blocks with the same cursive mark embossed on each square. A glass-and-a-half motif of milk pouring into a bar appears in cream and white. Gold and purple foil, cream accents, rounded friendly curves. Gift boxes use purple with a gold border, ribbon bows and chocolate swirl graphics. Warm, traditional, comforting British confectionery look with a rich purple dominating the shelf.
