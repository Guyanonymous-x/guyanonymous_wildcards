In the style of Tong Ren Tang, imperial, scholarly, aromatic, sombre and prosperous, like an old apothecary of lacquer, brass and paper. Long dark wooden lattice facades with carved eaves, lacquered red pillars, a horizontal black board with large gold brush calligraphy, tall hanging vertical signs and red paper lanterns. Interiors have floor-to-ceiling rows of small dark wood drawers with brass pulls and ink-labelled white tags, a high counter with brass scale and abacus, and glass jars of dried roots and flowers. Imperial yellow, vermilion red and gold with embossed dragon or lotus borders, traditional calligraphic characters and red seal stamps.
