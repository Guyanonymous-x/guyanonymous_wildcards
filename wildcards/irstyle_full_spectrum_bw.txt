Shot through a full-spectrum camera with the filter removed and converted to black and white, the image shows a soft, low-contrast black-and-white image with a luminous haze. Anything that reflects or gives off a lot of infrared, such as leaves, grass, skin, clouds, flames, hot metal and glowing lamps, shows bright, and anything that absorbs it, such as clear blue sky, deep water and some dark dyed fabrics, shows dark.
