the New Jerusalem comes down out of the sky, a great square city of clear gold, its high walls of green jasper set with twelve gates of single pearls, an angel at each gate, a man in his thirties in white
the holy city descends onto the plain below Pepuza on banks of white cloud, its foundations in twelve bands of colored gems, blue, green, red and gold, the river of life flowing out from its center
the descending city fills the sky over the canyon of Pepuza, its walls glowing gold, the Lamb, a white lamb with a gold halo, standing on its highest tower
