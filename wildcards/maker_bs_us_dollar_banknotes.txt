In the style of United States dollar banknotes, rectangular notes printed in black and green ink on cream cotton paper, with a central oval portrait of a statesman engraved in dense fine line work, ornate guilloche rosettes in each corner, scrollwork borders and a heavy numeral in the corners. A circular green seal with a ring of text, a black Federal Reserve seal, serial numbers in green and black, a gothic blackletter style heading, microprinting and fine engraving, and on the reverse a green building vignette framed by a lace-like decorative border. Newer notes add pastel peach, blue or yellow tints, a colour shifting numeral and a security thread. Traditional intaglio engraving, banking authority and tradition.
