the ruins of the city of Ur around the ziggurat, the low walls of mud-brick houses and temples showing as lines in the sand, mounds of broken pottery
the reconstructed house called the house of Abraham, a courtyard house of baked brick with rooms opening off a small open court
the royal cemetery, a broad dug-out pit where the tombs of the kings and queens of Ur were found
