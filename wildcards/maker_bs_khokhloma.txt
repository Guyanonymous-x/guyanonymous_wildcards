In the style of Khokhloma, Russian folk painted woodenware, turned bowls, spoons, ladles, cups and trays painted with glowing gold-like swirling patterns on black or deep red lacquer, creating curling vines, rowan berries, strawberries, leaves and flowers in red, black, gold and green. Glossy varnish hardened by oven firing, shiny golden-yellow undertone, rich, warm, curvilinear, festive, fairy-tale peasant kitchen treasure.
