a dreamcatcher, a willow hoop woven with a web and hung with feathers and beads, a Native American Ojibwe tradition, hung over a bed
a willow hoop woven inside with a web of thread, beads caught in the web and feathers hanging from strings below, the hoop as wide as two hands, wrapped in brown suede, the web of thin cord spiralling to a small hole at the centre, a few wooden and turquoise beads caught in it, three long striped feathers hanging below on beaded strings, hanging above a bed by an open window, the feathers stirring. Around it the wall is plain painted plaster, a light switch and a coat hook beside the door, the floor below scuffed wood. Soft daylight comes in from a window to one side
