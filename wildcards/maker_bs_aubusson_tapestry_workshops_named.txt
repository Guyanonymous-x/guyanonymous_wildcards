In the style of Aubusson Tapestry Workshops, flat, pile-free surfaces with a slightly chalky, dusty, antique hand, in soft pastel-leaning colour. The palette is faded rose pink, powder blue, sage green, cream, pale gold and muted terracotta. Designs use leafy verdure with birds, large acanthus leaves, floral bouquets and romantic medallions framed by ribbons, garlands and scrolling cartouches. Compositions feel airy, graceful, curved and symmetrical in a Rococo and Neoclassical manner, with delicate cream borders. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Aubusson Tapestry Workshops in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
