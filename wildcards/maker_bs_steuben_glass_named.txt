In the style of Steuben Glass, crystal clear, flawless, heavy optical glass with pure transparency and thick, polished walls. Shapes are clean, simple and modernist, with crisp curves, tapered columns, deep facets and thick bases. Decoration is minimal, thin engraved lines of animals and plants, or sharp geometric cuts that refract light. Colour appears rarely, in subtle tints such as pale gold or aqua. The mood is cool, luminous, precise and quiet, like a block of ice shaped by hand. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
