In the style of Volkswagen do Brasil, simple, sunny, pragmatic small-scale design. Rounded boxy body with rear-heavy proportions, round forms in chrome bezels, a small emblem, thin chrome edging with rubber strips and rounded bubble supports. Hot-weather details include pop-out vents and plain dish discs. Palette of bright yellow, sandy beige, bright orange, green and mustard. Friendly, sturdy, tropical and unpretentious. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
