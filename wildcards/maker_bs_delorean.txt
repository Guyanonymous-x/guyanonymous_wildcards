In the style of DeLorean, a 1980s wedge sports coupe with a flat, angular body of bare brushed stainless steel panels with visible grain, no paint, a low long nose and razor-sharp creases. Gull-wing doors that open upward, a flat louvred rear window cover, black plastic bumpers and a rear engine grille. Slim flat headlamps in a plain black plastic nose strip, a rectangular rear lamp bar, and five-spoke dished wheels in dark grey. The interior is grey leather with a simple flat dash and rectangular gauge pods. Matte, metallic, sci-fi, raw and unfinished.
