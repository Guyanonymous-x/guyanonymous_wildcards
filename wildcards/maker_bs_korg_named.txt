Styled in a design language of functional, technical, compact, precise late 1970s and 1980s electronic design with a futuristic hi-fi flavour. Long, shallow wedge shapes with a slanted metal control surface in brushed or anodised charcoal and silver, covered in rows of small round knobs, chunky slider caps and tiny lever switches in orange, red, blue and white. Side cheeks are dark stained wood or black vinyl. Cream white and matte black, square-edged elements. Cool grey digital mood. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
