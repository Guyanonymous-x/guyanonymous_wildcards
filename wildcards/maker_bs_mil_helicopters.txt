Styled in a design language of rugged, heavy, utilitarian and Cold War. Large, chunky forms with bulbous pod-like volumes, big round windows, a long tapering rear, massive repeating blades on a thick hub, and exposed machinery in boxy housings. Short stubby supports. Colour is plain, either military olive-drab and matte green with stencil markings, or white and sky blue with a red star or winged emblem and a broad blue or red stripe. Riveted seams, patches, rows of screws and hatches look hardworking and slightly weathered. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
