In the style of Venini Murano Glass, hand-blown art glass in elegant simple shapes with thin walls and flowing asymmetric curves. Colours are translucent and luminous, emerald green, amber, ruby, aquamarine, lilac and opaque milk white, sometimes layered under clear casing. Techniques create fused patches of colour like woven fabric, delicate lace threads, vertical ribbons, trapped bubbles and acid-etched satin surfaces. Forms feel light, sculptural and playful, with clean mid-century modern lines, polished edges and a tiny etched mark.
