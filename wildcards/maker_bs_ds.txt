In the style of DS in the 1950s and 1960s, a spaceship-like French saloon with a long sloping shark nose, a streamlined tapered body, a rear that tapers narrowly, and a floating roof in white fibreglass. A very low front end with a simple slit grille, headlamps behind glass, rear wheels covered by smooth skirts, and a thin pillar. Chevron badge. Wheels are steel discs with small hubcaps. Paint in pale grey, ivory, sea green, pastel blue and sand. Interior has a one-spoke steering wheel, plush seats in cloth or leather, a dashboard shaped in smooth curves with wide fabric, and a mushroom-shaped brake pedal. Mood is futuristic, serene and floating.
