inside the winter house, a dim room under heavy log rafters, a hearth of stones in the middle under the smoke hole, a notched log ladder rising to the roof opening and fur sleeping rooms along the walls
inside the yaranga, the polog, a box-shaped sleeping room of thick reindeer hides set within the tent, its front flap lifted, a stone oil lamp burning on the floor
beside the hearth, the sacred fire board, a flat plank of wood carved roughly into a human figure with a head, small enough to rest in two cupped hands, used to make fire with a bow drill, wrapped in a strip of fur and decorated with grass and beads
hung from the rafters, the guardians of the house, small carved wooden figures and bundles of sacred grass, beside dried fish and folded seal skins
