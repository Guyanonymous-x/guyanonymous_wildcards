Five, the kitchen maid in to rake and light the range, which takes an hour and a half to reach working heat and is the reason she is the second person awake in the house
Half past six, the fires in
Seven, the staff breakfast cooked for twenty-four
Eight to nine, the family breakfast and the nursery
The morning, bread, stock, pastry, the order slate read, written and rubbed out
One, the staff dinner for twenty-four, which the kitchen cooks for itself and the household
Two to seven, dinner, five hours for eight courses with everything timed to the minute off three clocks
Half past seven, the service
Nine, the last course gone up
Ten to midnight, the copper scoured, the floor washed, the range damped, the lantern lights closed
One in the morning
Four hours later it starts again
