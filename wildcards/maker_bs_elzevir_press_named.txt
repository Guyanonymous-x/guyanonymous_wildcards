Styled in a design language of tiny pocket-sized proportions with neat. Paper is white to cream, and surfaces are plain vellum or brown calf with gilt tooling and small marbled patterns. The mood is compact, practical, tidy and scholarly. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
