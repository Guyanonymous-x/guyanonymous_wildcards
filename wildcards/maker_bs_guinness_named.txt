In the style of Guinness, dark Irish heritage with polished, premium restraint. Rich near-black grounds with creamy off-white and warm gold accents, with a single golden harp emblem placed centrally, drawn in fine, elegant outlines. Lettering is slim, tall, classic serif capitals with a looping handwritten flourish beneath, often gold-foiled or embossed. Packaging uses matte black and bright, patterned colour panels with gold detailing, and sometimes festive illustrated motifs in blue, green, yellow and red. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Guinness in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
