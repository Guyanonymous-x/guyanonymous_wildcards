Styled in a design language of soft, understated Australian rural craft. Surfaces are dense rabbit-fur felt, matte, slightly fuzzy and suede-like, in fawn, tan, oat, cream, stone grey, olive, charcoal and black, with a darker tonal ribbon band and neat bound edges. Shapes are generous, flat and gently sloping with a soft dent and pinched creases that look broken in with use. Leather trim, small metal pins and plain stitched details appear sparingly. Practical, sun-faded, rugged and honest. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
