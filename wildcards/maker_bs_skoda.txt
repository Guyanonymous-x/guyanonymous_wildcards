In the style of Skoda in the 1960s to 1980s, a small, plain and boxy Czech rear-engined saloon or coupe with a flat bonnet, round headlamps, a simple small grille, and chrome-rimmed bumpers. The body is smooth and unfussy, with a slim roofline, large flat windows, and sloping rear with louvred engine cover. A winged arrow badge on the nose. Wheels are steel discs with tiny hubcaps. Paint in pale beige, olive green, sky blue, mustard yellow, and brick red. Interior has plain vinyl seats, a flat plastic dash in grey or brown with a strip speedometer, and a big thin wheel. Mood is honest, rugged and modest.
