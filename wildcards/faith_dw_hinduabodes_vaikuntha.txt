in Vaikuntha, a palace of gold and jewels above the clouds, Vishnu sits enthroned, a man in his thirties with blue skin and four arms holding the conch, the discus, the mace and the lotus, in yellow silk and a tall jeweled crown, Lakshmi beside him in red silk
Vishnu lies on the coils of Shesha, the many-headed serpent, floating on the ocean of milk, a man in his thirties with blue skin, the hoods of the serpent spread above his head as a canopy, Lakshmi, a woman in her twenties in red silk, pressing his feet
the gates of Vaikuntha are guarded by Jaya and Vijaya, two four-armed doorkeepers in their thirties holding maces, and Garuda, with a man's body and an eagle's beak and wings, kneels beside the throne
