Styled in a design language of mid-century American speed graphics. A streamlined racing dog silhouette in long, low, stretched motion, in white or silver on deep cobalt blue, set inside a wide oval or rounded plate with thick border. Colour blocking in navy, royal blue, white, silver and a punchy orange or red. Surfaces recall glossy porcelain enamel, polished aluminium, stainless metal and pressed cut-out shapes with rivets. Smooth swept lines, ovals and speed streaks give a confident, optimistic, simple, flat and highly legible. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
