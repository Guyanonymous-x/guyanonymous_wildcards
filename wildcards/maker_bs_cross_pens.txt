Styled in a design language of slim cylindrical forms with a clean, classical shape and a smooth finish in chrome, brushed gold-filled or lacquer. Surfaces carry a tight diamond-patterned engraving or fine ridged stripes, with a straight narrow accent ending in a tiny ball. Rings are thin and the profile is a perfectly even tube with a rounded top. Colours are silver, gold, black and deep blue. Leather or velvet linings add softness. The mood is business-like, classic, restrained and subtly formal. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
