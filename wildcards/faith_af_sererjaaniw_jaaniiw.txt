Jaaniiw, the land of the dead, a bright village of round thatched houses among baobabs, the ancestors in their sixties to nineties seated on mats under a great tree
the ancestors of Jaaniiw, men and women in their fifties to nineties in white and indigo cloth, welcome the soul as it arrives, their arms open
from Jaaniiw a soul goes back as a ray of light into a round house in the village of its kin, the place where it will be born again
