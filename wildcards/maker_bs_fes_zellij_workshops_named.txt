In the style of Fes Zellij Workshops, a mosaic of hand-cut glazed clay pieces fitted into an interlocking geometric puzzle. Stars with eight, twelve or sixteen points radiate from the centre, surrounded by kite, hexagon and chevron pieces in vibrant glossy colour, cobalt blue, emerald green, saffron yellow, white, black, deep red-brown and turquoise. Edges are crisp with thin pale grout lines, and surfaces are glassy. Bands may carry calligraphy or carved plaster. Precise, endlessly repeating tessellations create a jewel-like pattern with no empty space.
