In the style of Bugatti, sensuous, artistic, elegant and aristocratic coachbuilt sculpture. A tall horseshoe-shaped nickel-polished front with a small red oval mark, fine louvres, flowing swept curves, a boat-tail or teardrop end and steeply raked glazing. French racing blue, black over red, deep cream and two-tone schemes with a polished metal spine. Soft red or brown leather, engine-turned aluminium and small round faces.
