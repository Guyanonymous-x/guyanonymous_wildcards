Styled in a design language of a precise Swiss spectrum of intense, saturated colour arranged in tidy graded rows. Hexagonal faceted forms carry glossy lacquer in deep tones, and each is dipped at the end in a matching colour. Palette is vivid scarlet, cobalt, emerald, sunflower yellow and violet, set against dark charcoal, matte black and bright white. Metal surfaces are gold, silver, anodised and satin, with sharp chiselled edges and fine engraved lines. Lettering is a crisp, upright, widely spaced sans-serif in white or gold, with a small mountain-like emblem and a red square mark. Luxurious, exact, jewel-like and colourful. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
