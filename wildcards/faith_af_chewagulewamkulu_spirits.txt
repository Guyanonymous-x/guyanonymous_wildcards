from the dark trees of the dambwe beside the graves the mizimu come out as masked figures, the ancestors of the village returning among the living
behind the dancers the spirits of the dead, pale figures of men and women in their fifties to nineties in old wrap cloths, walk beside their masked forms, as the dance makes them present
the soul of the dead chief, a man in his seventies in a white cloth, walks away with the masked spirits along a path into the dark forest of the dambwe
