Styled in a design language of each page is framed by luxuriant woodcut borders of winding acanthus, vines, flowers, leaves and fruit, white on black, interlaced thickly. Soft vellum and silk ties add tactile richness. The mood is medieval, rich and romantic. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
