Styled in a design language of Art Nouveau iridescent ceramics. The signature is a shimmering metallic lustre glaze that shifts between gold, green, magenta, purple, cobalt and copper-red as the light changes, like a beetle wing or an oil slick. Forms are sinuous and sculpted, with swelling volumes, flared tops, whiplash curves and organic extensions like tendrils and lizards. Decoration is relief-modelled flowers, leaves, dragonflies, peacock feathers, seaweed and faces, with matte backgrounds and glossy iridescence on selected areas. Warm orange, green, turquoise, ochre and cobalt glazes appear in patterns of scales and chevrons. Lavish, jewel-like, organic and fin-de-siecle. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
