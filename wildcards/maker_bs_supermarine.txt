Styled in a design language of sleek, purposeful British aerodynamic purity of the 1930s and 1940s. Graceful elliptical curves, long slim volumes, tapering ends and a narrow stance, all in polished metal skin with flush, tidy rivets. Colour is either dark green and earth brown shading to pale sky blue, or glossy blue and silver lacquer. Bright round markings in red, white and blue give crisp contrast. Fine, thin lines and smooth rounded transparent forms. Fast-looking, elegant, lean and precise, pared down to speed alone. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
