Styled in a design language of modernist, simple and functional design in matte black plastic and brushed stainless steel. Forms are straight cylinders with faceted triangular sections, and a thin sprung wire accent extends along the body as a single flat bar. Surfaces are matte and fine-grained, with only small dots or windows as detail. Colours are black, charcoal grey, white, and later bright red, yellow and blue. Metal parts are silvery, clean and plain. The mood is German industrial design, school-like and elegantly austere.
