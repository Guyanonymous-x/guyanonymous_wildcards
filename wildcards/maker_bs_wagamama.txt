Styled in a design language of austere, canteen-like Japanese-influenced minimalism with a cheerful bustle. Long communal wooden benches in a bright minimal hall with white walls. Thin paper in black and red in a hand-written style. Large simple bowls and plain utensils. Palette is black, red, white and pale wood. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
