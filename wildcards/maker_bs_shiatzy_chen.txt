In the style of Shiatzy Chen, elegant East meets West womenswear that blends tailored modern cuts with Chinese tradition, standing mandarin collars, frog knot buttons, asymmetric side-fastening jackets, silk qipao-inspired dresses and cropped tailoring. Fabrics are silk satin, brocade, jacquard, embroidered tulle and wool crepe, with embroidered or woven motifs of peonies, bamboo, cranes, clouds and calligraphy brush strokes. Palette favours jade green, imperial red, ink black, ivory, deep blue and gold thread, with delicate piping, braided trim and tassel details. Silhouettes are slim, refined and graceful, with a poised, quiet-luxury, nostalgic Shanghai-style mood and soft studio lighting.
