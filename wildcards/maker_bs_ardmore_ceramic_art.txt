In the style of Ardmore Ceramic Art, flamboyant hand sculpted South African ceramics. Pieces are thrown, modelled and painted by several artists, and are teapots, lamps, vases, candle holders, platters and egg cups. Forms are exuberant and playful, covered with sculpted animals such as leopards, zebras, giraffes, monkeys, antelope, snakes and birds that climb handles, perch on lids or curl around spouts. The surface is covered with a dense riot of dots, lines and leafy patterns, painted in bright glossy colours, including cobalt, emerald, orange, purple, turquoise, red and gold, with iridescent scales and shimmering lustres. Flowers and vines twist across the body in fine relief. Edges are outlined in dark, and every piece looks like a miniature jungle. Mood is witty, lush, whimsical, tropical and highly decorative, bursting with colour and detail.
