Tap Day at Yale University, the spring day when the senior societies pick their new members. In a university courtyard of old stone buildings, college juniors in their twenties wait in small groups on the lawn, and older members of the senior societies walk among them in dark suits. A member steps up behind a waiting junior and slaps him hard on the shoulder, and the junior turns, startled, as the member leans close to give the offer.
