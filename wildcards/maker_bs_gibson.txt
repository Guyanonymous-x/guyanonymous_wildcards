Styled in a design language of rich, warm, heavy and luxurious. Carved arched forms with a glossy cherry red or golden amber burst finish, cream binding around the edges and a flamed maple figure. Bold mother-of-pearl block and trapezoid inlays on dark rosewood, gold-plated fittings and deep black gloss. Surfaces glow with warm translucent lacquer and fine cracking like a patina of age. Lettering is a tall, condensed, hand-drawn gold script with lively slanted strokes, set on black or inlaid in pearl. A swept-back pointed silhouette and diamond shapes recur as emblems. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
