In the style of Casio, Japanese digital watches and calculators. Lightweight resin cases in black, grey, silver or bright primary colours with squared-off octagonal or rectangular bezels, grey-green LCD displays showing chunky segmented digits, and small rubber push buttons on the sides labelled in tiny white capitals. Resin straps with moulded loops, steel bracelet versions with a gold finish, and tough rubberised protective bezels in G-shock style. Calculators have grid layouts of coloured keys in grey, orange and blue. Printed text is dense, small and technical, with many function labels around the dial. Mood is cheap, functional, geeky and indestructible.
In the style of Seiko, Japanese wristwatches of the 1960s to 1980s. Stainless steel cases with sharp faceted lugs, polished and brushed surfaces, and a clear domed crystal. Dials come in deep blue, black, silver, champagne or sunburst green with fine radial brushing, applied baton hour markers filled with luminous paint, a day and date window at three, and slim bright hands with a central sweep seconds. Divers have a rotating bezel with luminous pip and chunky bracelet links. Dial text is small silver capitals and a wordmark in stylised sans-serif. Mood is clean, practical, quietly sophisticated and slightly retro.
In the style of Rolex, Swiss luxury steel and gold watches. Round robust cases of polished and brushed stainless steel or yellow gold with a fluted or smooth bezel, a screw-down winding crown protected by shoulders and topped with a small crown logo, and a magnifying cyclops bubble over the date at three. Dials are glossy black, white, blue, green or champagne with applied polished hour markers and luminous dots, plus broad sword-shaped hands. Three-link Oyster-style bracelets with satin centre links and polished outer links. Lettering is crisp small serif capitals in gold or white. Mood is solid, imposing, polished and status-heavy.
In the style of Omega, Swiss sports and dress watches. Round steel cases with clean faceted lugs and a polished or brushed finish, domed hesalite-like crystals, and tachymeter bezels in black aluminium with white numerals. Dials are matte black or silver with contrasting sub-dials in chronograph models, baton hands, applied index markers with luminous infill, and a small Greek letter logo at twelve. Straps in black leather with white stitching or steel bracelets with tapering links. Lettering is crisp, slim white sans-serif. Mood is purposeful, elegant and exploratory with a vintage space-age touch.
In the style of Patek Philippe, Swiss haute horlogerie. Slim round or oval cases in polished rose gold, yellow gold or platinum with elegant fine lugs, a thin bezel and a refined hand-stitched alligator leather strap in brown or black. Dials are cream, silvered or deep blue with finely guilloched surfaces, slender dauphine hands, applied gold Breguet numerals or baton markers, and subtle small seconds or moon-phase windows with small scripts. Cases have clean proportions with discreet crowns and a Calatrava-style cross mark. Typography is fine serif capitals and delicate cursive. Mood is refined, understated, heirloom and timeless.
In the style of Nomos Glashutte, German minimalist watches. Slim round stainless steel cases with thin straight lugs, a very narrow bezel and a flat domed crystal over a pure white or silvery-white dial. The dial has slender blued steel hands, thin black Arabic numerals in a clean geometric sans-serif and a tiny subsidiary seconds dial at six, plus a thin minute track ring. Straps in plain brown or cordovan leather. Sapphire back reveals a decorated movement with stripes and blued screws. Mood is spare, precise, calm and modern.
In the style of Junghans, German modernist clocks and watches. Round or rectangular dials with extremely clean layouts, slim baton or simple stick hands, thin black line markers or short numerals in a geometric sans-serif, and a dome-shaped crystal. Case in matte steel or gold with minimal detail. Colours are white, black, grey with occasional warm orange or yellow second hands. Table clocks are small cylinders or squares of off-white enamel or wood with tiny hole speaker-style detail. Mood is pared-back, functional and Bauhaus-like in discipline.
In the style of Citizen Watch, Japanese quartz and solar watches with a practical, refined look. Cases are round or slightly cushion shaped in brushed and polished stainless steel or titanium, with a flat sapphire crystal and a slim bezel. Dials are clean and legible, in silver white, deep navy, black or champagne, with applied baton hour markers, sharp sword hands in silver with pale luminous fill, a small date window at three, and a fine printed minute track. Divers add a rotating bezel with alternating smooth and serrated sections, bold luminous dots, a screw-down crown and a rugged rubber or steel bracelet. Fine dress models use dials of lacquered black with raised star or moon motifs, mother-of-pearl, or paper-textured white. Bracelets have brushed centre links and polished edges with a hidden clasp. Small printed text lines the dial in tidy thin capitals. Mood is reliable, modern, understated, engineered and quietly elegant, with lit-from-above photography and cool metallic shine.
In the style of HMT Watches, Indian hand wound mechanical wristwatches of the 1960s to 1980s. Cases are small round or slightly cushioned steel or gold plated, with a thin bezel and a plain case back stamped with a lion mark. Dials are simple, in silver white, cream or sunburst gold, with slim baton hour markers, thin dauphine style hands in gilt or steel, and a tiny printed minute ring. Some models have a small date window or a day-date window, and the dial text is in neat serif capitals reading the name and the word incabloc or jewels. Skeleton variants show gears and a gold plated movement under a clear plate. Straps are thin leather in black or brown, with plain steel buckles. Table clocks and wall clocks share the same cream dial look. Packaging is a modest cardboard box lined in blue velvet. Mood is dependable, modest, sentimental, quiet and middle-class, reminiscent of a gift watch given at graduation.
