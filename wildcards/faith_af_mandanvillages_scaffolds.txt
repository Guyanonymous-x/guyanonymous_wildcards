on the prairie beyond the village, the dead lie on high wooden scaffolds of four forked posts, each body wrapped in a buffalo robe tied with rawhide, food and pots set at the foot of the posts
a Mandan woman in her fifties in a long dress of pale elk hide stands under the scaffold of her husband, her face lifted to him, a bowl of corn in her hands
