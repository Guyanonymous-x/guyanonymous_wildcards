Styled in a design language of arts and Crafts silver, enamel and jewellery in a hand-wrought manner. Hammered metal shows visible tool marks, curved lines, wire twists and loose, flowing Art Nouveau lines. Peacocks, ships, tendrils, stylised flowers and hearts are set with cabochon moonstones, opals, turquoise or blister pearls and translucent coloured enamel in green, blue and rose. Objects look soft and slightly irregular, with a matte, pale silver surface and delicate suspended drops. The mood is romantic, medieval-influenced, simple and craftsmanlike. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
