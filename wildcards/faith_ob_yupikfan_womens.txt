the women's fans small flat ovals of tightly coiled golden beach grass with a loop behind for the fingers, each edged with a deep fringe of long white caribou neck hair as fine as silk
each woman's fan a little round mat of pale grass stitched in rings with a few rows dyed dark red and blue, the long white hair around its rim falling over the back of her hand
a pair of finger masks, small round wooden disks each carved with a tiny smiling face and two finger holes below it, ringed with short feathers and tufts of white caribou hair
