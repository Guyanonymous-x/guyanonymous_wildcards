a tiler laying tiles. The place is a bathroom stripped back to bare walls, boxes of tiles and a bucket of adhesive on the floor. Close at hand are a notched trowel, a tile cutter, spacers, a rubber grout float and a spirit level. The work under way is a wall half tiled in white rows, plastic spacers still in the joints and adhesive combed in ridges on the bare part. The end result is a finished bathroom wall of glossy patterned tiles with clean white grout lines. The worker is pressing a tile into combed adhesive and tapping it level against its neighbours, with hard skin on the knees, grout in the creases of the fingers and small cuts from tile edges, wearing knee pads, grey work trousers with many pockets and a plain t-shirt
