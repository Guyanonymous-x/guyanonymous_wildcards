In the style of Trabant in the 1960s to 1980s, a tiny, boxy East German two-door saloon or estate with a flat bonnet, a pressed plastic body, flat sides, and square corners with slight rounding. Round headlamps, a small slim grille, small chrome bumpers, a short upright tail with a tiny rear window, and thin pillars. Tiny wheels on steel rims with plain hubcaps and a small letter T badge. Paint in pastel hues like pale blue, beige, sage green, orange, and white, with a faded matt finish. Interior has bare flat dash with a single round speedometer, thin-rimmed wheel, plain hard seats in vinyl, and a column gear lever. Mood is austere, tiny, humble and slightly smoky.
