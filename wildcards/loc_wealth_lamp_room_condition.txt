the room of 1780 as a candle and rush-light store with a locked candle box, which is its ancestor and is small
the room of 1830 with colza-oil Argand lamps, which is when it becomes a real department
the room of 1870 with paraffin and forty-eight lamps, a dedicated lamp man or a footman spending four hours a day in it, which is the arrangement this record describes and is the type at its fullest; where this room is suddenly redundant for the principal floor and keeps going for the service side, the stables and the lanterns; where the whole department ends within a year and the room becomes a store, which is the single most abrupt functional death in this tab, one installation and forty-eight lamps go to the attic
the room as an electrical switch room and meter room, which is a direct and very common succession and is quietly appropriate
the room as a store for forty-eight lamps that are still there, boxed, which is why so many country houses have a lamp collection
the room mothballed, oil removed as a hazard, lamps racked
the room open to the weather, where the zinc is stolen for scrap and the chimney rack's slotted shelving survives and puzzles people
