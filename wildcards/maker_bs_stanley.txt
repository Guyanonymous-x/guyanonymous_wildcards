Styled in a design language of rugged, honest, mid-century working-man design with rounded cylindrical forms, stout shoulders and squat proportions. The signature finish is hammertone enamel in deep forest green with a subtle dimpled stipple texture, paired with brushed stainless steel, rolled metal edges and sturdy riveted details. Further colourways include cream, navy, charcoal, matte black and soft pastel tones in powder-coated finishes. Slightly dented, workshop-honest, practical and durable, with a nostalgic workshop heritage feel. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
