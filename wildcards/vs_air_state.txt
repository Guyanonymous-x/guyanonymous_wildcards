taxiing on the runway with the landing lights on
taking off with the nose raised
in a steep bank turn
in a level cruise above clouds
landing with the wheels just touching down
parked on the apron with the engine covers on
parked in a hangar with the cowling open
flying low over water
descending with the flaps and wheels out
parked at night with the navigation lights glowing
with the passenger door open and a stair attached
in a loop with a smoke trail
