Styled in a design language of enchanted storybook animation. Rounded, soft, bouncy forms with squash and stretch, big expressive eyes, clean flowing ink outlines and flat cel colour with gentle shading. Painted backgrounds are lush, softly blended watercolour in storybook pastel and jewel tones, with fairy-tale castles, forests and glowing magical sparkle. Palette is bright but gentle, with royal blue, rosy pink, sunny gold and woodland green. Characters have graceful, exaggerated poses and clear silhouettes. Warm, wholesome, whimsical and musical. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
