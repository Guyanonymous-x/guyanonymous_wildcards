Styled in a design language of festive, tropical, confident and slightly retro heraldic design. Bright, clean white or cream with a red, black or gold border. Premium versions use deeper amber. Dark oak and iron hoops appear in the background. Sunny beaches, palm fronds and bright red umbrellas. Art Deco geometry in blue-and-white tiles with stepped crowns. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
