In the style of Fender, bright, twangy, sunny California mid-century. Sleek slab forms with offset contours, sunburst finishes fading from amber yellow through red to black, or solid Surf Green, Daphne Blue, Fiesta Red and cream. White, mint or tortoiseshell panels, chrome fittings, pale maple wood with black dot markers and gold-tan tweed with dark diagonal weave. Brown and silver nameplates with raised letters, black vinyl, silver cloth and chrome corner trim add rich surface variety.
In the style of Gibson, rich, warm, heavy and luxurious. Carved arched forms with a glossy cherry red or golden amber burst finish, cream binding around the edges and a flamed maple figure. Bold mother-of-pearl block and trapezoid inlays on dark rosewood, gold-plated fittings and deep black gloss. Surfaces glow with warm translucent lacquer and fine cracking like a patina of age. A swept-back pointed silhouette and diamond shapes recur as emblems.
In the style of Marshall Amplification, loud, bold, rock and roll and imposing. Matte black pebbled vinyl covers every surface, set against woven basket-weave cloth in cream, tan or black. Trim is gold or silver piping and rows of brass-toned metal studs and corner caps. Palette is black, cream, warm gold and chrome with occasional oxblood leather and brown. Shapes are blocky, rectangular and heavy with softly rounded edges and a stacked, monumental feel. Surfaces suggest tough road-worn craft, with bright metal glints against deep black.
In the style of Moog Music, warm, handcrafted, scientific vintage modernism. Accents of orange, red and cream appear sparingly. Forms are slanted, wedge-like and sturdy with softly eased corners, exposed joinery, and a laboratory-meets-furniture honesty. Monochrome stamped badges and tidy technical diagrams in black on white.
In the style of Roland, crisp, technical Japanese electronics identity. Palette is graphite black, charcoal grey, white and a signature orange with cobalt blue and red as secondary accents. Forms are angular, flat, wedge-profiled and slim with sharp edges, matte textured plastic and satin metal, and tidy grids of small coloured markings and fine white printed lines. Packaging uses simple flat colour fields, minimal and confident.
In the style of Yamaha Synthesizers, sleek, digital and early-electronic with soft grey and teal tones. Long slim cream-white or black plastic with flat membrane buttons in grey, a small blue-green backlit display, slim sliders and a full-length keyboard. Restrained, tidy and calm.
In the style of Teenage Engineering, minimalist Scandinavian-geek graphic design. The emblem is a chunky black hexagonal nut-like shape fused with a bold round form, set above a tiny. Everything is black on white or white on black with exact grids, thin technical outlines. Materials are anodised aluminium, matte white plastic and soft silicone in signal orange, light grey, cream and occasional pastel or neon pops. Forms are thin, flat, rectangular and precisely rounded, like clean engineering drawings made physical, playful and deadpan.
In the style of Korg, functional, technical, compact, precise late 1970s and 1980s electronic design with a futuristic hi-fi flavour. Long, shallow wedge shapes with a slanted metal control surface in brushed or anodised charcoal and silver, covered in rows of small round knobs, chunky slider caps and tiny lever switches in orange, red, blue and white. Side cheeks are dark stained wood or black vinyl. Cream white and matte black, square-edged elements. Cool grey digital mood.
