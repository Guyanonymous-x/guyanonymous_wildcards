two fires burn on the earth floor on either side of the center post, the smoke rising through holes in the roof
Lenape men and women in their thirties to sixties in cloth shirts, leggings and moccasins dance in a line around the fires, stopping at each carved face
a speaker in his sixties with a turtle shell rattle stands by the center post and tells the vision he dreamed
a strip of painted wampum and an armful of sacred cedar lie at the foot of the center post
two attendants in their forties stand by the center post holding the twelve prayer sticks, slender rods of peeled pale wood each as long as a forearm, ready to hand them to the dancers
