a wall bed folded up with a sofa in front of it, which is the single mechanism the whole dwelling depends on and which the occupant either operates twice a day for years or quietly stops folding, and which of those two it is tells you everything
a mirror the size of a wall, which is in almost every one of these because the only way to make eighteen square meters feel larger is optical and it genuinely works
a ceiling-mounted drying rack, because there is no room for an airer and often no space for a washing machine and drying laundry in a sealed micro-flat is a real and chronic problem that causes condensation
a drop-down table that seats two when fully extended and therefore determines the maximum number of guests the dwelling can host, which is a social fact and not a furniture one
a bicycle on a wall hook, which is both transport and decoration and is near-universal
a heat interface unit in a cupboard, because these are almost always on a communal heating system whose standing charge is a frequent source of complaint
a storage wall where every item in the household has one allocated place and the system collapses if anything is bought
a fold-down bed taking up most of the room
