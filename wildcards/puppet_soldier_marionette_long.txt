a toy-soldier marionette, about as tall as an adult's forearm. Its carved wooden face is painted with round red circles on the cheeks, a thin black mustache and wide staring eyes under a tall black hat of fur with a gold chin strap. It wears a bright red jacket with white crossed belts and gold buttons, blue trousers with a white stripe down each side and black painted boots. Its legs are stiff wooden pegs jointed only at the hip, so it marches stiffly when the bar rocks, and it carries a small wooden rifle on its shoulder. __puppet_soldier_marionette_var__.
