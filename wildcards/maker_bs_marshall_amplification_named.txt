In the style of Marshall Amplification, loud, stacked, rock and roll and imposing. Large black vinyl-covered volumes with a woven basket-weave cloth front in tan or black, trimmed with gold or silver piping and a bold white script mark on a gold or black panel. A row of chunky black skirted knobs with white numerals, a red jewel lamp and a rocker switch. Tall rectangular blocks stacked like a wall. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
