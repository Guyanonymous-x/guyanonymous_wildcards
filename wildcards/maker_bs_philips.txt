Styled in a design language of optimistic Dutch industrial design. The emblem is a rounded shield containing wavy parallel lines and several four-pointed stars, drawn in bold cobalt blue on white or in white on blue, with a wide, clean, uppercase sans-serif wordmark. Palette is strong electric blue, white, pale grey, with ivory, mustard, tangerine and pastel tints in older pieces and fresh cyan, navy and sunrise gold in newer ones. Forms are friendly, rounded, smooth and gently curved with glossy and soft matte plastics, bakelite, light wood and satin metal. Clear, calm, geometric graphics with generous spacing, cheerful and trustworthy. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
