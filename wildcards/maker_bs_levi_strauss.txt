Styled in a design language of durable American workwear heritage. Indigo denim in deep to faded blue with raised contrast stitching in orange-tan thread, rivets, bar-tack reinforcements and a small red tab accent. Surfaces show honest wear, whiskering, fading and visible twill diagonals. Graphics are vintage leather-patch and woodcut-style badges with two-horse-like pulling imagery, bold serif and slab lettering in cream, red and black. Construction is straight, sturdy and flat-felled with exposed seams. Casual, rugged, democratic, with warm, grainy, sun-bleached documentary photography.
