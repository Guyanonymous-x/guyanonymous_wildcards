Styled in a design language of thin white porcelain used as a canvas for striking graphic energy. Also a classic cobalt net pattern with gilded cell edges and small white relief bumps. Flat bold colour, dynamic diagonal compositions, a stern idealistic mood and a historical museum feel. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
