In the style of Tiger Balm, vivid, nostalgic, bold medicinal design. Tiny faceted hexagonal or octagonal forms in bright red or white, tightly wrapped in red or white printed paper, with small steel tins painted red and gold. The hero is a striding side-view big cat with a curved tail, drawn in an orange and black woodcut manner, set beside names in Latin and Chinese characters and tidy lines of tiny print. Vertical panels in red, yellow and green hold an arc of text and a leaping central figure. Cartoon animals, hand lettering and a rich orange, yellow and black palette. Old pharmacy feel, pungent and strong. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
