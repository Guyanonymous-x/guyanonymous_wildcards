In the style of Hispano-Suiza in the 1920s and 1930s, a grand luxury tourer with a long bonnet with louvres, a tall narrow radiator grille, and a stork mascot in flight on the radiator cap. Large freestanding round headlamps, flowing separate wings, running boards, and coachbuilt bodies often in polished wood or aluminium. Wheels are wire spokes or large steel discs with chrome. Paint in deep blue, burgundy, black, and silver with polished brass or nickel brightwork. Interior has deep buttoned leather, polished burr walnut, and fine instruments in round brass or chrome bezels. Mood is aristocratic, grand and sculptural.
