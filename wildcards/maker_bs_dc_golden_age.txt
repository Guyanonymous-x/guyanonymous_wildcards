Styled in a design language of muscular flat-coloured heroes in tight capes and trunks over bold chest symbols, stiff but powerful poses, bold black ink outlines and simple shading. Limited four-colour palette of red, blue, yellow and green with coarse halftone dots on yellowing newsprint, city skylines with art deco towers, dramatic scenes of villains and lurid rays, wartime patriotic motifs, bold stacked lettering and a naive pulp-adventure energy.
