Avalokiteshvara with a thousand arms, the bodhisattva of compassion in Tibetan art, a white bodhisattva with eleven serene heads stacked in tiers and a thousand arms spread like a great halo, wearing silk scarves, jeweled crowns, earrings and necklaces, each palm holding an eye and the central hands pressed together at the heart, on a lotus throne against a blue sky of clouds and rainbow light, the eleven faces calm and kind, stacked in tiers to a single small face at the top, the thousand arms spreading in a great circle like a wheel, an open eye painted in the palm of each hand, the scarves pastel pink and green, the jewels gold and red, set against a deep blue background
