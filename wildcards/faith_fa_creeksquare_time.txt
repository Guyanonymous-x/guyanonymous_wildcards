at noon in July, the air thick with heat, hard white light on the bare earth and deep cool shade under the leafy roofs, cicada husks on the posts
at first light, mist lying in the low ground, the sky pale pink behind the trees, the fire sunk to red coals and dew on the benches
in the late afternoon, thunderheads piled white and gray in the east, long gold light slanting through the trees and leaf shadows across the square
deep in a midsummer night, the fire blazing high and lighting a long spiral of men and women in their twenties to sixties stepping one behind another around it, orange light on the roofs and a black wall of trees beyond
