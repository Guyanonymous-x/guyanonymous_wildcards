In the style of Bosendorfer, Viennese concert grand pianos with a distinctive case in glossy black or rich warm wood, such as walnut, cherry or maple, with a longer, extended bass end of extra black keys. The rim is slender and curved, with a refined and quiet profile. Legs are carved and tapering, and the music desk is lace-like with a shallow ornate shape. Edging is thin, and trim in gold or brass is tasteful. Some models have elaborate inlay, gilt carving and painted panels in the Baroque and Secession manner. The mood is aristocratic, warm, ornate and old-world.
