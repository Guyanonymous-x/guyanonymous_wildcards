Styled in a design language of glamorous screen-style couture, lavishly embellished and sensual. Sheer net, tulle, georgette and silk are covered with sparkling sequins, crystals, beads, pearls and gold or silver thread embroidery concentrated along fitted areas, borders and trailing edges. Palette includes champagne, blush pink, ivory, emerald, wine red, midnight blue, black and metallic gold. Ornate gold accents complete the look. Glossy, red-carpet mood, high-contrast lighting and shimmering texture. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
