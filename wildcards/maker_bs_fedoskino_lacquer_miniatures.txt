Styled in a design language of glossy black lacquer built up layer upon layer, often with a bright red or vermilion hidden interior, carrying a small, finely detailed realistic painting framed by the dark. Edges may be framed in gold ornament, braids and flowers. The finish is mirror-smooth, deep and jewel-like in black, crimson, gold and sky blue. Polished, narrative and luxurious. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
