Styled in a design language of British portable record players in a suitcase-like case covered in two-tone vinyl, such as red and cream, turquoise and cream, or black and white check. Silver plastic control knobs, a front grille in cream cloth, a chrome-edged lid, a swing-out tone arm and a spindle for stacked records. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
