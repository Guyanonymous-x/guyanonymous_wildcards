Styled in a design language of muscular, heavy and handcrafted British grandeur. Wide, powerful, broad-shouldered forms with deep sculpted creases and a planted, substantial presence. Polished chrome and brushed steel accents, knurled metal texture, diamond-quilted leather in contrasting two-tone hides with fine piping and neat stitching, and open-pore burr wood veneer in glowing honey, walnut and dark brown. Palette of deep racing green, midnight blue, burgundy, cream, silver and black. A winged B emblem in a simple black and silver roundel with spread feathers repeats as the signature mark. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
