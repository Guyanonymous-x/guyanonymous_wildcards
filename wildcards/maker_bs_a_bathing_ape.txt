Styled in a design language of loud, cartoonish, hype-driven streetwear. Giant graphic mouths with white teeth and blue eyes run boldly across whole surfaces, and a simple cartoon head in profile serves as the mark. Camouflage appears in bright unexpected colours such as pink, purple, green, yellow and blue, with multi-colour gradients, stacked rainbow stripes, bold white lettering and glow-in-the-dark prints. Star-shaped glossy patent panels add shine. Proportions are oversized and boxy in heavy, cosy materials. Playful, colourful, hip-hop inspired urban street mood.
