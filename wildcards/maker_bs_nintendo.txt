Styled in a design language of compact rounded rectangles with chunky friendly proportions in glossy and matte plastic. Palette is bold signature red with white, light grey and black, plus bright primary or pastel accents in blue, yellow and green arranged in tidy symmetrical layouts. Packaging is clean and confident, with big flat colour fields, and cheerful illustrated characters in thick outlines. Cross shapes and small round coloured discs recur as motifs. Rounded, often in red or white. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
