on the open salt flat, the procession a long white line under the sky
in an avenue of carved salt-block sculptures, the platform passing between them
at the edge of the salt pans, workers raking white salt into heaps
at a salt cathedral cut inside a mine, pillars and arches of gray rock salt
on a thin sheet of water over the salt, the procession reflected below them
beside a long row of white salt heaps with pointed tops and steep sloping sides
at a stage of stacked salt blocks where the queen is crowned
