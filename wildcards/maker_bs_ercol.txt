In the style of Ercol, English post-war furniture in pale elm and beech, with slender spindle backs, sweeping hoops and curved arms, splayed tapering legs, and simple bentwood construction. Light blond finishes and darker stained elm with visible swirling grain, round-cornered sideboards with sliding doors and tapered feet, daybeds and stacking chairs with spoke-like backs. Cushions in rust, olive and mustard tweed. Warm, domestic, gentle, suburban 1950s sitting room.
