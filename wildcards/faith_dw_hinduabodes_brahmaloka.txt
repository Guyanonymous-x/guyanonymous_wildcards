in Brahmaloka, the highest world, Brahma sits on a lotus, an old man in his sixties with four bearded faces turned to the four directions and four arms holding palm-leaf books, a water pot, a rosary and a ladle, his white swan beside him
Brahma sits with Sarasvati, the goddess of learning, a woman in her twenties in white silk holding a vina across her lap, a white swan and a peacock beside her, in a palace of white light above all the other worlds
above all the realms Brahmaloka shines as a white lotus of a thousand petals in a sea of light, the sages, men in their sixties to their eighties with white beards and matted hair, sitting in meditation on its petals
