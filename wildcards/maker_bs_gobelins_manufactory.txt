In the style of Gobelins Manufactory, huge woven wool and silk wall tapestries that look like paintings, with dense hatching of finely graded coloured threads. Compositions are grand and theatrical, showing mythological and historical scenes, hunting parties, hanging garlands and architectural frames with heavy woven borders of acanthus, laurel, fruit, shields and gilt-looking scrollwork. Palette is deep and slightly aged, with crimson, royal blue, forest green, ochre, gold and warm brown, plus pale highlights. Figures are idealised and baroque in pose with billowing drapery and dramatic clouds, and the weave is tight, flat and smooth.
