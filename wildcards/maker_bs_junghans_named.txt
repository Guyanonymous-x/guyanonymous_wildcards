In the style of Junghans, pared-back, functional and disciplined modernist. Round or rectangular faces with extremely clean layouts, slim baton or simple stick hands, thin black line markers or short numerals in a geometric sans-serif, and gently domed glass. Matte steel or gold with minimal detail. Colours are white, black and grey with occasional warm orange or yellow accents. Small cylinders or squares of off-white enamel or wood with tiny perforation details. Quiet and rational. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
