Styled in a design language of polished French modernism with streamlined, pointed forms in a glossy two-tone scheme of blue-grey and deep silver or white, accented by a sweeping carmine red or magenta slash. Rounded smooth volumes and slim openings. Clean pictograms in white on dark blue and carmine, modern glass roofing and a cool blue-grey scheme. Elegant, clean curves, fluid and streamlined. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
