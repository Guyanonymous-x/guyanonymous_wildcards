__bs_subject_media__ styled in a design language of flat limited-colour cartoon design with confident thick dark outlines and solid fills with almost no shading. Shapes are simple, blocky and rounded, with big ovals, wide stylised noses and bold geometric simplification of anything natural. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
