Styled in a design language of venetian hand-blown glass in lavish, light-filled forms, with delicate curved arms spreading from a central axis and bearing glass flowers, leaves and curling ribbons in clear crystal, rose pink, aqua and amber. Ornate scrolling branches and floral cups sit beside mid-century sculptural versions with cascading rods, tubes and ball drops in white and gold. Glossy, translucent, festive, ornate and glittering like a palace ballroom.
