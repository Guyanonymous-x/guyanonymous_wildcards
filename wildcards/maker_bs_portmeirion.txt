Styled in a design language of lush, abundant botanical decoration embossed and printed on a cream or white ground. Naturalistic flowers, ferns, fruit, butterflies and bees are crowded with leaves and vines in the manner of a Victorian botanical print book. Rich colours of burgundy, fern green, gold and cobalt blue. Chunky forms, scalloped and wide-edged outlines and relief texture beneath the print. Cottage garden, apothecary, cosy and plentiful. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
