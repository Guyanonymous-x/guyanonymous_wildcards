Styled in a design language of athletic motion and defiant confidence. Forms are streamlined and aerodynamic, with sweeping check-like curves, sharp tapered cuts and layered panels that suggest speed. Palette is stark black, white and grey with a single electric accent such as volt yellow, hot orange or crimson, and bold colour-blocked contrasts. Technical mesh, foam, rubber and glossy synthetic skins show engineered detail. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
