Styled in a design language of richly woven silk brocade with metallic gold and silver foil threads, fine glossy surfaces, dense jacquard patterns and a subtle changing sheen. Patterns include geometric lattices, waves, clouds, flowers, bark textures and abstract digital-like lines in very fine repeat. Colours are deep ink black, vermilion red, indigo, ivory, aged gold, silver and soft muted purple, with tiny metallic sparkle. The cloth is stiff, flat and ornate, suited to architectural surfaces as well as dress, with a thin layered gleam where metallic strips meet silk. Traditional and contemporary at once, luxurious, patient and handcrafted, in soft side light. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
