In the style of Swissair, rigorous Swiss International Style design built on pure red and white. A bold red field carries a white square-armed cross, and a slanted parallelogram of red cuts diagonally across white surfaces as a crisp shape. Lettering is lowercase, heavy, tight Helvetica-like grotesque in red or white, flush left, with immaculate spacing and a grid-based layout. Finishes are glossy enamel and flat printed colour with sharp edges, set against bare white with generous empty space. A secondary palette of dark grey, silver and black is used sparingly for fine rules and small captions. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Swissair in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
