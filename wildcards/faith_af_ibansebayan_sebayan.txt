Sebayan, the land of the dead, a long longhouse on tall posts beside a broad river, the dead men and women in their thirties to eighties going about their work on its long open porch of split bamboo
in the older dress, the women of Sebayan in their twenties to sixties are bare above the waist in short woven skirts with silver belts and brass coils, the men in loincloths and hornbill feathers
in present-day dress, the dead of Sebayan, women in their twenties to sixties in woven ikat sarongs and blouses and men in shirts and sarongs, greet the newly dead at the river landing
