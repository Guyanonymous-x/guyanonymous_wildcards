Nine in the morning, the room aired with the sashes up and the brass flaps open for an hour whatever the weather
Mid-morning, laid for dinner, three hours of work for two footmen and the butler, measuring from the table edge with a stick
Four in the afternoon, the fire lit so the room will be warm by eight
Dinner itself, two and a half hours and five services
Half past ten, the women gone to the drawing room and the men still at the table with the decanters, the chamber pot in use
Midnight, the table cleared and the flaps open again
The small hours, cold, the candle ends collected into a box for the servants' perquisite
