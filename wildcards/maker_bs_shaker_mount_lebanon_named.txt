Styled in a design language of plain and sturdy construction in pine, maple and cherry with no ornament. Slender turned posts, arching slats, woven tape or rush texture and tapered legs give light, upright forms. Flat panels, simple pegs, finger joints, small wooden knobs and a smooth surface define the details. Colours are muted milk-paint tones, barn red, mustard yellow, olive green, blue and natural honey wood. Thin bent wood bands with swallowtail joints and copper tacks hold shapes together. The mood is serene, orderly, utilitarian and pure. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
