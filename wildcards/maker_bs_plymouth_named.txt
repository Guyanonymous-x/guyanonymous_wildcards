Styled in a design language of bold and cheeky American design from the late 1950s through the 1960s. Wide, flat, low proportions with long sculpted sides, a sweeping two-tone contrast line and bright chrome bars running the full width. Saturated purple, vivid orange, lemon yellow and bright green against shiny metal. Confident, playful, exaggerated and optimistic, with a love of speed and showmanship. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
