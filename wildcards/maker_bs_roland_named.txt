Styled in a design language of crisp, technical Japanese electronics identity. Palette is graphite black, charcoal grey, white and a signature orange with cobalt blue and red as secondary accents. Forms are angular, flat, wedge-profiled and slim with sharp edges, matte textured plastic and satin metal, and tidy grids of small coloured markings and fine white printed lines. Packaging uses simple flat colour fields, minimal and confident. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
