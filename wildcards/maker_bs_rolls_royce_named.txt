In the style of Rolls-Royce, hushed, monumental and hand-built grandeur. Tall formal verticals, long calm unbroken surfaces and a fine bright line drawn along the length of a form, finished with a tiny sculpted silver figure leaning into the wind as a crowning ornament. Mirror-polished stainless metal, slim vertical fluting, deep lustrous paint in claret, midnight blue, silver and black, often two-toned. Interiors in cream, tan and oxblood leather with tight hand stitching, deep wool pile, glossy burr wood veneer with delicate inlay, and tiny pinpoint lights glowing like a night sky overhead. Interlocked double-R monogram in restrained serif lettering. Photography is dim, warm, reverent and quiet, with soft reflections.
