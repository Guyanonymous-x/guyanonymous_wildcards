Styled in a design language of sleek, dark, formal, sculptural and timeless. Perfectly symmetrical, hand-built forms with a full rounded volume and a crisp change of angle where the curve turns. The surface is burnished to a glass-smooth gloss and smoked to a deep jet black. Designs appear in matte black against the shining black field, visible only as light shifts across them. Motifs are bold and graphic, such as coiled serpents, rain clouds, stepped shapes, lightning zigzags, mountains and small tracks, arranged in one confident band. Perfect balance, calm and ceremonial. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
