In the style of El Al, crisp, clean, secure, patriotic and cool identity in blue and white. White grounds with a sweeping blue line outlined in silver, curving and fading along the form, with plain white or blue areas and a flag. Interiors use blue with white accents, slim wood-look trim and blue and white carpets. Smart navy tailoring with pale blue shirts. Sunlit blue sky.
