In the style of Maserati, graceful, smooth, refined and elegant Italian grand touring. A long mass, a tight sloping line, a large oval shape with vertical chrome bars bearing a trident mark, boomerang-shaped forms and wide sculpted curves. Deep blue, silver, dark burgundy, pearl white and black. Tan, red or cream leather with fine stitching. Polished wood or carbon trim and a single analogue clock at the centre of the panel add refinement.
