In the style of Thai Airways, a Thai flag carrier identity in orchid purple and gold. Aircraft have a white body with a sweeping band from the nose to the tail in shades of magenta, violet and gold, merging into a curved tail fin whose logo is a tall stylised flame like roof curve, a pair of hands pressed together in a traditional greeting, built from gold and magenta. The name is set in a softly rounded italic sans serif in purple. Cabins are decked in purple and orange seats, silk-like fabrics in orchid pink, and patterned carpets, with orchids on the head rests. Crew uniforms are silk wrap skirts and jackets in orchid purple, gold or magenta, with a small orchid corsage and a neat bun. Special liveries use royal barges and classical dancers in gold. Mood is graceful, silky, ornate, warm and festive, with a rich palette of violet, magenta and gold.
