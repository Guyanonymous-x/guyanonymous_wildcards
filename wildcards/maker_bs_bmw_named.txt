Styled in a design language of precise, driver-focused and athletic. A crisp, low, forward-leaning stance with a sharp shoulder line running end to end and a twin rounded-slot motif repeated as a face. A round blue and white quartered badge inside a black ring is the key mark. Tricolour motorsport stripes in light blue, dark blue and red run as thin parallel bands on any surface or trim. Palette is silver, black, white, deep blue and grey with sparing red. Materials are brushed metal, carbon weave, perforated leather and clean instrument-style dials angled toward the viewer. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
