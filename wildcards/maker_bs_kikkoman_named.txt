In the style of Kikkoman, practical, precise and restrained design with a sense of effortless function. Tall, narrow, softly curved and faceted forms in clear glassy material with dark amber-brown depth, finished with red accents and a conical taper. Plain white fields carry a bold red-and-black hexagonal crest, black and red calligraphic lettering and a thin gold border. Palette is deep red, black, white and amber. Mood is clean, ergonomic, understated and instantly recognisable at a glance, where every curve serves use.
