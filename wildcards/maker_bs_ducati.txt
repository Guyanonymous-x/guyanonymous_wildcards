Styled in a design language of fast, sculpted, muscular and racing-bred Italian intensity. Glossy lacquer red dominates in broad colour fields with matte black, bright white and brushed silver as sharp counterpoints. Forms are sharply tapered, with angular facets, aggressive creases and a taut, coiled, ready-to-pounce stance. Shield-shaped badges in red, white and green appear in small crisp enamel or raised gel finishes, often with domed edges. Details use carbon-fibre weave, anodised gold accents, mesh and fine contrast stitching. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
