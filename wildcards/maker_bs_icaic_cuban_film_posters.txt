Styled in a design language of vibrant, witty, politically charged and strongly handmade silkscreen design. A few flat, saturated colours in layers with no gradients, bright red, yellow, cobalt blue, orange, green, pink and black, laid down one screen at a time on cheap rough paper with slightly uneven edges and visible misregistration. Lettering is hand-drawn, chunky, irregular, sometimes stencil-like. Pop art, psychedelia and constructivism blended with satire. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
