In the style of Penkala, Croatian fountain pens and mechanical pencils. The pens are slim cylindrical writing instruments with a hard rubber or celluloid barrel in glossy black, mottled red-and-black, pale marbled tones, dark blue or olive, with gold-plated or nickel bands near the cap, a tapered cap end, a long clip with a small ball tip, and a gold nib with a tiny scroll engraving. The mechanical pencils are metal tubes with fine ribbed or knurled grips, silver or gold finish, geometric engraved stripes, and a long sliding mechanism, often with an elegant tassel loop. Boxes are small hinged cases lined with velvet in dark green or burgundy, with a gold-stamped lid. The look is precise, early Art Deco, slim and quietly luxurious. Everything is made with a hand-finished glow, smooth seams, tiny engraved lines and a small stamped maker mark near the clip, giving a jewel-like desk object feel in muted period colours.
