Styled in a design language of modern California craftsmanship, clean, bright and precise. Wood is light, finely grained and blond, with koa, sapele and rosewood in rich reddish-brown for contrast. Finishes are very thin, flawless and glossy, with smooth rounded bevelled edges and a sleek, slim, contemporary silhouette. Inlay is minimal, with slender abalone and mother-of-pearl in organic shapes of birds, feathers, vines and leaves, with fine black and cream edge lines. Palette is honey, caramel, cream, chestnut and burgundy. Friendly, polished and refined. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
