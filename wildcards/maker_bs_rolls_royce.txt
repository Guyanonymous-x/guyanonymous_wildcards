In the style of Rolls-Royce, a monumental upright limousine with a tall flat-topped radiator shell of polished steel slats crowned by a small winged lady mascot, long bonnet, flat slab sides, and a formal near-vertical windscreen. Rear-hinged coach doors, thin chrome swage line along the flanks, large round or rectangular headlamps set wide beside the grille, and tall narrow wheels with plain polished hubcaps carrying a small double-R badge. Two-tone paint is classic, deep claret, silver sand, midnight blue, or black over silver. Interior is hand-stitched cream or tan leather, deep lambswool carpets, burr walnut veneer with fine inlay, chrome organ-stop vents, slim thin-rimmed steering wheel and a pleated headlining. Mood is hushed, imposing, dignified, with mirror-polished brightwork and long smooth rolling proportions.
