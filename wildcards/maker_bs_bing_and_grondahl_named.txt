In the style of Bing and Grondahl, Danish thin white porcelain in soft blue-grey and white, with naturalistic Art Nouveau brushwork of seabirds, herons, stylised flowers and tall reeds in shaded cobalt. Muted pastel greys, creams, sea blues and lilac, smooth curved forms, delicate relief moulding with winter village scenes, and crackled and sculpted surfaces. Delicate, serene and quietly poetic. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Bing and Grondahl in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
