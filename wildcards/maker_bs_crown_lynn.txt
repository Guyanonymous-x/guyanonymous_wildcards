In the style of Crown Lynn, New Zealand mid-century ceramics. Everyday tableware is thick, durable and rounded, including heavy white cups, bowls, plates, jugs and teapots in a glossy glaze, along with the famous swan vases with an elegant curved neck and a hollow back, produced in white, matte pastel, pink, turquoise and black. Glazes include speckled turquoise, mustard yellow, tangerine, sage green, a dripping trickle glaze where one colour runs over another, and dark manganese brown with scratched-line sgraffito patterns. Decals show modern abstract florals, bamboo, diamond shapes, starburst, ferns and Maori-inspired koru spirals in mid-century colours. A classic cafe set has a deep brown-and-turquoise cup with a chunky handle and shallow saucer. Shapes are plump, rounded and friendly with simple lines. The mood is retro, warm, optimistic and distinctly 1950s and 1960s New Zealand.
