Styled in a design language of a glossy black lacquer ground, sometimes red, blue, green or white, carrying a lush bouquet of large garden and wild flowers loosely tossed with leaves, buds and fruit. Painting is quick and full-brushed with soft shaded modelling, in oil colours of deep crimson, pink, orange, yellow, blue and fresh green against the dark. A smaller scatter of blossoms and berries surrounds the main bunch. Edges are trimmed with gold or silver scrolls, dots and little leaf ornaments. The finish is polished to a mirror shine under many layers of lacquer. Lush, sumptuous, festive and warm.
