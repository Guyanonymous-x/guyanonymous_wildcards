the manda, the grave grove, stands beside the village as a thick dark wall of uncut trees and tangled bush, a narrow path leading into it between two big trees
a hidden clearing deep in the grove where the dancers dress, piles of straw, rags and painted wooden masks laid on the ground and a small fire burning
tall old trees close over the grove, their roots and vines twisting among the mounds, shafts of pale light falling into the dark
