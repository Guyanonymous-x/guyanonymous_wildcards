the Arian Baptistery, a small eight-sided brick building with a low roof, a squat tower of thin warm red-brown bricks with a round-arched window high in each face and a shallow tiled roof, small rounded chapels bulging from its lower walls, its base sunk below the level of the paved square around it
the baptistery seen from the street, plain brick with small windows, the weathered red and ocher brickwork rising behind a low iron railing at the end of a narrow lane, bare of all ornament except a thin band of brick teeth under the eaves, a little cross on the roof peak
the baptistery's inside, bare brick walls below a shining dome, a single tall eight-sided room stripped down to rough red brick and patches of old plaster, empty except for the worn stone floor, with the whole round ceiling overhead a bowl of glittering gold mosaic
