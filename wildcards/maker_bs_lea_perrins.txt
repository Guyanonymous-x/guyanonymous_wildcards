Styled in a design language of dense, ornate Victorian heritage design. Deep glossy near-black glass with gold-tinted small type, and squat rectangular forms. Palette is orange, black, cream and tan, with a pale green stripe accent. Traditional, authentic, old English and reassuring. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
