Indonesian wayang golek rod puppets, carved and painted wooden figures about as tall as an adult's arm. Each puppet has a carved wooden head painted in strong colors, a pink or red face for a hot-tempered figure and a pale gold one for a calm noble, with narrow curving eyes, a long nose and a mouth with a painted mustache. A central rod runs down through the body and comes out under the skirt as a handle, and the head turns on it. __puppet_wayang_golek_var__.
