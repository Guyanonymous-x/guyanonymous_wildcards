Styled in a design language of handwoven wool with a deep brick red ground, charcoal black, ivory and soft grey, in strong contrast. A bold central diamond with stepped, serrated edges, or a cross, sits in the centre, with smaller diamonds or hooks echoing in the corners. A single frame with notches or crosses surrounds it with neat edges. The surface is tight and flat with a slight handspun irregularity. Geometry is crisp, rectilinear and strongly symmetrical, with a warm desert look. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
