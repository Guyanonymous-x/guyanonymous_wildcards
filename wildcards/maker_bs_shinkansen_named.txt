In the style of Shinkansen, sleek, precise, futuristic Japanese engineering expressed through clean colour blocking. Glossy pearl white and pale grey surfaces are cut by a single long, tapering stripe in deep cobalt or navy, sometimes joined by a thin line of sky blue, green, red or gold. Shapes are streamlined, with long smooth curves, softly pointed tips and seamless panel joins that feel aerodynamic. Finishes are lacquer-like, satin and flawless, with pale aluminium accents. Lettering is neat, geometric sans-serif in both Latin and Japanese characters, aligned on strict grids with calm, even spacing. Small circular and wing-like emblems appear in crisp flat vector form, with a feeling of speed, order, safety and quiet confidence. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
