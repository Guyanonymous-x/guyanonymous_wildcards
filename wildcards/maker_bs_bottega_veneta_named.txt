In the style of Bottega Veneta, quiet luxury built on woven leather, thick flat ribbons interlaced in a tight, even, diagonal checkerboard weave that covers the whole surface of any object. The weave is supple, plump and slightly puffy, sometimes oversized and chunky, sometimes in fine knotted braids. Colours are rich and unshowy, such as pale powder blue, butter cream, parakeet green, lilac, burnt orange, taupe, chocolate, ruby red and black, often one saturated tone per piece. Metal details are sparse, polished gold or brass, in simple triangular, knot-like or bar forms. No external logos, only tiny discreet lettering. Understated, tactile, soft, sculptural and wonderfully crafted, with a sleek, modern, sophisticated calm. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Bottega Veneta in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
