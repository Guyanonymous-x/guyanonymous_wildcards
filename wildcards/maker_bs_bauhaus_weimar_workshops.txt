Styled in a design language of expressionist and early constructivist work in wood, metal, glass and textile. Forms are built from stacked cubes, slats and planes in primary red, yellow, blue, black and white, with bold geometric shapes such as circle, square and triangle. Woven abstracts are compositions of rectangles and stripes in saturated, earthy reds, blues, ochres and black. Rounded cylindrical forms use nickel or plain glaze. The mood is utopian, experimental, geometric and artisan. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
