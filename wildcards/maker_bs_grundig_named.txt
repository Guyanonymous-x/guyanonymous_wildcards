In the style of Grundig, dependable postwar German craftsmanship. A heraldic crest emblem with a small crown above a shield holding a three-leaf clover shape in black and white, beside a wide, rounded, bold uppercase wordmark in orange, red or deep blue. Palette is walnut brown, cream, soft gold, hammered grey-green, black and bright orange. Materials are figured wood veneer, brushed gold and chrome metal trim, enamelled badge plates, cream and brown plastic and fine woven fabric. Forms are plain, solid, gently curved and carefully proportioned with neat embossed lettering, reliable, modest and long-lasting in character. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries only the words Grundig in that lettering style and no other words. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
