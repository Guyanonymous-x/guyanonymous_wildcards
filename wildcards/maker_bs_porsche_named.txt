Styled in a design language of athletic, low and compact, design driven by function. Smooth, rounded, sculpted forms with a sloping, curved profile, soft arching haunches, round pairs of lamps and clean, taut surfaces with minimal trim. Finishes are glossy paint, satin black, brushed aluminium, titanium and carbon weave in silver, black, graphite, white, guards red and deep blue, with occasional bright accent colours. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
