Styled in a design language of bold, oversized and confidently sensual graphic design with a gallery-grade polish. Forms are large clean rectangles with crisp square edges and generous margins. Surfaces are thick matte or satin boards, linen, leatherette and glossy printed art, with foil stamping and embossing used sparingly. Palette is rich saturated colour, a single flat field of deep red, jet black, cream or electric yellow, set beside vivid full-bleed imagery of strong contrast. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
