Styled in a design language of witty, surreal, cheeky Italian pop design. Recurring motifs are a solid red heart, a pair of mirrored question marks inside a circle, a teddy-bear silhouette drawn in simple black and white line, and oversized playful icons. Palette is jet black, chalk white, signal red, hot pink, sunny yellow and glittering gold. Surfaces include glossy enamel, black sequins, gold foil, quilted metallic sheen and flat printed colour. Trompe-l'oeil humour turns ordinary forms into ironic, exaggerated parodies. The surfaces are plain and unmarked, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
