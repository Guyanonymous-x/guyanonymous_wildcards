Styled in a design language of quirky, futuristic and a bit strange American design of the 1950s. Low, wide stance, a slim body, a low profile, an almost European flat-sided silhouette and a gently tapering end. Chrome edges, pointed bullet-nose forms like an aircraft spinner and a streamlined panel with a big round dial and brushed metal. Gold or turquoise tops. Pale pastel two-tone paint in pink and white, sky blue and cream. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
