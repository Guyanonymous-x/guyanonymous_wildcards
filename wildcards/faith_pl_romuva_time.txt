on a summer night, the fire glowing, a short pale northern night with a deep blue sky and a few stars above the black crowns of the oaks, the flames throwing warm orange light and long moving shadows across the trunks and the hanging curtain
in fall, the oaks gold, every crown turned to bronze, russet and deep yellow, leaves spinning down through low slanting sunlight and lying thick on the ground among the acorns, thin mist between the far trunks
in winter, snow, a deep even white cover over the ground and along every limb of the bare black oaks, the fire a single spot of orange in the gray light, its smoke rising straight into a heavy pale sky
