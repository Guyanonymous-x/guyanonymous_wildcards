In the style of Lotte Confectionery, Japanese and Korean sweets packaging with cheerful animal mascots. Chewing gum packs are slim mint green or cool blue sleeves with a cartoon penguin on an iceberg wearing a tiny bow tie, drawn in smooth outline with a bright white belly. Biscuits come in a small hexagonal box in pastel blue and white with a round-eyed koala bear mascot in a different pose on each pack, and the bear shaped cookies are cream filled with a printed face on top. Chocolate bars are wrapped in rich red, gold and brown foil with an embossed curved wordmark and shaped squares inside. Snack sticks sit in thin tubes and bright boxes of orange, yellow and green with playful bouncing type. Surfaces are glossy, graphic and clear. Mood is cute, light, childlike and refreshing, with flat colour, bold outlines, whimsical animals and a nostalgic convenience store candy shelf.
