Styled in a design language of restrained Parisian elegance built on black and white. Strict monochrome of black, ivory and cream with gold, pearls and chains as the only sparkle, and a quiet touch of beige or pale pink. Forms are simple, boxy, balanced and tailored, with quilted diamond surfaces, braided trim, tweed boucle texture and interlocked double-C style marks. Lettering is thin, spaced, upright sans-serif or clean classic capitals. Nothing fussy, minimal ornament chosen with precision, luxurious soft materials and crisp craft. Photography is monochrome, high-contrast and refined, with timeless, understated chic.
