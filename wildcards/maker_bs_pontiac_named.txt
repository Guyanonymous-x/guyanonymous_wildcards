Styled in a design language of aggressive, swaggering street-racer American muscle of the 1960s and 70s. Split twin-nostril shapes divided by a body-coloured beak, stacked or hidden forms, a waistline swelling over tall haunches, one long raised bulge and bold parallel stripes. Brushed metal rally patterns with bright trim rings and a red arrowhead mark. Carousel red, deep green, ivory and bright orange. Black vinyl bucket shapes with engine-turned panels add attitude. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
