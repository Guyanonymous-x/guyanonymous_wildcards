beside him a man in his forties slaughters a camel as an offering, its blood running onto the ground, the meat cut for the poor
around him stand the many idols of the Kaaba, stone figures and standing stones, some draped in cloth, the walls hung with striped cloth
worshipers stand before him, men in their thirties and forties with long hair and beards in wrapped white garments, their hands raised in prayer
