Styled in a design language of bold, oversized and confidently sensual graphic design with a gallery-grade polish. Forms are large clean rectangles with crisp square edges and generous margins. Surfaces are thick matte or satin boards, linen, leatherette and glossy printed art, with foil stamping and embossing used sparingly. Palette is rich saturated colour, a single flat field of deep red, jet black, cream or electric yellow, set beside vivid full-bleed imagery of strong contrast. Lettering is tidy, modern sans-serif, small and spaced, in white or black, with a tiny wordmark in a corner. Any label, tab, tag, badge, packaging or sign on the object carries no readable words or letters, only the marks, shapes and patterns described. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
