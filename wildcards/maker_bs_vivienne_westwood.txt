Styled in a design language of theatrical, rebellious and romantic British design that mixes punk with historical grandeur. Tartan is the main pattern, in clashing red, black, green, yellow, pink and lilac checks, cut on the bias and often twisted. Materials include crushed velvet, brocade, corset-like satin, leather, pearls, chains and safety-pin hardware. Shapes are asymmetric, draped, nipped and exaggerated, with ruffles and bows, both aristocratic and defiantly anarchic. The surfaces are smooth and plain, and the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern alone. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
