In the style of Sprungli, small round pastel truffles and rectangular pralines nestled in pleated paper cups inside slim boxes in turquoise-blue, silver and cream. Tall elegant tea-room interiors with pale marble tables, mirrored walls, and polished wooden bars, set on a Swiss lakeside square. Wordmark in a clean, thin, spaced serif in silver or white on pale aqua blue, with fine linear borders. Palette of pale aqua, silver, cream and chocolate brown. The look is genteel, Swiss, understated, delicate confectionery craft, with soft pastel boxes and refined restraint.
