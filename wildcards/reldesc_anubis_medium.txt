Anubis, the god of embalming, a tall god with the sleek black head of a jackal, pointed ears and golden eyes, a muscular human body, wearing a striped royal headdress, a broad gold and lapis collar, gold arm bands and a white pleated kilt, his jackal head glossy black with sharp ears and a long muzzle, his eyes rimmed with gold, the collar of lapis blue and gold beads in many rows, the kilt crisp and white, the scales of gold with a heart on one pan and a white feather on the other, torchlight flickering on painted hieroglyphs on the walls
