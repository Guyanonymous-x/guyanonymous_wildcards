Styled in a design language of technical, balanced and flexible engineering shown honestly. Slender jointed members, exposed coiled springs and small pivots make a lean, angular, skeletal geometry, finished in crisp, tidy, engineered details. Enamelled and powder-coated metal in jet black, cream, pale grey, signal red, soft teal and mustard sits beside polished brass, chrome and brushed steel. Surfaces are smooth, satin and slightly industrial, with tidy rivets and neatly turned details. Inspired by technical drawing, functional British modernism, precise, witty and understated. There is no large logo, wordmark or lettering on the main surfaces, because the style is carried by colour, shape, material and pattern. Any label, tag, badge, packaging or sign that appears is small and discreet, and the only words on it are "__name_brand_invented__", with no other words or letters anywhere. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
