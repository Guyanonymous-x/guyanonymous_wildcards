In the style of Lufthansa, a white fuselage with a dark blue tail carrying a circular yellow disc enclosing a stylised crane in flight, drawn in a few strokes, with dark blue lettering in a clean, sober sans-serif. A thin blue stripe along the belly in earlier variants. Yellow and blue are paired on documents and in-flight items, with cobalt blue seats and a grey interior. Palette of deep navy, canary yellow, white and grey. The look is orderly, efficient, geometric, modernist, and strictly typographic, with a bird in a circle as the seal.
