In the style of Zeppelin, stately, monumental, silver-giant 1920s-30s elegance. An enormous cigar-shaped form stretched taut over a light frame, covered in silvery metallic cloth, with a small streamlined volume beneath and neat rows of windows. Cruciform tail shapes and bold black angular lettering along the length, plus a red accent at the rear. Smooth, gleaming, serene and huge, with a majestic, floating, slow-moving presence and a dignified period grandeur.
