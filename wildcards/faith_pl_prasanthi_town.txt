the small town of Puttaparthi, narrow busy streets of low concrete shops with open fronts selling framed photos of the guru, flower garlands, beads and souvenirs, with motor rickshaws and motorbikes parked along the edges
dry rocky hills rising all around the town, slopes of red-brown earth strewn with huge rounded granite boulders and dotted with thorn bushes and thin dusty trees under a wide pale sky
the bed of the Chitravathi river at the edge of the town, a wide flat stretch of pale sand with a few shallow pools and thin streams of water, low green bushes along its banks
