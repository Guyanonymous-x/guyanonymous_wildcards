Styled in a design language of frugal, dour, boxy Soviet-era practicality. Simple squared-off massing built from flat panels, thin chrome edging, short overhangs and an upright posture. Details are plain, round or rectangular, with chrome rims and one horizontal bar as the only flourish. Generous glass, slim supports, small rectangular accents in orange and red. Brown or beige vinyl and brown plastic textures indoors. Palette of dusty pink, beige, ochre, brown, dark green and cherry red. Modest, utilitarian and quietly stubborn, with no attempt at glamour. This styling applies to the object itself and not to its surroundings.
