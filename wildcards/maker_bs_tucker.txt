Styled in a design language of futuristic and streamlined postwar American design. A very low, smooth, rounded teardrop body, a narrow chrome-bar front with a wide mouth and a swept-back profile. Chrome edges and a thin trim line, a padded warm interior and a central cluster of round gauges. Waltz blue, beige, maroon and dark green. Small whitewall roundness and a fully enclosed louvred finish at the back add futurism.
