In the style of Iznik Ceramic Workshops, white quartz-frit ceramic painted under a brilliant clear glaze with vivid fresh colour. The characteristic palette is cobalt blue, turquoise, emerald green and a thick raised tomato-red, drawn crisply with black outlines on a bright white ground. Patterns are tulips, carnations, hyacinths, rosebuds, saz leaves, curling feathery leaves, and cloud bands arranged symmetrically in flowing arabesques. Tiles, plates, jugs and mosque lamps are glossy, with a crisp, fresh look and rhythmic, balanced repetition. Large wall panels form garden-like compositions around arches and borders.
